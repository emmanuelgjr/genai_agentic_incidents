## How to read the evidence column — and why this distinction is in the document

Two evidence classes, marked per row. The distinction is not pedantry: it is the
honest answer to "re-derived, not recalled", and it was prompted by a gate's own
statement of its limits during the D8 re-verify, which is worth quoting because
this document inherits the same constraint:

> *"I can verify that the repo records 'OECD task PASS' and that the citation
> resolves to it. I **cannot** independently verify the gate's verdict text —
> that exists only in a session transcript, not in the repo. … Verified to a
> contemporaneous foreman record, not to the gate's own words."*

- **[R] Re-derived** — measured from the repo on 2026-07-30, with the command or
  file:line shown. Anyone can reproduce it.
- **[A] Attested** — the deliverable's artifacts are present and verifiable, and
  a gate PASS is recorded contemporaneously on `PROGRESS.md`, but the reviewer's
  verdict text itself exists only in session transcripts. **The artifact is
  evidence; the verdict is testimony.** Rows marked [A] are not weaker claims
  about whether the work exists — they are honest about what kind of record
  supports "it was independently gated".

**No row below is marked ✅ on [A] evidence alone where the criterion asserts a
measurable property.** Where a criterion asserts a *property of the repo*, that
property is re-derived.

## D19 packet — v2.9.0 release notes, DRAFT for user approval

**Not committed as a release. Draft only.** Version ruled by D19: **v2.9.0** —
minor, not patch, because it ships the licensing rebuild, the markers and the
notice surfaces (additive schema and data changes, no API break).

> ### v2.9.0 — Honest provenance
>
> This release completes the "Honest" phase of the improvement plan. It changes
> how the dataset describes its own provenance, licensing and freshness. No
> consumer-facing field was removed; the schema additions are additive.
>
> **Corpus:** 12,986 → **13,119** entries (+133 net; landmark 1,905). No entry
> was deleted — status and tombstones only (invariant 3).
>
> **Licensing and provenance**
> - **Row-level content-licence marking.** 1,517 AIAAIC-derived rows carry
>   `content_license: CC-BY-SA-4.0`, machine-readable, with the same value
>   mirrored into STIX as `x_content_license`. Rationale in `docs/audits/` (E13,
>   D11).
> - **AIAAIC facts-reduction.** AIAAIC-derived rows carry facts and a source
>   pointer rather than upstream narrative prose (D2/D9/D10).
> - **AIID ingestion moved to the sanctioned snapshot channel.** The former
>   high-volume scraper is disabled; AIID data now comes from AIID's own weekly
>   snapshot, with a committed pinned provenance record. AIID's excluded
>   `reports.text` is never opened.
> - **Source-licence register** (`docs/SOURCE_LICENSES.md`) now resolves every
>   row: **two** remain pending-with-dates (MIT AIRI Navigator, OECD AIM), down
>   from three.
>
> **Freshness honesty**
> - **`source_freshness`** ships on 1,380 rows derived from a source whose feed
>   went stale on 2026-05-31, carrying `status`, `as_of` and `sources`. `active`
>   means *emitted, not live*. Defined in `docs/DATA_DICTIONARY.md` and on the
>   HF card.
> - A published **source-freshness registry** (`data/source_freshness.json`)
>   declares which copy of the health counter it was reconciled against, and
>   `validate.py` holds it to that claim — reporting **VERIFIED**, **NOT
>   COMPARABLE HERE** or **FAILED** distinguishably. Passing means the registry
>   matches the copy it read; it is **not** a claim that the registry is current.
>
> **Ingestion conduct**
> - **A single network chokepoint.** All HTTP(S) fetching routes through
>   `ingest/common.py`, which enforces an identifying User-Agent, a fail-closed
>   robots.txt check and per-host rate limiting, with one enumerated, evidenced
>   host exception. Non-HTTP egress is **registered**, not exempted.
>   `docs/INGESTION_CONDUCT.md` states what the code enforces.
>
> ### ⚠ Supersession notice — read this if you consume v2.7.0 or v2.8.0
>
> **v2.7.0 and v2.8.0 both shipped mid-phase, before this licensing work
> landed.** If you are consuming **AIAAIC-derived rows** from either release you
> are on **pre-marker, pre-reduction data**: those rows carry no
> `content_license` marker and may carry upstream narrative prose that this
> release replaces with facts-plus-pointer. **v2.9.0 supersedes both for that
> purpose.** Re-pull rather than diffing.
>
> **Why AIID-derived rows carry no marker, and why that is not an omission
> (E23, resolved 2026-07-30).** AIID-derived rows carry no `content_license`
> marker — **0 of 1,466**, counting the union of every AIID-identifying signal
> (tag, `aiid_id`, source-ID, reference URL), not merely the narrowest one.
> **That is a decision, not a gap.** AIID's maker is **US-situated**, and under
> US law titles and short phrases are **categorically excluded** from copyright
> (Circular 33 / 37 C.F.R. 202.1(a)); a US-situated maker also fails both the
> UK (reg 18) and EEA (Art 11) qualification tests, so **no sui generis database
> right subsists in AIID's collections at all.** The only AIID text that ships
> is the `title`; every row's `description` is project-authored template text,
> and **nothing from AIID's licence-excluded `reports.text` reaches the corpus.**
> Attribution is present where it is owed: **1,463 of 1,465** rows name AIID in
> the description and **1,464** carry a citation link — the two exceptions ship
> no AIID text at all. **This is why AIID and AIAAIC are treated differently:
> not two postures toward the same question, but two legal systems answering it
> differently** — the identical question stays open for AIAAIC precisely because
> UK/EU law has no categorical rule. See `docs/audits/E23-aiid-marking-ruling-2026-07-30.md`
> and `docs/SOURCE_LICENSES.md` **§1.2a** (the active AIID section; §1.2 is
> retired).

**✅ E23 answered before the cut, and the answer was "no second population."** The
notice above is therefore **rewritten rather than annotated** — it now states the
resolution and its reasoning instead of disclosing an open question. **One
residual is deliberately not in the release notes and belongs in the counsel
packet instead:** Berne Art 5(2)'s *lex loci protectionis* points at the forum
where protection is sought rather than the rights-holder's home, so a UK/EEA
forum would apply its own law and Circular 33 would not reach it — and E16 found
**42 of 47** AIAAIC titles indefensible under Infopaq, so in such a forum the
fallback is adverse rather than neutral. red-reviewer ruled this **advisory, not
a defect**, because the situs method is this project's own gated precedent (E13
§5.1 item 8) and **E13 itself routed that question to counsel** — so it folds
into the existing AIAAIC counsel ask, where the same question is already pending
from the other side. It is a known limit of a method the project already adopted,
not a hole opened by this release.
