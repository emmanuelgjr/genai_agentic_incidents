### INC-06481  [↗](/incident/INC-06481.html)

**Controversy Over GendNotes Police App and Automated Sensitive Data Collection in France**  
_2020-03-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

The French police's GendNotes app, which uses AI for automated collection and processing of sensitive personal data, has sparked controversy over potential privacy violations and risks to fundamental rights. Critics, including the CNIL, warn of possible misuse and insufficient safeguards, though no direct harm has yet been reported.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`
