### INC-06606  [↗](/incident/INC-06606.html)

**Facial Recognition in Real Estate and Residential Areas Raises Privacy and Financial Concerns in China**  
_2020-11-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

Facial recognition AI systems are widely deployed in Chinese real estate sales offices and residential communities, often without residents' or customers' consent. This has led to privacy violations, unauthorized biometric data collection, and financial harm, such as denial of housing discounts. The practice raises significant legal and ethical concerns over data security and personal rights.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`
