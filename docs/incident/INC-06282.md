### INC-06282  [↗](/incident/INC-06282.html)

**AI-Enabled Armed Drones Used in Fatal Border Operation Against PKK Militants**  
_2020-06-08 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Turkish armed forces, supported by AI-enabled armed drones (SİHA), conducted a military operation on the Iran border, resulting in the neutralization of four PKK militants. The drones provided aerial surveillance and fire support, directly contributing to the operation's lethal outcome.
