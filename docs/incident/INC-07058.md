### INC-07058  [↗](/incident/INC-07058.html)

**UK Trials Unsupervised AI-Driven Pods in Public Spaces**  
_2020-01-21 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Autonomous, AI-powered driverless pods are being tested at Cribbs Causeway mall in the UK, operating unsupervised among pedestrians and obstacles. The trials, run by AECOM and the CAPRI consortium, aim to assess real-world navigation and safety, presenting potential future risks but with no reported incidents or harm so far.
