**Facial Recognition at Cardiff-Swansea Football Match Sparks Rights Concerns and Police Row**  
_2020-01-08 · real-world · Severity: High_

South Wales Police deployed facial recognition AI at Cardiff-Swansea football matches to identify banned individuals, leading to arrests. The move sparked criticism from fans, privacy advocates, and police officials over privacy violations, potential bias, and risks of wrongful identification, highlighting concerns about human rights and the technology's proportionality.
