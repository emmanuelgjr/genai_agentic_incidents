### INC-08031  [↗](/incident/INC-08031.html)

**Court Orders Removal of AI-Generated Defamatory Images Targeting Senator Magno Malta**  
_2026-06-01 · real-world · Severity: High_

A São Paulo court ordered social media platforms, including Facebook, to remove AI-generated images that spread false and defamatory narratives about Senator Magno Malta. The images, created with artificial intelligence, were used to damage the senator's reputation, prompting legal action and a judicial mandate for content removal and user data preservation.

**Attack vector:** `misinformation`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MEASURE-2.8`
