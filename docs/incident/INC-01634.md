### INC-01634  [↗](/incident/INC-01634.html)

**Pentagon Signs $500 Million AI Contract with Scale AI for Military Data Analysis**  
_2026-05-07 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The Pentagon has signed a $500 million contract with Scale AI, a company partly owned by Meta, to enhance military data analysis and decision-making using AI systems. The agreement reflects the U.S. Department of Defense's increasing integration of AI in military planning and operations, raising potential future risks associated with military AI deployment.
