### INC-08139  [↗](/incident/INC-08139.html)

**South Korea Launches AI-Powered Robotic Endoscope Development Project**  
_2026-06-07 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A South Korean consortium led by Medintech, KERI, Seoul National University, and DGIST has begun a national project to develop an AI-based autonomous robotic endoscope. The initiative aims to automate navigation and precision treatment, challenging Japan's dominance in the global endoscope market. No harm or malfunction has been reported.
