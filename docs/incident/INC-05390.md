**AI Surveillance Technologies Raise Privacy Concerns in South Asia Amid Efforts to Curb Crimes Against Women**  
_2021-04-09 · real-world · Severity: High_

Authorities in India and Pakistan are deploying AI-based facial and emotion recognition systems to combat violence against women. Privacy experts and activists warn these technologies lack evidence of effectiveness, operate without data protection laws, and may increase privacy violations and harm to women rather than improving safety.
