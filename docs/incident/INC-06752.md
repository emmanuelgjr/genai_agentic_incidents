### INC-06752  [↗](/incident/INC-06752.html)

**Kitty Hawk's Autonomous Air Taxis Highlight Future AI Safety Risks**  
_2020-01-21 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Sebastian Thrun and his company Kitty Hawk are developing autonomous flying taxis that rely on AI for navigation and control. While no incidents have occurred, the technology is not yet at required safety levels, and certification is pending, raising concerns about potential future risks if AI systems malfunction.
