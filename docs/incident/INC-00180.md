**AI Misidentification Leads to False Arrests and Wrongful Detentions in U.S. Policing**  
_2026-05-11 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI-enhanced surveillance cameras and facial recognition software in U.S. law enforcement led to false arrests and wrongful detentions, including a Baltimore student misidentified as carrying a gun and a Tennessee grandmother wrongly jailed for months. Overreliance on AI outputs caused significant harm and rights violations.
