### INC-05791  [↗](/incident/INC-05791.html)

**Oxford AI Tool Predicts COVID-19 Mortality Risk from Medical Imaging**  
_2021-06-09 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Researchers at Oxford University developed an AI system that analyzes CT scans to detect vascular inflammation in COVID-19 patients, predicting mortality risk up to eight times higher for severe cases. The tool enables faster, personalized anti-inflammatory treatment decisions, potentially reducing death rates and improving patient outcomes.
