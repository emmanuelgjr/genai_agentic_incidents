### INC-01466  [↗](/incident/INC-01466.html)

**MoonPay Launches AI Agents for Autonomous Crypto Transactions**  
_2026-02-24 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

MoonPay has launched 'MoonPay Agents,' enabling AI agents to autonomously manage crypto wallets and execute trades, swaps, and transfers after initial user authorization. While the system is non-custodial and includes safeguards, the autonomous operation of financial transactions introduces plausible risks of unauthorized or erroneous activity.

**Attack vector:** `other`  

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`
