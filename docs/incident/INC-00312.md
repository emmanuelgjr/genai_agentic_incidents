### INC-00312  [↗](/incident/INC-00312.html)

**AI-Driven Online Financial Scams Surge in Bulgaria**  
_2026-03-02 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

European financial regulators warn of a sharp rise in online financial scams in Bulgaria, enabled by AI-generated fake messages, profiles, voices, and videos. Criminals use these technologies to impersonate trusted individuals, leading to financial loss, identity theft, and psychological harm among victims.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
