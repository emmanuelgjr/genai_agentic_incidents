### INC-01007  [↗](/incident/INC-01007.html)

**First Conviction in Cyprus for AI-Generated Child Sexual Abuse Material**  
_2026-03-24 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A Limassol court in Cyprus issued the country's first conviction for crimes involving child sexual abuse material created or distributed using artificial intelligence. Two young individuals pleaded guilty and received suspended prison sentences. The case highlights the growing concern over AI-facilitated exploitation and the legal system's response.
