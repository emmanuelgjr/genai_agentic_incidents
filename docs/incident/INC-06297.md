### INC-06297  [↗](/incident/INC-06297.html)

**AI-Generated Fake Blog Posts Fool Readers on Hacker News**  
_2020-08-16 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

College student Liam Porr used OpenAI's GPT-3 to generate fake blog posts, one of which reached the top of Hacker News after readers believed it was human-written. The incident highlights the potential for AI-generated content to spread misinformation and erode trust in online information.

**Attack vector:** `misinformation`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MEASURE-2.8`
