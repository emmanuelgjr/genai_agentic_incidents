### INC-01975  [↗](/incident/INC-01975.html)

**Uncontrolled Enterprise AI Use Increases Cybersecurity and Data Risks**  
_2026-04-27 · real-world · Severity: High_

A Lenovo survey of 6,000 employees worldwide reveals that over 70% use AI weekly, with up to a third doing so without IT oversight. This rise in 'shadow AI' expands attack surfaces, increases unmanaged risks, and heightens the likelihood of data exposure and cybersecurity threats due to insufficient governance and training.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.10`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0057`
