### INC-03544  [↗](/incident/INC-03544.html)

**Alleged Deepfake Video Depicts Former President of Kenya Uhuru Kenyatta Announcing 2027 Presidential Bid**  
_2024-03-18 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1025`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1025/)

A widely shared TikTok video purportedly manipulated by AI depicts former Kenyan president Uhuru Kenyatta announcing a 2027 presidential run. Reported digital forensics and visual inconsistencies confirm the video is an AI-generated deepfake, using archival footage from his presidency with synthesized audio. The clip spread rapidly online but has reportedly been debunked by fact-checkers. Kenyatta has made no such announcement, and such a move would violate Kenya&#x27;s two-term constitutional limit.
