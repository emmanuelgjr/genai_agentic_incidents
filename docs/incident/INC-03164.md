### INC-03164  [↗](/incident/INC-03164.html)

**Reported Disqualification of Two Books from the Ockham New Zealand Book Awards Due to Alleged AI-Generated Cover Art**  
_2025-11-17 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1282`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1282/)

In late 2025, two books by distinguished New Zealand authors - 'Obligate Carnivore' by Stephanie Johnson and 'Angel Train' by Elizabeth Smither - were disqualified from the 2026 Ockham New Zealand Book Awards fiction prize worth approximately $36,000. The disqualification occurred because their book covers contained AI-generated artwork created by Sugarcube Studios, violating the competition's new AI regulations introduced in August 2025. A bookseller flagged the covers as potentially AI-generated, prompting the New Zealand Book Awards Trust to investigate and confirm AI use with the publisher Quentin Wilson and the designers. The AI regulations were released in August with an October submission deadline, but both books were published in November, creating a timing issue. The authors, who had not used AI in their writing and were unaware of the AI-generated covers, expressed disappointment. Johnson had previously won New Zealand's 2022 Prime Minister's Award for Literature, while Smither won the 2018 Ockham Award in poetry. The incident highlighted broader challenges facing creative industries in establishing fair AI regulations as technology rapidly evolves.

**Affected:** Unknown AI Image Generator Developer, Sugarcube Studios
