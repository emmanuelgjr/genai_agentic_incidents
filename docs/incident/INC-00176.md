### INC-00176  [↗](/incident/INC-00176.html)

**AI Language Models Reinforce Gender Stereotypes and Inequality Among Young Women**  
_2026-03-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

A study by LLYC found that major AI language models, including ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, Mistral, and Llama, systematically reinforce gender stereotypes. The AI systems label young women as "fragile," recommend external validation, and steer their aspirations toward traditional roles, perpetuating inequality and harming self-perception among women aged 16-25 in 12 countries.
