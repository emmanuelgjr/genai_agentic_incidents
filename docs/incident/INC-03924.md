**Keras Lambda layer marshalled-code RCE**  
_2024-04 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

CVEs: `CVE-2024-3660`
CWEs: `CWE-94`
CVSS: **9.8** — `CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:U/C:H/I:H/A:H`
