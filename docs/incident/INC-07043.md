### INC-07043  [↗](/incident/INC-07043.html)

**UK and France Invest in Autonomous AI Minehunting Systems for Naval Defense**  
_2020-11-26 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The UK and France have jointly invested £184 million in developing AI-enabled autonomous minehunting systems to detect and neutralize sea mines, aiming to protect naval personnel and shipping lanes. While no harm has occurred, the deployment of these autonomous military systems presents potential future risks if malfunctions or misuse arise.
