### INC-05833  [↗](/incident/INC-05833.html)

**Rotterdam Welfare Fraud Algorithm Risks Discrimination and Bias**  
_2021-04-13 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The Rotterdam municipality's use of AI algorithms to detect welfare fraud has led to biased outcomes, disproportionately targeting individuals with migration backgrounds due to proxy variables. Reports highlight insufficient transparency, oversight, and responsibility, raising concerns about discrimination, profiling, and unfair treatment of citizens.

**Attack vector:** `other`  

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`
