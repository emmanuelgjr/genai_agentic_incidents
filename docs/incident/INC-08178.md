### INC-08178  [↗](/incident/INC-08178.html)

**Alleged Deepfake Investment Scam Uses Economist David Rosenberg&#x27;s Likeness on Meta Platforms**  
_2025-04-15 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1115`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1115/)

Fraudsters allegedly used AI-generated content and social media ads to impersonate economist David Rosenberg and promote a fraudulent investment scheme. Victims were reportedly directed to WhatsApp groups with fake stock tips, resulting in substantial financial losses, with some exceeding $500,000. The ads reportedly appeared on Meta platforms, including Facebook and Instagram. Rosenberg and his family reported the scam to authorities.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
