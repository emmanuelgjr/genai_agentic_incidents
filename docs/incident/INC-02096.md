### INC-02096  [↗](/incident/INC-02096.html)

**WinZO Accused of Using AI and Bots to Defraud Players of Rs 734 Crore**  
_2026-01-25 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Enforcement Directorate has chargesheeted online gaming app WinZO and its promoters for allegedly embedding AI and bots to manipulate game algorithms, causing players to lose Rs 734 crore. The AI-driven manipulation led to significant financial and mental harm to users across India.
