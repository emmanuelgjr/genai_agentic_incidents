### INC-01238  [↗](/incident/INC-01238.html)

**Influencer Investigated for Using AI Deepfake to Sexualize Minors in São Paulo Churches**  
_2026-04-22 · real-world · Severity: High_

Jefferson de Souza, a digital influencer in São Paulo, is under police investigation for using AI deepfake technology to manipulate and sexualize images of adolescent girls from the Congregação Cristã do Brasil. The AI-generated content was published on social media, causing psychological harm and prompting legal action.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`

**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`
