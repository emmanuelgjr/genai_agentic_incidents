**Tesla Robotaxis Involved in 14 Crashes in Austin Within Eight Months**  
_2026-02-17 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Tesla's AI-driven robotaxis in Austin, Texas, were involved in 14 crashes over eight months since launching in June, according to reports to U.S. regulators. Some incidents resulted in minor injuries and property damage, highlighting safety concerns with the autonomous driving system.
