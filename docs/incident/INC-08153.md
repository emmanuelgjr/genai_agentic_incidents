**Meta AI Suicide Alert Enables Police to Prevent Suicide in Meerut**  
_2026-06-08 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Meta's AI system flagged a suicide risk on Instagram, alerting Uttar Pradesh Police, who intervened within eight minutes to save a 25-year-old man in Meerut. The AI-driven alert enabled rapid response, preventing harm and highlighting the effectiveness of AI in suicide prevention through social media monitoring.
