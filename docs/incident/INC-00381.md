**AI-Generated Deepfake Nudes of 18 Minors Spark Investigation in Almería**  
_2026-03-17 · real-world · Severity: High_

Spanish authorities are investigating the use of the AI application ClothOff to generate fake nude and sexual images of at least 18 underage female students from an Almería institute. The incident, revealed by the provincial cybercrime prosecutor, highlights severe privacy violations and criminal offenses enabled by AI deepfake technology.
