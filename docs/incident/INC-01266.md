**Japan Plans AI-Enabled Defense Upgrades to Counter Drone Threats**  
_2026-02-21 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Japan is planning to revise its security policies to strengthen defenses against mass drone attacks, inspired by recent warfare trends. The government aims to deploy AI-enabled systems such as high-power lasers and microwaves for drone interception, addressing the cost-inefficiency of using expensive missiles against cheap, AI-driven drones.
