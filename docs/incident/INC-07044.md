**UK Announces Major Military Investment Including AI Intelligence Agency**  
_2020-11-19 · real-world · Severity: High_

UK Prime Minister Boris Johnson announced the country's largest military investment since the Cold War, allocating £16.5 billion over four years. The plan includes establishing an AI intelligence agency, a space command, and a cyber force, signaling increased development and deployment of AI in defense, with potential future risks.
