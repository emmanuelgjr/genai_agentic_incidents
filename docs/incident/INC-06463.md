### INC-06463  [↗](/incident/INC-06463.html)

**Clearview AI's Facial Recognition App Sparks Privacy Violations and Regulatory Action**  
_2020-02-27 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Clearview AI's facial recognition app, which scrapes billions of images from the web without consent, has been used by law enforcement and private entities, leading to significant privacy violations. Apple banned the app for policy violations, and leaked customer data further highlighted the risks of unauthorized surveillance and misuse.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM05`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`, `ASI05`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MEASURE-2.10`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0057`
