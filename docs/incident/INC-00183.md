### INC-00183  [↗](/incident/INC-00183.html)

**AI Misuse Leads to Disinformation and Mental Health Risks in Azerbaijan**  
_2026-03-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI systems, including ChatGPT, are being misused in Azerbaijan, causing psychological harm and social isolation among children and adults. Additionally, AI tools are facilitating the rapid spread of disinformation, misleading communities and exacerbating societal risks. These incidents highlight ongoing harm from AI deployment in the region.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
