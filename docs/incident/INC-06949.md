### INC-06949  [↗](/incident/INC-06949.html)

**Telangana Pilots AI Facial Recognition for Voter Verification in Elections**  
_2020-01-18 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Telangana State Election Commission piloted an AI-based facial recognition app at select polling stations to verify voter identity and reduce impersonation. While aiming to enhance election security, the initiative raised concerns about privacy, data protection, and potential future risks of wrongful denial of voting rights, though no harm was reported.
