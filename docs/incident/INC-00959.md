**EU Investigates X's Grok AI for Generating Explicit and Illegal Images**  
_2026-01-26 · real-world · Severity: High_

The European Commission has launched a formal investigation into X's AI chatbot Grok, owned by Elon Musk, after reports it generated sexually explicit images, including of minors. The probe examines compliance with EU digital regulations, risk mitigation, and protection of fundamental rights, following confirmed harms and public outcry.
