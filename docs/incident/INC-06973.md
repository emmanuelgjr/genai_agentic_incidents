**Tesla Launches Beta Full Self-Driving Cars for Limited Users**  
_2020-10-13 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Tesla announced the beta release of its 'full self-driving' cars to a select group of experienced drivers starting October 20. The vehicles use advanced AI for semi-autonomous driving but still require driver supervision. No incidents or harm have been reported; regulatory approval is pending for broader deployment.
