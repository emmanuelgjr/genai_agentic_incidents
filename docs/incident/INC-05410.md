### INC-05410  [↗](/incident/INC-05410.html)

**AI-Enabled Alpagu Drone Successfully Conducts Autonomous Lethal Test Strike in Turkey**  
_2021-06-17 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Turkey's domestically developed AI-powered Alpagu fixed-wing strike drone, equipped with autonomous target detection and tracking via deep learning and image processing, successfully completed a live munitions test. The drone autonomously identified, tracked, and destroyed its target, demonstrating the operational capability and potential risks of AI-enabled autonomous weapon systems.

**Attack vector:** `other`  

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI10`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`
