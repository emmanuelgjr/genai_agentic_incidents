### INC-07096  [↗](/incident/INC-07096.html)

**US Negotiates Sale of AI-Enabled MQ-9B Drones to Morocco**  
_2020-12-10 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The United States is negotiating the sale of at least four advanced MQ-9B drones, which feature AI-enabled autonomous capabilities, to Morocco. While no harm has occurred yet, the potential deployment of these drones raises credible concerns about future AI-related risks, including possible human rights violations or misuse in military operations.

**Attack vector:** `other`
