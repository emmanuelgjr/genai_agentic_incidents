### INC-07359  [↗](/incident/INC-07359.html)

**"Jewish Baby Strollers" Provided Anti-Semitic Google Images, Allegedly Resulting from Hate Speech Campaign**  
_2017-08-15 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1057`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1057/)

An online extremist group called 'raid' coordinated a campaign to manipulate Google's image search results by associating anti-Semitic content with innocent keywords. The campaign involved posting images of portable ovens tagged with terms like 'Jewish Baby Stroller' to create offensive Holocaust allusions in search results. According to the Network Contagion Research Institute, this coordinated effort began in 2017 with posts on the 4chan message board that purposefully paired oven images with the search term. When users searched for 'Jewish Baby Stroller', they would see portable ovens with inscriptions like 'Made in Germany', symbolizing Holocaust gas chambers and crematoria. Google explained that their image search algorithm relies on matching query words to text that appears next to images on webpages, and for this non-existent product, the closest matches were offensive content. Civil rights activist Brooke Goldstein stated that these manipulated search results spread hate and discrimination. Google acknowledged the results were 'disturbing' and claimed they were working on solutions, but noted the challenge of 'data voids' where only offensive content exists for certain search terms. The EndJewHatred group announced efforts to remove these images from search results.
