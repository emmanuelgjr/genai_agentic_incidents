**Turkish AI-Enabled Drone Strike Kills Three at Iraqi Kurdish Refugee Camp**  
_2021-06-06 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

A Turkish military drone attack, likely involving AI-enabled targeting, struck the Machmur refugee camp in northern Iraq, killing three civilians. The camp, home to about 12,000 people, was hit near a kindergarten and school, raising concerns over civilian safety and escalating regional tensions.
