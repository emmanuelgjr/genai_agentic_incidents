### INC-00091  [↗](/incident/INC-00091.html)

**AI Character 'Amelia' Repurposed to Spread Far-Right Hate Speech in the UK**  
_2026-01-25 · real-world · Severity: High_

An AI-generated character named 'Amelia', originally created as an educational tool against extremism in the UK, has been hijacked and widely used on social media to spread racist and far-right rhetoric. The AI's adaptability enabled viral dissemination of hate speech, causing social harm and concern among its original developers.

**Attack vector:** `rce`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
