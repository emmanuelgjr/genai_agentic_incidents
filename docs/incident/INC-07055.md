**UK Plans to Deploy Robot Soldiers in Army by 2030s**  
_2020-11-08 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

UK military leaders, including General Sir Nick Carter, have announced plans for up to 30,000 AI-enabled robot soldiers to serve alongside humans by the 2030s. While no harm has yet occurred, the large-scale deployment of autonomous or remotely controlled military robots raises significant future risks of AI-related harm.
