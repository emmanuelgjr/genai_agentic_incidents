**Azure AI Language Authoring Elevation of Privilege Vulnerability can Lead to RCE**  
_2026-02 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: Critical_

CVEs: `CVE-2026-21531`
CWEs: `CWE-502`
CVSS: **9.8** — `CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:U/C:H/I:H/A:H`
