### INC-05568  [↗](/incident/INC-05568.html)

**Deepfakes Threaten Security and Trust as AI-Generated Media Advances**  
_2021-03-10 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Researchers demonstrated that AI-generated deepfakes can reliably fool commercial facial recognition systems, raising risks of identity fraud. Simultaneously, the FBI and experts warn that increasingly realistic deepfakes are being used or could soon be used for misinformation, cyberattacks, and influence operations, highlighting the urgent need for detection and defense strategies.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
