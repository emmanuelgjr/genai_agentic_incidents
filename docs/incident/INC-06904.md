### INC-06904  [↗](/incident/INC-06904.html)

**Scientists Warn of Mass Surveillance Risks from AI-Enabled COVID-19 Contact-Tracing Apps**  
_2020-04-20 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Over 300 scientists from 26 countries warn that AI-powered COVID-19 contact-tracing apps, especially those using centralized data storage like PEPP-PT, could enable unprecedented mass surveillance and privacy violations. They caution that such risks may erode public trust and acceptance of these technologies.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`  

**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-4.1`, `MEASURE-2.10`
