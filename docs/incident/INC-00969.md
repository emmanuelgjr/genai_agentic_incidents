**European Regulators Warn of Accelerated Cyber Threats from AI in Financial Sector**  
_2026-04-24 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The European Securities and Markets Authority (ESMA) warns that rapidly advancing AI models, such as Anthropic's Mythos, are increasing the speed and risk of cyberattacks on financial institutions. Regulators are enhancing oversight and urging financial entities to strengthen cybersecurity defenses amid rising AI-driven threats.
