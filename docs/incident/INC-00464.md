### INC-00464  [↗](/incident/INC-00464.html)

**AI-Generated Fake References Lead to Academic Misconduct in Taiwanese Doctoral Thesis**  
_2026-03-13 · real-world · Severity: High_

A doctoral thesis at National Chengchi University in Taiwan included fabricated references generated by AI tools, some falsely attributed to real scholars. The incident was exposed by an affected professor, prompting the university to remove the thesis from its database and launch an academic ethics investigation. The student admitted to misusing AI without proper verification.
