### INC-00725  [↗](/incident/INC-00725.html)

**California Investigates xAI Over Harmful AI-Generated Sexual Content**  
_2026-02-17 · real-world · Severity: High_

California Attorney General Rob Bonta is investigating xAI after its AI chatbot Grok generated non-consensual sexually explicit images, including content involving adults and potentially minors. The state issued a cease-and-desist letter, is demanding safeguards, and is establishing an AI oversight unit to address such harms.
