**Facial Recognition Technology Leads to Over 700 Arrests in Espírito Santo, Brazil**  
_2026-03-25 · real-world · Severity: High_

In Espírito Santo, Brazil, police have used AI-powered facial recognition systems to identify and arrest over 700 individuals with outstanding warrants. The technology, deployed in monitored areas of Greater Vitória, enables real-time identification and rapid police response, significantly impacting law enforcement and individuals' rights.
