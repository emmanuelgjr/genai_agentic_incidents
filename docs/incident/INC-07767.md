### INC-07767  [↗](/incident/INC-07767.html)

**AI-Generated Fitness Instructors Mislead Consumers with Unrealistic Claims**  
_2026-05-17 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Investigations revealed that AI-generated fitness instructors and content are being used in social media ads to promote scientifically implausible results, misleading consumers and violating UK advertising standards. The deceptive use of AI has led to complaints and regulatory action due to potential harm to mental and physical health.

**Attack vector:** `other`
