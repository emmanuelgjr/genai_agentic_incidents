### INC-08926  [↗](/incident/INC-08926.html)

**stata-mcp has insufficient validation of user-supplied Stata do-file content that can lead to command execution**  
_2026-04 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: High_

CVEs: `CVE-2026-31040`
CWEs: `CWE-20`

A vulnerability was identified in stata-mcp prior to v1.13.0 where insufficient validation of user-supplied Stata do-file content can lead to command execution.

**Affected:** pip/stata-mcp  
**Attack vector:** `rce`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM03`, `LLM05`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI04`, `ASI05`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-6.1`, `MEASURE-2.7`
