### INC-01272  [↗](/incident/INC-01272.html)

**Japanese Poetry Contest Ends Due to AI-Generated Submissions**  
_2026-03-28 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The Yokai Senryu poetry contest in Sakaiminato, Japan, was discontinued after 20 years because organizers could no longer distinguish between human and AI-generated poems. The widespread use of generative AI undermined the contest's fairness and integrity, prompting its termination.
