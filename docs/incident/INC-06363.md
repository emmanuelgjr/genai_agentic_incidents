**Anduril Deploys AI-Powered Drones and Surveillance Towers for Border and Military Use**  
_2020-09-10 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Anduril, led by Palmer Luckey, has secured major contracts to deploy AI-powered autonomous surveillance towers and advanced drones, including the Ghost 4, for US border security and military operations. These systems use AI for real-time surveillance, target identification, and autonomous operation, raising concerns about potential privacy violations and risks of misuse.
