**US Congress Probes Spotify's AI-Driven Discovery Mode Over Artist Royalties**  
_2021-06-03 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The US House Judiciary Committee is investigating Spotify's AI-powered Discovery Mode, which allows artists to boost track recommendations in exchange for lower royalties. Lawmakers and the music community are concerned this could economically harm artists and create unfair labor conditions, though no direct harm has yet been confirmed.
