### INC-01232  [↗](/incident/INC-01232.html)

**Indian Cricketer Gautam Gambhir Files Lawsuit Over AI Deepfakes and Identity Misuse**  
_2026-03-19 · real-world · Severity: High_

Indian cricketer and coach Gautam Gambhir filed a civil suit in Delhi High Court after AI-generated deepfakes and voice cloning led to widespread impersonation, misinformation, and unauthorized commercial use of his identity. The fabricated videos, viewed millions of times, prompted legal action seeking damages and urgent content removal.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`
