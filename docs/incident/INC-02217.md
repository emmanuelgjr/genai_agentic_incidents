### INC-02217  [↗](/incident/INC-02217.html)

**AI-Aided Scam in Thailand Allegedly Impersonates Police to Defraud 163 Victims**  
_2025-02-04 · real-world · Severity: Critical_
AIID: [`#918`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/918/)

Thai police arrested Ramil Pantawong and Thanawut Kanyaphan for allegedly using AI technology to impersonate police officers in a call scam. Based in Poipet, Cambodia, the gang tricked 163 victims, the most prominent of whom was Thai-British beauty queen Charlotte Austin, who lost 4 million baht (approximately $118,000 USD). Victims were falsely told they were under investigation and instructed to transfer money.
