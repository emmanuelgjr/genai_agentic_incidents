### INC-04547  [↗](/incident/INC-04547.html)

**ChatGPT used to collect users' personal information**  
_2023 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Research and journalism showed that ChatGPT could be manipulated, both through direct prompts and indirect web content, into harvesting personal identifying information from users and from sources it had memorized in training data.

**Affected:** OpenAI ChatGPT users  
**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM01`, `LLM02`, `LLM03`, `LLM07`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI02`, `ASI03`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `GOVERN-6.1`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.10`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0010`, `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0024`, `AML.T0051`, `AML.T0053`, `AML.T0056`, `AML.T0057`
