### INC-06560  [↗](/incident/INC-06560.html)

**Facebook Considers Political Ad 'Kill Switch' to Prevent Post-Election Misinformation**  
_2020-08-21 · real-world · Severity: High_

Facebook is considering halting political ads after the U.S. election to prevent the spread of misinformation, particularly regarding disputed results. The company, which uses AI for ad targeting and content moderation, is exploring this measure as a precaution, though no actual harm has yet occurred.
