**Planned Launch of Autonomous Robotaxi Services in Munich and Madrid**  
_2026-06-02 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Uber, WeRide, Autobrains, and AVOMO plan to launch pilot robotaxi services using AI-driven autonomous vehicles in Munich and Madrid. Initial deployments will include safety operators, with gradual expansion toward full autonomy. No incidents have occurred yet, but the projects present potential future AI-related risks.
