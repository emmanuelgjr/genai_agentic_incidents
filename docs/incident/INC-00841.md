### INC-00841  [↗](/incident/INC-00841.html)

**Colombian President Raises Concerns Over Electoral Software Transparency**  
_2026-03-08 · real-world · Severity: High_

Colombian President Gustavo Petro expressed doubts about the transparency and reliability of the AI-driven electoral software used for vote counting, citing lack of source code disclosure and exclusive control by a private company. He called for a technical audit to ensure election integrity ahead of upcoming legislative elections.
