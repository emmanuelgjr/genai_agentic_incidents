**Unlabeled Purportedly AI-Generated 'Jessica Foster' Account Reportedly Posed as Pro-Trump Army Service Member to Attract Followers and Funnel Users to Paid Adult Content**  
_2025-11-27 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1422`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1422/)

An anonymous operator created Jessica Foster, an AI-generated character posing as a U.S. Army soldier, who gained over 1 million Instagram followers in just a few months starting from her first post on Thanksgiving 2024. The account posted over 50 photos and videos showing Foster in military uniforms alongside fabricated meetings with Donald Trump, Melania Trump, Vladimir Putin, Volodymyr Zelensky, and celebrities like Lionel Messi. The content mixed patriotic messaging with suggestive imagery, particularly featuring her feet. The account originally linked to OnlyFans but was removed for violating their human verification requirements, then moved to Fanvue, a competitor that allows AI-generated content. Instagram removed the Foster account for policy violations after media inquiry. Military experts noted obvious fabrication signs including incorrect uniform details, such as her first name 'JESSICA' appearing where her last name should be on military name tapes, and mixed military insignia suggesting impossible rank combinations. The Army confirmed no records of Foster existed. Thousands of users engaged with the content believing she was real, with many leaving supportive comments and heart emojis. The account represents a broader trend of AI-generated influencers combining political messaging with adult content monetization strategies.
