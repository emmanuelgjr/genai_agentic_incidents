### INC-00774  [↗](/incident/INC-00774.html)

**China Warns of AI Patent Application Risks with OpenClaw**  
_2026-03-29 · real-world · Severity: High_

China's National Intellectual Property Administration issued warnings about using AI agents like OpenClaw for drafting patent applications. The agency highlighted risks including technical information leaks, AI-generated errors, and dishonest filings, which can lead to loss of patent rights, financial harm, and legal penalties for applicants and agencies.
