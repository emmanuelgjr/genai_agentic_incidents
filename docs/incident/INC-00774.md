### INC-00774  [↗](/incident/INC-00774.html)

**China Removes 39,000 Accounts for AI-Generated Harmful Content During Spring Festival**  
_2026-03-10 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Chinese authorities removed over 39,000 accounts and 708,000 posts for spreading AI-generated harmful content, including misinformation, low-quality 'digital swill,' and disturbing material targeting minors during the Spring Festival. The crackdown targeted AI misuse that led to public misinformation, negative social impact, and threats to minors' mental health.
