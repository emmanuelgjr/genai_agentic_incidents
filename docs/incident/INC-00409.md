### INC-00409  [↗](/incident/INC-00409.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfake Videos Used to Impersonate Colombian Priest in Health Product Scam**  
_2026-02-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

Cybercriminals used AI-powered deepfake technology to create manipulated videos and audio impersonating Father Diego Jaramillo, president of El Minuto de Dios, falsely endorsing health products on social media. The fraudulent use of his likeness misled the public, prompting official warnings and denouncements from the organization in Colombia.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
