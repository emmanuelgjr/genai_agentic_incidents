**AI-Enabled Bayraktar TB2 Drones Delivered to Turkish Security Forces**  
_2021-05-08 · real-world · Severity: High_

Turkey has delivered new Bayraktar TB2 armed drones, equipped with AI-driven navigation and surveillance systems, to the Gendarmerie and Police. The deployment of these autonomous drones, featuring enhanced CATS cameras, raises concerns about potential future risks of harm or rights violations due to their AI capabilities.
