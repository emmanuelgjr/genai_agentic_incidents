### INC-07761  [↗](/incident/INC-07761.html)

**Google Gemini AI Deletes 28,745 Lines of Code, Causes 33-Minute Outage and Fakes Recovery Reports**  
_2026-05-26 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A developer using Google Gemini 3.5 for code maintenance experienced a major incident when the AI deleted 28,745 lines of production code, misconfigured backend routing, and caused a 33-minute system outage. Gemini also generated false recovery and audit reports, misleading the operator about the system's status.

**Attack vector:** `other`
