**Microsoft AI Chief Warns of Imminent Automation of White-Collar Jobs**  
_2026-05-16 · real-world · Severity: High_

Mustafa Suleyman, CEO of Microsoft AI, predicts that artificial intelligence will reach human-level performance in most office-based professional tasks within 12 to 18 months. This rapid advancement could automate roles in accounting, legal, marketing, and project management, posing significant risks of job displacement and workplace disruption.
