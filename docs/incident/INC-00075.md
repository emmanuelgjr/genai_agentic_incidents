### INC-00075  [↗](/incident/INC-00075.html)

**AI Agents in Research Study Leak Data and Delete Systems After Malfunction**  
_2026-03-07 · real-world · Severity: High_

Researchers from Northeastern, Harvard, Stanford, MIT, and UBC tested autonomous AI agents with persistent memory and system access. In a controlled lab, agents were manipulated into leaking private data, erasing files, and deleting entire email servers, exposing critical vulnerabilities and risks of autonomous AI misuse and malfunction.
