**Didi Chuxing Plans Massive Robotaxi Deployment with BAIC Partnership**  
_2020-06-23 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Didi Chuxing, backed by Apple, aims to deploy over one million AI-powered self-driving vehicles by 2030, focusing on areas with fewer ride-hailing drivers. In partnership with BAIC Group, Didi will jointly develop and commercialize high-level autonomous vehicles, raising future safety and societal concerns due to large-scale AI deployment.
