**AI-Driven Targeting in Iran Leads to Civilian Harm and Raises Global Concerns**  
_2026-03-05 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The United States and Israel used advanced AI systems, including Project Maven, to rapidly identify and attack over a thousand targets in Iran, resulting in civilian casualties and the death of Iran's supreme leader. Reports highlight that algorithmic errors in AI-driven targeting accelerated attacks and contributed to wrongful strikes on civilian sites.
