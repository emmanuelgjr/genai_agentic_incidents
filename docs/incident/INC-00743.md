### INC-00743  [↗](/incident/INC-00743.html)

**ChatGPT Aids in Correct Diagnosis of Rare Disease After Years of Medical Errors**  
_2026-04-10 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Phoebe Tesoriere, a 23-year-old from Cardiff, used ChatGPT to input her symptoms after years of misdiagnoses by doctors. The AI suggested hereditary spastic paraplegia, which was later confirmed by genetic testing, highlighting both the potential and limitations of AI in healthcare diagnostics.

**Attack vector:** `other`
