**Facial Recognition Attendance System Fails After Worker Shaves Head in Telangana**  
_2026-05-29 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

In Mahabubabad, Telangana, a facial recognition app used for attendance under the MGNREGA scheme failed to identify a worker after he shaved his head for a religious ritual. The malfunction disrupted attendance marking until coworkers improvised a solution, highlighting limitations of AI-based identification systems in welfare programs.
