**AI-Enabled Drones Escalate Military Tensions and Destabilize Global Politics**  
_2020-12-13 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

AI-powered drones have intensified surveillance and armed conflict, notably between India and Pakistan and in wars in Libya, Nagorno-Karabakh, Syria, and Ukraine. Their deployment has led to airspace violations, increased military tensions, civilian harm, and destabilized geopolitical situations, demonstrating direct and significant harms from AI system use.
