**Greece Leases Israeli AI-Enabled Drones for Border Surveillance**  
_2020-05-06 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Greece has signed a €40 million deal to lease Israeli Heron AI-enabled drones for three years to enhance border and maritime surveillance, with an option to purchase. The drones, equipped with advanced sensors and autonomous capabilities, raise concerns about potential future harms in a sensitive geopolitical context.
