**Lyft Driver Uses AI-Generated Images for Fraudulent Damage Claims in Florida**  
_2026-05-18 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A Florida Lyft driver used AI-generated images to falsely claim car damages and charge passengers a $75 cleaning fee. The incident involved teenage riders and led to the driver's removal from the platform, raising concerns about AI misuse, fraud controls, and consumer protection in rideshare services.
