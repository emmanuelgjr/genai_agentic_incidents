**New York Times Accused of AI Surveillance Violating Tech Workers' Rights**  
_2026-05-27 · real-world · Severity: High_

The New York Times is accused by its unionized tech workers of using AI tools (DX and Glean) to monitor and evaluate employee performance without proper notification, allegedly violating collective bargaining agreements and labor rights. The NewsGuild of New York has filed grievances and an unfair labor practice charge in response.
