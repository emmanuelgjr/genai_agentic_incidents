### INC-08114  [↗](/incident/INC-08114.html)

**Niulinx Launches Autonomous Vehicle Testing on Public Roads in Lombardy**  
_2026-06-06 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Niulinx, a startup spin-off from Politecnico di Milano, has received government authorization to test its AI-driven autonomous vehicles on public roads in Cernusco sul Naviglio and Brescia, Lombardy. The trials involve advanced sensor-equipped Fiat 500e cars, marking a potential AI hazard due to real-world deployment.
