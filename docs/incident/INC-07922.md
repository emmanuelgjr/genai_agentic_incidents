### INC-07922  [↗](/incident/INC-07922.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfakes and Fraud Target Colombian Elections**  
_2026-05-26 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Cybersecurity experts warn that, ahead of Colombia's presidential elections, AI-generated deepfakes, cloned audios, and manipulated images are being used to spread misinformation, commit fraud, and steal personal data. These tactics threaten digital security and public trust in the electoral process, prompting calls for increased vigilance.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
