### INC-03130  [↗](/incident/INC-03130.html)

**Purportedly AI-Generated TikTok Videos Reportedly Urged &#x27;Polexit&#x27; Campaign, Prompting Polish Government Complaint to EU**  
_2025-12-13 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1381`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1381/)

Purportedly AI-generated TikTok videos depicting young women in patriotic dress reportedly urged Poland to leave the EU (&quot;Polexit&quot;) and spread widely in late December 2025. Poland&#x27;s junior digital minister reportedly said the wave appeared to test narratives ahead of the 2027 election, and asked the European Commission to examine TikTok&#x27;s DSA compliance. TikTok reportedly said it removed content that violated its rules.
