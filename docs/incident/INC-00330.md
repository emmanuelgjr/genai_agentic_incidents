**AI-Enabled Armed Robots Used in Ukraine War Cause Battlefield Harm**  
_2026-03-07 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Ukrainian and Russian forces are deploying AI-enabled armed uncrewed ground vehicles (UGVs) in active combat, resulting in injury and death. These autonomous or semi-autonomous robots, equipped with lethal weapons, have engaged in direct combat and contributed to battlefield casualties, marking a significant shift in modern warfare.
