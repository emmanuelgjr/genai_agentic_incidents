### INC-00109  [↗](/incident/INC-00109.html)

**AI Chatbots Frequently Misdiagnose Medical Cases, Study Finds**  
_2026-04-14 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A study by Mass General Brigham found that AI chatbots, including ChatGPT and Gemini, gave incorrect medical diagnoses in over 80% of cases when provided with incomplete patient information. Even with full data, error rates remained high, raising concerns about the reliability of AI in medical diagnostics.
