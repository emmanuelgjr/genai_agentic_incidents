### INC-01778  [↗](/incident/INC-01778.html)

**Slovak Central Bank Warns of AI-Generated Fraudulent Crypto Websites**  
_2026-03-24 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The Národná banka Slovenska (NBS) has warned about numerous unauthorized, AI-generated websites offering crypto-asset investment services. These sites pose significant risks of financial fraud and loss to consumers in Slovakia. NBS published a list of such sites and advised caution against using them.

**Attack vector:** `other`  

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`
