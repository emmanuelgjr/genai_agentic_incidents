**Ukraine Shares Battlefield Data to Train Military AI Systems**  
_2026-03-12 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Ukraine has become the first country to open real battlefield data to allies and partners for training AI models, particularly for autonomous drones and combat systems. This initiative aims to accelerate the development of military AI, raising significant risks of future harm from autonomous weapon systems. The event is centered in Ukraine.
