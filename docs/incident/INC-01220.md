### INC-01220  [↗](/incident/INC-01220.html)

**Incomplete Patient Input to AI Chatbots Risks Medical Misdiagnosis**  
_2026-05-01 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Studies reveal that patients provide less detailed symptom information to AI chatbots and digital symptom checkers than to human doctors. This incomplete input could plausibly lead to incorrect medical assessments and patient safety risks as AI systems increasingly serve as the first point of contact in healthcare triage.

**Attack vector:** `other`
