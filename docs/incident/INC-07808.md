### INC-07808  [↗](/incident/INC-07808.html)

**Nonfiction Book on AI Contains Fabricated Quotes Generated by AI Tools**  
_2026-05-19 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1504`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1504/)

Steven Rosenbaum's nonfiction book, "The Future of Truth," was found to contain multiple fabricated and misattributed quotes generated by AI tools ChatGPT and Claude. The author acknowledged the errors, which misled readers and undermined information integrity, and is working with editors to correct future editions. The incident occurred in the United States.
