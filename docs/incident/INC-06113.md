### INC-06113  [↗](/incident/INC-06113.html)

**TikTok Removes Hundreds of Thousands of Misinformation Videos Using AI Systems**  
_2021-02-24 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

TikTok used AI-powered content moderation and recommendation algorithms to remove over 340,000 videos spreading misinformation about the US elections and COVID-19 in late 2020. The platform also deleted 1.75 million automated accounts and suppressed additional misleading content, highlighting AI's role in both propagating and mitigating harmful misinformation.
