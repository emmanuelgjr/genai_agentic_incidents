### INC-00513  [↗](/incident/INC-00513.html)

**AI-Generated Videos Used to Manipulate Voters in Kerala Elections**  
_2026-03-27 · real-world · Severity: High_

Political parties in Kerala, including BJP, Congress, and the Left, are using AI-generated videos and synthetic content to influence voters ahead of the April 9 Assembly elections. These realistic, misleading visuals blur reality and fiction, spreading misinformation and undermining democratic processes, causing harm to community trust and electoral integrity.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
