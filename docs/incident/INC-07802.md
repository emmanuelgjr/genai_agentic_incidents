### INC-07802  [↗](/incident/INC-07802.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfake Causes Reputational Harm to Lupillo Rivera**  
_2026-05-19 · real-world · Severity: High_

An AI-generated deepfake audio and video falsely attributed statements to Emiliano Aguilar, implicating Lupillo Rivera and Ángela Aguilar in a scandal. The viral content caused reputational harm and social disruption, prompting Lupillo Rivera to announce legal action against the creators and distributors of the manipulated material.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`

**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`
