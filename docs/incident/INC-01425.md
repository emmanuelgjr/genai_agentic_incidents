**Metropolitan Police Use Palantir AI to Uncover Officer Misconduct**  
_2026-04-24 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Metropolitan Police in London used Palantir's AI tool to analyze internal data, uncovering widespread misconduct, corruption, and criminality among hundreds of officers. The AI-led investigation resulted in arrests and disciplinary actions for offenses including fraud, sexual assault, and abuse of authority, prompting consideration of expanded AI use in future policing.
