**Li Auto MEGA Electric Vehicle Battery Recall Due to AI-Related Safety Risk**  
_2026-03-15 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Li Auto recalled over 11,000 MEGA electric vehicles in China due to a defect in the battery cooling system, which could cause thermal runaway and safety hazards. The issue, monitored by an AI-based cloud safety system, required battery and controller replacements to prevent potential harm. Recall completion exceeds 97.5%.

**Attack vector:** `other`
