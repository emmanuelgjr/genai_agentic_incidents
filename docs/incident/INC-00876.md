**Delhi High Court Addresses Deepfake Misuse Against Patanjali Co-Founder**  
_2026-03-22 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Acharya Balkrishna, co-founder of Patanjali Ayurved, filed a suit in the Delhi High Court seeking protection of his personality rights from AI-generated deepfake content.
