**Voice deepfake targets bank in failed transfer scam**  
_2023-08-30 · real-world · Severity: Medium_
AIID: [`#564`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/564/)

In spring 2023, Florida investor Clive Kabatznik became the target of an advanced scam attempt involving a voice deepfake mimicking his own voice. The fraudulent caller, using AI-generated speech, contacted Kabatznik&#x27;s Bank of America representative in an unsuccessful attempt to deceive the banker into transferring funds to a different account.
