**Elderly Woman Forced to Undergo Facial Recognition Sparks Outcry and Bank Apology in China**  
_2020-11-22 · real-world · Severity: High_

A 94-year-old woman in Hubei, China, was physically lifted by relatives to complete mandatory AI-based facial recognition at a bank for social security card activation. The incident, widely criticized for lacking accommodations for vulnerable users, led to public outcry and a formal apology from the bank, highlighting AI's exclusionary impact on the elderly.
