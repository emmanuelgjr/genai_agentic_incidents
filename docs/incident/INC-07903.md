**AI Name-Reading System Malfunctions at Arizona Graduation Ceremony**  
_2026-05-18 · real-world · Severity: High_

At Glendale Community College in Arizona, an AI system used to announce graduates' names malfunctioned, skipping and mispronouncing names during the ceremony. The incident caused emotional distress and disruption, leading to public outrage and apologies from college officials. The event highlighted the risks of relying on unproven AI technology for significant milestones.
