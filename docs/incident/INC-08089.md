**Anthropic's AI Model Used in NSA Cyberattacks Raises Safety Concerns**  
_2026-06-04 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Anthropic deployed engineers to help the US NSA customize its Mythos AI model for cyberattacks, enabling offensive operations such as network infiltration. This direct use of AI in harmful cyber activities has sparked industry debate and renewed calls from Anthropic for regulatory 'brakes' to prevent future AI loss of control and societal harm.
