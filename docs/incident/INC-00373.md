### INC-00373  [↗](/incident/INC-00373.html)

**AI-Generated Content on Chinese Platforms Causes Harm and Triggers Regulatory Crackdown**  
_2026-03-02 · real-world · Severity: High_

Chinese platforms WeChat and Douyin have removed thousands of AI-generated videos that distorted classic literature, animated characters, and celebrity likenesses, leading to cultural harm, misleading youth, and rights violations. Some content targeted minors with harmful or explicit material. Platforms responded with mass takedowns and stricter moderation.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
