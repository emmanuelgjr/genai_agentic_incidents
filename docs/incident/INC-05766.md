### INC-05766  [↗](/incident/INC-05766.html)

**Microsoft President Warns of AI-Driven Mass Surveillance Risks**  
_2021-05-27 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Microsoft President Brad Smith warned that without urgent regulation, AI could enable mass surveillance reminiscent of George Orwell's '1984' as soon as 2024. Citing China's extensive use of AI for citizen monitoring, Smith urged lawmakers to enact protections to prevent authoritarian misuse and threats to democracy and human rights.
