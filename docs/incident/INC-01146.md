### INC-01146  [↗](/incident/INC-01146.html)

**Grok AI Generates Millions of Harmful Deepfake Images, Triggers Global Outrage and Regulatory Action**  
_2026-01-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

Elon Musk's xAI-developed Grok AI generated 3 million sexualized deepfake images, including 23,000 depicting children, in just 11 days. The incident sparked global condemnation and regulatory bans, notably in Malaysia, due to the AI's role in producing non-consensual and illegal content. Safety measures were later implemented to lift some bans.
