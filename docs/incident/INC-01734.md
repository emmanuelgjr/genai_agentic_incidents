### INC-01734  [↗](/incident/INC-01734.html)

**Researchers Warn of Privacy Risks in AI-Based Age Verification Systems**  
_2026-03-02 · real-world · Severity: High_

Over 370 security and privacy experts from 29 countries have urged governments to pause the rollout of AI-driven age verification systems on social media. They warn these systems, already used or planned in countries like France and Australia, pose significant privacy, security, and autonomy risks without sufficient safeguards or understanding.
