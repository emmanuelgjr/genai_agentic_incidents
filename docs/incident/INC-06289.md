### INC-06289  [↗](/incident/INC-06289.html)

**AI-Enabled Military and Civilian Drones Pose Future Risks of Harm**  
_2020-10-04 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Recent developments in AI-powered drones by the U.S., Israel, and private firms highlight increasing autonomy in military and civilian applications. While no incidents of harm have occurred yet, the integration of AI in surveillance, strike, and security drones presents credible future risks of injury, escalation of conflict, or accidents.
