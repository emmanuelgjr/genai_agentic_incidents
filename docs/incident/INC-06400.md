**Boeing 737 Max AI System Malfunction Leads to Fatal Crashes and Regulatory Overhaul**  
_2020-06-28 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The Boeing 737 Max's automated flight control system (MCAS), an AI-driven component, malfunctioned and caused two fatal crashes, killing 346 people. Regulators now demand significant design changes and extensive testing of the AI system before the aircraft can return to service, highlighting the risks of flawed AI in aviation.
