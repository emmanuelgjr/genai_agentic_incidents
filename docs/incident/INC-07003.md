### INC-07003  [↗](/incident/INC-07003.html)

**Tinder and Grindr Accused of Selling Sensitive User Data via AI-Driven Advertising Systems**  
_2020-01-14 · real-world · Severity: High_

Norway's Consumer Council accused dating apps Tinder and Grindr of violating European privacy laws by selling users' sensitive personal data—including sexual orientation and location—to third-party companies. AI-driven data processing and targeted advertising systems enabled this profiling, resulting in privacy breaches and potential discrimination against users, especially the LGBT community.

**Attack vector:** `other`
