### INC-12053  [↗](/incident/INC-12053.html)

**Dolibarr ERP and CRM contain XSS Vulnerability**  
_2022-05 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: Medium_

CVEs: `CVE-2017-14239`, `CVE-2017-14241`, `CVE-2019-19210`
CWEs: `CWE-79`
CVSS: **5.4** — `CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:L/UI:R/S:C/C:L/I:L/A:N`

Dolibarr ERP/CRM before 10.0.3 allows XSS because uploaded HTML documents are served as text/html despite being renamed to .noexe files.

**Affected:** composer/dolibarr/dolibarr  
**Attack vector:** `xss`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM03`, `LLM05`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI04`, `ASI05`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-6.1`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0010`, `AML.T0050`
