### INC-00497  [↗](/incident/INC-00497.html)

**Greek Tax Authority Plans AI System to Combat Tax Evasion**  
_2020-11-26 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Greek tax authority is developing AI-powered data mining systems, funded by the national recovery plan, to enhance tax fraud detection and enforcement. While the AI aims to identify false tax declarations and reduce evasion, no harm or misuse has been reported as the system is still in development.
