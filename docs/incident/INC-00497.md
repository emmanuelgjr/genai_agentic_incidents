### INC-00497  [↗](/incident/INC-00497.html)

**AI-Generated Saint Paisios Scam Defrauds Greek Faithful**  
_2026-05-04 · real-world · Severity: High_

Scammers used AI to create fake videos of Saint Paisios, urging believers to comment "Amen" and then directing them to fraudulent websites to buy products, resulting in financial losses. Victims have reported the incident to Greek cybercrime authorities, highlighting AI's role in religious impersonation and fraud.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
