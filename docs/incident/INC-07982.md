### INC-07982  [↗](/incident/INC-07982.html)

**AI-Enabled Biometric Scams Target Job Seekers in India**  
_2026-05-29 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Cybercriminals in India are using AI-powered image processing and biometric data extraction during fake online job interviews to steal sensitive personal information. The Ministry of Home Affairs and its cybercrime unit have issued warnings as these scams lead to identity theft and financial fraud, harming job seekers nationwide.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`  

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`
