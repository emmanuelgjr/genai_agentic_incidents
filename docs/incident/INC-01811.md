**Student Faces Trial for AI-Generated Sexual Images of Schoolmates in Córdoba**  
_2026-03-18 · real-world · Severity: High_

A student in Córdoba, Argentina, used AI to create and publish manipulated sexual images of female classmates, including minors, on adult websites, identifying them by name and linking to their social media. The victims suffered psychological harm and privacy violations, prompting a criminal trial for gender-based violence.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
