### INC-00707  [↗](/incident/INC-00707.html)

**Brazilian Police Dismantle AI-Driven Deepfake Fraud Ring**  
_2026-04-14 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Brazilian police dismantled a criminal group that used generative AI to create deepfake facial biometrics, bypassing telecom security systems. The group committed large-scale electronic fraud and identity theft, taking over victims' phone lines and accessing financial accounts, causing widespread financial harm across Brazil.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
