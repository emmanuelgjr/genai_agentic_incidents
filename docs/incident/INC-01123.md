### INC-01123  [↗](/incident/INC-01123.html)

**Google Uses AI to Remove 160 Million Fake Reviews and Block Fraudulent Apps**  
_2026-02-21 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Google deployed generative AI to detect and remove 160 million fake reviews and block 266 million risky app installations on Google Play. The AI also restricted sensitive data access for over 255,000 apps, preventing fraud, review bombing, and reputational harm to developers across 185 markets.
