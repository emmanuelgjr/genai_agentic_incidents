### INC-00595  [↗](/incident/INC-00595.html)

**Anthropic accuses Chinese AI firms of mass data theft via fake accounts**  
_2026-02-23 · threat-report · Severity: High_

Anthropic accused DeepSeek, Moonshot AI and MiniMax of creating more than 24,000 fake accounts to extract conversations and outputs from Claude, with data from over 16 million interactions allegedly used to train competing models, raising distillation-as-IP-theft concerns.

**Affected:** Anthropic Claude  
**Attack vector:** `model-extraction`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM03`
