### INC-07365  [↗](/incident/INC-07365.html)

**AI-Designed Phone Cases Are Unexpected**  
_2017-07-10 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#56`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/56/)

Amazon's third-party seller 'my-handy-design' deployed an AI bot that automatically generates smartphone case designs by pulling royalty-free stock images and creating product listings on Amazon. The bot was intended to create cases based on trending and popular image searches, but instead generated over 30,000 bizarre and inappropriate phone case designs. The cases featured disturbing imagery including someone preparing heroin for injection, armpit waxing procedures, medical equipment like asthma inhalers, adult diapers on elderly men, pregnancy tests, and various medical procedures. The bot appeared to have focused heavily on medical stock photos and other inappropriate content. Customers discovered these products and left reviews expressing shock and criticism, with one reviewer stating 'You should be ashamed to offer this product. Trying to profit on the pain of others. And shame on Amazon for allowing this.' The cases were priced between $15-$30 each and were printed on-demand when ordered. After the incident gained media attention, most of the inappropriate cases were removed from Amazon's platform, though over 31,000 products were listed at the peak of the incident.
