### INC-05683  [↗](/incident/INC-05683.html)

**Google's FLoC AI Ad Targeting Sparks Privacy Backlash and Blocking by Browsers**  
_2021-04-13 · real-world · Severity: High_

Google's AI-driven FLoC system, designed to replace third-party cookies by grouping users for targeted ads, was deployed in Chrome without user consent, leading to privacy violations. Major browsers like Brave and Vivaldi blocked FLoC, and privacy advocates, including EFF, raised concerns over unauthorized data collection and user tracking.
