**Merlin Labs and Google Back Fully Autonomous Aircraft Fleet, Raising Safety Concerns**  
_2021-05-26 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Merlin Labs, backed by Google and other investors, is developing AI-powered autonomous flight systems for a 55-aircraft King Air fleet in partnership with Dynamic Aviation. While no incidents have occurred, the deployment of AI in safety-critical aviation raises credible risks if the technology malfunctions or is misused.
