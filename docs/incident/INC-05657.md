### INC-05657  [↗](/incident/INC-05657.html)

**Global Child Advocacy Groups Oppose Facebook's AI-Driven Instagram for Kids**  
_2021-04-15 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Over 35 consumer and child advocacy groups, along with experts, urged Facebook to abandon plans for an AI-powered Instagram for children under 13, citing risks to mental health, privacy, and exposure to harmful content due to algorithmic recommendations and inadequate safeguards.
