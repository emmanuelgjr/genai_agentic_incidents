### INC-03819  [↗](/incident/INC-03819.html)

**Google Books Appears to Be Indexing Works Written by AI**  
_2024-04-04 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#684`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/684/)

Google Books is indexing low-quality, AI-generated books, degrading its database and potentially distorting Google Ngram Viewer&#x27;s analysis of language trends. This integration of inaccurate or misleading information undermines trust, disseminates poor-quality content, and wastes resources as researchers must spend time clearing up the misinformation.
