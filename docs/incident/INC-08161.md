**China Cracks Down on AI-Generated Military Misinformation**  
_2026-06-08 · real-world · Severity: High_

Chinese authorities have taken action against multiple online accounts for using AI to generate and spread false or defamatory military-related stories and videos. These AI-generated contents misled the public, damaged the reputation of military personnel, and undermined military morale, prompting a coordinated crackdown and account closures.
