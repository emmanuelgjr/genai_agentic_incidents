**Zimbabwe Faces Surge in AI-Driven Cybercrime and Fraud**  
_2026-04-10 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Zimbabwe is experiencing a sharp rise in AI-powered cybercrime, including deepfakes, voice cloning, and adaptive malware, leading to millions in financial losses and eroding public trust. At the 2026 Cyber Fraud and AI Summit, officials highlighted the urgent need for legislative and technological responses to combat these AI-enabled threats.
