**Starlink Outage Disrupts US Navy Drone Operations, Exposing Critical Vulnerabilities**  
_2026-04-13 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

A global outage of SpaceX's Starlink satellite network in August disrupted US Navy tests of autonomous unmanned surface vessels off California, halting operations and communications for nearly an hour. The incident exposed the Pentagon's heavy reliance on Starlink's AI-enabled systems, raising concerns about vulnerabilities in critical military infrastructure.
