### INC-00477  [↗](/incident/INC-00477.html)

**AI-Generated Images Used in Fraudulent 'Case Fixing' Scheme in Vietnam**  
_2026-01-24 · real-world · Severity: High_

A criminal group in Vietnam, led by Nguyễn Thụy Saly, used AI technology to fabricate images of themselves with high-level officials to deceive victims seeking legal favors. The AI-generated images were central to the fraud, resulting in significant financial losses for victims. Authorities have arrested and charged several suspects.

**Attack vector:** `other`  

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`
