### INC-01943  [↗](/incident/INC-01943.html)

**UK Firms Face AI Data Governance Risks from Overseas Processing**  
_2026-04-27 · real-world · Severity: High_

Research shows most large UK companies lack oversight of how AI systems process sensitive data overseas, with frequent cross-border data flows. This governance gap exposes boards to compliance, security, and potential data leak risks as AI adoption outpaces regulatory controls. No realized harm reported, but significant hazards identified.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`
