**Vietnam Uses AI for Online Propaganda and Censorship**  
_2026-05-08 · real-world · Severity: High_

Vietnam's Communist Party is implementing a strategy to use AI-powered moderation tools and social media influencers to control online narratives and suppress dissent. The plan involves recruiting thousands of AI experts to remove content and guide discussions, leading to ongoing violations of freedom of expression and information.
