**BlackRock CEO Warns AI Could Deepen Economic Inequality**  
_2026-01-25 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

At the World Economic Forum in Davos, BlackRock CEO Larry Fink warned that unchecked AI development could exacerbate economic inequality, turning workers into "spectators" rather than beneficiaries of growth. He urged reforms to ensure AI-driven wealth is distributed more equitably, highlighting risks to capitalism and labor markets.
