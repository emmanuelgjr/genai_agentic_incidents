**Tesla Announces Imminent Release of Full Self-Driving Beta with Zero-Intervention Capability**  
_2020-10-06 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Tesla, led by Elon Musk, plans to release a Full Self-Driving (FSD) Beta capable of zero-intervention autonomous driving, including in new markets like Japan. While no incidents have occurred, the deployment raises concerns about potential risks and safety issues if the AI system fails to handle local driving rules.
