### INC-06455  [↗](/incident/INC-06455.html)

**Civilian Harm and Risks from AI-Driven Military and Police Drones**  
_2020-09-21 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI-powered drones are increasingly used in warfare and policing, with documented civilian casualties in conflicts like Yemen and Ukraine. Police use drones for surveillance and arrests, raising rights concerns. While new AI systems enhance drone threat mitigation and military exercises test drone swarms, the proliferation of autonomous drones poses ongoing risks and harm.
