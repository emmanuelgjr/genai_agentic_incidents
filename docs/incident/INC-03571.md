### INC-03571  [↗](/incident/INC-03571.html)

**Anthropic reveals 'many-shot jailbreaking' vulnerability in LLMs**  
_2024-04-02 · research-demonstrated · Severity: High_

Anthropic researchers disclosed 'many-shot jailbreaking', where padding context windows with hundreds of in-context examples of harmful Q&A reliably defeats safety alignment on Claude and other frontier LLMs.

**Affected:** Frontier LLMs (Claude, GPT-4, Gemini, Llama)  
**Attack vector:** `jailbreak`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM01`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI01`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MANAGE-2.4`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0051`, `AML.T0054`
