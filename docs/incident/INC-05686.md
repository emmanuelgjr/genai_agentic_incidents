### INC-05686  [↗](/incident/INC-05686.html)

**GPT-3 Used to Generate Persuasive Disinformation on Social Media**  
_2021-05-21 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Researchers at Georgetown University demonstrated that OpenAI's GPT-3 can generate convincing disinformation, including misleading tweets about climate change and foreign affairs. Human readers found these AI-generated posts persuasive, highlighting the real-world risk of AI systems amplifying misinformation and manipulating public opinion.

**Attack vector:** `misinformation`
