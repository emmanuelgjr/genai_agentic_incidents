### INC-00913  [↗](/incident/INC-00913.html)

**Dutch Authors and Journalists Demand Meta Stop Using Copyrighted Works for AI Training**  
_2026-02-27 · real-world · Severity: High_

Dutch writers, translators, and journalists, represented by the Auteursbond, NVJ, and Stichting Lira, have formally demanded that Meta cease using their copyrighted texts without permission or payment to train AI models like Llama. They allege this practice violates intellectual property rights and undermines creators' economic interests.
