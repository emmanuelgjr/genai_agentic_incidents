### INC-01906  [↗](/incident/INC-01906.html)

**TikTok's AI Meme Remixer Sparks Privacy and Consent Backlash**  
_2026-04-21 · real-world · Severity: High_

TikTok tested an AI-powered Meme Remixer feature that allowed users' public videos to be altered into AI-generated memes without explicit consent, as the opt-in was enabled by default. This led to significant creator backlash over privacy, consent, and potential misuse of their likeness, prompting TikTok to pause the feature.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
