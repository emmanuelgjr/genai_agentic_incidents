**AnyVision Patents AI-Driven Facial Recognition Drones, Raising Surveillance Concerns**  
_2021-02-22 · real-world · Severity: High_

Israeli company AnyVision has patented an AI system enabling drones to autonomously adjust their position for optimal facial recognition from the air. While not yet deployed, the technology raises significant concerns about potential privacy violations, mass surveillance, and human rights abuses if used by law enforcement or governments without safeguards.
