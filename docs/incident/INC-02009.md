**US Lawmakers Move to Tighten Export Controls on AI Chip-Making Equipment to China**  
_2026-04-01 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

US lawmakers have introduced the MATCH Act to restrict the sale of advanced semiconductor manufacturing equipment to China, aiming to curb Beijing's AI development. The legislation targets loopholes allowing China to acquire critical AI chip-making tools, reflecting concerns over potential future AI-related security risks.
