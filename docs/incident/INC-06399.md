### INC-06399  [↗](/incident/INC-06399.html)

**BJP Used Deepfake Videos of Manoj Tiwari to Target Haryanvi Voters in 2020 Delhi Election**  
_2020-02-07 · real-world · Severity: Critical_
AIID: [`#1336`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1336/)

In February 2020, India&#x27;s BJP used purported deepfake videos of party leader Manoj Tiwari to influence voters in the Delhi legislative election. The videos reportedly showed Tiwari delivering scripted campaign messages in Haryanvi and English, which are languages he did not speak, by altering his lip movements and voice. Distributed via thousands of WhatsApp groups, the deepfakes reportedly reached millions of voters without disclosure.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
