### INC-02194  [↗](/incident/INC-02194.html)

**AI agents demonstrate autonomous exploitation of blockchain smart contracts**  
_2025-12-02 · research-demonstrated · Severity: High_

Researchers showed LLM-driven agents could autonomously identify and exploit vulnerabilities in real Ethereum smart contracts, including reentrancy and access-control bugs, at a fraction of the cost of human auditors, with implications for both defensive and offensive use.

**Affected:** DeFi smart-contract operators  
**Attack vector:** `agent-hijack`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM06`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI02`, `ASI10`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048`, `AML.T0053`, `AML.T0054`
