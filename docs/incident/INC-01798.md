**SPARC AI Expands AI-Powered Drone Navigation in Ukraine Amid Military Applications**  
_2026-04-20 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

SPARC AI has expanded its distribution of AI-powered, GPS-independent drone navigation systems in Ukraine, partnering with the National Guard for frontline deployment. While the technology addresses vulnerabilities in GPS-denied combat environments, no direct harm or malfunction has been reported. The deployment highlights potential future risks of autonomous AI in warfare.
