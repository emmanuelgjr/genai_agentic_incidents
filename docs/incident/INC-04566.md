### INC-04566  [↗](/incident/INC-04566.html)

**Chinese Businessman Reportedly Defrauded of 4.3 Million Yuan by AI-Generated Deepfake Impersonating Friend**  
_2023-04-15 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#992`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/992/)

A scammer in China used AI-generated deepfake technology to impersonate a businessman’s trusted friend in a video call, convincing him to transfer 4.3 million yuan ($612,000). The fraudster mimicked the friend’s face and voice, claiming another associate needed funds for a business transaction. Without verifying the transfer, the victim sent the money. He later realized the deception and contacted police, who managed to recover 3.4 million yuan.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
