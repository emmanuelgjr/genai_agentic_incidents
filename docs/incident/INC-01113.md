### INC-01113  [↗](/incident/INC-01113.html)

**Google Detects First AI-Developed Zero-Day Exploit in Major Cyberattack Attempt**  
_2026-05-12 · real-world · Severity: High_

Google's Threat Intelligence Group identified hackers using generative AI, including large language models, to develop zero-day exploits targeting two-factor authentication systems. The AI-enabled attack, intended for mass exploitation, was proactively detected and stopped, highlighting the growing use of AI in sophisticated global cyber threats.
