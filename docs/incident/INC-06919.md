### INC-06919  [↗](/incident/INC-06919.html)

**Snapchat Faces Backlash Over AI Juneteenth Filter Requiring Users to Smile to Break Chains**  
_2020-06-19 · real-world · Severity: High_

Snapchat released an AI-powered Juneteenth filter that prompted users to smile to break virtual chains, causing widespread offense for its insensitivity. The filter, which used facial recognition to trigger effects, was quickly removed after public backlash. Snap apologized and launched an internal investigation into the incident.
