### INC-07774  [↗](/incident/INC-07774.html)

**AI-Enabled Drone Used for Unauthorized Military Surveillance in China**  
_2026-05-18 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

An individual in China illegally used an AI-enabled drone with a high-definition camera to capture sensitive images of aircraft carriers and military facilities. This unauthorized surveillance led to a significant breach of national security, as the images could reveal classified information about military technology and construction progress.
