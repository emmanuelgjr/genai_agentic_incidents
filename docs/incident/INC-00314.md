### INC-00314  [↗](/incident/INC-00314.html)

**AI-Driven Phishing Attacks Bypass Microsoft Security, Compromise Thousands**  
_2026-03-02 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI-powered phishing toolkits like Tycoon2FA and InboxPrime AI have enabled attackers to bypass Microsoft 365 security, including multi-factor authentication, leading to widespread account takeovers, especially in the US, UK, and Germany. Microsoft Defender struggles to block sophisticated, AI-generated phishing, resulting in significant harm to organizations and users.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`
