### INC-07372  [↗](/incident/INC-07372.html)

**Amazon Alexa Plays Loud Music when Owner is Away**  
_2017-11-09 · real-world · Severity: Medium_
AIID: [`#33`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/33/)

Oliver Haberstroh's Amazon Alexa device in Hamburg, Germany spontaneously activated at 1:50 AM on a Friday night/Saturday morning while he was out at the Reeperbahn enjoying a beer. The device began playing music from Spotify at full volume without any command from the user or use of his mobile phone. The loud music continued from 1:50 AM to 3:00 AM, disturbing neighbors who initially banged on his door asking him to turn it down. When there was no response because Haberstroh was not home, the frustrated neighbors called police. The police also received no response when they knocked, so concerned that something terrible had happened, they broke down the door to discover only the Alexa device playing music on its own. The police turned off the music, replaced the door lock with a new one, and left. When Haberstroh returned home, he found his keys no longer worked and had to visit the police station to get new keys along with a 500 euro ($582) locksmith bill. Amazon's logs showed the device started playing music just three minutes after Haberstroh left his apartment, suggesting he may have accidentally triggered Alexa while trying to use Spotify on his phone before leaving WiFi range. Amazon later offered to pay the 500 euro bill as a goodwill gesture.
