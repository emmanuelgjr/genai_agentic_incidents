**AI-Enabled Military Ground Vehicle BARKAN 3 Unveiled in Turkey**  
_2026-05-09 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

HAVELSAN unveiled the AI-integrated unmanned ground vehicle BARKAN 3 at SAHA 2026 in Istanbul. The vehicle features autonomous navigation, 360-degree sensing, UAV management, and AI-supported target detection. While no harm has occurred, its military capabilities pose plausible future risks if misused or malfunctioning.
