### INC-01527  [↗](/incident/INC-01527.html)

**New Zealand Develops AI Tool to Redirect Extremist Users to Deradicalization Support**  
_2026-04-13 · real-world · Severity: High_

ThroughLine, contracted by OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google, is developing an AI system in New Zealand to detect users exhibiting violent extremist tendencies on platforms like ChatGPT and redirect them to human and chatbot-based deradicalization support. The tool aims to prevent harm but is still in testing.
