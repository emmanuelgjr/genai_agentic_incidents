### INC-00331  [↗](/incident/INC-00331.html)

**AI-Enabled Autonomous Kamikaze Drones Demonstrated in Turkey**  
_2026-04-24 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Baykar showcased its new AI-powered kamikaze drones, K2 and Sivrisinek, in Keşan, Turkey. The demonstration highlighted autonomous swarm navigation, target detection, and attack capabilities. These AI-enabled weapon systems, set to debut at SAHA 2026, pose potential risks of harm if deployed in conflict scenarios.

**Attack vector:** `rce`
