**Facebook Sued for Alleged Illegal Biometric Data Collection via Instagram's AI Facial Recognition**  
_2020-08-11 · real-world · Severity: High_

Facebook faces a class-action lawsuit alleging Instagram used AI-powered facial recognition to collect, store, and profit from biometric data of over 100 million users without consent, violating privacy laws. Facebook denies the claims, asserting Instagram does not use facial recognition technology.
