### INC-06634  [↗](/incident/INC-06634.html)

**German Debate on AI-Enabled Armed Drones Raises Ethical and Political Concerns**  
_2020-06-28 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

German political and religious groups are debating the potential deployment of AI-enabled armed drones by the Bundeswehr. Critics warn of ethical risks, including machines making life-or-death decisions, while the SPD supports arming drones only under strict conditions to address legal, psychological, and transparency concerns. No actual harm has occurred yet.
