### INC-00054  [↗](/incident/INC-00054.html)

**Ahmedabad Aadhaar Fraud Racket Reportedly Used Purportedly AI-Generated Deepfakes to Change Businessman&#x27;s Linked Mobile Number**  
_2026-04-29 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1472`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1472/)

Ahmedabad Cyber Crime police reportedly arrested four people after businessman Amit Patel alleged that his Aadhaar-linked mobile number had been changed without consent. Police reportedly said the accused allegedly used purportedly AI-generated deepfake videos made from Patel&#x27;s photo to bypass facial authentication, access DigiLocker/e-KYC services, open bank accounts, and apply for loans.
