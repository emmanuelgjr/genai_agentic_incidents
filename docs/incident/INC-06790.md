**Microsoft Refuses to Sell Facial Recognition AI to Police Amid Misuse Concerns**  
_2020-06-11 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Microsoft announced it will not sell its facial recognition AI technology to U.S. police until federal laws ensuring human rights protections are enacted. The decision follows concerns, heightened by George Floyd's death, that such AI could be misused against protesters and minorities, prompting calls for regulation.
