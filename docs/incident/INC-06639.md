**Giorgia Meloni Reportedly Targeted by Deepfake Pornography**  
_2020-01-01 · real-world · Severity: Critical_
AIID: [`#926`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/926/)

In 2020, alleged deepfake pornographic videos falsely depicting Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni were uploaded to a U.S. website and viewed millions of times. At the time, she was leader of the Brothers of Italy but not yet PM. In 2024, now serving as PM, Meloni reportedly sued Alessio Scurosu, 40, and his father, Roberto Scurosu, 73, seeking €100,000 in damages. She testified in court, calling the act &quot;a form of violence.&quot;
