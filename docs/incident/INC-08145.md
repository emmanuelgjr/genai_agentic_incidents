### INC-08145  [↗](/incident/INC-08145.html)

**Anthropic's Claude Mythos AI Raises Global Cybersecurity Risks**  
_2026-06-07 · real-world · Severity: High_

Anthropic's advanced AI model, Claude Mythos, can autonomously discover and chain zero-day vulnerabilities, potentially enabling rapid, large-scale cyberattacks. While currently restricted to select organizations for defensive use, experts and governments warn that broader access could democratize cyberattack capabilities, outpacing defensive measures and threatening critical infrastructure worldwide.
