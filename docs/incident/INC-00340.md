**AI-Enabled Drones Cause Significant Harm in Ukraine Conflict**  
_2026-02-20 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

AI-powered drones are causing extensive casualties and psychological harm in the ongoing Russia-Ukraine war, with reports indicating up to 1,000 Russian casualties daily and Ukrainian soldiers enduring relentless drone attacks. These autonomous or semi-autonomous systems are reshaping modern warfare, inflicting direct harm on combatants and altering battlefield tactics.
