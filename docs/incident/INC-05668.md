**Google Fires AI Ethics Researcher After Warning of Discrimination Risks**  
_2021-06-08 · real-world · Severity: High_

Google fired AI ethics researcher Timnit Gebru after she raised concerns that large language models could perpetuate discrimination against marginalized groups. Gebru was pressured to retract a critical research paper, highlighting organizational suppression of ethical concerns and labor rights violations linked to AI system development.
