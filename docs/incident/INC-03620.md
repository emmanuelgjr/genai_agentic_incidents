### INC-03620  [↗](/incident/INC-03620.html)

**Celebrities deepfaked in AI scam to endorse self-help course**  
_2024-02-24 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

AI-generated deepfake voices and videos of Piers Morgan, Nigella Lawson, Oprah Winfrey and others were used without consent to falsely endorse a paid self-help course on social media, channeling viewers into a paid funnel.

**Affected:** Public; named celebrities  
**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048`, `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
