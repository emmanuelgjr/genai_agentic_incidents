### INC-00395  [↗](/incident/INC-00395.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfake Video of António Horta Osório Used in Investment Scam**  
_2026-03-26 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

AI-generated deepfake videos featuring Portuguese banker António Horta Osório were circulated on social media, falsely depicting him promoting fraudulent investment schemes. The manipulated content, designed to appear as credible news, aimed to deceive users into sharing personal data or transferring money, highlighting the growing misuse of AI for sophisticated scams in Portugal.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`
