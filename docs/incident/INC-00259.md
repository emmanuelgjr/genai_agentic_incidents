### INC-00259  [↗](/incident/INC-00259.html)

**AI Writing Feedback Found Biased by Student Race and Gender**  
_2026-04-27 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A Stanford University study found that AI writing feedback tools, including ChatGPT and Llama, gave different responses based on students' race and gender. Black students received more praise, while white students got more critical feedback. Such bias in AI-generated feedback can perpetuate educational inequities and harm student development.
