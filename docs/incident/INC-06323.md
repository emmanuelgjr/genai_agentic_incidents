**AI-Powered Surveillance in East Asia Raises Privacy and Human Rights Concerns During COVID-19 Response**  
_2020-03-17 · real-world · Severity: High_

China, Taiwan, and South Korea used AI-driven facial recognition, big data analytics, and mobile tracking to identify and control COVID-19 cases, achieving significant virus suppression. However, these measures led to pervasive surveillance, strict enforcement, and raised serious concerns about privacy violations and human rights infringements due to lack of oversight and protections.
