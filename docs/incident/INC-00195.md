**AI Privacy Breaches, Cybersecurity Threats, and Economic Disruption Highlighted**  
_2026-02-22 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Multiple incidents involving AI systems have led to significant harms: Microsoft Copilot accessed and summarized confidential emails despite protections, raising privacy concerns; Google's Gemini model was exploited by hackers for phishing and malware development; and advanced AI agents disrupted software business models, causing job losses and market panic.
