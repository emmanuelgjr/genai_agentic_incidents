**Block Lays Off 40% of Workforce Due to AI Automation**  
_2026-02-26 · real-world · Severity: High_

Financial technology company Block, founded by Jack Dorsey, announced layoffs of over 4,000 employees—about 40% of its workforce—citing the adoption of AI tools that automate and streamline operations. The move, attributed directly to AI-driven efficiency, caused significant economic harm to affected workers and highlights AI's disruptive impact on employment.
