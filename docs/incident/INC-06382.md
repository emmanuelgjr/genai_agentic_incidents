### INC-06382  [↗](/incident/INC-06382.html)

**Autonomous Bus Malfunctions and Hits Guardrail During Japanese Field Test**  
_2020-12-14 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

During a field test of a medium-sized autonomous bus in Hitachi City, Japan, the AI-driven vehicle suddenly swerved and struck a guardrail. No injuries occurred, but the incident caused property damage and led to the immediate suspension of the experiment pending a safety investigation.
