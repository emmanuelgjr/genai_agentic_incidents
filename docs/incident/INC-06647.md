**GM's Cruise and Waymo Receive Approval for Driverless Autonomous Vehicle Deployment in San Francisco**  
_2020-10-16 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

General Motors' Cruise and Alphabet's Waymo have received regulatory approval to deploy fully autonomous vehicles without human drivers in San Francisco. While no incidents have occurred, the move raises potential safety concerns as these AI-driven vehicles begin operating on public roads without human oversight.
