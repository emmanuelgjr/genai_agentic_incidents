### INC-06705  [↗](/incident/INC-06705.html)

**India Deploys AI Facial Recognition to Monitor Protesters, Raising Human Rights Concerns**  
_2020-01-06 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The Indian government, led by Prime Minister Narendra Modi, has deployed AI-powered facial recognition systems during protests and public events, including in Delhi. Critics warn this mass surveillance threatens privacy and suppresses dissent, especially amid ongoing protests against a controversial citizenship law, with at least 25 protest-related deaths reported.
