### INC-06371  [↗](/incident/INC-06371.html)

**Artist Manipulates Google Maps AI to Create Fake Traffic Jams in Berlin**  
_2020-02-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

Berlin-based artist Simon Weckert used a cart loaded with 99 smartphones to trick Google Maps’ AI-driven traffic system into displaying false traffic jams. This manipulation misled drivers and disrupted normal navigation, highlighting vulnerabilities in AI-based traffic prediction and the potential for harm through data input exploitation.
