**UK Advances AI-Enabled 'Loyal Wingman' Drones for Apache Helicopters**  
_2026-01-24 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The UK Ministry of Defence has selected seven companies to develop AI-powered autonomous 'loyal wingman' drones under Project NYX. These drones will support Apache attack helicopters in reconnaissance, strike, and electronic warfare missions, raising credible future risks associated with autonomous military systems. No harm has yet occurred.
