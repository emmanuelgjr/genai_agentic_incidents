### INC-01095  [↗](/incident/INC-01095.html)

**Global Regulators Respond to Harm from Non-Consensual AI-Generated Images**  
_2026-02-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

Data protection authorities from 61 countries issued a joint warning after AI systems, notably Grok on X, generated and shared millions of non-consensual intimate images of real people. The incident led to regulatory action, including blocking Grok's image generation and new legal requirements to protect privacy and dignity.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
