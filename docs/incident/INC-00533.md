**AI-Powered Apple Watch App Trial Aims to Detect Infections in Pediatric Cancer Patients**  
_2026-05-06 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Researchers at Murdoch Children's Research Institute in Australia are trialing an AI-powered app that analyzes Apple Watch health data to detect early signs of infection in children undergoing cancer treatment. The system aims to enable earlier intervention for immunocompromised patients, though no harm or malfunction has been reported.
