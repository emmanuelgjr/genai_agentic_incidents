### INC-00428  [↗](/incident/INC-00428.html)

**AI-Generated Disinformation Campaign Targets Singapore's Prime Minister**  
_2026-02-25 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A coordinated disinformation campaign used AI-generated, Chinese-language YouTube videos to spread false narratives and conspiracy theories about Singapore and Prime Minister Lawrence Wong. Nearly 300 videos, featuring synthetic voiceovers and deepfake avatars, amassed millions of views, undermining political trust and exploiting search engine optimization tactics.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
