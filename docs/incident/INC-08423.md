### INC-08423  [↗](/incident/INC-08423.html)

**ImageMagick: Heap Buffer Over-Write in json and yaml encoder of a single byte due to incorrect fix**  
_2026-05 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: High_

CVEs: `CVE-2026-33899`, `CVE-2026-33908`, `CVE-2026-40169`
CWEs: `CWE-122`, `CWE-191`, `CWE-193`, `CWE-674`
CVSS: **6.2** — `CVSS:3.1/AV:L/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:U/C:N/I:N/A:H`
