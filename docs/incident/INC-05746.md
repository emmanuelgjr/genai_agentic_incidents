### INC-05746  [↗](/incident/INC-05746.html)

**Lisbon Law School Drops AI Exam Surveillance After Privacy Backlash**  
_2021-03-25 · real-world · Severity: High_

The University of Lisbon Law School planned to use the AI-based Proctorio system for online exam monitoring, recording students' movements, sounds, and screens. Widespread student protests over privacy and data protection violations led the institution to abandon the AI system and revert to in-person exams.

**Attack vector:** `other`
