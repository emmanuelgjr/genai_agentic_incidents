**Dutch Scientists Call for Ban on Lethal Autonomous Weapons**  
_2020-11-30 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Over 150 Dutch AI and robotics scientists have urged the government to support a ban on lethal autonomous weapons—AI systems capable of selecting and attacking targets without meaningful human control. They warn such weapons could lower the threshold for war, escalate conflicts, and raise accountability concerns if deployed.
