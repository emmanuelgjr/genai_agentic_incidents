### INC-01263  [↗](/incident/INC-01263.html)

**IXOPAY and Zip Launch Framework to Address AI Risks in Agentic Commerce**  
_2026-03-24 · real-world · Severity: High_

IXOPAY and Zip have launched a joint initiative to develop a "Unified Trust Layer" framework aimed at addressing trust, identity, and liability challenges in AI-driven, agent-initiated commerce. The framework seeks to mitigate potential risks such as fraud as AI agents increasingly conduct autonomous transactions. No actual harm has occurred yet.
