**Uber Self-Driving Car Fatality Leads to Negligent Homicide Charge for Safety Driver**  
_2020-09-15 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

In 2018, an Uber self-driving car in Tempe, Arizona, struck and killed pedestrian Elaine Herzberg. The AI system failed to respond appropriately, and the distracted safety driver, Rafaela Vasquez, did not intervene in time. Vasquez was charged with negligent homicide, highlighting risks in autonomous vehicle deployment.
