**Virginia Police Violate ALPR Laws, Raising Privacy Concerns**  
_2026-03-01 · real-world · Severity: High_

A Virginia State Crime Commission report found that several law enforcement agencies in Virginia have violated new statutes regulating AI-powered automatic license plate readers (ALPRs), including improper data retention and unauthorized data sharing, resulting in breaches of privacy rights. Nearly one-third of agencies failed to report their ALPR use.
