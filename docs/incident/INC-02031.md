**US Regulators Warn Banks of AI-Driven Cyber Risks from Anthropic Model**  
_2026-04-11 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

US Treasury Secretary Janet Yellen and Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell convened major bank CEOs to address concerns that Anthropic's new AI model, Claude Mitos, could identify software vulnerabilities and facilitate cyberattacks on financial infrastructure. Authorities warned that AI-enabled attacks pose a significant risk to the financial sector.
