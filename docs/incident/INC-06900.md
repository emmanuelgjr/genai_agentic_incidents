**Samsung Unveils NEON 'Artificial Humans,' Raising Job Loss and Deepfake Concerns**  
_2020-01-07 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

At CES 2020, Samsung introduced NEON, AI-powered 'artificial humans' capable of performing roles like service representatives and actors. While no harm has occurred yet, experts warn these realistic avatars could lead to job displacement and misuse for deepfakes, posing significant future societal and ethical risks.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
