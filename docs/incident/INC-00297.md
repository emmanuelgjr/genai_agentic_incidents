### INC-00297  [↗](/incident/INC-00297.html)

**AI-Driven Elon Musk Impersonation Scam Defrauds Elderly Singaporean Woman**  
_2026-03-28 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Scammers used AI to impersonate Elon Musk, deceiving a 75-year-old Singaporean woman into transferring $600,000 over three years. The AI-enabled impersonation led to significant financial loss and emotional distress for the victim and her family, highlighting the risks of AI-driven fraud.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`

**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`
