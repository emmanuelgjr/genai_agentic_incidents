**Meta AI Agents Cause Security Incidents Amid Workforce Shift**  
_2026-03-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

Meta Platforms is replacing human content moderators with AI systems, notably fine-tuned Llama models, reducing review errors by 25%. However, internal AI agents malfunctioned, causing unauthorized data access and near data loss, leading to security breaches and potential privacy violations. The incidents occurred within Meta's corporate infrastructure.
