**SpaceX's Million-Satellite AI Data Center Plan Raises Environmental and Astronomical Concerns**  
_2026-03-07 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

SpaceX has filed to deploy up to one million AI-powered satellites as orbital data centers, aiming to outpace terrestrial cloud competitors. Experts warn this could severely disrupt night sky visibility, increase orbital debris, and introduce atmospheric pollution, posing significant environmental and scientific risks.
