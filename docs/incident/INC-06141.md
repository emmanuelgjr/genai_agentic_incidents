**UK Regulator Warns of Privacy Risks from Live Facial Recognition in Public Spaces**  
_2021-06-17 · real-world · Severity: High_

The UK Information Commissioner's Office has warned about the risks of live facial recognition (LFR) technology in public places, citing concerns over mass biometric data collection, privacy violations, and potential misuse. The regulator urges companies to ensure lawful, proportionate use and compliance with data protection laws to prevent harm.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`
