### INC-06638  [↗](/incident/INC-06638.html)

**Germany Funds Research on Safety of Automated AI-Driven Rail Operations**  
_2020-11-24 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Siemens Mobility and partners, funded by Germany’s DZSF, are leading two research projects to define safety and approval criteria for fully automated regional and mainline trains (GoA 3 and 4). The studies aim to ensure AI-driven trains match or exceed human safety standards, addressing potential future risks.
