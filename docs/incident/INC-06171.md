### INC-06171  [↗](/incident/INC-06171.html)

**US Regulators Alarmed by Tesla Autopilot Safety Vulnerability**  
_2021-04-23 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

US road safety agency NHTSA expressed concern after Consumer Reports engineers demonstrated they could disable safety features in Tesla's autonomous driving system, potentially allowing unsafe vehicle operation. NHTSA may take action if this vulnerability is found to pose a serious road safety risk.
