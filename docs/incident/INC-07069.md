**US Air Force Awards $400M Contracts for AI-Enabled Skyborg Combat Drones**  
_2020-07-24 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The US Air Force has awarded contracts worth up to $400 million to Boeing, General Atomics, Kratos, and Northrop Grumman to develop Skyborg, an AI-powered autonomous combat drone. While no harm has occurred yet, the program poses future risks due to the potential deployment of autonomous AI in military operations.
