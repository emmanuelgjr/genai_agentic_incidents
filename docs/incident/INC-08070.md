### INC-08070  [↗](/incident/INC-08070.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfake Scam Impersonates Greek Celebrity Anna Vissi**  
_2026-06-03 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Scammers used AI to create fake videos and voices impersonating Greek singer Anna Vissi, promising large cash prizes to fans on TikTok. Victims were contacted via WhatsApp by a fake manager and tricked into sharing personal and banking information, resulting in financial harm. The incident occurred in Greece.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
