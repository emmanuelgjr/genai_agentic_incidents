### INC-07850  [↗](/incident/INC-07850.html)

**AI-Powered Monitoring System Reduces Motorcycle Accidents in Brazil**  
_2026-05-21 · real-world · Severity: High_

Mobility platform 99 deployed an AI-driven monitoring system using sensors and algorithms to track and alert partner motorcyclists about risky driving behaviors. The initiative, piloted in Rio de Janeiro, led to a significant reduction in accidents, with up to 82% of riders correcting unsafe practices after receiving app alerts.
