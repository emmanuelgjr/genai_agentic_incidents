### INC-00402  [↗](/incident/INC-00402.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfake Videos Target Belgian Crown Princess Elisabeth**  
_2026-03-28 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI-generated deepfake videos and images of Belgian Crown Princess Elisabeth circulated widely on Facebook via a fake profile, causing reputational harm and public distress. The Royal Palace intervened to report and remove the content, highlighting the risks of AI-driven impersonation and misinformation.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
