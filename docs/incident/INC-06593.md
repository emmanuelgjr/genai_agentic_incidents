### INC-06593  [↗](/incident/INC-06593.html)

**Facial Recognition AI Expands Amid COVID-19, Raising Civil Liberties Concerns**  
_2020-04-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

Facial recognition AI systems, often combined with thermal imaging, have seen increased deployment by governments and companies during the COVID-19 pandemic. This expansion has led to ongoing privacy violations and civil rights concerns, particularly for marginalized groups, as legal frameworks like Washington state's new law further legitimize their use.
