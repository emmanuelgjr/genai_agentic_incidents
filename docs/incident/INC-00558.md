### INC-00558  [↗](/incident/INC-00558.html)

**AI-Powered Synthetic Identity Fraud Hits Financial Sector**  
_2026-04-24 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Fraud rings are using generative AI to create synthetic identities and fake documents, successfully deceiving lenders and businesses. This has led to significant financial losses, with incidents reported in India and warnings from Equifax about the global threat of AI-driven fraud targeting digital lending and payment systems.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
