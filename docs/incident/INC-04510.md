### INC-04510  [↗](/incident/INC-04510.html)

**Canada investigates ChatGPT privacy concerns**  
_2023-04 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Canada's federal privacy commissioner and three provincial counterparts opened a joint investigation into OpenAI's collection, use and disclosure of personal information through ChatGPT under PIPEDA, paralleling EU regulatory action.

**Affected:** Canadian residents  
**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM03`, `LLM07`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI02`, `ASI03`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.1`, `GOVERN-1.4`, `GOVERN-6.1`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.10`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0010`, `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0024`, `AML.T0053`, `AML.T0056`, `AML.T0057`
