### INC-00472  [↗](/incident/INC-00472.html)

**AI-Generated False Citations Prompt Procurement Overhaul in Newfoundland and Labrador**  
_2026-03-11 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Newfoundland and Labrador government overhauled its procurement process after AI-generated false citations appeared in official reports, including one by Deloitte Canada. Vendors must now disclose AI use, and the province can audit AI involvement, aiming to prevent misinformation in government documents.
