**Tesla Autopilot Misuse and Safety Incidents Highlight AI Risks**  
_2020-09-29 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Multiple incidents involving Tesla's AI-based Autopilot system have raised safety concerns, including drivers sleeping or sitting in the passenger seat while the car is in motion. These misuses, along with reports of accidents and critical safety ratings from agencies, underscore the risks and realized harms associated with semi-autonomous driving technology.
