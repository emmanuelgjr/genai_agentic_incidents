**Deepfake Recordings Allegedly Influence Slovakian Election**  
_2023-10-07 · real-world · Severity: Critical_
AIID: [`#573`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/573/)

Days before Slovakia&#x27;s election, deepfake audio recordings surfaced, allegedly featuring conversations between a journalist and a leading liberal politician discussing vote-rigging and other controversial topics. The recordings were spread on social media platforms and may have influenced the election outcome, which saw the pro-Russian populist party winning.
