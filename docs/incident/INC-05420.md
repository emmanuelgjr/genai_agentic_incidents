**AI-Enabled Drones Used in Houthi Attacks on Saudi Targets Intercepted by Coalition**  
_2021-04-06 · real-world · Severity: High_

Houthi forces launched multiple AI-enabled or autonomous drones targeting Saudi airports and cities, including Jazan, Abha, and Khamis Mushait. The Saudi-led coalition intercepted and destroyed the drones, preventing harm to civilians and infrastructure. The incidents highlight the use of AI-driven weaponry in armed conflict, posing significant risks to civilian safety.
