### INC-07738  [↗](/incident/INC-07738.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfakes and Synthetic Voters Disrupt Brazilian Election Campaigns**  
_2026-05-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI tools are being used in Brazil's 2026 election campaigns to create manipulated content, including deepfakes, segment messages, and generate synthetic voter profiles. Despite legal restrictions, these practices are causing misinformation and disruption, undermining democratic processes and violating electoral laws.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
