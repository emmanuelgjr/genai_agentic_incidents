### INC-05483  [↗](/incident/INC-05483.html)

**Apple AirTags Misused for Stalking and Privacy Violations After Jailbreak**  
_2021-05-11 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Apple AirTags, designed for item tracking using AI-enabled location networks, have been jailbroken, allowing malicious firmware modifications. Experiments and expert warnings reveal AirTags can be covertly used for stalking and unauthorized tracking, bypassing anti-stalking features and raising significant privacy and safety concerns.
