### INC-07917  [↗](/incident/INC-07917.html)

**Malaysia Demands TikTok Address AI-Generated Offensive Content Targeting King**  
_2026-05-21 · real-world · Severity: High_

Malaysian authorities have ordered TikTok to explain its failure to promptly moderate and remove AI-generated offensive and defamatory content, including fake videos and manipulated images targeting the king. The inadequate response of TikTok's AI moderation system has raised concerns about public order and national harmony in Malaysia.
