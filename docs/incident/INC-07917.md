### INC-07917  [↗](/incident/INC-07917.html)

**AI-Generated Legal Submissions Increase Court Workload and User Costs in Basel**  
_2026-05-26 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Courts in Basel-Stadt report that increased use of AI-generated legal documents by law firms and laypersons has led to a higher workload for judges and financial risks for users who misunderstand or misuse AI outputs. This trend is causing procedural complications and unnecessary costs in the justice system.
