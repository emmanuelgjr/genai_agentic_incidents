**Controversy and Policy Responses to Facial Recognition Expansion in Law Enforcement and Public Spaces**  
_2020-10-26 · real-world · Severity: High_

U.S. and Dubai authorities are expanding facial recognition use in law enforcement, raising concerns about privacy, bias, and civil rights. In contrast, Hangzhou, China, is considering banning facial recognition in residential areas to address privacy risks, reflecting growing global debate over AI surveillance and its societal impacts.
