### INC-06619  [↗](/incident/INC-06619.html)

**First AI-Guided Munition Fired from Turkish UAV**  
_2020-09-10 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Turkey successfully tested the AI-enabled Aksungur UAV by firing the Teber laser-guided munition, marking the first time a drone launched a munition previously used by fighter jets. This milestone demonstrates the operational deployment of AI-guided weapon systems, raising future risks associated with autonomous military technologies.

**Attack vector:** `other`  

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI10`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048`
