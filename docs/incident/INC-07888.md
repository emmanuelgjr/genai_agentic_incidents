**Argentina's AI-Driven Government Project Raises Privacy Concerns**  
_2026-05-25 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The Argentine government launched the "Social Digital Twin" program, using AI to process vast public and private data for policy-making and governance. While officials tout efficiency gains, opposition parties and digital rights groups warn of privacy risks, potential data misuse, and lack of legal safeguards, prompting calls for legislative investigation.
