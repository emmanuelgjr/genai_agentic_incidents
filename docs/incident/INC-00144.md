### INC-00144  [↗](/incident/INC-00144.html)

**AI Deepfake Celebrity Scams Target TikTok Users**  
_2026-04-29 · real-world · Severity: High_

Scammers are using AI-generated deepfake videos of celebrities like Taylor Swift and Rihanna on TikTok to promote fraudulent rewards programs and phishing scams. These sophisticated fakes, featuring cloned voices and manipulated footage, deceive users into sharing personal data, causing financial and privacy harm. The incidents have prompted celebrities to seek legal protections.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
