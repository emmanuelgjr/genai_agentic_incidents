### INC-08035  [↗](/incident/INC-08035.html)

**AI System in Japan Prevents Suicides at Train Stations and Commercial Buildings**  
_2026-06-01 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

In Japan, an AI system developed by Asilla Inc. analyzes CCTV footage at around 40 train stations and commercial buildings to detect behaviors indicating suicide risk. The system alerts security staff, enabling timely intervention, and has reportedly helped prevent at least two suicide attempts.

**Attack vector:** `unsafe-advice`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`
