### INC-08156  [↗](/incident/INC-08156.html)

**AI-Generated Fake Video of Lebanese Prime Minister Causes Misinformation**  
_2026-06-08 · real-world · Severity: High_

An AI-generated video falsely attributed to Lebanese Prime Minister Nawak Salam circulated online, spreading misinformation about his statements regarding Iran and Israel. The government confirmed the video is fabricated, urged citizens to rely on official sources, and is coordinating with social media platforms to address the issue.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
