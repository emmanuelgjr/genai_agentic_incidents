### INC-11355  [↗](/incident/INC-11355.html)

**Remarshal expands YAML alias nodes unlimitedly, hence Remarshal is vulnerable to Billion Laughs Attack**  
_2023-11 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: High_

CVEs: `CVE-2023-47163`
CWEs: `CWE-400`, `CWE-674`
CVSS: **7.5** — `CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:U/C:N/I:N/A:H`
