**UK Government Approves AI-Driven Self-Driving Cars with ALKS Technology Amid Safety Concerns**  
_2021-04-27 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The UK government is set to allow self-driving vehicles equipped with Automated Lane Keeping Systems (ALKS) on public roads, enabling limited autonomous driving in slow motorway traffic. While officials highlight potential safety benefits, critics warn of possible risks if the AI system fails, though no incidents have yet occurred.
