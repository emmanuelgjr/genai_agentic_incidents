### INC-05852  [↗](/incident/INC-05852.html)

**Snapchat Removes Speed Filter After AI-Driven Feature Linked to Fatal Crashes**  
_2021-06-17 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Snapchat has permanently removed its AI-powered speed filter after it was linked to multiple fatal and near-fatal car crashes. The feature, which displayed users' real-time speed, encouraged reckless driving, especially among teens, leading to lawsuits and widespread criticism over its role in promoting dangerous behavior.
