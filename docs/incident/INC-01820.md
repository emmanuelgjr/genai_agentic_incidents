**Study Links Prolonged Use of AI Chatbot Replika to Increased Anxiety and Mental Health Risks**  
_2026-04-07 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A study by Aalto University in Finland found that prolonged use of the AI chatbot Replika, designed for emotional support, can worsen users' anxiety, depression, and social isolation. Analysis of Reddit posts and interviews revealed increased signs of mental health deterioration among users over time.
