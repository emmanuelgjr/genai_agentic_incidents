### INC-02341  [↗](/incident/INC-02341.html)

**Attackers Reportedly Deployed Simulated AI Support Chatbot to Trick Instagram Business Users into Adding Malicious 2FA Login**  
_2025-03-12 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#999`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/999/)

A phishing campaign has reportedly been impersonating Meta support using a fake chatbot interface to hijack Instagram Business accounts. Victims received emails claiming ad violations and were directed to a fraudulent site mimicking Meta&#x27;s support. There, a simulated chatbot instructed users to add the attacker’s Authenticator app as a secure login method, enabling account takeover. It remains unclear whether the chatbot used AI or was human-operated via a bot-like interface. See editor&#x27;s note.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
