### INC-04111  [↗](/incident/INC-04111.html)

**Purported AI-Cloned Voice Depicts Kenyan President William Ruto Speaking French in Viral Video**  
_2024-02-19 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1023`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1023/)

A purportedly AI-manipulated video widely shared on TikTok and Facebook presents Kenyan President William Ruto speaking in French during a speech in Tokyo on February 7, 2024. A reported reverse image search confirmed the original footage shows Ruto speaking in English to the Kenyan diaspora. The reportedly manipulated version used AI-generated French audio over the original video, making it a case of voice cloning rather than a full audiovisual deepfake.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
