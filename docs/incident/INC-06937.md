**Study Finds Automated Vehicle AI Reduces Driver Attention, Raising Accident Risks**  
_2020-11-30 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

University of Toronto research shows that drivers using AI-powered automated vehicles become overly reliant on automation, leading to reduced attention and road monitoring. Eye-tracking studies revealed that certain in-vehicle displays further distract drivers, increasing the risk of accidents if human intervention is needed.
