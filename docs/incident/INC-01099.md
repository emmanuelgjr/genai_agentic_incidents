### INC-01099  [↗](/incident/INC-01099.html)

**Gmail AI Spam Filter Malfunction Disrupts Global Email Management**  
_2026-01-25 · real-world · Severity: High_

A malfunction in Gmail's AI-powered spam and email classification system caused widespread misclassification, with spam flooding users' primary inboxes and legitimate emails being mislabeled or delayed. The incident disrupted email management, raised privacy and security concerns, and affected millions of users globally before Google resolved the issue.
