### INC-00281  [↗](/incident/INC-00281.html)

**AI-Driven Cyberattacks Escalate in Indonesia, Causing Increased Harm**  
_2026-03-12 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Cybercriminals in Indonesia are increasingly using AI and machine learning to automate and scale cyberattacks, including phishing, credential theft, and ransomware. These AI-enabled attacks are more sophisticated and harder to detect, resulting in significant harm to organizations through data breaches and operational disruptions.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`, `ASI05`, `ASI09`, `ASI10`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.10`, `MEASURE-2.7`
