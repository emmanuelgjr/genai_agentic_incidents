### INC-06895  [↗](/incident/INC-06895.html)

**Russian Interior Ministry to Create Centralized Biometric Database Using AI**  
_2020-11-22 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Russian Interior Ministry plans to develop a centralized biometric database over three years, using AI technologies such as facial recognition, fingerprint, and genome analysis for identifying citizens and foreigners. While no harm is reported yet, the system poses significant future risks to privacy and human rights due to potential misuse or data breaches.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`
