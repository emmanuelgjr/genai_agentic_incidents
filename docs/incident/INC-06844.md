### INC-06844  [↗](/incident/INC-06844.html)

**Pinterest AI Misclassifies 'Chiquititas' Search as Child Exploitation Risk**  
_2020-10-01 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Pinterest's AI content moderation system mistakenly flagged searches for 'Chiquititas'—a popular children's TV show—as potential child exploitation, displaying alarming warnings to users. The error, caused by the algorithm misinterpreting the term, led to user confusion, distress, and widespread discussion on social media before Pinterest adjusted its system.
