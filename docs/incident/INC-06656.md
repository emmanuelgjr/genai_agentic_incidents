**Google AI Search Labels Agbani Darego as 'Ugliest Miss World,' Sparks Outrage in Nigeria**  
_2020-10-02 · real-world · Severity: High_

Google's AI-powered search engine surfaced Agbani Darego, the first indigenous African Miss World, as the 'ugliest Miss World,' leading to widespread outrage in Nigeria. The AI system's output caused reputational harm and accusations of racism and colorism, highlighting the societal impact of biased algorithmic content ranking.
