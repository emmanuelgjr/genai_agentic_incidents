### INC-08973  [↗](/incident/INC-08973.html)

**Roundcube Webmail: Insufficient HTML attachment sanitization in preview mode**  
_2026-04 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: Medium_

CVEs: `CVE-2026-35539`, `CVE-2026-35544`
CWEs: `CWE-669`, `CWE-79`
CVSS: **6.1** — `CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:R/S:C/C:L/I:L/A:N`

An issue was discovered in Roundcube Webmail before 1.5.14 and 1.6.14. XSS exists because of insufficient HTML attachment sanitization in preview mode. A victim must preview a text/html attachment.

**Affected:** composer/roundcube/roundcubemail  
**Attack vector:** `xss`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM03`, `LLM05`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI04`, `ASI05`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-6.1`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0010`, `AML.T0050`
