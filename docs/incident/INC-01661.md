### INC-01661  [↗](/incident/INC-01661.html)

**Portugal Approves Autonomous Vehicle Testing on Public Roads**  
_2026-04-16 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The Portuguese government has approved a decree-law allowing the testing of autonomous vehicles, which use AI-driven systems, on public roads. The new legal framework aims to foster innovation and attract investment, while requiring licensing and safety measures to mitigate potential risks associated with AI-driven mobility.
