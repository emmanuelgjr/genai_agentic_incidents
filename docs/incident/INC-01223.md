**India Develops AI-Enabled Bodyguard Satellites for Space Security**  
_2026-03-04 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

India is developing AI-powered bodyguard satellites equipped with robotic arms and autonomous threat detection to protect its critical space assets from orbital threats. Triggered by a 2024 close encounter with a neighboring country's spacecraft, these satellites are being engineered by private startups, with test launches planned for 2026-2027.
