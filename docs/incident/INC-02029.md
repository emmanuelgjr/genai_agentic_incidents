**US Regulators Probe Tesla FSD After Collisions Linked to AI System Failures**  
_2026-03-19 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The US National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) has escalated its investigation into Tesla's Full Self-Driving (FSD) AI system after multiple collisions, including a fatality, where the system failed to warn drivers of low visibility hazards. The probe covers 3.2 million vehicles and focuses on FSD's detection and warning capabilities.
