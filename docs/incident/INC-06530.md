### INC-06530  [↗](/incident/INC-06530.html)

**Ethical Gaps in AI for Autonomous Vehicles Pose Future Risks**  
_2020-07-06 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Researchers warn that current AI ethical frameworks for self-driving cars fail to address potential malicious misuse, such as using autonomous vehicles for harmful acts. The oversimplified ethical models could lead to future risks if not improved, though no actual incidents or harm have yet occurred.
