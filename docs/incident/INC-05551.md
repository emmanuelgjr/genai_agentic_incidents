**Coded Bias Exposes Real-World Harms of AI Discrimination**  
_2021-04-06 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The documentary 'Coded Bias' highlights how AI systems, including facial recognition and automated hiring tools, have caused real harm by perpetuating racial and gender bias. These technologies have led to discrimination, wrongful police matches, and violations of civil rights, prompting calls for greater oversight and regulation.
