### INC-06660  [↗](/incident/INC-06660.html)

**Google Home Smart Speakers Accidentally Enable Always-On Listening, Raising Privacy Concerns**  
_2020-08-02 · real-world · Severity: High_

A software update accidentally enabled Google Home smart speakers to listen for sounds like smoke alarms and glass breaking without user consent, even when wake words were not used. This unintended AI-driven feature led to privacy violations, as devices recorded and transmitted ambient sounds, prompting user backlash and a swift rollback by Google.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`  

**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-4.1`, `MEASURE-2.10`
