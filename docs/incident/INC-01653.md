### INC-01653  [↗](/incident/INC-01653.html)

**Police Officer Used AI Voice Cloning to Deceive Victims' Family in Triple Homicide Case**  
_2026-04-14 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

In Cachoeirinha, Brazil, police officer Cristiano Domingues Francisco used AI-based voice cloning to create fake audio messages imitating his ex-wife, Silvana Aguiar, to deceive her parents after their disappearance. This AI misuse facilitated the cover-up of multiple homicides and obstructed the investigation.
