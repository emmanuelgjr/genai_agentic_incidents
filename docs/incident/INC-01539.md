### INC-01539  [↗](/incident/INC-01539.html)

**North Korean Hackers Use AI Tools to Steal $12 Million in Cryptocurrency**  
_2026-04-22 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

A North Korean state-sponsored hacking group used AI tools, including ChatGPT, to generate malware, build fake websites, and conduct phishing campaigns. This enabled relatively unskilled hackers to steal approximately $12 million in cryptocurrency from over 2,000 victims, primarily targeting developers in the cryptocurrency and Web3 sectors.

**Attack vector:** `phishing`  

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`
