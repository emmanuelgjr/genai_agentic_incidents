### INC-08113  [↗](/incident/INC-08113.html)

**AI Uncovers Critical Zcash Vulnerability, Triggers Market Turmoil**  
_2026-06-06 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Security researcher Taylor Hornby used Anthropic's Claude Opus 4.8 AI model to discover a critical counterfeiting bug in Zcash, undetected since 2022. The flaw, which could have enabled unlimited counterfeit coins, was quickly patched, but its disclosure caused ZEC's value to drop over 30%, highlighting AI's growing role in blockchain security.
