**US Lawmakers Urge Tighter Controls on AI Chip Manufacturing to Prevent Chinese Acquisition**  
_2026-06-05 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

US lawmakers from both parties have urged stricter regulations on semiconductor foundries like TSMC to prevent Chinese companies from circumventing export controls and acquiring advanced AI chips via overseas subsidiaries or shell companies. The concern centers on potential national security and competitiveness risks if these regulatory loopholes remain unaddressed.
