**Flock Safety License Plate Reader Data Sharing Sparks Privacy and Rights Concerns in California**  
_2026-03-02 · real-world · Severity: High_

Flock Safety's AI-powered license plate readers, used by law enforcement in California, have come under scrutiny after data was shared with federal agencies, including ICE and Border Patrol, without proper oversight. This has led to privacy violations, public backlash, and contract terminations by cities and Amazon's Ring, highlighting risks of AI surveillance misuse.
