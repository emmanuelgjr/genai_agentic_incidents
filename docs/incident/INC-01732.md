### INC-01732  [↗](/incident/INC-01732.html)

**Researchers Bypass Apple Intelligence AI Protections with Prompt Injection**  
_2026-04-09 · real-world · Severity: High_

Security researchers demonstrated that Apple's on-device AI system, Apple Intelligence, could be manipulated using prompt injection and Unicode obfuscation techniques, bypassing safety filters and executing unauthorized commands. The vulnerability, affecting millions of devices, was disclosed to Apple and subsequently patched, highlighting significant AI security risks.

**Attack vector:** `prompt-injection`
