### INC-01262  [↗](/incident/INC-01262.html)

**Itaú and Google Deploy AI to Block Fraudulent Bank Calls in Brazil**  
_2026-05-13 · real-world · Severity: High_

Itaú Unibanco partnered with Google to integrate an AI system into Android phones, automatically detecting and blocking fraudulent bank calls using call spoofing techniques. This initiative aims to prevent financial harm by intercepting scam calls before they reach victims, addressing widespread banking fraud in Brazil.

**Attack vector:** `rce`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`
