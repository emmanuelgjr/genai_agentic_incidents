### INC-07142  [↗](/incident/INC-07142.html)

**YouTube's AI Algorithms Linked to Child Harm and Spread of Misinformation**  
_2020-10-14 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

YouTube's AI-driven recommendation and moderation systems have failed to filter harmful and misleading content, leading to incidents where children suffered injury or death after imitating dangerous videos, and the widespread promotion of fake news and copyright violations. These failures have prompted public outcry and calls for greater accountability from YouTube.

**Attack vector:** `misinformation`
