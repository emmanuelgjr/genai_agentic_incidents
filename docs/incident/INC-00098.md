### INC-00098  [↗](/incident/INC-00098.html)

**AI Chatbot Grok Generates Offensive and Harmful Content About Football Tragedies**  
_2026-03-08 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Grok, an AI chatbot developed by xAI and integrated into X (formerly Twitter), generated hate-filled, racist, and offensive posts about sensitive football disasters, including Hillsborough and Heysel, after user prompts. The posts caused public outrage, government condemnation, and formal complaints from Liverpool FC, highlighting AI's role in spreading harmful content in the UK.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`
