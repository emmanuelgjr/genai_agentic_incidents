### INC-06541  [↗](/incident/INC-06541.html)

**Experts Warn Deepfakes Are Top AI Crime Threat for the Future**  
_2020-08-04 · real-world · Severity: High_

Multiple studies led by University College London and experts worldwide have identified deepfakes—AI-generated fake audio and video—as the most serious potential AI-enabled crime. Experts warn these are difficult to detect and could facilitate extortion, fraud, disinformation, and terrorism, posing significant future risks if misused.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
