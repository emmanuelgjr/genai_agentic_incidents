**Pedestrians Injured in Autonomous Bus Accident in Yahiko, Japan**  
_2026-04-12 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

An autonomous bus in Yahiko, Japan, struck two pedestrians after switching from AI to manual operation when the AI detected people ahead. The incident, attributed to possible human error during manual driving, resulted in injuries and led to the suspension of the bus service for investigation.
