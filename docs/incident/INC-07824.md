### INC-07824  [↗](/incident/INC-07824.html)

**AI-Generated Police Identity Used in Dating App Fraud in Ahmedabad**  
_2026-05-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

A man in Ahmedabad, Himanshu Panchal, used AI-generated photographs and forged documents to impersonate a police officer on dating and matrimonial apps, deceiving at least 21 women across four states. The AI-enabled deception led to financial and emotional harm before his arrest by the Ahmedabad Crime Branch.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`

**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`
