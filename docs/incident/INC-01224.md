**India Develops AI-Enabled Electric Air Taxi with NVIDIA Partnership**  
_2026-02-23 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The ePlane Company, a Chennai-based startup, is developing India's first electric air taxi, the e200x, in partnership with NVIDIA. Using NVIDIA's Omniverse software, they are creating a digital twin to simulate and test AI-powered autonomous flight systems, aiming to ensure safety before real-world deployment. No incidents have occurred yet.
