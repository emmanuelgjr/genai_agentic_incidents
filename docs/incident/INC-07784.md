**Douyin's AI Model Significantly Reduces Rumor Spread**  
_2026-05-28 · real-world · Severity: High_

Douyin deployed an advanced AI large model to detect and manage misinformation on its platform, reducing the average viewership of rumors by 62% and shortening their exposure window. The AI system uses semantic analysis, multi-source verification, and automated removal, directly mitigating the harm caused by viral misinformation in China.
