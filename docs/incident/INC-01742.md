### INC-01742  [↗](/incident/INC-01742.html)

**Romanian Authorities Warn of Viral AI-Generated Fake Video Depicting False Conflict**  
_2026-01-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

Romania's Ministry of Internal Affairs (MAI) warned the public about a viral AI-generated video falsely depicting a conflict between a priest and a gendarme inside a church. The incident never occurred, but the deepfake's spread online risks misinformation and social disruption. Authorities urge citizens to verify information from official sources.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
