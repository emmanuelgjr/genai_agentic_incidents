**Iowa Sues GM Over AI-Driven Vehicle Data Collection and Sale**  
_2026-02-26 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Iowa's Attorney General is suing General Motors for allegedly using AI-enabled telematic systems like OnStar to collect detailed driving data from consumers without consent and selling it to third parties. This data was reportedly used by insurers to raise rates, deny coverage, or cancel policies, violating consumer privacy rights.
