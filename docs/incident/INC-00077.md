### INC-00077  [↗](/incident/INC-00077.html)

**AI Algorithms Used for Content Suppression and Low-Quality Content Cleanup in China**  
_2026-03-07 · real-world · Severity: High_

Chinese authorities removed nearly 40,000 accounts and 708,000 posts to combat AI-generated low-quality content, misinformation, and illegal activity. Simultaneously, coordinated manipulation of platform algorithms by fake accounts suppressed dissenting voices, causing psychological harm and limiting freedom of expression. Both incidents highlight AI's role in online harm and censorship.
