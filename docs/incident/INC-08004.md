**Waymo Robotaxi Malfunctions Raise Safety Concerns in the US**  
_2026-05-31 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Waymo's autonomous vehicles have experienced multiple AI-driven malfunctions, including driving into floodwaters, failing to slow in construction zones, and ending rides prematurely, sometimes endangering passengers. These incidents, mainly in the US, have led to service suspensions and raised questions about the safety and reliability of AI-powered robotaxis.
