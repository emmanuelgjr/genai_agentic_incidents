### INC-06239  [↗](/incident/INC-06239.html)

**AI Proctoring Software Causes Privacy Violations and Racial Bias in Online Exams**  
_2020-12-16 · real-world · Severity: High_

Universities' use of AI-based proctoring software like Proctorio to prevent online exam cheating has led to significant harms, including student privacy violations, racial bias—such as failing to recognize students of color—and emotional distress. These issues have sparked protests, legal complaints, and concerns over student rights.
