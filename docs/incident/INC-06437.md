### INC-06437  [↗](/incident/INC-06437.html)

**China's AI-Driven Mass DNA Collection Enables Unprecedented Surveillance and Rights Violations**  
_2020-06-18 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Chinese government, with assistance from international biotech firms, has used AI-enabled systems to collect and analyze DNA from tens of millions of citizens—often without consent—to build the world’s largest police DNA database. This facilitates mass surveillance, targeting of minorities and dissidents, and widespread human rights violations.
