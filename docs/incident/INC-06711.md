### INC-06711  [↗](/incident/INC-06711.html)

**Indonesia Urged to Develop AI-Enabled Armed Drones Amid Rising Military Tech Risks**  
_2020-01-23 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

President Joko Widodo has called on Indonesia's defense sector to develop and adopt AI-enabled armed drones and autonomous military technologies, citing recent global incidents and the rapid advancement of such systems. While no incident has occurred yet, the move raises concerns about future risks of AI-driven military harm.
