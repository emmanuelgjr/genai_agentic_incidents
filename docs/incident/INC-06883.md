### INC-06883  [↗](/incident/INC-06883.html)

**Robot in Chinese Shopping Mall Fell off the Escalator, Knocking down Passengers**  
_2020-12-25 · real-world · Severity: Medium_
AIID: [`#134`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/134/)

On December 25, 2023, a shopping guide robot operating in Fuzhou Zhongfang Marlboro Mall fell off an escalator and struck two passengers. The incident occurred when the robot autonomously moved toward the escalator without human operation, according to mall management. Following the accident, the robot was suspended from service on December 27. Mall officials confirmed that the incident was not caused by human operation, emphasizing that the robot moved to the escalator on its own. The robot company has committed to conducting an analysis to determine the cause of the accident. The two passengers who were struck by the falling robot were reported to have sustained no serious injuries. The incident highlights potential safety risks associated with autonomous robotic systems operating in public spaces with human foot traffic.
