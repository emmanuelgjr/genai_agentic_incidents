### INC-01269  [↗](/incident/INC-01269.html)

**AI-Powered CT Diagnostics Aid COVID-19 Detection and Mitigate False Negatives in China**  
_2020-02-07 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI systems, including those from Huawei Cloud and startups like 推想, were rapidly deployed in Chinese hospitals to analyze CT scans for COVID-19. These AI tools improved diagnostic speed and accuracy, helped identify cases missed by nucleic acid tests, and reduced doctors' workload, directly contributing to better patient outcomes during the pandemic.
