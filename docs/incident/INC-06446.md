**Chinese Brain-Control AI Technologies Cause Harm to Citizens**  
_2020-09-10 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Chinese authorities have developed AI-enabled brain control technologies, reportedly used by military and research institutions to manipulate emotions and behavior. Victims claim to suffer continuous psychological harassment and physical symptoms, indicating direct harm and rights violations from unauthorized use of these AI systems.
