### INC-04329  [↗](/incident/INC-04329.html)

**Uruguayan TV Program Santo y Seña Uses a Deepfake of Political Candidate Yamandú Orsi Without His Consent**  
_2024-10-13 · real-world · Severity: Medium_
AIID: [`#828`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/828/)

Uruguayan TV program Santo y Seña, hosted by Ignacio Álvarez, used AI to create a virtual representation of political candidate Yamandú Orsi, who declined an appearance. Nevertheless, without Orsi&#x27;s permission, an AI-generated &quot;Orsi&quot; was shown alongside Andrés Ojeda, a rival candidate who appeared in person on the program.
