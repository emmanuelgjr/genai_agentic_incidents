**Anthropic's Mythos AI Raises Cybersecurity and Governance Concerns in US and UK**  
_2026-04-18 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Anthropic's advanced AI model, Mythos, has sparked significant concern due to its powerful cybersecurity capabilities, which could be misused for large-scale cyberattacks. Despite Pentagon bans, US and UK intelligence agencies have accessed Mythos, highlighting risks of misuse and governance challenges, though no actual harm has yet occurred.
