### INC-04396  [↗](/incident/INC-04396.html)

**AI voice impersonation scams Canadian couple of USD 21,000**  
_2023 · real-world · Severity: High_

A Canadian couple were defrauded of approximately USD 21,000 after scammers used AI voice cloning to impersonate their adult son in a distress call demanding bail money.

**Affected:** Canadian retail victims  
**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM03`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI02`, `ASI03`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `GOVERN-6.1`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.10`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0010`, `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0048`, `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0053`, `AML.T0057`, `AML.T0058`
