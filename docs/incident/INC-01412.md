### INC-01412  [↗](/incident/INC-01412.html)

**Meta Sued Over AI-Enhanced Fraudulent Ads on Facebook and Instagram**  
_2026-04-22 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Meta faces lawsuits from U.S. consumer protection groups alleging it failed to protect users from AI-generated fraudulent ads on Facebook and Instagram. The complaints claim Meta profited from misleading ads that use AI to appear credible, resulting in consumer harm. Meta denies wrongdoing, citing efforts to combat fraud.
