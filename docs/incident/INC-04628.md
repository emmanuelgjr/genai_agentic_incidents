### INC-04628  [↗](/incident/INC-04628.html)

**Drake / The Weeknd AI voice-cloning 'Heart on My Sleeve'**  
_2023-04 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The viral track 'Heart on My Sleeve' used AI-cloned voices of Drake and The Weeknd, prompting Universal Music Group takedowns across streaming platforms and exposing major IP and impersonation risk for voice models.

**Affected:** Drake, The Weeknd, UMG  
**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.1`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048`, `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
