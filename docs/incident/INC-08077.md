### INC-08077  [↗](/incident/INC-08077.html)

**Researchers Demonstrate Adaptive AI-Powered Computer Worm Threat**  
_2026-06-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

Researchers from the University of Toronto, Vector Institute, and University of Cambridge have developed a proof-of-concept AI-driven computer worm using large language models. The worm autonomously adapts its attack strategies, posing a significant cybersecurity risk if deployed, though tests were limited to controlled environments with no real-world harm reported.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM05`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI02`, `ASI03`, `ASI05`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.10`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0053`, `AML.T0057`
