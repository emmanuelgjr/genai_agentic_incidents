### INC-05625  [↗](/incident/INC-05625.html)

**Facebook's AI Moderation Fails to Curb Misinformation in Europe**  
_2021-04-20 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Facebook's AI-driven content moderation systems have been ineffective at removing COVID-19 and climate misinformation in non-English European languages, leaving harmful falsehoods widely accessible. This disparity has undermined public health and climate action, as AI systems failed to flag or remove misleading content, causing harm to communities and public trust.
