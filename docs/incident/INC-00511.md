### INC-00511  [↗](/incident/INC-00511.html)

**AI-Generated Videos Simulate Violence Against PT Women, Prompt Legal Action in Brazil**  
_2026-04-11 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

AI-generated videos simulating aggression and 'exorcism' against women affiliated with Brazil's PT party circulated on social media, inciting political and religious intolerance. The PT filed legal actions with the Electoral Court to remove the content and identify those responsible, citing grave harm and violation of rights.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`
