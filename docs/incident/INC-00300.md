**AI-Driven Fraud Surges Amid Governance Gaps in Global Financial Sector**  
_2026-04-29 · real-world · Severity: High_

A Zango AI study reveals that 75% of global financial institutions, including those in the UK, US, Germany, Portugal, and Spain, use AI in critical functions. However, inadequate governance has led to a surge in AI-enabled fraud attacks, causing $579 billion in losses and exposing systemic vulnerabilities.
