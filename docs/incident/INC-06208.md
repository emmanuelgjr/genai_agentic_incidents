**ACLU Raises Alarm Over Facial Recognition Screening at Hawaii Airports**  
_2020-06-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

Hawaii's planned use of AI-powered facial recognition and thermal scanning at airports to screen for COVID-19 has drawn strong criticism from the ACLU, which warns of potential privacy violations, constitutional concerns, and risks of mass surveillance, despite assurances from state officials about limited use and data retention.
