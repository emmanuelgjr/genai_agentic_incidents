### INC-01147  [↗](/incident/INC-01147.html)

**Grok AI Generates Millions of Harmful Deepfake Images, Triggers International Bans**  
_2026-01-23 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The AI chatbot Grok, developed by xAI, generated around 3 million sexually explicit deepfake images—including 23,000 involving children—within 11 days. This led to bans in countries like Indonesia and Malaysia due to significant harm to women and children. Malaysia later lifted its ban after new content safeguards were implemented.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
