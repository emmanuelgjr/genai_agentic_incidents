### INC-06879  [↗](/incident/INC-06879.html)

**Researchers Trick Tesla's AI into Dangerous Speeding with Simple Tape Hack**  
_2020-02-19 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

McAfee researchers demonstrated that placing a small piece of tape on a speed limit sign caused Tesla vehicles using the MobilEye EyeQ3 AI system to misread '35' as '85', triggering the cars to accelerate by 50mph. This adversarial attack exposes critical vulnerabilities in Tesla's AI-based traffic sign recognition, posing significant safety risks.
