### INC-03066  [↗](/incident/INC-03066.html)

**Purported AI-Generated Video Reportedly Used in RM5,800 (~$1,400) Sextortion Attempt Targeting Malaysian Minor via Telegram**  
_2025-10-17 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1241`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1241/)

A Malaysian family reported that scammers used purported AI-generated explicit video content to target their teenage son in a sextortion attempt. The perpetrators allegedly contacted the boy via Telegram, sending manipulated footage that depicted him in compromising situations and demanding RM5,800 (~$1,400) to prevent its release. The family alerted authorities and warned others about the growing use of generative AI in targeting minors.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
