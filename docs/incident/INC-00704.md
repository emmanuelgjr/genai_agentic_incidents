### INC-00704  [↗](/incident/INC-00704.html)

**Brazil Investigates TikTok AI Algorithms Over Viral Trend Inciting Violence Against Women**  
_2026-03-10 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Brazil's Ministry of Justice demanded explanations from TikTok regarding the spread of misogynistic videos linked to the "caso ela diga não" trend, which incited violence against women. Authorities questioned the effectiveness of TikTok's AI-driven moderation and recommendation systems after the harmful content went viral, prompting a federal investigation.
