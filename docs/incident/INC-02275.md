### INC-02275  [↗](/incident/INC-02275.html)

**Alleged Deepfake Videos Impersonate Lithuanian Politicians and Doctors in Purported Cross-Border Scam Network**  
_2025-06-28 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1119`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1119/)

An alleged series of high-quality AI-generated deepfake videos reportedly mimicked Lithuanian TV segments, politicians, and doctors to promote fraudulent health products and anti-vaccine disinformation. Distributed mainly via Facebook, the videos reportedly reached audiences in up to 15 EU countries. Fact-checkers and cybersecurity experts reportedly described the campaign as part of a coordinated scam network designed to extract money by impersonating trusted public figures and media outlets.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
