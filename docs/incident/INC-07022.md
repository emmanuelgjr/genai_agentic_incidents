**Turkish Company Develops AI-Enabled Unmanned Helicopter for Civil and Military Use**  
_2020-12-19 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Turkish technology company Titra has developed an AI-enabled unmanned helicopter capable of autonomous flight and heavy payload delivery for both civilian logistics and military operations. While no harm has occurred, the system's autonomous and military applications present plausible future risks associated with AI-driven aerial vehicles.
