**AI-Generated Pornography and Illegal Content Distribution Chain Exposed in China**  
_2026-04-12 · real-world · Severity: High_

Multiple investigations reveal a widespread illegal industry in China using AI to generate and distribute pornographic content, including deepfake videos and explicit chat software. Tutorials and tools are openly sold online, enabling mass production and evasion of regulation, causing harm to individuals and exposing minors to inappropriate material.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
