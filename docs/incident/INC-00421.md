### INC-00421  [↗](/incident/INC-00421.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfakes Used in Celebrity Scam Ads in France**  
_2026-05-04 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI-generated deepfake videos and images of French celebrities, including Émilien from "Les 12 Coups de midi," have been widely circulated on social media to promote fraudulent financial schemes. These unauthorized deepfakes have misled victims, resulting in financial losses and reputational harm, prompting public warnings from those targeted.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
