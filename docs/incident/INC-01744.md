**Romanian Company Unveils AI-Powered Autonomous Cruise Missile**  
_2026-02-17 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Romanian tech firm OVES Enterprise has developed and presented the Sahara Autonomous System, the country's first privately built cruise missile featuring integrated AI (Nemesis AI) for autonomous navigation, target identification, and mission execution. The system's autonomous capabilities raise significant concerns about potential future harm if deployed in military operations.
