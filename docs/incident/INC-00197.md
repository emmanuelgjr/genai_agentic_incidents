### INC-00197  [↗](/incident/INC-00197.html)

**AI Raises Concerns Over Algorithmic Collusion and Market Entry Barriers in India**  
_2026-03-16 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

NITI Aayog member Rajiv Gauba warned that artificial intelligence could introduce new competition risks, such as algorithmic collusion and AI-driven entry barriers, potentially allowing incumbents to foreclose rivals. He emphasized the need for evolving regulatory tools to address these challenges in India.
