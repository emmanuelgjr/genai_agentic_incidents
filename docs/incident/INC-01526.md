**New Zealand Defence Force to Trial AI-Enabled Combat Drones**  
_2026-02-25 · real-world · Severity: High_

The New Zealand Defence Force will trial advanced AI-enabled uncrewed drones and vehicles from Syos Aerospace, previously used in the Ukraine war. The initiative aims to modernize military capability and support local industry, but raises potential future risks associated with autonomous military systems.
