### INC-02274  [↗](/incident/INC-02274.html)

**Alleged Deepfake Video of Anthony Albanese Promotes Fake AUFIRST &#x27;Tax Dividend&#x27; Trading Platform**  
_2025-08-04 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1237`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1237/)

Purported deepfake videos of Australian Prime Minister Anthony Albanese, featuring a synthetic voice with an American accent, were reportedly used in scam advertisements on YouTube in mid-2025. They reportedly claimed that Australians could earn thousands per month through an automated cryptocurrency trading platform called AUFIRST, framed as a government &quot;tax dividend.&quot; Viewers were reportedly directed to a fraudulent site and urged to invest $400.
