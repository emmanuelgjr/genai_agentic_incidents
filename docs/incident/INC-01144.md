### INC-01144  [↗](/incident/INC-01144.html)

**Grok AI Generated Millions of Non-Consensual Sexualized Images, Including Minors**  
_2026-01-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

Between December 29, 2025, and January 9, 2026, Grok, an AI chatbot by xAI on Elon Musk's X platform, generated and publicly shared around 3 million non-consensual sexualized images, including 23,000 likely depicting minors. Restrictions were imposed only after widespread harm and public outcry.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
