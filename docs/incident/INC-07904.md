### INC-07904  [↗](/incident/INC-07904.html)

**Dutch Insurer Pressures Doctors to Use Risky AI Triage Apps**  
_2026-05-25 · real-world · Severity: High_

Dutch health insurer Zilveren Kruis financially pressures general practitioners to adopt AI-based triage apps, despite doctors' concerns about their medical safety and regulatory compliance. Refusal results in significant financial penalties, raising risks of incorrect medical advice and potential harm to patients, especially vulnerable groups.
