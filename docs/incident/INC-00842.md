### INC-00842  [↗](/incident/INC-00842.html)

**Colombian President Shares AI-Generated Deepfake Video Targeting Ecuadorian Leader**  
_2026-04-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

Colombian President Gustavo Petro shared an AI-generated deepfake video on social media falsely implicating Ecuadorian President Daniel Noboa in criminal activity. The video, styled as a Noticias Telemundo report, was quickly debunked by the network, highlighting the dangers of AI-driven misinformation and sparking diplomatic tensions between Colombia and Ecuador.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
