### INC-05677  [↗](/incident/INC-05677.html)

**Google Translate AI Reinforces Sexist Stereotypes in Pronoun Selection**  
_2021-03-09 · real-world · Severity: High_

Google Translate's AI has come under criticism for translating the gender-neutral Finnish pronoun 'hän' into gendered English pronouns based on stereotypical roles, such as associating leadership with men and domestic tasks with women. This bias has sparked public outcry over the reinforcement of harmful gender stereotypes.
