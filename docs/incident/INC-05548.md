### INC-05548  [↗](/incident/INC-05548.html)

**Clearview AI's Facial Recognition System Violates Canadian Privacy Laws**  
_2021-01-31 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Canadian privacy authorities found that Clearview AI's facial recognition technology illegally collected and used billions of images, including those of Canadians and children, without consent. The mass surveillance enabled by this AI system violated federal and provincial privacy laws, prompting calls for enforcement and regulatory action against the company.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`
