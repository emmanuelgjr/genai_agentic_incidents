### INC-12020  [↗](/incident/INC-12020.html)

**XML injection in Crafter CMS**  
_2022-05 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: High_

CVEs: `CVE-2017-15680`, `CVE-2017-15683`, `CVE-2017-15685`
CWEs: `CWE-862`, `CWE-91`
CVSS: **8.6** — `CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:C/C:H/I:N/A:N`
