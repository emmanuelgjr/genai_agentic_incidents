### INC-06396  [↗](/incident/INC-06396.html)

**Beijing Court Rules TikTok/Douyin Infringed User Personal Information Rights**  
_2020-07-30 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Beijing Internet Court ruled that TikTok/Douyin and WeChat Reading infringed users' personal information rights by using AI-driven features to recommend contacts and share data without proper consent. The court found unauthorized data collection and processing, constituting a legal violation, though not an invasion of privacy. Both companies responded to the ruling.
