### INC-00110  [↗](/incident/INC-00110.html)

**AI Chatbots Generate Defamatory Content, Prompting Lawsuits**  
_2026-02-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

Generative AI systems like ChatGPT and Meta AI chatbots produced false and defamatory statements about individuals, including public figures in Australia and the USA. These outputs caused reputational harm and led to defamation lawsuits against AI companies, highlighting the risks of AI-driven misinformation and the legal challenges it creates.

**Attack vector:** `misinformation`
