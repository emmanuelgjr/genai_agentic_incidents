**South Korea presidential election candidate AI deepfakes**  
_2022-03 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Presidential candidates' campaigns in South Korea deployed AI-generated 'AI Yoon' and similar deepfake avatars that introduced real risk of voter deception and set early precedent for synthetic-media use in elections.
