### INC-07133  [↗](/incident/INC-07133.html)

**Xiaomi Accused of AI-Driven User Data Tracking and Privacy Violations**  
_2020-05-01 · real-world · Severity: High_

Security researchers found that Xiaomi smartphones and browsers collect and transmit extensive user data—including browsing history and device identifiers—even in incognito modes, without user consent. The data, processed by AI-enabled systems, is sent to remote servers, raising serious privacy concerns for millions of users. Xiaomi denies wrongdoing.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`  

**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-4.1`, `MEASURE-2.10`
