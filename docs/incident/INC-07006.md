**Toronto Smart City Proposal Faces Ongoing AI-Driven Privacy Concerns**  
_2020-02-26 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Alphabet subsidiary Sidewalk Labs' Toronto smart city project continues to face scrutiny from Waterfront Toronto's advisory panel over its AI-enabled data-gathering technologies. Experts remain concerned about the justification, privacy risks, and potential negative impacts of extensive digital surveillance, despite revised proposals and scaled-back plans.
