### INC-12005  [↗](/incident/INC-12005.html)

**Woocommerce Cross-site Scripting via Additional tax classes field when taxes are enabled**  
_2022-05 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: Medium_

CVEs: `CVE-2021-24323`
CWEs: `CWE-79`
CVSS: **4.8** — `CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:H/UI:R/S:C/C:L/I:L/A:N`

When taxes are enabled, the "Additional tax classes" field was not properly sanitised or escaped before being output back in the admin dashboard, allowing high privilege users such as admin to use XSS payloads even when the unfiltered_html is disabled

**Affected:** composer/woocommerce/woocommerce  
**Attack vector:** `xss`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM03`, `LLM05`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI04`, `ASI05`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-6.1`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0010`, `AML.T0050`
