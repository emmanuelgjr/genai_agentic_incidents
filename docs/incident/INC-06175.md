### INC-06175  [↗](/incident/INC-06175.html)

**Viral Tom Cruise Deepfakes on TikTok Raise Misinformation Concerns**  
_2021-02-26 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI-generated deepfake videos of Tom Cruise, created by the TikTok account @deeptomcruise, have gone viral due to their realism, sparking concerns about the technology's potential to mislead the public and erode trust. While no direct harm has occurred yet, the incident highlights significant risks of misinformation and impersonation.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`
