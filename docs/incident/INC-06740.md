**Japan Launches Large-Scale Autonomous Ship Trials, Raising Future AI Safety Concerns**  
_2020-06-12 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Japanese organizations, including Mitsubishi Shipbuilding and the Nippon Foundation, are conducting large-scale trials of AI-powered unmanned ships and amphibious buses. While no harm has occurred, these experiments highlight potential future risks associated with autonomous navigation, such as operational failures or accidents, if AI systems malfunction.
