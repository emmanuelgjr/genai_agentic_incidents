**Startup Offers Free Home Cleaning in Exchange for AI Training Data, Raising Privacy Concerns**  
_2026-05-29 · real-world · Severity: High_

Shift, a startup in New York City, offers free home cleaning services in exchange for recording the process with head-mounted cameras to collect data for training AI and household robots. While personal data is reportedly anonymized, the practice raises concerns about privacy and potential future misuse of sensitive video data.
