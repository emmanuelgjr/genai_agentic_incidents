**UN Condemns US Drone Strike Killing Iranian General Soleimani as Unlawful Use of AI Weapons**  
_2020-07-09 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

UN Special Rapporteur Agnes Callamard condemned the US drone strike that killed Iranian General Qassem Soleimani in January 2020, labeling it an unlawful, extrajudicial killing. The incident highlights the dangers and legal violations associated with the unregulated use of AI-enabled armed drones in targeted killings.
