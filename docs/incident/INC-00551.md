**AI-Powered Phishing Kits Like Bluekit Drive Surge in Sophisticated Attacks**  
_2026-04-29 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Researchers have identified Bluekit, an AI-driven phishing kit that automates and enhances phishing attacks, including bypassing 2FA and mimicking over 40 brands. Reports show 86% of recent phishing campaigns now use AI, increasing the scale and effectiveness of credential theft and financial fraud globally.
