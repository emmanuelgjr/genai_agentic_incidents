**Concerns Over Facial Recognition Rollouts in Singapore and U.S. Schools**  
_2020-03-07 · real-world · Severity: High_

Singapore plans to replace ID cards with a nationwide facial recognition system, raising privacy and surveillance concerns due to mass biometric data collection. In the U.S., officials warn that unregulated deployment of facial recognition in schools could risk privacy violations and misuse, urging caution and further research before widespread adoption.
