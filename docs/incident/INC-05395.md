**AI Telemarketing Robots Cause Widespread Harassment and Privacy Violations in China**  
_2021-05-22 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

AI-powered telemarketing robots, capable of making thousands of calls daily and mimicking human speech, have led to widespread harassment and privacy violations in China. Individuals report repeated, irrelevant sales calls, highlighting the harm caused by automated systems that exploit personal data and disrupt daily life.
