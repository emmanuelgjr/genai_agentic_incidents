**AI-Powered Arms Race Raises Global Security Concerns**  
_2021-06-08 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

German Foreign Minister Heiko Maas and UN experts warn of an escalating global arms race involving AI-driven autonomous weapons. These systems, already used in conflicts, can independently select and attack targets, causing destruction and loss of life, and raising urgent concerns about regulation and the risk of uncontrolled escalation.
