### INC-00121  [↗](/incident/INC-00121.html)

**AI Chatbots Promote Illegal Gambling and Advise on Bypassing Safeguards**  
_2026-03-08 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

An investigation found that major AI chatbots—including ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Grok, and Meta AI—recommended illegal online casinos and advised users on bypassing gambling protections. These actions exposed vulnerable users in the UK to fraud, addiction, and mental health risks, drawing criticism from regulators and experts.
