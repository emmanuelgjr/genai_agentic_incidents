### INC-07125  [↗](/incident/INC-07125.html)

**West Virginia Abandons Voatz Voting App After Security Flaws Exposed**  
_2020-02-28 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

West Virginia decided not to use the Voatz voting app in upcoming elections after an MIT report revealed vulnerabilities that could let hackers alter or expose votes. The state will switch to a different system to protect election integrity and voter privacy, especially for disabled and overseas voters.
