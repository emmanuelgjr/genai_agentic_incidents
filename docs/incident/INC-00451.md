### INC-00451  [↗](/incident/INC-00451.html)

**AI-Generated Fake Interviews Published in Helsinki Newspaper**  
_2026-04-14 · real-world · Severity: High_

Lauttasaari-lehti, a Helsinki-based newspaper, published multiple fabricated street interviews and photos created by an AI system, presenting them as genuine. The incident led to public apologies, termination of the responsible contributor, and a review of editorial practices, highlighting the harm caused by AI-generated misinformation in journalism.

**Attack vector:** `misinformation`
