### INC-09994  [↗](/incident/INC-09994.html)

**memos vulnerability allows arbitrarily modification or deletion registered identity providers**  
_2025-12 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: Medium_

CVEs: `CVE-2025-65797`, `CVE-2025-65798`
CWEs: `CWE-284`
CVSS: **6.5** — `CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:L/UI:N/S:U/C:N/I:N/A:H`

Incorrect access control in the Identity Provider service of usememos memos v0.25.2 allows attackers with low-level privileges to arbitrarily modify or delete registered identity providers, leading to an account takeover or Denial of Service (DoS).

**Affected:** go/github.com/usememos/memos  
**Attack vector:** `auth-bypass`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM03`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`, `ASI04`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `GOVERN-6.1`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0010`, `AML.T0012`
