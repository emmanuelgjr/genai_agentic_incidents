**AI Virtual Digital Human Technology Trade Secret Infringement Case in Guangzhou**  
_2026-04-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

A Guangzhou court ruled that seven defendants, including former employees and a competing company, illegally obtained and used the source code and technical secrets of an AI-based 'virtual digital human' system, causing significant economic harm and market disruption. The court ordered punitive damages of 4.95 million yuan and immediate cessation of infringement.
