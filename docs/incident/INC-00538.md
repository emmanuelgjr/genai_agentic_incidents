**AI-Powered Cyberattacks Threaten Global Financial Stability**  
_2026-05-07 · real-world · Severity: High_

The IMF and cybersecurity experts warn that advanced AI models, such as Anthropic's Mythos, can autonomously discover and exploit software vulnerabilities, enabling high-frequency, complex cyberattacks on financial systems. These AI-driven threats have already heightened risks to critical infrastructure, prompting regulatory responses and industry concern over potential systemic financial instability.
