### INC-05558  [↗](/incident/INC-05558.html)

**Court Rules Snapchat Can Be Sued Over Speed Filter Linked to Fatal Crash**  
_2021-05-04 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The Ninth Circuit Court of Appeals ruled that Snap Inc. can be sued for the design of Snapchat's AI-powered speed filter, which allegedly encouraged reckless driving and led to a fatal crash killing three young people. The court rejected Section 230 immunity, holding Snap potentially liable for harm caused by the AI feature.
