### INC-05463  [↗](/incident/INC-05463.html)

**Amazon Ring Enables Widespread Warrantless Police Surveillance via AI-Driven Home Cameras**  
_2021-05-16 · real-world · Severity: High_

Amazon's Ring doorbell system, using AI for video processing, has enabled over 1,800 US police departments to access footage from millions of private cameras without warrants. This expansion of law enforcement surveillance into private spaces raises significant privacy and civil liberties concerns, constituting a realized harm to fundamental rights.
