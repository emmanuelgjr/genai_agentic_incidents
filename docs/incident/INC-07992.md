### INC-07992  [↗](/incident/INC-07992.html)

**Vandalism of Sidewalk Assessment Robots in Hollister, California**  
_2026-05-30 · real-world · Severity: High_

In Hollister, California, two Daxbot AI robots deployed to autonomously assess sidewalk safety and accessibility were vandalized by individuals who kicked, struck, and poured beer on them. The incidents, captured by the robots' cameras, led to police investigations and arrests for unrelated crimes.
