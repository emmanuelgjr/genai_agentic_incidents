### INC-07992  [↗](/incident/INC-07992.html)

**AI-Driven Online Ads Raise Privacy and Transparency Concerns**  
_2026-05-30 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Major tech companies like Google, Meta, and ByteDance are rapidly expanding AI-driven advertising systems that personalize and generate ads, making them harder to distinguish and raising concerns about privacy, profiling, and regulatory compliance. Studies show AI can infer sensitive personal information from ad exposure, posing significant privacy risks.
