### INC-06234  [↗](/incident/INC-06234.html)

**AI Moderation on Social Media Censors Anti-Fatphobia Magazine Cover**  
_2020-02-06 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

AI-driven content moderation algorithms on Facebook and Instagram censored Télérama's cover featuring activist Leslie Barbara Butch, intended to raise awareness about fatphobia. The non-explicit image led to account suspensions and restricted visibility, sparking criticism for silencing marginalized voices and violating rights to expression and inclusion.
