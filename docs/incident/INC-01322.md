### INC-01322  [↗](/incident/INC-01322.html)

**Legislative Action Against AI-Driven Surveillance Pricing in Grocery Stores**  
_2026-03-16 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

New York and New Jersey lawmakers are advancing bills to ban the use of AI algorithms for surveillance pricing in grocery stores, which set individualized prices based on personal data. The practice has led to discriminatory pricing, disproportionately impacting vulnerable populations and prompting regulatory scrutiny and consumer protection efforts.
