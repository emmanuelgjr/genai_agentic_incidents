**Civil Rights Groups Challenge Harmful Use of Clearview AI Facial Recognition by US Agencies**  
_2021-04-16 · real-world · Severity: High_

The ACLU and over 70 organizations are pressuring US agencies, including ICE, CBP, and DHS, to halt the use of Clearview AI's facial recognition system. The AI has led to wrongful arrests, privacy violations, and human rights concerns, particularly affecting immigrants and marginalized communities, prompting lawsuits and calls for transparency and oversight.
