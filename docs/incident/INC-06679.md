**Guangzhou Expands Autonomous Vehicle Testing with Driverless Road Trials**  
_2020-08-07 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Guangzhou authorities are expanding pilot zones for intelligent connected vehicles, including permitting road tests of fully driverless cars in Nansha District. While no incidents have occurred, these AI-driven vehicle tests on public roads present plausible future risks of harm if malfunctions arise, highlighting the need for strict oversight and safety measures.
