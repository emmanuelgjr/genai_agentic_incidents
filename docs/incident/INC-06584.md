**Facebook's AI Moderation Fails to Stop Hate Speech in Myanmar Election**  
_2020-11-05 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Facebook's AI-driven content moderation struggled to curb hate speech and misinformation targeting Muslim candidate Sithu Maung during Myanmar's election. Despite AI tools for detection, false and racist content spread widely, raising concerns about AI's role in amplifying ethnic and religious tensions and undermining democratic processes.
