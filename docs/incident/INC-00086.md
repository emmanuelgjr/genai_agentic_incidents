**AI Beauty Filter Malfunction Causes Influencer to Lose 140,000 Followers**  
_2026-02-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

A Chinese influencer lost approximately 140,000 followers after an AI-powered beauty filter malfunctioned during a live stream, briefly revealing her real appearance. The incident sparked widespread debate about digital authenticity and beauty standards, highlighting the reputational harm caused by AI system failures in social media contexts.
