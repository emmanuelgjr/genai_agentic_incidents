**Robot Malfunction at Chinese University Event Leads to Unintended Physical Contact**  
_2026-04-24 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

During a university sports event in Xi'an, China, a humanoid robot malfunctioned due to signal interference, unexpectedly hugging a female student during a dance performance. The incident, attributed to program errors from drone signal interference, raised safety concerns about AI systems in public settings, though no injuries occurred.
