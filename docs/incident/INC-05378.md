**AI Bias Leads to Racial Discrimination and Wrongful Arrests in the US**  
_2021-05-09 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI systems, including facial recognition and automated gender recognition, have caused harm to people of color and marginalized groups in the US. Incidents include wrongful arrests, discrimination, and exclusion from services due to algorithmic bias, prompting lawsuits and calls for bans on such technologies.
