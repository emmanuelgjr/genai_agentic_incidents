**Chinese Underwater AI Drone Breaches Indonesian Waters, Sparks Security Concerns**  
_2021-01-01 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

An autonomous underwater drone, suspected to be Chinese and equipped with advanced sensors, was discovered in Indonesian waters by a local fisherman. The incident triggered national security concerns, diplomatic protests, and calls for Indonesia to strengthen its AI-driven defense and surveillance capabilities to prevent future territorial breaches.
