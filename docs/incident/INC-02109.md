### INC-02109  [↗](/incident/INC-02109.html)

**YouTube AI Recommendation System Outage Disrupts Global Service**  
_2026-02-18 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A malfunction in YouTube's AI-powered recommendation system caused a global outage, preventing videos from displaying across YouTube's platforms, including YouTube.com, YouTube Music, and YouTube Kids. The disruption affected hundreds of thousands of users worldwide, impairing access to information and services before being resolved within about an hour.
