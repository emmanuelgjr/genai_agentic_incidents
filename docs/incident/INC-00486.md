### INC-00486  [↗](/incident/INC-00486.html)

**AI-Generated Misinformation Campaigns Harm Chinese Companies**  
_2026-04-13 · real-world · Severity: High_

In China, criminal groups used AI tools to mass-produce and distribute defamatory articles targeting companies like Xiaomi, Li Auto, and Huawei. These AI-generated 'black articles' caused significant reputational and economic harm. Police shut down over 8,000 accounts, exposing the industrial-scale misuse of AI for malicious misinformation.
