**AI-Driven Drones Cause Major Losses in Ukraine Conflict**  
_2026-03-10 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI-enabled autonomous drones have become the primary cause of military losses in the ongoing Ukraine war, marking a significant shift in warfare. Former Finnish Defence Forces research director Jyri Kosola highlights the direct harm caused by these AI systems and warns of future risks from fully autonomous drones.
