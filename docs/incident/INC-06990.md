### INC-06990  [↗](/incident/INC-06990.html)

**TikTok and WeChat AI Algorithms Enable Global Censorship and Fail to Prevent Harmful Content Spread**  
_2020-09-04 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

AI-powered content moderation algorithms on TikTok and WeChat have been used to censor political, minority, and protest-related content globally, violating freedom of expression and human rights. Additionally, TikTok's AI failed to prevent the spread of a graphic suicide video, exposing users to traumatic content and raising concerns over its content moderation capabilities.
