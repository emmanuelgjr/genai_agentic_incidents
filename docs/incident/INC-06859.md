### INC-06859  [↗](/incident/INC-06859.html)

**Privacy Concerns Raised Over India's Aarogya Setu COVID-19 App**  
_2020-05-02 · real-world · Severity: High_

Congress leader Rahul Gandhi criticized the Aarogya Setu COVID-19 contact tracing app, alleging it poses risks to user privacy and data security due to lack of oversight and outsourcing to private operators. The government denied these claims, asserting the app is secure and essential for pandemic management.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`
