**Automators AI online-sales coaching FTC fraud case**  
_2023-08 · real-world · Severity: High_

The US Federal Trade Commission's first AI-marketing fraud action targeted Automators LLC, which pitched 'AI-powered' Amazon storefronts and Walmart dropshipping coaching that allegedly delivered no working AI and cost consumers more than USD 22 million in losses.

**Affected:** US consumers  
**Attack vector:** `fraud`
