**Automated License Plate Readers Cause Wrongful Stops and Privacy Harms in the U.S.**  
_2026-05-16 · real-world · Severity: High_

Automated license plate reader (ALPR) systems, using AI for vehicle tracking, have led to wrongful police stops and harassment in Colorado, violating individuals' rights. Nationwide, concerns grow over mass surveillance, privacy violations, and potential misuse as agencies like the FBI seek expanded access to ALPR data without sufficient oversight or regulation.
