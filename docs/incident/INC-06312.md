**AI-Powered Pandemic Drones Raise Privacy and Misidentification Concerns**  
_2020-03-26 · real-world · Severity: High_

Draganfly, in partnership with the University of South Australia, is developing AI-enabled drones to detect COVID-19 symptoms in crowds using computer vision and sensors. While intended for public health monitoring, the technology poses plausible risks of privacy violations and misidentification, though no actual harm has been reported yet.
