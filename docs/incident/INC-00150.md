### INC-00150  [↗](/incident/INC-00150.html)

**AI Deepfake Technology Enables Identity Theft via Leaked ID Photos in China**  
_2026-04-27 · real-world · Severity: High_

Multiple reports from China highlight the misuse of AI face-swapping technology by criminals who obtain unprotected ID card photos. These AI-generated deepfake videos bypass facial recognition systems, enabling identity theft, fraudulent account registrations, and financial scams, causing legal and credit harm to victims. Authorities urge enhanced protective measures for ID photos.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`

**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
