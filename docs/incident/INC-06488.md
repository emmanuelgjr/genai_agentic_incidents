**Cruise Receives Approval to Test Fully Driverless Cars in San Francisco**  
_2020-10-15 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

General Motors' Cruise has received permits from California regulators to test autonomous vehicles without human backup drivers on San Francisco streets. While no incidents have occurred, this marks a significant step in AI deployment, raising plausible future risks of harm from potential AI system malfunctions in complex urban environments.
