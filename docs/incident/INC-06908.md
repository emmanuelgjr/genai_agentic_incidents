### INC-06908  [↗](/incident/INC-06908.html)

**Security Risks of Chinese AI-Enabled Drones in Australia Raise National Concerns**  
_2020-09-20 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Millions of Chinese-made drones, including DJI models with AI capabilities, are widely used in Australia. Experts and government reports warn these drones have software vulnerabilities that could be exploited by hackers for espionage or attacks, posing significant national security and privacy risks, though no actual harm has yet occurred.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MEASURE-2.10`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0057`
