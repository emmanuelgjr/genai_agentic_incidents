### INC-08135  [↗](/incident/INC-08135.html)

**AI-Assisted Copyright Infringement in Vietnamese Fashion Industry**  
_2026-06-07 · real-world · Severity: High_

Hoàng Thị Bích Ngọc, owner of the 'Vải áo dài Đất Lành' brand in Ho Chi Minh City, was prosecuted for using AI tools to illegally copy and modify copyrighted áo dài designs by Đỗ Trịnh Hoài Nam. The AI-enabled infringement led to unauthorized commercial production and legal action for violating intellectual property rights.
