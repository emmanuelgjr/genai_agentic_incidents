### INC-07894  [↗](/incident/INC-07894.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfake Images of Minors Lead to Investigation in Cáceres**  
_2026-05-27 · real-world · Severity: High_

A man in Cáceres, Spain, is under investigation for creating and distributing sexualized deepfake images of minors using AI applications. The images, generated from photos taken from social media, resulted in multiple complaints and legal action due to violations of minors' rights and psychological harm.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
