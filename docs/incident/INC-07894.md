### INC-07894  [↗](/incident/INC-07894.html)

**AI-Generated Starbucks Ad Sparks Outrage in South Korea**  
_2026-05-25 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Starbucks Korea released an AI-assisted advertisement promoting reusable cups as "Tank Day" on May 18, coinciding with the anniversary of the 1980 Gwangju massacre. The AI-generated content evoked painful historical memories, triggering national outrage, boycotts, and significant financial losses for Starbucks. The incident highlights the risks of insufficient human oversight in AI-generated marketing.

**Attack vector:** `other`
