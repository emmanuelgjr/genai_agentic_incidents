### INC-06946  [↗](/incident/INC-06946.html)

**Tech Giants Face Lawsuits Over AI-Driven Biometric Privacy Violations**  
_2020-05-09 · real-world · Severity: High_

Apple and Facebook faced lawsuits for using AI-powered facial and voice recognition features that collected biometric data from Illinois residents without proper consent, violating state privacy laws. Facebook agreed to a $550 million settlement, while Apple faces ongoing legal action over similar alleged privacy breaches.
