**Facial Recognition AI Raises Privacy Concerns in Public Spaces and Transit Systems**  
_2020-01-07 · real-world · Severity: High_

Facial recognition AI is being deployed or considered in public housing, railway stations in India, and New York's subway system, prompting lawsuits and public outcry over potential privacy violations and misuse. While no direct harm has been reported, advocates warn of risks to civil liberties and human rights.
