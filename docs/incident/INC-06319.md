### INC-06319  [↗](/incident/INC-06319.html)

**AI-Powered Smart Intersections Planned to Reduce Traffic Accidents in Russia**  
_2020-02-06 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Russian cities plan to deploy AI-driven smart intersections to reduce traffic accidents and predict congestion. Developed by Glonass, the system will prioritize emergency vehicles and adapt to real-time conditions, with pilot projects launching in Novosibirsk and Perm. Experts anticipate a 20-25% reduction in intersection accidents.
