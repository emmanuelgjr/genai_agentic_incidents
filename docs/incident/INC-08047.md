**US Cities and States Move to Temporarily Ban AI Datacenters Amid Environmental Concerns**  
_2026-06-02 · real-world · Severity: High_

New York and Seattle are advancing one-year moratoriums on new large datacenters powering AI, citing concerns over energy use, environmental impact, and community disruption. These precautionary bans aim to allow time for regulations addressing the rapid expansion and resource demands of AI infrastructure.
