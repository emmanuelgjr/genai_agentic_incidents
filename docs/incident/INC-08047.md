### INC-08047  [↗](/incident/INC-08047.html)

**Lawyer Suspended for Alleged Prompt Injection to Manipulate Judicial AI in Rondônia**  
_2026-06-02 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Ordem dos Advogados do Brasil in Rondônia suspended a lawyer for allegedly using prompt injection to manipulate AI systems analyzing legal documents in a judicial process. The AI misuse aimed to influence case outcomes, raising concerns about ethical and legal breaches in the justice system. Authorities also formed a task force to combat AI-driven legal fraud.

**Attack vector:** `prompt-injection`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM01`, `LLM05`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI01`, `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0051`
