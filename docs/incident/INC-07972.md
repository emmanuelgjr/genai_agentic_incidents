**Naga Chaitanya Seeks Legal Protection Against AI-Generated Deepfakes and Defamatory Content**  
_2026-05-29 · real-world · Severity: High_

Telugu actor Naga Chaitanya has filed a suit in the Delhi High Court to protect his personality rights after AI-generated deepfakes, voice-cloned videos, and explicit content falsely depicted him in objectionable situations, causing reputational harm. The court has issued summons and is considering interim relief to curb ongoing AI-driven misuse.
