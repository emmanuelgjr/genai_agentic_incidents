**Instagram Fails to Fully Enforce Ban on Harmful Plastic Surgery AI Filters**  
_2020-01-26 · real-world · Severity: High_

Despite Instagram's ban on AI-powered plastic surgery filters due to concerns over mental health and body image harm, several such filters remain available on the platform. This enforcement failure allows continued exposure to filters that promote unrealistic beauty standards and may negatively impact users, especially young people.
