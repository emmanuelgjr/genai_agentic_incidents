**Facebook Algorithm Change Suppresses Progressive News Outlets, Causes Financial Harm**  
_2020-10-16 · real-world · Severity: High_

In 2017, Facebook, with CEO Mark Zuckerberg's approval, intentionally altered its news feed AI algorithm to reduce visibility for left-leaning news sites like Mother Jones. This led to significant traffic and revenue losses for affected outlets, raising concerns about biased information access and the harmful impact of AI-driven content moderation.
