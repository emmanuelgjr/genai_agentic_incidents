**Japan's Super City Law Raises AI-Driven Privacy and Surveillance Concerns**  
_2020-05-27 · real-world · Severity: High_

Japan passed the Super City Law enabling AI and big data-driven urban development, sparking strong opposition over risks to privacy and individual rights. Critics warn that centralized data management and integrated AI services could lead to mass surveillance and personal information misuse, highlighting unresolved governance and protection issues.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`
