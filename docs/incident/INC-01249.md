**Iranian AI-Enabled Drone Attacks Target Tech Company Data Centers in Middle East**  
_2026-03-11 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Iran has published a list of regional targets, including data centers and offices of major US tech firms like Google, Amazon, and Microsoft, in Israel, Dubai, Abu Dhabi, and Bahrain. Iranian AI-enabled drones have already attacked Amazon data centers, causing harm to critical infrastructure and property.
