### INC-06438  [↗](/incident/INC-06438.html)

**China's AI-Powered Network ID System Raises Privacy and Human Rights Concerns**  
_2020-11-25 · real-world · Severity: High_

China's Ministry of Public Security has deployed an AI-driven 'network ID' system in Fujian and Guangdong, requiring biometric data for online authentication. Critics warn it enables mass surveillance, centralized sensitive data, and potential government control over internet access, raising significant privacy, security, and human rights concerns.
