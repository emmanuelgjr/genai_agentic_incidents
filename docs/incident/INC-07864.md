**Taiwan Cracks Down on Smuggling of Nvidia AI Chips to China**  
_2026-05-22 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Taiwanese authorities raided 12 locations and arrested three individuals for smuggling high-performance Nvidia AI chips and Supermicro servers to China, Hong Kong, and Macau, violating export controls. The suspects allegedly forged documents and made false declarations to bypass regulations, raising significant legal and geopolitical concerns.
