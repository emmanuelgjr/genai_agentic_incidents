### INC-01162  [↗](/incident/INC-01162.html)

**Health Experts Warn of Risks in AI-Driven Self-Diagnosis in India**  
_2026-02-27 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Indian health experts, including Dr. Jitender Nagpal, warn that increasing use of AI-generative tools for self-diagnosis and self-treatment poses significant safety and ethical risks. They stress that AI should support, not replace, clinical judgment, cautioning against overreliance and highlighting concerns about patient safety and data privacy.
