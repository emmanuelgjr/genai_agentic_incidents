### INC-06369  [↗](/incident/INC-06369.html)

**Apple's Siri AI Accused of Mass Privacy Violations by Whistleblower**  
_2020-05-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

Whistleblower Thomas Le Bonniec revealed that Apple's Siri AI system recorded and processed users' conversations without consent, violating privacy rights and data protection laws. Despite public outcry and regulatory attention in the EU, Apple allegedly continued these practices, prompting calls for investigation and enforcement against the company.
