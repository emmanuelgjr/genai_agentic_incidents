### INC-05603  [↗](/incident/INC-05603.html)

**Facebook AI Moderation Wrongly Censors French Town 'Bitche' Over Name Confusion**  
_2021-04-12 · real-world · Severity: High_

Facebook's automated content moderation algorithm mistakenly censored the official page of the French town Bitche, misinterpreting its name as an English insult. The wrongful suspension disrupted the town's communication until Facebook acknowledged the error and restored the page, highlighting flaws in AI language understanding.
