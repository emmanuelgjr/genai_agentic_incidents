### INC-03980  [↗](/incident/INC-03980.html)

**Meta AI Image Generator Reportedly Fails to Accurately Represent Interracial Relationships**  
_2024-04-03 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#686`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/686/)

Meta&#x27;s AI image generator is alleged to produce inaccurate and biased images, consistently failing to depict interracial relationships involving Asian individuals and Caucasian or Black individuals. Instead, it generates images featuring two Asian people or stereotypes, erasing the diversity and representation of Asian people.
