**TikTok's AI Moderation and Data Practices Lead to Censorship and Privacy Violations**  
_2021-06-11 · real-world · Severity: High_

TikTok's AI-driven content moderation has reportedly censored Jewish creators, including those countering antisemitism, raising concerns of discrimination and rights violations. Separately, TikTok faces a $1.7 billion Dutch lawsuit for allegedly using AI to collect children's personal data without proper consent, violating EU privacy laws and endangering minors' rights.
