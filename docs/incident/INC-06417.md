**California Grants AutoX Permit for Driverless AI Vehicle Testing**  
_2020-07-17 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

AutoX, an autonomous vehicle startup, has received a permit from the California DMV to test its AI-driven cars without a safety driver on public roads in a limited area of San Jose. This regulatory milestone introduces potential risks associated with driverless AI systems operating in real-world environments.
