### INC-00055  [↗](/incident/INC-00055.html)

**AI Accelerates Password Cracking and Exposes Security Detection Gaps**  
_2026-05-05 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI systems have enabled attackers to rapidly crack passwords, with even long passwords breached in under a minute, leading to widespread account compromises. Additionally, AI-powered side-channel attacks can infer sensitive AI interaction content from encrypted traffic, exposing fundamental limitations in traditional security detection methods.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MEASURE-2.10`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0057`
