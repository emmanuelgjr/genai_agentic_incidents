### INC-05665  [↗](/incident/INC-05665.html)

**Google Algorithm Wrongly Labels Engineer as Serial Killer**  
_2021-06-25 · real-world · Severity: High_

Google's AI-powered Knowledge Panel and search algorithms mistakenly displayed the photo of Swiss engineer Hristo Georgiev alongside information about a Bulgarian serial killer with the same name. This error caused significant reputational harm to the innocent engineer before Google corrected the issue.
