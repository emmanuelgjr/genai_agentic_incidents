**Egypt Launches AI-Powered Digital Pathology Network to Improve Cancer Diagnosis**  
_2026-03-08 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Egypt's Ministry of Health, in partnership with Roche Diagnostics, launched a national digital pathology network using AI algorithms to enhance the speed and accuracy of cancer diagnosis. The initiative aims to modernize healthcare infrastructure, enabling earlier and more precise detection and treatment of cancer across the country.
