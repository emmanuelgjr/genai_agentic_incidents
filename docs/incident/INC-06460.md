### INC-06460  [↗](/incident/INC-06460.html)

**Clearview AI Misused for Private Surveillance and Privacy Violations**  
_2020-03-06 · real-world · Severity: High_

Clearview AI's facial recognition software, intended for law enforcement, was misused by private individuals and investors for personal purposes, including identifying people at parties, dates, and in stores without consent. This unauthorized use led to significant privacy violations and breaches of fundamental rights, raising serious concerns about misuse and potential harm.
