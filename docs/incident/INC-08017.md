### INC-08017  [↗](/incident/INC-08017.html)

**Hanoi Police Trial AI-Enabled Drones for Urban Surveillance**  
_2026-06-01 · real-world · Severity: High_

Hanoi police have begun trialing AI-powered drones to monitor traffic and urban order in key districts. The UAVs, equipped with high-resolution cameras and real-time data transmission, use AI to detect violations and transmit information to command centers. No harm or rights violations have been reported during this testing phase.
