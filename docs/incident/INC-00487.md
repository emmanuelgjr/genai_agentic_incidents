**AI-Generated Misinformation Spreads After Trump Assassination Attempt**  
_2026-04-30 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Following an assassination attempt on President Trump at a White House Correspondents' dinner, generative AI was used to create fake images and videos, fueling conspiracy theories and false information online. This AI-driven misinformation has misled the public and undermined trust in factual reporting.
