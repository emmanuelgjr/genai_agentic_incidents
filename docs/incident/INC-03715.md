### INC-03715  [↗](/incident/INC-03715.html)

**Deepfake Pierce Brosnan scam cripples Nottingham art gallery**  
_2024 · real-world · Severity: High_

Nottingham gallery owner Simone Simms spent months negotiating what she believed was an exclusive art exhibition with actor Pierce Brosnan; in fact the entire correspondence was an AI-driven impersonation. She advertised meet-and-greet tickets at up to GBP 500 each; the fraud caused severe financial losses, the closure of her gallery and reputational damage.

**Affected:** SMS Art Gallery, UK  
**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM03`, `LLM09`
