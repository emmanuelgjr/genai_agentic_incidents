**Iran Unveils AI-Enabled Automated Ballistic Missile Launch System**  
_2020-11-04 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Iran’s Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps unveiled an AI-enabled, automated system for launching long-range ballistic missiles. The system, capable of rapid, successive launches, raises significant concerns about the risks of autonomous weapons, including potential harm, escalation, and violations of international norms if deployed in conflict.
