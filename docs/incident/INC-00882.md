### INC-00882  [↗](/incident/INC-00882.html)

**Delhi High Court Restrains AI Deepfake Misuse of Ramdev's Persona**  
_2026-02-25 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The Delhi High Court issued an interim injunction against the unauthorized use of yoga guru Ramdev's name, image, and voice in AI-generated deepfakes and manipulated videos. The court found such misuse violated his personality rights, misled the public, and ordered removal of offending content within 72 hours.
