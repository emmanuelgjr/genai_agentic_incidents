**AI System Enables Real-Time Vehicle Seizure for Unpaid Taxes in Taiwan**  
_2026-03-24 · real-world · Severity: High_

Taiwan's Hualien Branch deployed the 'AI 行動神捕' system for real-time license plate recognition, enabling enforcement officers to identify and seize vehicles with unpaid taxes. This led to the legal seizure and subsequent payment of overdue taxes by a vehicle owner, demonstrating AI's direct impact on property rights enforcement.
