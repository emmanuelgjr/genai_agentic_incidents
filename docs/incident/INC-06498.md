### INC-06498  [↗](/incident/INC-06498.html)

**Deepfake AI Used in Financial Fraud and Phishing Scams**  
_2020-03-16 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI-generated deepfakes have been used in phishing scams and financial fraud, causing real harm to individuals and businesses. While many articles discuss the potential risks of deepfake technology, one report confirms that malicious actors have already exploited these AI systems to deceive and defraud victims.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
