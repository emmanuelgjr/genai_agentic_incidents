### INC-00387  [↗](/incident/INC-00387.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfake Scam Impersonates Spanish TV Host Pablo Motos**  
_2026-03-06 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

AI-generated deepfake videos impersonating Spanish TV host Pablo Motos have been used in online investment scams, leading to significant financial losses for victims. Motos publicly denounced the fraud and criticized major tech platforms for inadequate action in preventing the spread of such AI-driven scams in Spain.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
