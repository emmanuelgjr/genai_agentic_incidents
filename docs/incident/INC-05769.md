**Mobileye Plans Launch of Autonomous Robotaxi Service in Germany Pending Legal Approval**  
_2021-04-16 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Intel subsidiary Mobileye is preparing to launch self-driving robotaxi services in Germany as early as 2022, pending the establishment of necessary legal frameworks. The company has been testing autonomous vehicles in Munich, highlighting potential future risks associated with deploying AI-driven taxis on public roads.
