### INC-02634  [↗](/incident/INC-02634.html)

**Google Gemini Reportedly Generates Sexual Role‑Play for Account Registered as Minor**  
_2025-07-14 · real-world · Severity: Critical_
AIID: [`#1157`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1157/)

In spring 2025, a journalist reportedly created a Google Gemini account registered to a fictitious 13‑year‑old to test the chatbot&#x27;s teen‑safety protections. Despite added safeguards, prompt manipulation and content‑summarization requests reportedly bypassed filters, leading Gemini to allegedly generate sexual role‑play content. Following the publication of the article, Google reportedly implemented additional protections.
