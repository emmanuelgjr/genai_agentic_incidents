**UK Regulators Warn of Cyber Risks from Frontier AI Models in Finance**  
_2026-05-15 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

UK financial authorities, including the finance ministry, Bank of England, and Financial Conduct Authority, have warned that advanced AI models could amplify cyber threats to financial stability and market integrity. Firms are urged to plan and mitigate risks as these AI systems surpass human capabilities in speed and scale.
