### INC-01051  [↗](/incident/INC-01051.html)

**French Political Party Uses AI to Fabricate Celebrity Endorsements for Book Promotion**  
_2026-04-21 · real-world · Severity: High_

The French political party Renaissance used AI to generate fake images and endorsements from celebrities like Emma Watson, Cristiano Ronaldo, and Rosalía to promote Gabriel Attal's book without their consent. The unauthorized use of AI-generated content led to reputational harm, public backlash, and the posts' removal.
