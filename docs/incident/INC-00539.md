**AI-Powered Cyberwarfare Attacks Impact Australia and UK**  
_2026-03-17 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Nation-state actors are increasingly using AI to conduct sophisticated cyberwarfare attacks, causing significant harm to organizations in Australia and the UK. These AI-driven attacks have led to cybersecurity breaches, financial losses, and disruptions to critical infrastructure, highlighting the urgent need for enhanced cybersecurity measures.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`
