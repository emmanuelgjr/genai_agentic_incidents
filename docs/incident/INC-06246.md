**AI Surveillance and Fever Detection Technologies Raise Privacy Concerns Amid COVID-19 Response**  
_2020-03-17 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Governments and companies are deploying AI-powered surveillance, facial recognition, and thermal cameras to track and detect potential COVID-19 carriers. While aimed at public health, privacy advocates and experts warn these technologies could lead to significant data privacy violations and civil liberties infringements if misused or left unchecked.
