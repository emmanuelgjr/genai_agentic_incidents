### INC-06496  [↗](/incident/INC-06496.html)

**Deepfake AI Technology Causes Harm Through Fraud and Misinformation**  
_2020-11-15 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI-powered deepfake technology has enabled the creation and spread of highly realistic fake videos and audio, leading to financial fraud, reputational damage, and widespread misinformation. Incidents include scams using synthetic voices and videos, such as a fake Mark Zuckerberg video, deceiving individuals and undermining public trust.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
