**Yuval Noah Harari Warns Davos Elites of AI-Driven 'Technological Dictatorships'**  
_2020-01-23 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Historian Yuval Noah Harari warned world leaders at Davos about the risks posed by artificial intelligence, specifically the potential for increased digital surveillance and control, especially targeting those in positions of power. He urged global cooperation to prevent the rise of 'technological dictatorships' enabled by AI.
