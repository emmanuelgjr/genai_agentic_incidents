**Tesla AI System Failures Lead to Multiple Accidents and Public Outcry in China**  
_2021-03-14 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Multiple accidents involving Tesla vehicles in China, attributed to suspected AI system malfunctions such as sudden acceleration and brake failure, have resulted in injuries and property damage. Tesla often blames driver error, but disputes over data transparency and system reliability have fueled consumer anger and calls for stronger regulation of intelligent connected vehicles.
