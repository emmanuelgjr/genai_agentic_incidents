**Reportedly Fatal Xiaomi SU7 Ultra Crash in Chengdu Purportedly Involves Automated Driving Failure and Door Lock Malfunction**  
_2025-10-12 · real-world · Severity: Medium_
AIID: [`#1232`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1232/)

On Monday, a fatal crash occurred in Chengdu, China involving a Xiaomi SU7 electric sedan that collided with another vehicle after the driver, a 31-year-old male, was suspected of driving under the influence of alcohol. The vehicle caught fire after the collision, and bystanders attempted but failed to open the doors to rescue the occupant due to malfunctioning electronic door handles that require electrical power to function. The driver died at the scene. The incident caused Xiaomi's shares to fall over 5%, with drops as steep as 8.7% in Hong Kong markets. This crash follows a previous fatal SU7 incident earlier in the year that also raised questions about the vehicle's safety features. The electronic door handle design, popularized by Tesla and common in modern EVs, relies on sensors and electricity and may fail during fires or power outages. China is reportedly considering banning such electronic door handles due to safety risks, while the US National Highway Traffic Safety Administration has launched an investigation into about 174,000 Tesla Model Y vehicles over similar door handle failures. The incident has renewed concerns about electric vehicle safety and the prioritization of aesthetics over reliable emergency access in modern car design.
