### INC-06268  [↗](/incident/INC-06268.html)

**AI-Driven Automation Threatens 800 Million Jobs Globally**  
_2020-10-20 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Multiple reports highlight that advances in AI and automation technologies could put up to 800 million jobs worldwide at risk, particularly in countries with large segments of routine or low-skilled labor. Economists warn this shift may increase income inequality and disrupt global employment stability.
