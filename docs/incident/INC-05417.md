**AI-Enabled Combat Drones Used in Attacks on US Forces in Iraq**  
_2021-06-04 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Iran-backed militias in Iraq have increasingly used sophisticated, AI-enabled combat drones to target US military bases and sensitive facilities, including those used by the CIA and Special Operations. These drone attacks have evaded defenses, caused property damage, and posed significant threats to personnel, prompting urgent countermeasures by US forces.
