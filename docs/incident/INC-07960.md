### INC-07960  [↗](/incident/INC-07960.html)

**AI-Generated Scams Cause Surge in Investment Fraud in Ireland**  
_2026-05-28 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Fraudsters in Ireland are using AI-generated images and videos of public figures to create convincing fake ads for bogus state-backed investment schemes. These scams have led to over €20 million in losses, with a 20% rise in investment fraud, particularly targeting people in their 50s.

**Attack vector:** `other`  

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`
