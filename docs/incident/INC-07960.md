**UK Develops Autonomous AI-Enabled Drones as Apache Helicopter 'Wingmen'**  
_2026-05-15 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The UK Ministry of Defence is investing £10 million in Project NYX to develop fully autonomous, AI-enabled drones to operate alongside Apache helicopters. Four companies, including BAE Systems and Tekever, are advancing designs for these armed drones, which will perform reconnaissance, precision strikes, and electronic warfare, raising future risks of autonomous weapon system harm.
