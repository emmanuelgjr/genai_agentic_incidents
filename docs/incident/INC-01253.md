**Israel Uses AI to Improve Missile Alert System During Conflict**  
_2026-04-05 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Israel has deployed artificial intelligence to enhance its missile early warning system amid ongoing conflicts with Gaza and Iran. The AI enables more precise, localized alerts, reducing unnecessary sheltering and stress for civilians by minimizing false alarms and improving response to missile threats.
