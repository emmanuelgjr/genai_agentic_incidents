### INC-03492  [↗](/incident/INC-03492.html)

**AI-generated deepfakes fuel misinformation in Taiwan election**  
_2024-01-10 · real-world · Severity: High_

During Taiwan's January 2024 presidential election, AI-generated deepfake videos and synthetic audio falsely portrayed candidate Lai Ching-te's running mate Hsiao Bi-khim and fabricated statements, with circulation linked to PRC-aligned networks.

**Affected:** Taiwan electorate
