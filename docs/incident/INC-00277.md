### INC-00277  [↗](/incident/INC-00277.html)

**AI-Driven Cyberattacks and Military Targeting Lead to Civilian Harm**  
_2026-05-09 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Google's Threat Intelligence Group reported a surge in AI-enabled cyberattacks, including AI-developed zero-day exploits that bypass security and autonomous malware. Separately, US and Israeli military operations used AI for rapid target selection, resulting in civilian deaths, including a mistaken attack on an Iranian school. These incidents highlight direct harm from AI use in cybersecurity and warfare.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`
