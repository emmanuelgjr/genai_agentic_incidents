### INC-07855  [↗](/incident/INC-07855.html)

**AI Data Center Power Surge Threatens US Grid Stability and Consumer Costs**  
_2026-05-21 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Wood Mackenzie warns that the rapid expansion of AI-driven data centers in the US is straining electricity grids, causing near grid failures and threatening project viability, market stability, and consumer costs. Technical and regulatory challenges, along with unprecedented power demands, have already led to harmful power fluctuations and increased risks for infrastructure and ratepayers.
