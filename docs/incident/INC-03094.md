### INC-03094  [↗](/incident/INC-03094.html)

**Purported Deepfake Video and Fake News Articles Allegedly Used to Impersonate Guernsey&#x27;s Chief Minister in Investment Scam**  
_2025-08-01 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1288`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1288/)

Scammers allegedly used AI-generated deepfake video, fabricated images, and fake local news articles to impersonate Guernsey Chief Minister Lindsay de Sausmarez and promote a non-existent investment scheme. Authorities reportedly warned that the materials falsely depict her endorsing the opportunity. Police advised residents to disregard the posts and avoid providing personal or financial information.
