### INC-08198  [↗](/incident/INC-08198.html)

**LMDeploy — Rce (CVE-2026-46517)**  
_2026-06 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: High_

CVEs: `CVE-2026-46517`
CWEs: `CWE-1188`, `CWE-915`, `CWE-94`
CVSS: **7.8** — `CVSS:3.1/AV:L/AC:L/PR:N/UI:R/S:U/C:H/I:H/A:H`

LMDeploy is a toolkit for compressing, deploying, and serving large language models. In versions 0.12.3 and prior, hardcoded "trust_remote_code=True" enables HF supply-chain RCE without user opt-in. At time of publication, there are no publicly available patches.

**Attack vector:** `rce`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM03`, `LLM05`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI04`, `ASI05`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-6.1`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0010`, `AML.T0050`
