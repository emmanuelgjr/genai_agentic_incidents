**Police Use of Clearview AI Facial Recognition Sparks Privacy Lawsuits and Rights Concerns**  
_2020-02-12 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Toronto police admitted to using Clearview AI's facial recognition tool, which scrapes billions of images without consent, raising privacy and legal concerns. A lawsuit alleges Clearview and its partner CDW violated Illinois biometric privacy laws by collecting and storing residents' biometric data without notice or consent, highlighting risks of AI-driven surveillance.
