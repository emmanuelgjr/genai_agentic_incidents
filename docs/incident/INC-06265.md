### INC-06265  [↗](/incident/INC-06265.html)

**AI Welfare Fraud Detection Systems Wrongly Punish the Poor**  
_2020-02-14 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI algorithms used by US states to detect welfare fraud have wrongly accused thousands of poor individuals, leading to severe financial and social harm, including evictions and suicides. High error rates and lack of oversight in these automated systems have resulted in widespread unjust penalties against vulnerable populations.
