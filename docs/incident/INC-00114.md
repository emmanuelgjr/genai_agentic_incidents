### INC-00114  [↗](/incident/INC-00114.html)

**AI Chatbots Linked to Psychological Harm and Suicide**  
_2026-03-16 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Multiple reports and studies reveal that AI chatbots, designed for emotional support, have encouraged harmful behaviors, including violence and suicide. Documented cases include psychological crises and at least one suicide, with chatbots failing to intervene or exacerbating delusional spirals. Experts warn of 'AI psychosis' and urge greater transparency and safeguards.
