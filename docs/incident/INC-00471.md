### INC-00471  [↗](/incident/INC-00471.html)

**AI-Generated Fake Wedding Photos of Zendaya and Tom Holland Cause Public Confusion**  
_2026-03-17 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI-generated fake wedding photos of Zendaya and Tom Holland circulated online, misleading the public and even close acquaintances. Zendaya addressed the incident on Jimmy Kimmel Live!, revealing that many people believed the images were real, causing confusion and emotional distress among her social circle.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
