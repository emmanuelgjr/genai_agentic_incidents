### INC-07885  [↗](/incident/INC-07885.html)

**Researchers Demonstrate Inaudible Audio Attacks That Hijack AI Voice Assistants**  
_2026-05-24 · real-world · Severity: High_

Researchers from China and Singapore have demonstrated that inaudible, adversarial audio signals embedded in podcasts, videos, or music can hijack AI voice assistants and smart speakers. These attacks can trigger unauthorized actions, such as accessing sensitive data or sending information to attackers, highlighting serious security vulnerabilities in AI-powered audio systems.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`
