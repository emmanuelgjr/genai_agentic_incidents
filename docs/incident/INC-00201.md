### INC-00201  [↗](/incident/INC-00201.html)

**AI Search Engines Cause Major Publisher Traffic Loss**  
_2026-03-17 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

AI-powered search engines, including Google's AI Mode, have significantly reduced web traffic to publishers by scraping and repurposing content without permission, causing economic harm. Research shows small publishers lost 60% of search traffic in 2024-2025. Yahoo's Scout aims to counteract this trend by supporting the open web.
