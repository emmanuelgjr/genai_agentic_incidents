### INC-03757  [↗](/incident/INC-03757.html)

**ESPN's AI Coverage Overlooks Alex Morgan in Her Final Match Recap**  
_2024-09-08 · real-world · Severity: Medium_
AIID: [`#785`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/785/)

ESPN deployed AI-powered content generation services called ESPN Generative AI Services to provide recaps of National Women's Soccer League (NWSL) and Premier Lacrosse League (PLL) games. On September 8, 2024, the AI system generated a 215-word recap of a San Diego Wave vs North Carolina Courage match that ended 4-1. The match was significant as it was U.S. soccer legend Alex Morgan's final professional game, featuring an emotional farewell ceremony where she removed her cleats at midfield and received applause from the crowd. However, the AI-generated recap completely omitted any mention of Morgan or the historic nature of the event, instead focusing only on standard game statistics and noting that Kennedy Wesley was the standout player. The recap was posted to ESPN's website and apps at 8:52 pm ET on September 8. ESPN states that each AI-generated recap is reviewed by a human editor to ensure quality and accuracy, though it's unclear if the human editor failed to notice Morgan's absence or decided it wasn't worth mentioning. ESPN did publish a separate human-written article about Morgan's farewell by Jeff Kassouf, but it was placed in a side menu and could be easily missed. The incident was updated on September 9 at 8:59 am ET to include a mention of Alex Morgan.
