**Senator Criticizes Tesla Autopilot Over Safety and Misleading Branding**  
_2020-01-24 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

U.S. Senator Ed Markey criticized Tesla's AI-powered Autopilot system, urging the company to rebrand it due to concerns that its name and marketing mislead drivers into overestimating its capabilities, potentially leading to unsafe use. Regulatory bodies and other countries have also raised safety concerns, prompting calls for clearer communication and additional safety measures.
