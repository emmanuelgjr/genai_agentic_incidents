### INC-00042  [↗](/incident/INC-00042.html)

**Advanced AI Models Spark Global Cybersecurity and Financial System Fears**  
_2026-05-04 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The release of advanced AI models like Anthropic's Claude Mythos and OpenAI's GPT-5.5-Cyber has triggered global concern among regulators and financial institutions. These models can autonomously identify thousands of critical software vulnerabilities, raising fears of systemic cyberattacks and financial instability. Authorities in the US, Australia, and Singapore have issued urgent warnings and called for enhanced cybersecurity measures.
