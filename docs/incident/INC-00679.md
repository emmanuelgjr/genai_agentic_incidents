### INC-00679  [↗](/incident/INC-00679.html)

**AI-Generated Fake Crime Scene Image Causes Public Misinformation in Romania**  
_2026-01-25 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Romanian Police warned about a fake image, generated entirely by AI, falsely linked to a real murder case in Timiș County. The AI-created photo spread widely online, misleading the public and causing emotional distress to victims' families, highlighting the harm caused by AI-driven disinformation.

**Attack vector:** `misinformation`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0058`
