### INC-00679  [↗](/incident/INC-00679.html)

**AI Systems Tested for Autonomous Aircraft Piloting in the US**  
_2026-05-25 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Aviation companies, notably Merlin Labs, are testing AI systems capable of autonomously piloting aircraft, interpreting air traffic instructions, and communicating with control towers. Demonstrations in Rhode Island showed AI controlling a Cessna Caravan without human intervention, raising potential safety concerns for future autonomous flight operations.
