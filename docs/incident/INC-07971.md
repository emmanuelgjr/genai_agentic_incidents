### INC-07971  [↗](/incident/INC-07971.html)

**AI Chatbots and Social Media Pose Risks to Vulnerable Children in Hungary**  
_2026-05-29 · real-world · Severity: High_

ESET cybersecurity experts warn that children in Hungary often form emotional attachments to AI chatbots, leading to risks such as emotional harm, misinformation, and privacy breaches. Sharing selfies on social media further increases long-term security and data protection concerns for young users.

**Attack vector:** `misinformation`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MEASURE-2.8`
