### INC-00371  [↗](/incident/INC-00371.html)

**AI-Generated Code Increases Engineer Workload and Software Defects in Japan**  
_2026-04-21 · real-world · Severity: High_

A survey of 322 Japanese IT engineers revealed that the widespread use of AI-generated code has led to a significant increase in reviewer workload, with 78.6% experiencing bugs or defects caused by AI code. Nearly 90% reported increased review burdens, often requiring over three extra hours per week to maintain software quality.
