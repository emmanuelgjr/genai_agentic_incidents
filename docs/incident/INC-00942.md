**Ergo to Cut 1,000 Jobs in Germany Due to Increased AI Automation**  
_2026-02-17 · real-world · Severity: High_

German insurer Ergo, a Munich Re subsidiary, will cut around 1,000 jobs by 2030 as AI systems automate traditional insurance tasks. The reduction, about 200 jobs annually, will occur through voluntary measures without forced layoffs. Ergo is also investing in reskilling programs to help affected employees transition to new roles.
