**AI-Powered Age Verification Sparks Privacy and Surveillance Fears in the US**  
_2026-03-06 · real-world · Severity: High_

US states are increasingly mandating AI-driven age and identity verification for online access, requiring facial recognition and ID scans. This expansion has triggered privacy concerns, fears of mass surveillance, and legal challenges, as experts warn of potential data breaches and erosion of online anonymity.
