### INC-04119  [↗](/incident/INC-04119.html)

**Purported Deepfake Facebook Advertisement Reportedly Used Bulgarian Actor, Director, and Playwright Kamen Donev&#x27;s Likeness to Market Joint-Pain Product**  
_2024-05-04 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1463`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1463/)

A reportedly AI-manipulated Facebook advertisement appeared to repurpose footage of Bulgarian actor, director, and playwright Kamen Donev, allegedly overlaying fabricated audio and altered lip movements to market a purported joint-pain product. The ad reportedly linked to a presumably fabricated article imitating a Bulgarian news outlet and to an online sales funnel for a product described as unavailable through pharmacies.
