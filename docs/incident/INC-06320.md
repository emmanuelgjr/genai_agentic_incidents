**AI-Powered Social Media Surveillance by Dataminr Sparks Debate on Civil Liberties**  
_2020-09-30 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Dataminr, using AI to monitor public tweets and alert US authorities about planned protests, including Black Lives Matter events, has raised concerns over surveillance and potential threats to privacy and freedom of assembly. Twitter defends the practice, citing public safety benefits, prompting significant debate on ethical boundaries.
