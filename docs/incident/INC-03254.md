**Tennessee Meteorologist&#x27;s Likeness Reportedly Used in Sextortion Campaign Involving Purported AI-Generated Content**  
_2025-01-10 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1048`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1048/)

Bree Smith, a meteorologist in Nashville, Tennessee, was reportedly targeted in a sextortion campaign involving purported AI-generated deepfakes that manipulated her likeness into explicit content. According to reporting, Smith&#x27;s face was digitally placed onto semi-nude and nude bodies, with the resulting media circulated online by impersonators seeking money. Smith documented the spread of these accounts and has advocated for legislative responses, including a new Tennessee bill addressing deepfake-related harms.
