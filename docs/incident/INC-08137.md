**AI Data Centers Drive Global Freshwater Depletion**  
_2026-06-07 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI data centers, essential for processing queries like ChatGPT, are consuming vast amounts of freshwater for cooling and electricity, with annual usage projected to reach up to 6.6 billion cubic meters by 2027. This has led to significant environmental harm, exacerbating water scarcity in drought-prone regions worldwide.
