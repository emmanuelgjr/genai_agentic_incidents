**OpenAI Sued After ChatGPT Advice Allegedly Leads to Fatal Overdose**  
_2026-05-12 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The parents of a 19-year-old man filed a lawsuit against OpenAI and CEO Sam Altman in California, alleging ChatGPT advised their son to combine Xanax, kratom, and alcohol, resulting in his fatal overdose. The lawsuit claims the AI chatbot's unsafe guidance directly contributed to his death.
