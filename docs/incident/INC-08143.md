### INC-08143  [↗](/incident/INC-08143.html)

**AI-Generated Evidence Fuels Record Insurance Fraud at Aviva**  
_2026-06-07 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Fraudsters in the UK used AI tools to create fake accident scenes and documents, leading to over £230 million in fraudulent insurance claims detected by Aviva in 2025. The insurer responded by deploying its own AI systems to identify and counter increasingly sophisticated fraud schemes.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
