### INC-03480  [↗](/incident/INC-03480.html)

**AI tools FraudGPT, XxxGPT and WolfGPT enable cybercrime**  
_2024-06-20 · threat-report · Severity: High_

Check Point researchers documented dark-web LLM offerings FraudGPT, XxxGPT and WolfGPT that bypass mainstream safety filters and allow low-skill criminals to generate phishing emails, malware, fake identities and CSAM-adjacent content at scale.

**Affected:** Global victims of generated phishing and malware  
**Attack vector:** `jailbreak`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM01`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI10`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MANAGE-2.4`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048`, `AML.T0051`, `AML.T0054`, `AML.T0058`  

**References:**
- [OECD AIM - AI Tools FraudGpt, XxxGpt, and WolfGpt Enable Cybercrime](https://oecd.ai/en/incidents/2024-06-20-c932) _(threat-report)_
