**Presentation of AI-Enabled Autonomous Armored Vehicle Taurus XL in Spain**  
_2026-05-26 · real-world · Severity: High_

Miriad Global, with Funditec, presented the Taurus XL, an AI-enabled autonomous armored 6x6 ground vehicle, to Spain's Ministry of Defense and CDTI. Designed for military and civil missions, it integrates AI for perception, autonomous operation, and remote weapon systems, posing credible future risks if deployed.
