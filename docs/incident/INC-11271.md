### INC-11271  [↗](/incident/INC-11271.html)

**@lobehub/chat vulnerable to unauthorized access to plugins**  
_2024-01 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: Medium_

CVEs: `CVE-2024-24566`
CWEs: `CWE-284`
CVSS: **5.3** — `CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:U/C:N/I:L/A:N`
