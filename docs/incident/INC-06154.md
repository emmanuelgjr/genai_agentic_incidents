### INC-06154  [↗](/incident/INC-06154.html)

**US Army Plans Large-Scale Deployment of AI-Enabled HoloLens Headsets**  
_2021-05-04 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The US Army has contracted Microsoft to supply 120,000 HoloLens augmented reality headsets, integrated with AI and cloud processing, for battlefield use. While no harm has occurred yet, the planned deployment of these AI-enabled systems in combat environments presents potential risks of injury or operational disruption if the technology malfunctions or is misused.
