### INC-07934  [↗](/incident/INC-07934.html)

**Smartwatch AI App Accurately Detects Epileptic Seizures, Reducing Health Risks**  
_2026-05-27 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

A study published in Neurology Open Access found the EpiWatch smartwatch app, using AI algorithms, accurately detected 98% of tonic-clonic seizures in epilepsy patients with a low false alarm rate. The app enables timely caregiver alerts, potentially reducing injury and sudden unexpected death in epilepsy (SUDEP).

**Attack vector:** `other`
