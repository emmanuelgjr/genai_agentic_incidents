### INC-00975  [↗](/incident/INC-00975.html)

**Experts Warn AI Chatbots May Homogenize Human Thought and Reduce Creativity**  
_2026-03-11 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Researchers from the University of Southern California warn that widespread use of AI chatbots and large language models could standardize human communication and thinking, potentially reducing cognitive diversity and creativity. They urge developers to include more real-world diversity in AI training data to mitigate these risks.
