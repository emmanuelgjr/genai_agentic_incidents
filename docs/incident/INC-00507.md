### INC-00507  [↗](/incident/INC-00507.html)

**AI-Generated Translations Introduce Errors in Wikipedia Articles**  
_2026-03-04 · real-world · Severity: High_

Wikipedia articles translated using AI systems like ChatGPT and Gemini introduced factual errors and unrelated content, undermining article reliability. The Open Knowledge Association paid contributors to use AI for translations, prompting Wikipedia editors to restrict such practices and enforce new policies to address the spread of misinformation.
