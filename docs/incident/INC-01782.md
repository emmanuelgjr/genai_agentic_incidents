### INC-01782  [↗](/incident/INC-01782.html)

**Social Media Algorithms Drive Digital Addiction Among Youth**  
_2026-03-11 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

AI-driven social media algorithms, designed to maximize user engagement by promoting emotionally triggering content, have led to increased digital addiction and psychological harm among children and adolescents. These harms have prompted legal actions and public health concerns, particularly in Turkey.

**Attack vector:** `other`
