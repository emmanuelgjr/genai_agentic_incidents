### INC-07851  [↗](/incident/INC-07851.html)

**Japanese Voice Actor Sues TikTok Over AI-Generated Voice Mimicry**  
_2026-05-22 · real-world · Severity: High_

Japanese voice actor Kenjiro Tsuda has sued TikTok in Tokyo District Court, demanding the removal of 188 videos that used generative AI to mimic his voice without permission. The videos, posted by an unknown user, generated significant revenue, raising legal concerns over unauthorized AI use and publicity rights infringement.
