### INC-00470  [↗](/incident/INC-00470.html)

**AI-Generated Fake War Videos Spread via Hacked Accounts on X**  
_2026-03-04 · real-world · Severity: High_

A Pakistani user hacked 31 X (formerly Twitter) accounts to spread AI-generated fake videos about the Iran-US-Israel conflict, promoting pro-Iran content and misleading the public. X's team, led by product head Nikita Bier, has taken action against these accounts to curb misinformation.
