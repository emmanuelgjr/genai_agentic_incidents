### INC-00145  [↗](/incident/INC-00145.html)

**AI Deepfake Scam Causes $25 Million Loss in Vietnam**  
_2026-05-12 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

At the Digital Trust in Finance 2026 forum in Hanoi, cybersecurity expert Ngô Minh Hiếu (Hiếu PC) highlighted a major AI incident where deepfake technology was used to impersonate company leaders in a Zoom call, leading a finance employee to transfer $25 million to scammers. The event underscores rising AI-driven fraud risks.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
