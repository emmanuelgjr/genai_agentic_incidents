**US Military Considers Reducing Human Control Over AI in Drone Warfare**  
_2021-01-25 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

US Army officials, including Gen. John Murray, warn that AI-enabled drone swarms are becoming too fast for humans to counter, prompting discussions about relaxing Pentagon rules requiring human oversight of lethal AI systems. This shift raises significant risks of autonomous AI making life-or-death decisions in combat without direct human intervention.
