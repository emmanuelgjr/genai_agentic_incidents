### INC-00178  [↗](/incident/INC-00178.html)

**AI License Plate Readers Breach Privacy Laws in California**  
_2026-03-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

Automated license plate readers powered by AI, supplied by Flock Safety, were misconfigured, allowing unauthorized access to sensitive vehicle data by out-of-state and federal agencies, including ICE. This breach violated California privacy laws and enabled police misuse, raising significant concerns about surveillance and data protection.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`
