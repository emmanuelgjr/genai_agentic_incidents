### INC-05424  [↗](/incident/INC-05424.html)

**AI-Enabled Mood-Tracking Wristband Raises Workplace Privacy Concerns**  
_2021-01-19 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The Moodbeam wristband, used by companies to monitor remote employees' psychological states via AI-driven data collection, allows managers to track workers' moods. While intended to support employee well-being, the system raises concerns about privacy and potential misuse of sensitive mental health data in workplace decisions.

**Attack vector:** `other`
