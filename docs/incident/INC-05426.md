### INC-05426  [↗](/incident/INC-05426.html)

**AI-Enabled Turkish Armed Drones Influence Conflicts and Raise Future Risks**  
_2021-03-02 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Turkish defense company Baykar has developed and deployed AI-supported armed drones (SİHA), which have played a decisive role in conflicts in Azerbaijan, Libya, and Syria, causing harm to adversaries. Ongoing projects aim to create even more advanced AI-enabled unmanned combat aerial vehicles, raising concerns about future risks from autonomous weapon systems.

**Attack vector:** `other`
