### INC-11030  [↗](/incident/INC-11030.html)

**Privilege Escalation in TYPO3 Neos**  
_2024-06 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: Medium_
CVSS: **4.9** — `CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:H/PR:L/UI:N/S:C/C:L/I:L/A:N`

It has been discovered that TYPO3 Neos is vulnerable to Privilege Escalation. Logged in editors could access, create and modify content nodes that exist in the workspace of other editors.

**Affected:** composer/typo3/neos, composer/typo3/neos  
**Attack vector:** `other`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM03`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI04`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-6.1`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0010`
