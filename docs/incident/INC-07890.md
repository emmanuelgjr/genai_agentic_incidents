### INC-07890  [↗](/incident/INC-07890.html)

**TrapDoor Malware Exploits AI Coding Assistants to Steal Crypto and Credentials**  
_2026-05-25 · real-world · Severity: High_

The TrapDoor malware campaign targeted crypto and AI developers by distributing over 34 malicious packages across npm, PyPI, and Crates.io. It exploited AI coding assistants like Claude and Cursor via prompt injection, enabling theft of sensitive credentials, crypto wallet data, and cloud keys, causing significant harm to developer security.

**Attack vector:** `prompt-injection`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM01`, `LLM02`, `LLM03`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI01`, `ASI02`, `ASI04`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-6.1`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.10`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0010`, `AML.T0051`, `AML.T0053`, `AML.T0057`
