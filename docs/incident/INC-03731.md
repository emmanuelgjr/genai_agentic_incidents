### INC-03731  [↗](/incident/INC-03731.html)

**Department for Work and Pensions (DWP) Algorithm Wrongly Flags 200,000 for Housing Benefit Fraud**  
_2024-06-23 · real-world · Severity: Critical_
AIID: [`#738`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/738/)

A Department for Work and Pensions (DWP) algorithm wrongly flagged over 200,000 UK housing benefit claims as high risk, resulting in unnecessary investigations. Two-thirds of these flagged claims were legitimate, causing wasted public funds and stress for claimants. Despite initial success in a pilot, the algorithm&#x27;s real-world performance fell short. This incident highlights the risks of overreliance on automated systems in welfare administration.
