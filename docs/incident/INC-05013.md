**Acclarent TruDi Navigation System Was Alleged to Have Misguided Sinus Surgery, Reportedly Contributing to Patient&#x27;s Stroke**  
_2022-06-23 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1436`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1436/)

During a reported sinus procedure in Texas, Acclarent&#x27;s AI-enabled TruDi Navigation System was alleged to have misinformed surgeon Marc Dean about the position of his instruments inside patient Erin Ralph&#x27;s head, purportedly contributing to carotid-artery injury and a stroke. Reuters reported that Ralph later sued, alleging the system&#x27;s AI contributed to her injuries. Acclarent and Integra denied a causal connection.
