**Renault Develops AI-Enabled Ground-Based Military Drone**  
_2026-03-30 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Renault, in partnership with John Cockerill, is developing a ground-based military drone equipped with AI for autonomous navigation and reconnaissance. The project, prompted by interest from the French defense ministry, is in the exploratory phase and poses potential future risks if deployed in military contexts.
