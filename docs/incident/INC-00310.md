**AI-Driven Medicare Prior Authorization Delays and Denials Harm Patients in Washington**  
_2026-04-22 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services' WISeR pilot program uses AI to automate prior authorization for Medicare procedures in six states, including Washington. Reports show the AI system is causing significant delays and denials of medically necessary care, worsening patient health outcomes and increasing administrative burdens for providers.
