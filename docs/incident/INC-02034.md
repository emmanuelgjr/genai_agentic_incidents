**US-China Consider Formal AI Talks to Prevent Military and Economic Crises**  
_2026-05-07 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The US and China are considering formal, regular dialogues to address risks from AI competition, including potential crises from autonomous military systems, loss of AI control, and misuse by non-state actors. The talks aim to establish safeguards and prevent AI-driven military or economic crises amid escalating technological rivalry.
