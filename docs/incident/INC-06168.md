**US Panel Urges Development of AI-Powered Autonomous Weapons Despite Global Concerns**  
_2021-01-26 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

A US government-appointed panel, led by former Google CEO Eric Schmidt, advised against banning AI-powered autonomous weapons, citing a 'moral imperative' to develop them for national security. The panel argues these systems could reduce battlefield errors and casualties, despite international concerns over potential future harm and ethical risks.
