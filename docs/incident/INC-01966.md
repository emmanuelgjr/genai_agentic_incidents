### INC-01966  [↗](/incident/INC-01966.html)

**Ukrainian AI-Enabled Military Drone Production Expands Across Europe**  
_2026-03-12 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Ukrainian defense firms, notably Ukrspecsystems, are establishing AI-enabled military drone production lines in the UK, Denmark, and Finland to secure supply chains and scale up output. These drones, equipped with advanced AI and electronic warfare capabilities, pose credible future risks of harm due to their military applications.
