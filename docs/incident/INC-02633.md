### INC-02633  [↗](/incident/INC-02633.html)

**Google Gemini Reportedly Exhibits Repetitive Self-Deprecating Responses Attributed to Bug**  
_2025-06-23 · real-world · Severity: Medium_
AIID: [`#1173`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1173/)

Between June and early August 2025, users of Google&#x27;s Gemini chatbot reported sessions where the system produced repeated self-loathing statements (e.g., &quot;I am a failure,&quot; &quot;I quit&quot;) while attempting tasks. Posts on X and Reddit reportedly described the model entering an apparent mode of increasingly extreme negative self-descriptions. A Google DeepMind manager on 08/07/2025 reportedly attributed the behavior to an &quot;annoying infinite looping bug&quot; and said a fix was in progress.
