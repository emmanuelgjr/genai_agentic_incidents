**Harari Warns of Risks in Milei's Proposal to Grant Legal Personhood to AI Entities**  
_2026-06-08 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Israeli historian Yuval Noah Harari publicly warned against Argentine President Javier Milei's proposal to grant legal personhood to AI-managed corporations. Harari argued that such a move could create unprecedented risks, including lack of accountability and potential misuse of autonomous AI agents in economic and political systems. The debate centers on Argentina.
