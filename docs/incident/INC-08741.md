### INC-08741  [↗](/incident/INC-08741.html)

**Spring AI has a VectorStore FilterExpression Converter injection**  
_2026-04 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: High_

CVEs: `CVE-2026-40967`
CWEs: `CWE-94`
CVSS: **8.6** — `CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:U/C:H/I:L/A:L`

In Spring AI, various FilterExpressionConverter implementations accept a filter expression object and translate them to specific vector store query languages. In several cases, keys and values are not properly escaped, leading to the ability to alter the query. Affected versions: Spring AI: 1.0.0 - 1.0.5 (fixed in 1.0.6), 1.1.0 - 1.1.4 (fixed in 1.1.5)

**Affected:** maven/org.springframework.ai:spring-ai-vector-store, maven/org.springframework.ai:spring-ai-vector-store  
**Attack vector:** `other`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM03`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI04`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-6.1`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0010`
