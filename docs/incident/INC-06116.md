### INC-06116  [↗](/incident/INC-06116.html)

**TikTok Updates Privacy Policy to Collect Biometric Data Using AI**  
_2021-06-07 · real-world · Severity: High_

TikTok updated its US privacy policy to allow collection of users' biometric data, including faceprints and voiceprints, likely using AI technologies. The vague policy and lack of clear user consent raise significant privacy and legal concerns, though no direct harm has yet been reported.
