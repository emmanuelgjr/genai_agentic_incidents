### INC-01576  [↗](/incident/INC-01576.html)

**OpenAI Faces Lawsuit Over ChatGPT Data Sharing With Meta and Google**  
_2026-05-14 · real-world · Severity: High_

OpenAI is facing a class-action lawsuit in California alleging it embedded Meta's Facebook Pixel and Google Analytics in ChatGPT, resulting in users' sensitive queries and personal data being shared with Meta and Google without consent. The suit claims this violates U.S. and California privacy laws.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`
