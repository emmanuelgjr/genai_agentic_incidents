### INC-12339  [↗](/incident/INC-12339.html)

**Exposure of Sensitive Information to an Unauthorized Actor in Jenkins**  
_2022-05 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: High_

CVEs: `CVE-2017-1000393`, `CVE-2017-1000398`, `CVE-2018-1000169`, `CVE-2018-1000862`, `CVE-2018-1999046`
CWEs: `CWE-200`, `CWE-78`
CVSS: **4.3** — `CVSS:3.0/AV:N/AC:L/PR:L/UI:N/S:U/C:L/I:N/A:N`

A exposure of sensitive information vulnerability exists in Jenkins 2.137 and earlier, 2.121.2 and earlier in Computer.java that allows attackers With Overall/Read permission to access the connection log for any agent.

**Affected:** maven/org.jenkins-ci.main:jenkins-core, maven/org.jenkins-ci.main:jenkins-core  
**Attack vector:** `info-disclosure`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM03`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI02`, `ASI04`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-6.1`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.10`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0010`, `AML.T0024`, `AML.T0053`, `AML.T0057`
