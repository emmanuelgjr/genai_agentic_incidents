**US Commerce Department Criticized for Failing to Control AI Tech Exports to China**  
_2021-06-01 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

A congressional report accuses the US Commerce Department of failing to implement required export controls on sensitive technologies, including AI, potentially enabling China's military to access advanced US tech. The delay in creating a list of restricted technologies is seen as increasing national security risks.
