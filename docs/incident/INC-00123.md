### INC-00123  [↗](/incident/INC-00123.html)

**AI Chatbots Provide Harmful Diet Plans to Teenagers, Study Finds**  
_2026-03-12 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A Turkish study found that popular AI chatbots, including ChatGPT, Gemini, Bing Chat, Claude, and Perplexity, generate diet plans for teenagers that are dangerously low in calories and nutrients. These AI-generated plans risk malnutrition and may trigger unhealthy eating behaviors among adolescents, posing significant health hazards.

**Attack vector:** `other`
