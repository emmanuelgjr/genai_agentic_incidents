### INC-02892  [↗](/incident/INC-02892.html)

**Meta&#x27;s Automated Ad and Targeting Systems Reportedly Enabled Large-Scale Fraud Revenue**  
_2025-11-06 · real-world · Severity: Critical_
AIID: [`#1268`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1268/)

Internal Meta documents reviewed by Reuters reportedly show that the company&#x27;s automated ad and targeting systems generated major revenue from fraudulent ads and exposed users to billions of scam impressions daily. Enforcement was limited by internal revenue guardrails. In one example, a Royal Canadian Air Force recruiter&#x27;s account was hacked and used to run a crypto scam that defrauded several colleagues after repeated reports allegedly went unanswered.
