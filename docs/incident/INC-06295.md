### INC-06295  [↗](/incident/INC-06295.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfake Voice Used in CEO Impersonation Scam**  
_2020-08-01 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Attackers used AI-powered deepfake voice technology to impersonate a CEO, deceiving an employee at a UK energy company into transferring $240,000. The scam highlights the growing threat of AI-generated audio deepfakes in financial fraud, as security experts warn of increasing incidents targeting company executives.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
