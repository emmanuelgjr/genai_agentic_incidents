### INC-06401  [↗](/incident/INC-06401.html)

**Boeing 737 Max MCAS AI System Linked to Fatal Crashes and Regulatory Misconduct**  
_2020-02-21 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Boeing's automated MCAS flight control system, classified as AI, was central to two fatal 737 Max crashes. Investigations allege Boeing employees, including the chief technical pilot, misled regulators about MCAS's safety, leading to administrative suspensions and a federal criminal probe into the company's conduct.
