**Pentagon Plans AI-Driven Social Media Surveillance of Military Personnel**  
_2021-05-18 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Pentagon, led by adviser Bishop Garrison, is developing an AI-based program to continuously monitor military personnel's social media for extremist content. The pilot, using private firms and keyword searches, raises concerns about potential violations of privacy and free speech due to possible misclassification and overreach.
