**Germany Plans to Deploy AI in Fight Against Organized Crime**  
_2026-02-25 · real-world · Severity: High_

German ministries announced plans to modernize law enforcement by integrating AI systems to analyze data and identify suspects in organized crime, money laundering, and drug cases. The initiative aims to enhance cooperation between customs and federal police, but no AI-related harm or incidents have been reported.
