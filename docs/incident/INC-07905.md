### INC-07905  [↗](/incident/INC-07905.html)

**AI-Powered Cyberattack Compromises Nine Government Agencies**  
_2026-05-25 · real-world · Severity: High_

Between December 2025 and February 2026, a single hacker used commercial AI tools to automate over 5,000 commands, successfully breaching nine government agencies. The attack, analyzed by Check Point Software, marks a shift to routine, AI-driven cybercrime, highlighting significant vulnerabilities in current cybersecurity defenses.
