**Italy Deploys AI Algorithm for Tax Evasion Detection, Raising Privacy Concerns**  
_2020-01-19 · real-world · Severity: High_

Starting April, Italian tax authorities will use a powerful AI algorithm to analyze bank accounts and financial transactions to identify potential tax evaders. While the system aims for selective enforcement, privacy advocates and regulators warn of risks to personal data protection and possible limitations on taxpayer rights.
