### INC-07884  [↗](/incident/INC-07884.html)

**Journalists Detained in San Luis Potosí for AI-Generated Content**  
_2026-05-24 · real-world · Severity: High_

In San Luis Potosí, Mexico, several journalists and communicators were detained and investigated under the 'Ley IA' for allegedly using AI-generated images and videos. Human rights organizations denounced these actions as arbitrary, targeting critical voices and violating freedom of expression and press rights.
