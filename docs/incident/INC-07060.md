### INC-07060  [↗](/incident/INC-07060.html)

**Ultrasonic 'Surfing Attacks' Exploit AI Voice Assistants for Unauthorized Access**  
_2020-02-28 · real-world · Severity: High_

Researchers from Washington University in St. Louis demonstrated that ultrasonic waves can activate AI voice assistants like Siri and Google Assistant on smartphones, enabling attackers to make calls, take photos, or read texts without the owner's knowledge. This vulnerability allows privacy breaches and unauthorized access using inexpensive hardware.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`, `ASI09`
