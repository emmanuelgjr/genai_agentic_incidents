**AI Bots Force Digg to Shut Down Open Beta and Downsize Team**  
_2026-03-13 · real-world · Severity: High_

Digg's attempt at a platform revival was derailed by a surge of sophisticated AI-driven bots that manipulated posts, comments, and voting, undermining user trust and engagement. Despite banning tens of thousands of accounts and deploying anti-bot measures, Digg shut down its open beta and significantly downsized its team.
