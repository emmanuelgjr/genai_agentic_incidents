### INC-07076  [↗](/incident/INC-07076.html)

**US Army Deploys AI-Enabled Smart Glasses for Combat Targeting**  
_2020-02-16 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The US Army plans to purchase 40,000 AI-powered smart glasses, developed by Microsoft, to enhance soldiers' battlefield awareness and enable precise targeting of enemies without direct visual contact. While no harm has occurred yet, the deployment of such AI systems poses significant risks of misuse or malfunction in military operations.
