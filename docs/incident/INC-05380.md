### INC-05380  [↗](/incident/INC-05380.html)

**AI Customer Service Bots Deliberately Obstruct Access to Human Support**  
_2021-04-14 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Multiple reports reveal that AI-powered customer service chatbots are intentionally configured to delay or prevent users from reaching human agents, causing widespread frustration and denying timely assistance. This practice harms consumer rights and service quality, particularly affecting vulnerable groups like the elderly.
