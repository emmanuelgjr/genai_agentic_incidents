### INC-07074  [↗](/incident/INC-07074.html)

**US and Russia Advance AI-Controlled Fighter Jet Combat Trials**  
_2020-09-11 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The US and Russia are developing and testing AI systems to control fighter jets in air combat scenarios. The US, led by DARPA, plans AI-operated dogfights with safety pilots, while Russia explores AI-coordinated swarm tactics. No harm has occurred yet, but these military AI applications pose significant future risks.
