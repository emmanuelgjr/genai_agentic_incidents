### INC-02280  [↗](/incident/INC-02280.html)

**Alleged Fraudulent Prompts via AIXBT Dashboard Led Purported AI Trading Agent to Transfer 55.5 ETH from Simulacrum Wallet**  
_2025-03-18 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1003`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1003/)

A reported hacker attack allegedly compromised the autonomous AI crypto bot AIXBT, purportedly resulting in the theft of 55.5 ETH (approximately $106,200). The attacker is reported to have infiltrated the secure dashboard of the AIXBT autonomous system at 2:00 AM UTC on March 18, 2025, and allegedly queued two fraudulent prompts that instructed the AI agent to transfer funds from its simulacrum wallet.

**Attack vector:** `other`
