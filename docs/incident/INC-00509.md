### INC-00509  [↗](/incident/INC-00509.html)

**AI-Generated Videos Cause Misinformation and Legal Violations in China**  
_2026-03-15 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI systems have been used to generate fake videos and images, spreading misinformation about geopolitical conflicts and altering film content. These AI-modified videos infringe intellectual property and portrait rights, causing harm to rights holders and public trust. The incidents have prompted legal and regulatory scrutiny in China.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM04`, `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI06`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MAP-4.2`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0020`, `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`, `AML.T0066`
