**Romania Deploys AI-Powered Drone Interceptors Amid Ukraine Conflict**  
_2026-04-24 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Romania is deploying and testing the AI-powered Merops drone interceptor system, developed by Project Eagle, to counter escalating drone threats from the Ukraine war. The autonomous system, capable of detecting and engaging drones, is being rapidly integrated into Romania's air defenses following repeated Russian drone incursions near its border.
