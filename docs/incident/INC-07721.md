**Ukraine Deploys AI-Driven Drone Swarms in Conflict with Russia**  
_2026-05-15 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Ukraine has developed and deployed AI-powered drone swarms capable of autonomous target identification and attacks, significantly impacting military operations against Russia. These systems have been used for reconnaissance and precision strikes, causing destruction of property and military assets, marking a shift in modern warfare tactics.

**Attack vector:** `other`
