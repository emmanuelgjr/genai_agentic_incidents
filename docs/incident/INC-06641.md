### INC-06641  [↗](/incident/INC-06641.html)

**Global Concerns Rise Over AI Facial Recognition Surveillance and Regulation**  
_2020-04-13 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI-powered facial recognition systems are being adopted by law enforcement and institutions worldwide, raising significant concerns about privacy, human rights, and potential misuse. While some regions, like Washington State, are introducing regulations, critics argue these measures are insufficient, highlighting the urgent need for robust oversight to prevent harm.
