### INC-06273  [↗](/incident/INC-06273.html)

**AI-Driven Information Warfare Tool Repurposed for US Political Campaigns**  
_2020-05-02 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

A Democratic-aligned PAC, advised by Gen. Stanley McChrystal, is deploying an AI-powered tool—originally funded by DARPA to counter ISIS propaganda—to map and influence social media discussions about President Trump. The system boosts selected counter-narratives via influencers, raising concerns about AI-enabled manipulation of political discourse and public opinion.
