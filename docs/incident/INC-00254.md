**AI Voice Assistant Malfunction Nearly Causes Child Injury in Car Seat Incident**  
_2026-04-30 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

In China, a car's AI voice assistant ('Xiaoyi') misinterpreted a command and began folding the front passenger seat while a child was seated, nearly causing injury. The incident raised concerns about the safety of AI-controlled seat mechanisms, sensor limitations, and the effectiveness of anti-pinch features in vehicles.
