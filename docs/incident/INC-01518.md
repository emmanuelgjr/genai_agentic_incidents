**NATO Orders AI-Enabled Parrot Micro-Drones for Military Use**  
_2026-03-17 · real-world · Severity: High_

French company Parrot has received its first NATO orders for AI-powered ANAFI UKR micro-drones, with deliveries starting in early 2026. The drones, intended for military surveillance and defense, are being supplied to Finland and another undisclosed defense client, raising concerns about potential future risks from autonomous military AI systems.
