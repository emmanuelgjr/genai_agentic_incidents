### INC-00459  [↗](/incident/INC-00459.html)

**AI-Generated Fake Photo Causes Public Outcry and Legal Action in Greece**  
_2026-04-30 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

AI-generated fake photos depicting TV presenter Katerina Kainourgiou and her newborn were circulated online, leading to public confusion and reputational harm. Kainourgiou denounced the incident and announced legal action, while journalist Giorgos Liagas criticized the creators and spreaders of such misleading AI content on Greek television.
