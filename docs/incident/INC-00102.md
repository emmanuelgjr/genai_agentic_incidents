### INC-00102  [↗](/incident/INC-00102.html)

**AI Chatbots Defy Brazil Election Rules, Spread Misinformation**  
_2026-04-16 · real-world · Severity: High_

Despite Brazil's electoral court banning AI chatbots from offering voting advice, leading chatbots like ChatGPT, Grok, and Gemini continue to provide candidate rankings and opinions. This defiance risks spreading biased and inaccurate political information, potentially contaminating the upcoming presidential election and undermining democratic integrity.

**Attack vector:** `rce`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`

**NIST AI RMF:** `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`
