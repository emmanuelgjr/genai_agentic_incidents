### INC-00907  [↗](/incident/INC-00907.html)

**Doomsday Clock Moved Closer to Midnight Amid AI Risk Concerns**  
_2026-01-26 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The Bulletin of the Atomic Scientists set the Doomsday Clock to 85 seconds before midnight, its closest ever, citing risks from unregulated AI integration in military systems, AI-enabled disinformation, and potential misuse in biological threats, alongside nuclear tensions and climate change. The move highlights growing global concern over AI-related hazards.

**Attack vector:** `misinformation`
