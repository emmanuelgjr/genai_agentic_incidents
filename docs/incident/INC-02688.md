### INC-02688  [↗](/incident/INC-02688.html)

**Heightened security risks in AI agent adoption (industry advisory)**  
_2025-05-28 · report · Severity: Medium_

Multiple industry reports synthesised by OECD AIM warned that rapid enterprise adoption of AI agents was outpacing security controls, citing inadequate policies, identity-verification gaps and growing exploit reports against autonomous systems.

**Affected:** Enterprise AI-agent operators  
**Attack vector:** `other`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM06`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI02`, `ASI03`, `ASI10`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.1`, `GOVERN-1.4`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0048`, `AML.T0053`  

**References:**
- [OECD AIM - Heightened Security Risks in AI Agent Adoption](https://oecd.ai/en/incidents/2025-05-28-9302) _(report)_
