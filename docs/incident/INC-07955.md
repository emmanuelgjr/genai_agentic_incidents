**AI-Generated Beauty Standards Drive Risky Cosmetic Surgeries; Microsoft Faces Massive AI Bill**  
_2026-05-28 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

AI chatbots are generating idealized facial images, prompting individuals to seek cosmetic surgeries that pose serious health risks, as warned by surgeons. Separately, Microsoft incurred a $500 million bill from excessive use of Anthropic Claude AI, leading to financial harm and subscription cancellations. Both incidents highlight AI's direct and indirect harms.
