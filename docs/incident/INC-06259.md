### INC-06259  [↗](/incident/INC-06259.html)

**AI Systems Exhibit Racial Bias, Leading to Discriminatory Outcomes**  
_2020-06-20 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Multiple AI systems, including Microsoft's Tay chatbot, facial recognition tools, and beauty contest algorithms, have demonstrated racist biases, resulting in offensive outputs and discriminatory decisions. These incidents have caused harm to minority groups and reignited concerns about AI-driven racial discrimination amid global anti-racism protests.
