### INC-06797  [↗](/incident/INC-06797.html)

**MIT's TextFooler Exposes Vulnerabilities in Leading AI Language Models**  
_2020-02-07 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

MIT researchers developed TextFooler, a system that generates adversarial text to deceive advanced natural language processing models like Google's BERT, Alexa, and Siri. By swapping key words with synonyms, TextFooler exposes significant vulnerabilities, highlighting the risk of AI systems being manipulated, though no real-world harm has yet occurred.
