**MTA Urges Apple to Improve Face ID Amid Mask-Related Health Risks**  
_2020-08-10 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

New York's Metropolitan Transportation Authority asked Apple to enhance its Face ID technology, as riders have been removing masks to unlock iPhones, potentially increasing COVID-19 transmission risk. Despite recent updates, the AI system's limitations pose a public health hazard, prompting calls for accelerated improvements.
