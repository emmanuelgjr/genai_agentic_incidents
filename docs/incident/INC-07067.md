### INC-07067  [↗](/incident/INC-07067.html)

**Urban Canyons Pose GPS Signal Risks for Autonomous Vehicles**  
_2020-07-14 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Experts warn that AI-driven autonomous vehicles relying on GPS navigation face potential safety and operational hazards in cities with tall buildings, known as 'urban canyons.' Signal loss or degradation could cause vehicles to stop, become confused, or behave unpredictably, highlighting a credible risk of harm in dense urban environments.
