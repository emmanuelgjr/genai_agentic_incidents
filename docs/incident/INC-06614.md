**FBI Investigates NSO Group's Pegasus Spyware for Hacking and Surveillance Abuses**  
_2020-01-30 · real-world · Severity: High_

The FBI has been investigating Israeli firm NSO Group's Pegasus spyware since at least 2017 for its role in unauthorized hacking and surveillance of US residents, companies, and governments. The AI-powered spyware has been linked to privacy violations, data breaches, and human rights abuses, prompting concerns over its misuse.
