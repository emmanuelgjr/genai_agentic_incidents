### INC-00767  [↗](/incident/INC-00767.html)

**China Plans AI-Powered Robots and Data Centers for Space Missions**  
_2026-01-25 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Chinese companies are preparing to deploy the world's first humanoid robot astronaut, PM01, equipped with autonomous AI, for space missions. Simultaneously, China plans to build AI-powered data centers in space, intensifying competition with SpaceX. These developments raise potential future risks associated with autonomous AI systems in hazardous space environments.

**Attack vector:** `other`
