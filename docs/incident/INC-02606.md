### INC-02606  [↗](/incident/INC-02606.html)

**GhostGPT: AI tool for cybercrime**  
_2025-01-23 · threat-report · Severity: High_

Abnormal Security reported on GhostGPT, an uncensored LLM service marketed to criminals for generating evasive malware, business-email-compromise lures and phishing kits, illustrating the productization of jailbroken models on Telegram.

**Affected:** Phishing and malware victims  
**Attack vector:** `jailbreak`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM01`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI10`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048`, `AML.T0051`, `AML.T0054`, `AML.T0058`

- [OECD AIM - GhostGPT: AI Tool for Cybercrime](https://oecd.ai/en/incidents/2025-01-23-cf19) _(threat-report)_
