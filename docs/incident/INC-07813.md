### INC-07813  [↗](/incident/INC-07813.html)

**Brazilian Superior Court Investigates AI Prompt Injection Fraud in Legal Petitions**  
_2026-05-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Brazilian Superior Tribunal de Justiça (STJ) launched investigations after detecting prompt injection attacks in legal petitions, where hidden AI commands were used to bypass filters in the court's AI system, STJ Logos. The suspected fraud, allegedly by lawyers, threatens judicial process integrity. Technical safeguards are in place, and sanctions are being considered.

**Attack vector:** `prompt-injection`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM01`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI01`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0051`, `AML.T0058`
