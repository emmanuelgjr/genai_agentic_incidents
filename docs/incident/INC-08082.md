**AI-Driven Cyberattacks Escalate, Lowering Barriers for Hackers**  
_2026-06-03 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Anthropic's analysis of 832 banned accounts (March 2025–March 2026) reveals a surge in AI-assisted cyberattacks, with AI enabling less skilled actors to perform advanced techniques like malware development, lateral movement, and account discovery. This shift has increased cyber risk, disrupted critical infrastructure, and contributed to major hacks, especially in the crypto sector.

**Attack vector:** `malware`
