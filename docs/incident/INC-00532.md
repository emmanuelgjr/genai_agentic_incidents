### INC-00532  [↗](/incident/INC-00532.html)

**AI-Powered API Attacks Cause Disruption and Losses Across Asia-Pacific**  
_2026-04-01 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI-powered bots and adversaries are increasingly targeting APIs in Asia-Pacific, leading to a surge in sophisticated attacks that disrupt digital services and cause financial and operational harm. Security maturity lags behind rapid AI adoption, exposing critical infrastructure, especially in sectors like retail and finance.
