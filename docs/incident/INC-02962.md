### INC-02962  [↗](/incident/INC-02962.html)

**Norwegian parliament bans Chinese chatbot DeepSeek over security concerns**  
_2025-02-13 · regulatory · Severity: Medium_

Norway's Storting banned use of the Chinese-developed DeepSeek chatbot for parliamentary work, citing data-protection, sovereignty and national-security concerns. Several allied governments imposed similar bans within weeks.

**Affected:** Norwegian parliament  
**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`, `ASI04`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.1`, `GOVERN-1.4`, `GOVERN-6.1`, `MEASURE-2.10`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0010`, `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0024`, `AML.T0057`
