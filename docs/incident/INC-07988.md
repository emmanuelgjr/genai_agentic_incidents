**Autonomous Humanoid Combat Robots Tested in Ukraine by US Startup**  
_2026-05-30 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Foundation Future Industries, a US startup linked to the Trump family, is testing autonomous humanoid robots in Ukraine's war zone. These dual-use robots, designed for military and industrial applications, are currently performing logistics tasks but are intended for future combat roles, raising concerns about AI-driven battlefield risks.
