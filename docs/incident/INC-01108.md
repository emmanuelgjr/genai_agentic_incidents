### INC-01108  [↗](/incident/INC-01108.html)

**Google Chrome Gemini AI Vulnerability Exposes Users to Surveillance and Data Theft**  
_2026-03-02 · real-world · Severity: High_

A high-severity vulnerability in Google Chrome's Gemini AI assistant allowed malicious browser extensions to exploit the AI panel's elevated privileges, enabling unauthorized access to users' cameras, microphones, local files, and sensitive data. Discovered by Palo Alto Networks' Unit 42, the flaw was patched by Google in January 2026.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM05`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`, `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.10`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0057`
