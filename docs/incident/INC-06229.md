**AI Imaging Systems Aid COVID-19 Diagnosis and Harm Reduction in China**  
_2020-04-10 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Chinese tech firms, notably Shukun Technology and Tsinghua University, rapidly developed and deployed AI-powered imaging and diagnostic systems during the COVID-19 pandemic. These systems enabled fast, accurate CT analysis, supported doctors in screening and treatment, reduced cross-infection risks, and improved patient outcomes in hospitals across China and abroad.
