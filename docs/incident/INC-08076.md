### INC-08076  [↗](/incident/INC-08076.html)

**Google Gemini AI Vulnerability Exploited via Malicious Notifications on Android**  
_2026-06-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

Security researchers from SafeBreach discovered a vulnerability in Google Gemini's Android voice assistant, where crafted notifications from apps like WhatsApp, Slack, and SMS could inject malicious prompts. This allowed attackers to manipulate Gemini, potentially leading to unauthorized actions, privacy breaches, and data exfiltration. Google has since patched the flaw.

**Attack vector:** `prompt-injection`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM01`, `LLM02`, `LLM04`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI01`, `ASI02`, `ASI06`, `ASI09`
