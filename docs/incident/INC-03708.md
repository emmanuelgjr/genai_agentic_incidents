### INC-03708  [↗](/incident/INC-03708.html)

**Deepfake ID Sales Allegedly Persist as OnlyFake Reportedly Relaunches with New Fraud Tools**  
_2024-03-01 · real-world · Severity: Critical_
AIID: [`#787`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/787/)

Researchers from Au10tix discovered the relaunch of OnlyFake, a site offering AI-generated fake IDs. Despite an earlier takedown, the site reemerged with disclaimers and new tools, including handwritten signature generation. These fakes are challenging biometric verification systems and are reportedly being used to perpetuate fraudulent activity.
