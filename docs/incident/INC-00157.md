### INC-00157  [↗](/incident/INC-00157.html)

**AI Deepfakes Used in Fraudulent Medical Product Scams in Germany**  
_2026-05-03 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Criminal networks have used AI-generated deepfake videos and audio to impersonate German doctor Eckart von Hirschhausen, promoting fake medical products online. This has led to widespread deception, financial loss, and potential health risks for victims. Despite legal action, many deepfake ads remain online, highlighting ongoing harm and regulatory challenges.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
