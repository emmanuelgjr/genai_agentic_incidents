### INC-06587  [↗](/incident/INC-06587.html)

**Facebook's AI Removes Millions of Harmful COVID-19 Misinformation Posts**  
_2020-08-20 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Facebook's AI-driven algorithms labeled 98 million COVID-19 misinformation posts and removed 7 million pieces of content that could cause physical harm between April and June. The action followed reports that Facebook's algorithm had enabled health misinformation to reach billions of views, prompting large-scale harm mitigation efforts.

**Attack vector:** `misinformation`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0058`
