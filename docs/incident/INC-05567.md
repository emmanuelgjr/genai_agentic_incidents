### INC-05567  [↗](/incident/INC-05567.html)

**Deepfakes Successfully Fool Leading Facial Recognition AI, Exposing Security Risks**  
_2021-03-05 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

South Korean researchers demonstrated that AI-generated deepfakes can reliably deceive major facial recognition APIs from Microsoft and Amazon. This vulnerability undermines the reliability of face biometrics for authentication, raising significant concerns about privacy, security, and the potential for fraud and misinformation due to AI system failures.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`

**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
