### INC-01374  [↗](/incident/INC-01374.html)

**Mass Removal of AI-Generated Music Over Copyright Violations**  
_2026-02-17 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI systems generated 13.4 million music tracks using human works without consent, leading to mass removal from platforms. This caused significant economic harm and intellectual property rights violations for music creators, sparking calls for transparency and fair compensation in Greece's creative sector.

**Attack vector:** `other`
