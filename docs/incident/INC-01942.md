**UK Fines Reddit $19.5M for Failing to Protect Children's Data and Exposing Minors to Harmful Content**  
_2026-02-24 · real-world · Severity: High_

The UK Information Commissioner's Office fined Reddit £14.5 million for failing to implement effective age verification, unlawfully processing data of children under 13, and exposing them to harmful content. Reddit's reliance on self-declared ages and lack of robust safeguards led to violations of children's privacy rights.
