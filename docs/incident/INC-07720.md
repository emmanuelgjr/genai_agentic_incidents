### INC-07720  [↗](/incident/INC-07720.html)

**AI-Generated Obscene Images Used for Blackmail in Uttar Pradesh**  
_2026-05-16 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

In Bhadohi, Uttar Pradesh, a cyber cafe operator and his brother used AI to create obscene images of a woman from her social media photos, then blackmailed her for money. The accused extorted Rs 50,000 and threatened further exposure, with police investigating possible additional victims.
