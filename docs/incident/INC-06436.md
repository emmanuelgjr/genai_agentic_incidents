### INC-06436  [↗](/incident/INC-06436.html)

**China's AI-Driven DNA Surveillance Raises Human Rights Concerns**  
_2020-07-24 · real-world · Severity: High_

Chinese authorities have collected DNA samples from millions of men and boys nationwide, using AI systems to build a vast genetic database for surveillance. This enables tracking individuals and relatives, even without suspicion of crime, leading to widespread human rights and privacy violations through AI-enabled genetic monitoring.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`  

**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-4.1`, `MEASURE-2.10`
