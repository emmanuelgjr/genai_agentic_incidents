### INC-07945  [↗](/incident/INC-07945.html)

**AI-Generated Images Fuel Surge in Insurance Fraud in the US**  
_2026-05-27 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Generative AI tools are increasingly used to create or alter images supporting fraudulent insurance claims in the US, making fraud easier and more widespread. This misuse has led to significant financial harm for insurers and consumers, with experts warning of a growing threat as AI-generated evidence becomes harder to detect.
