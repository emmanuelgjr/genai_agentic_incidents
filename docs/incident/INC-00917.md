### INC-00917  [↗](/incident/INC-00917.html)

**Dutch Government Warns of AI Model Mythos' Cybersecurity Risks**  
_2026-04-12 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Dutch National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) warns that Anthropic's AI model Mythos can autonomously identify and exploit software vulnerabilities, potentially accelerating and automating cyberattacks. Mythos' advanced capabilities have raised concerns among government and financial sectors, prompting restricted access and calls for improved cybersecurity measures in the Netherlands.

**Attack vector:** `other`
