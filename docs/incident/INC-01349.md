### INC-01349  [↗](/incident/INC-01349.html)

**LLM-Driven Attack Compromises AWS Administrator Privileges in 8 Minutes**  
_2026-03-18 · real-world · Severity: High_

Sysdig observed an attack where large language models (LLMs) were used to automate intrusion into AWS environments, resulting in the compromise of administrator privileges within eight minutes and affecting 19 AWS principals. This incident highlights the security risks posed by malicious use of AI systems.

**Attack vector:** `other`
