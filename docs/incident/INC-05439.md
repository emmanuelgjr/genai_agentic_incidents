### INC-05439  [↗](/incident/INC-05439.html)

**AI-Powered Smart Doorbells Ruled to Infringe Neighbor Privacy in China**  
_2021-03-01 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI-enabled smart doorbells with features like facial recognition and automated video recording have led to privacy violations in China. Courts ruled that such devices, when aimed at neighbors’ doors, infringe on personal privacy, ordering their removal and deletion of footage. The incident highlights regulatory gaps in smart home surveillance technology.
