### INC-05805  [↗](/incident/INC-05805.html)

**Portuguese University Ordered to Suspend AI Exam Surveillance Software Over Data Protection Violations**  
_2021-05-28 · real-world · Severity: High_

The University of Minho used AI-powered Respondus software to monitor students during online exams, analyzing biometric and behavioral data. Portugal's data protection authority (CNPD) found this surveillance excessive and unlawful, violating GDPR principles and students' rights, leading to the software's suspension and an order to delete collected personal data.

**Attack vector:** `other`
