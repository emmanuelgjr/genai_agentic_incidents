### INC-08034  [↗](/incident/INC-08034.html)

**Berlin Plans AI-Powered Video Surveillance at Key Locations**  
_2026-06-01 · real-world · Severity: High_

Berlin authorities plan to pilot AI-supported video surveillance at sites including the Jewish Museum, Red City Hall, and crime hotspots. The AI system will analyze movement patterns to detect unusual incidents, raising concerns about potential privacy violations and rights risks, though no harm has yet occurred.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`  

**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-4.1`, `MEASURE-2.10`
