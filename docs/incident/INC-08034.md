**Merlin Advances AI Autonomy for Military and Commercial Aircraft**  
_2026-06-01 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

US-based Merlin has completed a critical design review for its AI-powered autonomy system for C-130J military aircraft and is developing similar technology for large commercial and cargo planes. While no harm has occurred, the deployment of AI-controlled flight systems raises potential future safety and operational risks.
