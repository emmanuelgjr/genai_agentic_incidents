**AI Model Evo2 Raises Bioethics Concerns Over DNA Editing Capabilities**  
_2026-03-04 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Evo2, a generative AI developed by Arc Institute, Nvidia, and US universities, can analyze and rewrite entire DNA sequences, identify disease-causing mutations, and design new genomes. While promising for genetic research, its potential misuse—such as creating dangerous organisms—has prompted ethical concerns and safeguards to prevent harm.
