### INC-05658  [↗](/incident/INC-05658.html)

**Global Coalition Demands Ban on Harmful Biometric Surveillance AI**  
_2021-06-07 · real-world · Severity: High_

Over 175 civil society groups worldwide have called for a global ban on facial recognition and biometric surveillance technologies, citing documented harms such as privacy violations, discrimination, and suppression of civil liberties. The coalition highlights abuses in multiple countries and warns that current regulations risk enabling further AI-driven rights violations.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`
