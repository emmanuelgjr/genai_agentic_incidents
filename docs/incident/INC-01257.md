### INC-01257  [↗](/incident/INC-01257.html)

**Italian Court Rules AI-Driven Employee Dismissal Lawful**  
_2026-02-26 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The Rome Labor Court ruled that the dismissal of a graphic designer, whose role became redundant due to the adoption of AI tools during a company reorganization, was lawful. This marks one of Italy's first legal decisions explicitly addressing AI's impact on employment rights.
