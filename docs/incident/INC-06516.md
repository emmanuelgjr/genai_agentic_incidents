### INC-06516  [↗](/incident/INC-06516.html)

**Dubai's Al Habtoor Partners with Mobileye to Deploy Self-Driving Robo-Taxis**  
_2020-09-23 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Al Habtoor Group has partnered with Mobileye, Intel's autonomous driving division, to deploy self-driving robo-taxis in Dubai. The initiative involves equipping 1,000 vehicles with Mobileye's AI technology for mapping and data collection, with plans for public trials and future autonomous taxi services, raising potential future AI safety concerns.
