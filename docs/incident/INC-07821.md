**Ukraine Deploys AI-Guided Naval Drone to Intercept Russian Attacks**  
_2026-05-20 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Ukraine has unveiled the Katran X1.2, an unmanned naval platform equipped with AI-guided interceptor drones. Developed with MAC HUB and military units, the system autonomously detects and neutralizes Russian Shahed drones used in attacks on Ukrainian cities, marking a significant AI-driven intervention in active warfare.
