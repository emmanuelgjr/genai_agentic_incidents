**Deployment of AI-Powered Surveillance Radars on Minas Gerais Highways**  
_2026-05-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Minas Gerais government is installing 1,300 AI-powered surveillance radars on state highways. These systems use real-time automatic license plate recognition and behavioral pattern analysis to detect stolen vehicles, unusual routes, and suspicious activities, raising potential future risks related to privacy and data misuse.
