### INC-00146  [↗](/incident/INC-00146.html)

**AI Deepfake Scam Targets Hospital Director in Taiwan**  
_2026-05-12 · real-world · Severity: High_

Fraudsters used AI deepfake technology to create convincing fake videos of Changhua Christian Hospital director Chen Mu-Kuan, falsely endorsing medical products. The deepfakes misled both staff and the public, causing financial and health risks. The hospital is pursuing legal action to protect its reputation and public health.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
