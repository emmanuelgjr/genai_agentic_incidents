### INC-06913  [↗](/incident/INC-06913.html)

**Singapore Deploys AI Facial Verification in National ID Scheme, Raising Privacy Concerns**  
_2020-09-25 · real-world · Severity: High_

Singapore has become the first country to integrate AI-powered facial verification into its national identity scheme, SingPass, granting access to government and private services. While officials claim privacy safeguards and consent, privacy advocates warn of potential rights violations and intrusive surveillance risks for over four million residents.
