### INC-06338  [↗](/incident/INC-06338.html)

**Algorithmic Risk Assessment in Probation Leads to Unfair Treatment and Rights Violations**  
_2020-02-06 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

In Philadelphia and other regions, AI-driven risk assessment algorithms are used to determine probation conditions, often without individuals' knowledge. These systems have led to impersonal, opaque, and potentially biased decisions, restricting freedoms and violating rights, as seen in the case of Darnell Gates and others.

**Attack vector:** `algorithmic-bias`
