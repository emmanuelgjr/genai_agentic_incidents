### INC-07865  [↗](/incident/INC-07865.html)

**Turkey to Deploy AI-Powered Surveillance for School and Exam Security**  
_2026-05-22 · real-world · Severity: High_

Turkey's Ministry of Education announced plans to implement AI-supported image processing camera systems and card-based entry for enhanced security in schools and during the national high school entrance exam (LGS). While no harm has occurred yet, the AI surveillance raises potential privacy and misuse concerns.

**Attack vector:** `other`
