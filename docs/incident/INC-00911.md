### INC-00911  [↗](/incident/INC-00911.html)

**Drug Cartels Use Social Media Algorithms to Recruit Mexican Youth**  
_2026-02-21 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Drug cartels in Mexico, notably the Jalisco New Generation Cartel, exploit social media algorithms to target and recruit minors. By leveraging AI-driven recommendation systems and digital language, these groups efficiently reach vulnerable youth, leading to increased criminal recruitment and harm to individuals and communities.
