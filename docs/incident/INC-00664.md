### INC-00664  [↗](/incident/INC-00664.html)

**Autonomous Combat Drone Competition at TEKNOFEST Highlights AI Risks**  
_2026-01-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

At TEKNOFEST in Turkey, a competition led by BAYKAR features autonomous combat drones using AI for perception, decision-making, and maneuvering in simulated battle scenarios. While no harm has occurred, the development and testing of AI-enabled weaponized drones raise concerns about potential future risks if deployed in real-world combat.
