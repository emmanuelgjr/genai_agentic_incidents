### INC-06923  [↗](/incident/INC-06923.html)

**South Korea Plans AI Surveillance Cameras for Crime Prediction in Seoul**  
_2020-01-02 · real-world · Severity: High_

South Korea's Seocho district and ETRI plan to install 3,000 AI-powered cameras in Seoul to analyze behavior, appearance, and context to predict potential crimes and alert authorities. While aiming to prevent crime, the system raises concerns about privacy, wrongful suspicion, and potential misuse, but no harm has yet occurred.
