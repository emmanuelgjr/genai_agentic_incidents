### INC-00745  [↗](/incident/INC-00745.html)

**ChatGPT and Gemini Spread Misinformation from Fabricated Blog**  
_2026-02-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

A BBC journalist created a fake blog post with invented facts, which was quickly cited as truth by AI systems ChatGPT, Google Gemini, and AI Overviews. The incident exposed a vulnerability where these AI tools disseminated false information to users, highlighting risks of unverified content influencing AI-generated responses.

**Attack vector:** `rce`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
