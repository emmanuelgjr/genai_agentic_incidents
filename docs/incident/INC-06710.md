**Indiana City Plans AI Surveillance for COVID-19 Contact Tracing Raises Privacy Concerns**  
_2020-10-02 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Crown Point, Indiana, is considering installing AI-powered facial recognition and analytical cameras downtown to monitor social distancing and aid COVID-19 contact tracing. While not yet deployed, the proposed system raises concerns about potential privacy violations, misidentification, and bias, highlighting risks associated with public AI surveillance.
