### INC-00567  [↗](/incident/INC-00567.html)

**AI-Powered WaTracker App Circumvents WhatsApp Privacy Controls**  
_2026-03-01 · real-world · Severity: High_

iToolab's WaTracker 1.3.3 uses AI to intercept and store WhatsApp's 'view once' media, allowing repeated access to photos, videos, and messages intended to be ephemeral. This undermines user privacy and violates WhatsApp's intended protections, resulting in ongoing privacy and rights violations. The incident is centered in New York, USA.
