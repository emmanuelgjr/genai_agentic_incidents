### INC-00130  [↗](/incident/INC-00130.html)

**AI Clinical Decision Support System Reduces Vascular Events in Stroke Patients**  
_2026-03-19 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A cluster-randomized clinical trial across 77 hospitals in China found that an AI-powered clinical decision support system (CDSS) for stroke care led to a 27% reduction in new vascular events and improved long-term outcomes. The AI tool integrates imaging analysis and treatment recommendations, demonstrating significant health benefits over conventional care.
