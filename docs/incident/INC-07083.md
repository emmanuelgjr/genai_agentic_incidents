**US Considers TikTok Ban Over AI-Driven Security and Misinformation Concerns**  
_2020-07-08 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The US government, citing national security and misinformation risks linked to TikTok's AI-driven content and data practices, is considering banning the app. Officials allege potential misuse of user data and propaganda, while China and TikTok deny wrongdoing, highlighting escalating tensions over AI-enabled platforms.
