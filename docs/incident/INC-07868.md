**Waymo Suspends Robotaxi Services After AI Malfunctions Cause Safety Incidents**  
_2026-05-22 · real-world · Severity: High_

Waymo suspended its autonomous taxi services on US freeways and in several cities after AI-driven vehicles drove into flooded roads, stalled, and caused traffic disruptions. Incidents included a car trapping a woman for an hour and another striking a child. The company is updating its software to address these safety issues.
