### INC-07868  [↗](/incident/INC-07868.html)

**AI Misused for Terror Engineering in Delhi Red Fort Blast**  
_2026-05-22 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The National Investigation Agency (NIA) revealed that accused linked to Al-Qaida used ChatGPT, an AI platform, to obtain instructions for making rockets and explosive mixtures, enabling the fabrication and testing of IEDs. This misuse directly contributed to a deadly vehicle-borne blast in Delhi, causing fatalities and injuries.
