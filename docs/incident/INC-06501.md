### INC-06501  [↗](/incident/INC-06501.html)

**Deepfake Audio Used in Financial Fraud Raises Concerns Over AI Risks**  
_2020-11-16 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Experts warn that AI-generated deepfakes, particularly audio, have been used to successfully defraud a CEO, highlighting the technology's potential for financial harm. While large-scale incidents remain rare, concerns are growing about deepfakes undermining trust in financial institutions and biometric security systems.
