**AI Traffic Cameras in Western Australia Overturn $1 Million in Fines After Accuracy Concerns**  
_2026-04-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI-powered traffic cameras in Western Australia issued over 53,000 seatbelt fines, but more than $1 million in penalties were overturned after 60% of challenged fines were found unjustified. The incident raised concerns about the accuracy and fairness of AI enforcement, prompting formal reviews and public scrutiny.
