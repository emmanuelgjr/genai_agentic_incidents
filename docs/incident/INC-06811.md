### INC-06811  [↗](/incident/INC-06811.html)

**Nevada Approves Motional to Test Fully Driverless Cars in Las Vegas**  
_2020-11-17 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Motional, a Hyundai-Aptiv joint venture, has received approval from Nevada to test fully autonomous vehicles without human safety drivers on Las Vegas roads. While no harm has occurred, the deployment of these AI-driven vehicles introduces potential safety risks as they operate without direct human oversight.
