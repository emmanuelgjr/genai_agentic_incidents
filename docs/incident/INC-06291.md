**AI-Enabled Turkish Drones Cause Mass Casualties in Syrian Conflict**  
_2020-03-01 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Turkish Armed Forces used AI-enabled armed drones (SİHA), produced by Baykar, to destroy over 600 targets and neutralize thousands of enemy soldiers in Syria following attacks on Turkish troops. The incident highlights the direct and large-scale harm caused by autonomous AI systems in modern warfare.
