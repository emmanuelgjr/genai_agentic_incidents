**ByteDance Settles TikTok US Privacy Lawsuit Over AI-Driven Data Collection**  
_2021-02-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

ByteDance agreed to pay $92 million to settle a US class-action lawsuit alleging TikTok's AI-powered data collection, including facial recognition and biometric data from minors, violated user privacy laws. The settlement addresses claims of unauthorized data extraction and tracking for profiling and advertising, with new restrictions on biometric data collection and storage.
