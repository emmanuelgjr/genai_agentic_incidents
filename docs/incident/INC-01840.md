### INC-01840  [↗](/incident/INC-01840.html)

**Tech Companies Propose Massive AI Data Centers in Space, Raising Future Risks**  
_2026-03-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

Blue Origin, SpaceX, Starcloud, and other tech firms have proposed deploying tens of thousands of satellites to create orbital AI data centers. While aiming to address terrestrial resource constraints, experts warn of potential hazards including space congestion, environmental impacts, and interference with astronomy. No harm has yet occurred; projects remain in approval stages.
