**ICE's Deployment of AI-Powered Iris Scanners Leads to Rights Violations in Immigration Enforcement**  
_2026-05-27 · real-world · Severity: High_

U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) has expanded its use of AI-driven iris scanning technology through a $25 million contract with BI2 Technologies. The deployment has directly resulted in the identification, detention, and deportation of immigrants, raising concerns about privacy violations, trauma, and human rights abuses, particularly in Chicago.
