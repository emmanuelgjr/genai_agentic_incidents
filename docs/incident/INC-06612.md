### INC-06612  [↗](/incident/INC-06612.html)

**FarEasTone Uses AI to Block Over 99% of Scam SMS, Preventing Financial Harm**  
_2020-05-12 · real-world · Severity: High_

FarEasTone Telecom deployed AI and big data analytics to intercept over 99% of scam SMS messages, blocking more than 330,000 fraudulent texts and 450 malicious URLs since February. This proactive use of AI has significantly reduced financial fraud and protected users, earning recognition from Taiwan’s Criminal Investigation Bureau.
