### INC-00357  [↗](/incident/INC-00357.html)

**AI-Enabled Tycoon 2FA Phishing Platform Disrupted After Global Harm**  
_2026-03-04 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The AI-powered Tycoon 2FA phishing-as-a-service platform enabled attackers to bypass multi-factor authentication, leading to widespread account takeovers and harm to organizations and individuals globally, including over 160 affected in Portugal. TrendAI and partners, coordinated by Europol, used AI-driven threat intelligence to help dismantle the malicious service.

**Attack vector:** `rce`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`, `ASI10`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`
