**Proposed AI Data Centre in Durban Raises Resource and Governance Concerns**  
_2026-03-02 · real-world · Severity: High_

A proposed $9.8 billion AI data centre project in Durban, South Africa, backed by an Asian consortium, has sparked concerns over potential strain on local electricity and water resources. Local officials and politicians highlight a lack of transparency and fear significant environmental and infrastructure impacts if the project proceeds.
