### INC-07064  [↗](/incident/INC-07064.html)

**UN Warns AI in Policing Reinforces Racial Profiling and Discrimination**  
_2020-11-26 · real-world · Severity: High_

The UN Committee on the Elimination of Racial Discrimination warns that AI technologies like facial recognition and predictive policing risk reinforcing racial profiling and discrimination in law enforcement, potentially leading to human rights violations. The committee urges regulation to prevent algorithmic bias and misuse of personal data.
