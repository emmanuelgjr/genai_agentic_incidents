### INC-01733  [↗](/incident/INC-01733.html)

**Researchers Call for Stricter Regulation of AI-Powered Children's Toys**  
_2026-03-13 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A Cambridge University study warns that generative AI toys interacting with young children may pose developmental, emotional, and privacy risks. Researchers urge stricter regulations and new safety certifications, highlighting the lack of research on these toys' effects during early childhood development. No direct harm has yet been reported.
