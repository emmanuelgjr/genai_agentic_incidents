### INC-06668  [↗](/incident/INC-06668.html)

**Google's AI Autocomplete Suppresses Negative Black Lives Matter Suggestions**  
_2020-09-01 · real-world · Severity: High_

Google's AI-powered autocomplete system is accused of filtering out negative search suggestions related to Black Lives Matter, unlike other search engines that display both positive and negative options. This selective suppression raises concerns about informational bias, censorship, and potential violations of users' rights to access diverse viewpoints.
