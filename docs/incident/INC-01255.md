### INC-01255  [↗](/incident/INC-01255.html)

**Israeli Firms Deploy AI-Powered Vehicle Surveillance Tools**  
_2026-02-17 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Israeli companies, notably Toka, have developed and deployed AI-driven 'CARINT' technologies that infiltrate connected vehicles to conduct surveillance, including real-time tracking, eavesdropping, and data fusion. These tools, used globally, raise significant privacy and human rights concerns due to unauthorized monitoring and potential misuse for offensive operations.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM05`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`, `ASI05`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MEASURE-2.10`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0057`
