### INC-05589  [↗](/incident/INC-05589.html)

**European Politicians Deceived by Deepfake Impersonating Navalny's Chief of Staff**  
_2021-04-22 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

European politicians, including Dutch, Baltic, and UK parliamentarians, were deceived in live video calls by a deepfake impersonating Leonid Volkov, chief of staff to Russian opposition leader Alexei Navalny. The AI-generated deepfake led to misinformation and raised concerns about political manipulation and the challenges of detecting such advanced AI-driven disinformation.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
