### INC-00327  [↗](/incident/INC-00327.html)

**AI-Driven Tax Scams Surge in the US During Filing Season**  
_2026-03-30 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

In the US, tax season has seen a sharp rise in scams using AI-powered automated calls, voice imitation, and phishing messages to impersonate the IRS. These AI-enabled tactics have led to increased identity theft and financial fraud, prompting warnings from consumer advocates and government officials.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
