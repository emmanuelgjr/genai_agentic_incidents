### INC-06294  [↗](/incident/INC-06294.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfake Videos Fuel Disinformation About Biden's Health**  
_2020-09-16 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI-powered deepfake technologies have been used to create and spread manipulated videos falsely depicting Joe Biden as mentally unfit. These videos, widely circulated during the U.S. presidential campaign, have misled voters and undermined trust, directly harming democratic processes through disinformation.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
