### INC-06305  [↗](/incident/INC-06305.html)

**AI-Powered Exam Proctoring Sparks Privacy Backlash in French Universities**  
_2020-05-21 · real-world · Severity: High_

French universities and grandes écoles, including HEC and Rennes 1, deployed AI-based remote proctoring systems for online exams, triggering student protests and concerns over privacy and data protection. The controversy led to exam postponements and highlighted the risks of intrusive AI surveillance in education.
