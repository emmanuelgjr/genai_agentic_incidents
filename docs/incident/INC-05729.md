**Japan Develops AI-Enabled Autonomous Fighter Drones Amid Rising Tensions with China**  
_2021-01-01 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Japan's Defence Ministry is developing AI-powered autonomous fighter drones, aiming for deployment by 2035 to counter China's military advancements. The project involves a phased approach from remote-controlled to fully autonomous squadrons, raising concerns about future risks due to the lack of international regulations on autonomous weapons.
