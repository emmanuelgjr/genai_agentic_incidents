### INC-06494  [↗](/incident/INC-06494.html)

**Deep Learning in Search Engines Causes Sexist Bias, Study Finds**  
_2020-06-19 · real-world · Severity: High_

Researchers from the University of Linz found that search engines using deep learning algorithms, such as Google and Bing, produce particularly sexist and discriminatory results. Their study highlights how AI systems trained on biased human data perpetuate harmful gender stereotypes and discrimination through search outputs, causing real-world harm.
