**AI Surveillance Systems Spark Privacy Violations and Misuse Across U.S. Cities**  
_2026-05-01 · real-world · Severity: High_

Flock's AI-powered surveillance systems have led to privacy violations in multiple U.S. cities. Incidents include unauthorized access to sensitive camera footage in Dunwoody, Georgia, wrongful police stops in Colorado due to license plate misreads, and widespread tracking and profiling in Shreveport and Sarasota, raising significant privacy and civil rights concerns.
