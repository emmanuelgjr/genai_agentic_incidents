### INC-00265  [↗](/incident/INC-00265.html)

**AI-Augmented Cyberattack Compromises 600+ FortiGate Firewalls Globally**  
_2026-02-21 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

A Russian-speaking, financially motivated threat actor used commercial generative AI tools to automate and scale cyberattacks, compromising over 600 FortiGate firewalls across 55 countries in early 2026. The attacker exploited weak credentials and exposed management ports, demonstrating how AI lowers the barrier for large-scale cybercrime.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`
