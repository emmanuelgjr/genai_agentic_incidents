**AI-Enabled Armed Drone and Munition Systems Complete Successful Tests in Turkey**  
_2021-04-22 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Turkey's Bayraktar Akıncı TİHA, an AI-enabled armed drone, and Roketsan's MAM-T smart munition completed successful test firings. While no harm occurred, the deployment of these AI-driven autonomous weapon systems poses credible future risks of injury or damage if used in conflict, highlighting significant AI hazard concerns.
