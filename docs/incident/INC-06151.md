**US Air Force Tests AI-Driven Skyborg Autonomy Core System in Unmanned Aircraft**  
_2021-05-05 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The US Air Force, in partnership with Kratos and other defense contractors, successfully tested the AI-powered Skyborg autonomy core system aboard unmanned aircraft. The system demonstrated autonomous flight and navigation capabilities, marking a milestone in developing AI-driven military drones, though no harm or malfunction was reported during the tests.
