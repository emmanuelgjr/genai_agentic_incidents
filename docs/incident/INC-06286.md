### INC-06286  [↗](/incident/INC-06286.html)

**AI-Enabled Drones Escalate Conflict and Pose Future Warfare Risks**  
_2020-10-11 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

AI-powered drones have been actively used in the Nagorno-Karabakh conflict, causing significant casualties and destruction. Turkish-made drones, linked to Erdogan's son-in-law, and advanced autonomous systems have directly contributed to harm. Meanwhile, India and China are developing similar AI-enabled combat drones, raising concerns about future military escalation and risks.
