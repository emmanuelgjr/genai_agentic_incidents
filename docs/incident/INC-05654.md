### INC-05654  [↗](/incident/INC-05654.html)

**German Police AI Tool 'VeRA' Sparks Major Privacy Concerns**  
_2021-05-28 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Bavarian police's planned use of the AI system 'VeRA' to analyze large datasets for criminal connections has alarmed data protection authorities, who warn of potential privacy violations and legal breaches. Critics fear the system could enable unauthorized data processing and surveillance, raising significant concerns about fundamental rights.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM05`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`, `ASI05`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MEASURE-2.10`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0057`
