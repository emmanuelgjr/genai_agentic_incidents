### INC-07804  [↗](/incident/INC-07804.html)

**AI-Fabricated Evidence Used in Defamation Case Against Actor Kim Soo-hyun**  
_2026-05-19 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

South Korean police concluded that allegations against actor Kim Soo-hyun, claiming he dated the late Kim Sae-ron as a minor, were false and based on AI-generated audio and manipulated chat logs. The fabricated evidence, spread by YouTuber Kim Se-eui, caused significant reputational and economic harm, prompting legal action and arrest warrants.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
