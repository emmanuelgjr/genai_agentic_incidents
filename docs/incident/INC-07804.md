**Delhi High Court Grants Varun Dhawan Interim Relief Against AI-Generated Deepfakes**  
_2026-05-29 · real-world · Severity: High_

Bollywood actor Varun Dhawan secured interim relief from the Delhi High Court after AI-generated deepfakes and manipulated content misusing his identity circulated online. The court ordered social media platforms to remove such content and prevent further unauthorized use of his name, image, and voice, reflecting growing legal action against AI misuse in India.
