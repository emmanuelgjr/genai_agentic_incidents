**AI-Driven Cyber Attacks Impact Businesses in US and Singapore**  
_2026-05-19 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Recent surveys reveal that nearly 40% of Singapore businesses and 29% of US businesses experienced AI-related cyber events, including AI-generated malware and phishing attacks. These incidents led to revenue loss and operational disruption, highlighting the growing risks of AI misuse in cyberattacks.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM03`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI04`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-6.1`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0010`, `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
