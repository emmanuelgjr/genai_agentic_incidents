**NVIDIA Faces Lawsuit Over AI Training With Copyrighted Data**  
_2026-05-06 · real-world · Severity: High_

A U.S. federal court rejected NVIDIA's motion to dismiss a class-action lawsuit alleging its NeMo Megatron AI framework used 197,000 unauthorized e-books for training large language models, violating copyright. The court found NVIDIA's data processing scripts were primarily for infringement, allowing the case to proceed.
