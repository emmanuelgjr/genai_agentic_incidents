### INC-03125  [↗](/incident/INC-03125.html)

**Purportedly AI-Generated Home Intruder Videos Allegedly Prompt Dozens of Dutch Police Call-Outs**  
_2025-10-05 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1272`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1272/)

Dutch police reported that children used purported AI tools to generate fake videos showing a homeless intruder inside their homes, which parents believed were real. The clips allegedly triggered dozens of emergency call-outs and at least two helicopter searches for nonexistent burglars. Police posted a TikTok warning after repeated false alarms, noting the strain on resources and urging families to stop the prank.
