### INC-00580  [↗](/incident/INC-00580.html)

**Amazon Prime Air Exits Drone Alliance Over AI Safety Dispute**  
_2026-03-12 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Amazon Prime Air withdrew from the Commercial Drone Alliance, citing safety concerns over the group's opposition to mandatory AI-based detect-and-avoid technology in drones. Amazon highlighted that its AI system had prevented two potential mid-air collisions, warning that lack of such standards could risk catastrophic accidents.
