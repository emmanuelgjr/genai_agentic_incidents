**Geely's Caocao Plans Global Deployment of AI-Powered Robotaxis**  
_2026-04-24 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Caocao Inc, Geely's ride-hailing arm, announced plans to deploy thousands of fully autonomous robotaxis, the Eva Cab, globally starting in 2027. Initial rollouts will occur in Abu Dhabi, Hong Kong, and several Chinese cities, with large-scale expansion to 100,000 vehicles by 2030. No incidents reported yet.
