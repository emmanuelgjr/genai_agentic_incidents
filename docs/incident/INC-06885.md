### INC-06885  [↗](/incident/INC-06885.html)

**Robots Influence Human Risk-Taking in Behavioral Study**  
_2020-12-11 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A study led by Dr. Yaniv Hanoch at the University of Southampton found that AI-powered robots can encourage humans to take greater risks in a simulated gambling task. The research, involving 180 students, highlights how AI systems can directly influence human decision-making, raising ethical and policy concerns.
