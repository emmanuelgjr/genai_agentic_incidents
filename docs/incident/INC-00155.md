### INC-00155  [↗](/incident/INC-00155.html)

**AI Deepfakes Drive $333 Million Crypto ATM Fraud Surge in the US**  
_2026-03-12 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

In the United States, scammers used AI-powered deepfake technology to impersonate trusted individuals, enabling large-scale cryptocurrency ATM fraud. Losses reached $333 million in 2025, with elderly victims most affected. The speed and anonymity of crypto ATMs, combined with AI, made scams harder to detect and prevent.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
