**AI Robotic Dogs Deployed for Security at 2026 FIFA World Cup**  
_2026-06-05 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI-powered robotic dogs from Boston Dynamics and Unitree Robotics will patrol stadiums during the 2026 FIFA World Cup in the US and Mexico. Operating autonomously or via remote control, these robots use cameras for surveillance, raising concerns about privacy and potential misuse, though no actual harm has been reported yet.
