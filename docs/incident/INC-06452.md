### INC-06452  [↗](/incident/INC-06452.html)

**Chinese Safari Park Sued Over Facial Recognition Sparks National Privacy Debate**  
_2020-01-08 · real-world · Severity: High_

Law professor Guo Bing sued Hangzhou Safari Park after being required to scan his face, highlighting widespread use of facial-recognition AI in China. The case ignited public concern over privacy violations and data abuse, as biometric data is collected without adequate safeguards, leading to legal and societal backlash.
