**New York Lawmakers Propose Ban on Armed Police Robots Amid AI Safety Concerns**  
_2021-03-22 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Following public outcry over the NYPD's use of Boston Dynamics' unarmed robotic dog, New York City council members proposed legislation to ban police from deploying armed robots. The move addresses concerns that AI-enabled weaponized robots could pose serious risks of harm or misuse in law enforcement.
