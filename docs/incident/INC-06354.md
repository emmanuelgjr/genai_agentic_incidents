**Amazon's Zoox Approved for Fully Driverless Car Testing in California**  
_2020-09-18 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Amazon-owned Zoox has received a permit from California authorities to test autonomous vehicles without human backup drivers on public roads in Foster City. Zoox is now one of only four companies allowed such testing, raising potential safety concerns as AI-driven vehicles operate without direct human oversight.
