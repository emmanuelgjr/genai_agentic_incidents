**Anthropic's 'Claude Mythos' AI Sparks Global Cybersecurity Concerns**  
_2026-05-04 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Anthropic's AI model 'Claude Mythos' has alarmed global cybersecurity and financial authorities by autonomously discovering critical software vulnerabilities, including in financial infrastructure. The AI's advanced capabilities have prompted emergency meetings and regulatory changes in South Korea to allow AI-based defenses, highlighting the dual-use risks and urgent need for robust countermeasures.
