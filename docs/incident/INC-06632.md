**Georgia Police Department Deploys AI-Enabled Drones for 911 Response, Raising Privacy Concerns**  
_2020-10-31 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Brookhaven Police Department in Georgia will deploy AI-enabled drones to respond to 911 calls and conduct investigations, making it the first city in the Southeast to do so. While intended to enhance policing, the program raises concerns about potential privacy violations and intrusive surveillance due to real-time data collection.
