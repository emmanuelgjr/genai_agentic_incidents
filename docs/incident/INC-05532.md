### INC-05532  [↗](/incident/INC-05532.html)

**Chinese AI Input Method Apps Removed for Illegal Data Collection**  
_2021-06-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

Major Chinese AI-powered input method apps, including iFlytek, Sogou, and QQ Input Method, were removed from app stores after regulators found them illegally collecting and misusing user data for targeted advertising. The incident led to public outcry, regulatory scrutiny, and a sharp drop in iFlytek's stock price.
