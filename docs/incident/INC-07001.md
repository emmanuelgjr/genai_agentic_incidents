**TikTok/Douyin AI Algorithms Used for Censorship and Propaganda, Raising Global Concerns**  
_2020-05-13 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

TikTok (Douyin) uses AI-driven content moderation and recommendation to censor anti-government content, spread Chinese state propaganda, and promote misinformation. These practices have misled users, violated privacy, and raised national security concerns, with experts and officials warning of the platform's role in information manipulation and potential espionage.
