**AI-Driven 'Kill Switch' Law Raises Privacy and Surveillance Concerns in U.S. Vehicles**  
_2026-06-01 · real-world · Severity: High_

The U.S. is set to require AI-based driver impairment monitoring systems ('kill switches') in new cars under the HALT Drunk Driving Act. While aimed at preventing impaired driving, the technology raises significant concerns about privacy, surveillance, and potential misuse, with regulatory details and safeguards still unclear.
