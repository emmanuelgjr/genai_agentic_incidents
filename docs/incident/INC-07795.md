### INC-07795  [↗](/incident/INC-07795.html)

**AI Accelerates Cybersecurity Threats and Vulnerability Exploits**  
_2026-05-19 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Hackers are increasingly using AI, including Anthropic's Mythos Preview, to rapidly detect and exploit software vulnerabilities, leading to a surge in data breaches. AI enables automation and chaining of exploits, shrinking defense windows and fundamentally reshaping cybersecurity risks. Cloudflare warns of the model's powerful capabilities and potential for harm.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM05`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`, `ASI05`, `ASI09`
