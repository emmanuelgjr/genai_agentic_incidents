### INC-05547  [↗](/incident/INC-05547.html)

**Clearview AI's Facial Recognition Sparks Global Privacy Violations and Regulatory Action**  
_2021-02-09 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Clearview AI's facial recognition system scraped billions of online images without consent, leading to privacy law violations in Canada and Sweden. Authorities fined Swedish police for unlawful use and ordered Clearview to delete Canadians' biometric data, highlighting significant risks and harms from unauthorized AI-driven biometric surveillance.
