### INC-06367  [↗](/incident/INC-06367.html)

**Apple's AI System Scans iCloud and Email for Child Abuse Images**  
_2020-02-11 · real-world · Severity: High_

Apple uses automated hash-based AI systems to scan iCloud and email content for known child abuse images. When flagged, emails or files are quarantined and reviewed, with confirmed cases reported to authorities. This AI-driven process aims to prevent the distribution of illegal content while raising privacy concerns.
