**Global Voter Manipulation and Privacy Violations by Cambridge Analytica's AI Systems**  
_2020-01-06 · real-world · Severity: High_

Cambridge Analytica used AI-driven psychographic profiling and illegally obtained Facebook data to manipulate voters and influence elections in over 60 countries. Leaked documents reveal widespread privacy violations and targeted political advertising, resulting in significant harm to democratic processes and individual rights worldwide.
