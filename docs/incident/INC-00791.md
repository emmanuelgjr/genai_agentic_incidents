### INC-00791  [↗](/incident/INC-00791.html)

**Chinese Courts Rule Against AI Platforms for Defamation and Copyright Infringement**  
_2026-05-07 · real-world · Severity: High_

In China, Baidu's AI system falsely claimed a lawyer was convicted, causing reputational harm and resulting in a court-ordered apology. Separately, an AI search platform displayed pirated TV links, leading to a copyright lawsuit. Courts found Baidu liable for defamation, while the search platform was exempted due to prompt remedial action and lack of intent.
