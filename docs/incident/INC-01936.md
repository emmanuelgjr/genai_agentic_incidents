**Uber, Verne, and Pony.ai Launch First Robotaxi Service in Europe**  
_2026-03-26 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Uber, Verne, and Pony.ai have partnered to launch Europe's first commercial robotaxi service in Zagreb, Croatia. Pony.ai provides the autonomous driving system, Verne operates the fleet, and Uber offers its ride-hailing platform. The service is currently in testing, with plans for future expansion across Europe.
