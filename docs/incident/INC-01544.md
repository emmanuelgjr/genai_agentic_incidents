**Nuro Receives California Permits for Driverless Lucid-Uber Robotaxi Testing**  
_2026-05-08 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Nuro, in partnership with Lucid and Uber, has secured key California permits to test and deploy autonomous robotaxis, including fully driverless operations in Santa Clara and San Mateo counties. While no harm has occurred, the regulatory approvals enable large-scale AI-driven robotaxi deployment, raising potential future safety concerns.
