### INC-06905  [↗](/incident/INC-06905.html)

**Second Autonomous Bus Trial Conducted in Ibaraki Prefecture**  
_2020-09-28 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Ibaraki Prefecture, in partnership with several companies, conducted a second autonomous bus trial on the Hitachi BRT line to verify safety and operational management using AI-driven buses with real passengers. The experiment aims to identify challenges before full-scale deployment, but no AI-related incidents or harm have been reported.
