### INC-06310  [↗](/incident/INC-06310.html)

**AI-Powered Kamikaze Drones Deployed in Turkish Military Operations**  
_2020-03-04 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

STM-developed AI-enabled kamikaze drones, including the Kargu model, have been delivered to and actively used by the Turkish Armed Forces in counter-terrorism operations. These autonomous drones, capable of targeting and neutralizing threats with explosives, represent a realized deployment of lethal AI systems in military conflict.
