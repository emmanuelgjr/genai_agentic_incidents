**AI-Powered System Boosts Child Abuse Investigations in NRW**  
_2020-10-11 · real-world · Severity: High_

The North Rhine-Westphalia State Criminal Police Office (LKA) has deployed an advanced AI-based analysis network to combat child pornography, enabling rapid keyword searches and data extraction. This technology has improved law enforcement effectiveness, leading to successful interventions against child abuse and is being expanded to other crime areas.
