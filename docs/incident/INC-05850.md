### INC-05850  [↗](/incident/INC-05850.html)

**Singapore Trials Autonomous Road Sweepers in Controlled Environments**  
_2021-01-14 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Singapore's National Environment Agency and Ministry of Transport have launched trials of AI-powered autonomous road sweepers at three locations. The vehicles operate under strict safety protocols with onboard safety drivers and real-time monitoring. No harm has occurred, but the trials highlight potential future risks associated with autonomous vehicle deployment.
