**Alleged AI-Enabled PRISONBREAK Influence Operation on X Reportedly Synchronizes Deepfake of Evin Prison Strike with Ongoing Attacks in Tehran**  
_2025-06-23 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1221`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1221/)

Researchers from Citizen Lab and Clemson documented &quot;PRISONBREAK,&quot; a coordinated network of 50+ inauthentic X accounts running a purportedly AI-enabled influence operation. The network reportedly synchronized with the June 23 Evin Prison strikes, posting an AI-generated &quot;attack&quot; video during the events and urging Iranians to free inmates, alongside other synthetic media and media impersonations. They assess, with medium confidence, likely Israeli government or contractor involvement.
