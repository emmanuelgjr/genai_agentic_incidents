### INC-06736  [↗](/incident/INC-06736.html)

**Italian Antitrust Investigates AI-Based Insurance Fraud Detection Project for Competition Risks**  
_2020-11-16 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The Italian Antitrust Authority has launched an investigation into ANIA's AI-driven 'antifraud project,' which involves shared algorithms and databases for fraud detection among insurance companies. Authorities are concerned the system could facilitate collusion and harm market competition, though no actual harm has yet occurred.

**Attack vector:** `other`  

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`
