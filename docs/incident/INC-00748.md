### INC-00748  [↗](/incident/INC-00748.html)

**ChatGPT Flags Republican Fundraising Links as Unsafe, Raising Bias Concerns**  
_2026-03-21 · real-world · Severity: High_

OpenAI's ChatGPT erroneously flagged links to the Republican fundraising platform WinRed as potentially unsafe, while similar Democratic links to ActBlue were not flagged. OpenAI attributed this to a technical glitch, but the incident raised concerns about AI bias and its potential impact on political participation in the U.S.
