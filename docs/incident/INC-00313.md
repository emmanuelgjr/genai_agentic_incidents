### INC-00313  [↗](/incident/INC-00313.html)

**AI-Driven Online Violence Against Women in Spain**  
_2026-03-03 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

In Spain, 70% of digital platform complaints involve AI-driven violence against women, including deepfakes, non-consensual image sharing, and algorithmic amplification of hate. The Spanish government plans stricter regulations on platforms and access for minors to address these AI-related harms.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
