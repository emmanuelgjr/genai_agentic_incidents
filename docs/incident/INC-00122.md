### INC-00122  [↗](/incident/INC-00122.html)

**AI Chatbots Provide Biased Voting Advice, Neglecting Local Parties in Dutch Elections**  
_2026-03-12 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Dutch Data Protection Authority (AP) found that popular AI chatbots, including ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, and Mistral, rarely recommend local political parties in voting advice for municipal elections, despite these parties' significant electoral presence. This bias risks misleading voters and distorting the democratic process.
