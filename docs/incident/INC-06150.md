### INC-06150  [↗](/incident/INC-06150.html)

**US Air Force Test of AI-Enabled Collaborative Bombs Fails Due to Software Error**  
_2021-01-07 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The US Air Force tested AI-enabled collaborative Small Diameter Bombs designed to autonomously prioritize and engage targets. Due to a software loading error, the bombs' navigation systems failed to update target locations, causing them to miss intended targets. No harm occurred, but the malfunction highlights risks in autonomous weapon systems.
