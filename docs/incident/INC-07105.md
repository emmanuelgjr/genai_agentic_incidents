**US Universities Develop AI-Powered COVID-19 Surveillance and Risk Scoring System**  
_2020-05-04 · real-world · Severity: High_

Researchers at USC, Emory University, and the University of Texas Health Science Center are developing an AI-based mobile app to track individuals’ locations and symptoms, assigning personal COVID-19 risk scores. The system raises concerns about privacy, potential discrimination, and social control, echoing aspects of China’s social credit system.
