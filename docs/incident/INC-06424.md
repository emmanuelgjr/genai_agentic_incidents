**Canadian Privacy Commissioner Investigates RCMP's Use of Clearview AI Facial Recognition**  
_2020-02-28 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Canadian federal privacy commissioner is investigating the RCMP's use of Clearview AI's facial-recognition software due to major privacy concerns. The probe focuses on potential risks of privacy violations and data misuse, as the RCMP has been vague about the full scope of its use beyond child protection.
