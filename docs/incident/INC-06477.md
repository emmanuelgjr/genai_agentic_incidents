### INC-06477  [↗](/incident/INC-06477.html)

**Connected Cars Hacked: AI Systems Compromised, Causing Loss of Control and Theft**  
_2020-01-09 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

AI-powered connected vehicles are increasingly targeted by hackers, leading to incidents where critical functions like steering, brakes, and autopilot are remotely disabled or manipulated. Demonstrations and real cases show that such attacks can cause loss of vehicle control, theft, and pose significant safety risks to drivers and property.
