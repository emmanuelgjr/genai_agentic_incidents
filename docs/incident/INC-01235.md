**Indonesia and Turkey Sign Deal for AI-Enabled Combat Drones**  
_2026-05-05 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Indonesia and Turkey's Baykar have signed agreements to export and jointly develop Bayraktar Kizilelma AI-enabled unmanned combat aerial vehicles (UCAVs) for the Indonesian Armed Forces. The deployment of these autonomous weapons, capable of AI-driven navigation and targeting, raises credible risks of future harm or misuse. Deliveries are set to begin in 2028.
