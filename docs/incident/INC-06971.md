**Tesla Full Self-Driving Beta Expansion Raises Safety Concerns Amid Improvements**  
_2020-10-31 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Tesla is expanding its Full Self-Driving (FSD) beta software to Canada and Norway after the US, with updates aiming to reduce driver interventions by a third.
