### INC-00304  [↗](/incident/INC-00304.html)

**AI-Driven Insurance Claim Denials Lead to Harm and Lawsuits in the US**  
_2026-03-30 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

AI systems are increasingly used by US insurers to process and deny claims for medical and property coverage, often without human oversight. This has resulted in wrongful denials, restricted access to care, and even deaths, prompting lawsuits—such as against UnitedHealth Group—and raising regulatory concerns, especially in Florida.
