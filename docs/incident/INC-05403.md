### INC-05403  [↗](/incident/INC-05403.html)

**AI-Driven Price Discrimination Against Loyal Customers in China Spurs Regulatory Action**  
_2021-06-07 · real-world · Severity: High_

Chinese online platforms have used AI algorithms and big data to implement discriminatory pricing, charging loyal or frequent users higher prices than new users. This practice, known as 'big data price discrimination,' has harmed consumer rights and fairness, prompting widespread complaints and proposed local regulations in Shenzhen to impose heavy fines on violators.
