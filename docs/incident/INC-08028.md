### INC-08028  [↗](/incident/INC-08028.html)

**AI-Generated Ghost Image Causes Public Panic in Palembang**  
_2026-06-01 · real-world · Severity: High_

Police in Palembang, Indonesia, arrested a man for creating and spreading an AI-generated image of a 'pocong' (ghost) that went viral and caused public panic. The manipulated image, made using AI technology, led to widespread fear and speculation among residents before authorities intervened.
