**Tesla Robotaxi Malfunctions Raise Safety Concerns in Texas**  
_2026-04-19 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Tesla's autonomous Robotaxi service, tested in Dallas and Houston, Texas, has experienced significant AI malfunctions, including navigation errors, abrupt stops on highways, and unsafe passenger drop-offs. Passengers reported safety hazards due to the absence of onboard operators, highlighting risks associated with the AI-driven vehicles' deployment on public roads.
