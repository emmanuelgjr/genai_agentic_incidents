### INC-02185  [↗](/incident/INC-02185.html)

**Adversarial poetry exposes systemic jailbreak vulnerability in AI chatbots**  
_2025-11-21 · research-demonstrated · Severity: High_

Researchers from DEXAI, Sapienza University of Rome and Sant'Anna School showed that adversarial poetic prompts reliably bypass safety guardrails across major LLMs, achieving jailbreak success rates up to 90% on harmful-content requests.

**Affected:** Frontier LLMs  
**Attack vector:** `jailbreak`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM01`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI01`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MANAGE-2.4`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0051`, `AML.T0054`
