### INC-04601  [↗](/incident/INC-04601.html)

**Deepfake MrBeast iPhone giveaway scam on TikTok**  
_2023-10 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

An AI-generated deepfake of MrBeast circulated on TikTok promoting a fake iPhone 15 giveaway to harvest personal data and payments; TikTok pulled the ad after the creator publicly flagged it.

**Affected:** TikTok users / MrBeast brand  
**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM03`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI02`, `ASI03`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `GOVERN-6.1`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.10`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`
