### INC-02036  [↗](/incident/INC-02036.html)

**Utah Approves AI Chatbot to Renew Psychiatric Medication Prescriptions**  
_2026-04-03 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Utah has approved a pilot program allowing Legion Health's AI chatbot to autonomously renew certain psychiatric medication prescriptions for stable patients. While safeguards and restrictions are in place, experts warn of potential risks to patient safety due to reduced human oversight and possible prescription errors.
