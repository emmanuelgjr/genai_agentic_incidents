### INC-05495  [↗](/incident/INC-05495.html)

**AutoX Launches Fully Driverless Robotaxi Service to Public in Shenzhen**  
_2021-01-28 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

AutoX, backed by Alibaba, has launched China's first public pilot of fully driverless robotaxis in Shenzhen, allowing users to book rides without a safety driver. The AI-driven vehicles operate in a densely populated area, with remote support available, presenting potential safety risks but no reported incidents so far.

**Attack vector:** `other`
