### INC-01686  [↗](/incident/INC-01686.html)

**Publishers Sue Meta Over Unauthorized Use of Copyrighted Works for AI Training**  
_2026-05-05 · real-world · Severity: High_

Major publishers, including Elsevier, Cengage, Hachette, Macmillan, and McGraw Hill, along with author Scott Turow, have filed a lawsuit against Meta in Manhattan. They allege Meta used millions of copyrighted books and articles without permission to train its Llama AI model, violating intellectual property rights.
