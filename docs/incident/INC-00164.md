### INC-00164  [↗](/incident/INC-00164.html)

**AI Facial Recognition Error Leads to Wrongful Arrest in UK**  
_2026-02-25 · real-world · Severity: High_

Alvi Choudhury, a Bangladeshi-origin software engineer in Southampton, was wrongfully arrested and detained after UK police facial recognition software misidentified him as a burglary suspect in Milton Keynes. The AI system's false positive, compounded by prior mugshot retention, led to significant distress and raised concerns about racial bias and police reliance on flawed AI.
