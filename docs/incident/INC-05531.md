### INC-05531  [↗](/incident/INC-05531.html)

**Chinese AI Fighter Pilots Routinely Defeat Human Pilots in Simulated Dogfights**  
_2021-06-15 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

China's military has developed AI systems capable of piloting fighter jets that consistently outperform human pilots in simulated aerial combat. Used in training, these AI pilots learn and adapt from each engagement, raising concerns about future risks if such autonomous systems are deployed in real combat scenarios.
