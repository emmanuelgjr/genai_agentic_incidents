**AI-Enabled Social Engineering Scams Cause Nearly $1 Billion in Consumer Losses, Visa Reports**  
_2026-05-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

Visa's Spring 2026 Biannual Threats Report reveals a surge in AI-enabled social engineering scams, with criminals exploiting artificial intelligence to manipulate consumers into authorizing fraudulent payments. From July to December 2025, nearly $1 billion in scam-related losses were identified, marking a significant shift from technical breaches to human-targeted fraud.
