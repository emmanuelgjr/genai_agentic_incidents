**Dutch Forensic Institute Uses AI to Detect and Prevent Violent Crime via Encrypted Chats**  
_2021-05-05 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The Dutch Forensic Institute (NFI) developed and deployed a deep learning model to scan millions of intercepted encrypted EncroChat messages for serious threats. The AI system enabled police to quickly identify and act on life-threatening messages, helping prevent kidnappings, torture, and murders by facilitating timely interventions.
