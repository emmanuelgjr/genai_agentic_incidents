### INC-06380  [↗](/incident/INC-06380.html)

**Austrian Job Agency's AI Algorithm Increases Social Inequality, Study Finds**  
_2020-11-17 · real-world · Severity: High_

A study commissioned by the Austrian Chamber of Labour found that the AMS algorithm, used to allocate job support, promoted social inequality by disadvantaging older, less qualified, and vulnerable job seekers. The data protection authority halted its use, citing discrimination and rights violations caused by the AI system.
