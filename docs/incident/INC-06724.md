**Iran Unveils AI-Powered Unmanned Combat Submarine, Joining Elite Naval Powers**  
_2020-05-29 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Iran has officially unveiled an AI-enabled unmanned underwater combat vehicle with autonomous navigation and remote control capabilities. This military system, comparable to advanced U.S. and U.K. models, is designed for offensive operations, raising concerns about potential future harm and escalation in maritime conflict zones.
