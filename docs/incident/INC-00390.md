### INC-00390  [↗](/incident/INC-00390.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfake Video Causes Defamation Dispute Involving Indonesian Political Figures**  
_2026-04-12 · real-world · Severity: High_

A deepfake video created using AI falsely depicted digital forensics expert Rismon Sianipar accusing former Vice President Jusuf Kalla of funding a fake diploma case against President Joko Widodo. The AI-generated video led to reputational harm, legal complaints, and calls for police investigation in Indonesia.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
