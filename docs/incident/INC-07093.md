**US Navy Accelerates Deployment of AI-Enabled Armed Ocean Drones**  
_2020-02-04 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The US Navy is rapidly developing and deploying AI-enabled armed surface and underwater drones for surveillance, reconnaissance, and attack missions. These autonomous systems, capable of lethal force and complex coordination, present significant risks of future harm or escalation in conflict, though no actual incidents have yet occurred.
