### INC-00667  [↗](/incident/INC-00667.html)

**Autonomous Lawn Mowers Cause Harm to Wildlife, Prompting Calls for Nighttime Ban**  
_2026-04-10 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Animal welfare groups in Austria and Germany report that AI-powered lawn mowing robots, unable to detect small nocturnal animals like hedgehogs, are causing severe injuries and deaths. Activists and local organizations are demanding nighttime bans and technical improvements to protect wildlife from these autonomous machines.

**Attack vector:** `other`
