**NVIDIA-Unitree Humanoid Robot Collaboration Sparks National Security Concerns in China**  
_2026-06-06 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

NVIDIA and China's Unitree partnered to launch the Isaac GR00T AI-powered humanoid robot, integrating Unitree's H2 Plus platform. The collaboration triggered widespread criticism in China, with concerns over potential technology and data leakage to the US, raising national security and technology dependence risks. No actual harm has occurred yet.
