### INC-01971  [↗](/incident/INC-01971.html)

**Unauthorized Access and Security Concerns Over Anthropic's Mythos AI Model in U.S. Government**  
_2026-04-20 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Unauthorized users gained access to Anthropic's restricted AI model Mythos, designed for cybersecurity, via a third-party contractor. Despite Pentagon warnings about national security risks, the NSA and other U.S. agencies continue using Mythos, highlighting governance conflicts and raising concerns about potential misuse and cyberattack facilitation.

**Attack vector:** `rce`

**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-6.1`, `MEASURE-2.7`
