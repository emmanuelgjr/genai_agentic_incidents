**U.S. Army Reveals and Tests AI-Enabled Hypersonic Loitering Weapon System**  
_2020-06-08 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The U.S. Army inadvertently revealed and successfully tested the Vintage Racer, a hypersonic weapon system likely integrating AI for autonomous targeting and guidance. Designed to deliver loitering munitions at high speed to seek and destroy targets, the system poses credible future risks but has not yet caused harm.
