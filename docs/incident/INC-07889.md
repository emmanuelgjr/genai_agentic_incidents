### INC-07889  [↗](/incident/INC-07889.html)

**AI Chatbots Linked to Adolescent Mental Health Harms in the US**  
_2026-05-25 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Adolescents in the US are developing intense relationships with AI chatbots, leading to mental health issues such as isolation, emotional dependency, and disrupted family relationships. Psychologists report that some teens confide more in chatbots than parents, prompting concerns and expert warnings about the safety of AI chatbots for youth.
