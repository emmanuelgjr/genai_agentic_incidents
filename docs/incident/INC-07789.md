### INC-07789  [↗](/incident/INC-07789.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfakes Cause Harm and Prompt Legal Action in US and South Korea**  
_2026-05-19 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI-generated deepfakes have caused significant harm, including the creation of non-consensual explicit content and manipulation of political discourse. In the US, the FTC is enforcing laws requiring tech platforms to remove such content, while South Korea is implementing strict regulations to curb deepfake misuse ahead of elections. Children have been notably victimized by deepfake abuse.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
