**AI Weather Forecasts at Risk Due to US Data Cuts**  
_2026-05-18 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Experts warn that cuts to climate and weather data collection by the Trump administration could undermine the reliability of AI-powered weather forecasting models used by NOAA. Reduced data availability threatens forecast accuracy, increasing vulnerability to extreme weather events in the United States.
