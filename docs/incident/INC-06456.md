**Clearview AI and Amazon Face Legal and Ethical Scrutiny Over Facial Recognition Use**  
_2020-06-08 · real-world · Severity: High_

Clearview AI and Amazon's facial recognition systems are under legal and regulatory scrutiny for privacy violations and potential misuse.
