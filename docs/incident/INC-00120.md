### INC-00120  [↗](/incident/INC-00120.html)

**AI Chatbots Pose Psychological and Privacy Risks to Children**  
_2026-02-19 · real-world · Severity: High_

Digital security firm ESET highlights that nearly two-thirds of children use AI chatbots, raising concerns about psychological and social development, exposure to misinformation, harmful content, and privacy violations. These risks stem from children treating chatbots as digital friends or advisors, potentially impacting their well-being.

**Attack vector:** `misinformation`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0058`
