### INC-00561  [↗](/incident/INC-00561.html)

**AI-Powered Translation Threatens Minority Languages with Extinction**  
_2026-05-05 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

UNESCO warns that rapid adoption of AI-based automatic translation devices may accelerate the extinction of thousands of minority languages. These technologies, which enable real-time multilingual communication, risk promoting linguistic homogenization and undermining cultural diversity by reducing the need to learn and use less common languages.

**Attack vector:** `other`
