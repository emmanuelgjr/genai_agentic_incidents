**UK Collaboration with China on Facial Recognition Tech Raises Human Rights Concerns**  
_2020-03-14 · real-world · Severity: High_

The UK Home Office and police partnered with Chinese state institutions on the FaceR2VM project to develop advanced facial recognition technology capable of identifying masked individuals. Experts warn this AI system could enable oppressive surveillance and human rights abuses in China, raising concerns about UK complicity in potential harm.
