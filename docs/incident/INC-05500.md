**Bavarian Police Use AI Facial Recognition to Identify Hundreds of Suspects**  
_2021-04-15 · real-world · Severity: High_

In 2020, Bavarian police used an AI-powered facial recognition system to identify 649 suspects, a significant increase from previous years. The system compares surveillance images with a national database, directly impacting individuals by facilitating law enforcement actions and raising concerns about privacy and potential rights violations.
