**IBM Urges US to Restrict Export of Facial Recognition AI Over Human Rights Concerns**  
_2020-09-11 · real-world · Severity: High_

IBM has called on the US government to impose export controls on facial recognition technology, warning it could be misused by authoritarian regimes for mass surveillance and ethnic profiling, leading to human rights violations. The company has also ceased offering such technology, citing ethical concerns.
