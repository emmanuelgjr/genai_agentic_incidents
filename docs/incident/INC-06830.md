**Pakistan Repurposes AI Surveillance Tech for COVID-19 Tracking, Raising Privacy Concerns**  
_2020-05-28 · real-world · Severity: High_

Pakistan's government, with the help of its intelligence agency ISI, repurposed AI-driven surveillance technologies—originally used for militant tracking—to monitor coronavirus patients and their contacts. This use of geo-fencing and phone monitoring has raised significant privacy and human rights concerns due to the intrusive tracking of citizens.
