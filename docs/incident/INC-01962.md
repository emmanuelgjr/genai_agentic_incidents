**Ukraine Launches Defense AI Center "A1" to Integrate AI in Military Operations**  
_2026-04-18 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Ukraine's Ministry of Defense, supported by the UK, launched the Defense AI Center "A1" to integrate artificial intelligence into military systems. The center focuses on developing AI-driven battlefield analytics, autonomous drones, and robotic platforms, aiming to enhance Ukraine's combat effectiveness and decision-making with minimal human intervention.
