### INC-00302  [↗](/incident/INC-00302.html)

**AI-Driven Health Misinformation Harms Patients in Slovakia**  
_2026-02-27 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Slovak doctors report that AI-powered systems, especially generative AI, are accelerating the spread of health misinformation online. This has led to patients making harmful health decisions, such as refusing or delaying evidence-based treatments. The initiative 'Lekári nahlas' was launched to counteract these AI-enabled risks.

**Attack vector:** `misinformation`
