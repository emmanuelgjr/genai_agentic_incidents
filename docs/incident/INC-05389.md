### INC-05389  [↗](/incident/INC-05389.html)

**AI Recruitment Systems Cause Bias and Unfair Job Rejections**  
_2021-06-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

Multiple companies are using AI systems to evaluate and select job candidates, often without human oversight. These systems, including those using personality games and automated assessments, have led to unfair rejections and documented bias, such as Amazon's AI discriminating against women, raising concerns about labor rights and discrimination in hiring.

**Attack vector:** `algorithmic-bias`  

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`
