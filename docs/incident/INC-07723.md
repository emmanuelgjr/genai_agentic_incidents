### INC-07723  [↗](/incident/INC-07723.html)

**Kenya's $1 Billion AI Data Center Project Stalls Over Power Constraints**  
_2026-05-06 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Kenya's $1 billion data center project, a partnership with Microsoft and AI firm G42 to deliver Azure cloud and AI services, has stalled due to insufficient national power capacity. The facility's energy demands would consume a third of Kenya's electricity supply, halting AI infrastructure deployment.
