**ASU Faculty Protest AI Platform's Unauthorized Use of Teaching Materials**  
_2026-05-07 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Arizona State University's AI-powered platforms, Atom and ASU Atomic, repurposed faculty teaching materials without their consent to generate personalized online courses. Faculty expressed concerns over intellectual property violations, lack of consultation, and inaccuracies in AI-generated content, potentially harming educational quality and academic reputations.
