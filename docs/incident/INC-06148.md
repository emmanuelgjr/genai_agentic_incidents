**UN Chief Warns of AI-Driven Data Manipulation Violating Human Rights**  
_2021-02-22 · real-world · Severity: High_

UN Secretary-General António Guterres warned that some governments are exploiting large data sets and AI technologies to manipulate citizens' behavior and perceptions, violating human rights.
