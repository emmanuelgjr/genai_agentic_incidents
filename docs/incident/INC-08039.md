### INC-08039  [↗](/incident/INC-08039.html)

**AI-Generated Content Spurs Fraud; Multimodal Detection System Deployed in China**  
_2026-06-01 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI-generated fake images and videos have enabled fraud in e-commerce and financial sectors in China, including identity theft and false claims. In response, the company 合合信息 launched a multimodal AI detection system, now deployed across over 30 industries, significantly improving the interception of deepfakes and reducing related harms.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
