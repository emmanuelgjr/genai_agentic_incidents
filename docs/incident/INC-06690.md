**IIT Madras Develops AI Drone to Counter Rogue Drones, Raising Future Risk Concerns**  
_2020-03-05 · real-world · Severity: High_

Researchers at IIT Madras have developed an AI-powered drone capable of autonomously detecting, tracking, and hacking rogue drones by spoofing their GPS to force them to land. While no harm has occurred, the technology poses future risks if misused or malfunctioning, potentially disrupting critical infrastructure or violating rights.
