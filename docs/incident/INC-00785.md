### INC-00785  [↗](/incident/INC-00785.html)

**Chinese AI Search System Misidentifies Historical Figure, Spreading Misinformation**  
_2026-05-05 · real-world · Severity: High_

Doubao, a Chinese AI assistant, mistakenly displayed an altered image of actor Fan Wei instead of historical figure Li Yuanhong due to widespread online misinformation and flawed training data. The incident exposed AI's vulnerability to 'hallucinations,' leading to public concern over AI reliability and the spread of false information. The issue was later corrected.

**Attack vector:** `hallucination`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0058`
