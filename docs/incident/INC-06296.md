### INC-06296  [↗](/incident/INC-06296.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfakes Used for Non-Consensual Pornography and Blackmail**  
_2020-11-16 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

AI systems using deep learning and GANs have enabled the creation and mass distribution of deepfake videos, particularly non-consensual pornographic content and blackmail material. These AI-generated deepfakes have caused significant harm to individuals and communities, including psychological distress, privacy violations, and exploitation, especially in regions lacking clear legal protections.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
