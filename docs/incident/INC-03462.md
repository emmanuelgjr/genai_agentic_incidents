### INC-03462  [↗](/incident/INC-03462.html)

**AI Firm Lovo Accused of Illegally Replicating Voice Actors&#x27; Voices**  
_2024-05-16 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#728`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/728/)

Two voice actors, Paul Skye Lehrman and Linnea Sage, are suing AI start-up Lovo for allegedly creating and promoting unauthorized clones of their voices. Lovo&#x27;s synthetic voices were discovered in various media, including a podcast and promotional videos. The actors claim they were misled into providing voice samples, which were then used without consent, violating trademark and privacy laws.
