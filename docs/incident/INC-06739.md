**Jackson Police Pilot AI-Enabled Real-Time Surveillance via Amazon Ring Cameras**  
_2020-11-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

Jackson, Mississippi police launched a 45-day pilot program allowing real-time livestreaming of private Amazon Ring camera footage to a police command center. Enabled by AI-powered surveillance and cloud integration, the program raises significant privacy and civil liberties concerns, with critics warning of mass surveillance and potential human rights violations.
