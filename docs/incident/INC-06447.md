### INC-06447  [↗](/incident/INC-06447.html)

**Chinese City Uses AI Facial Recognition to Publicly Shame Pajama Wearers**  
_2020-01-21 · real-world · Severity: High_

Authorities in Suzhou, China, used AI-powered facial recognition to identify and publicly shame residents for wearing pajamas in public, posting their photos, names, and ID numbers online. The incident sparked public backlash over privacy violations, leading to an official apology and removal of the posts.
