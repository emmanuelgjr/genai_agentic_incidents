### INC-01047  [↗](/incident/INC-01047.html)

**French Cinema Artists Protest Unauthorized AI Voice and Image Cloning**  
_2026-02-22 · real-world · Severity: High_

Around 4,000 French actors, actresses, and filmmakers have publicly denounced the unauthorized use of AI tools to clone their voices and images, calling it a "systematic plundering" of their work. They demand stronger legal protections against AI-driven violations of intellectual property and artists' rights.
