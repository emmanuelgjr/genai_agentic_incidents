### INC-07790  [↗](/incident/INC-07790.html)

**AI-Driven Surge in Online Sexual Exploitation of Minors in Belgium**  
_2026-05-19 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Child Focus reports a doubling of online sexual exploitation cases involving minors in Belgium, reaching 837 victims in 2025. The rise is attributed to the use of AI in generating and manipulating images and videos for sexting, sextortion, and grooming, causing significant harm to children.

**Attack vector:** `csam-generation`  

**NIST AI RMF:** `MEASURE-2.6`
