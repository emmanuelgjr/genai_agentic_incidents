### INC-06950  [↗](/incident/INC-06950.html)

**Telegram bot creates non-consensual deepfake porn at scale**  
_2020-10 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

A Telegram bot built on the DeepNude code was found by Sensity to have generated non-consensual sexual deepfakes of more than 100,000 women, including minors, from photographs submitted by users. The case is a landmark for industrialized AI sexual-violence-as-a-service.

**Affected:** >100,000 women and girls  
**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM06`, `LLM07`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI08`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.1`, `MANAGE-4.1`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`
