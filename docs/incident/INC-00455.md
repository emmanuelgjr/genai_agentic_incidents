### INC-00455  [↗](/incident/INC-00455.html)

**AI-Generated Fake Media Fuels Misinformation in Middle East Conflict**  
_2026-03-09 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

During the US-Israel and Iran conflict, generative AI has been widely used to create and spread fake videos, images, and satellite photos on social media. This AI-driven misinformation has misled the public, fueled propaganda, and undermined trust in factual reporting, causing significant harm to communities in the region.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
