### INC-05646  [↗](/incident/INC-05646.html)

**Fatal Tesla Autopilot Crashes in California and China Raise Safety Concerns**  
_2021-05-17 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Multiple fatal accidents involving Tesla vehicles with Autopilot engaged occurred in California and China. In both cases, drivers appeared to rely heavily on the AI system, with one posting videos of hands-free driving. The incidents resulted in deaths and injuries, highlighting risks associated with misuse or overreliance on Tesla's AI-assisted driving technology.
