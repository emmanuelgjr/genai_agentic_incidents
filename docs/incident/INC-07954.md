**AI Robot Testing in San Francisco Airbnb Causes Property Damage, Lawsuit Filed**  
_2026-05-28 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The Bot Company, a robotics startup, secretly tested an AI-powered domestic robot in a San Francisco Airbnb without the host's consent, resulting in significant property damage and missing items. The homeowner, Sean Donovan, is suing for over $12,000 in damages, highlighting risks of unauthorized AI system deployment in private residences.
