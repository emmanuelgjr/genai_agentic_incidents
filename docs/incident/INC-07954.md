### INC-07954  [↗](/incident/INC-07954.html)

**AI-Generated Fake QRIS Used in Surabaya Store Fraud**  
_2026-05-28 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A man in Surabaya, Indonesia, used an AI application to create fake QRIS payment proofs, deceiving a local store and fraudulently withdrawing over 3.3 million rupiah. The suspect, AY, repeated the scam multiple times before being apprehended by police. The incident highlights AI's role in enabling financial fraud.

**Attack vector:** `other`  

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`
