**Moroccan Education Council Warns of AI Risks, Calls for Urgent Regulation**  
_2026-06-02 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Morocco's Higher Council for Education, Training, and Scientific Research warns of potential cognitive and educational harms from unregulated AI use in schools. The council urges the government to establish a national framework to guide and control AI adoption in education, highlighting risks to learning quality and student development.
