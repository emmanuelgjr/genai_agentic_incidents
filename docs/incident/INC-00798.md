**Chuck Norris' Family Condemns Harmful AI-Generated Misinformation After His Death**  
_2026-04-01 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Following Chuck Norris' death, his family publicly denounced a surge of AI-generated videos and posts spreading false and misleading information about the circumstances of his passing and personal life. The family urged the public not to trust or share these AI-created fabrications, highlighting the harm caused by such misinformation.
