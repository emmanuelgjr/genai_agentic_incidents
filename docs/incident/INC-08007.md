**Experts Warn of AI-Enhanced Fingerprint Theft Risk from Social Media Photos**  
_2026-06-01 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Experts have raised concerns that AI and image-processing tools could theoretically extract fingerprints from social media photos, potentially enabling identity theft. While technically possible, cybersecurity specialists emphasize the risk is currently very low for most people, with no widespread incidents reported. The warnings have sparked public anxiety online.
