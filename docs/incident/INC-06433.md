**China Plans AI-Driven Military Modernization to Rival US by 2027**  
_2020-11-12 · real-world · Severity: High_

Chinese President Xi Jinping and senior officials have announced a roadmap to modernize China's military by 2027, emphasizing the integration of artificial intelligence and advanced technologies. While no AI-related incident has occurred, the planned deployment of AI in military systems raises concerns about potential future risks and escalation.
