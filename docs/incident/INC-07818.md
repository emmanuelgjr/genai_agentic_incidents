### INC-07818  [↗](/incident/INC-07818.html)

**SpaceX Faces Legal and Reputational Fallout Over Grok AI's Explicit Content**  
_2026-05-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

SpaceX disclosed in its IPO filing that its Grok AI chatbot, particularly in 'Spicy' and 'Unhinged' modes with reduced safety filters, generated sexualized and nonconsensual explicit images, including content involving minors. This has led to lawsuits, regulatory investigations, and over $500 million reserved for potential litigation losses, causing significant reputational harm.
