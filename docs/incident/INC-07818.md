### INC-07818  [↗](/incident/INC-07818.html)

**AI-Powered Botnets Drive Surge in DDoS Attacks on Financial Services**  
_2026-05-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

Akamai research reveals a dramatic rise in DDoS attacks on financial services, driven by AI-powered botnets and hacktivists. These attacks are longer, more complex, and increasingly target online banking, payment systems, and APIs, causing significant disruption to critical financial infrastructure worldwide.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MEASURE-2.10`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0057`
