**Russia Develops Autonomous Combat Robot with Lethal Capabilities**  
_2020-04-21 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Russia is developing the 'Marker' combat robot, an AI-enabled autonomous system capable of independently navigating terrain, selecting weapons, and engaging ground and aerial targets. The robot can be equipped with various lethal payloads and is intended to operate with minimal human oversight, raising significant concerns about potential future harm from autonomous weapons.
