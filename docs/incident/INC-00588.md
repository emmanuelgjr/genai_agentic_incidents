**Anduril Acquires ExoAnalytic to Expand AI-Driven Space Defense Capabilities**  
_2026-03-11 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Anduril Industries is acquiring ExoAnalytic Solutions to enhance its AI-enabled space situational awareness and missile defense systems. The move aims to strengthen Anduril's position in U.S. space defense, particularly for the Golden Dome initiative, by integrating advanced AI-driven tracking, sensor, and command-and-control technologies.
