**Dutch Police Facial Recognition System Retains Innocent Individuals in Database**  
_2021-03-16 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Dutch police's AI-based facial recognition system, CATCH, has wrongfully retained tens of thousands of individuals in its database, including those acquitted or no longer suspects. This mismanagement violates privacy and legal rights, raising serious concerns about fundamental rights and the lawful use of AI in law enforcement.
