### INC-06351  [↗](/incident/INC-06351.html)

**Amazon's AI-Driven Choice Badge Misleads Consumers Through Fake Reviews**  
_2020-02-06 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Consumer group Which? found that Amazon's AI-powered Choice badge is easily manipulated by fake and incentivized reviews, leading to poor-quality or unsafe products being promoted as top picks. This undermines consumer trust and exposes customers to potential harm, as many mistakenly view the badge as a mark of quality.
