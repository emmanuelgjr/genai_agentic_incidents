### INC-06071  [↗](/incident/INC-06071.html)

**Tesla AI System Failures and Data Transparency Spark Safety Concerns in China**  
_2021-05-09 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Multiple Tesla drivers in China reported brake failures and unintended acceleration, leading to accidents and injuries. Incomplete and potentially unreliable driving data from Tesla’s AI systems hindered accident investigations, prompting experts to call for mandatory 'black box' recorders and improved data transparency to address ongoing safety and accountability concerns.
