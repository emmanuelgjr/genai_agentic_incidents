### INC-01779  [↗](/incident/INC-01779.html)

**Slovakia Plans National AI Cybersecurity Laboratory**  
_2026-02-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

Slovakia's Ministry of Investments, Regional Development and Informatization is planning a National AI Cybersecurity Laboratory (AI CyberLab) to develop, test, and validate AI solutions for protecting critical infrastructure. The initiative aims to enhance national resilience against cyber threats, with funding from national and EU sources. No AI-related incident has occurred yet.
