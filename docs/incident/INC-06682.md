**Hungarian Police Deploy Facial Recognition for Identity Checks, Raising Rights Concerns**  
_2020-04-30 · real-world · Severity: High_

From May 1, Hungarian police began using AI-powered facial recognition to identify individuals who refuse to show ID, matching photos against government databases. Amnesty International warns this new procedure could violate privacy and fundamental rights due to automated identification without consent.
