**AI Drives Majority of Global Security Breaches in 2026**  
_2026-05-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

A 2026 Gigamon survey reveals that AI is involved in 83% of reported security breaches worldwide, enabling attackers to outpace defenders despite increased security investments. Organizations face rising breach rates as adversaries leverage AI throughout the attack chain, highlighting a growing imbalance in cybersecurity defenses.
