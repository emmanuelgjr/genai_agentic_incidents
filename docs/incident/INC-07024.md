**Turkish Military Deploys AI-Powered Swarming Kamikaze Drones with Facial Recognition**  
_2020-06-15 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Turkey's military is deploying over 500 Kargu-2 kamikaze drones, developed by STM, featuring AI-driven autonomous targeting, facial recognition, and swarming capabilities. These lethal drones can identify and attack specific individuals, raising significant concerns about AI-enabled harm, including injury, death, and potential human rights violations in conflict zones.
