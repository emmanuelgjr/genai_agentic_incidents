**AI-Enabled Drone Countermeasure Systems Developed and Deployed in Taiwan**  
_2026-03-22 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Taiwanese company Wistron integrates AI technologies into the Aegis drone countermeasure system, deployed at over 1,200 critical sites. The government plans a NT$44.2 billion investment over five years to foster the domestic drone industry, highlighting potential future risks from AI-enabled military systems but no current harm reported.
