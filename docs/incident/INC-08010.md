**Philadelphia Police Monitor Social Media for Threats Against AI Data Centers**  
_2026-06-01 · real-world · Severity: High_

Philadelphia police, through the Delaware Valley Intelligence Center, are monitoring social media posts critical of AI data centers, warning that such criticism could signal potential physical or cyber attacks by domestic extremists. No actual attacks have occurred, but authorities consider the risk to AI infrastructure credible.
