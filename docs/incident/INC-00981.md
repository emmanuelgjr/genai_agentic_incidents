**Facial Recognition AI Leads to Arrest of Rhondda Drug Dealers**  
_2026-04-09 · real-world · Severity: High_

South Wales Police used retrospective facial recognition AI to identify Coran Davies from a selfie he sent after dental treatment in Turkey. The image, found on a phone under investigation, led to the arrest and conviction of Davies and Dale Howell for drug offenses in Rhondda, Wales.
