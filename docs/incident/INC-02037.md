### INC-02037  [↗](/incident/INC-02037.html)

**Utah Medical Board Calls for Suspension of AI Prescription Renewal Program**  
_2026-04-24 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Utah's Medical Licensing Board has urged the immediate suspension of a state pilot program using Doctronic's AI system to autonomously renew prescriptions. The board cites concerns over patient safety and lack of medical oversight, warning that the AI's clinical decision-making could plausibly lead to harm if not properly regulated.
