### INC-12144  [↗](/incident/INC-12144.html)

**Fat Free CRM vulnerable to Exposure of Sensitive Information**  
_2022-05 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: Medium_

CVEs: `CVE-2013-7249`
CWEs: `CWE-200`

Fat Free CRM before 0.12.1 does not restrict XML serialization, which allows remote attackers to obtain sensitive information via a direct request, as demonstrated by a request for `users/1.xml`, a different vulnerability than CVE-2013-7224.

**Affected:** rubygems/fat_free_crm  
**Attack vector:** `info-disclosure`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM03`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI02`, `ASI04`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-6.1`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.10`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0010`, `AML.T0024`, `AML.T0053`, `AML.T0057`
