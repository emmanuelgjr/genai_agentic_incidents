**AI Surveillance Drones Raise Civil Liberties and Safety Concerns in U.S. Cities**  
_2020-09-15 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

U.S. government agencies have begun deploying AI-enabled surveillance drones, such as Gorgon Stare, over American cities. These systems, originally used in military operations, raise significant concerns about violations of privacy, civil liberties, and public safety due to persistent monitoring and potential for accidents in populated areas.
