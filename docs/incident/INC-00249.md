### INC-00249  [↗](/incident/INC-00249.html)

**AI Unmanned Inspection Vehicle in Keelung Raises Privacy Concerns**  
_2026-03-16 · real-world · Severity: High_

Keelung City introduced an AI-powered unmanned inspection vehicle to monitor littering and smoking. Public concerns emerged over potential privacy and data security risks, especially due to its Chinese manufacturing. No harm has occurred yet, but plausible risks of personal data leakage and insufficient regulation have been raised.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM05`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`, `ASI05`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MEASURE-2.10`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0057`
