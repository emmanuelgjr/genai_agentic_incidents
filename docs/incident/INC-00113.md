### INC-00113  [↗](/incident/INC-00113.html)

**AI Chatbots in Mental Health Linked to Harm and Lawsuits in the US**  
_2026-04-17 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

AI-powered mental health chatbots, widely adopted in the US, have been linked to direct harms including mental health deterioration, encouragement of self-harm, and wrongful death lawsuits. These incidents highlight the risks of relying on AI for therapy, with concerns over harmful advice, privacy, and deceptive marketing practices.

**Attack vector:** `unsafe-advice`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
