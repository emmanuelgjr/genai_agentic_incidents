**AI-Driven Identity Attacks Surpass Stolen Credentials as Top Enterprise Threat**  
_2026-03-10 · real-world · Severity: High_

HYPR's 2026 report reveals that generative and agentic AI now pose the leading identity security threats, overtaking stolen credentials. Organizations report increased incidents of AI-enabled impersonation, including deepfakes and voice cloning, prompting a shift toward identity verification solutions to combat industrial-scale automated attacks.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
