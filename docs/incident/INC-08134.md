**AI-Driven Cyberattacks Surge in Taiwan, Semiconductor Sector Hit Hard**  
_2026-06-07 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

AI-powered cyberattacks have sharply increased in Taiwan, with organizations facing nearly 4,000 attacks weekly—92% above the global average. The semiconductor industry is a primary target, suffering from a tripling of ransomware victims. Attackers leverage AI for rapid, automated, and large-scale breaches, overwhelming traditional defenses.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`, `ASI05`, `ASI10`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MEASURE-2.10`, `MEASURE-2.7`
