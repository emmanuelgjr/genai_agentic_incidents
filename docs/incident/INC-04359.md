### INC-04359  [↗](/incident/INC-04359.html)

**WPP CEO Mark Read impersonated in deepfake voice-cloning scam**  
_2024-05 · real-world · Severity: High_

Fraudsters created a WhatsApp account purporting to belong to WPP CEO Mark Read and set up a Microsoft Teams meeting with another senior WPP executive in which the attackers deployed a deepfake video and voice clone of Read, aiming to solicit money and personal details. The attempt failed but illustrated executive impersonation risk at one of the world's largest marketing groups.

**Affected:** WPP (UK marketing services)  
**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
