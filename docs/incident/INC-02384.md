### INC-02384  [↗](/incident/INC-02384.html)

**Canadian Fraud Ring Allegedly Used AI Voice Cloning in Multi-Year $21 Million Grandparent Scam Targeting Elderly Americans Across 46 States**  
_2025-03-05 · real-world · Severity: Critical_
AIID: [`#973`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/973/)

A Canadian fraud ring allegedly used AI-generated voice cloning to defraud victims across 46 U.S. states by targeting grandparents in a $21 million scam between 2021 and 2024. Operating from call centers in Montreal, the scammers spoofed U.S. phone numbers and used AI-cloned voices of grandchildren to convince victims to pay fake bail fees.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
