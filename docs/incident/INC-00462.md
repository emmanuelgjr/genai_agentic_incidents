### INC-00462  [↗](/incident/INC-00462.html)

**AI-Generated Fake Pregnancy Image Causes Harm to João Gomes' Family**  
_2026-03-16 · real-world · Severity: High_

Ary Mirelle, wife of singer João Gomes, publicly denounced a social media profile for using AI to create and spread a fake pregnancy image of their family. The AI-generated content led to misinformation, reputational damage, and emotional distress, highlighting the misuse of AI for deceptive purposes in Brazil.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
