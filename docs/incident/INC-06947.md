### INC-06947  [↗](/incident/INC-06947.html)

**Tech Giants Sued for Unlawful Use of Biometric Data in AI Facial Recognition Training**  
_2020-07-15 · real-world · Severity: High_

Illinois residents Steven Vance and Tim Janecyk filed lawsuits against Microsoft, Amazon, and Google, alleging the companies used their facial images without consent to train AI facial recognition systems, violating Illinois' Biometric Information Privacy Act. The images were sourced from IBM's Diversity in Faces database, raising significant privacy and legal concerns.
