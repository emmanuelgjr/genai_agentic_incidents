### INC-00516  [↗](/incident/INC-00516.html)

**AI-Generated Voice Scam Impersonates Bharti Singh in Slimming Product Fraud**  
_2026-03-09 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Indian comedian Bharti Singh warned fans after fraudsters used AI to generate her voice and image in online ads falsely endorsing slimming products. The scam deceived consumers and violated her rights, highlighting the harmful misuse of AI for identity theft and fraudulent marketing in India.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
