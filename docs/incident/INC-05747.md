### INC-05747  [↗](/incident/INC-05747.html)

**London Councils Use AI Surveillance Linked to Uyghur Human Rights Abuses**  
_2021-02-18 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Over half of London’s borough councils have deployed AI-powered surveillance systems from Chinese firms Hikvision and Dahua, both linked to human rights abuses against Uyghurs in Xinjiang. The use of these facial recognition and video analytics technologies has raised concerns about indirect involvement in repression and potential harm to local communities.
