### INC-00434  [↗](/incident/INC-00434.html)

**AI-Generated Disinformation Threatens Democracies, Study Finds**  
_2026-04-16 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A study by Agência Lupa, analyzing 1,294 professional fact-checks in over ten languages, found that 81.2% of AI-driven disinformation cases emerged in the past two years. AI-generated deepfakes and misinformation, especially on elections and conflicts, are rapidly spreading, undermining public trust and threatening democratic processes globally.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
