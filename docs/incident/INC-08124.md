### INC-08124  [↗](/incident/INC-08124.html)

**Xuxa Meneghel Sues Company Over Unauthorized Deepfake Use in Online Advertising**  
_2026-06-06 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Brazilian presenter Xuxa Meneghel filed a lawsuit against Bagy Soluções de Comércio Digital after her image was manipulated with deepfake AI technology for an online ad campaign without consent. The deepfake falsely associated her with a promotional service, prompting legal action for damages and reputational harm.
