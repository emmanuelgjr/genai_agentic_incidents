**Waymo Autonomous Taxi Disrupts London Crime Scene During Testing**  
_2026-04-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

A Waymo autonomous taxi, under manual control, drove into a police crime scene in Harlesden, London, breaching police tape and narrowly missing a police car during a double stabbing investigation. The incident disrupted police operations and raised concerns about the safety and readiness of AI-driven vehicles in complex urban environments.
