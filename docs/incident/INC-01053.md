### INC-01053  [↗](/incident/INC-01053.html)

**Frontier AI Models Accelerate Cyberattack Capabilities**  
_2026-04-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

Recent testing by cybersecurity researchers, including Unit 42 and Palo Alto Networks, reveals that advanced frontier AI models can autonomously discover software vulnerabilities and generate exploits at unprecedented speed. This development significantly increases the risk and scale of AI-enabled cyberattacks, potentially shrinking defenders' response windows and threatening critical infrastructure.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM03`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI02`, `ASI04`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-6.1`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.10`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0010`, `AML.T0053`, `AML.T0057`
