**Global Militaries Advance AI-Enabled Autonomous Combat Drones**  
_2021-01-11 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

India, Russia, and the US are advancing AI-powered autonomous drones for military use, including drone swarms and 'wingman' systems capable of independent targeting and lethal operations. Recent demonstrations and tests highlight the growing potential for AI-driven weapon systems to cause future harm in warfare, despite no current incidents of realized harm.
