**German Police Use AI-Generated Child Abuse Material in Investigations**  
_2020-08-13 · real-world · Severity: High_

Police in North Rhine-Westphalia, Germany, have used AI-generated child sexual abuse material for the first time to infiltrate criminal networks. Enabled by a recent legal change, investigators deployed synthetic images and videos to gain trust and access in online groups during ongoing child abuse investigations, notably the Bergisch Gladbach case.
