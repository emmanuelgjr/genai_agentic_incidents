**AI-Generated Legal Documents Cause Harm in Fraudulent Online Law Firms in China**  
_2026-03-18 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Several clients in China suffered financial and procedural harm after online law firms used AI-generated legal documents containing errors, leading to case dismissals and loss of money. Deceptive marketing and fraudulent practices, including misleading AI use, were reported, prompting regulatory scrutiny and penalties for involved firms.
