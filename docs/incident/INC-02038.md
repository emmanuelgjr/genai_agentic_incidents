**Valve Develops SteamGPT AI for Moderation and Anti-Cheat on Steam**  
_2026-04-10 · real-world · Severity: High_

Valve is developing SteamGPT, an internal AI tool designed to automate moderation and analyze cheating reports on Steam, including games like Counter-Strike 2. While not yet deployed, the system could impact user management and risk wrongful bans or privacy issues if misused or malfunctioning.
