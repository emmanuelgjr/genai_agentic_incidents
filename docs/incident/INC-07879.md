**Executives Anticipate Widespread AI-Driven Layoffs Despite Unclear Financial Benefits**  
_2026-05-24 · real-world · Severity: High_

Multiple surveys reveal that 99% of CEOs and executives expect AI-driven automation to cause significant workforce reductions within two years, with layoffs already occurring in some sectors. Despite these cuts, most companies have not seen improved financial returns, raising concerns about the impact of AI on employment and worker well-being.
