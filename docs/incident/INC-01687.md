### INC-01687  [↗](/incident/INC-01687.html)

**Punjab Cyber Fraudsters Use AI Voice-Changing and Deepfake Videos for Extortion**  
_2026-03-10 · real-world · Severity: High_

Punjab Police arrested three cyber fraudsters who used AI-powered voice-changing software and AI-generated objectionable videos to impersonate women, create fake social media profiles, and extort money from victims, primarily women. The gang earned around Rs 40 lakh, targeting individuals in India and abroad, including a victim residing in Canada.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`
