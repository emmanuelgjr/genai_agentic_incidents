### INC-11677  [↗](/incident/INC-11677.html)

**Dromara Hutool Deserialization of Untrusted Data vulnerability**  
_2023-01 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: Critical_

CVEs: `CVE-2023-24162`
CWEs: `CWE-502`
CVSS: **9.8** — `CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:U/C:H/I:H/A:H`

**Affected:** maven/cn.hutool:hutool-all  
**Attack vector:** `deserialization`
