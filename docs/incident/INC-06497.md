### INC-06497  [↗](/incident/INC-06497.html)

**Deepfake AI Technology Raises Concerns Over Fraud and Misinformation Risks**  
_2020-01-27 · real-world · Severity: High_

Multiple reports highlight growing concern among cybersecurity and media experts about the potential harms of AI-generated deepfakes, including fraud in financial services and the spread of misinformation. Despite widespread awareness of these risks, few organizations have implemented effective countermeasures, leaving industries and the public vulnerable to future AI-driven incidents.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
