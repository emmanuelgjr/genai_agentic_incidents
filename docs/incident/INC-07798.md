### INC-07798  [↗](/incident/INC-07798.html)

**AI Chatbot Romances Undermine Real-Life Relationships Among Young Adults**  
_2026-05-19 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A BYU and Institute for Family Studies report finds that 1 in 7 young adults in committed U.S. relationships regularly engage in secret romantic interactions with AI chatbots. This trend is linked to decreased relationship stability, secrecy, and emotional harm, highlighting negative social impacts of AI companion technology.
