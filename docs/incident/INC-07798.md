### INC-07798  [↗](/incident/INC-07798.html)

**ESET Invests €40 Million to Counter AI-Driven Cybersecurity Risks**  
_2026-05-19 · real-world · Severity: High_

ESET, a European cybersecurity firm, is investing €40 million to enhance AI-based cyber defense after identifying new risks from autonomous AI agents and 'AI Skills.' Since March 2026, ESET analyzed 800,000 AI components, flagged 25,000 as suspicious, and blocked over 3,000, highlighting emerging software supply chain threats.

**Attack vector:** `supply-chain`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM03`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI04`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-6.1`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0010`
