### INC-06272  [↗](/incident/INC-06272.html)

**AI-Driven Bots and Ransomware Escalate Cyberattack Threats in Indonesia**  
_2020-01-11 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Cyberattacks in Indonesia are increasingly conducted by AI-enabled bots and ransomware, which use machine learning to optimize and intensify attacks on sectors like finance. These AI-driven attacks result in data breaches and financial losses, prompting experts to call for AI-based cybersecurity defenses.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`, `ASI05`, `ASI09`, `ASI10`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.10`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0048`, `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0057`, `AML.T0058`
