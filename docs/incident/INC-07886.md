### INC-07886  [↗](/incident/INC-07886.html)

**AI Voice Cloning Used in Fake Kidnapping Scam Leads to Financial Loss**  
_2026-05-24 · real-world · Severity: Medium_
AIID: [`#1505`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1505/)

In Martinez, California, scammers used AI voice cloning technology to convincingly mimic a woman's daughter, falsely claiming she had been kidnapped. The mother, Deborah Del Mastro, was deceived into wiring thousands of dollars, highlighting the growing threat of AI-enabled fraud and its direct financial and emotional harm.
