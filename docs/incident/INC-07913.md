**AI License Plate Readers Spark Privacy Uproar and State of Emergency in Troy, NY**  
_2026-05-16 · real-world · Severity: High_

In Troy, New York, the deployment of AI-assisted license plate reader cameras by Flock Safety triggered public outcry over privacy violations and civil liberties. The controversy led to political conflict between the mayor and city council, culminating in a state of emergency to keep the cameras operational.
