### INC-06580  [↗](/incident/INC-06580.html)

**Facebook's AI Fails to Block Political Disinformation During US Elections**  
_2020-11-02 · real-world · Severity: High_

Facebook's AI-driven ad moderation system failed to effectively block and remove false and misleading political ads during the US elections, allowing disinformation to be widely disseminated. Despite efforts to curb misinformation, technical flaws and weak enforcement enabled harmful content to reach millions, undermining electoral integrity.
