### INC-05578  [↗](/incident/INC-05578.html)

**DHS Implements AI-Driven Social Media Monitoring for Security Threats**  
_2021-05-10 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Department of Homeland Security is developing and deploying AI-based systems to analyze public social media posts for narratives predicting security threats, aiming to prevent incidents like the January 6 Capitol attack. While intended for public safety, the initiative raises concerns about civil liberties and potential misuse.
