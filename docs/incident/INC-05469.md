### INC-05469  [↗](/incident/INC-05469.html)

**Amazon's AI-Driven Mentor App Raises Privacy and Labor Rights Concerns for Delivery Drivers**  
_2021-02-12 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Amazon's AI-powered Mentor app continuously monitors delivery drivers, scoring their performance and influencing disciplinary actions. Drivers report privacy invasions and unfair treatment due to app bugs, raising significant concerns about labor rights violations and the misuse of AI surveillance in the workplace.
