### INC-06715  [↗](/incident/INC-06715.html)

**Instagram Algorithm Manipulated by Coordinated 'Pods' Using AI Detection Tools**  
_2020-04-27 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Researchers found that organized groups, or 'pods', systematically coordinate likes and comments to manipulate Instagram's recommendation algorithms, artificially boosting post visibility and distorting information dissemination. Machine learning tools were developed to detect such manipulation, highlighting ongoing harm to community trust and the integrity of online content.
