**AI-Driven Real-Time Ride Monitoring Launched by DiDi and Mexico City Police**  
_2026-06-02 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

DiDi and Mexico City's police (SSC) have partnered to deploy an AI system that monitors all rides in real time, detecting anomalies such as route deviations and unusual speed. The AI enables rapid police response, with special focus on gender-based incidents, enhancing passenger safety during urban mobility.
