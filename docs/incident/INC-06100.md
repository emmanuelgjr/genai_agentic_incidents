### INC-06100  [↗](/incident/INC-06100.html)

**Tesla Recalls 135,000 Vehicles Over AI System Safety Defect**  
_2021-02-02 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Tesla is recalling approximately 135,000 Model S and Model X vehicles in the U.S. due to a malfunction in the onboard computer, which affects AI-driven driver assistance features. The defect, identified by the NHTSA, can disable critical safety functions, increasing accident risk and prompting the large-scale recall.
