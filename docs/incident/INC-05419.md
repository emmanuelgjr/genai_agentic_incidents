**AI-Enabled Drones Raise Global Security and Privacy Concerns**  
_2021-04-25 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

North Korea, the UAE, and the EU are advancing AI-powered drones for military, surveillance, and border control purposes. North Korea tested offensive and reconnaissance drones, UAE police use facial recognition drones to track criminals, and Frontex plans long-range surveillance drones, all raising risks of future harm or rights violations.
