### INC-01237  [↗](/incident/INC-01237.html)

**Influencer Faces Backlash for AI Deepfake of Deceased Celebrity**  
_2026-04-15 · real-world · Severity: High_

Chilean influencer Cristóbal Romero used AI deepfake technology to create a video depicting the late Sebastián "Cangri" Leiva, sparking public outrage and emotional distress among followers and Leiva's family. The unauthorized use of AI to recreate the deceased was widely criticized as disrespectful and harmful.
