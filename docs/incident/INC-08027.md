**New York Times Accuses AI Firms of Copyright Infringement**  
_2026-06-01 · real-world · Severity: High_

New York Times publisher A.G. Sulzberger publicly accused AI companies, including OpenAI and Microsoft, of using copyrighted news content without permission or compensation to train AI systems. The Times is pursuing legal action, highlighting concerns over intellectual property violations and threats to journalism at the World News Media Congress in Marseille.
