### INC-08752  [↗](/incident/INC-08752.html)

**k8sGPT has Prompt Injection through its k8sGPT-Operator**  
_2026-04 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: High_
CWEs: `CWE-20`, `CWE-502`, `CWE-915`

### Summary In the auto-remediation pipeline, `object_to_execution.go` was deserializing the AI-generated YAML directly into a Deployment object, but there was lack of validation from the original Deployment object. ### Details This issue was fixed after coordination with Alex Jones. ### PoC To minimize the impact, the PoC of this vulnerability wasn't released, but was shared with the maintainers.

**Affected:** go/github.com/k8sgpt-ai/k8sgpt  
**Attack vector:** `prompt-injection`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM03`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI04`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-6.1`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0010`
