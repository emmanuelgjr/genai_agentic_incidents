**U.S. Air Force Deploys Semi-Autonomous Robot Dogs for Security Patrols**  
_2020-11-11 · real-world · Severity: High_

The U.S. Air Force's 325th Security Forces Squadron at Tyndall Air Force Base is the first military unit to deploy semi-autonomous, AI-powered robot dogs from Ghost Robotics for security patrols. While no harm has occurred, their use introduces potential future risks associated with autonomous military systems.
