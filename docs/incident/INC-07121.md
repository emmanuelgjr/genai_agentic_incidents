### INC-07121  [↗](/incident/INC-07121.html)

**WeChat Uses AI to Monitor and Censor International Users' Content for Chinese Censorship System**  
_2020-05-07 · real-world · Severity: High_

Investigations reveal that WeChat employs AI-driven surveillance and censorship on content shared by international users, not just those in China. Data from overseas accounts is analyzed and used to train censorship algorithms, infringing on users' privacy and freedom of expression, and extending Chinese political censorship globally.
