### INC-07801  [↗](/incident/INC-07801.html)

**AI-Driven Data Management System Prevents Data Loss in Cyberattack**  
_2026-05-19 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Everpure's AI-enabled data management system successfully prevented data loss and operational downtime during a sophisticated cyberattack that deleted endpoints and virtual clusters. The system's use of immutable storage, automated recovery, and human oversight ensured business continuity, highlighting AI's critical role in modern cyber resilience.
