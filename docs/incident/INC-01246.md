**Iran Attacks and Threatens AI Data Centers of Major Tech Companies**  
_2026-03-31 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Iran's Revolutionary Guard attacked Amazon and Oracle data centers in Bahrain and the UAE, targeting AI and intelligence infrastructure used by US and Israeli military operations. Iran also threatened further attacks on 18 major tech companies, including Google, Microsoft, Meta, and Apple, marking AI infrastructure as military targets.
