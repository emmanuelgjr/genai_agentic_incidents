### INC-08115  [↗](/incident/INC-08115.html)

**AI Misidentification of Mushroom Leads to User Poisoning in China**  
_2026-06-05 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A user in China relied on the Douyin Group's AI tool 'Doubao' to identify wild mushrooms, resulting in misidentification and consumption of a toxic mushroom. Despite explicit warnings from the AI, the user suffered acute kidney failure. The incident highlights risks of overreliance on AI in safety-critical situations.

**Attack vector:** `model-poisoning`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM04`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI06`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-4.2`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0020`, `AML.T0066`
