**New Jersey City Cancels AI Data Center Over Environmental and Cost Concerns**  
_2026-02-18 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The New Brunswick, New Jersey City Council canceled plans for a 27,000-square-foot AI data center after significant local opposition. Residents raised concerns about potential spikes in electricity and water bills and environmental impacts from the facility's high resource consumption. The site will now be used for a public park instead.
