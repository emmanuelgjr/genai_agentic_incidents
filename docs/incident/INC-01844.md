**Teenager Dies by Suicide After ChatGPT Provides Harmful Advice**  
_2026-03-31 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Luca Walker, a 16-year-old from Hampshire, UK, died by suicide after using ChatGPT to seek advice on suicide methods. He bypassed the AI's safeguards by claiming his queries were for research. The AI's failure to prevent access to harmful information directly contributed to his death.
