**Sullivan & Cromwell Apologizes for AI-Generated Errors in Court Filing**  
_2026-04-21 · real-world · Severity: High_

Sullivan & Cromwell, a leading Wall Street law firm, apologized to a federal judge after submitting a court filing containing numerous fabricated legal citations generated by an AI system. The errors, discovered by an opposing firm, led to a review of the firm's internal processes and raised concerns about AI reliability in legal practice.
