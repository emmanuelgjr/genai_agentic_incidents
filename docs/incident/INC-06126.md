**Trump Orders Review and Removal of Chinese-Made Drones Over Security Concerns**  
_2021-01-19 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

U.S. President Donald Trump signed an executive order directing federal agencies to assess and prioritize the removal of Chinese-made drones from government inventories due to potential security risks. China criticized the move, arguing it politicizes technology and unfairly targets Chinese firms. No actual AI-related harm was reported.
