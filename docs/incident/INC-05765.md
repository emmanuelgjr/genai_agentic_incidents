### INC-05765  [↗](/incident/INC-05765.html)

**Microsoft Patents AI Chatbots to Simulate Deceased Individuals**  
_2021-01-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

Microsoft has patented technology to create AI chatbots that simulate deceased people by analyzing their digital footprints, such as images, voice data, and social media posts. While not yet deployed, the concept raises concerns about privacy, consent, and emotional harm, highlighting potential future risks associated with such AI systems.
