**Senator Warren Warns of AI Industry Debt Bubble and Financial Crisis Risk**  
_2026-04-22 · real-world · Severity: High_

Senator Elizabeth Warren warned that the rapid expansion and heavy borrowing by AI companies, often from unregulated sources, could create a financial bubble similar to the 2008 crisis. She highlighted the risk of cascading economic harm if AI industry revenues fail to meet massive debt obligations, urging regulatory reforms to prevent systemic instability.
