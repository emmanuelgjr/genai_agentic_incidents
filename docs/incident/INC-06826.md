### INC-06826  [↗](/incident/INC-06826.html)

**OnePlus 8 Pro's AI Camera Filter Disabled Over Privacy Concerns**  
_2020-05-17 · real-world · Severity: High_

The OnePlus 8 Pro's AI-enabled color filter camera, capable of seeing through certain materials and fabrics, raised significant privacy concerns. In response, OnePlus temporarily disabled the feature via software updates, acknowledging the risk of privacy violations and committing to address the issue before re-enabling the function.
