### INC-07114  [↗](/incident/INC-07114.html)

**Volvo Recalls Over 700,000 Vehicles Due to AI-Driven Emergency Braking System Malfunction**  
_2020-03-18 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Volvo recalled over 700,000 vehicles globally from model years 2019-2020 after discovering a software defect in the AI-powered automatic emergency braking system, which may fail to detect obstacles or pedestrians, increasing crash risk. No injuries have been reported, and a software update is being provided to fix the issue.

**Attack vector:** `other`
