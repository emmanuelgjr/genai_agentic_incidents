### INC-00379  [↗](/incident/INC-00379.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfake Investment Scams Defraud Victims in Turkey**  
_2026-02-18 · real-world · Severity: High_

A criminal group in Turkey used AI to create deepfake videos of celebrities promoting fake investment schemes on social media, leading to at least one victim losing 882,000 lira. Authorities uncovered 20 million lira in fraudulent transactions over one month, arresting four suspects across four cities.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
