### INC-02224  [↗](/incident/INC-02224.html)

**AI-generated deepfake videos of Portuguese public figures used in crypto scams**  
_2025-09-11 · real-world · Severity: High_

Deepfake videos and synthetic audio of Portuguese public figures, including Prime Minister Luis Montenegro, circulated on social media promoting fraudulent crypto-trading platforms that took payments from victims.

**Affected:** Portuguese public  
**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`

**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`
