**Indonesia Urges Stronger Digital Financial Security Amid AI-Driven Cyber Threats**  
_2026-06-03 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Indonesian authorities have called on financial institutions to strengthen digital security as cybercriminals increasingly use AI for sophisticated fraud and ransomware attacks. The warnings highlight the growing risk of AI-enabled cybercrime in Indonesia’s rapidly expanding digital finance sector, though no specific incident of harm has been reported.
