**Israel Procures AI-Enabled Spike FireFly Loitering Munitions for Urban Combat**  
_2020-05-04 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The Israeli Ministry of Defense has ordered Rafael's Spike FireFly loitering munitions, AI-enabled drones capable of autonomously searching for and attacking targets in urban environments. While no harm has yet occurred, the deployment of these autonomous weapons poses credible risks of future injury or lethal incidents.
