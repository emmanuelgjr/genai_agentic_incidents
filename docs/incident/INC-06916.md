### INC-06916  [↗](/incident/INC-06916.html)

**Smart Speakers' AI Malfunction Leads to Frequent Accidental Eavesdropping and Privacy Violations**  
_2020-02-22 · real-world · Severity: High_

A Northeastern University study found that AI-powered smart speakers like Alexa, Siri, and Google Assistant are accidentally activated up to 19 times daily, often by mishearing words from conversations or TV. This malfunction results in unintended recordings, raising significant privacy concerns and potential security risks for users.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`
