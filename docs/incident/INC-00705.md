### INC-00705  [↗](/incident/INC-00705.html)

**Brazilian Government Orders Google to Remove AI-Generated Deepfake Nude Sites**  
_2026-04-01 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The Brazilian Attorney General's Office (AGU) has ordered Google to remove and block search results for over 40 sites that use AI and deepfake technology to create non-consensual sexualized images of real people. The main victims are women, children, and adolescents. Google has five days to comply.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
