### INC-05122  [↗](/incident/INC-05122.html)

**Glovo Driver in Italy Fired via Automated Email after Being Killed in Accident**  
_2022-10-03 · real-world · Severity: Critical_
AIID: [`#384`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/384/)

On October 1, 2022, Sebastian Galassi, a 26-year-old graphic design student working as a delivery driver for Glovo in Florence, Italy, died when his moped collided with a Land Rover SUV while making a late-night delivery. The following day, October 2, Galassi's family received an automated email from Glovo's system informing them that Sebastian had been fired for 'non-compliance with the terms and conditions' due to missing delivery times. The email was an automated response triggered when riders miss delivery deadlines. Glovo representatives called the family to apologize, claiming the message was sent 'by mistake' and offering to pay some funeral costs. The incident sparked a 24-hour strike by delivery drivers organized by the CGIL trade union on October 5, 2022. The mayor of Florence criticized the 'inhumane system' where algorithms send firing emails without ethical considerations. The union highlighted how the piecework payment system forces drivers to rush and take risks to earn more money, contributing to high accident rates in the delivery sector. This was part of a pattern of delivery driver deaths in Italy, with three other fatalities reported in Tuscany in 2022.
