### INC-05708  [↗](/incident/INC-05708.html)

**Instagram AI Search Algorithm Promotes Harmful Diet Content to Vulnerable Users**  
_2021-04-15 · real-world · Severity: High_

Instagram's AI-powered search recommendation system mistakenly suggested harmful dieting terms, such as 'appetite suppressants' and 'fasting', to users with eating disorders. This led to psychological harm and risk of relapse. Instagram has apologized, acknowledged the error, and removed the problematic terms from its platform.
