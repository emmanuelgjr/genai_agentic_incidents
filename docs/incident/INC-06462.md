### INC-06462  [↗](/incident/INC-06462.html)

**Clearview AI Suspends Facial Recognition Services in Canada Amid Privacy Investigation**  
_2020-07-06 · real-world · Severity: High_

Clearview AI has halted its facial recognition services in Canada following an ongoing investigation by federal and provincial privacy commissioners into potential privacy rights violations. The company also ended its contract with the RCMP, reflecting concerns over the AI system's collection and use of biometric data.
