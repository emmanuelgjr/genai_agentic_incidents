**Conflict in Middle East Disrupts AI Data Centers and Infrastructure**  
_2026-05-25 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Ongoing conflict in the Middle East has led to attacks on AI-related data centers, including Amazon AWS facilities in the UAE and Bahrain, causing physical damage and operational disruption. The instability threatens regional AI infrastructure, increases costs, and delays investments, undermining ambitions to become a global AI hub.
