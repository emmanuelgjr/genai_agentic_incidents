**Alleged AI-Generated Photo of Burning Truck in Manila Reportedly Triggered Firefighter Response**  
_2025-04-26 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1197`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1197/)

Firefighters in Manila reportedly responded to a supposed burning truck after being alerted with a photo later determined to have purportedly been AI-generated. Four fire trucks, including units from the Bureau of Fire Protection, reportedly arrived on the scene but found the vehicle intact. The false alert allegedly wasted emergency resources.
