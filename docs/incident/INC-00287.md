**AI-Driven Data Centers Spark Environmental and Social Conflicts in Latin America**  
_2026-03-10 · real-world · Severity: High_

The rapid expansion of AI-driven data centers in Latin America is causing significant environmental and social harm, including water and energy shortages, noise pollution, and land use conflicts. These impacts are affecting local communities and raising concerns about human rights and environmental sustainability.
