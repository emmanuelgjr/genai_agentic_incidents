### INC-08102  [↗](/incident/INC-08102.html)

**Meta Secretly Embeds Facial Recognition in Smart Glasses App, Raising Privacy Concerns**  
_2026-06-05 · real-world · Severity: High_

Meta has covertly integrated facial recognition AI code, known as 'NameTag', into its Meta View app for Ray-Ban and Oakley smart glasses, now present on millions of devices. Although not yet activated, the system could identify individuals in real time, raising significant privacy and human rights concerns.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`  

**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-4.1`, `MEASURE-2.10`
