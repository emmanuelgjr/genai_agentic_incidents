### INC-01094  [↗](/incident/INC-01094.html)

**Global Financial Leaders Warn of AI Risks to Financial Systems from Anthropic's Mythos**  
_2026-04-17 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Bank of Canada Governor Tiff Macklem and international financial officials have raised concerns about the potential risks posed by Anthropic's upcoming AI model, Mythos, which can rapidly detect cybersecurity vulnerabilities. Discussions among regulators and banks highlight fears that such AI advances could disrupt global financial systems if not properly managed.
