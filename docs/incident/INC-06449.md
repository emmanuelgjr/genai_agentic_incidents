### INC-06449  [↗](/incident/INC-06449.html)

**Chinese Court Rules AI-Generated Article Protected by Copyright**  
_2020-01-08 · real-world · Severity: High_

A Shenzhen court ruled that an article generated by Tencent's AI system, Dreamwriter, is entitled to copyright protection. The court found Shanghai Yingxun Technology Company liable for copying the AI-written article without permission, marking a significant legal precedent for intellectual property rights in AI-generated content.
