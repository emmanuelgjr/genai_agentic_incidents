**Over a Million London Jobs at Risk from AI Automation, Mayor Warns**  
_2026-04-27 · real-world · Severity: High_

A report commissioned by London Mayor Sadiq Khan warns that over a million Londoners are at high or significant risk of job disruption due to generative AI, with administrative roles most exposed. Nearly half of the city's workforce could see tasks automated, raising concerns about economic disruption and inequality.
