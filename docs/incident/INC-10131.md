### INC-10131  [↗](/incident/INC-10131.html)

**NextAuthjs Email misdelivery Vulnerability**  
_2025-10 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: Medium_
CWEs: `CWE-200`

### Summary NextAuth.js's email sign-in can be forced to deliver authentication emails to an attacker-controlled mailbox due to a bug in `nodemailer`'s address parser used by the project (fixed in `nodemailer` **v7.0.7**). A crafted input such as: ``` "e@attacker.com"@victim.com ``` is parsed incorrectly and results in the message being delivered to `e@attacker.com` (attacker) instead of `"<e@attacker.com>@victim.com"` (the intended recipient at `victim.com`) in violation of RFC 5321/5322 semantics.
