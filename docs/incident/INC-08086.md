**AI-Generated Deepfake Political Ad Sparks Election Law Controversy in Minnesota**  
_2026-06-04 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A political action committee aired an attack ad against Minnesota Lt. Gov. and Senate candidate Peggy Flanagan, using an AI-generated deepfake image to misrepresent her. The ad has drawn criticism for misleading voters and raised legal concerns under Minnesota's law banning deepfakes in election ads, highlighting AI's impact on political integrity.
