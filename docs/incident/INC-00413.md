### INC-00413  [↗](/incident/INC-00413.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfakes and Online Abuse Drive Women from Public Life**  
_2026-04-30 · real-world · Severity: High_

A UN Women report reveals that AI-generated deepfakes and technologically advanced online abuse are increasingly targeting women journalists, activists, and human rights defenders globally. These AI-enabled attacks have led to psychological harm, self-censorship, and withdrawal from public life, undermining women's rights and participation.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
