### INC-06899  [↗](/incident/INC-06899.html)

**Safari's AI-Powered Anti-Tracking Feature Exposes User Privacy**  
_2020-01-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

Apple's Safari Intelligent Tracking Prevention, a machine learning-based anti-tracking system, inadvertently enabled attackers to infer users' browsing and search histories and create persistent identifiers, leading to privacy breaches. Researchers found that the AI-driven protections could be exploited, undermining user privacy instead of safeguarding it.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`  

**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-4.1`, `MEASURE-2.10`
