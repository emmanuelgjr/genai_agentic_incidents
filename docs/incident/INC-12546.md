### INC-12546  [↗](/incident/INC-12546.html)

**Cross-site Scripting in HTML2PDF**  
_2022-01 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: High_

CVEs: `CVE-2021-45394`
CWEs: `CWE-79`, `CWE-918`
CVSS: **8.8** — `CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:R/S:U/C:H/I:H/A:H`

An issue was discovered in Spipu HTML2PDF before 5.2.4. Attackers can trigger deserialization of arbitrary data via the injection of a malicious <link> tag in the converted HTML document.

**Affected:** composer/spipu/html2pdf  
**Attack vector:** `deserialization`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM03`, `LLM05`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI04`, `ASI05`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-6.1`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0010`, `AML.T0010.001`, `AML.T0050`
