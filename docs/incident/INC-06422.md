### INC-06422  [↗](/incident/INC-06422.html)

**Canadian Mall Operator Illegally Collected Shoppers' Biometric Data Using AI Facial Recognition**  
_2020-10-29 · real-world · Severity: High_

Cadillac Fairview, a major Canadian mall operator, used AI-powered facial recognition in kiosks at 12 malls to collect images and biometric data from five million shoppers without their consent. Privacy watchdogs found this violated privacy laws, as sensitive data was stored by a third party, raising significant privacy and security concerns.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MEASURE-2.10`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0057`
