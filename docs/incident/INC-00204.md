### INC-00204  [↗](/incident/INC-00204.html)

**AI Startup Accused of Stealing Artist's Work for Ad Campaign**  
_2026-05-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI startup Artisan used KC Green's copyrighted "This is fine" comic without permission in an ad campaign promoting its AI product. The unauthorized use, displayed in a subway ad, violated Green's intellectual property rights, prompting the artist to seek legal action. The incident highlights AI-related copyright concerns.
