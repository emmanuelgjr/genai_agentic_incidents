### INC-00392  [↗](/incident/INC-00392.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfake Video Falsely Claims Indonesian Finance Minister Offers Business Grants**  
_2026-04-20 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A deepfake video created using AI falsely depicted Indonesia's Finance Minister, Purbaya Yudhi Sadewa, offering Rp85 million in business grants. The Ministry of Finance confirmed the video as a hoax and warned the public against misinformation and potential scams resulting from AI-generated content.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`
