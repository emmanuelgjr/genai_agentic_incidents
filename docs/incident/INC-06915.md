**Siri Labels Footballer Lorenzo Insigne with Offensive Term, Prompting Outcry and Apple Response**  
_2020-12-15 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Apple's AI assistant Siri displayed the derogatory term "terrone" alongside footballer Lorenzo Insigne's name, sparking widespread social media protests and accusations of discrimination. The incident, discovered by writer Maurizio De Giovanni, led Apple to quickly remove the offensive label from Siri's responses.
