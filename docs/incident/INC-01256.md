### INC-01256  [↗](/incident/INC-01256.html)

**Israeli Military Uses AI-Generated Image to Justify Killing Lebanese Journalist**  
_2026-04-15 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The Israeli military used an AI-manipulated image to falsely portray Lebanese journalist Ali Shuaib as a militant, justifying his killing in a March airstrike. The Foreign Press Association condemned this misuse of AI, warning it undermines journalist credibility and endangers media professionals. The incident occurred in southern Lebanon.
