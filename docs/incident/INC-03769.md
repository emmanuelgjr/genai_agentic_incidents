**Facial Recognition Misidentification at New World Westend in New Zealand**  
_2024-04-02 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#665`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/665/)

A facial recognition system at New World Westend supermarket misidentified a Māori woman as a known offender during its trial. The woman was wrongfully accused of trespassing and experienced public embarrassment, raising concerns about racial bias and the technology&#x27;s accuracy. The supermarket acknowledged its error and apologized.
