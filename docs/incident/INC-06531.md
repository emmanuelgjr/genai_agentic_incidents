**EU Consumer Groups Warn of AI-Driven Privacy Risks in Google-Fitbit Merger**  
_2020-05-13 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The European consumer group BEUC warns that Google's $2.1 billion acquisition of Fitbit could harm consumer interests and stifle innovation by giving Google access to vast health data collected via AI-powered fitness trackers, raising concerns over privacy, competition, and potential misuse of sensitive personal information.
