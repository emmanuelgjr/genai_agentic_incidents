### INC-06101  [↗](/incident/INC-06101.html)

**Tesla Recalls 285,000 Cars in China Over AI Driving Software Malfunction Leading to Collisions**  
_2021-06-26 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Tesla is recalling over 285,000 Model 3 and Y vehicles in China after authorities found that the AI-assisted driving software could be accidentally activated, causing sudden acceleration and collisions, including fatal ones. The recall follows multiple incidents and regulatory investigations into the software's safety risks.
