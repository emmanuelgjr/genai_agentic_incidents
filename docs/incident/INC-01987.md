**US Army Launches AI Integration Initiative for Military Systems**  
_2026-05-05 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

US Army Secretary Dan Driscoll, alongside major defense contractors, has initiated the 'Right to Integrate Hackathon' to enhance interoperability and AI integration in military systems. The effort aims to enable weapons, sensors, and autonomous platforms to communicate and share data, potentially increasing future risks associated with AI-enabled weaponry.
