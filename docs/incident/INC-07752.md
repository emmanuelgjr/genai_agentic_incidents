**North Korea Tests AI-Guided Missiles, Raising Regional Security Concerns**  
_2026-05-25 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

North Korea, under Kim Jong Un's supervision, tested AI-guided ballistic and cruise missiles, including nuclear-capable systems, near the South Korean border. The AI technology enhances missile accuracy and lethality, escalating risks to regional security and raising concerns about the deployment of autonomous weapons in modern warfare.
