**Paperclip: Approval decision attribution spoofing via client-controlled `decidedByUserId` in paperclip server**  
_2026-04 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: Medium_
CWEs: `CWE-345`
CVSS: **4.3** — `CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:L/UI:N/S:U/C:N/I:L/A:N`

## Summary The approval-resolution endpoints (`POST /approvals/:id/approve`, `/reject`, `/request-revision`) accept a client-supplied `decidedByUserId` field in the request body and write it verbatim into the authoritative `approvals.decidedByUserId` column — without cross-checking it against the authenticated actor. Any board user who can access an approval's company can record the decision as having been made by another user (e.g. the CEO), forging the governance audit trail.
