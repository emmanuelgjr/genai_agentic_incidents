### INC-00986  [↗](/incident/INC-00986.html)

**Fake Gemini AI Chatbot Used in Google Coin Crypto Scam**  
_2026-02-18 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Scammers deployed a fake AI chatbot impersonating Google's Gemini assistant to promote a fraudulent "Google Coin" cryptocurrency presale. The chatbot used convincing branding and real-time interaction to pressure victims into sending irreversible crypto payments, resulting in direct financial harm. The incident highlights the growing use of AI in online fraud schemes.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
