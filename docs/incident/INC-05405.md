**AI-Driven Sex Robots and Virtual Companions Raise Exploitation and Social Risks**  
_2021-05-02 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Experts warn that AI-powered sex robots, virtual friends, and matchmaking platforms could exploit users, manipulate emotions, and increase social inequality. While these technologies offer companionship and support, they also pose significant risks of blackmail, manipulation, and the erosion of genuine human relationships, especially among vulnerable groups.
