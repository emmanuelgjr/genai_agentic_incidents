### INC-06266  [↗](/incident/INC-06266.html)

**AI-Based Cybersecurity Trials for Buildings and Connected Cars Launched in Japan**  
_2020-01-14 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Tokyo Tatemono, Panasonic, and Fujitsu are conducting demonstration experiments using AI to detect cyberattacks in building automation systems and connected cars. These AI systems are being tested to identify simulated threats, aiming to prevent future cyber incidents, but no actual harm or incidents have occurred yet.

**Attack vector:** `other`
