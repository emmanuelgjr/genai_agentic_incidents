### INC-06252  [↗](/incident/INC-06252.html)

**AI System Developed to Detect Illicit Cryptocurrency Mining on Supercomputers**  
_2020-08-25 · real-world · Severity: High_

Researchers at Los Alamos National Laboratory have developed an AI-powered system to detect unauthorized cryptocurrency mining (cryptojacking) on supercomputers. The system analyzes program behavior using graph-based methods to identify malicious mining activity, aiming to protect critical computing resources from misuse and prevent harm to infrastructure.
