**Israeli Brothers Used AI to Fabricate Military Intelligence for Iranian Agent**  
_2026-03-24 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Two brothers from Jerusalem were indicted for using AI tools like ChatGPT, Grok, and Gemini to generate fake military documents and intelligence, which they sent to an Iranian agent via Telegram. They received over 100,000 shekels in cryptocurrency, causing security risks and wrongful harm through AI-generated deception.
