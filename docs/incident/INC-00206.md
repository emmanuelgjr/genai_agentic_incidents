### INC-00206  [↗](/incident/INC-00206.html)

**AI Surveillance Prevents Major Poaching Attempt in Odisha's Similipal Sanctuary**  
_2026-02-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI-enabled cameras in Odisha's Similipal sanctuary detected the movement of 39 armed poachers, triggering real-time alerts that enabled authorities to mobilize quickly. The operation led to the surrender and arrest of the poachers, seizure of weapons, and prevention of significant harm to wildlife and the environment.
