### INC-01869  [↗](/incident/INC-01869.html)

**Tesla Robotaxi AI System Involved in Multiple Low-Speed Crashes**  
_2026-03-16 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Tesla's Robotaxi program has reported its 15th crash, with a Model Y hitting a fixed object at 9 mph while the autonomous driving system was engaged. All incidents involved the AI system, causing property damage but no injuries. The crash rate is significantly higher than human drivers, raising safety concerns.

**Attack vector:** `other`
