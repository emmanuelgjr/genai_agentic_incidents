### INC-06642  [↗](/incident/INC-06642.html)

**Global Inaction on Regulating Lethal Autonomous Weapons Raises AI Risks**  
_2020-01-14 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Major nations are rapidly developing lethal autonomous weapons systems (LAWS) that use AI to independently identify and kill targets, but international laws to govern their use lag behind. Experts warn that lack of regulation could lead to significant future harm, including civilian casualties and human rights violations.
