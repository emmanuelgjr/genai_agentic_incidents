### INC-00222  [↗](/incident/INC-00222.html)

**AI System Recovers Stolen Painting After 50 Years in Italy**  
_2026-04-09 · real-world · Severity: High_

Italian authorities used the AI-powered Stolen Works of Art Detection System (Swoads) to scan online platforms and identify a painting stolen from Feltre's art gallery in 1972. The system matched the artwork to a database of stolen items, enabling its recovery and return, resolving long-standing harm to cultural property.
