### INC-05709  [↗](/incident/INC-05709.html)

**Instagram AI Wrongly Flags Al-Aqsa Mosque Posts as Terrorism**  
_2021-05-12 · real-world · Severity: High_

Instagram's AI-driven content moderation system mistakenly labeled posts and hashtags about the Al-Aqsa Mosque—a major Islamic holy site—as linked to terrorism, leading to widespread removal and censorship of legitimate content during ongoing Israeli-Palestinian violence. The error restricted information and violated users' rights to free expression.
