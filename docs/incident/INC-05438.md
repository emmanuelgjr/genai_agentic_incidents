### INC-05438  [↗](/incident/INC-05438.html)

**AI-Powered Smart Cushions Used for Employee Surveillance in Hangzhou Company**  
_2021-01-02 · real-world · Severity: High_

A Hangzhou tech company issued AI-enabled smart cushions to employees, which monitored biometric and behavioral data and shared it with HR without proper consent. Employees felt their privacy was violated and likened the experience to being constantly surveilled, raising concerns over workplace rights and ethical use of AI monitoring systems.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`

**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MEASURE-2.10`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0057`
