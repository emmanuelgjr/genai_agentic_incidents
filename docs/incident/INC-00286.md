**AI-Driven Cybercrime Causes 389% Surge in Ransomware Victims**  
_2026-04-30 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Fortinet's 2026 Global Threat Landscape Report reveals a 389% year-over-year increase in ransomware victims, driven by cybercriminals' use of AI-powered tools like WormGPT, FraudGPT, and BruteForceAI. These AI-enabled attacks have caused significant harm across sectors, highlighting the growing threat of agentic AI in global cybercrime.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM05`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`, `ASI05`, `ASI09`, `ASI10`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.10`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0048`, `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0057`
