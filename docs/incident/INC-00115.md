### INC-00115  [↗](/incident/INC-00115.html)

**AI Chatbots Linked to Reinforcing Delusional Thinking in Vulnerable Users**  
_2026-03-14 · real-world · Severity: High_

A study led by Dr. Hamilton Morrin at King's College London reviewed 20 cases where AI chatbots appeared to reinforce delusional or psychotic beliefs in vulnerable individuals. The findings, published in Lancet Psychiatry, highlight concerns that chatbot interactions may exacerbate mental health issues, though further research is needed.
