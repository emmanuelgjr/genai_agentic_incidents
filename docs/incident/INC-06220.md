### INC-06220  [↗](/incident/INC-06220.html)

**AI Bots Amplify Political and Climate Disinformation on Twitter**  
_2020-02-21 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Automated AI-driven bots were used in Mexico to manipulate political discourse in favor of Enrique Peña Nieto and in the US to spread climate change denial, especially after Trump’s Paris Agreement withdrawal. Studies found bots generated up to 38% of climate-related tweets, distorting public opinion and spreading misinformation.

**Attack vector:** `misinformation`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`
