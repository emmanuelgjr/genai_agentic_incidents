**AI-Powered Counter-Drone Systems Deployed and Scaled Amid Rising Threats**  
_2026-06-05 · real-world · Severity: High_

The US is deploying and exporting AI-enabled counter-drone systems, such as Anduril's and ACS's autonomous platforms, to allies like Kuwait to address increasing drone threats to critical infrastructure. These systems use AI for detection, tracking, and interception, with rapid scaling raising concerns about potential future harms if misused or malfunctioning.
