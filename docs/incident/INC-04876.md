### INC-04876  [↗](/incident/INC-04876.html)

**South African Legal Team Reportedly Relied on Unverified ChatGPT Case Law in Johannesburg Body Corporate Defamation Matter**  
_2023-03-01 · real-world · Severity: Medium_
AIID: [`#1138`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1138/)

In a defamation case at the Johannesburg Regional Court, Rodrigues Blignaut Attorneys, representing plaintiff Michelle Parker, reportedly relied on purportedly non-existent legal judgments generated by ChatGPT to help argue their case. Magistrate Arvin Chaitram reportedly found the case names and citations were fictitious, causing a two-month delay. The court issued a punitive costs order and rebuked the plaintiff&#x27;s legal team for uncritically accepting AI-generated research.
