### INC-07042  [↗](/incident/INC-07042.html)

**UK Allows Limited Huawei Participation in 5G Amid AI Security Concerns**  
_2020-01-27 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The UK government decided to allow Huawei's AI-enabled 5G equipment in non-core parts of its network, capping its market share at 35%, despite US pressure citing security risks. The move reflects ongoing concerns about potential future harm from AI-driven infrastructure, but no actual incident has occurred.
