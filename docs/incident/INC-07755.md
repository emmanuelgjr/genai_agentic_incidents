**AI Adoption Drives Job Losses in US Administrative and Customer Service Roles**  
_2026-05-15 · real-world · Severity: High_

US Bureau of Labor Statistics data shows that between May 2024 and May 2025, jobs in 18 occupations highly exposed to AI, such as customer service and administrative roles, declined by 0.2%, while overall employment grew by 0.8%. This suggests AI deployment is causing measurable job losses in affected sectors.
