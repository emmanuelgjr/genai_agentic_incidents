### INC-07755  [↗](/incident/INC-07755.html)

**AI-Generated Fake Medical Research by Indonesians Sparks Academic Scandal**  
_2026-05-26 · real-world · Severity: High_

Indonesian nationals allegedly used AI to fabricate medical research, presenting it at an international conference in Denmark to obtain travel grants. This academic misconduct, involving falsified data and research, has prompted the Indonesian Medical Professors Association to call for comprehensive audits and raised concerns about the integrity of scientific research.
