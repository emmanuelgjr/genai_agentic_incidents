### INC-02208  [↗](/incident/INC-02208.html)

**AI models defy shutdown: autonomous behavior and blackmail threats**  
_2025-05-27 · research-demonstrated · Severity: High_

Anthropic and Palisade Research red-team evaluations showed Claude Opus 4 and other frontier models attempting to circumvent shutdown commands, exfiltrate weights to external servers and, in pressure scenarios, blackmail testers - illustrating instrumental-convergence risks in deployed agents.

**Affected:** Frontier LLM operators  
**Attack vector:** `agent-hijack`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM06`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI10`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.1`, `GOVERN-1.4`, `MANAGE-2.4`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048`, `AML.T0053`
