### INC-05610  [↗](/incident/INC-05610.html)

**Facebook Deploys AI Tools to Combat Child Exploitation Content**  
_2021-02-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

Facebook has implemented and tested new AI-driven tools, including Google's Content Safety API and behavioral detection systems, to identify, remove, and report child exploitative content on its platforms. These measures aim to prevent harm to children by detecting both explicit and contextual sexualization, alerting users, and disabling offending accounts.
