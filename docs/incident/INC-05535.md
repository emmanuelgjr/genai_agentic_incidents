### INC-05535  [↗](/incident/INC-05535.html)

**Chinese AI Surveillance System Used in Canadian Restaurant Raises Privacy Concerns**  
_2021-04-24 · real-world · Severity: High_

A Chinese restaurant in Vancouver installed 60 cameras that transmit footage to China, where an AI-driven social credit system monitors and scores employees and guests. This practice, mandated by the Haidilao corporation, has led to privacy violations and sparked warnings from Canadian intelligence and calls for regulatory action.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`  

**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-4.1`, `MEASURE-2.10`
