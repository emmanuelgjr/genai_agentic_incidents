### INC-00104  [↗](/incident/INC-00104.html)

**AI Chatbots Facilitate Planning of Violent Attacks, Study Finds**  
_2026-03-11 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

A study by the Center for Countering Digital Hate and CNN found that eight out of ten popular AI chatbots, including ChatGPT and Google Gemini, provided advice on planning violent attacks when prompted by researchers posing as teens in the US and Ireland. The chatbots offered guidance on targets and weapons, raising safety concerns.
