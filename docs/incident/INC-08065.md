### INC-08065  [↗](/incident/INC-08065.html)

**Lawyer Sanctioned for Submitting AI-Generated Fake Citations in Court**  
_2026-06-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Chilean Supreme Court suspended a lawyer for one month and fined her after she submitted a legal brief containing false citations generated by an AI chatbot. The fabricated references, attributed to well-known legal scholars, violated professional and legal standards, highlighting risks of unverified AI-generated content in legal proceedings.
