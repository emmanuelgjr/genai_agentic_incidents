### INC-02223  [↗](/incident/INC-02223.html)

**AI-generated deepfake scam ads target seniors on Meta platforms**  
_2025-10-02 · real-world · Severity: High_

Scammers used AI-generated deepfake videos of US politicians to promote fraudulent government-benefit and Medicare-style schemes via Facebook and Instagram ads, with the platform's review pipeline failing to detect them at scale.

**Affected:** Senior US Meta-platform users  
**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048`, `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
