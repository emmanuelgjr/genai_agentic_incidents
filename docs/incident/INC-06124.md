**Toyota, Aurora, and Denso Partner to Develop Autonomous Taxis**  
_2021-02-10 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Toyota, Aurora, and Denso have announced a partnership to develop and test AI-powered autonomous taxis, starting with the Toyota Sienna minivan. The companies plan to begin road testing by the end of 2021, with large-scale production and deployment for ride-hailing services to follow, raising future safety and operational risks.
