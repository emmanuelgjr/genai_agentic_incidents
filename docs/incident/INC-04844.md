### INC-04844  [↗](/incident/INC-04844.html)

**Rite Aid Facial Recognition Disproportionately Misidentified Minority Shoppers as Shoplifters**  
_2023-12-20 · real-world · Severity: Medium_
AIID: [`#619`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/619/)

Rite Aid used facial recognition technology from October 2012 to July 2020, allegedly leading to disproportionate misidentifications of women, Black, Latino, and Asian shoppers as &quot;likely&quot; shoplifters. The FTC settlement prohibits Rite Aid from using this technology in stores for five years.
