### INC-06260  [↗](/incident/INC-06260.html)

**AI Systems Fail Amid COVID-19 Mask Usage and Behavioral Shifts**  
_2020-05-25 · real-world · Severity: High_

During the COVID-19 pandemic, AI systems for facial recognition and fraud detection malfunctioned due to widespread mask-wearing and abrupt behavioral changes. Companies in Spain and globally reported increased errors, impacting security, access control, and automated services, highlighting the vulnerability of AI to unexpected real-world changes.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`  

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`
