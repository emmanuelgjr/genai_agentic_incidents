### INC-05523  [↗](/incident/INC-05523.html)

**China Deploys AI-Powered Mass Surveillance and Social Credit System**  
_2021-03-15 · real-world · Severity: High_

China is implementing AI-driven surveillance systems, including emotion recognition cameras and social credit scoring, to monitor and control its citizens. These technologies enable pervasive monitoring, social discrimination, and human rights violations, with citizens' behavior tracked and punished or rewarded, raising global concerns about privacy and freedom.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`
