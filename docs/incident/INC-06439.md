### INC-06439  [↗](/incident/INC-06439.html)

**China's AI-Powered Social Credit System Restricts Millions' Rights**  
_2020-02-11 · real-world · Severity: High_

China's Social Credit System uses AI, facial recognition, and algorithmic scoring to monitor and rate citizens' behavior, impacting access to jobs, travel, and social services. Millions have already faced travel bans and other penalties, raising concerns over human rights violations and large-scale harm caused by AI-driven surveillance and scoring.
