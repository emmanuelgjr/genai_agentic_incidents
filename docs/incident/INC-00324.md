### INC-00324  [↗](/incident/INC-00324.html)

**AI-Driven Social Security Crackdown in Turkey Leads to Mass Benefit Cancellations**  
_2026-04-05 · real-world · Severity: High_

Turkey's Social Security Institution (SGK) used AI-powered data analysis and algorithms to detect fraudulent insurance and retirement claims. As a result, over 245,000 individuals had their insurance and pensions revoked, with financial penalties imposed. The AI system targeted fake employment records and unauthorized benefit claims, causing significant financial and legal consequences.
