### INC-05619  [↗](/incident/INC-05619.html)

**Facebook Settles Lawsuit Over Unconsented Facial Recognition Data Collection in Illinois**  
_2021-01-15 · real-world · Severity: High_

Facebook agreed to a $650 million settlement after its AI-powered facial recognition system collected and stored Illinois users' biometric data without consent, violating the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act. Millions of affected users will receive around $340–$350 each as compensation for these privacy violations.
