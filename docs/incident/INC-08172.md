### INC-08172  [↗](/incident/INC-08172.html)

**Apple Intelligence Allegedly Flags Scam Messages as High Priority**  
_2024-08-05 · real-world · Severity: Medium_
AIID: [`#892`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/892/)

Apple’s AI notification summarization and prioritization feature, part of the Apple Intelligence update, has reportedly flagged scam and phishing messages as high-priority, and is allegedly misleading users into trusting fraudulent communications. By rephrasing and condensing messages, the system is allegedly amplifying scam risks inadvertently and reportedly increasing the likelihood of financial harm for users.

**Attack vector:** `phishing`
