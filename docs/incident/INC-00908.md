### INC-00908  [↗](/incident/INC-00908.html)

**Douyin E-commerce Cracks Down on AI-Generated Celebrity Impersonation Scams**  
_2026-03-09 · real-world · Severity: High_

Douyin E-commerce has taken action against widespread misuse of AI to create unauthorized celebrity impersonations for deceptive marketing, infringing on intellectual property and misleading consumers. Over 165,000 infringing videos and thousands of fake products were removed, with violators penalized. The platform is enhancing AI detection to combat ongoing abuses.
