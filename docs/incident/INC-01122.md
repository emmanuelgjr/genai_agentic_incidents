**Google Sued by Indie Musicians for Training AI Music Models on Copyrighted Works**  
_2026-03-09 · real-world · Severity: High_

Independent musicians have filed a lawsuit against Google in Illinois, alleging the company used millions of copyrighted songs and lyrics from YouTube and the internet to train its AI music generators, including Lyria 3 and MusicLM, without permission or compensation, violating intellectual property rights.
