### INC-06465  [↗](/incident/INC-06465.html)

**Clearview AI's Facial Recognition Sparks Global Privacy and Rights Violations**  
_2020-05-11 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Clearview AI's facial recognition system, which scraped billions of images without consent, was used by law enforcement and trialled by police in several countries without proper oversight, violating privacy laws and biometric rights. Legal actions and public backlash followed, highlighting significant risks and realized harms to privacy and civil liberties.
