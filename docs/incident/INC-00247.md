### INC-00247  [↗](/incident/INC-00247.html)

**AI Uncovers Massive Restaurant Tax Evasion in India**  
_2026-03-09 · real-world · Severity: High_

Indian tax authorities used AI-driven analytics to examine transactional data from over 1.77 lakh restaurants, uncovering large-scale sales suppression and tax evasion. The investigation revealed at least ₹408 crore in undeclared sales and exposed a potential ₹70,000 crore scam, prompting nationwide enforcement actions against fraudulent eateries.
