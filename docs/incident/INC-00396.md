### INC-00396  [↗](/incident/INC-00396.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfake Video of Brady Tkachuk Causes Controversy**  
_2026-02-25 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The White House published an AI-generated deepfake video on TikTok falsely depicting U.S. hockey player Brady Tkachuk making derogatory remarks about Canadians after the 2026 Olympic final. The video, which went viral, led to reputational harm for Tkachuk, who publicly denied making the statements.
