### INC-06117  [↗](/incident/INC-06117.html)

**TikTok Updates US Privacy Policy to Allow Biometric Data Collection via AI**  
_2021-06-03 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

TikTok updated its US privacy policy to permit collection of users' biometric data, including faceprints and voiceprints, likely using AI systems. The vague consent terms and lack of clarity on data use raise significant privacy concerns, though no specific harm or misuse has yet been reported.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM05`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`, `ASI05`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MEASURE-2.10`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0057`
