### INC-07122  [↗](/incident/INC-07122.html)

**WeChat's AI-Driven Censorship and Surveillance Causes Harm to Users**  
_2020-09-04 · real-world · Severity: High_

WeChat's AI-powered content moderation and surveillance systems have enabled widespread censorship, misinformation, and social control. These AI mechanisms have led to real-world harms, including police interrogations, threats to activists, and suppression of free speech, resulting in violations of human rights and harm to communities.
