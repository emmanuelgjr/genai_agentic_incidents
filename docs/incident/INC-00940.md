**Epirus and DFT Integrate AI-Enabled Counter-Drone Defense System**  
_2026-02-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

Epirus and Digital Force Technologies have partnered to integrate AI-enabled Seraphim sensor fusion and command software with the Leonidas high-power microwave platform. The system autonomously detects, tracks, and neutralizes drone threats, including AI-controlled swarms, raising potential risks if misused or malfunctioning. No harm has been reported yet.
