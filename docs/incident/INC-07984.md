### INC-07984  [↗](/incident/INC-07984.html)

**French Newspapers Sue Brave Over AI Use of Press Content**  
_2026-05-29 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Alliance de la presse d'information générale (Apig) and 53 French newspapers have filed a lawsuit in Paris against U.S. company Brave, alleging its AI-powered search engine and browser used their content without authorization for AI training and synthesis, violating intellectual property rights. The plaintiffs seek €80 million in damages.
