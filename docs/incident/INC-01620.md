### INC-01620  [↗](/incident/INC-01620.html)

**Palantir's AI Maven System Adopted by U.S. Military Raises Global Security Concerns**  
_2026-03-26 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The U.S. Department of Defense has officially adopted Palantir's AI system Maven for military data analysis and decision-making. This expansion highlights risks of foreign AI reliance, including potential espionage and data exposure, especially for countries like Brazil lacking domestic AI systems. No direct harm has yet occurred.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`
