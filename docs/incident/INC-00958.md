### INC-00958  [↗](/incident/INC-00958.html)

**EU Investigates X's Grok AI for Enabling Harmful Deepfake Creation**  
_2026-01-26 · real-world · Severity: High_

The European Commission has launched a formal investigation into X and its AI chatbot Grok over concerns that its features enable users to create sexualized deepfakes, including of women and minors. The probe examines potential violations of the Digital Services Act and X's failure to prevent illegal and harmful content.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
