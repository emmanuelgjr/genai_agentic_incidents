### INC-07082  [↗](/incident/INC-07082.html)

**US Bans DJI Over AI Surveillance and Human Rights Concerns**  
_2020-12-18 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The US government added Chinese drone giant DJI to its export ban list, citing the use of AI-powered surveillance drones in large-scale human rights abuses, including repression of Uyghurs and Hong Kong protesters. The move aims to curb the spread of AI surveillance technology linked to human rights violations.
