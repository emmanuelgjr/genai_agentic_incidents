### INC-00221  [↗](/incident/INC-00221.html)

**AI System Prevents Sewer Blockages and Pollution in Southern England**  
_2026-04-22 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Southern Water deployed an AI-powered digital sewer system using 34,000 sensors to monitor sewer flows and detect blockages. The AI system enabled early detection and rapid intervention, preventing fatbergs from polluting rivers in Hampshire and the Isle of Wight, thus averting environmental harm.
