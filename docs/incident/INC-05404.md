### INC-05404  [↗](/incident/INC-05404.html)

**AI-Driven Price Discrimination in Ride-Hailing Apps Harms Consumers**  
_2021-03-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

Multiple reports and a Fudan University study reveal that ride-hailing platforms use AI algorithms to analyze user data, such as phone type and loyalty, resulting in higher fares for wealthier or long-term users. This 'big data price discrimination' leads to economic harm and unfair treatment of consumers.

**Attack vector:** `other`
