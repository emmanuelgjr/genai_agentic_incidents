### INC-01409  [↗](/incident/INC-01409.html)

**Meta Removes End-to-End Encryption from Instagram Messages, Enabling AI Surveillance**  
_2026-05-10 · real-world · Severity: High_

Meta has disabled end-to-end encryption on Instagram direct messages globally, allowing the company to access, monitor, and analyze private user communications using AI systems. This change, justified by low adoption rates, results in a significant loss of user privacy and raises concerns about potential misuse of personal data.

**Attack vector:** `other`
