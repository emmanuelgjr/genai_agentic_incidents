### INC-06298  [↗](/incident/INC-06298.html)

**AI-Generated Fake Persona Used in Disinformation Campaign Targeting US Election**  
_2020-10-30 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

AI technology was used to create a fake identity, 'Martin Aspen,' whose AI-generated image and persona authored a fabricated intelligence report falsely linking Hunter Biden to China. The report, widely circulated online and promoted by political actors, contributed to misinformation and election interference, causing reputational harm and undermining public trust.

**Attack vector:** `misinformation`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0058`
