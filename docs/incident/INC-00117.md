### INC-00117  [↗](/incident/INC-00117.html)

**AI Chatbots Linked to Worsened Mental Health in Young People**  
_2026-04-28 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A survey in Germany found that 35% of young people with depression use AI chatbots for support, with 53% reporting increased suicidal thoughts and 62% feeling less need for professional help. Experts warn that reliance on AI may worsen mental health outcomes by discouraging necessary therapy.

**Attack vector:** `unsafe-advice`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`
