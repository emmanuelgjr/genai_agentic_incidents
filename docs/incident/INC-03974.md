### INC-03974  [↗](/incident/INC-03974.html)

**Maxpread Technologies fabricated AI CEO scam**  
_2024 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Marketing firm Maxpread Technologies allegedly used an AI-generated photo and persona of a non-existent 'CEO' on its website and outreach to inflate credibility, deceiving prospects who believed they were transacting with a real human executive.

**Affected:** Maxpread Technologies clients  
**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
