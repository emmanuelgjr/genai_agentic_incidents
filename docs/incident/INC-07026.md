**TuSimple Launches Autonomous Freight Network with Major Logistics Partners**  
_2020-07-01 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

TuSimple announced the rollout of an autonomous freight network in partnership with UPS, Penske, U.S. Xpress, and McLane, featuring AI-driven self-driving trucks, digital mapped routes, and monitoring systems. While the deployment raises potential future risks, no AI-related harm or incidents have been reported.
