**Anthropic Limits AI Cybersecurity Capabilities Amid U.S. Government Concerns**  
_2026-04-16 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Anthropic's advanced AI model Mythos raised cybersecurity concerns due to its ability to find critical software bugs. In response, the U.S. government is considering protective measures for its use, and Anthropic released Opus 4.7 with intentionally reduced cybersecurity features to mitigate misuse risks.
