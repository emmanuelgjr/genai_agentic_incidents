**AI Use in Judicial Decision Leads to Acquittal in Child Rape Case in Brazil**  
_2026-02-24 · real-world · Severity: High_

A judge in Minas Gerais, Brazil, used an AI tool (ChatGPT) to draft a judicial opinion that acquitted a man accused of raping a 12-year-old girl. The AI-generated prompt was found in the official court document, raising concerns about AI's influence on legal decisions and potential human rights violations.
