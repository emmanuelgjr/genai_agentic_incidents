**Tesla Sudden Acceleration Incident in China Causes Fatalities and Injuries**  
_2020-09-07 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

A Tesla vehicle in Sichuan, China, lost control and crashed, resulting in 2 deaths and 6 injuries. Multiple similar incidents involving sudden unintended acceleration and loss of braking have been reported by Tesla owners. While Tesla denies system faults, the AI-based driving system's malfunction is suspected as a contributing factor.
