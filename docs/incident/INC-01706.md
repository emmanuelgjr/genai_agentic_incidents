### INC-01706  [↗](/incident/INC-01706.html)

**Purportedly AI-Enhanced Images of Iranian Women Protesters Were Reportedly Spread With Unverified Execution Claims**  
_2026-04-21 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1468`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1468/)

In April 2026, AI image enhancement tools were used to alter authentic photographs of Iranian women political prisoners, creating visually enhanced images with stylized backgrounds and beauty filtering that appeared manufactured. President Trump amplified a collage of eight women's faces on Truth Social, claiming Iran was preparing to execute them, based on a post by pro-Israel activist Eyal Yakoby. The images were real photographs of actual political prisoners but had been processed with AI enhancement tools that added smoothing artifacts and stylized elements. Six of the eight women were confirmed as real political prisoners, including Bita Hemmati who has a confirmed death sentence, and others facing various charges including capital offenses. The Iranian judiciary used the AI-enhanced appearance of the images to dismiss the claims as 'fake news,' even though the underlying cases were real. This created a pattern where authentic human rights documentation became contaminated by AI processing, making it easier for authorities to dismiss legitimate concerns. The incident demonstrates how AI enhancement of real images can undermine the credibility of human rights documentation by blurring the line between authentic and synthetic content.
