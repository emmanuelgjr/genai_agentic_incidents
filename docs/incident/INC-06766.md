### INC-06766  [↗](/incident/INC-06766.html)

**Macy's Sued for Biometric Privacy Violations Over Use of Clearview AI Facial Recognition**  
_2020-08-06 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Macy's faces a class action lawsuit alleging it violated Illinois' Biometric Information Privacy Act by using Clearview AI's facial recognition software to identify over 6,000 customers without consent. The suit claims Macy's actions infringed on customer privacy and civil liberties through unauthorized biometric data collection and surveillance.
