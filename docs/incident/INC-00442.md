### INC-00442  [↗](/incident/INC-00442.html)

**AI-Generated Fake Content Used to Blackmail Turkish Celebrity**  
_2026-05-13 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Turkish entertainer Mehmet Ali Erbil was targeted by unidentified individuals who used AI-generated manipulated images to blackmail him for money. After refusing their demands, Erbil faced reputational attacks and has initiated legal action. The incident highlights the misuse of AI for extortion and reputational harm in Turkey.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
