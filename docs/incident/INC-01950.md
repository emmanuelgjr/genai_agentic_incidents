### INC-01950  [↗](/incident/INC-01950.html)

**UK Regulators Pressure Social Media Platforms to Strengthen AI-Driven Child Safety Measures**  
_2026-03-12 · real-world · Severity: High_

UK regulators Ofcom and ICO have demanded that Meta, TikTok, Snap, and YouTube improve AI-based age verification and content moderation to better protect children from harmful algorithmic feeds. The authorities warn that current AI systems are failing to enforce age restrictions, raising concerns about potential exposure to harmful content.
