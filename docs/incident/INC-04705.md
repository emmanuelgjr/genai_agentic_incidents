### INC-04705  [↗](/incident/INC-04705.html)

**Italy bans ChatGPT over GDPR privacy concerns (Garante)**  
_2023-03 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Italy's data-protection authority Garante temporarily banned ChatGPT in March 2023, citing the prior Redis breach, lack of legal basis for training-data collection and absence of age verification. ChatGPT was reinstated a month later after OpenAI introduced opt-out and age-gating controls.

**Affected:** OpenAI ChatGPT (EU/Italy)  
**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM07`
