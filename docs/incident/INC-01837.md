**Taiwan Warns Against Use of Gaode Map App Over AI-Driven Data Security Risks**  
_2026-04-22 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Taiwanese authorities have raised national security and privacy concerns over China's Gaode Map app, which uses AI to infer traffic light timings from user data. Officials warn that sensitive location and movement data could be transmitted to Chinese servers and accessed by the Chinese government, urging a ban in government agencies and cautioning the public.
