### INC-00855  [↗](/incident/INC-00855.html)

**Convict Uses AI to Forge Identity and Evade Arrest in Istanbul**  
_2026-02-25 · real-world · Severity: High_

In Istanbul's Mecidiyeköy district, a convict with a 19-year prison sentence used AI to alter his facial image and create a fake biometric ID, successfully evading police and facial recognition systems for an extended period. He was eventually caught due to a police officer's observation of his wife's tattoo.
