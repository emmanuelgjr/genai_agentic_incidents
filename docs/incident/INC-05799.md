### INC-05799  [↗](/incident/INC-05799.html)

**Plzeň Plans to Test Fully Autonomous AI-Driven Tram by 2027**  
_2021-05-24 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Plzeň, in partnership with technology firms and universities, plans to deploy and test a fully autonomous tram using AI, sensors, and 5G connectivity by 2027. While aiming to improve safety and efficiency in public transport, the project introduces potential future risks if the AI system malfunctions during live operation.

**Attack vector:** `other`
