### INC-05522  [↗](/incident/INC-05522.html)

**China Deploys AI Surveillance for Racial and Ethnic Profiling**  
_2021-03-31 · real-world · Severity: High_

Chinese authorities have enlisted surveillance companies to develop AI-powered facial recognition systems that classify individuals by race and skin color. These systems, used nationwide and exported abroad, enable large-scale discriminatory surveillance and targeting of ethnic minorities, raising serious human rights and privacy concerns.

**Attack vector:** `rce`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
