### INC-06706  [↗](/incident/INC-06706.html)

**India Deploys AI-Powered COVID-19 Surveillance Amid Privacy Concerns**  
_2020-11-10 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

India's government and private sector have implemented AI-driven technologies, such as trackers and mask detectors, to monitor COVID-19 compliance. While intended to curb virus spread, these systems raise significant privacy and surveillance concerns, with experts warning of potential rights violations due to insufficient data protection safeguards.
