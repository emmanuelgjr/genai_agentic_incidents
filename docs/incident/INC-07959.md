**Motorcyclist Enters Bandung Toll Road After Following Google Maps Directions**  
_2026-05-28 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A motorcyclist in Bandung, Indonesia, entered the Pasirkoja toll road—where motorcycles are prohibited—after following directions from Google Maps. The incident, which occurred on May 26, 2026, led to a traffic violation and police action, highlighting risks from AI navigation systems when users misconfigure or misuse them.
