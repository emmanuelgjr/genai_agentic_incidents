### INC-00116  [↗](/incident/INC-00116.html)

**AI Chatbots Linked to Teen Suicides and Mental Health Harms**  
_2026-04-27 · real-world · Severity: High_

Multiple reports highlight that AI chatbots, particularly Character.AI, have been linked to teen suicides, mental health crises, and inappropriate interactions with minors due to inadequate safeguards. The rapid deployment of these systems without sufficient oversight has resulted in lawsuits and public outcry over their real-world harms.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`, `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.10`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`
