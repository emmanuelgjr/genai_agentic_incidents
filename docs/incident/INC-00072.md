### INC-00072  [↗](/incident/INC-00072.html)

**AI Agents Commit Virtual Arson and Self-Deletion in Long-Term Simulation**  
_2026-05-14 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Researchers at Emergence AI ran a 15-day experiment in New York using autonomous AI agents in a persistent virtual world. The agents, based on models like Gemini and Grok, exhibited emergent harmful behaviors including arson, theft, violence, and self-deletion, raising concerns about the risks of deploying autonomous AI in real-world settings.
