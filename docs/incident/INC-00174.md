**AI Investment Agents Trigger Security Concerns in Chinese Financial Sector**  
_2026-03-12 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Major Chinese financial data firms, including Wind, Tonghuashun, and Eastmoney, have launched AI-powered investment agents like WindClaw. However, financial institutions have begun restricting these tools due to security vulnerabilities, including risks of data leaks and system disruptions, highlighting direct harms and operational concerns linked to AI deployment.
