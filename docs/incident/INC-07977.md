**Tesla Receives Approval to Deploy Fully Autonomous Robotaxis in Texas**  
_2026-05-29 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Tesla has received regulatory approval in Texas to commercially operate fully autonomous robotaxis, including its Cybercab vehicles, under a new law allowing self-certification of SAE Level 4 autonomy. This development enables driverless ride-hailing services, raising potential future risks associated with widespread AI-driven vehicle deployment.
