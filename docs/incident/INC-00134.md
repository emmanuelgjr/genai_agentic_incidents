**AI Voice Robots Enable Large-Scale Scam Calls in China’s '95' Number Segment**  
_2020-04-20 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Criminals in China are using AI-powered voice robots bundled with '95' segment phone numbers to automate mass scam and harassment calls. These systems can make thousands of calls daily, screen potential victims, and facilitate financial fraud, causing widespread financial loss and distress despite regulatory crackdowns.
