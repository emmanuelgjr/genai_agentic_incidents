**IAI and Iron Drone Integrate Autonomous AI Interceptor Drones for Anti-Drone Defense**  
_2020-06-29 · real-world · Severity: High_

Israel Aerospace Industries and Iron Drone have partnered to integrate AI-powered autonomous interceptor drones into the Drone Guard anti-drone system. Using radar, sensors, and computer vision, the system detects, tracks, and neutralizes drone threats without human intervention. No harm or incidents have been reported; the technology aims to enhance security.
