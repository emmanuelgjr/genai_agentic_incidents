### INC-03721  [↗](/incident/INC-03721.html)

**Deepfake tool ProKYC exploited for crypto exchange fraud**  
_2024-10-11 · real-world · Severity: High_

Cybercriminals used the ProKYC AI deepfake tool to generate synthetic identity documents and live-video verifications, bypassing know-your-customer protections at cryptocurrency exchanges and enabling fraud and money laundering at scale.

**Affected:** Crypto exchanges; depositors  
**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MANAGE-2.4`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`
