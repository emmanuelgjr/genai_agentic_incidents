### INC-00884  [↗](/incident/INC-00884.html)

**Delhi High Court Reviews Legal Challenge to AI-Enabled Biometric Data Collection**  
_2026-02-25 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Two university students have petitioned the Delhi High Court, challenging the constitutionality of the Criminal Procedure (Identification) Act, 2022, which enables police to collect and store extensive biometric data using AI systems. The petition cites privacy violations and potential misuse, prompting the court to seek responses from authorities.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`
