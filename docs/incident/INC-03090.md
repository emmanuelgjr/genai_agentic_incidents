### INC-03090  [↗](/incident/INC-03090.html)

**Purported Deepfake Reportedly Circulated on Facebook Impersonating Thai PBS World Anchors and Business Figures to Solicit Investments**  
_2025-08-28 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1255`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1255/)

A purported deepfake video circulating on Facebook allegedly used AI-generated visuals and voice synthesis to impersonate Thai PBS World anchors and prominent business figures, promoting a fraudulent &quot;Crystallum AI&quot; investment promising fivefold returns. The original clip was reportedly traced to a 2013 NHK report, but it misused logos and likenesses.
