**Watchdog Group Criticizes Facebook's AI for Failing to Prevent Election Misinformation**  
_2020-09-29 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

A watchdog group of academics and advocates accused Facebook of failing to prevent the spread of misinformation and violent incitement through its AI-driven content moderation systems, contributing to real-world harm and threats to democracy ahead of the 2020 U.S. election. They demanded urgent reforms to protect electoral integrity.
