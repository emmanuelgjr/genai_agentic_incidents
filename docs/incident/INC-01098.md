**GM Begins Supervised Testing of Next-Gen Autonomous Vehicles in Michigan and California**  
_2026-03-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

General Motors has deployed 200 vehicles equipped with advanced autonomous driving technology for supervised public-road testing on highways in Michigan and California. Trained drivers are present to intervene if needed. The testing aims to refine GM's 'eyes-off' driving system, slated for launch in 2028, but poses plausible future risks if AI malfunctions.
