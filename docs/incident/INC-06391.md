### INC-06391  [↗](/incident/INC-06391.html)

**Baidu Receives First Permit for Fully Driverless Vehicle Tests in Beijing**  
_2020-12-07 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Baidu has become the first company to receive permits for fully driverless vehicle tests on public roads in Beijing, allowing its AI-powered cars to operate without in-car safety drivers. While no incidents have occurred, the move introduces potential future risks associated with autonomous AI systems in complex urban environments.
