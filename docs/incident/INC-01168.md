**Humanoid Robot Causes Psychological Distress Leading to Police Intervention in Macau**  
_2026-03-13 · real-world · Severity: High_

A humanoid robot, Unitree G1, autonomously followed a 70-year-old woman in Macau, causing her severe psychological distress and hospitalization. Police intervened, escorting the robot back to its owner. The incident highlights the potential for AI systems to cause harm through unintended interactions in public spaces.
