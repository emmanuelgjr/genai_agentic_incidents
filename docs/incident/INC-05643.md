**Facial Recognition AI Triggers Legal Action and Human Rights Concerns Globally**  
_2021-05-14 · real-world · Severity: High_

Facial recognition AI systems have led to wrongful arrests, privacy violations, and increased surveillance, prompting lawsuits and shutdowns in the US and Brazil. Incidents include Amazon's alleged secret data collection, police misuse resulting in wrongful detention, and legal rulings citing human rights violations, highlighting significant societal harm from AI misuse.
