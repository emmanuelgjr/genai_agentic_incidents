**US Deploys AI-Powered Autonomous Maritime Drones for 24/7 Surveillance at Souda Bay**  
_2026-03-11 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The US has deployed four AI-enabled autonomous surface vehicles (Seasats) to conduct continuous surveillance and patrols around the NATO naval base at Souda Bay, Crete. These unmanned maritime drones use artificial intelligence for navigation and mission execution, raising concerns about potential risks in a sensitive military context.
