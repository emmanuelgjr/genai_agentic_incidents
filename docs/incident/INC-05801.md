### INC-05801  [↗](/incident/INC-05801.html)

**Police Surge Use of Clearview AI Facial Recognition After Capitol Riot Raises Privacy Concerns**  
_2021-01-09 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Following the January 6 Capitol riot, law enforcement agencies across the U.S. increased use of Clearview AI's facial recognition system by 26% to identify suspects. The system, which scrapes billions of images without consent, has sparked significant privacy and civil liberties concerns, prompting legal challenges and public backlash.
