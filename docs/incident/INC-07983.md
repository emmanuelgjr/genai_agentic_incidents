**Egyptian Ministry of Military Production Plans AI Integration in Weapons Development**  
_2026-05-29 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Egypt's Ministry of Military Production, led by Dr. Salah Suleiman Gamblat, announced plans to integrate AI applications into the development of weapons, ammunition, and military equipment. The initiative aims to enhance defense capabilities and keep pace with global advancements, raising potential future risks associated with AI-enabled military technologies.
