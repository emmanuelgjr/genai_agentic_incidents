**New York Moves Toward Moratorium on AI Data Center Construction Over Environmental Concerns**  
_2026-06-02 · real-world · Severity: High_

New York legislators are advancing a one-year moratorium on new AI data center construction, citing concerns about energy grid strain, rising utility costs, and environmental impact. The move, if enacted, would make New York the first state to halt AI-driven data center expansion as a precaution against potential harms.
