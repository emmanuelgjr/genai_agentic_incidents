### INC-08101  [↗](/incident/INC-08101.html)

**AI Misuse Linked to Surge in Student Failures at UC Berkeley**  
_2026-06-04 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

UC Berkeley computer science courses saw a sharp rise in student failures, with over 35% failing an entry-level class. Faculty and commentators, including Zoho's Sridhar Vembu, attribute this to students' heavy reliance on AI tools like large language models for coursework, leading to academic dishonesty and skill degradation.
