**Humanoid AI Robots Deployed for Border Patrol in China, Raising Safety Concerns**  
_2026-04-14 · real-world · Severity: High_

China is deploying humanoid robots with embodied AI for border patrol duties at the Vietnam border, led by UBTECH Robotics. While no harm has occurred, the use of autonomous robots in critical, high-traffic environments raises concerns about potential safety and operational risks if malfunctions occur.
