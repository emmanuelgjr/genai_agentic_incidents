**Anthropic's Mythos AI Raises Global Cybersecurity Concerns**  
_2026-04-08 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Anthropic's new AI model, Mythos, has identified thousands of software vulnerabilities, prompting major tech firms and financial authorities in the US and UK to restrict its release due to fears it could be exploited for cyberattacks.
