**French Army Tests Boston Dynamics' Spot Robot in Combat Training, Revealing Operational Risks**  
_2021-04-08 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The French military tested Boston Dynamics' Spot robot in urban combat training, where its AI-assisted reconnaissance reportedly helped avoid soldier casualties. However, operational issues like battery failure and slowed maneuvers highlighted potential risks if deployed in real combat, raising concerns about the reliability and future use of AI-enabled robots in warfare.
