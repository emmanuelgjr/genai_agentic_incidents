### INC-05521  [↗](/incident/INC-05521.html)

**China Bans Tesla Cars from Government Compounds over AI Security Concerns**  
_2021-05-18 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Chinese government offices have banned staff from parking Tesla vehicles in their compounds due to security concerns over the cars' AI-powered cameras and sensors potentially capturing sensitive data. The restriction, which follows a similar military ban, reflects fears of data privacy risks, though no actual harm has been reported.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`
