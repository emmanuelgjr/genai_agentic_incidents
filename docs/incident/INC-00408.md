### INC-00408  [↗](/incident/INC-00408.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfake Videos Used in Scams and Misinformation in Taiwan**  
_2026-01-25 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Researchers at Taiwan's Institute for Information Industry revealed that AI-generated fake videos, including deepfakes and virtual avatars, are increasingly used for scams, impersonation, and spreading misinformation. These AI videos have led to fraud and public deception, prompting experts to advise the public on detection methods to mitigate harm.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
