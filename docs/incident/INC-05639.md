### INC-05639  [↗](/incident/INC-05639.html)

**Facial Recognition AI Blocks Unemployment Benefits for Eligible Applicants**  
_2021-06-16 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Facial recognition software by ID.me, used in over 20 US states to verify unemployment claims, has inconsistently identified applicants, leading to wrongful denials and delays in benefits. Many affected individuals struggle to resolve issues, while the company attributes failures to user error, despite mounting complaints and evidence of harm.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`
