### INC-06532  [↗](/incident/INC-06532.html)

**EU Lawmaker Warns of AI Hazards: Hacking Robots and Deepfake Software Pose New Challenges**  
_2020-02-16 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Bulgarian MEP Emil Radev warned that emerging AI technologies, such as hacking robots and deepfake-generating software, present significant moral, political, and security challenges for states. He emphasized the urgent need for regulation to address potential risks in both civilian and military applications of AI.
