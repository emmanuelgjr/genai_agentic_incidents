### INC-06219  [↗](/incident/INC-06219.html)

**AI Bias Risks False Positives in Search for Alien Life**  
_2020-01-28 · real-world · Severity: High_

Researchers warn that AI systems used to detect extraterrestrial life can misidentify patterns and amplify human cognitive biases, potentially leading to false claims of alien discovery. Such misinterpretations could cause public misinformation, highlighting the need for caution in relying on AI for scientific analysis in astrobiology.
