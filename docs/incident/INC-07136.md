### INC-07136  [↗](/incident/INC-07136.html)

**YouTube AI Moderation System Censors Comments Critical of Chinese Communist Party**  
_2020-05-26 · real-world · Severity: High_

YouTube's automated moderation system erroneously deleted comments containing Chinese-language phrases critical of the Chinese Communist Party, such as "communist bandit" and "50-cent party." The deletions, which suppressed political speech and violated users' rights to free expression, were attributed to an AI enforcement error that YouTube has since begun to address.
