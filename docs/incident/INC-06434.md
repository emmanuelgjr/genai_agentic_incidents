### INC-06434  [↗](/incident/INC-06434.html)

**China Uses AI Surveillance to Monitor and Intimidate Uyghurs Abroad**  
_2020-12-17 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Chinese government deployed an AI-powered social media surveillance system to monitor Uyghurs living overseas, leading to intimidation, suppression of free speech, and the detention of relatives in China. This AI-enabled monitoring has resulted in significant human rights violations against Uyghur individuals and their families.
