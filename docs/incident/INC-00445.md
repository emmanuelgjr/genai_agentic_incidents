### INC-00445  [↗](/incident/INC-00445.html)

**AI-Generated Fake Family Photos Cause Distress for Julián Gil and Marjorie de Sousa**  
_2026-05-05 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI-generated images falsely depicting a reunion between actors Julián Gil, Marjorie de Sousa, and their son Matías went viral, causing emotional distress and privacy concerns. Both Gil and de Sousa publicly condemned the misuse of AI for spreading misinformation and violating their family's rights, with de Sousa reporting the offending page.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
