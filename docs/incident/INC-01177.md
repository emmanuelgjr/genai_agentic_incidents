**ICE Agents Use AI Surveillance Apps and Quotas, Leading to Unlawful Arrests in Oregon**  
_2026-03-13 · real-world · Severity: High_

Court testimony revealed that US Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) agents in Oregon used Palantir's AI-powered ELITE and Mobile Fortify apps to identify targets and neighborhoods for arrest, driven by daily quotas. This AI-assisted targeting led to warrantless, unconstitutional arrests and widespread harm to immigrant communities.
