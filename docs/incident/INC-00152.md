### INC-00152  [↗](/incident/INC-00152.html)

**AI Deepfake Videos Impersonate Doctor, Spread Harmful Medical Misinformation in South Korea**  
_2026-03-26 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

AI-generated deepfake videos impersonating Dr. Lee Guk-jong circulated widely on YouTube, spreading unverified and potentially dangerous medical advice to hundreds of thousands of viewers. The misuse of AI led to public misinformation, risked health harm, and violated the doctor's personal rights, prompting legal and privacy complaints.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
