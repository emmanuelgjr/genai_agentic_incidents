### INC-06299  [↗](/incident/INC-06299.html)

**AI-Generated Virtual Avatars of the Deceased Raise Privacy and Ethical Concerns**  
_2020-04-17 · real-world · Severity: High_

Tech startups in countries like South Korea and the US are developing AI systems to create digital avatars of deceased individuals. Experts warn that the lack of regulation could lead to privacy violations and ethical issues, as these virtual afterlives may be created without proper consent or oversight.

**Attack vector:** `other`
