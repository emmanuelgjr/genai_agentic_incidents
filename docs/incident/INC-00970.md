**Europol Warns of AI-Driven Cyber Threats Amid Iran Crisis**  
_2026-03-05 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Europol has warned that the ongoing Middle East conflict, particularly involving Iran, increases the risk of terrorism, violent extremism, and cyberattacks in the European Union. The agency highlights the potential use of increasingly sophisticated AI in cyberattacks and online fraud, posing a credible future threat to EU infrastructure and security.
