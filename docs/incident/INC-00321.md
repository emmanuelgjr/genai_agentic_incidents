### INC-00321  [↗](/incident/INC-00321.html)

**AI-Driven Scams Lead to Surge in UK Fashion Consumer Complaints**  
_2026-02-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

The increased use of AI by fashion retailers has enabled scammers to deceive UK consumers with misleading images and advertisements, resulting in nearly 18,000 complaints to Citizens Advice in one year—a 21% rise. Most complaints involved online orders, with issues including faulty goods, delivery failures, and scams.

**Attack vector:** `other`  

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`
