### INC-02960  [↗](/incident/INC-02960.html)

**North Korea-Linked Actors Allegedly Use AI Executive Deepfakes in Zoom Phishing Targeting Web3 Employee**  
_2025-06-22 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1117`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1117/)

An alleged phishing scheme involving actors linked to North Korea used purported AI-generated deepfake videos of company executives to deceive a Web3 employee during a fake Zoom call. The target was reportedly tricked into installing macOS malware disguised as a &quot;Zoom extension,&quot; leading to the deployment of spyware, a keylogger, and a crypto wallet stealer. The attackers reportedly used Telegram and spoofed Zoom domains to orchestrate the breach.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
