### INC-06269  [↗](/incident/INC-06269.html)

**AI-Driven Automation Threatens Mass Job Loss in Transport and Retail Sectors**  
_2020-01-16 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Advances in AI, such as autonomous trucks and checkout-free retail systems, are expected to eliminate large numbers of jobs in transport and retail. Companies like TuSimple and Coles are leading this shift, raising concerns about significant future economic and social disruption for workers lacking adaptable skills.
