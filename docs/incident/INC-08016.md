### INC-08016  [↗](/incident/INC-08016.html)

**Investigation into Alleged AI-Manipulated Audio Evidence in Argentine Corruption Case**  
_2026-06-01 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Argentine authorities are investigating whether audio recordings implicating Diego Spagnuolo in corruption were manipulated using AI. The defense claims the audios are AI-generated, seeking to nullify the evidence. Gendarmería Nacional is conducting forensic analysis to determine authenticity, with potential legal consequences if AI tampering is confirmed.
