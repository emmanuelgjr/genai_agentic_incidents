**AI Algorithms Promote and Recommend Neo-Nazi Products on Major Platforms**  
_2020-07-25 · real-world · Severity: High_

Amazon, Google, and Wish removed neo-Nazi and white-supremacist products after a BBC investigation revealed their AI-powered recommendation algorithms were promoting and suggesting such items. The incident highlights the role of AI systems in amplifying harmful, extremist content before company intervention and removal.
