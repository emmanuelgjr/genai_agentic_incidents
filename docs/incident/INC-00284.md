### INC-00284  [↗](/incident/INC-00284.html)

**AI-Driven Cyberattacks Render Passwords Insufficient for Security**  
_2026-05-06 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Check Point Software warns that AI-powered tools, including generative AI, deepfakes, and automated malware, have enabled cybercriminals to bypass even strong passwords, leading to increased credential theft, data breaches, and financial losses. The rise of AI-enhanced cybercrime has rendered traditional password security measures inadequate, especially in Portugal.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`, `ASI05`, `ASI09`, `ASI10`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.10`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0048`, `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0057`, `AML.T0058`
