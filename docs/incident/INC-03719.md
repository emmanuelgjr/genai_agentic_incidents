### INC-03719  [↗](/incident/INC-03719.html)

**Deepfake scam uses AI to impersonate UK leaders for crypto fraud**  
_2024-08-12 · real-world · Severity: High_

Fadel Alkilani and Fenimore Harper Communications researchers identified more than 250 AI-generated deepfake ads using the likenesses of UK Prime Minister Keir Starmer and Prince William to direct viewers to a fraudulent cryptocurrency platform, with the underlying video generated using Meta's LLaMA 3.1 model.

**Affected:** UK public; Meta-platform ad audiences  
**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048`, `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
