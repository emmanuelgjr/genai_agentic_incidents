**AI-Powered Biometric ID and Vaccine Payment System Trialed in Africa Raises Rights Concerns**  
_2020-07-15 · real-world · Severity: High_

A partnership between GAVI, Mastercard, and AI firm Trust Stamp is trialing an AI-powered biometric digital identity platform in West Africa, integrating vaccination records and payment systems. While no harm has yet occurred, the system's deployment in vulnerable communities raises concerns about potential privacy violations and human rights risks.
