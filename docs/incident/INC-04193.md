### INC-04193  [↗](/incident/INC-04193.html)

**SearchGPT Reportedly Misleads Users with Incorrect Festival Dates in Demo**  
_2024-07-25 · real-world · Severity: Medium_
AIID: [`#751`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/751/)

OpenAI’s prototype AI tool, SearchGPT, provided incorrect dates for An Appalachian Summer Festival in Boone, North Carolina during a demonstration video. The AI listed dates that were incorrect, potentially misleading users planning to attend the event, but also harming the reputation of OpenAI as the incident occurred during a high-profile event.
