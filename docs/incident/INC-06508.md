### INC-06508  [↗](/incident/INC-06508.html)

**Delivery of AI-Enabled Kargu-2 Armed Drones to Security Forces Raises Risk Concerns**  
_2020-04-04 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Turkey's Defense Industry Presidency announced new deliveries of Kargu-2 drones, which use AI for autonomous threat detection and engagement, to security forces. These explosive-equipped drones are intended for use in conflict zones, raising credible concerns about potential future harm from autonomous lethal AI systems, though no specific incident has yet occurred.
