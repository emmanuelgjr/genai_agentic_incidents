### INC-06324  [↗](/incident/INC-06324.html)

**AI-Powered Surveillance in Xinjiang Enables Human Rights Abuses**  
_2020-11-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

China's government uses AI-driven surveillance systems, powered by U.S.-made Intel and Nvidia chips, to monitor, track, and suppress minority populations in Xinjiang. These systems enable mass facial recognition, behavior analysis, and predictive policing, directly contributing to widespread human rights violations and repression of mostly Muslim minorities.
