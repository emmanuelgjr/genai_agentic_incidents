### INC-03133  [↗](/incident/INC-03133.html)

**Purportedly AI-Generated Video Reportedly Depicted Bulgarian Politician Kostadin Kostadinov Falling During Protest**  
_2025-06-12 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1465`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1465/)

On June 12, 2025, an AI-generated video was published on Facebook showing Bulgarian politician Kostadin Kostadinov appearing to fall while jumping over a fence during a protest. The 6-second video was created using Haliuo AI, a platform for generating video from images, based on a photograph taken by a Bulgarian Telegraph Agency (BTA) photographer during an anti-euro adoption protest organized by Kostadinov's 'Vazrazhdane' party on June 8. The video was first published at 12:47 Bulgarian time by the 'Visoko naprezenie' Facebook page, then republished by 'Mario Stefanov' profile at 15:08, and later by Budilnikbg.com at 19:59. The three posts collectively received nearly 2,800 likes and over one million views. The video contained several technical indicators of AI generation including square format typical of AI generators, 6-second duration matching free AI tool limitations, partially obscured Haliuo AI watermark in the bottom right corner, and visual artifacts such as unnaturally changing faces, deformed hands, and distorted facial features. BTA confirmed they published no authentic video of Kostadinov actually jumping the fence. The incident represents a case of AI-generated misinformation spreading false information about a political figure to a large audience.

**Affected:** Haliuo AI, Visoko Naprezhenie, Mario Stefanov, Budilnikbg.com
