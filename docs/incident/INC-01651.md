### INC-01651  [↗](/incident/INC-01651.html)

**Planned Use of AI in Azerbaijan's Court System Raises Future Risks**  
_2026-02-20 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Azerbaijan plans to implement AI in its court system to assist with procedural checks, simple case decisions, and predicting court outcomes. While intended as a support tool, experts warn of potential future risks such as bias, privacy violations, and threats to judicial fairness if not properly managed.
