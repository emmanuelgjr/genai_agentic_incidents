**Facebook Considers Facial Recognition in Smart Glasses, Raising Privacy Concerns**  
_2021-02-26 · real-world · Severity: High_

Facebook is considering integrating facial recognition AI into its upcoming smart glasses, prompting internal and public debate over privacy, stalking, and legal risks. Executives acknowledge both the benefits and significant risks, referencing past biometric privacy controversies, but no final decision or deployment has occurred yet.
