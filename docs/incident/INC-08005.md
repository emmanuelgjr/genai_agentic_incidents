**Manitoba Rejects Hyperscale AI Data Centre Over Environmental Concerns**  
_2026-05-31 · real-world · Severity: High_

Manitoba Premier Wab Kinew has rejected a proposed hyperscale AI data centre south of Winnipeg, citing concerns about its massive energy consumption and potential environmental and community impacts. The project, planned by Jet.AI and Consensus Core, was halted to prevent plausible future harm from AI infrastructure.
