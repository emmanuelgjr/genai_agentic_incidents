### INC-00143  [↗](/incident/INC-00143.html)

**AI Deepfake and Facial Recognition Misuse Raise Privacy and Fraud Concerns in China**  
_2026-03-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

In China, AI-powered facial recognition systems and deepfake technologies have led to privacy risks and rights violations. Users' full camera feeds during facial recognition may expose private scenes, while unauthorized AI face-swapping services create realistic fake videos for fraud and impersonation, prompting regulatory and public concern over data security and misuse.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
