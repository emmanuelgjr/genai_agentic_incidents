### INC-03716  [↗](/incident/INC-03716.html)

**Deepfake porn engulfs Korean schools ('New Nth Room')**  
_2024-08 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

South Korean police identified hundreds of Telegram chat rooms with up to 220,000 members trading AI-generated sexual deepfakes of schoolgirls, teachers and military personnel, drawn from social-media photos. The scandal triggered emergency legislation.

**Affected:** South Korean students and military personnel
