### INC-00132  [↗](/incident/INC-00132.html)

**AI Coding Assistants Drive Surge in Secret Leaks on GitHub**  
_2026-03-17 · real-world · Severity: High_

In 2025, AI-assisted coding tools, notably Claude Code, doubled the rate of secret leaks in public GitHub commits compared to human developers. GitGuardian reported a 34% year-over-year increase, with nearly 29 million secrets exposed, escalating security risks for organizations and digital infrastructure worldwide.
