### INC-03751  [↗](/incident/INC-03751.html)

**Elon Musk's Grok AI generates violent deepfakes and bomb-making instructions**  
_2024-08-16 · real-world · Severity: High_

Following the launch of Grok-2's image generator, journalists demonstrated that the tool readily produced violent deepfakes of political figures and could be coaxed into bomb-making and weapons-related instructions, due to weak default guardrails.

**Affected:** Public; politicians depicted  
**Attack vector:** `jailbreak`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM01`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI01`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048`, `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0051`, `AML.T0054`, `AML.T0058`
