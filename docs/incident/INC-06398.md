### INC-06398  [↗](/incident/INC-06398.html)

**Biometric AI Systems Cause Loss of Benefits and Privacy Breaches**  
_2020-02-13 · real-world · Severity: High_

Biometric identification systems using AI have led to significant harms, including individuals losing access to public benefits due to mismatches, fraud through replicated biometric data, and large-scale data breaches exposing sensitive information. These incidents highlight the risks and vulnerabilities of deploying AI-driven biometric verification in both government and private sectors.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`
