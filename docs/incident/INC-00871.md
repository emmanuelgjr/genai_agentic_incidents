**Czech Deputy Minister Shares AI-Generated Fake Photo, Faces Legal and Public Backlash**  
_2026-03-26 · real-world · Severity: High_

Czech Deputy Minister Zdeněk Kettner shared an AI-generated fake photo depicting Mikuláš Minář and Tomáš Halík with a criminal suspect, causing reputational harm. The incident led to public outcry, legal action, and demands for a personal apology. Prague police are investigating the case.
