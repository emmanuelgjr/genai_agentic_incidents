### INC-00040  [↗](/incident/INC-00040.html)

**ADAC Test Reveals AI Driver Assistance Failures in Adverse Weather**  
_2026-04-13 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

ADAC tested AI-based driver assistance systems in modern vehicles under simulated rain, fog, and glare. Results showed significant performance failures, especially in obstacle detection and emergency braking, with some systems not warning drivers of limitations. These malfunctions pose safety risks, particularly in poor visibility conditions. Tests were conducted in Germany.
