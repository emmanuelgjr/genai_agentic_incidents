**Facial Recognition Error Leads to Wrongful Arrest and Jailing of Tennessee Grandmother**  
_2026-05-13 · real-world · Severity: High_

Angela Lipps, a Tennessee grandmother, was wrongfully arrested and jailed for over five months after facial recognition AI software falsely matched her to a bank fraud suspect in North Dakota. Authorities relied on poor-quality images and failed to verify the AI's output, resulting in significant personal and reputational harm to Lipps.
