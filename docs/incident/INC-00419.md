### INC-00419  [↗](/incident/INC-00419.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfakes Fuel Surge in Child Sexual Abuse Material**  
_2026-01-24 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

AI-powered deepfake and nudification tools, such as those linked to Grok, have led to a dramatic rise in AI-generated child sexual abuse material. Reports in the US soared from 4,700 in 2023 to over 440,000 in the first half of 2025, causing significant harm to children and prompting legislative concern.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
