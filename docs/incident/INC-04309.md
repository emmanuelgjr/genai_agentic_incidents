### INC-04309  [↗](/incident/INC-04309.html)

**Two Members of Highline Public Schools Community in King County, Washington Reportedly Targeted in Deepfake Kidnapping Scam**  
_2024-09-25 · real-world · Severity: Critical_
AIID: [`#947`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/947/)

Two members of the Highline Public Schools community in Burien, a town in King County, Washington, were reportedly targeted in a deepfake kidnapping scam. Scammers used AI-generated voice cloning technology to convincingly mimic the voices of their family members. The scammers used the altered voices to falsely claim they had been kidnapped and demanding a ransom be paid.
