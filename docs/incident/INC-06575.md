### INC-06575  [↗](/incident/INC-06575.html)

**Facebook's AI Ad Systems Lock Out Small Advertisers, Causing Financial Harm**  
_2020-12-21 · real-world · Severity: High_

Facebook's automated AI ad management systems have locked out small advertisers from their accounts, leaving them unable to control ongoing ad campaigns and resulting in uncontrolled spending of clients' money. The inflexible AI-driven lockouts and lack of customer support have caused significant financial and operational harm to small businesses.
