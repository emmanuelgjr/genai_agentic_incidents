### INC-05374  [↗](/incident/INC-05374.html)

**Adaptive Cruise Control Linked to Increased Driver Speeding, Raising Crash Risks**  
_2021-03-11 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Multiple studies by IIHS and MIT found that drivers using adaptive cruise control (ACC) or partial automation are significantly more likely to speed, often setting speeds above legal limits. This AI-driven behavior increases the risk of fatal crashes, highlighting a potential safety hazard associated with ACC systems.
