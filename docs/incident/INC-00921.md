### INC-00921  [↗](/incident/INC-00921.html)

**Dutch Privacy Authority Warns of Rising AI Risks and Urges Immediate Regulation**  
_2026-03-05 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Dutch Data Protection Authority (AP) warns that rapid AI development in the Netherlands is outpacing regulation and oversight, increasing risks of privacy breaches, discrimination, fraud, and psychological harm. The AP urges urgent government action to prevent incidents similar to past scandals and protect fundamental rights.
