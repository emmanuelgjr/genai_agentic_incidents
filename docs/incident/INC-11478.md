**Obfuscated email addresses should not be sorted**  
_2023-07 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: Medium_

CVEs: `CVE-2023-38509`
CWEs: `CWE-402`
CVSS: **4.3** — `CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:L/UI:N/S:U/C:L/I:N/A:N`
