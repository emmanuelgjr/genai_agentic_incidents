### INC-06784  [↗](/incident/INC-06784.html)

**Microsoft Contractors in China Accessed Skype and Cortana Recordings Without Security Measures**  
_2020-01-10 · real-world · Severity: High_

Microsoft allowed contractors in China to access and review thousands of Skype and Cortana audio recordings, including sensitive conversations, without proper security measures or employee vetting. The lack of safeguards exposed users’ private data to potential criminal or state access, constituting a significant privacy violation linked to AI system use.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`
