**Punjab Police Use AI Voice Recognition to Combat Organized Crime**  
_2026-03-10 · real-world · Severity: High_

Punjab Police in India have deployed the Punjab Artificial Intelligence System (PAIS), which uses a database of over 72,000 voice samples to identify and track gangsters, especially those operating abroad. The AI system has directly enabled arrests and prevented violent crimes, significantly enhancing harm prevention and public safety.
