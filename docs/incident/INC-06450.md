### INC-06450  [↗](/incident/INC-06450.html)

**Chinese Facial Recognition AI Identifies Masked Individuals, Raising Human Rights Concerns**  
_2020-03-09 · real-world · Severity: High_

Chinese firm Hanvon developed AI facial recognition technology capable of identifying masked individuals with 95% accuracy, even in crowds. Deployed by authorities for surveillance, including during COVID-19, the system raises significant concerns about privacy violations and human rights abuses, particularly in the context of state monitoring and targeting of minorities.
