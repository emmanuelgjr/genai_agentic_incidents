### INC-00698  [↗](/incident/INC-00698.html)

**BlueNoroff Uses AI-Generated Deepfakes in Cryptocurrency Phishing Attacks**  
_2026-04-27 · real-world · Severity: High_

North Korea's BlueNoroff group targeted North American cryptocurrency executives using AI-generated deepfake avatars and videos in fake Zoom meetings. The campaign enabled malware deployment, credential theft, and unauthorized access to crypto wallets, resulting in financial loss and persistent system compromise. AI-generated content was central to deceiving victims and facilitating the attacks.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.10`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0057`, `AML.T0058`
