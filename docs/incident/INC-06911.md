### INC-06911  [↗](/incident/INC-06911.html)

**Serbia's AI-Powered Biometric Surveillance Sparks Human Rights Concerns**  
_2020-08-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Serbian government, in partnership with Huawei, is deploying thousands of AI-driven facial recognition cameras in Belgrade under the 'Safe City' project. Civil society groups and privacy advocates warn that the lack of legal safeguards has already led to violations of privacy rights and poses significant risks to human rights and civil liberties.
