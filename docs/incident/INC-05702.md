**India Unveils AI-Enabled Combat Drone Teaming System**  
_2021-02-01 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

India's Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL) has unveiled the Combat Air Teaming System (CATS), featuring AI-enabled autonomous drones like the Warrior and Infinity. Designed to operate alongside manned fighter jets, these drones use AI for target detection, mission coordination, and surveillance, raising future risks of autonomous warfare-related harm.
