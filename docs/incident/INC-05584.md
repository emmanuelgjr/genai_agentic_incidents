### INC-05584  [↗](/incident/INC-05584.html)

**Dutch Parents Sue TikTok Over AI-Driven Harm to Children**  
_2021-06-02 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Thousands of Dutch parents, represented by the SOMI foundation, are demanding over €1.4 billion in damages from TikTok. They allege TikTok's AI-driven recommendation and data collection systems exposed minors to dangerous challenges and targeted advertising without consent, resulting in harm to children's safety and privacy.
