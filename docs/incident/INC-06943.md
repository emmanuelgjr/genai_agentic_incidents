**São Paulo Metro Faces Legal Action Over Facial Recognition System and Privacy Concerns**  
_2020-02-10 · real-world · Severity: High_

Multiple public defenders and civil society groups have filed a legal action against São Paulo Metro, challenging its planned facial recognition system. They argue the AI technology is flawed and violates passengers' privacy and data protection rights, demanding transparency and compliance with legal standards before deployment.
