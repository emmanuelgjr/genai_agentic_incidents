### INC-01827  [↗](/incident/INC-01827.html)

**Supreme Court Flags Use of AI-Generated Fake Judgments in Indian Trial Court**  
_2026-03-02 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Supreme Court of India has taken serious note of a trial court's reliance on AI-generated fake or non-existent judgments in a civil dispute, warning that such conduct constitutes judicial misconduct and undermines the integrity of the legal process. The court is examining the consequences and accountability of this misuse.
