### INC-00469  [↗](/incident/INC-00469.html)

**AI-Generated Fake Visuals Spark Controversy for 'Dhruvandar: The Revenge'**  
_2026-03-26 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI-generated fake images and videos depicting actor Ranveer Singh in controversial scenes have been widely circulated online, causing reputational harm and community outrage for the film 'Dhruvandar: The Revenge.' Director Aditya Dhar condemned the manipulation and warned of legal action against those spreading misleading content.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
