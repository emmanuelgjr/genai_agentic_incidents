### INC-06550  [↗](/incident/INC-06550.html)

**FaceApp's AI Gender-Swap Feature Raises Privacy and Rights Concerns**  
_2020-06-14 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

FaceApp, an AI-powered app that alters user photos to change gender, has raised significant concerns over privacy violations and potential misuse of biometric data. Reports highlight the app's broad data collection, vague privacy policies, and potential for sharing or selling user information, leading to actual and potential harm to user rights and communities.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`, `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.10`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0057`, `AML.T0058`
