### INC-00359  [↗](/incident/INC-00359.html)

**AI-Enabled Wi-Fi Signal Analysis Raises Privacy Concerns**  
_2026-03-19 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI systems can analyze Wi-Fi signals to infer sensitive personal information and monitor behaviors, even without internet access or device connection. This technology enables unauthorized surveillance, posing significant privacy and human rights risks by allowing individuals to be spied on without their consent.
