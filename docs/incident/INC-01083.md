### INC-01083  [↗](/incident/INC-01083.html)

**German Voice Actors Protest AI Use of Their Voices Without Consent**  
_2026-03-14 · real-world · Severity: High_

Prominent German voice actors protested in Munich against streaming platforms, including Netflix, for contract clauses allowing their recorded voices to train AI systems without consent or extra pay. Many actors refused to sign such contracts, causing production delays and raising concerns over intellectual property and labor rights violations.
