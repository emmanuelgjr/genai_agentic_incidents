**Automakers Advance 'Eyes-Off' AI Driving Amid Safety and Liability Concerns**  
_2026-02-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

Automakers, including Ford, are developing Level 3 autonomous driving systems that use AI to allow drivers to take their eyes off the road. The push raises significant safety and liability concerns, as these systems may require sudden human intervention, but no actual harm has yet occurred.
