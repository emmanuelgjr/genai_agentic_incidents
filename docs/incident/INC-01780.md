### INC-01780  [↗](/incident/INC-01780.html)

**Smart Locks' Facial Recognition Vulnerabilities Exposed in China**  
_2026-04-16 · real-world · Severity: High_

Consumer associations in Beijing, Tianjin, and Hebei tested 30 smart lock models and found that three facial recognition locks could be easily unlocked with photos, revealing serious AI anti-spoofing flaws. Additional risks include unencrypted data transmission and easily copied IC cards, posing threats to property and privacy.
