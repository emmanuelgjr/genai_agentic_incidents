**AI-Powered Wearable Patch Predicts and Prevents Heart Failure Readmissions**  
_2020-02-24 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Researchers from the University of Utah and VA Salt Lake City developed an AI-powered wearable patch that monitors heart failure patients' physiological data to predict worsening conditions. The system enables early intervention, potentially preventing up to one-third of hospital readmissions and improving patient outcomes by detecting crises days in advance.
