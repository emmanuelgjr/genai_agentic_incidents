**UK Government Underestimates AI Data Center Carbon Emissions by Over 100-Fold**  
_2026-04-24 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The UK government drastically underestimated the carbon emissions from AI data centers, revising its estimate from 0.142 million to 123 million tonnes of CO₂ over the next decade. This significant increase raises concerns about AI's environmental impact and the urgency of addressing its contribution to climate change.
