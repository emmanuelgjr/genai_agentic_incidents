**NHTSA Investigates Tesla AI-Driven Unintended Acceleration Incidents**  
_2020-01-17 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The US NHTSA is investigating reports that Tesla vehicles, equipped with AI-based driver-assistance systems, have experienced unintended acceleration, leading to 110 accidents and 52 injuries. The incidents, often occurring during parking, have prompted consumer complaints and calls for a large-scale recall, highlighting potential AI system malfunctions.
