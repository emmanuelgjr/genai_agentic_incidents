### INC-07786  [↗](/incident/INC-07786.html)

**Ukrainian Fraud Ring Used Deepfake AI in International Scam**  
_2026-05-28 · real-world · Severity: High_

Ukrainian police dismantled a criminal organization in Odesa that operated international fraudulent call centers targeting Kazakh citizens. The group used deepfake technology and AI-generated images to impersonate officials, applying psychological pressure and tricking victims into installing malware, resulting in significant financial losses.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
