### INC-07786  [↗](/incident/INC-07786.html)

**AI-Powered Dolunay Project Rapidly Tackles Cyber Fraud in Turkey**  
_2026-05-18 · real-world · Severity: High_

Turkey's Interior Ministry launched the AI-supported Dolunay project to combat cyber fraud. The system analyzes citizen reports and digital evidence, enabling rapid intervention and operational case creation within 18 minutes. Multiple agencies collaborate via a unified platform, aiming to reduce financial harm and streamline law enforcement response.
