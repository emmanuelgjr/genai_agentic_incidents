### INC-00052  [↗](/incident/INC-00052.html)

**Agentic AI Empowers Solo Cybercriminals to Rapidly Develop Advanced Malware**  
_2026-03-20 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

In early 2026, cybercriminals leveraged agentic AI systems to autonomously create advanced malware, such as VoidLink, in days rather than months. This shift, highlighted by Check Point Software, has enabled individuals to execute complex cyberattacks, significantly increasing cybersecurity risks and reducing barriers to cybercrime.

**Attack vector:** `other`  

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`
