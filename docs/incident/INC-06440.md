### INC-06440  [↗](/incident/INC-06440.html)

**China's AI-Run Smart City Raises Privacy Concerns**  
_2020-12-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

Danish firm BIG and Chinese tech company Terminus plan to build Cloud Valley, an AI-managed smart city in Chongqing, China, using sensors to collect extensive personal data and automate services for residents. The project has sparked concerns over potential privacy violations and human rights risks due to pervasive surveillance and data collection.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`
