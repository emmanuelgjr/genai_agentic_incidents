### INC-06163  [↗](/incident/INC-06163.html)

**US Lab Develops AI System to Predict Names from Faces, Raising Privacy Concerns**  
_2021-04-09 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The US research center Mitre Corp developed an AI system that can predict a person's name from a facial image with up to 80% accuracy, using a dataset of over 13,000 labeled photos. While no harm has been reported, the technology raises significant privacy and misuse concerns.

**Attack vector:** `other`
