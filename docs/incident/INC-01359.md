### INC-01359  [↗](/incident/INC-01359.html)

**Madrid Bar Association Proposes Penal Reform to Address AI Legal Advice Risks**  
_2026-02-18 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The Madrid Bar Association (ICAM) has proposed a reform of Spain's Penal Code to criminalize the unauthorized provision of legal advice by AI platforms and chatbots without professional oversight. The initiative aims to prevent potential harm to citizens from relying on unsupervised automated legal advice, highlighting risks of errors and lack of accountability.
