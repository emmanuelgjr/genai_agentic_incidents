### INC-03922  [↗](/incident/INC-03922.html)

**Kaspersky warns of data privacy risks in ChatGPT's custom GPTs**  
_2024-02-01 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: Medium_

Kaspersky researchers reported that ChatGPT custom GPTs commonly expose their system prompts and 'knowledge files' via direct queries or prompt-injection, leaking proprietary configurations and sometimes PII embedded by creators.

**Affected:** OpenAI custom-GPT builders and users  
**Attack vector:** `prompt-injection`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM07`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI01`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MEASURE-2.10`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0051`, `AML.T0056`, `AML.T0057`  

**References:**
- [OECD AIM - Kaspersky warns of data privacy risks in ChatGPT's custom GPTs](https://oecd.ai/en/incidents/2024-02-01-ec1a) _(vendor)_
