### INC-06579  [↗](/incident/INC-06579.html)

**Facebook's AI Algorithms Promote Holocaust Denial Content Despite Ban**  
_2020-11-24 · real-world · Severity: High_

Despite Facebook's announced ban on Holocaust denial content, its AI-driven recommendation algorithms continued to promote and network such material, leaving numerous denial pages active. This failure in AI enforcement facilitated the spread of anti-Semitic misinformation and hate speech, undermining the platform's stated policy and causing harm to affected communities.

**Attack vector:** `rce`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`

**NIST AI RMF:** `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`
