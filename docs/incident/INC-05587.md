**Ele.me's AI-Driven Price Discrimination Harms Members and Consumers**  
_2021-03-14 · real-world · Severity: High_

Ele.me's AI-powered pricing algorithms have led to widespread consumer complaints in Shanghai, as members and frequent users are charged higher prices or receive fewer discounts than non-members. This algorithmic price discrimination, known as 'big data price gouging,' has resulted in unfair treatment and violation of consumer rights.
