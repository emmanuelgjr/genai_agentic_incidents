**Global Workers Fear Job Loss from AI and Automation by 2025, WEF Report Finds**  
_2021-04-02 · real-world · Severity: High_

A World Economic Forum report, based on a PwC survey of 32,000 workers in 19 countries, reveals that 40% of workers fear job loss within five years due to AI and automation, with 60% worried about machines taking over jobs. The findings highlight widespread concern about future employment risks from AI-driven automation.
