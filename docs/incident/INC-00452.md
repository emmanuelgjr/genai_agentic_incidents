### INC-00452  [↗](/incident/INC-00452.html)

**AI-Generated Fake Job Offers Lead to Widespread Scams and Data Theft**  
_2026-04-21 · real-world · Severity: High_

Scammers are increasingly using AI to create highly personalized and convincing fake job offers, deceiving job seekers into providing money or sensitive personal data. These AI-driven recruitment scams, difficult to detect due to their sophistication, have resulted in significant financial losses and privacy breaches for thousands of victims.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.10`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0057`
