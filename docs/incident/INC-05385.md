### INC-05385  [↗](/incident/INC-05385.html)

**AI Forecasts Massive Sovereign Credit Downgrades Due to Climate Inaction**  
_2021-03-18 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

University of Cambridge economists used artificial intelligence to predict that, without emissions cuts, 63 countries will face sovereign credit rating downgrades by 2030, leading to hundreds of billions in additional interest payments. The AI-driven analysis highlights significant future economic risks from climate change, not from AI malfunction.
