**First Arrests in Japan for AI-Generated Deepfake Porn Involving Celebrities**  
_2020-10-01 · real-world · Severity: High_

Two men in Japan were arrested for using AI deepfake technology to create and sell non-consensual pornographic videos featuring the faces of female celebrities. The videos, distributed online for profit, resulted in charges of defamation and copyright infringement, marking Japan's first arrests for deepfake-related offenses.
