### INC-06557  [↗](/incident/INC-06557.html)

**Facebook Audit Finds AI Moderation Failures Harm Civil Rights**  
_2020-07-08 · real-world · Severity: High_

An independent audit found that Facebook's AI-driven content moderation and recommendation systems failed to curb hate speech, misinformation, and discrimination, causing significant setbacks for civil rights. The report warns that these algorithmic decisions risk turning the platform into an echo chamber for extremism if stronger measures are not taken.
