**AI-Powered Loitering Munitions and Military Drones Cause and Pose Harm in Modern Warfare**  
_2021-04-21 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Russia's Lancet loitering munition, an AI-driven suicide drone, has autonomously identified and attacked targets, causing harm in conflict zones. Meanwhile, Russia and China are developing AI-enabled UAV swarms and precision strike drones, raising significant risks of future harm through autonomous military operations and infrastructure attacks.
