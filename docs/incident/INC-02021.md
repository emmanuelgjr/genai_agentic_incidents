**US Military Integrates AI Chatbot Grok Amid Safeguard Controversy**  
_2026-02-24 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The US Department of Defense has contracted xAI to integrate its AI chatbot Grok into classified military systems, replacing Anthropic's Claude, which refused to remove safety restrictions. The Pentagon is pressuring AI firms to lift safeguards for uses including autonomous weapons and mass surveillance, raising concerns about harm and rights violations.
