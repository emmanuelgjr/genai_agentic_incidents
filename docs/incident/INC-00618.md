### INC-00618  [↗](/incident/INC-00618.html)

**Anthropic's Mythos AI Exposes Security Flaws in Banking and macOS Systems**  
_2026-05-12 · real-world · Severity: High_

Anthropic's AI model Mythos identified numerous cybersecurity vulnerabilities in major US banks, prompting urgent patches and operational changes. Researchers also used Mythos to exploit a critical macOS vulnerability, bypassing Apple's security measures. The incidents highlight both the defensive and offensive capabilities of advanced AI in cybersecurity.

**Attack vector:** `other`
