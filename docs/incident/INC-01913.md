### INC-01913  [↗](/incident/INC-01913.html)

**Trump Accuses Iran of Using AI for Disinformation During Wartime**  
_2026-03-16 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

U.S. President Donald Trump accused Iran of using artificial intelligence to generate fake images and misinformation about wartime events, alleging Western media outlets spread these AI-generated materials. The claims highlight concerns about AI-driven disinformation but lack evidence of confirmed harm or incidents.
