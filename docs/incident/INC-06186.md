**WeRide Receives Permit to Test Driverless Cars in California**  
_2021-04-12 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Chinese startup WeRide has been granted a permit by the California DMV to test fully driverless AI-powered vehicles on public roads in San Jose. The permit allows testing without a human safety driver, raising potential future risks but no incidents or harm have been reported so far.
