### INC-03122  [↗](/incident/INC-03122.html)

**Purportedly AI-Generated Deepfake Reportedly Used to Impersonate DNB Bank CFO and CEO in Live Teams Meeting**  
_2025-01-21 · real-world · Severity: Critical_
AIID: [`#1246`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1246/)

Unknown cybercriminals reportedly used purported deepfakes to impersonate DNB Bank&#x27;s CFO and CEO during a live Microsoft Teams meeting, instructing employees at the bank&#x27;s Singapore office to transfer millions of Singapore dollars. The video and voice were allegedly cloned from public footage and appeared convincing. DNB identified the attempt as a &quot;CEO fraud&quot; and avoided losses, calling it their first encounter with a live deepfake meeting.
