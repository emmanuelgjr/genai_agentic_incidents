### INC-05640  [↗](/incident/INC-05640.html)

**Facial Recognition AI in China: Security Flaws, Privacy Violations, and Positive Outcomes**  
_2021-04-06 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI-powered facial recognition systems in China have led to both positive and negative incidents. Police successfully used the technology to reunite missing persons with families. However, widespread misuse and security flaws enabled unauthorized data collection, privacy violations, and system breaches, prompting regulatory scrutiny and calls for stricter governance and industry standards.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
