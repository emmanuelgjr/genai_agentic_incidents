### INC-08055  [↗](/incident/INC-08055.html)

**AI Algorithm in Australian Aged Care Causes Funding Errors and Harm**  
_2026-06-02 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

An AI-driven Integrated Assessment Tool used by the Australian government to allocate at-home aged care funding has led to over 1,000 complaints, with reports of inadequate support and inability to override algorithmic decisions. Lack of clinical validation and auditing has resulted in harm to elderly recipients.
