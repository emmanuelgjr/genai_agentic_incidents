**US Army Plans Deployment of AI Facial Recognition at Base Checkpoints Raises Concerns**  
_2021-04-05 · real-world · Severity: High_

The US Army is soliciting proposals to deploy AI-powered facial recognition systems at base entry points, aiming to automate identity verification through vehicle windshields in all conditions. While intended to enhance security, concerns have been raised about potential misidentification, racial bias, and privacy violations from the technology’s future use.
