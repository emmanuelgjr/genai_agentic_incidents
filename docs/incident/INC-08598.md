### INC-08598  [↗](/incident/INC-08598.html)

**Postorius is vulnerable to XSS**  
_2026-05 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: High_

CVEs: `CVE-2026-44742`
CWEs: `CWE-79`
CVSS: **7.2** — `CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:C/C:L/I:L/A:N`

Postorius through 1.3.13 does not escape HTML in the message subject when rendering it in the Held messages pop-up, as exploited in the wild in May 2026.

**Affected:** pip/postorius  
**Attack vector:** `xss`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM03`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI04`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-6.1`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0010`
