### INC-06185  [↗](/incident/INC-06185.html)

**WeChat's AI-Driven Political Censorship Extends to North American Users**  
_2021-01-09 · real-world · Severity: High_

Tencent's WeChat employs AI algorithms to censor and block politically sensitive content, even for users in North America. This automated censorship has led to account suspensions, content invisibility, and psychological and financial harm, violating users' rights to free expression and access to information. Lawsuits have been filed in response to these harms.

**Attack vector:** `other`
