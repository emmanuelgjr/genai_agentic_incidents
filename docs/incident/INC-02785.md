### INC-02785  [↗](/incident/INC-02785.html)

**Joann Fabrics Shoppers Reportedly Defrauded by AI-Generated Scam Sites, Part of Purported Wave of ~100,000 Fake Domains Across 194 Brands**  
_2025-08-20 · real-world · Severity: Medium_
AIID: [`#1189`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1189/)

Consumers were allegedly defrauded by AI-generated scam websites impersonating Joann Fabrics following the retailer&#x27;s bankruptcy. The alleged impostor sites reportedly used Joann&#x27;s branding to steal credit card details and personal data and leaving victims without products. Cybersecurity firm Netcraft estimated that nearly 100,000 domains created with AI tools were impersonating 194 brands, accounting for a reported 6–7% of global phishing activity.
