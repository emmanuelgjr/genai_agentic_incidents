### INC-01632  [↗](/incident/INC-01632.html)

**Pentagon Flags National Security Risks Over Anthropic's Foreign AI Workers**  
_2026-03-18 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The U.S. Pentagon warned that Anthropic's employment of foreign nationals, including Chinese citizens, in developing its Claude AI language model could pose national security risks. The concern centers on potential espionage or unauthorized access to sensitive military AI technology due to foreign worker involvement.
