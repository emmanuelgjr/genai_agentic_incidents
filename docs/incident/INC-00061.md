**AI Adoption in Spain Projected to Displace Up to 2.3 Million Jobs by 2035**  
_2026-04-29 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A report by Funcas warns that accelerated adoption of AI technologies in Spanish companies could lead to the loss of 1.7 to 2.3 million jobs between 2025 and 2035, particularly affecting administrative and technical roles. The forecast highlights significant potential societal harm from AI-driven labor market disruption in Spain.
