### INC-06650  [↗](/incident/INC-06650.html)

**Google Accused of AI-Driven Political Bias in Voter Reminders**  
_2020-11-06 · real-world · Severity: High_

Republican senators have demanded answers from Google after a study found its AI-driven systems sent get-out-the-vote reminders only to liberal users before the 2020 election. The selective targeting, revealed by Dr. Robert Epstein's monitoring project, raises concerns about AI-enabled political bias and potential harm to democratic processes.
