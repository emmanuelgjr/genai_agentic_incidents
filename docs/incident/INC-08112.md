**Nashville Zoo Opposes AI Data Center Over Animal Welfare Concerns**  
_2026-06-05 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Nashville Zoo is opposing the construction of a 69,000-square-foot data center near its animal enclosures, citing concerns that noise from the facility, which may serve AI companies, could disturb sensitive species like leopards and tigers. The zoo has launched a petition urging city intervention.
