### INC-03498  [↗](/incident/INC-03498.html)

**AI-Generated Media Reportedly Used in Russian Disinformation Campaign in Moldova**  
_2024-09-18 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#840`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/840/)

Russian-linked entities allegedly deployed AI-generated images and videos to spread disinformation aimed at swaying Moldova’s referendum on E.U. membership. The AI-enhanced media campaign included fabricated stories and doctored visuals, the purpose of which was reportedly designed to amplify fear and undermine pro-E.U. sentiment in the days leading up to the referendum.
