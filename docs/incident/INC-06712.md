### INC-06712  [↗](/incident/INC-06712.html)

**Indonesian Minister Warns 23 Million Jobs at Risk from AI and Automation**  
_2020-12-04 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Indonesia's Minister of Manpower, Ida Fauziyah, cited studies predicting that 23 million types of jobs in Indonesia could be replaced by robots and automation as the country enters the Industry 4.0 era. The warnings highlight potential large-scale job displacement due to increasing adoption of AI and digital technologies.
