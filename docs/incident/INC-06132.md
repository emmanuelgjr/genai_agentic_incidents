**Turkey Prepares to Deploy AI-Enabled Armed Robotic Vehicles for Military Use**  
_2021-05-23 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

HAVELSAN is set to deploy Barkan, an AI-powered armed unmanned ground vehicle capable of coordinated missions with drones, for military operations. The system uses AI-supported autonomy and swarm algorithms, raising credible risks of harm if malfunction or misuse occurs, though no incidents have been reported yet.

**Attack vector:** `other`
