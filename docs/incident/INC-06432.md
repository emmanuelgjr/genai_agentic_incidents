**China Launches Robotaxi Pilot Projects Amid AI Safety Concerns**  
_2020-08-09 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Chinese tech firms including Baidu, AutoX, and DiDi Chuxing have launched pilot robotaxi services in several cities, using autonomous driving AI. While no harm has occurred, the technology remains in testing with safety drivers, highlighting potential future risks as China races to compete with US autonomous vehicle efforts.
