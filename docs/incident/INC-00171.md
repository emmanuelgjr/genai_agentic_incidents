**AI Hallucination in Police Report Leads to Fan Ban and Public Apology**  
_2026-03-04 · real-world · Severity: High_

West Midlands Police used Microsoft's Copilot AI tool to draft a report containing false information, which led to Maccabi Tel Aviv fans being banned from a football match in Birmingham. The AI-generated inaccuracies prompted a public apology, suspension of the AI tool, and an official review into the incident.
