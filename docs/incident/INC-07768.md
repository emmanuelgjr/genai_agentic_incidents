**Tesla Plans Nationwide Expansion of Fully Autonomous Cars Amid Safety Concerns**  
_2026-05-18 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Elon Musk announced Tesla's plan to expand fully self-driving cars without human safety monitors across the US this year, following initial deployment in Texas.
