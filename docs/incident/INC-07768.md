**AI-Powered License Plate Readers Enable Widespread Surveillance and Legal Violations in the US**  
_2026-05-26 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI-powered automated license plate readers (ALPRs) have been widely deployed by law enforcement and on school buses across the US, leading to privacy violations, unconstitutional surveillance, and breaches of state law. Data has been improperly shared with federal agencies, and mission creep has enabled unrestricted access and misuse of sensitive location data.
