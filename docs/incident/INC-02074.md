### INC-02074  [↗](/incident/INC-02074.html)

**Waymo Robotaxi AI Failures Lead to Vehicle Recall and Community Disruption in the US**  
_2026-05-13 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Waymo's autonomous vehicles experienced multiple AI-related incidents in the US, including a robotaxi in Texas ignoring flood warnings and being swept away, and numerous vehicles in Atlanta repeatedly circling neighborhoods, causing traffic and safety concerns. These malfunctions prompted Waymo to recall nearly 3,800 vehicles and suspend local services for software updates.
