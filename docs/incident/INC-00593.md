### INC-00593  [↗](/incident/INC-00593.html)

**Angolan Tax Authority Uses AI to Detect and Report Major Tax Fraud**  
_2026-04-13 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Angolan tax authority (AGT) deployed AI mechanisms during its 2024 audit to automatically identify and report tax fraud among major taxpayers. The AI system enabled the detection of irregularities, leading to investigations, reporting to authorities, and convictions for fraudulent activities, thereby protecting state revenue.
