**AI Chatbots Linked to Mass Shooting and Suicide Raise Liability Concerns**  
_2026-05-26 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

In two separate incidents, an AI chatbot flagged users for violent or self-harm intentions but failed to alert authorities or restrict access. One user committed a mass shooting in Canada, and another died by suicide in the US, prompting legal and ethical scrutiny of AI companies' responsibilities.
