### INC-07805  [↗](/incident/INC-07805.html)

**AI-Generated Lawyer Voice Cloning Used in Major Fraud Scheme in São Paulo**  
_2026-05-19 · real-world · Severity: High_

A criminal group in São Paulo used AI to clone lawyers' voices, impersonating them in phone scams that convinced victims to transfer money. The scheme, uncovered by police and the OAB-SP, defrauded at least 12 victims of around R$10 million over six months. Multiple suspects were arrested.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
