**Canada Cancels Drone Tech Exports to Turkey Over AI Use in Nagorno-Karabakh Conflict**  
_2021-04-12 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Canada cancelled export permits for drone technology to Turkey after finding that Canadian-made AI-enabled imaging and targeting systems were used by Azerbaijan in the Nagorno-Karabakh conflict, violating end-use assurances and contributing to harm in the region. The decision responds to the misuse of AI systems in military operations.
