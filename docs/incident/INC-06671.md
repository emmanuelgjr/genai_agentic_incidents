### INC-06671  [↗](/incident/INC-06671.html)

**Google's AI-Enabled Nest Hub Max Raises Privacy Concerns with Continuous User Monitoring**  
_2020-12-21 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Google is developing new features for its Nest Hub Max smart display that use AI, cameras, microphones, and ultrasonic sensors to detect user presence and environmental sounds without explicit activation. This continuous monitoring capability raises significant privacy concerns, as it could potentially lead to unauthorized surveillance or misuse of personal data.

**Attack vector:** `other`
