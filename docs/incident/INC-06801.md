### INC-06801  [↗](/incident/INC-06801.html)

**Moscow's Facial Recognition System Breached, Enabling Illegal Surveillance for $200**  
_2020-11-09 · real-world · Severity: High_

Moscow's AI-powered facial recognition system, intended for law enforcement, was illicitly accessed and exploited, allowing personal movement data to be sold for $200. This breach exposed individuals to privacy violations and potential harm, prompting lawsuits and police investigations into misuse and systemic vulnerabilities in the city's surveillance infrastructure.
