### INC-06716  [↗](/incident/INC-06716.html)

**Instagram Deploys AI to Detect and Remove Self-Harm Content in Europe**  
_2020-11-11 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Instagram has introduced AI-powered tools in the UK and Europe to proactively detect and moderate self-harm and suicide-related content, aiming to reduce harm to vulnerable users. Full intervention features are limited by GDPR, but the system can automatically reduce visibility or remove harmful posts, enhancing user safety.
