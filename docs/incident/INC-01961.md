### INC-01961  [↗](/incident/INC-01961.html)

**Ukraine Increases Procurement and Deployment of AI-Enabled Military Drones**  
_2026-03-11 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Ukraine's Ministry of Defense has contracted a record number of AI-enabled drones, such as Mavic, Autel, and Matrice, for frontline military use. These drones are used for reconnaissance, fire correction, and offensive operations, raising credible risks of harm due to their deployment in active conflict zones.

**Attack vector:** `other`
