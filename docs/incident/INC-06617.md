### INC-06617  [↗](/incident/INC-06617.html)

**Filipino Actress Maine Mendoza Targeted by Deepfake Pornography, Legal Action Pursued**  
_2020-12-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

Filipino actress Maine Mendoza became the victim of a deepfake pornographic video, where AI technology was used to falsely depict her in explicit content. Her agency, All Access to Artists, Inc., confirmed the video's inauthenticity and announced plans for legal action against those responsible for creating and sharing the manipulated material.
