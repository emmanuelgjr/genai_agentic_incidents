### INC-06688  [↗](/incident/INC-06688.html)

**ICE Uses AI Facial Recognition for Warrantless Searches on Maryland Immigrant Drivers' Licenses**  
_2020-02-26 · real-world · Severity: High_

U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) used AI-powered facial recognition to conduct warrantless searches on millions of Maryland driver's license photos, including those of undocumented immigrants. This practice violated privacy and trust, leading to arrests and fear among immigrant communities, and sparked legislative efforts to restrict such AI use.
