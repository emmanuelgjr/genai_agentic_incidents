### INC-00483  [↗](/incident/INC-00483.html)

**AI-Generated Minor Persona Used to Expose Suspected Pedophile in France**  
_2026-05-12 · real-world · Severity: High_

A streamer used AI to create a realistic adolescent avatar and voice, engaging a 66-year-old ex-sports teacher in explicit conversations online. The AI-enabled sting led to the suspect's detention and investigation for sexual propositions to a minor, exposing predatory behavior and prompting legal action in France.
