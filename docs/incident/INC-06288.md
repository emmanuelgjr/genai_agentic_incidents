**AI-Enabled Drones Used for Policing and Warfare Cause Privacy and Physical Harm Concerns**  
_2020-05-08 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Elizabeth, New Jersey, deployed Chinese AI-powered drones to enforce social distancing, raising privacy and data-sharing concerns. Separately, military and terrorist use of autonomous drones has resulted in injuries, deaths, and infrastructure damage, highlighting the direct harms and risks associated with AI-driven drone technology in both civilian and combat contexts.
