### INC-00512  [↗](/incident/INC-00512.html)

**AI-Generated Videos Used in Religious Charity Scam in Taiwan**  
_2026-05-03 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Fraudsters in Taiwan used AI-generated videos and images to impersonate religious and charity representatives, promoting fake fundraising campaigns on social media after the Baishatun Mazu pilgrimage. Victims were deceived into donating money, causing financial loss and damaging public trust. Authorities and the charity have reported the incident to police and issued warnings.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
