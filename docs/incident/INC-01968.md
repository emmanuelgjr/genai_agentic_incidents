**UN AI Advisor Warns of Risks: Human Impersonation and Neural Data Commercialization**  
_2026-05-08 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Carme Artigas, co-chair of the UN AI Advisory Council, highlighted two major AI risks at a conference in Oleiros, Spain: technologies that simulate humans and the commercialization of neural data. She emphasized the need for robust regulation to address these potential hazards.
