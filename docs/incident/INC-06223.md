### INC-06223  [↗](/incident/INC-06223.html)

**AI Expert Warns of Greater Threat from AI Robots than Climate Change**  
_2020-01-19 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

AI expert Dr. David Levy warns that AI-equipped robots and drones could pose a more immediate threat to humanity than climate change, citing risks from malfunction, misinformation, or malicious hacking. He criticizes slow governmental regulation and urges urgent legal frameworks to prevent potential harm from these technologies.

**Attack vector:** `misinformation`
