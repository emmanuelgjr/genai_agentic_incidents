**Meta's AI-Driven Employee Monitoring and Layoffs Spark Privacy and Labor Concerns**  
_2026-06-01 · real-world · Severity: High_

Meta implemented AI systems to monitor employee activity and collect data for AI training, leading to privacy violations and employee protests. Additionally, Meta and other companies conducted mass layoffs to redirect resources toward costly AI investments, causing significant harm to workers. European regulators are scrutinizing Meta's practices for potential privacy breaches.
