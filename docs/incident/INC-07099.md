**US Police Test AI-Equipped Drones for Covid-19 Symptom Detection in Crowds**  
_2020-04-24 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Westport, US police tested DraganFly's AI-powered 'pandemic drones' to detect Covid-19 symptoms—such as fever, coughing, and sneezing—in public crowds using computer vision and biometric sensors. While no harm has been reported, the technology raises concerns about privacy and potential misuse.
