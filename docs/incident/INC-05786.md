**NTSB Criticizes Tesla for Testing Unfinished Autopilot AI on Public Roads**  
_2021-03-13 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The US National Transportation Safety Board (NTSB) criticized Tesla for testing its AI-driven Autopilot and Full Self-Driving systems on public roads with limited oversight, citing past fatal crashes and ongoing safety risks. The NTSB urged stricter federal regulations to prevent harm from insufficiently regulated autonomous vehicle technology.
