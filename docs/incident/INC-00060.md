**AI Adoption Drives Wage Gap and Job Losses for Entry-Level Workers**  
_2026-04-29 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

AI systems are displacing entry-level jobs by automating codified knowledge tasks, leading to significant job losses and wage disparities between junior and senior workers. This shift, highlighted by a Federal Reserve Bank of Dallas study, is fundamentally altering career trajectories and causing realized harm in the labor market.
