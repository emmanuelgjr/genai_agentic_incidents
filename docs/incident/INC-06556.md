### INC-06556  [↗](/incident/INC-06556.html)

**Facebook Apologizes for AI Moderation Failures in 2018 Sri Lanka Violence**  
_2020-05-13 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Facebook admitted its AI-driven content moderation failed to curb hate speech and disinformation during Sri Lanka's 2018 anti-Muslim riots, contributing to violence that left at least three dead and 20 injured. The company apologized after investigations linked its platform's shortcomings to real-world harm and human rights violations.
