### INC-03565  [↗](/incident/INC-03565.html)

**An SSRF (Server-Side Request Forgery) vulnerability exists in the gradio-app/gradio repository, allowing attackers to scan and identify open ports within an internal network.**  
_2024-04 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: Medium_

CVEs: `CVE-2024-1183`
CVSS: **6.5**

An SSRF (Server-Side Request Forgery) vulnerability exists in the gradio-app/gradio repository, allowing attackers to scan and identify open ports within an internal network. By manipulating the 'file' parameter in a GET request, an attacker can discern the status of internal ports based on the presence of a 'Location' header or a 'File not allowed' error in the response.

**Affected:** gradio_project/gradio  
**Attack vector:** `ssrf`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM03`, `LLM05`
