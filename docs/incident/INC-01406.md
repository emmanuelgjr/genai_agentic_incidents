### INC-01406  [↗](/incident/INC-01406.html)

**Meta Faces Legal Action for Training AI on Millions of Pirated Books**  
_2026-03-10 · real-world · Severity: High_

Meta is under legal scrutiny for using millions of pirated books, downloaded via BitTorrent from sites like Z-Library and Anna's Archive, to train its AI models, including Llama. The company claims this constitutes "fair use," but authors and publishers allege massive copyright violations and economic harm.
