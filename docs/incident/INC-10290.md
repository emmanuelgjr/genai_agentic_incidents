### INC-10290  [↗](/incident/INC-10290.html)

**gnark is vulnerable to signature malleability in EdDSA and ECDSA due to missing scalar checks**  
_2025-08 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: High_

CVEs: `CVE-2025-57801`
CWEs: `CWE-347`
CVSS: **9.1** — `CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:U/C:N/I:H/A:H`

In version before, `sig.s` used without asserting `0 ≤ S < order` in `Verify function` in [eddsa.go](https://github.com/Consensys/gnark/blob/d9a42397979b05f95f21a601fd219b06a8d60b7b/std/signature/eddsa/eddsa.go) and [ecdsa.go](https://github.com/Consensys/gnark/blob/d9a42397979b05f95f21a601fd219b06a8d60b7b/std/signature/ecdsa/ecdsa.go), which will lead to *signature malleability* vulnerability. ### Impact Since gnark’s native EdDSA and ECDSA circuits lack essential constraints, multiple distinct witnesses can satisfy the same public inputs.
