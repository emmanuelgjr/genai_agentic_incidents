### INC-07567  [↗](/incident/INC-07567.html)

**Inappropriate Gmail Smart Reply Suggestions**  
_2015-11-03 · real-world · Severity: Medium_
AIID: [`#17`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/17/)

Google developed Smart Reply, an AI-powered feature for Gmail that analyzes email content and suggests three brief response options to help users quickly reply to messages. The system uses machine learning and neural networks to analyze billions of Gmail conversations and generate contextually appropriate responses. During development and early testing phases, the AI system exhibited problematic behavior by frequently suggesting inappropriate responses. Early prototypes had a 'propensity to respond with I love you to seemingly anything' according to Google's director of product management Ajit Varma. The system also suggested 'Sent from my iPhone' as a response option, having identified this as a common phrase in emails without understanding it was an automatic signature. Google engineers discovered these issues during internal testing and modified the algorithms before public release. The feature launched in Google's Inbox app in 2015 and later became available to Gmail's 1.4 billion users. Around 10 percent of Gmail responses are now generated using Smart Reply. Some users reported feeling that automated responses made communication feel less personal and human.
