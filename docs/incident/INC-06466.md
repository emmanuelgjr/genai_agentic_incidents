### INC-06466  [↗](/incident/INC-06466.html)

**Clearview AI's Facial Recognition Sparks Global Privacy Violations and Investigations**  
_2020-07-13 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Clearview AI scraped billions of images from the internet without consent to build a facial recognition database, selling access to law enforcement and private clients. This led to privacy violations in the EU, Canada, and Australia, triggering regulatory investigations, legal complaints, and public backlash over unauthorized biometric data use.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`
