### INC-03043  [↗](/incident/INC-03043.html)

**Prime Minister of Thailand Paetongtarn Shinawatra Claims AI Voice Scam Impersonated ASEAN Leader Requesting Money**  
_2025-01-15 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#902`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/902/)

Thailand&#x27;s Prime Minister Paetongtarn Shinawatra reported being targeted by an AI-generated voice scam that mimicked a well-known but undisclosed ASEAN leader. The scam is alleged to have involved a realistic voice message requesting a donation, falsely claiming Thailand was the only ASEAN nation yet to contribute.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
