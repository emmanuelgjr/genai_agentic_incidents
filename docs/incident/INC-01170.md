**Hungarian Government Uses AI Surveillance Tools for Mass Tracking in Violation of EU Laws**  
_2026-04-09 · real-world · Severity: High_

Hungarian intelligence agencies secretly used AI-powered surveillance tools, including Cobwebs Technologies' Webloc, to track hundreds of millions via smartphone ad data without consent, violating EU privacy laws. A domestic AI espionage platform, Q-VASZ, failed after significant investment. The mass surveillance raises serious privacy and legal concerns.
