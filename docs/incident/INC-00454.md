### INC-00454  [↗](/incident/INC-00454.html)

**AI-Generated Fake Magazine Cover Broadcast on CNews Causes Misinformation**  
_2026-05-04 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

CNews presenter Pascal Praud broadcast an AI-generated fake magazine cover featuring Yaël Braun-Pivet and Najat Vallaud-Belkacem without verification, leading to misinformation and reputational harm. Braun-Pivet reported the incident to France's audiovisual regulator, Arcom. The error was later acknowledged and corrected on air.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
