### INC-05711  [↗](/incident/INC-05711.html)

**Instagram Alters Algorithm After Suppression of Pro-Palestinian Content**  
_2021-05-30 · real-world · Severity: High_

Instagram changed its content ranking algorithm after employees and users reported that pro-Palestinian posts were being suppressed during the Gaza conflict. The AI-driven system had prioritized original content over re-shared posts, unintentionally limiting the reach of certain viewpoints. Instagram now treats original and re-shared content equally to address the issue.
