**Theft of AI-Enabled Military Drones Sparks Security Fears in the US**  
_2026-03-13 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Several AI-equipped Skydio X10D drones were stolen from Fort Campbell military base in Kentucky, USA. The drones feature advanced AI for obstacle avoidance and 5G connectivity. Authorities fear their potential misuse in attacks, prompting a reward for information and heightened security alerts, but no actual harm has occurred yet.
