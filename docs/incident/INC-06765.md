**Lower Saxony Deploys AI to Combat Child Pornography**  
_2020-01-15 · real-world · Severity: High_

Lower Saxony police have introduced AI-based software, developed by the state criminal office, to analyze large volumes of digital data and identify child sexual abuse material. The system aims to accelerate investigations, reduce manual workload for officers, and enable faster prosecution of offenders.
