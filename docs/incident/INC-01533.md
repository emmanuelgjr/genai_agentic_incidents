**NHTSA Investigates Avride-Uber Robotaxi Crashes in Texas**  
_2026-05-08 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The US National Highway Traffic Safety Administration is investigating Avride, Uber's autonomous vehicle partner, after 16 crashes—including property damage and a minor injury—in Dallas and Austin. The incidents, linked to failures in Avride's AI driving system, raise concerns about the safety and competence of self-driving robotaxis.
