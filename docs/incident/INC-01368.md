**Man Arrested in Salta for Creating and Distributing AI-Generated Fake Intimate Images**  
_2026-05-12 · real-world · Severity: High_

In Salta, Argentina, a man was arrested for using AI tools to create and distribute fake explicit images of at least eleven women, including a university dean. He sourced photos from social media, manipulated them with AI, and published them online, causing significant psychological and social harm to the victims.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
