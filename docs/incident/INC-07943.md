### INC-07943  [↗](/incident/INC-07943.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfake Video Falsely Announces Death of Brazilian Actress**  
_2026-05-20 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Brazilian actress Cláudia Abreu filed a lawsuit against Google after a YouTube channel published an AI-generated deepfake video falsely announcing her death. The video, using synthetic image and voice resembling journalist Renata Vasconcellos, caused reputational and emotional harm, prompting legal action for content removal and identification of the creator.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
