### INC-00225  [↗](/incident/INC-00225.html)

**AI Systems Accelerate Cybersecurity Risks and Real-World Incidents**  
_2026-05-13 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI models such as Microsoft's MDASH, Anthropic's Mythos, and OpenAI's GPT-5.5 are rapidly advancing in autonomously finding and exploiting software vulnerabilities, leading to both the discovery of new security flaws and increased risks of AI-enabled cyberattacks. Authorities and experts warn of urgent threats to critical infrastructure, especially in Europe.
