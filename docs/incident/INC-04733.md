### INC-04733  [↗](/incident/INC-04733.html)

**Martin Lewis deepfake scam ad on Facebook**  
_2023-07 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A deepfake video of UK consumer-finance figure Martin Lewis was used in a Facebook ad falsely showing him endorsing an investment scheme. Lewis publicly disowned the video; Meta removed instances after the disclosure.

**Affected:** Facebook users / Martin Lewis  
**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM03`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI02`, `ASI03`, `ASI09`
