### INC-07253  [↗](/incident/INC-07253.html)

**Waze Allegedly Frequently Routed Drivers through the Town of Los Gatos, Blocking Its Single Wildfire Escape Route**  
_2019-09-06 · real-world · Severity: Medium_
AIID: [`#234`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/234/)

The Waze navigation app, owned by Alphabet Inc., has been routing traffic through residential neighborhoods in Los Gatos, California, to bypass congestion on Highway 17, particularly during summer weekends when beach traffic is heavy. Jeffrey Siegel, a tech consultant and founder of the Los Gatos Historical Society, reports that this traffic diversion creates dangerous conditions for residents in the Glenridge and Almond Grove neighborhoods. According to a USA Today wildfire risk assessment, Los Gatos scored 4.06 on a 1-5 wildfire hazard scale, higher than Paradise, California (3.81), where 85 people died in a 2018 fire that caused $16.5 billion in damage. Santa Clara County Fire Captain Bill Murphy confirmed that traffic congestion along the Highway 17 corridor presents unique challenges for wildfire response. Siegel reports that weekend trips that take 5 minutes during weekdays can take 45 minutes due to Waze-directed traffic. The Los Gatos Historical Society has launched a campaign to pressure Google and government officials to address what they describe as a life-threatening situation where residents could be trapped during wildfire evacuations. Multiple attempts to contact Waze's government affairs team have been unsuccessful, and the company did not respond to media requests for comment.
