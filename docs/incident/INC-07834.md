### INC-07834  [↗](/incident/INC-07834.html)

**Apple Uses AI to Block Billions in App Store Fraud, but False Positives Harm Developers**  
_2026-05-20 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

In 2025, Apple used AI and human review to block over $2.2 billion in fraudulent App Store transactions, reject more than 2 million malicious app submissions, and stop over 1 billion fake accounts. However, the AI system also wrongly flagged legitimate developers, causing economic harm and access issues.
