**Anthropic and OpenAI Hire Weapons Experts to Prevent AI Misuse in Weapon Creation**  
_2026-03-13 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Anthropic and OpenAI are recruiting experts in chemical, radiological, and biological weapons to strengthen safeguards against the misuse of their AI systems, such as Claude and ChatGPT, for creating weapons of mass destruction. This move addresses growing concerns about AI's potential to facilitate catastrophic harm if exploited.
