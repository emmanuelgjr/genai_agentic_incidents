### INC-06821  [↗](/incident/INC-06821.html)

**NSO Group's AI Systems Breach Privacy in Demos and Spyware Attacks**  
_2020-12-29 · real-world · Severity: High_

NSO Group used real, unsuspecting individuals' location data in demos of its AI-powered contact-tracing system, Fleming, violating privacy rights. Additionally, its advanced AI-enabled spyware infected journalists' phones without interaction, leading to unauthorized surveillance and data breaches, raising significant concerns over misuse and harm from AI surveillance technologies.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`

**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MEASURE-2.10`
