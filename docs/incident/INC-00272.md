### INC-00272  [↗](/incident/INC-00272.html)

**AI-Driven Bot Attacks Surge 12.5x, Dominate Internet Traffic in 2025**  
_2026-04-29 · real-world · Severity: High_

According to Thales' 2026 Bad Bot Report, AI-driven bot attacks surged 12.5 times in 2025, with bots now making up over half of all internet traffic. These AI bots increasingly target APIs and identity systems, causing widespread security breaches, data theft, and account takeovers across industries globally.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.10`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0057`
