**Facebook Settles Facial Recognition Privacy Lawsuit for $650 Million**  
_2020-07-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

Facebook agreed to pay $650 million to settle a class-action lawsuit after its facial recognition AI system violated Illinois' Biometric Information Privacy Act by collecting users' biometric data without consent. The settlement follows legal action by Illinois residents and addresses privacy harms caused by the unauthorized use of AI technology.
