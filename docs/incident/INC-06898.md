### INC-06898  [↗](/incident/INC-06898.html)

**Russian State Media Uses Deepfake AI to Satirize Trump Post-Election**  
_2020-09-22 · real-world · Severity: High_

Russian state-owned network RT produced a deepfake video using AI to depict Donald Trump as a post-election anchor for their channel, employing his real voice and likeness. The video, intended as satire, highlights the use of AI-generated media for political propaganda and misinformation, raising concerns about election interference and public deception.
