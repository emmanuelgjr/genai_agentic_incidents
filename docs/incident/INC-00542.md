### INC-00542  [↗](/incident/INC-00542.html)

**AI-Powered Editing Tools Drive Surge in Insurance Fraud in the US**  
_2026-03-17 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A Verisk study reveals that AI-powered image editing tools are fueling a rise in digital insurance fraud, with 36% of US consumers willing to alter claim images or documents. Insurers report increasingly sophisticated manipulated media, challenging detection and eroding trust in the insurance process.
