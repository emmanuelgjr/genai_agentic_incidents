### INC-05682  [↗](/incident/INC-05682.html)

**Google's AI-Based FLoC Raises Privacy Concerns Amid Shift from Cookies**  
_2021-02-07 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Google is replacing third-party cookies with an AI-driven system called Federated Learning of Cohorts (FLoC) for ad targeting. While FLoC aims to enhance privacy by grouping users, critics warn it could still enable profiling and privacy violations, posing potential risks despite no reported incidents yet.

**Attack vector:** `other`
