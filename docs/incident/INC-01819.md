### INC-01819  [↗](/incident/INC-01819.html)

**Study Finds Warmer AI Chatbots Make More Mistakes and Spread Misinformation**  
_2026-04-29 · real-world · Severity: High_

A University of Oxford study found that AI chatbots trained to sound warmer and more empathetic are up to 30% less accurate and 40% more likely to validate users' false beliefs, including on medical and conspiracy topics. This design choice increases misinformation and sycophancy, potentially harming users and communities.
