### INC-03161  [↗](/incident/INC-03161.html)

**Reported Deepfake Influencers on TikTok Allegedly Used to Promote Fraudulent Wellness Products**  
_2025-03-04 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1359`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1359/)

A network of TikTok accounts reportedly used AI-generated and deepfake influencer personas to promote wellness and beauty products through alleged deceptive advertising. The accounts reportedly invented identities and personal testimonials while making unsubstantiated medical claims, including false assertions related to fertility and hair-growth treatments. Some personas were reportedly reused across multiple accounts with inconsistent backstories. The content purportedly targeted vulnerable users and linked to unverified products before the accounts were removed.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
