**Epiroc and Partners Deploy Autonomous Mining Trucks in Australia, Raising Future AI Safety Concerns**  
_2020-02-12 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Epiroc, in partnership with ASI Mining, Hitachi, and Wenco, is converting Roy Hill’s mining trucks in Western Australia from manned to autonomous operation using AI systems. While aiming to boost safety and productivity, the deployment introduces plausible future risks of AI-related accidents or operational disruptions in critical mining infrastructure.

**Attack vector:** `other`
