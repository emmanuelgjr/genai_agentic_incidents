**Italian Parliament Debates Privacy and Legal Safeguards for AI-Powered 'Immuni' Contact Tracing App**  
_2020-04-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

Italian political parties are demanding parliamentary oversight and legal safeguards for the AI-based 'Immuni' contact tracing app, citing concerns over privacy, data management, and potential discrimination. No harm has occurred, but there is debate over the app's governance to prevent possible rights violations during its deployment against COVID-19.
