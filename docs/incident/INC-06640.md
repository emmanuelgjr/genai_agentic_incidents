**Global Campaign Calls for Ban on AI-Powered Killer Robots**  
_2020-02-26 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Activists, including Nobel laureate Jody Williams, and a coalition of NGOs are urging a global ban on lethal autonomous weapons—AI-powered systems capable of killing without human intervention. At events in Buenos Aires, they highlighted ethical, legal, and technological risks, warning of potential future harm if such AI systems are deployed.
