**US Federal Agencies' Unregulated Use of Facial Recognition AI Leads to Civil Rights Violations**  
_2021-06-29 · real-world · Severity: High_

Multiple US federal agencies have used facial recognition AI systems with little oversight, resulting in privacy violations, wrongful arrests, and potential suppression of constitutional rights. Watchdog reports highlight untracked use, lack of accountability, and accuracy concerns, directly causing harm to individuals and communities.
