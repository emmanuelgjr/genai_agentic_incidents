**Tesla Autopilot Misuse and Safety Concerns Lead to Legal Action and Fatalities**  
_2020-09-22 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

A Canadian man was charged for sleeping while his Tesla, using Autopilot, drove at high speed, highlighting misuse of the AI system. Studies and crash investigations reveal that Tesla's Autopilot has contributed to driver distraction and multiple fatal accidents, raising ongoing safety concerns about the system's deployment.
