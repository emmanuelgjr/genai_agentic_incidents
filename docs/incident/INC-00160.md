### INC-00160  [↗](/incident/INC-00160.html)

**AI Detection Tools Falsely Accuse Human Content, Enable Extortion**  
_2026-03-30 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Investigations revealed that several AI-powered content detection tools falsely label genuine human-written texts as AI-generated, leading to reputational harm and extortion attempts. These tools mislead users, damage credibility, and exploit individuals financially by offering paid services to 'humanize' content, exacerbating misinformation and trust issues online.

**Attack vector:** `misinformation`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0058`
