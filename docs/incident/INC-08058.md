**Chinese Military-Affiliated Institutions Seek Advanced Nvidia AI Chips Despite Export Controls**  
_2026-06-02 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Multiple Chinese military and defense-linked universities have sought to acquire or rent advanced Nvidia AI chips, including the H200, for potential military applications. Despite U.S. export restrictions and blacklisting, procurement efforts continue, raising concerns about the use of these AI systems in military research and national security threats. No direct harm reported yet.
