### INC-05846  [↗](/incident/INC-05846.html)

**Shanghai Deploys AI Digital Twin for Real-Time Urban Safety Management**  
_2021-02-25 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Shanghai, in partnership with Huawei, launched an AI-powered digital twin system in the historic Nanjing Building to monitor real-time risks. The system detected a high-risk behavior (open window), alerted security, and enabled immediate intervention, preventing potential harm to pedestrians. This marks a significant step in AI-driven urban safety and governance.
