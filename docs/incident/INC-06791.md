**Microsoft's AI Editor Causes Job Losses and Misidentification Incident at MSN News**  
_2020-07-14 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Microsoft replaced dozens of MSN news staffers with AI editors, resulting in job losses. The AI system subsequently misidentified two mixed-race singers from Little Mix, publishing the wrong image and causing public offense and reputational harm, highlighting risks of AI-driven editorial errors and their impact on individuals and communities.
