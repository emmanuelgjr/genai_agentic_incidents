### INC-06707  [↗](/incident/INC-06707.html)

**India's Faulty Facial Recognition System Misidentifies Delhi Rioters**  
_2020-03-11 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Indian law enforcement used low-accuracy facial recognition software to identify over 1,900 alleged rioters in Delhi, relying on government ID databases. The system's inability to distinguish genders and its rushed, unregulated deployment have raised concerns about misidentification, privacy violations, and potential harm to innocent individuals.
