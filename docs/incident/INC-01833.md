### INC-01833  [↗](/incident/INC-01833.html)

**Suspected Terrorist Attack Targets Czech AI Drone Factory Supplying Ukraine**  
_2026-03-21 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A fire broke out at LPP Holding's factory in Pardubice, Czechia, which produces AI-powered attack drones for Ukraine. Authorities are investigating the incident as a possible terrorist attack, following claims of responsibility by an anti-arms group. The fire disrupted production of critical AI military systems.

**Attack vector:** `other`
