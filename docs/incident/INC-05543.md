### INC-05543  [↗](/incident/INC-05543.html)

**Chinese Retailers Investigated for Illegal AI Facial Recognition Data Collection**  
_2021-03-19 · real-world · Severity: High_

Multiple Chinese retailers, including Miniso and Kohler, used AI-powered facial recognition cameras to covertly collect and analyze customers' biometric data without consent, violating privacy laws. Authorities launched investigations, removed cameras, and highlighted risks of data misuse, financial fraud, and threats to personal safety from unauthorized AI-driven data collection.
