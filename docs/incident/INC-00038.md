**Actors and Voice Artists Protest Unauthorized AI Voice and Image Use**  
_2026-02-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

Actors and voice artists, including Hong Kong dubbing professionals and Oscar-winner Matthew McConaughey, are protesting the unauthorized use of their voices and images by AI systems. They argue that AI-generated voice and image synthesis infringes on intellectual property and labor rights, threatening their livelihoods and creative autonomy.
