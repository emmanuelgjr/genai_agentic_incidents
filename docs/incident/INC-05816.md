### INC-05816  [↗](/incident/INC-05816.html)

**RCMP AI facial recognition surveillance ruled unlawful**  
_2021-06 · real-world · Severity: High_

Canada's Privacy Commissioner found the Royal Canadian Mounted Police violated the Privacy Act by using Clearview AI's facial recognition to collect Canadians' personal information from publicly posted images without knowledge or consent.

**Affected:** Canadian public  
**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.1`, `GOVERN-1.4`, `MEASURE-2.10`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0024`, `AML.T0057`
