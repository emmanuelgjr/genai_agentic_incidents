### INC-00571  [↗](/incident/INC-00571.html)

**Airbus Equips AI-Enabled Valkyrie Combat Drones for German Air Force**  
_2026-03-13 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Airbus, in partnership with Kratos, is preparing AI-enabled Valkyrie combat drones for the German Air Force. These drones, equipped with the MARS mission system and MindShare AI, will operate autonomously and coordinate with manned aircraft, raising concerns about future risks of autonomous lethal operations. Initial flights are planned in Germany in 2024.
