**InvestCloud Replaces Entire Italian Workforce with AI, Leading to Mass Layoffs**  
_2026-03-11 · real-world · Severity: High_

InvestCloud Italy, a fintech company in Marghera, laid off all 37 employees and closed its only Italian office, citing a shift to an AI-driven organizational model. The decision, part of a global restructuring, directly replaced human workers with AI systems, raising concerns about labor rights and job security.
