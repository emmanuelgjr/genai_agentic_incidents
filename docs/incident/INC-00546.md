**AI-Powered Halupedia Generates Fabricated Encyclopedia Entries, Raising Misinformation Risks**  
_2026-05-14 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Halupedia, an online encyclopedia created by Bartłomiej Strama, uses a large language model to generate entirely fabricated articles on demand. While intended as a playful demonstration, the site exposes risks of AI-driven misinformation and data contamination, raising concerns about future harm to knowledge integrity if such content spreads unchecked.
