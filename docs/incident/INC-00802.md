### INC-00802  [↗](/incident/INC-00802.html)

**CISA Excluded from Anthropic's AI Cybersecurity Tool Rollout**  
_2026-04-21 · real-world · Severity: High_

Anthropic's AI-powered cybersecurity tool, Mythos Preview, is being used by several U.S. federal agencies to detect vulnerabilities, but the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA)—a key player in national cybersecurity—has been excluded from access. This exclusion raises concerns about potential increased cybersecurity risks for critical infrastructure.
