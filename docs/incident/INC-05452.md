**Amazon Alters AI Productivity Monitoring After Worker Injuries Linked to Algorithmic Pressure**  
_2021-06-01 · real-world · Severity: High_

Amazon is revising its AI-driven productivity monitoring system, which tracked 'time off task' and penalized workers, after reports linked the system to increased musculoskeletal injuries among warehouse employees. The change follows criticism that the algorithm imposed unsafe work demands, directly harming workers' health.
