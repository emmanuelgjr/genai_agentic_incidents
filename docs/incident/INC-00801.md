**Circus SE Deploys Autonomous AI Robots for Lithuanian Military Supply**  
_2026-04-08 · real-world · Severity: High_

Circus SE has secured a contract to supply its autonomous, AI-powered robots for tactical troop supply to the Lithuanian armed forces. The robots will be integrated into military infrastructure in Vilnius and evaluated during real-world and multinational NATO exercises, raising potential future risks associated with autonomous AI deployment in military contexts.
