**Amazon Ring Considers Adding AI-Powered Facial and License Plate Recognition to Home Cameras**  
_2020-04-21 · real-world · Severity: High_

Amazon's Ring is exploring the addition of AI-driven facial and license plate recognition features to its home security cameras, as revealed by confidential surveys to beta testers. While not yet implemented, these potential features raise significant privacy and surveillance concerns, especially given Ring's extensive law enforcement partnerships and past security issues.
