### INC-00376  [↗](/incident/INC-00376.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfake Ads Target Turkish Pop Star Demet Akalın**  
_2026-04-03 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Turkish singer Demet Akalın was targeted by fraudsters who used AI to create deepfake videos and images, falsely depicting her endorsing a slimming product. The misuse of AI led to reputational harm and public deception, prompting Akalın to publicly denounce the fraudulent ads and warn her followers.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
