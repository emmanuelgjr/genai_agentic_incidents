### INC-07741  [↗](/incident/INC-07741.html)

**Generative AI Increases Cybersecurity Risks and Human Error**  
_2026-05-10 · real-world · Severity: High_

Gartner reports that widespread adoption of generative AI in organizations is expanding cybersecurity risks and increasing the likelihood of human error. Employees often use GenAI tools without proper oversight, inputting sensitive data and creating new opportunities for misuse and sophisticated cyberattacks.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI02`, `ASI03`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.10`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0053`, `AML.T0057`, `AML.T0058`
