**AI-Powered Traffic Cameras Enforce In-Car Violations on Brazilian Highways**  
_2026-05-06 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Brazilian Federal Highway Police (PRF) is testing AI-enabled cameras donated by four companies to detect and fine drivers for 82 types of traffic violations, including not wearing seatbelts and using cell phones while driving. The system uses AI for license plate recognition and behavioral analysis inside vehicles.
