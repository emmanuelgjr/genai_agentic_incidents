### INC-05498  [↗](/incident/INC-05498.html)

**Baidu Receives California Permit for Fully Driverless Vehicle Testing**  
_2021-01-27 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Baidu has been granted a permit by the California DMV to test fully autonomous vehicles without safety drivers on public roads in Sunnyvale. This makes Baidu the sixth company to receive such approval, highlighting the potential risks associated with deploying AI-driven vehicles in real-world environments, though no incidents have occurred yet.
