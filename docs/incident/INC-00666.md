**Autonomous Drone Swarms Transform Modern Warfare, Raising AI Risk Concerns**  
_2026-03-23 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

China and the US have demonstrated AI-coordinated autonomous drone swarms capable of reconnaissance and precision attacks without direct human intervention. These systems, already used in conflicts like Ukraine and the Middle East, highlight significant risks as AI-driven swarms can cause injury, death, and escalate warfare autonomously.
