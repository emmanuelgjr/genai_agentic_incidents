### INC-04509  [↗](/incident/INC-04509.html)

**Campaign Featuring Purported AI-Generated Audio Attributed to Omar al-Bashir Spreads During Sudanese Conflict**  
_2023-08-20 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1087`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1087/)

Beginning in late August 2023, a TikTok channel called The Voice of Sudan circulated purported AI-generated audio claiming to be leaked recordings of former Sudanese president Omar al-Bashir. Experts identified several recordings as voice-cloned fabrications, derived from unrelated broadcasts by a political commentator. The campaign emerged during Sudan&#x27;s civil war and appeared designed to influence political perceptions. TikTok later removed the account for violating synthetic media policies.
