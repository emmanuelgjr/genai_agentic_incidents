**AI-Enabled Drones Decisively Shape Nagorno-Karabakh Conflict**  
_2020-11-17 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

AI-powered drones and loitering munitions, primarily from Turkey and Israel, played a decisive role in Azerbaijan's victory over Armenia in Nagorno-Karabakh. These autonomous or semi-autonomous systems inflicted significant destruction on military assets and personnel, demonstrating the transformative and harmful impact of AI in modern warfare.
