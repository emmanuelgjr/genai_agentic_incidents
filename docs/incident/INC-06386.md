**AutoX and Didi Launch Major Robotaxi Operations in Shanghai**  
_2020-04-10 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Chinese startups AutoX and Didi are launching large-scale robotaxi operations and pilot programs in Shanghai, deploying AI-driven autonomous vehicles for ride-hailing. While these initiatives mark significant AI deployment, no incidents or harm have been reported, but the projects highlight potential future risks associated with autonomous vehicle technology.
