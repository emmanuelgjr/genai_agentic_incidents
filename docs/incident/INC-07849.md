**Widespread AI-Assisted Cheating Among University Students in the US**  
_2026-05-21 · real-world · Severity: High_

A large study of nearly 100,000 students at US universities found that a quarter of daily generative AI users engage in academic cheating. The research highlights significant misuse of AI, raising concerns about academic integrity and prompting calls for reform in assessment methods.
