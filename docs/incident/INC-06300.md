**AI-Powered 'Virtual Wall' Deployed on US-Mexico Border Raises Rights Concerns**  
_2020-07-02 · real-world · Severity: High_

The US government contracted Anduril Industries to deploy hundreds of AI-driven surveillance towers along the US-Mexico border. These towers use cameras, sensors, and AI to detect and classify people, vehicles, and animals, enabling border patrol actions. The system's deployment raises concerns about privacy, potential misidentification, and human rights impacts.
