**Ukraine Deploys AI-Powered Autonomous Drones to Intercept Russian Shahed UAVs**  
_2026-06-08 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Ukrainian defense developers, supported by Brave1, have deployed AI-driven interceptor drones capable of autonomously detecting, tracking, and destroying Russian Shahed drones. The system automates 95% of the interception process, requiring minimal operator input, and has proven effective in combat trials in Kharkiv region, enhancing Ukraine's air defense capabilities.
