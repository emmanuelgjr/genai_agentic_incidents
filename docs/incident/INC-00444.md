### INC-00444  [↗](/incident/INC-00444.html)

**AI-Generated Fake Damage Photos Used in Vinted Refund Scam**  
_2026-05-08 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Fraudsters on Vinted are using AI-powered image editing tools to create realistic fake photos of product damage, enabling them to falsely claim refunds. This scam results in sellers losing both their items and money, as the platform often accepts manipulated images as evidence. The incident is widespread in Italy.

**Attack vector:** `other`  

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`
