**Sony Unveils AI-Equipped Autonomous EV Prototype for Public Road Testing**  
_2020-01-07 · real-world · Severity: High_

Sony announced a prototype electric vehicle equipped with AI-driven autonomous driving technology and over 30 sensors, aiming for public road testing in 2020. While the vehicle features advanced driver assistance, no incidents or harm have occurred, and it is not yet intended for commercial release.
