### INC-01236  [↗](/incident/INC-01236.html)

**Indonesia Implements AI and Social Media Restrictions for Children**  
_2026-03-15 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The Indonesian government has enacted new regulations restricting children's access to AI tools and social media, aiming to prevent potential negative impacts on their development. The measures, supported by legislators, require adult supervision and responsible use of digital technologies by minors in educational settings.
