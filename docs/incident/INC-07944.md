**Uklon Pilots Remote-Driven Vehicle Technology as Step Toward Robotaxi in Ukraine**  
_2026-05-27 · real-world · Severity: High_

Ukrainian ride-hailing company Uklon has begun pilot testing AI-powered remote driving technology at Boryspil Airport. The project, in partnership with Estonian firm Elmo, aims to evaluate remote-controlled vehicles as a precursor to autonomous taxis, with regulatory groundwork underway. No incidents or harm have been reported during testing.
