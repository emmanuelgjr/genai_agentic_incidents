### INC-07944  [↗](/incident/INC-07944.html)

**LaLiga to Implement AI for Referee Evaluation and Assignment**  
_2026-05-21 · real-world · Severity: High_

LaLiga, led by president Javier Tebas, announced plans to implement AI systems next season to objectively score and assign referees in Spanish football. The AI aims to reduce subjectivity in referee evaluations, but no incidents or harm have been reported as the system is still under development.
