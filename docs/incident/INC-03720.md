### INC-03720  [↗](/incident/INC-03720.html)

**Deepfake Taylor Swift fake Le Creuset cookware giveaway scam**  
_2024-01 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

AI-generated adverts using Taylor Swift's likeness appeared to endorse a fake Le Creuset cookware giveaway, harvesting money and personal data from victims clicking through. The campaign ran across Facebook/Instagram before takedowns.

**Affected:** Meta platforms users / Taylor Swift / Le Creuset  
**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM03`, `LLM07`, `LLM09`
