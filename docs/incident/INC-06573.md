### INC-06573  [↗](/incident/INC-06573.html)

**Facebook Uses AI to Remove Millions of Ads Ahead of US Election**  
_2020-10-18 · real-world · Severity: High_

Facebook, using AI tools, removed 2.2 million ads and 120,000 posts from Facebook and Instagram for attempting to disrupt participation in the US presidential election. The AI system detected and deleted fake accounts and disinformation, aiming to prevent election interference and protect the democratic process.

**Attack vector:** `rce`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
