**Unauthorized Access and Security Concerns Surround Anthropic's Mythos AI**  
_2026-04-19 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Anthropic's advanced AI system, Mythos, designed for cybersecurity applications, has faced unauthorized access by hackers, raising significant concerns among regulators and financial institutions about its potential misuse. Investigations are ongoing, and authorities in the US, Europe, and Australia are intensifying oversight to mitigate risks to critical infrastructure.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`
