### INC-01801  [↗](/incident/INC-01801.html)

**Spotify Launches Artist Profile Protection to Combat AI-Generated Music Misattribution**  
_2026-03-21 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Spotify is beta testing 'Artist Profile Protection,' allowing artists to review and approve music releases before they appear on their profiles. This tool addresses harm caused by AI-generated tracks being misattributed to real artists, protecting their identity and preventing fraudulent streams and impersonation on the platform.
