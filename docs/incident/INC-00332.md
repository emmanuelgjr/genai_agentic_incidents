**AI-Enabled Brain-Computer Interfaces Advance, Raising Future Risks**  
_2026-05-07 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Neuralink and startup Sabi are developing AI-powered brain-computer interfaces (BCIs) that translate neural activity into digital commands, with Neuralink using invasive implants and Sabi offering non-invasive EEG-based wearables. While no harm has occurred, these technologies pose plausible future risks related to privacy, safety, and misuse.
