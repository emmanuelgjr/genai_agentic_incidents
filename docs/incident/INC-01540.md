### INC-01540  [↗](/incident/INC-01540.html)

**North Korean Hackers Use AI-Enhanced Social Engineering to Steal $100K from Zerion**  
_2026-04-15 · real-world · Severity: High_

North Korean-affiliated hackers used AI-powered social engineering tactics to compromise Zerion employees, stealing approximately $100,000 from the company's internal crypto wallets. The attack exploited employee credentials and private keys, but did not affect user funds or core infrastructure. Zerion has since strengthened its security measures.
