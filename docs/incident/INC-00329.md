**AI-Driven Workplace Surveillance Leads to Employee Dismissals and Privacy Concerns in China**  
_2026-05-07 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Chinese companies are increasingly using AI-powered surveillance tools, such as intelligent cameras and monitoring software, to track employee behavior. These systems have led to employee dismissals and raised significant concerns over privacy violations and labor rights, sparking legal disputes and public debate about the ethical boundaries of digital workplace monitoring.
