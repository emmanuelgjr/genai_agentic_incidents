### INC-06856  [↗](/incident/INC-06856.html)

**Potential Accessibility Risks in Self-Driving Taxi Deployment**  
_2020-06-22 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Multiple articles warn that autonomous vehicle (self-driving taxi) AI systems could reduce transportation access for people with disabilities or different needs if not designed inclusively. Without accessible features, these systems risk excluding vulnerable groups, highlighting the need for proactive, accessible design to prevent future harm.

**Attack vector:** `other`
