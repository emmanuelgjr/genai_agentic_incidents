**OpenAI deleted training datasets believed to contain copyrighted books**  
_2024-05 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Court filings in the Authors Guild v. OpenAI litigation alleged OpenAI deleted training datasets believed to contain large-scale copyrighted books (books1 and books2) before they could be examined, raising spoliation and supply-chain transparency concerns.

**Affected:** Authors / OpenAI
