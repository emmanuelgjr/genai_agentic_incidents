**US Seeks Resolution to National Security Concerns Over TikTok's AI Platform**  
_2020-11-12 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The US Treasury is seeking solutions to national security concerns arising from ByteDance's 2017 acquisition of Musical.ly, later merged with TikTok, an AI-driven social media app. ByteDance is challenging a US order to divest TikTok, while negotiations continue to address risks related to the platform's AI and data use.
