### INC-06115  [↗](/incident/INC-06115.html)

**TikTok Sued for Unauthorized Use of Actress's Voice in AI Text-to-Speech Feature**  
_2021-05-11 · real-world · Severity: High_

Voice actress Bev Standing has sued TikTok and its parent company ByteDance for using her voice without consent in the platform's AI-powered text-to-speech feature. The unauthorized use has led to reputational and economic harm, as her voice is now widely used, including in offensive contexts, violating her intellectual property rights.

**Attack vector:** `other`
