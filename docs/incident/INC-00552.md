**AI-Powered Robot Police Deployed in Chinese Cities Raise Privacy Concerns**  
_2026-04-17 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI-equipped robot police officers have been deployed in several Chinese cities for traffic control and law enforcement, collecting large amounts of data. While authorities promote their efficiency, public concerns have emerged online about potential personal information leaks and privacy violations due to the robots' extensive data collection capabilities.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`
