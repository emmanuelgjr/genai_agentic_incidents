**EDGE Unveils UAE-Made AI-Enabled Loitering Munitions and Autonomous Military Drones**  
_2021-02-21 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

EDGE, a UAE defense technology group, unveiled its first family of AI-enabled loitering munitions and autonomous drones at IDEX 2021. These systems, designed for military use with autonomous targeting and strike capabilities, present significant future risks of harm due to their AI-driven lethal functions, though no incidents have yet occurred.
