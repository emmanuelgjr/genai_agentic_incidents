**Secret Facial Recognition System Used to Identify and Arrest Lafayette Square Protester**  
_2020-10-30 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

U.S. law enforcement used a previously undisclosed facial recognition AI system to identify and arrest a protester accused of assault during the 2020 Lafayette Square protests. The system's secretive use raised concerns over privacy, transparency, and potential civil rights violations, as defendants were often unaware of its deployment.
