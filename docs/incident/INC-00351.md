**AI-Enabled Military Drones Cause Civilian Harm and Proliferate Through Strategic Partnerships in Ukraine**  
_2026-03-31 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

AI-powered military drones have been widely used in the Ukraine conflict, causing civilian casualties and property damage. Japanese company Terra Drone invested in Ukraine's Amazing Drones to develop and export AI-enabled interceptor drones, accelerating their deployment and global spread. These actions highlight the direct and indirect harm caused by AI systems in warfare.
