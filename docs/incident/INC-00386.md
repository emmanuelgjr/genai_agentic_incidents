### INC-00386  [↗](/incident/INC-00386.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfake Scam Impersonates Johor Official on TikTok**  
_2026-02-24 · real-world · Severity: High_

Multiple TikTok accounts used AI-generated images and voice to impersonate Johor state executive councillor Khairin-Nisa Ismail, promoting a fake aid scheme to steal personal data. Authorities shut down 12 accounts and are investigating, highlighting the misuse of AI for fraud and privacy violations in Malaysia.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
