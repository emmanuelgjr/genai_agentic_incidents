### INC-06388  [↗](/incident/INC-06388.html)

**Babylon Health AI Chatbot Faces Criticism Over Unsafe Medical Advice and Data Privacy Breach**  
_2020-02-26 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Babylon Health's AI triage chatbot has been criticized by Dr. David Watkins for providing unsafe medical advice, potentially endangering patient health. In response, Babylon publicly attacked Watkins and posted his data online, raising additional concerns about privacy and the company's handling of safety issues.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`  

**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-4.1`, `MEASURE-2.10`
