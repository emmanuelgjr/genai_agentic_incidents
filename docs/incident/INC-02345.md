### INC-02345  [↗](/incident/INC-02345.html)

**Australian Analyst Allegedly Targeted by Scam Using Purportedly Sophisticated AI-Generated Corporate Materials**  
_2025-04-27 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1047`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1047/)

A report by ABC News Australia describes how scammers allegedly used AI tools to create detailed fake websites for two fabricated companies, APPC Capital Singapore and Thackeray Mines and Minerals Inc., as part of an investment scam. A pseudonymous Australian fraud analyst (&quot;Leon&quot;) was reportedly targeted. Experts cited in the article warn that AI lowers the barrier for generating convincing scams that can deceive even experienced analysts.
