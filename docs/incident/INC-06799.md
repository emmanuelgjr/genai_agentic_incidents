### INC-06799  [↗](/incident/INC-06799.html)

**Moroccan Journalist Targeted by AI-Driven Spyware Pegasus**  
_2020-06-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

Amnesty International revealed that Moroccan journalist Omar Radi was repeatedly targeted and surveilled by the Moroccan government using NSO Group's AI-powered Pegasus spyware. The attacks, which violated Radi's privacy and freedom of expression, continued even after NSO pledged to respect human rights, highlighting the direct harm caused by AI surveillance technology.
