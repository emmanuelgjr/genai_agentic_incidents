### INC-04291  [↗](/incident/INC-04291.html)

**Thomson Reuters Fraud Detect 'incorrectly' identifies fraud against welfare claimants**  
_2024 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

An AI-driven fraud-detection tool licensed from Thomson Reuters and used by US state agencies was alleged to wrongly flag welfare applicants and recipients as fraudulent, leading to benefit denials and harms based on opaque scoring.

**Affected:** US welfare recipients  
**Attack vector:** `adversarial-input`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM06`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.1`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048`, `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0053`, `AML.T0058`
