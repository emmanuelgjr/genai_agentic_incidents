**AI-Powered License Plate Readers at Home Depot and Lowe's Spark Privacy Lawsuit**  
_2026-05-11 · real-world · Severity: High_

Home Depot and Lowe's have deployed AI-powered license plate readers in parking lots across several U.S. states to combat theft. The technology captures data on all vehicles, raising privacy concerns and leading to a class action lawsuit alleging covert surveillance and unauthorized data sharing with law enforcement.
