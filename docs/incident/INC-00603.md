### INC-00603  [↗](/incident/INC-00603.html)

**Anthropic Faces AI Model Theft by Chinese Firms and Major Source Code Leak**  
_2026-03-31 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

US AI company Anthropic accused Chinese firms of illegally extracting capabilities from its Claude model using mass account networks, violating terms and raising security concerns. Separately, Anthropic accidentally leaked 500,000 lines of Claude Code's source code due to a packaging error, exposing proprietary technology and internal plans.
