**Symbiosis Engineering Students Suspended for Wearing AI-Enabled Smart Glasses During Exam**  
_2026-05-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

Two engineering students at Symbiosis Institute of Technology, Pune, were caught wearing Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses, which have AI capabilities, during an exam. Although no evidence of cheating was found, the students were suspended and the case referred to the university's unfair means committee for investigation.
