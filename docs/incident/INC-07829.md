### INC-07829  [↗](/incident/INC-07829.html)

**SPARC AI and Rate Manufacturing Partner to Deploy AI-Enabled GPS-Denied Military Drones**  
_2026-05-20 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

SPARC AI and Rate Manufacturing have partnered to integrate SPARC AI's Overwatch AI platform into Rate's Model-F military drones, enabling accurate positioning and targeting in GPS-denied environments. The deployment of this AI technology in defense drones presents plausible future risks of harm in contested military settings, though no incidents have yet occurred.

**Attack vector:** `other`
