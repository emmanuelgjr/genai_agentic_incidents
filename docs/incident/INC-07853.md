### INC-07853  [↗](/incident/INC-07853.html)

**Puebla Launches AI-Powered Surveillance Program for Crime Prevention**  
_2026-05-21 · real-world · Severity: High_

The municipal government of Puebla, led by Mayor Pepe Chedraui, has launched the Puebla 360 program, deploying AI-enabled video surveillance across key city areas to prevent crime. The system uses real-time monitoring and automated image analysis to detect suspicious activities, aiming to shift from reactive to preventive security measures.
