### INC-01058  [↗](/incident/INC-01058.html)

**Gartner Warns of AI Misconfiguration Risks to Critical Infrastructure by 2028**  
_2026-03-16 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Gartner forecasts that by 2028, misconfigured AI in cyber-physical systems could disrupt critical infrastructure in at least one G20 country. The risk stems from operational errors during AI implementation or updates, not cyberattacks, highlighting the need for robust security and oversight as AI adoption accelerates.

**Attack vector:** `other`
