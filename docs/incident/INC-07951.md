**CNN Sues Perplexity AI for Copyright Infringement Over News Content Scraping**  
_2026-05-28 · real-world · Severity: High_

CNN has filed a lawsuit against AI startup Perplexity in New York, alleging the company unlawfully scraped, copied, and distributed CNN's copyrighted news content using its AI-powered platform without authorization or compensation, violating intellectual property rights and bypassing paywalls. The case highlights growing legal tensions between media organizations and AI firms.
