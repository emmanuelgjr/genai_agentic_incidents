**AI and Drones Heighten Global Nuclear Terrorism Threat**  
_2026-05-18 · real-world · Severity: High_

UN officials and experts warn that the proliferation of AI and militarized drones has raised the risk of nuclear terrorism to unprecedented levels. While no nuclear terrorist attack has occurred, the potential misuse of AI-enabled technologies by terrorist groups poses a credible, high-impact global threat, prompting urgent calls for enhanced governance.
