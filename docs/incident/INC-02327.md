### INC-02327  [↗](/incident/INC-02327.html)

**Anysphere AI Support Bot for Cursor Reportedly Invents Login Policy, Leading to Subscription Cancellations**  
_2025-04-19 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1039`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1039/)

In April 2025, users of Cursor, an AI-powered coding assistant developed by Anysphere, reported being logged out unexpectedly. An AI-powered support bot, &quot;Sam,&quot; allegedly responded with an invented login policy to justify the behavior. The hallucinated policy was not based on any real company change. The incident reportedly led to subscription cancellations.
