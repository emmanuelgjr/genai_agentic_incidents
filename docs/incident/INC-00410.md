### INC-00410  [↗](/incident/INC-00410.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfake Videos Used to Scam French Investors**  
_2026-04-27 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Fraudsters used AI-generated deepfake videos to impersonate Banque de France officials, including Governor François Villeroy de Galhau, to promote fraudulent investments and deceive both individuals and companies. French authorities, including the Banque de France and ACPR, issued warnings about this sophisticated AI-enabled scam targeting the public.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
