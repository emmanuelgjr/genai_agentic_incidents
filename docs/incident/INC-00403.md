### INC-00403  [↗](/incident/INC-00403.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfake Videos Target Indian Army and Officials Amid Disinformation Campaign**  
_2026-03-16 · real-world · Severity: High_

Pakistani-linked social media accounts circulated AI-generated deepfake videos falsely depicting former Indian Army Chief Manoj Pande and other officials making controversial statements. Indian government fact-checkers exposed the videos, warning of ongoing disinformation campaigns undermining public trust in national institutions during regional tensions.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
