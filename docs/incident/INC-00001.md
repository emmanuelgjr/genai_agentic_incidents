### INC-00001  [↗](/incident/INC-00001.html)

**11 Arrested for Deepfake AI Scam Impersonating Ghana's Former President**  
_2026-05-07 · real-world · Severity: High_

Ghanaian police arrested 11 suspects, including Nigerian nationals, for using AI-generated deepfake videos to impersonate former President John Dramani Mahama. The suspects allegedly used the videos in online scams to solicit money and sensitive information, causing harm through deception and fraud. Operations occurred in the Volta Region in May 2026.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
