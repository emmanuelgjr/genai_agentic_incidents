### INC-03531  [↗](/incident/INC-03531.html)

**Air Canada found liable for chatbot's incorrect bereavement-fare advice**  
_2024-02 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A British Columbia tribunal ruled Air Canada liable for misleading information given by its website chatbot about bereavement fare refunds, rejecting the airline's argument that the chatbot was a 'separate legal entity'. The case became a landmark for AI-hallucination corporate liability.

**Affected:** Air Canada customers  
**Attack vector:** `hallucination`
