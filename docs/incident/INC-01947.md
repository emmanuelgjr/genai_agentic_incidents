**UK Plans AI-Powered Mass Location Tracking for Future Pandemics**  
_2026-03-30 · real-world · Severity: High_

The UK government has announced a £1 billion pandemic strategy to develop an AI-driven contact tracing system using real-time location data from major tech companies. Managed by the UK Health Security Agency, the system raises concerns about mass surveillance and privacy risks, though it is not yet deployed.
