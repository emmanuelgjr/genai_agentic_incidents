**New Zealand and Wisk Launch Autonomous Air Taxi Trial with Cora**  
_2020-02-04 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Wisk, backed by Boeing and Kitty Hawk, has partnered with the New Zealand government to conduct a passenger transport trial of its AI-powered, self-flying air taxi, Cora, in Canterbury. While no incidents have occurred, the trial introduces potential future risks associated with autonomous flight operations.
