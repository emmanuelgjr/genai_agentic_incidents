### INC-00248  [↗](/incident/INC-00248.html)

**AI Uncovers Massive Tax Evasion in Rajasthan Land Deals**  
_2026-03-09 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

India's Income Tax Department, in collaboration with IIT Delhi, used AI and satellite imaging to detect large-scale tax evasion in agricultural land transactions around Jaipur, Rajasthan. The AI system identified approximately 7,000 crore INR in evaded taxes, leading to notices being sent to about 900 individuals.
