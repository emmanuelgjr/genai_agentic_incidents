### INC-00521  [↗](/incident/INC-00521.html)

**AI-Generated WeChat IDs Lead to Privacy Breaches and Unsolicited Contacts**  
_2026-02-28 · real-world · Severity: High_

Multiple AI platforms, including Doubao and others, generated 'virtual' WeChat IDs that sometimes matched real users, resulting in unsolicited friend requests and privacy violations. Users reported distress and privacy invasion, raising concerns about AI data sourcing and the risk of personal information exposure in China.

**Attack vector:** `other`
