### INC-02340  [↗](/incident/INC-02340.html)

**Attacker Reportedly Bypasses AI Safety Filters to Obtain Guidance for Non-Fatal Hammer Assault in Denmark**  
_2025-02-05 · real-world · Severity: Critical_
AIID: [`#1296`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1296/)

A 22-year-old man in Ringsted, Denmark allegedly used an AI chatbot to research how to injure his former father-in-law &quot;as much as possible&quot; without killing him, reportedly bypassing safety guardrails by posing as an author. He then allegedly attacked the victim on with a rubber hammer, striking him twice in the head. The court reportedly found him guilty of aggravated assault, noting the AI-assisted planning as a central element of the case.
