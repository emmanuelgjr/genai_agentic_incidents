**Russian AI-Driven Cyberattacks Escalate Against Europe**  
_2026-04-21 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The Dutch military intelligence agency (MIVD) warns that Russia is increasingly using AI to automate and accelerate cyberattacks against European institutions and organizations. AI enables higher attack frequency and scale, with new models like Anthropic's Mythos raising concerns about advanced exploitation of software vulnerabilities. Ongoing attacks have already impacted critical infrastructure across Europe.
