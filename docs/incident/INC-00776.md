### INC-00776  [↗](/incident/INC-00776.html)

**China's First AI Model Infringement Case: Court Rules on Unfair Competition**  
_2026-04-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

In Beijing, a court ruled that a company unlawfully used another's AI model structure and parameters for a comic-style image transformation feature, constituting unfair competition and economic harm. This landmark case is China's first to protect AI model structures and parameters under anti-unfair competition law, setting a precedent for future AI-related disputes.
