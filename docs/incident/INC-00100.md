### INC-00100  [↗](/incident/INC-00100.html)

**AI Chatbot Interaction Leads to Discovery of Child Sexual Abuse in Paraná**  
_2026-04-30 · real-world · Severity: High_

In São José dos Pinhais, Paraná, Brazil, a 12-year-old girl used an AI chatbot to discuss her sexual abuse, prompting her family to discover the crime. The AI's response and the chat history led to police involvement and the arrest of the suspect, highlighting the AI system's pivotal role in uncovering the abuse.
