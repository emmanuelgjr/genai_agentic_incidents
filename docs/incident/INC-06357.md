### INC-06357  [↗](/incident/INC-06357.html)

**Amnesty International Raises Human Rights Concerns Over Argentina's Cuidar App**  
_2020-09-18 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Amnesty International expressed concerns about Argentina's Cuidar app, highlighting risks to privacy and data protection due to lack of transparency in handling sensitive health data. The organization warned of potential human rights violations and urged the government to implement safeguards, though no actual harm has been reported.
