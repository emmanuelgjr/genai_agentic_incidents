### INC-06474  [↗](/incident/INC-06474.html)

**Coded Bias Documentary Exposes Harm from Racially Biased AI Systems**  
_2020-11-11 · real-world · Severity: High_

The documentary 'Coded Bias' highlights real-world harms caused by racially biased AI systems, particularly facial recognition technologies that misidentify people of color and women. These biases have led to police harassment, discrimination, and systemic inequality, prompting activists and researchers to call for greater accountability and oversight of AI.
