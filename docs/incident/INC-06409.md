**Brazilian Police Deploy Live Facial Recognition at Carnival, Raising Privacy Concerns**  
_2020-02-24 · real-world · Severity: High_

Brazilian police used live facial recognition AI during Carnival, adding millions of faces to their database to identify wanted individuals in crowds. This large-scale surveillance raises significant privacy and human rights concerns, highlighting the risks of AI-driven mass data collection and potential wrongful identification.
