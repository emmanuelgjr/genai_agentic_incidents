### INC-06758  [↗](/incident/INC-06758.html)

**Legal Challenge Against AI-Powered Video Surveillance in Marseille**  
_2020-01-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

Two rights groups, La Quadrature du Net and the Ligue des droits de l'Homme, have filed a legal challenge against Marseille's AI-based video surveillance system, citing risks of mass surveillance and privacy violations. The system, currently in testing, uses algorithms for biometric analysis and automated anomaly detection across city cameras.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`  

**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-4.1`, `MEASURE-2.10`
