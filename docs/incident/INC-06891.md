**Russia Develops Global AI-Enabled Drone Network, Raising Fears of Autonomous Weapons Risks**  
_2020-06-10 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Russia's Central Design Bureau for Marine Engineering is developing a global network of AI-enabled autonomous underwater, surface, and air drones. While officially intended for research and communication, experts warn the system could be weaponized, raising concerns about loss of human control, potential lethal harm, and geopolitical instability.
