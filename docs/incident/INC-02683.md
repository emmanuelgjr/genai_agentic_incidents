### INC-02683  [↗](/incident/INC-02683.html)

**Hackers exploit prompt injection to corrupt Google Gemini's memory**  
_2025-02-11 · research-demonstrated · Severity: High_

Security researcher Johann Rehberger demonstrated a delayed-tool-invocation prompt-injection technique that permanently corrupts Google Gemini's long-term user memory, persisting attacker-supplied facts across future sessions.

**Affected:** Google Gemini users  
**Attack vector:** `memory-poisoning`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM01`, `LLM04`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI06`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-4.2`, `MEASURE-2.7`
