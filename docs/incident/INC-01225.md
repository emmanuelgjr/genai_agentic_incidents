**India Tests AI-Powered Swarm Interceptor for Drone Defence**  
_2026-03-05 · real-world · Severity: High_

Flying Wedge Defence & Aerospace has successfully tested FWD YAMA, India's first AI-driven autonomous swarm interceptor, designed to counter drone threats in military operations. The system uses artificial intelligence for autonomous targeting and interception, raising future risks associated with autonomous weapon deployment, though no harm has yet occurred.
