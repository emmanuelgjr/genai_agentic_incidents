### INC-05448  [↗](/incident/INC-05448.html)

**Algorithmic Pricing on Food Delivery App Harms Members with Higher Fees**  
_2021-01-29 · real-world · Severity: High_

A food delivery platform's AI-driven pricing algorithm charged members up to three times more for delivery than non-members, causing financial harm and sparking public outcry. The incident highlights consumer rights violations and privacy concerns from algorithmic profiling and data misuse by apps, prompting regulatory scrutiny in China.
