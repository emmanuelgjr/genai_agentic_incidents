**YouTube Sued Over Alleged AI-Driven Racial Discrimination Against Black Creators**  
_2020-06-19 · real-world · Severity: High_

A federal lawsuit accuses YouTube and its parent companies of using AI-powered profiling and filtering tools that allegedly discriminate against Black video creators by restricting, censoring, and demonetizing their content based on race, resulting in violations of rights and systematic exclusion from monetization opportunities.
