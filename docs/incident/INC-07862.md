**Indian Railways Plans AI Surveillance and Drones for Enhanced Passenger Safety**  
_2026-05-22 · real-world · Severity: High_

Indian Railways is set to deploy AI-powered surveillance systems, drones, and expanded CCTV networks to improve passenger safety and security across stations and trains. The initiative aims to enable real-time monitoring and faster response to security threats, but raises potential concerns about privacy and misuse of AI technologies.
