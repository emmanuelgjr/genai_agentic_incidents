### INC-00962  [↗](/incident/INC-00962.html)

**EU Legal Deadlock Reduces AI Detection of Child Abuse Online**  
_2026-03-17 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A deadlock among EU institutions over legal exemptions for AI-powered detection of child sexual abuse material has led to the suspension of automated monitoring by major platforms. This has already caused a 50% drop in detection and reporting, increasing risks to children and communities across the EU.
