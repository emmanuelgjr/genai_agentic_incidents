**Suspect Uses Taipei Metro AI Chatbot to Issue Bomb and Murder Threats, Causing Public Panic**  
_2026-05-05 · real-world · Severity: High_

A 28-year-old man repeatedly used the Taipei Metro's AI customer service system to send bomb and murder threats, causing public fear and disrupting metro operations. Despite not completing identity verification, his messages triggered police action. He was arrested in Changhua and detained for public intimidation. The incident highlights misuse of AI systems for criminal threats.
