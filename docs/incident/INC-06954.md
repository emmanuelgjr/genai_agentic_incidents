### INC-06954  [↗](/incident/INC-06954.html)

**Tesla App Glitch Allows Unauthorized Remote Control of Vehicles in China**  
_2020-09-16 · real-world · Severity: High_

Multiple Tesla owners in China reported a recurring app malfunction that allowed users to remotely control vehicles not their own, due to incorrect vehicle identification input during maintenance. The AI-enabled system's failure posed significant safety and privacy risks, with Tesla attributing the issue to staff error but not fully resolving underlying vulnerabilities.
