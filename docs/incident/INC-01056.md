**Fujitsu Launches AI Decision Support Program for Japanese Defense**  
_2026-03-10 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Fujitsu, commissioned by Japan's Acquisition, Technology & Logistics Agency, has launched a program to co-develop multi-AI agent systems for military decision support. The initiative seeks startup partners to accelerate AI technologies that could enhance command decisions, information analysis, and reduce personnel burden, raising potential future risks from military AI deployment.
