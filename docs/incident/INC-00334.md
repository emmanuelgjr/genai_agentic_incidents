**AI-Enabled Cyberattacks Surge, Slashing Breakout Times to Under 30 Minutes**  
_2026-02-24 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

CrowdStrike's 2026 Global Threat Report reveals an 89% surge in AI-enabled cyberattacks, with criminals using generative AI tools to automate and accelerate breaches. Average breakout time dropped to 29 minutes in 2025, with some attacks taking just seconds, leading to rapid data theft and compromised enterprise systems.

**Attack vector:** `prompt-injection`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM01`, `LLM02`, `LLM06`, `LLM09`
