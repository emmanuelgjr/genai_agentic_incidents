### INC-00446  [↗](/incident/INC-00446.html)

**AI-Generated Fake IDs Enable Exam Fraud in Turkey, 14 Arrested**  
_2026-04-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

In Tokat, Turkey, a criminal network used AI to merge faces and create fake ID photos, enabling imposters to take driving license exams for others. Authorities uncovered the scheme after a detailed investigation, leading to the arrest and detention of 14 individuals involved in the AI-driven identity and exam fraud.
