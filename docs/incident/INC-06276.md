### INC-06276  [↗](/incident/INC-06276.html)

**AI-Driven Social Credit Scores Deny Financial Services Globally**  
_2020-06-24 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

AI systems analyzing social media data to generate social credit scores are causing harm worldwide, with 18% of consumers denied loans or mortgages due to these automated assessments. The lack of transparency and regulation around such AI-driven scoring systems raises concerns about privacy, rights violations, and economic exclusion.

**Attack vector:** `other`
