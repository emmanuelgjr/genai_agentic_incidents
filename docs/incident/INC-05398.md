### INC-05398  [↗](/incident/INC-05398.html)

**AI Voice Profiling Raises Privacy and Discrimination Risks in Marketing**  
_2021-04-28 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

AI-powered voice profiling systems are increasingly used by companies to analyze callers' speech and route them to agents likely to influence their purchasing decisions. Experts warn this technology poses significant risks of privacy invasion, discrimination, and unfair profiling, highlighting the urgent need for regulation before harms materialize.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`  

**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-4.1`, `MEASURE-2.10`
