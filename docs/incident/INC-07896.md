### INC-07896  [↗](/incident/INC-07896.html)

**Hyundai Recalls 421,000 Vehicles Due to AI Braking System Malfunction**  
_2026-05-25 · real-world · Severity: High_

Hyundai is recalling over 421,000 2025-2026 Santa Cruz and Tucson models in the US after a software defect in the AI-powered collision-avoidance system caused unexpected braking. The malfunction led to 376 complaints, four crashes, and four injuries. Owners will receive a free software update to fix the issue.
