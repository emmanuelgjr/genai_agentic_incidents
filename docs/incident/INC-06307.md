### INC-06307  [↗](/incident/INC-06307.html)

**AI-Powered Facial Recognition Used for Quarantine Enforcement in Moscow**  
_2020-03-24 · real-world · Severity: High_

Moscow authorities deployed a vast AI-driven facial recognition camera network to monitor and enforce COVID-19 quarantine compliance, identifying and penalizing hundreds of violators. The system, installed despite legal and public protests, raised significant concerns over privacy violations and government overreach, highlighting the societal risks of AI surveillance.
