### INC-00525  [↗](/incident/INC-00525.html)

**AI-Manipulated Audio Recordings Target Slovenian Political Party**  
_2026-03-09 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

AI-generated and manipulated audio recordings of private conversations involving members of Slovenia's Svoboda party have circulated online, leading to reputational harm and political manipulation. The party claims these deepfake recordings are part of a campaign to undermine their credibility and distract from government achievements.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
