**Ohio Plans Major Expansion of Facial Recognition Database with Updated License Photos**  
_2020-02-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

Ohio officials plan to expand the state's AI-powered facial recognition system by adding millions of updated driver's license photos. While a task force found no current misuse, concerns remain about privacy and potential future risks, prompting recommendations for safeguards and guidelines to prevent abuse.
