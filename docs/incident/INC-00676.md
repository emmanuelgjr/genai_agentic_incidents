### INC-00676  [↗](/incident/INC-00676.html)

**Bank of England Stress-Tests AI Risks to UK Financial Stability**  
_2026-04-16 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The Bank of England, responding to parliamentary concerns, is conducting scenario analyses and stress tests to assess potential risks from AI in financial markets, such as herding behavior and cybersecurity threats. No harm has occurred yet, but regulators are proactively addressing plausible future AI-related financial system risks in the UK.
