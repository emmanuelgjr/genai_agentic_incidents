**Purported AI-Generated War Footage Reportedly Circulated Widely Online During the Opening Phase of the War in Iran**  
_2026-02-28 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Reports said that, in the early days of the war in Iran, purported AI-generated fakes showing nonexistent wartime scenes reached millions of viewers online.
