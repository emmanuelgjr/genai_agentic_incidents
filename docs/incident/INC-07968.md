### INC-07968  [↗](/incident/INC-07968.html)

**KAI and South Korea's Strategic Command Partner on AI-Driven Military Systems**  
_2026-05-29 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Korea Aerospace Industries (KAI) and South Korea's Strategic Command signed an agreement to jointly develop advanced military technologies, including AI-based pilots, unmanned systems, and networked combat platforms. While no harm has occurred, the collaboration highlights potential future risks associated with deploying AI in military applications.
