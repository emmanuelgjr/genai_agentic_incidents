### INC-00237  [↗](/incident/INC-00237.html)

**AI Tools Enable Cloning and Privatization of Open-Source Software, Raising IP Concerns**  
_2026-03-31 · real-world · Severity: High_

Researchers Dylan Ayrey and Mike Nolan demonstrated an AI tool, malus.sh, that can rapidly clone open-source software and generate proprietary versions, potentially bypassing copyright and licensing requirements. This use of AI threatens intellectual property rights and the sustainability of the open-source community.
