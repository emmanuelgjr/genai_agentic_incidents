### INC-01578  [↗](/incident/INC-01578.html)

**OpenAI Issues Urgent Security Update for Mac Apps After Supply Chain Attack**  
_2026-04-11 · real-world · Severity: High_

OpenAI detected a security vulnerability in its Mac applications due to a compromised external development tool, Axios, linked to a broader software supply chain attack. While no user data or systems were breached, OpenAI urged users to update their apps to prevent risks from counterfeit applications.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM03`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`, `ASI04`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `GOVERN-6.1`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.10`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0010`, `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0057`, `AML.T0058`
