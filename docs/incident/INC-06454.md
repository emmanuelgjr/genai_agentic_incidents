### INC-06454  [↗](/incident/INC-06454.html)

**Civil Society Groups Oppose Government Facial Recognition Surveillance Plans in Germany**  
_2020-01-09 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A coalition of digital rights organizations, including the Chaos Computer Club and Digitalcourage, is protesting German government plans to deploy automated facial recognition at train stations and airports. They warn the AI system poses significant risks of privacy violations, discrimination, and mass surveillance, and are calling for a nationwide ban.
