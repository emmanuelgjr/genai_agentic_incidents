**Russia Announces Near-Deployment of AI-Enabled Hypersonic and Nuclear Drone Weapons**  
_2020-07-26 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Russian President Vladimir Putin announced that the Russian Navy will soon be equipped with advanced hypersonic missiles and Poseidon underwater nuclear drones, both incorporating AI-enabled technologies. These autonomous or semi-autonomous weapons, currently in final testing, pose significant future risks of large-scale harm if deployed.
