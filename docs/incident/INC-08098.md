### INC-08098  [↗](/incident/INC-08098.html)

**AI-Driven Scam Detection and Fraudulent Activity Prevention in Japan and the US**  
_2026-06-04 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Google introduced an AI-powered scam call detection feature for Android devices in Japan to combat deepfake voice fraud, warning users of suspicious calls. Simultaneously, AI is making fraud schemes, including cryptocurrency and romance scams, more sophisticated, leading to significant financial losses in the US and Japan.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
