**AI-Driven Social Media Algorithms Harm U.S. Students' Mental Health and Education**  
_2026-06-04 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Internal documents reveal that Meta, Snap, TikTok, and YouTube used AI-powered recommendation and notification systems to engage students during school hours, despite internal warnings. These practices contributed to mental health issues and academic disruption, prompting over 1,400 U.S. school districts to file lawsuits against the companies.
