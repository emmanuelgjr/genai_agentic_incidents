### INC-06538  [↗](/incident/INC-06538.html)

**Expert Predicts AI Judges Will Replace Humans in Courts by 2070**  
_2020-10-24 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

AI expert Terence Mauri predicts that by 2070, robotic judges capable of detecting deception through body language and speech analysis will replace human judges in courts. These AI systems, equipped with advanced sensors and cameras, could significantly impact judicial processes, raising concerns about future risks and fairness in legal decisions.
