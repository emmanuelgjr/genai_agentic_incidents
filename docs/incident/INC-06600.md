**Facial Recognition AI Sparks Human Rights and Civil Liberties Backlash**  
_2020-07-05 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI-powered facial recognition systems have led to privacy violations, wrongful arrests, and disproportionate harm to marginalized groups. Activists and rights groups have challenged their use in Russia and the US, citing human rights abuses and racial bias, prompting calls for bans and regulatory action against law enforcement surveillance.
