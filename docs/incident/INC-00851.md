### INC-00851  [↗](/incident/INC-00851.html)

**Concerns Over AI-Driven Content Moderation in Hungarian Elections**  
_2026-03-19 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Hungarian official Orbán Balázs alleges the European Commission is using AI-enabled content filtering and fact-checking tools under the Digital Services Act (DSA), in cooperation with major tech platforms like Meta, to influence Hungary's election campaign. No direct harm is confirmed, but potential risks to democratic processes are raised.
