**DARPA Seeks Funding for AI-Enabled Unmanned 'Flying Gun' Weapon System**  
_2020-02-18 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

DARPA is developing an AI-enabled unmanned weapon system, dubbed 'Gunslinger,' capable of autonomously engaging multiple airborne and ground targets. The Pentagon has requested $13 million for this project, raising concerns about potential future harm from autonomous targeting and engagement in military operations, though no incidents have yet occurred.
