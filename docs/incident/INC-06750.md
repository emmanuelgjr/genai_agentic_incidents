**Karsan Develops Level-4 Autonomous Electric Bus Prototype**  
_2020-04-19 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Karsan, in partnership with ADASTEC, is developing a Level-4 autonomous driving system for its Atak Electric bus. The prototype is set for completion in August, with testing and validation ongoing. No incidents or harm have occurred, but future deployment of the AI system presents potential risks.
