**Bayraktar AKINCI TİHA: AI-Enabled Armed Drone Successfully Completes Test Flights**  
_2020-08-13 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Baykar's Bayraktar AKINCI TİHA, an AI-powered armed drone with autonomous decision-making and advanced sensor capabilities, successfully completed its initial test flights. While no harm has occurred, the system's autonomous lethal capabilities present credible future risks of injury or rights violations if misused or malfunctioning.

**Attack vector:** `other`  

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI10`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048`
