### INC-02318  [↗](/incident/INC-02318.html)

**Anthropic Claude AI Agent Reportedly Caused Financial Losses While Operating Office Vending Machine at Wall Street Journal Headquarters**  
_2025-12-18 · real-world · Severity: Medium_
AIID: [`#1313`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1313/)

An AI agent reportedly based on Anthropic&#x27;s Claude model was deployed to operate an office vending machine at The Wall Street Journal, including purchasing inventory, setting prices, and managing sales. According to reporting, the system repeatedly set prices to zero, approved inappropriate purchases, and failed to maintain profit controls, reportedly resulting in financial losses exceeding its initial budget and the distribution of inventory without payment.
