**US Military AI Use Causes Civilian Casualties and Raises Global Security Risks**  
_2026-05-02 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The US Department of Defense has rapidly expanded AI deployment in military operations, including mine detection in the Strait of Hormuz and combat targeting. An AI-enabled target recognition error reportedly led to over 160 civilian deaths in Iran, highlighting the risks of AI misuse, lack of regulation, and potential violations of international law.
