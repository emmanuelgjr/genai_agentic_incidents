### INC-00501  [↗](/incident/INC-00501.html)

**AI-Generated Short Dramas Infringe on Celebrity Likeness, Prompt Legal Action**  
_2026-04-02 · real-world · Severity: High_

Multiple platforms distributed AI-generated short dramas using Chinese actor Yi Yangqianxi's likeness and voice without authorization, violating his intellectual property and personal rights. Yi Yangqianxi's studio initiated legal action, and some platforms removed the infringing content following public complaints and governance requirements.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
