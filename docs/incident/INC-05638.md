### INC-05638  [↗](/incident/INC-05638.html)

**Facial Recognition Access in Chinese Residential Communities Raises Privacy Concerns**  
_2021-03-22 · real-world · Severity: High_

Multiple Chinese cities have implemented AI-powered facial recognition for residential access, often without residents' consent, sparking privacy and data security concerns. Despite regulations prohibiting mandatory biometric data collection, enforcement is weak, and experts warn of risks of information leakage and rights violations without stricter legal oversight.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`
