### INC-00436  [↗](/incident/INC-00436.html)

**AI-Generated Disinformation Undermines Nepal's Election**  
_2026-03-03 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

AI-generated fake videos and images have flooded Nepal's election campaigns, spreading misinformation and hate speech. This disinformation, amplified on social media, is misleading voters and undermining democratic processes, particularly in a context of low digital literacy and limited monitoring expertise.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`
