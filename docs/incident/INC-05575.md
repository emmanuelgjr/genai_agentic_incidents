### INC-05575  [↗](/incident/INC-05575.html)

**Deployment and Trials of Level 3 Unmanned Autonomous Vehicles in Japan and China Raise AI Safety Concerns**  
_2021-03-21 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Japan and China have begun deploying and trialing Level 3 autonomous vehicles, including unmanned buses and Baidu's Apollo system, on public roads. These AI-driven systems operate without onboard safety personnel, introducing plausible future risks of harm if malfunctions occur, though no incidents have been reported yet.

**Attack vector:** `other`
