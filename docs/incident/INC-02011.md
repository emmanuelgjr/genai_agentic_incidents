**US Lawmakers Propose Ban on Chinese AI Robots in Federal Agencies**  
_2026-03-26 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

US lawmakers, led by Senators Tom Cotton and Chuck Schumer, have introduced the American Security Robotics Act to ban federal agencies from purchasing or operating AI-enabled robots made by Chinese companies. The bill aims to prevent potential national security risks, such as data breaches or espionage, posed by these autonomous systems.
