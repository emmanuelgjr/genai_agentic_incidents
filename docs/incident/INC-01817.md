### INC-01817  [↗](/incident/INC-01817.html)

**Study Finds ChatGPT Health AI Fails in Emergency Medical Triage**  
_2026-02-24 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

An independent study found that ChatGPT Health, an AI medical guidance tool used by millions, failed to recommend emergency care in over half of serious cases and inconsistently flagged suicide risks. Researchers warn these triage failures pose significant health risks for users relying on the AI for urgent decisions.

**Attack vector:** `unsafe-advice`
