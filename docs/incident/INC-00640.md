**Apple Pays $95 Million Settlement Over Siri Privacy Violations**  
_2026-01-25 · real-world · Severity: High_

Apple is distributing payouts from a $95 million settlement after a class action lawsuit alleged its Siri voice assistant unlawfully recorded and shared users' private conversations without consent. The settlement covers Siri-enabled devices in the U.S., addressing privacy violations caused by the AI system's unintended activations and data sharing.
