**Changan Qiyuan A07 AI-Assisted Driving Failure Leads to Fatal Crash in China**  
_2026-05-30 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

A Changan Qiyuan A07 electric car, equipped with an L2-level AI-assisted driving system lacking lidar, failed to detect a stationary truck on a dark highway in Jiangxi, China. The driver, distracted and not holding the wheel, relied on the system, resulting in a collision that killed three occupants. Both driver error and AI system limitations contributed to the tragedy.
