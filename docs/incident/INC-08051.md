### INC-08051  [↗](/incident/INC-08051.html)

**Hong Kong Regulator Warns of Rising AI-Driven Cyber Threats**  
_2026-06-02 · real-world · Severity: High_

Hong Kong's Securities and Futures Commission warned licensed firms, especially internet brokers and virtual asset platforms, about a surge in AI-driven cyber threats. The regulator linked a 27% rise in cyber incidents to malicious actors using AI to exploit vulnerabilities, urging firms to strengthen cybersecurity to prevent data breaches and asset theft.
