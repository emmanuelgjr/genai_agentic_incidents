### INC-06238  [↗](/incident/INC-06238.html)

**AI Proctoring Software Causes Discrimination and Privacy Concerns in Online Exams**  
_2020-11-10 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI-powered exam proctoring software, including facial recognition systems like Proctortrack and Examplify, has led to discrimination against students with darker skin tones and disabilities, as well as privacy and security concerns. Students have reported being unfairly denied exam access and subjected to invasive surveillance, sparking widespread protests and legal challenges.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`
