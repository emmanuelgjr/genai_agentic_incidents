### INC-06074  [↗](/incident/INC-06074.html)

**Tesla Autopilot and FSD Beta Exhibit Dangerous Left-Turn Behavior in Real-World Tests**  
_2021-03-22 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Multiple videos by tester Chuck Cook reveal that Tesla's Autopilot and Full Self-Driving (FSD) beta systems struggle with unprotected left turns, often hesitating or making risky maneuvers in heavy traffic. Human intervention was repeatedly required to prevent accidents, highlighting significant safety concerns with the AI's real-world driving capabilities.
