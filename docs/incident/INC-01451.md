**Minors in Valladolid Tried for Using AI to Create and Share Non-Consensual Nude Images of Classmates**  
_2026-04-13 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Ten male minors in Valladolid, Spain, are on trial for using AI to generate and distribute pornographic images by placing classmates' faces onto nude bodies. The AI-generated images were shared without consent, leading to charges of child pornography and moral harm, and resulting in legal and psychological consequences for the victims.
