**US Judge Delays Approval of Anthropic's $1.5 Billion AI Copyright Settlement**  
_2026-05-14 · real-world · Severity: High_

A US federal judge has delayed final approval of Anthropic's $1.5 billion settlement with authors who allege their copyrighted books were used without permission to train the Claude AI system. The judge requested more details on attorney fees and payouts, highlighting ongoing concerns over AI-driven copyright infringement.
