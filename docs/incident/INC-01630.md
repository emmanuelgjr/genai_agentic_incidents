**Pentagon and Gulf States Consider Ukrainian AI Drones to Counter Iranian Attacks**  
_2026-03-05 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Pentagon and at least one Gulf country are negotiating to purchase Ukrainian AI-powered interceptor drones to defend against Iranian Shahed drones. These AI systems, already used by Ukraine to counter mass drone attacks, offer a cost-effective alternative to expensive Patriot missiles amid ongoing military threats in the region.
