### INC-03126  [↗](/incident/INC-03126.html)

**Purportedly AI-Generated Hunting Regulation Errors Reportedly Lead to Idaho Citation and Multi-State Warnings from Wildlife Agencies**  
_2025-10-15 · real-world · Severity: Medium_
AIID: [`#1251`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1251/)

Multiple state wildlife agencies including Idaho Department of Fish and Game and Wyoming Game and Fish Department reported incidents where AI-powered search engines provided incorrect hunting and fishing regulation information to the public. In Idaho, a waterfowl hunter was cited for hunting a day early after receiving incorrect opening date information from an AI search result that pulled from a failed proposal rather than official regulations. The Idaho agency found additional examples where AI searches for river-specific rules returned information for similarly named rivers in different states. Wyoming officials reported 'a number of inaccurate AI-generated search results' where AI referenced failed legislative bills as if they were current law, creating misleading information opposite to actual regulations. Google introduced AI-generated overviews in 2024 that provide quick answers but not always accurate ones. Officials in Illinois and Maine have also warned against using AI for hunting rule interpretations. The agencies emphasized that hunters remain accountable for following published regulations regardless of AI misinformation, with violations carrying potential fines, jail time, or suspension of hunting privileges.
