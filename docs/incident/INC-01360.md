### INC-01360  [↗](/incident/INC-01360.html)

**Major AI Chatbots Leak User Conversations to Advertising Trackers**  
_2026-05-05 · real-world · Severity: High_

A study reveals that leading AI chatbots—ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, and Perplexity—have been leaking sensitive user conversation data to third-party advertising companies like Meta, Google, and TikTok. This data sharing enables user profiling and targeted advertising, constituting a significant privacy violation and breach of data protection regulations.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MEASURE-2.10`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0057`
