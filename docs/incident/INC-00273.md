### INC-00273  [↗](/incident/INC-00273.html)

**AI-Driven Crackdown on Illegal Gambling Sites in Turkey**  
_2026-05-12 · real-world · Severity: High_

Turkish law enforcement used AI-supported programs to identify and disrupt illegal gambling and betting operations, leading to the blocking of 5,151 websites and the arrest of 108 suspects across 35 provinces, including Istanbul. The AI systems facilitated the detection and targeting of illicit activities, resulting in significant enforcement actions.
