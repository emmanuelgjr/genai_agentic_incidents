**MIT AI Predicts Catastrophic COVID-19 Surge if Social Distancing Ends Prematurely**  
_2020-04-16 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

MIT researchers developed an AI model to forecast COVID-19 spread, finding that prematurely relaxing social distancing or quarantine measures in the US could cause an exponential surge in cases. The AI warns that such actions would nullify previous prevention efforts, highlighting the potential for significant public health harm if ignored.
