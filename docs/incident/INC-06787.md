**Microsoft Launches Project Artemis AI to Detect and Prevent Online Child Grooming**  
_2020-01-09 · real-world · Severity: High_

Microsoft, in collaboration with partners like The Meet Group, Roblox, Kik, Thorn, and the UK Home Office, launched Project Artemis, an AI tool that analyzes online chat to detect and flag child grooming and predatory behavior. The system rates conversations and alerts moderators or law enforcement, aiming to prevent child exploitation on digital platforms.
