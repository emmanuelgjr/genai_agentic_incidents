### INC-06457  [↗](/incident/INC-06457.html)

**Clearview AI Faces Legal Action Over Facial Recognition Use by Private Firms**  
_2020-05-05 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Clearview AI's facial recognition system, which scraped billions of images from social media, was used by private companies in violation of Illinois' biometric privacy law. Legal action prompted Clearview to cancel all private and Illinois-based accounts, highlighting the risks of AI-driven privacy breaches and unauthorized biometric data use.
