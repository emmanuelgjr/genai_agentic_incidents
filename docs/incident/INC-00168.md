### INC-00168  [↗](/incident/INC-00168.html)

**AI Gateway LiteLLM Compromised by Malware Amid Compliance Scandal**  
_2026-03-30 · real-world · Severity: High_

LiteLLM, a widely used AI gateway, suffered a credential-stealing malware attack after obtaining security certifications from Delve, an AI compliance startup now accused of fabricating data and using unreliable auditors. The incident led LiteLLM to sever ties with Delve and seek recertification, highlighting risks from compromised AI systems and misleading compliance claims.
