**China's First Facial Recognition Lawsuit: Court Rules Against Zoo's Unconsented Data Collection**  
_2020-11-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

A university professor sued Hangzhou Safari Park for unilaterally switching from fingerprint to facial recognition for park entry without consent. The court ruled the zoo's collection of facial data exceeded necessity and lacked legitimacy, ordering compensation and deletion of biometric data, highlighting privacy risks in AI-driven biometric systems.
