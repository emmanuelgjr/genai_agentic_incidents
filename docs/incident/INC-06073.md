**Tesla Autopilot and Braking System Failures Spark Fatalities and Regulatory Scrutiny**  
_2021-04-22 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Multiple incidents involving Tesla's AI-driven Autopilot and braking systems have led to fatal crashes and public outcry. Investigations and tests revealed that Tesla vehicles can operate without a driver present, raising safety concerns. U.S. senators and Chinese regulators are demanding stricter oversight, while Tesla disputes claims of system malfunctions.
