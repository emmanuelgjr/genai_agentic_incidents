**Tesla Plans Wider Release of Full Self-Driving Beta Software Amid Safety Concerns**  
_2020-11-28 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Tesla CEO Elon Musk announced plans to expand the beta release of its 'Full Self-Driving' (FSD) AI software to more vehicles within two weeks. While the system promises increased autonomy, it remains in testing and requires driver supervision, raising concerns about potential safety risks with broader deployment.
