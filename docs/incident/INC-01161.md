**HD Hyundai Expands AI-Powered Unmanned Naval Vessel Collaboration in the US**  
_2026-04-23 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

HD Hyundai, in partnership with US defense AI firm Anduril and the American Bureau of Shipping (ABS), signed multiple MOUs to jointly develop AI-driven unmanned surface and underwater vessels. The collaboration includes establishing certification frameworks for autonomous maritime systems, highlighting future risks associated with military AI technologies. Activities are centered in the United States.
