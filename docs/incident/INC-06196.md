### INC-06196  [↗](/incident/INC-06196.html)

**YouTube AI Recommendations Linked to Increased COVID-19 Vaccine Hesitancy**  
_2021-06-03 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A study by Oxford and Southampton universities found that YouTube's AI-driven recommendation algorithms create echo chambers by suggesting content aligned with users' fears, leading to increased COVID-19 vaccine hesitancy. YouTube users showed significantly lower willingness to vaccinate, highlighting indirect public health harm caused by AI-powered content recommendations.
