### INC-00382  [↗](/incident/INC-00382.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfake of Jean Reno Used in Major Investment Scam in Lyon**  
_2026-03-11 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

In Lyon, France, scammers used AI-generated deepfake videos impersonating actor Jean Reno to promote a fraudulent trading platform. A 50-year-old engineer was deceived, losing €350,000—his life savings. The incident highlights the significant harm caused by AI-enabled identity fraud. Both the victim and Jean Reno have filed legal complaints.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
