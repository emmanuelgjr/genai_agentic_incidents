### INC-01679  [↗](/incident/INC-01679.html)

**Princeton Ends Unproctored Exams After Surge in AI-Enabled Cheating**  
_2026-05-12 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Princeton University has ended its 132-year tradition of unproctored exams due to widespread student cheating facilitated by generative AI tools like ChatGPT. With nearly 30% of students admitting to cheating, the university will now require proctored exams and implement detection software to restore academic integrity.
