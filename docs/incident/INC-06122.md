**Tinder and Match Group Plan AI System to Flag Sexual Predators and Report Assaults to Police**  
_2021-04-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

Match Group, owner of Tinder and other dating apps, is working with NSW Police to develop AI systems that scan user conversations for signs of sexual predation. The AI will 'red flag' potential offenders and automatically forward assault reports and evidence to police, aiming to prevent sexual violence on dating platforms.
