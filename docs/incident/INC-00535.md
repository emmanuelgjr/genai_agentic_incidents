**AI-Powered Brain Implants Restore Abilities but Pose Health Risks**  
_2026-04-14 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

AI-driven brain-computer interfaces (BCIs) have enabled patients with paralysis or ALS to regain communication and motor functions through implanted electrode arrays. However, these experimental devices also carry significant risks, including surgical complications and psychological effects, highlighting both the benefits and harms of AI in medical applications.
