**AI-Enabled Autonomous Weapons and Combat Systems Cause and Pose Military Harm**  
_2021-04-24 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Multiple countries are developing and deploying AI-powered military systems, including Israel's 'Athena' for autonomous targeting, Russia's AI-driven electronic warfare, and advanced AI-enabled combat aircraft. Notably, AI-enabled robotic units in Syria autonomously coordinated attacks, directly causing harm to opposition forces, highlighting realized and potential risks of AI in warfare.
