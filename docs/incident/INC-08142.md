**AI-Generated Misinformation Causes Social Disruption and Legal Action in China**  
_2026-06-07 · real-world · Severity: High_

Multiple incidents in China involved individuals using AI to fabricate and spread false videos and information, leading to public misunderstanding, social disruption, and harm to enterprises. Authorities responded with legal penalties, account closures, and coordinated debunking efforts to curb AI-driven rumor proliferation and restore public order.
