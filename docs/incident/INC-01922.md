**Turkish Defense Official Highlights Shift to AI-Driven Warfare**  
_2026-05-04 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

At an event in Bolu, Turkey, Deputy Defense Minister Salih Ayhan emphasized the end of traditional frontline warfare, highlighting a future dominated by AI-enabled drone swarms, smart machines, and electronic warfare. He warned that technological and data supremacy will shape global power, underscoring potential risks from military AI systems.
