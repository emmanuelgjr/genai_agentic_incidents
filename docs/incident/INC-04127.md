### INC-04127  [↗](/incident/INC-04127.html)

**Purportedly AI-Driven Phishing Scam Uses Spoofed Google Call to Attempt Gmail Breach of Security Expert**  
_2024-10-07 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#839`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/839/)

Scammers allegedly used a purportedly AI-generated voice to impersonate a Google representative in an attempt to steal Gmail account credentials from security expert Sam Mitrovic. The reportedly AI-driven phishing call used a spoofed Google phone number and a fabricated email, making the scam appear legitimate. Mitrovic reportedly noted that the caller&#x27;s professional demeanor, coupled with AI-generated speech and a Google-related number, could easily deceive unsuspecting users.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
