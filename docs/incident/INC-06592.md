**Facial Recognition AI Enables Arrest in NYC Subway Attempted Assault Case**  
_2020-08-31 · real-world · Severity: High_

New York police used facial recognition AI to identify and arrest José Reyes, who attempted to sexually assault a 25-year-old woman in a Manhattan subway station. Video evidence from witnesses and the AI system led to his capture and charges, highlighting AI's role in resolving violent crime incidents.
