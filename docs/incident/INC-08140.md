### INC-08140  [↗](/incident/INC-08140.html)

**AI-Driven Misinformation Campaigns Target Bangladeshi Celebrities**  
_2026-06-07 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

In Bangladesh, AI technologies like deepfake and chatbots have been used to launch harmful misinformation campaigns, notably targeting 29 female celebrities with 68 damaging rumors, including 50 manipulated videos. Experts call for urgent national and global AI regulation to address rising AI-enabled crimes.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
