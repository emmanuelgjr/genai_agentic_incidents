### INC-06453  [↗](/incident/INC-06453.html)

**Civil Liberties Groups Warn of Privacy Risks from AI-Powered Fever-Detecting Drones**  
_2020-05-04 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Police in Daytona Beach and Connecticut considered using AI-enabled drones to remotely detect fevers during the pandemic, but halted plans after civil liberties advocates raised concerns about privacy violations, data accuracy, and potential misuse of sensitive health information. No harm occurred, but significant risks of rights violations were highlighted.
