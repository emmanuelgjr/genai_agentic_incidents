### INC-07016  [↗](/incident/INC-07016.html)

**Turkey Develops AI-Controlled Swarm Drones for Military Use**  
_2020-12-30 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Turkey is advancing AI-powered swarm drone technology, enabling autonomous navigation, target recognition, and mission execution without human intervention. Developed by Savunma Teknolojileri ve Mühendislik A.Ş., these drones are set for military deployment, raising credible risks of harm in future conflicts due to their autonomous lethal capabilities.

**Attack vector:** `rce`

**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MEASURE-2.7`
