### INC-03621  [↗](/incident/INC-03621.html)

**Celebrities targeted by harmful deepfake AI videos in India**  
_2024-05-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI-generated deepfake videos targeted Indian celebrities such as Alia Bhatt, Rashmika Mandanna, Katrina Kaif, Kajol and Prime Minister Modi, causing reputational harm and contributing to a flood of synthetic content during India's 2024 election period.

**Affected:** Indian celebrities; Indian electorate  
**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.1`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048`, `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
