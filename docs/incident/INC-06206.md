**AAA Research Warns AI Driver Assistance Marketing May Lead to Dangerous Overconfidence**  
_2020-09-10 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

AAA Foundation research found that marketing of AI-based active driving assistance systems often exaggerates their capabilities, leading drivers to overestimate system safety and reliability. This overconfidence, fueled by misleading branding, could plausibly result in dangerous driving scenarios, though no actual harm has yet been reported.
