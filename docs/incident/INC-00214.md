### INC-00214  [↗](/incident/INC-00214.html)

**AI System 'AVCI' Enables Major Drug Trafficking Busts in Istanbul**  
_2026-04-06 · real-world · Severity: High_

Istanbul Police deployed the AI-powered AVCI system to infiltrate encrypted messaging apps used by drug traffickers. AVCI's advanced natural language processing and data analysis enabled authorities to identify, arrest, and prosecute 325 suspects, dismantling criminal networks and disrupting illegal drug trade across 14 provinces.
