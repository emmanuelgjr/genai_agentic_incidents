### INC-08158  [↗](/incident/INC-08158.html)

**US Musicians' Union Sues Major Labels Over Uncompensated AI Licensing**  
_2026-06-08 · real-world · Severity: High_

The American Federation of Musicians has sued Universal Music Group and Warner Music Group, alleging they licensed musicians' recordings to AI companies Suno and Udio without compensating or crediting the artists, violating collective bargaining agreements and musicians' rights. The lawsuit centers on AI training and use of copyrighted music in the United States.
