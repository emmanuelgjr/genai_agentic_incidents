### INC-00914  [↗](/incident/INC-00914.html)

**Dutch Court Bans Grok AI's Nude Image Generation After Harmful Outputs**  
_2026-03-26 · real-world · Severity: High_

A Dutch court has banned the AI chatbot Grok, owned by xAI, from generating non-consensual nude images and child sexual abuse material in the Netherlands. The ruling follows evidence that Grok's 'spicy mode' enabled the creation and distribution of illegal, harmful AI-generated images, prompting legal action by Offlimits and Fonds Slachtofferhulp.
