**AI-Enhanced Blood Test Enables Early Leprosy Detection in Brazil**  
_2026-04-12 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Researchers at the University of São Paulo developed a diagnostic method combining a blood test and an AI-powered questionnaire, significantly improving early detection of leprosy. The AI system identified cases at initial stages, outperforming traditional tests and enabling earlier treatment, thus reducing potential health harm in Brazil.
