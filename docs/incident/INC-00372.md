**AI-Generated Content Causes Consumer Harm and Legal Action in China**  
_2026-03-12 · real-world · Severity: High_

Multiple incidents in China highlight the misuse of AI for consumer fraud, including AI-generated product images and deepfake videos or voices used in deceptive advertising. Courts have ruled against companies for misleading consumers, mandating compensation and clarifying legal responsibilities for AI-generated content in e-commerce and advertising.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
