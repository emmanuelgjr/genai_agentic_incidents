### INC-00151  [↗](/incident/INC-00151.html)

**AI Deepfake Tools Bypass KYC, Fueling Financial Fraud in Crypto and Banking**  
_2026-04-06 · real-world · Severity: High_

A darknet actor known as Jinkusu is selling AI-powered tools, including JINKUSU CAM, that use real-time deepfake facial and voice manipulation to bypass Know Your Customer (KYC) systems at banks and major crypto platforms. This enables synthetic identity fraud, financial scams, and undermines biometric security globally.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`

**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
