**AI-Generated Short Dramas Cause Actor Unemployment in China**  
_2026-04-23 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Chinese actor Zhang Xiaolei, known for roles in short dramas, lost his job due to the rapid adoption of AI-generated actors and content in the entertainment industry. The shift led to a drastic reduction in live-action productions, forcing Zhang and others to leave acting and return to farming for livelihood.
