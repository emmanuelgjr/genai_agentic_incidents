**FDA Approves AI System for Early Sepsis Detection, Reducing Mortality**  
_2026-05-11 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The FDA has approved an AI-based early warning system for sepsis, developed by Johns Hopkins University and commercialized by Bayesian Health. Integrated with electronic health records, the system detects sepsis hours before clinicians, reducing sepsis mortality by nearly 20% in U.S. hospitals.
