### INC-07019  [↗](/incident/INC-07019.html)

**Turkey Launches First Armed Unmanned Naval Vessel with AI Capabilities**  
_2020-11-05 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Turkish firms ARES Shipyard and Meteksan Defense have developed SİDA, Turkey's first armed unmanned naval vessel featuring AI and autonomous capabilities. Set for launch in December, SİDA is designed for military operations such as surveillance, combat, and facility protection, raising concerns about potential future risks from autonomous weapon systems.
