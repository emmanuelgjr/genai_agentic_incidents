**Navistar and TuSimple Announce Partnership to Develop Autonomous Trucks**  
_2020-07-15 · real-world · Severity: High_

Navistar and TuSimple have formed a strategic partnership to co-develop and mass-produce SAE Level 4 autonomous semi-trucks by 2024, with Navistar taking a minority stake in TuSimple. While no incidents have occurred, the deployment of self-driving trucks presents potential future risks associated with AI-operated vehicles.
