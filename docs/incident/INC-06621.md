### INC-06621  [↗](/incident/INC-06621.html)

**Flawed Universal Credit Algorithm Pushes UK Claimants into Poverty**  
_2020-09-29 · real-world · Severity: High_

A poorly designed algorithm automating the UK's Universal Credit benefits system has caused significant harm, including hunger, debt, and psychological distress among claimants. Human Rights Watch reports the system's payment miscalculations and inflexibility are pushing vulnerable people into poverty, calling for urgent redesign to protect social security rights.
