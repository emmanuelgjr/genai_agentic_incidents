**US Air Force Tests AI-Enabled Valkyrie Drone Launching Autonomous Mini-Drone**  
_2021-04-05 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The US Air Force successfully tested the AI-enabled XQ-58A Valkyrie drone autonomously launching an ALTIUS-600 mini-drone from its internal bay. This demonstration marks a significant step in autonomous drone teaming for military operations, raising future concerns about the risks of AI-driven autonomous weapons, though no harm occurred in this test.
