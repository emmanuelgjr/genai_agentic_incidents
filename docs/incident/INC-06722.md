### INC-06722  [↗](/incident/INC-06722.html)

**Iran Bans Algorithmic Trading in Stock Markets Over Stability Concerns**  
_2020-09-23 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Iran's Securities and Exchange Organization has temporarily banned algorithmic (AI-driven) trading and order splitting for all online clients in Tehran and Iran Fara Bourse stock exchanges. The move aims to prevent potential market instability and unfairness, though no actual harm or incident has been reported yet.

**Attack vector:** `other`
