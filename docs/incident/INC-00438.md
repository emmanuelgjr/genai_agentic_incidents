### INC-00438  [↗](/incident/INC-00438.html)

**AI-Generated Fabricated Citations Undermine Biomedical Research Integrity**  
_2026-05-12 · real-world · Severity: High_

A Columbia University-led audit revealed that AI systems, particularly large language models, are responsible for fabricating over 4,000 citations in biomedical papers. These false references undermine clinical guidelines and scientific integrity, potentially leading to harmful medical decisions. The issue is ongoing and affects the reliability of healthcare research globally.

**Attack vector:** `misinformation`
