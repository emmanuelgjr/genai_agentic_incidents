### INC-00874  [↗](/incident/INC-00874.html)

**Deepfake Scam Targets Bulgarian News Anchor**  
_2026-02-26 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

AI-generated deepfake videos featuring Bulgarian news anchor Nikolay Doynov were circulated on social media, falsely showing him endorsing a hair loss lotion. The realistic videos were used in an online scam, deceiving viewers and potentially causing financial and reputational harm. Experts confirmed the content was created using advanced AI technology.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
