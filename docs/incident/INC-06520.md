### INC-06520  [↗](/incident/INC-06520.html)

**Dutch Scientists Warn of Risks in AI-Enabled COVID-19 Tracking Apps**  
_2020-04-13 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Sixty Dutch scientists and experts warned the government about potential risks of AI-enabled COVID-19 tracking and health apps, citing concerns over privacy, civil liberties, effectiveness, and possible false security. They urge thorough evaluation and safeguards to prevent societal and legal harms before deployment.

**Attack vector:** `other`
