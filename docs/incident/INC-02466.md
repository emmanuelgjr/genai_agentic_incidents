### INC-02466  [↗](/incident/INC-02466.html)

**Cursor AI support agent invents user policy, causing user revolt**  
_2025-04 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Cursor's AI-driven support agent fabricated a non-existent single-device login policy and confidently relayed it to multiple users, triggering a wave of subscription cancellations and public backlash. The agent's confabulated rule was treated by support workflows as authoritative until human staff intervened.

**Affected:** Cursor (Anysphere) users  
**Attack vector:** `hallucination`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI08`, `ASI09`
