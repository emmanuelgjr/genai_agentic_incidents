### INC-01790  [↗](/incident/INC-01790.html)

**South Korean Woman Used ChatGPT to Plan Fatal Drug Poisonings**  
_2026-02-19 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

A 21-year-old woman in Seoul, South Korea, used ChatGPT to research the lethal effects of mixing prescription drugs with alcohol, then applied this knowledge to poison drinks, resulting in two deaths and one injury. Police cited her AI-assisted planning as evidence of intent in charging her with murder.
