**Tesla Develops AI System to Detect Children in Cars and Prevent Heatstroke**  
_2020-08-21 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Tesla is developing an AI-powered radar system to detect children left inside vehicles, aiming to prevent heatstroke fatalities. The company has requested regulatory approval in the US for this technology, which distinguishes between living beings and objects to enhance passenger safety, especially during high temperatures.
