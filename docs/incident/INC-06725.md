**IRGC Deploys AI-Enabled Drones to Track US Aircraft Carrier in Persian Gulf**  
_2020-09-23 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The Iranian Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) announced the deployment of 188 AI-enabled drones and helicopters for surveillance, successfully tracking the US aircraft carrier Nimitz and its escorts in the Persian Gulf. While no harm occurred, the use of autonomous drones in a tense military context raises potential risks of escalation.
