**Grok Reportedly Disclosed Adult Performer Siri Dahl&#x27;s Legal Name and Birthdate, Allegedly Contributing to Doxxing and Harassment**  
_2026-02-19 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1443`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1443/)

Grok is reported to have publicly provided adult performer Siri Dahl&#x27;s legal name and birthdate without being asked for that information. Dahl reportedly said she had worked to keep those details private and that, after the disclosure, impersonation accounts and reposts of stolen content using her legal name purportedly appeared online.
