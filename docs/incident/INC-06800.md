**Moscow Uses AI Facial Recognition to Enforce Coronavirus Quarantine**  
_2020-02-21 · real-world · Severity: High_

Moscow authorities deployed AI-powered facial recognition to monitor and enforce coronavirus quarantine compliance for around 2,500 returnees from China. The system tracked individuals' movements, leading to involuntary surveillance and enforcement actions, raising concerns over privacy violations and human rights infringements due to AI-driven public health surveillance.
