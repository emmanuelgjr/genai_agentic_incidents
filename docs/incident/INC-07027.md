### INC-07027  [↗](/incident/INC-07027.html)

**Twitter Bots Amplify Climate Change Denial, Study Finds**  
_2020-02-18 · real-world · Severity: High_

A Brown University study found that about 25% of climate change-related tweets during the period around the US withdrawal from the Paris Agreement were generated by AI-powered Twitter bots. These bots predominantly spread climate science denial and misinformation, significantly distorting public discourse and amplifying denialist messages online.
