### INC-06936  [↗](/incident/INC-06936.html)

**Study Finds AI Driving Aids Increase Driver Disengagement and Road Safety Risks**  
_2020-11-19 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

A study by IIHS and MIT's AgeLab found that advanced driver-assist AI systems, such as adaptive cruise control and lane-keeping, lead to increased driver disengagement and inattention. This false sense of security is linked to higher accident risks and fatalities, highlighting unintended safety hazards from AI-assisted driving technologies.
