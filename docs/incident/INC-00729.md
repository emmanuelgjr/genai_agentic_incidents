**Canadian Musician Sues Google Over AI-Generated Defamation**  
_2026-05-04 · real-world · Severity: High_

Canadian fiddler Ashley MacIsaac filed a lawsuit against Google after its AI-generated search summary falsely identified him as a sex offender, leading to reputational harm and the cancellation of a concert. The lawsuit alleges Google's AI system produced and published defamatory misinformation, causing tangible personal and professional damage.
