**Ambulance Collides with AI Food Delivery Robot in Hollywood, Disrupting Emergency Services**  
_2026-01-23 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

In Hollywood, Los Angeles, an autonomous food delivery robot collided with an ambulance transporting a patient. Although no injuries were reported, a second ambulance was required to complete the patient’s transport, causing disruption to emergency medical services and raising safety concerns about AI-powered delivery robots.
