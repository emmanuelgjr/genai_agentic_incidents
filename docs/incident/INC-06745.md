**Japanese Police Arrest Three for Deepfake Porn Targeting Female Celebrities**  
_2020-11-19 · real-world · Severity: High_

Three men in Japan were arrested for using AI deepfake technology to create and distribute fake pornographic videos featuring the faces of well-known female celebrities. The videos, widely circulated online for profit, caused reputational harm to at least six actresses, prompting police action and ongoing efforts to curb such AI-generated abuse.
