### INC-01008  [↗](/incident/INC-01008.html)

**First Prosecution for AI-Generated Child Abuse Images in Germany**  
_2026-04-30 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Authorities in Baden-Württemberg, Germany, have charged a 59-year-old man from Karlsruhe with creating highly realistic child sexual abuse images using AI programs. This marks the first time German prosecutors have filed charges based on AI-generated child pornography, highlighting the technology's role in producing illegal and harmful content.
