### INC-06991  [↗](/incident/INC-06991.html)

**TikTok Apologizes for AI-Driven Suppression of Black Lives Matter Content**  
_2020-06-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

TikTok's AI-driven content display system malfunctioned, making posts with #BlackLivesMatter and #GeorgeFloyd appear to have zero views, leading users to believe their content was being censored. The incident caused harm by suppressing black creators' voices and eroding trust, prompting TikTok to apologize and pledge corrective measures.
