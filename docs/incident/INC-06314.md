**AI-Powered Police Body Cameras and Robot Vacuums Raise Privacy Concerns**  
_2020-03-06 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Police departments are deploying body cameras with real-time AI facial recognition, enabling instant identification and database searches without public consent, raising privacy and rights concerns. Separately, a robot vacuum with AI-driven video surveillance was found to have faulty encryption, exposing users' video feeds and compromising their privacy.
