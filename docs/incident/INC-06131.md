### INC-06131  [↗](/incident/INC-06131.html)

**Turkey Launches First Armed Unmanned Naval Vehicle ULAQ, Raising AI Weaponization Risks**  
_2021-02-12 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Turkey has launched its first armed unmanned surface vessel, ULAQ, developed by ARES Shipyard and Meteksan Defense. Equipped with AI-driven autonomous navigation and weapon systems, ULAQ is entering sea trials and planned firing tests, highlighting future risks of harm from AI-enabled military platforms, though no incidents have occurred yet.

**Attack vector:** `other`  

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI10`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048`
