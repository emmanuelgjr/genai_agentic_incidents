### INC-06255  [↗](/incident/INC-06255.html)

**AI System Identifies 11 Potentially Hazardous Asteroids Missed by NASA**  
_2020-02-17 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Researchers at Leiden University developed an AI system, the Hazardous Object Identifier, which identified 11 large asteroids as potentially hazardous to Earth—objects previously deemed safe by NASA. While no immediate threat exists, the AI's findings highlight future risks and the potential for improved asteroid detection using artificial intelligence.
