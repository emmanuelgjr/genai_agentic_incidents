### INC-05613  [↗](/incident/INC-05613.html)

**Facebook Encryption Plans Threaten AI Child Abuse Detection**  
_2021-04-18 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Facebook's proposed end-to-end encryption for Messenger and Instagram could disable AI systems used to detect child abuse images and grooming, raising concerns from child protection groups and officials. Experts warn this move may allow abuse to go undetected, jeopardizing children's safety online.
