### INC-05648  [↗](/incident/INC-05648.html)

**Finnish Regulator Warns of AI-Driven Personalized Pricing Risks**  
_2021-02-05 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Finnish Competition and Consumer Authority (KKV) warns that companies are using AI algorithms and customer data to personalize prices, potentially charging different customers varying amounts for the same product. This practice may reduce price transparency and fairness, though it can also benefit some consumers with lower prices.
