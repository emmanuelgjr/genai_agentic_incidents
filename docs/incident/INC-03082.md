### INC-03082  [↗](/incident/INC-03082.html)

**Purported Deepfake Impersonating Doctor Allegedly Used in $200,000 Investment Scam Targeting Florida Grandmother**  
_2025-12-02 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1314`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1314/)

An 82-year-old Florida woman reportedly lost approximately $200,000 after viewing a purportedly AI-generated deepfake video that impersonated a trusted doctor from the autism community and promoted an investment opportunity. According to reporting, the woman believed the investment would secure her autistic grandson&#x27;s future. The impersonation allegedly enabled scammers to gain access to her banking information, resulting in substantial financial loss and significant emotional distress.
