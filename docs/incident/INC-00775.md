**China Warns of AI Token-Related Scams and Data Security Risks**  
_2026-04-06 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Chinese authorities have warned that the rapid rise of AI tokens (词元) has led to scams, data theft, and privacy breaches. Criminals exploit token vulnerabilities for fraud, identity theft, and unauthorized access, posing threats to personal assets and national security. Official alerts urge public vigilance and improved security practices.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`
