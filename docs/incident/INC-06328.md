### INC-06328  [↗](/incident/INC-06328.html)

**AI-Powered Wearable Predicts Epileptic Seizures in Advance**  
_2020-09-29 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Israeli researchers at Ben-Gurion University have developed Epiness, a wearable device using machine-learning algorithms to predict epileptic seizures up to an hour before onset. The device aims to improve patient safety by providing early warnings, though it is still in development and clinical trials are pending.
