### INC-01514  [↗](/incident/INC-01514.html)

**NAACP Sues xAI Over Illegal Gas Turbine Use for AI Data Center, Citing Pollution and Health Risks**  
_2026-04-14 · real-world · Severity: High_

The NAACP has sued Elon Musk's xAI and its subsidiary MZX Tech, alleging they illegally operated 27 gas turbines without permits to power a data center supporting the Grok AI chatbot in Mississippi. The lawsuit claims this caused harmful pollution, violating the Clean Air Act and endangering local communities' health.
