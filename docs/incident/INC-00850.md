### INC-00850  [↗](/incident/INC-00850.html)

**Company Uses AI to Clone Departed Employees, Raising Legal and Ethical Concerns in China**  
_2026-04-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

Several Chinese companies have used AI to create digital clones of former employees by training models on their work documents and communications. These AI avatars continue performing tasks after the employees leave, sometimes without explicit consent, sparking public outcry and legal warnings over privacy, intellectual property, and labor rights violations.
