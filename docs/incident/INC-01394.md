### INC-01394  [↗](/incident/INC-01394.html)

**Meta Acquires Moltbook Amid AI Agent Security Concerns**  
_2026-03-10 · real-world · Severity: High_

Meta acquired Moltbook, a social network populated by autonomous AI agents. Prior to the acquisition, a security vulnerability allowed unauthorized users to impersonate AI agents and spread misleading content, causing panic and misinformation. The incident highlights risks of privacy breaches and manipulation inherent in AI-driven platforms.

**Attack vector:** `prompt-injection`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM01`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI01`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0051`, `AML.T0058`
