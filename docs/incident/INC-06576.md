### INC-06576  [↗](/incident/INC-06576.html)

**Facebook's AI Algorithm Promotes Holocaust Denial Content**  
_2020-08-13 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Investigations by the Institute for Strategic Dialogue found that Facebook's AI-powered algorithms actively promote Holocaust denial content by recommending denial pages and groups to users. Despite Facebook's claims of removing such material, the AI system continues to spread harmful misinformation and hate, causing significant harm to communities.

**Attack vector:** `misinformation`
