### INC-00479  [↗](/incident/INC-00479.html)

**AI-Generated Investment Scam Defrauds Retiree in Antalya**  
_2026-05-09 · real-world · Severity: High_

In Antalya, retiree Suna Ülger was deceived by an AI-supported investment scam using fake videos and promises of profit. Scammers gained remote access to her phone, stole personal data, and transferred 700,000 TL from her accounts. The incident highlights the misuse of AI in facilitating financial fraud.

**Attack vector:** `other`  

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`
