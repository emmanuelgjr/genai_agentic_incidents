**Oracle AI Surveillance Software Used by Chinese Authorities for Citizen Monitoring and Repression**  
_2021-02-19 · real-world · Severity: High_

Oracle's AI-powered surveillance software has been used by Chinese police and military, including in Xinjiang, to monitor, track, and predict citizen behavior. This technology enables large-scale data analysis, facial recognition, and predictive policing, directly contributing to human rights violations and repression of ethnic minorities.
