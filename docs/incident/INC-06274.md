### INC-06274  [↗](/incident/INC-06274.html)

**AI-Driven Price Discrimination in Online Tourism Platforms Harms Consumers**  
_2020-09-21 · real-world · Severity: High_

Online tourism platforms in China have used AI-powered big data analytics to charge loyal customers higher prices than new users for identical services, violating consumer rights and privacy. This discriminatory pricing practice has led to public outcry and prompted new regulations banning such AI-driven unfair treatment.
