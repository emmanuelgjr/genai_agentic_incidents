### INC-08103  [↗](/incident/INC-08103.html)

**Experts Warn of AI-Driven Deepfake and Fraud Epidemic in India**  
_2026-06-04 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

At a FICCI conference in Mumbai, experts highlighted the growing threat of AI-generated deepfakes and AI-enabled fraud rings, which have led to financial losses and eroded digital trust. They called for advanced forensic technology, real-time intelligence sharing, and stronger collaboration to combat these AI-driven cybercrimes.
