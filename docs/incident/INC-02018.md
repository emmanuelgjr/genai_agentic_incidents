**US Military Confirms Use of AI in Iran Strikes, Causing Civilian Harm**  
_2026-03-10 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The US military confirmed using advanced AI systems to process intelligence and identify targets during military operations in Iran. While humans made final strike decisions, AI accelerated data analysis, directly influencing lethal actions that resulted in civilian casualties. This marks a significant AI-related incident involving harm.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`
