**Zürich Approves Police Use of AI for Criminal Investigations**  
_2026-02-23 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The Zurich cantonal parliament has approved a revised police law allowing police to use AI for analyzing personal data and online investigations, with judicial oversight. The move aims to improve crime prevention and efficiency but raises concerns about potential privacy violations and surveillance risks.
