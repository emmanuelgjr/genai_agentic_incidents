### INC-04120  [↗](/incident/INC-04120.html)

**Purported Deepfake Nude Images of Students Circulated Without Consent at Valencia Educational Institute**  
_2024-12-01 · real-world · Severity: Critical_
AIID: [`#1342`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1342/)

Purported deepfake nude images of female students were circulated without consent at an educational institute in Valencia, Spain. At least 16 minors reported that original photographs had been digitally manipulated so they appeared completely naked, with some images shared via social media accounts created in victims&#x27; names. The alleged creation and distribution of the images is being examined under Spanish laws related to the protection of minors.
