### INC-01416  [↗](/incident/INC-01416.html)

**Meta's AI Moderation Fails to Prevent Teen Exposure to Explicit Content on Instagram**  
_2026-02-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

Meta's AI-driven content moderation on Instagram failed to prevent nearly 19% of users aged 13-15 from seeing unwanted nude or sexual images, according to internal surveys revealed in a California lawsuit. The incident highlights harm to minors from insufficient AI safeguards against explicit content on the platform.
