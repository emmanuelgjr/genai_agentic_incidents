### INC-01649  [↗](/incident/INC-01649.html)

**Pinterest's AI Moderation System Wrongfully Flags and Bans Users**  
_2026-02-18 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Pinterest's AI-powered content moderation and labeling systems are malfunctioning, frequently misclassifying human-made images—especially those featuring women—as AI-generated, and failing to block actual AI-generated content. Users report wrongful account bans and ineffective moderation, leading to user dissatisfaction and harm to the platform's community experience.
