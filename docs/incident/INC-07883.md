**Scottish Government Criticized for Omitting AI Data Centre Emissions in Climate Policy**  
_2026-05-24 · real-world · Severity: High_

Action to Protect Rural Scotland (APRS) criticized the Scottish Government for failing to include emissions from hyperscale AI data centres in its climate impact assessments. This oversight risks underestimating environmental harm as AI-driven data centre expansion is not properly regulated or accounted for in Scotland's climate targets.
