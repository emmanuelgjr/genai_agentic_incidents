### INC-07883  [↗](/incident/INC-07883.html)

**FBI Warns of AI-Powered Kali365 Phishing Attacks Targeting Microsoft 365 Accounts**  
_2026-05-25 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The FBI has issued an alert about Kali365, an AI-driven phishing platform that automates the theft of Microsoft 365 OAuth tokens, bypassing passwords and multi-factor authentication. This tool enables even low-skilled attackers to hijack accounts, leading to data breaches and unauthorized access for individuals and organizations.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`, `ASI05`, `ASI09`, `ASI10`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.10`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0048`, `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0057`
