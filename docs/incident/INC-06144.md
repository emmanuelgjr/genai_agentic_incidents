**UK to Allow Hands-Free Driving with Automated Lane-Keeping AI on Motorways**  
_2021-01-24 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The UK plans to become the first country to legalize Automated Lane Keeping Systems (ALKS), an AI technology that allows drivers to take their hands off the wheel in certain motorway conditions. While aiming to improve convenience, the move raises safety concerns about driver distraction and the AI's ability to handle unexpected situations.
