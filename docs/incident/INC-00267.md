**AI-Based Recruitment Test Excludes Applicants in Ghana**  
_2026-03-11 · real-world · Severity: High_

Ghana's Minority Leader, Alexander Afenyo-Markin, called for the abolition of AI-based aptitude tests in security service recruitment after widespread complaints. Many applicants, especially from rural areas, were disqualified due to difficulties with the AI system and irrelevant test questions, raising concerns about fairness and accessibility.
