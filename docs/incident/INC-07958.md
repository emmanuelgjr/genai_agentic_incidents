### INC-07958  [↗](/incident/INC-07958.html)

**AI Mass-Produces Finance Research Papers, Threatening Academic Integrity**  
_2026-05-28 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Researchers at Penn State and University of Rochester used AI to generate nearly 400 finance papers in 12 hours, raising concerns about overwhelming peer review systems, inflating citation counts, and eroding academic standards. The AI-generated papers are nearly indistinguishable from human work, posing risks to scientific integrity.
