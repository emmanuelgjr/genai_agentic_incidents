### INC-08006  [↗](/incident/INC-08006.html)

**China Implements AI Glasses for Agricultural Law Enforcement and Exam Security**  
_2026-06-01 · real-world · Severity: High_

In Chongqing, authorities deployed AI-enabled smart glasses for agricultural law enforcement, enabling real-time identification of banned substances and regulatory violations. Simultaneously, multiple Chinese regions tightened exam security by inspecting students' glasses to prevent cheating with AI-powered smart glasses, highlighting both the benefits and risks of AI in public administration and education.
