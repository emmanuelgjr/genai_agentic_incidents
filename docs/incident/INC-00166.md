**AI Facial Recognition Leads to 270 Arrests at São Paulo Stadiums**  
_2026-02-27 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Muralha Paulista program in São Paulo uses AI-powered facial recognition and license plate reading to identify fugitives at public events and on city streets. Over 100 monitored football matches, the system enabled police to arrest 270 individuals with outstanding warrants, demonstrating significant law enforcement impact through AI deployment.
