**China Unveils AI-Enabled Autonomous 'Machine Wolf' Combat Robots**  
_2026-05-12 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

China showcased AI-powered quadruped robots, dubbed 'machine wolves,' capable of autonomous, coordinated battlefield operations using a cloud-based AI system for real-time task distribution. These robots can perform reconnaissance, logistics, and firepower roles, raising concerns about potential risks of autonomous AI weapon deployment in military contexts.
