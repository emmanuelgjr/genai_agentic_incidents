### INC-07397  [↗](/incident/INC-07397.html)

**Facebook Reportedly Outed Sex Workers through Friend Recommendations**  
_2017-04-15 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#408`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/408/)

Facebook's 'People You May Know' (PYMK) recommendation feature caused serious privacy violations for sex workers who maintained separate identities for personal and professional lives. Leila, a sex worker who had only provided Facebook information from her personal identity, discovered that the platform was recommending her regular clients as potential friends, despite having no apparent connections through mutual friends, shared networks, or uploaded contacts. This exposed her real identity to clients and potentially exposed her professional identity to personal contacts. The incident affected multiple sex workers according to secret support groups. Ela Darling, an adult performer, reported similar issues where her professional account was recommended to family members, leading to harassment attempts and forcing her to delete her personal account. Facebook acknowledged using over 100 signals for PYMK recommendations but refused to explain the specific mechanisms or provide effective opt-out options. The company initially claimed users could disable the feature by changing friend request settings to 'No one' but later retracted this explanation, admitting it was incorrect information.
