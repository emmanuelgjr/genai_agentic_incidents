### INC-01430  [↗](/incident/INC-01430.html)

**Microsoft and Tech Giants Launch AI Health Chatbots, Raising Privacy and Safety Concerns**  
_2026-03-12 · real-world · Severity: High_

Microsoft and other tech companies have launched AI chatbots that aggregate and analyze users' sensitive health records from various sources. While these tools promise convenience and health insights, experts warn of significant privacy risks, potential data misuse, and the possibility of inaccurate medical advice leading to unnecessary anxiety or healthcare actions.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI02`, `ASI03`, `ASI05`
