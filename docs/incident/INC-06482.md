**Controversy Over Suzhou's AI-Driven 'Civilization Code' Scoring System**  
_2020-09-04 · real-world · Severity: High_

Suzhou launched the 'Civilization Code,' an AI-powered system assigning citizens a civility score based on behaviors like traffic violations and volunteer work, influencing access to social benefits. The initiative sparked public concern over fairness, privacy, and potential discrimination, though no actual harm has yet been reported.
