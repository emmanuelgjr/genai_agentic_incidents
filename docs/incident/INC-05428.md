### INC-05428  [↗](/incident/INC-05428.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfake Satellite Images Raise Misinformation Risks**  
_2021-04-21 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Researchers from the University of Washington warn that AI systems can now create highly realistic fake satellite images, a phenomenon called 'deepfake geography.' These falsified images could mislead users, threaten national security, and disrupt decision-making, prompting calls for geographic fact-checking and detection tools to mitigate potential harms.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
