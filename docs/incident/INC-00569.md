### INC-00569  [↗](/incident/INC-00569.html)

**AI-Related Data Breaches Expose Student Information in NYC Public Schools**  
_2026-05-04 · real-world · Severity: High_

An audit by New York State Comptroller revealed multiple data breaches in NYC public schools involving AI-enabled education technology vendors, such as Illuminate Education and PowerSchool. Lapses in data privacy policies, cybersecurity training, and breach reporting led to the exposure of sensitive student and staff information, raising concerns about AI use in education.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`
