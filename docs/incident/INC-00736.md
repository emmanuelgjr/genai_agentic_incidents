### INC-00736  [↗](/incident/INC-00736.html)

**CDU Proposes AI Cameras for Public Transport Safety in Hamburg**  
_2026-03-27 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The CDU has proposed equipping Hamburg's buses and trains with AI-powered cameras and assistance systems to enhance passenger safety by detecting threats in real time. A pilot project is planned, with assurances of data privacy compliance. The initiative aims to address rising incidents in public transport.
