### INC-00224  [↗](/incident/INC-00224.html)

**AI System Used in Germany to Detect and Remove Harmful Online Content for Youth Protection**  
_2026-03-05 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Landesanstalt für Kommunikation (LFK) in Baden-Württemberg, Germany, uses an AI-powered tool to systematically detect and flag harmful online content, such as hate speech, violence, and pornography, to protect children and adolescents. Human experts review flagged content and coordinate with platforms for removal, enhancing youth protection online.
