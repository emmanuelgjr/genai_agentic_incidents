**AI Industry Leaders Urge US Congress to Regulate Synthetic DNA Amid Biosecurity Risks**  
_2026-06-04 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Top executives from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, Microsoft AI, and Meta, alongside biosecurity experts, sent a joint letter to US Congress urging mandatory regulation of synthetic DNA and RNA orders. They warn that advanced AI could enable malicious actors to create biological weapons, highlighting urgent biosecurity concerns.
