**U.S. Establishes AI-Powered Autonomous Military Force for Latin America**  
_2026-04-21 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The U.S. Army has announced the creation of an autonomous military force using AI to support Southern Command operations in Central and South America and the Caribbean. The initiative aims to combat drug cartels and respond to crises, raising concerns about potential future harm from AI-enabled autonomous weapons systems.
