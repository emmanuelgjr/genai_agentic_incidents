### INC-04447  [↗](/incident/INC-04447.html)

**Amazon warns employees against sharing confidential data with ChatGPT**  
_2023-01-25 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Amazon corporate counsel warned employees not to share confidential information with ChatGPT after observing the model regurgitating data 'similar' to internal documents, raising concerns about employee data leaking into OpenAI training feedback loops.

**Affected:** Amazon  
**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`
