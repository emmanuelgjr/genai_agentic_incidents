### INC-00490  [↗](/incident/INC-00490.html)

**AI-Generated Parcel Scam Causes Financial Harm in France**  
_2026-03-16 · real-world · Severity: High_

A wave of parcel delivery scams in France uses generative AI to create highly realistic, personalized images in SMS phishing messages. This technological upgrade makes the fraud more convincing, leading to significant financial losses and data theft among victims. Authorities and transporters have raised alarms over the escalating threat.

**Attack vector:** `rce`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`
