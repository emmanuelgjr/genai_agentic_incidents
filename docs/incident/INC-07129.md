**Widespread Use of AI Facial Recognition Leads to Privacy Violations and Legal Action**  
_2020-04-22 · real-world · Severity: High_

Government agencies and private companies in the US have deployed AI-powered facial recognition and license plate readers for mass surveillance, leading to privacy violations and misidentification. Facebook faced a major class action settlement for illegally collecting biometric data, highlighting the real harms and legal risks of AI-driven surveillance technologies.
