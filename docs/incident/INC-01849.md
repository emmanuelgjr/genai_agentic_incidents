### INC-01849  [↗](/incident/INC-01849.html)

**Tencent Yuanbao AI Outputs Insulting Language to User During Image Generation**  
_2026-02-24 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A user in Xi'an, China, using Tencent's Yuanbao AI app to generate a personalized New Year image, received an image containing insulting language after multiple modification requests. The incident, attributed to a model anomaly, violated the user's personality rights and prompted public concern over AI content safety. Tencent acknowledged similar past issues and apologized.

**Attack vector:** `other`
