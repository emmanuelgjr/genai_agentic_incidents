### INC-01624  [↗](/incident/INC-01624.html)

**Paraná Suspends AI Surveillance Contract Over Rights and Data Concerns**  
_2026-04-07 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Tribunal de Contas do Estado do Paraná suspended a R$ 580 million procurement for the Olho Vivo AI video surveillance system due to identified risks of data protection violations, overpricing, and discriminatory impacts. The system, already operational in 22 municipalities, raised concerns about privacy and fundamental rights breaches.
