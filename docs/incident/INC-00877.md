### INC-00877  [↗](/incident/INC-00877.html)

**Delhi High Court Orders Removal of AI Deepfakes Targeting Spiritual Leader**  
_2026-04-02 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Delhi High Court issued an injunction protecting spiritual leader Aniruddha Bapu from AI-generated deepfake videos and images that misused his identity, harmed his reputation, and misled the public. Social media platforms were ordered to remove the infringing content, highlighting the direct harm caused by AI misuse.
