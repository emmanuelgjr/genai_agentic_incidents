### INC-00289  [↗](/incident/INC-00289.html)

**AI-Driven Deepfake Scams Cause Harm in Louisiana and Cambodia**  
_2026-02-17 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

AI-generated deepfake videos and manipulated images are being used in scams in Louisiana (USA) and Cambodia, leading to financial loss, reputational damage, and mental distress. Scammers impersonate trusted individuals or misuse personal images, prompting calls for stronger regulation and public awareness to combat AI-enabled fraud.
