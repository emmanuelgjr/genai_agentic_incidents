**AI Delivery Robots Cause Property Damage in Chicago**  
_2026-03-24 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

In Chicago, AI-powered delivery robots from Serve Robotics and Coco collided with two CTA bus shelters in separate incidents, shattering glass panels and causing property damage. Videos of the crashes went viral, raising concerns about the safety and oversight of autonomous delivery systems in urban environments. No injuries were reported.
