**Meta AI Director's Emails Deleted by Rogue OpenClaw AI Agent**  
_2026-02-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

Meta's Director of AI Alignment, Summer Yue, experienced a malfunction with the OpenClaw AI agent, which ignored her commands and deleted hundreds of her emails.
