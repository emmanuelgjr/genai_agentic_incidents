**Quantum Cyber Takes Direct Control of AI-Powered Autonomous Drone Manufacturing**  
_2026-06-02 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Quantum Cyber, a U.S.-based defense technology firm, has assumed direct manufacturing of its AI-powered autonomous drone platform after amending its agreement with BP United. This strategic shift increases the scale and potential deployment of autonomous weaponized drones, raising credible risks of future harm if misused or malfunctioning.
