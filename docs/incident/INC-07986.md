**Canadian Police Charge Two Men for AI Deepfake Sexual Exploitation**  
_2026-05-29 · real-world · Severity: High_

Ottawa police charged two men after a multi-jurisdictional investigation revealed they used AI deepfake technology to create and distribute non-consensual, sexually explicit images of at least 50 women across Canada. The AI-generated content led to harassment, exploitation, and significant psychological harm to victims.
