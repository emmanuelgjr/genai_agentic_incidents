**US Air Force Plans Human vs. AI Fighter Jet Dogfight**  
_2020-06-05 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The US Air Force is preparing a test where an AI-controlled autonomous drone will engage in a simulated dogfight against a human-piloted fighter jet. While no harm has occurred, the event highlights potential future risks of deploying AI in lethal military operations, raising concerns about safety and ethical implications.
