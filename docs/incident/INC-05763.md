### INC-05763  [↗](/incident/INC-05763.html)

**Microsoft Cancels AI Chatbot Project Simulating Conversations with the Deceased**  
_2021-01-28 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Microsoft halted development of an AI chatbot designed to simulate conversations with deceased individuals by using their digital data and social media profiles. The project, deemed 'disturbing' by company leadership, was abandoned due to ethical concerns over potential emotional harm and privacy violations.
