**Minnesota Lawmakers Propose Bipartisan AI Regulations to Protect Minors**  
_2026-03-10 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Minnesota legislators, in a rare bipartisan effort, are considering bills to regulate AI, including banning minors from using AI chatbots and requiring disclosure when interacting with AI. The proposed measures aim to prevent potential harms to children and consumers, reflecting growing concerns over unregulated AI deployment.
