**Iran Unveils AI-Enabled Swarming Suicide Drones for Military Use**  
_2021-04-25 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Iranian Army unveiled new AI-powered suicide drones capable of coordinated swarm attacks using networked flight control systems. These autonomous drones, designed for offensive military operations, can identify, surveil, and destroy targets, raising significant concerns about the potential for AI-driven harm in armed conflict.
