**Rheinmetall and Australian Partners Develop Autonomous Military Vehicle Technologies**  
_2020-02-27 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Rheinmetall, in collaboration with Australian research institutions and government agencies, has launched the Autonomous Combat Warrior program to develop AI-enabled robotics and automated military vehicle technologies. While no harm has occurred, the project raises future risks associated with autonomous military systems.
