### INC-00414  [↗](/incident/INC-00414.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfakes Cause Fraud and Undermine Democracy in Germany**  
_2026-03-31 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

German journalist Eckart von Hirschhausen warns that AI-generated deepfakes are enabling identity misuse, consumer fraud, and the spread of fake medical products, directly harming individuals and eroding trust in truth and democracy. The incidents highlight the urgent need for regulation and public awareness in Germany.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`

**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
