**Minnesota Bans AI 'Nudification' Apps After Deepfake Harms**  
_2026-04-30 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Minnesota lawmakers passed the nation's first ban on AI-powered 'nudification' apps, which use generative AI to create nonconsensual fake nude images. The legislation responds to widespread harm, including digital sexual abuse and rights violations, by prohibiting such apps and allowing victims to sue for damages.
