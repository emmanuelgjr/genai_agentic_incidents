**AI-Generated Political Video Sparks Outrage in Hungary**  
_2026-02-19 · real-world · Severity: High_

Hungary's ruling Fidesz party released an AI-generated campaign video depicting a child searching for her father, who is executed at the front. The video, intended to manipulate emotions and spread fear, has drawn widespread condemnation for violating ethical norms and causing psychological harm through manipulative AI use.
