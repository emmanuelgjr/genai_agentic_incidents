### INC-01116  [↗](/incident/INC-01116.html)

**Google Gemini AI Glitch Deletes User Chat Histories**  
_2026-02-24 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A technical malfunction in Google's Gemini AI platform caused the unexpected deletion of chat histories for numerous users worldwide, affecting both free and paid subscribers. Google acknowledged the issue, is working on a fix, and promised to restore all deleted conversations in an upcoming update.

**Attack vector:** `other`
