**Ford Recalls 254,640 SUVs in US Over AI-Driven Safety Feature Malfunction**  
_2026-03-24 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Ford is recalling 254,640 SUVs in the US due to a software defect in AI-powered image processing modules, causing loss of rearview camera and advanced driver assistance features. The malfunction increases crash risk, prompting a recall and free software update to restore safety functions.
