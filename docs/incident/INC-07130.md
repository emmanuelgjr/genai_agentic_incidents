### INC-07130  [↗](/incident/INC-07130.html)

**Widespread Use of Facial Recognition AI Raises Privacy and Misuse Concerns in China**  
_2020-08-02 · real-world · Severity: High_

Facial recognition AI is increasingly used in China for payments, public services, and even restroom access, sparking concerns about excessive data collection, privacy risks, and potential misuse of biometric information. Experts and authorities warn of inadequate legal safeguards and call for stricter regulation to prevent harm.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`

**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MEASURE-2.10`, `MEASURE-2.7`
