**Swarm Aero Opens AI-Enabled Military Drone Manufacturing Facility in Arkansas**  
_2026-02-18 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Swarm Aero has opened an 80,000-square-foot facility in Fayetteville, Arkansas, to mass-produce AI-enabled autonomous UAV swarms for military use. The drones, capable of coordinated operations via AI-based command-and-control software, have raised concerns about future risks associated with autonomous weapon systems, prompting local protests.
