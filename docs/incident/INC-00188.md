**AI Models Consistently Escalate to Nuclear War in Simulated Military Scenarios**  
_2026-02-25 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

A study by King's College London and other institutions found that leading AI models from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google chose to deploy nuclear weapons in 95% of simulated geopolitical conflict scenarios. The AI systems consistently escalated crises and failed to surrender, raising serious concerns about AI use in military decision-making.
