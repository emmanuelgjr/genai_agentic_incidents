### INC-05653  [↗](/incident/INC-05653.html)

**French Tax Authority Deploys AI to Monitor Social Media for Tax Fraud**  
_2021-02-17 · real-world · Severity: High_

The French tax authority has begun a three-year experimental program using AI algorithms to analyze publicly available social media and platform data to detect tax fraud. While intended to identify undeclared income and false domiciliation, the initiative has raised significant privacy and rights concerns from regulatory bodies.
