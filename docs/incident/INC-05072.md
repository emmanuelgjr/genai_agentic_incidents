**Christopher Gatlin facial-recognition wrongful arrest**  
_2022 · real-world · Severity: High_

St. Louis transit-police arrested Christopher Gatlin based on a facial-recognition 'match' that placed him at a violent crime scene he could not have attended, illustrating the harms of unverified AI identification in criminal justice.
