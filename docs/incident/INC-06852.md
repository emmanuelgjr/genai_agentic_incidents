_2020-12-08 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

A political dispute has erupted within Germany's ruling coalition over the planned acquisition of AI-enabled armed drones for the Bundeswehr. SPD leaders cite insufficient ethical and legal debate, resisting Defense Minister Kramp-Karrenbauer's push for procurement, highlighting concerns about future risks from deploying autonomous weapon systems.
