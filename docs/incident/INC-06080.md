### INC-06080  [↗](/incident/INC-06080.html)

**Tesla Autopilot Misuse and Overstated Capabilities Lead to Fatalities and Public Concern**  
_2021-05-14 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Multiple incidents involving Tesla's Autopilot AI system have resulted in fatalities and public alarm. Overstatements by Elon Musk about the system's capabilities, combined with misuse—such as drivers not being at the wheel—have led to deadly crashes and arrests, highlighting the risks of overreliance and inadequate supervision of AI-driven vehicles.
