### INC-05836  [↗](/incident/INC-05836.html)

**Russia Deploys AI-Enabled Drones and Robotic Systems in Military Operations**  
_2021-03-13 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The Russian military is rapidly deploying AI-enabled drones and robotic systems across airborne and engineering units for reconnaissance, precision strikes, mine clearance, and combat support. These autonomous and semi-autonomous systems have been actively used in conflict zones, directly contributing to enhanced combat capabilities and resulting in harm to persons and property.
