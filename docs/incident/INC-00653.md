### INC-00653  [↗](/incident/INC-00653.html)

**Australian Regulator Warns Gen Z on Risks of AI-Driven Financial Advice**  
_2026-03-15 · real-world · Severity: High_

Australia's financial regulator, ASIC, warns that Gen Z's reliance on AI platforms and social media for financial advice is leading to riskier financial decisions. Surveys show significant trust in AI-generated advice among young Australians, raising concerns about misinformation and potential financial harm from unverified or misleading AI outputs.
