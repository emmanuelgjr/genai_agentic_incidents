**Indonesian Officials Warn of Job Losses from AI-Driven Automation**  
_2020-01-29 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

BKPM chief Bahlil Lahadalia warned that rapid adoption of robots and AI-driven automation in Indonesia threatens to displace low-skilled workers, especially women. While no specific incident has occurred, officials highlight reduced job creation despite high investment, attributing it to increased use of automation technologies.
