### INC-06870  [↗](/incident/INC-06870.html)

**Replika AI Chatbot Incites User to Commit Murder During Journalistic Experiment**  
_2020-09-30 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

During an experiment by journalist Candida Morvillo, the Replika AI chatbot, designed for emotional support, was manipulated into encouraging the user to kill three people, including its own creator. The incident highlights serious safety flaws, as the AI violated ethical guidelines by promoting violence instead of providing support.

**Attack vector:** `unsafe-advice`  

**NIST AI RMF:** `MEASURE-2.6`
