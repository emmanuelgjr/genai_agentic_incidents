**US Army Receives First Autonomous-Ready Black Hawk Helicopter**  
_2026-03-20 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The US Army has received its first H-60Mx Black Hawk helicopter equipped with an AI-driven autonomy suite, enabling fully autonomous or piloted flight. Developed with DARPA and Sikorsky, the aircraft will undergo rigorous testing, marking a significant step toward scaling autonomous military aviation. No harm has occurred yet.
