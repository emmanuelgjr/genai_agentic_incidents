**Italy to Test AI-Enabled Michelangelo Air Defense System in Ukraine**  
_2026-03-12 · real-world · Severity: High_

Italian company Leonardo will test its AI-powered Michelangelo air defense system in Ukraine by the end of 2026, marking its first real-world deployment in an active conflict zone. The system uses AI to detect, track, and neutralize threats, raising potential future risks if malfunctioning or misused.
