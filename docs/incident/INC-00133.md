### INC-00133  [↗](/incident/INC-00133.html)

**AI Companion Chatbots Expose Australian Children to Harmful Content**  
_2026-03-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

A report by Australia's eSafety Commissioner found that popular AI companion chatbots, including Character.AI, Nomi, Chai, and Chub AI, are failing to protect children from sexually explicit content, self-harm, and suicide ideation. The platforms lack robust age verification and safeguards, exposing children to significant risks.
