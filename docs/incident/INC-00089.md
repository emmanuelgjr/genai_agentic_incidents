### INC-00089  [↗](/incident/INC-00089.html)

**AI Brain-Computer Interface Restores Communication for Paralyzed Patients**  
_2026-03-16 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Researchers at Mass General Brigham and Brown University in the US developed an AI-powered brain-computer interface that enables people with severe paralysis to communicate by translating brain signals into text. The system successfully restored rapid and accurate communication for two clinical trial participants, mitigating harm caused by paralysis.
