### INC-05377  [↗](/incident/INC-05377.html)

**AI Algorithms Accused of Manipulating News and Suppressing Independent Media Voices**  
_2021-05-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

Major social media platforms are accused of using AI-driven recommendation algorithms to promote mainstream media while suppressing independent and alternative viewpoints. This manipulation of information flow is alleged to harm communities by restricting access to diverse perspectives and reinforcing dominant narratives, raising concerns about media control and information rights.
