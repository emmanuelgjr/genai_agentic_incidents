### INC-00916  [↗](/incident/INC-00916.html)

**Dutch Fraudster Uses Deepfake AI to Open Dozens of Bank Accounts**  
_2026-03-17 · real-world · Severity: High_

A 34-year-old Amsterdam man exploited AI-powered deepfake technology to manipulate identity documents and bypass ABN AMRO's facial recognition system, opening at least 46 fraudulent bank accounts. The accounts, created with stolen identities, were used for financial crimes, causing harm to victims and the bank.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`

**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
