### INC-06789  [↗](/incident/INC-06789.html)

**Microsoft Patents AI Technology to Create Virtual Replicas of Deceased Individuals**  
_2020-12-10 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Microsoft has patented an AI technology that uses personal data, including voice recordings, photos, and social media activity, to create chatbots that mimic real or deceased people. Experts warn this could lead to privacy violations, identity theft, and cybercrime, though no actual harm has yet been reported.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`
