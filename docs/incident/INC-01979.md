### INC-01979  [↗](/incident/INC-01979.html)

**Unregulated AI Toys Expose Children to Inappropriate Content and Privacy Risks**  
_2026-05-08 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A surge in unregulated AI-powered children's toys has led to documented harms, including exposure to inappropriate content, privacy violations, and potential developmental issues. Researchers and advocates have found these toys discussing sensitive topics and displaying manipulative behaviors, prompting legislative proposals in the US for stricter oversight and bans.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`  

**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-4.1`, `MEASURE-2.10`
