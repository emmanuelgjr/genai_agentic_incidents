**UK Watchdog Warns of AI Algorithm Risks to Consumers and Competition**  
_2021-01-19 · real-world · Severity: High_

The UK Competition and Markets Authority (CMA) warns that AI-powered algorithms used by online platforms can manipulate consumers, reduce competition, and lead to higher prices. The CMA is investigating these risks and seeking evidence to inform potential regulation, highlighting the potential for significant consumer and market harm.
