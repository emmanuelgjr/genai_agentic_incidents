**AI-Powered WiFi Systems Enable Through-Wall Human Detection, Raising Privacy and Surveillance Concerns**  
_2026-03-02 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI systems developed by institutions like MIT and featured in projects such as WiFi DensePose can analyze WiFi signals to detect human poses and movements through walls without cameras. While offering benefits for security and rescue, these technologies raise significant privacy and surveillance risks, especially in military and law enforcement contexts.
