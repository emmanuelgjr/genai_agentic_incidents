### INC-07146  [↗](/incident/INC-07146.html)

**YouTube's Biased AI Recommendations Spread Health Misinformation and Worsen Disparities**  
_2020-07-14 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

YouTube's AI-driven recommendation algorithms promote misleading or medically invalid health videos, especially to users with limited health literacy. This bias exposes vulnerable populations to misinformation, exacerbating health disparities and potentially leading to harmful health decisions and outcomes.

**Attack vector:** `misinformation`

**NIST AI RMF:** `MEASURE-2.8`
