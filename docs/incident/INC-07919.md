**Generative AI Adoption Causes Sharp Decline in Entry-Level Hiring in the U.S.**  
_2026-05-26 · real-world · Severity: High_

Studies from Harvard and Stanford reveal that U.S. companies adopting generative AI have reduced entry-level hiring by up to 80% since 2023, disproportionately impacting young and early-career workers. While overall job losses remain modest, AI-driven automation is significantly harming employment opportunities for junior staff across industries.
