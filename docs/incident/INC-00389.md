### INC-00389  [↗](/incident/INC-00389.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfake Targets Slovenian Politicians, Prompting Legal Action Consideration**  
_2026-03-23 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Influencer Aleksandar Repić used AI to create and circulate a deepfake video falsely depicting Slovenian politicians Asta Vrečko and Luka Mesec making obscene gestures after elections. The manipulated content caused reputational harm, prompting Vrečko to consider legal action and raising concerns about AI-driven misinformation in Slovenia.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
