**Deployment of AI Robot Dogs for World Cup Security Raises Surveillance Concerns in Dallas**  
_2026-05-29 · real-world · Severity: High_

Boston Dynamics' AI-powered robot dogs, branded by Hyundai, are being deployed for security inspections at FIFA World Cup venues in Dallas, Texas. While public fears and viral videos allege facial recognition and surveillance, the companies deny such capabilities. No harm has occurred, but privacy concerns persist.
