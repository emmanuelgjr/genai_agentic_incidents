### INC-00361  [↗](/incident/INC-00361.html)

**AI-Facilitated Sexual Violence Against Children in Brazil**  
_2026-03-04 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A UNICEF-led report reveals that 19% of Brazilian children and adolescents (about 3 million) experienced technology-facilitated sexual violence in one year. AI systems were used to manipulate images, generate sexualized content, and enable abuse via social media and messaging platforms, causing significant psychological harm.

**Attack vector:** `other`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`
