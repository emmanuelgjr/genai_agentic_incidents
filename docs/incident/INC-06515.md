**Dubai Deploys Facial Recognition AI on Public Transport, Raising Privacy Concerns**  
_2020-10-25 · real-world · Severity: High_

Dubai has introduced AI-powered facial recognition on public transport to enhance security, including simulated use in attack scenarios. While no harm has been reported, the deployment raises significant privacy and human rights concerns due to the potential for misuse or rights violations inherent in such surveillance technologies.
