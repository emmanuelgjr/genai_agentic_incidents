**Waymo Wins $128 Million in Trade Secrets Case Against Uber Over Self-Driving AI Technology**  
_2020-01-08 · real-world · Severity: High_

Waymo, Alphabet's autonomous driving unit, was awarded $128 million after arbitrators found that former employees stole self-driving AI technology and brought it to Uber. The legal battle centered on the misappropriation of AI trade secrets, highlighting intellectual property violations in the development of autonomous vehicle systems.
