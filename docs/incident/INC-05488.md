### INC-05488  [↗](/incident/INC-05488.html)

**ARAV Develops AI-Based Autonomous Control Systems for Construction Machinery**  
_2021-03-08 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Tokyo University spin-off ARAV is developing AI-driven autonomous and remote control systems for construction machinery, supported by a major investment. While aiming to improve labor conditions and efficiency at construction sites, the deployment of such AI systems could plausibly pose future risks if malfunctions or misuse occur.
