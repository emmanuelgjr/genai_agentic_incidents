**New York Times Fires Freelance Critic for AI-Assisted Plagiarism in Book Review**  
_2026-03-31 · real-world · Severity: High_

The New York Times severed ties with freelance journalist Alex Preston after discovering he used AI to draft a book review that included plagiarized material from a Guardian review. The AI tool's use led to a breach of intellectual property rights and journalistic standards, prompting the paper's action.
