**Amnesty International Calls for Ban on Police Facial Recognition in Austria**  
_2021-05-04 · real-world · Severity: High_

Amnesty International has launched a campaign urging Austria to ban police use of facial recognition AI, citing risks of privacy violations, discrimination against marginalized groups, and suppression of protest and free expression. The organization warns these harms outweigh any security benefits provided by the technology.
