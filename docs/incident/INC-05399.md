**AI-Assisted Identification of US Capitol Rioters Using Facial Recognition and Cell Data**  
_2021-01-08 · real-world · Severity: High_

US authorities used AI-powered facial recognition and cellphone data analysis to identify and prosecute individuals involved in the January 6 Capitol riot. These technologies enabled law enforcement to track suspects, leading to legal charges and social consequences, while raising concerns about privacy and civil rights.
