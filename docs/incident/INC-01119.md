### INC-01119  [↗](/incident/INC-01119.html)

**Google Maps Glitch Causes Wrong-Way Traffic and Safety Hazards in Toronto**  
_2026-05-04 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A Google Maps AI routing error in Toronto misdirected drivers to travel the wrong way on a one-way street for several days, leading to near-misses and traffic chaos. Residents and officials intervened with signage and contacted Google, which eventually corrected the malfunction. The incident raised concerns about AI navigation reliability and public safety.
