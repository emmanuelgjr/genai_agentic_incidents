### INC-08023  [↗](/incident/INC-08023.html)

**AI Drug Monitoring System Fails to Detect Nurse's Fentanyl Theft in Tennessee Hospital**  
_2026-06-01 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

At Erlanger Baroness Hospital in Chattanooga, Tennessee, an AI-powered medication monitoring system, Sentri7, failed to detect months of fentanyl theft by a nurse. The nurse's drug diversion went unnoticed by the AI, allowing ongoing abuse and raising concerns about the reliability of AI in hospital drug monitoring.
