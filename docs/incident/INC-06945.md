### INC-06945  [↗](/incident/INC-06945.html)

**Tech Giants and NGOs Challenge NSO Group Over AI-Driven Pegasus Spyware Abuse**  
_2020-12-22 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Israeli firm NSO Group's AI-enabled Pegasus spyware exploited WhatsApp vulnerabilities to hack over 1,400 devices, targeting journalists and civil society members. Major tech companies and NGOs joined legal actions against NSO, citing privacy violations and human rights abuses resulting from the AI system's deployment.
