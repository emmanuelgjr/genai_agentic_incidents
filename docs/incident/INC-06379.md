**Austrian Data Protection Authority Halts Job Seeker Algorithm Over Legal and Discrimination Concerns**  
_2020-08-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

Austria's Data Protection Authority blocked the nationwide rollout of the AMS algorithm, an AI system used to categorize unemployed people and influence access to job training. The decision cites lack of legal basis, insufficient oversight, and risks of discrimination, halting deployment amid concerns over potential rights violations.
