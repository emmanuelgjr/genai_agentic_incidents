### INC-07059  [↗](/incident/INC-07059.html)

**Ukraine's Justice Ministry Deploys AI 'Kassandra' for Recidivism Risk Assessment**  
_2020-09-12 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Ukraine's Ministry of Justice has introduced an AI system, 'Kassandra', to automate the assessment of recidivism risk in criminal cases. The tool generates risk scores to support probation officers and judges in sentencing decisions, raising concerns about potential bias and rights violations if the system is inaccurate or misused.

**Attack vector:** `other`
