### INC-07780  [↗](/incident/INC-07780.html)

**Cisco Study Reveals Major AI Models Vulnerable to Multi-Turn Adversarial Attacks**  
_2026-05-27 · real-world · Severity: High_

Cisco researchers found that leading proprietary AI language models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Amazon, and xAI are significantly more vulnerable to multi-turn adversarial prompt attacks than previously reported. No tested model was immune, highlighting a critical gap in current safety benchmarks and raising concerns about potential misuse and future harm.

**Attack vector:** `prompt-injection`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM01`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI01`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0051`, `AML.T0058`
