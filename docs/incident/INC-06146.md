### INC-06146  [↗](/incident/INC-06146.html)

**Ukraine Unveils AI-Controlled Stealth Combat Drone ACE ONE**  
_2021-06-15 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Ukrainian developers presented the ACE ONE, a next-generation stealth combat drone managed by an AI system capable of controlling swarms. Designed for offensive and defensive military operations, it can carry up to one ton of weaponry, posing future risks of AI-enabled autonomous warfare and potential harm if deployed.
