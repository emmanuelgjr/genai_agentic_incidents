**Japan Responds to AI Cybersecurity Threats from Anthropic's Mythos Model**  
_2026-05-08 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Japan is negotiating with Anthropic for access to its advanced AI model, Claude Mythos, amid concerns it could be exploited for cyberattacks on critical infrastructure. The government has ordered a comprehensive cybersecurity strategy review to address the risks posed by the model's vulnerability-finding capabilities and potential misuse.
