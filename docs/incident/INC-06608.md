**Facial Recognition Toilet Paper Dispenser in Dongguan Halted Over Privacy Concerns**  
_2020-12-06 · real-world · Severity: High_

A public restroom in Dongguan, China, installed an AI-powered facial recognition dispenser to limit toilet paper use, sparking public privacy concerns. Authorities confirmed the device lacked network connectivity and deleted images promptly, but discontinued its use and reverted to traditional paper distribution following public outcry.
