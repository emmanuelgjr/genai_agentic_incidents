### INC-06854  [↗](/incident/INC-06854.html)

**Portugal Not Among Countries Seeking Ban on Autonomous Killer Robots**  
_2020-08-10 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A Human Rights Watch report highlights that Portugal, while supporting negotiations on regulating autonomous lethal weapons, is not among the 30 countries explicitly calling for a ban on fully autonomous AI-powered weapons. The report underscores ongoing international concerns about the potential risks of such systems.
