**Tesla Robotaxis Involved in Incidents and Injuries in Texas; Waymo Suspends Highway Operations After AI Malfunction**  
_2026-05-26 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Tesla's autonomous robotaxis in Texas have been involved in 17 incidents, including three with injuries, raising safety concerns about their AI systems. Meanwhile, Waymo suspended its robotaxi highway operations in several U.S. cities after an AI malfunction caused a vehicle to become stranded on a flooded road, prompting a software update.
