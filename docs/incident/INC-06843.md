### INC-06843  [↗](/incident/INC-06843.html)

**PimEyes Facial Recognition Tool Raises Global Privacy Concerns**  
_2020-06-10 · real-world · Severity: High_

PimEyes, a Polish AI-powered facial recognition search engine, enables users to find and track individuals online using just a photo. The service has sparked widespread criticism from privacy experts, who warn it facilitates privacy violations, stalking, and unauthorized surveillance by allowing identification and monitoring without consent.
