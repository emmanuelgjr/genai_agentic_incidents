**India's AI Data Centre Boom Raises Environmental Concerns**  
_2026-02-19 · real-world · Severity: High_

India's rapid expansion of AI data centres, driven by major tech companies, is raising concerns about future water and energy shortages. While no direct harm has occurred yet, experts warn that the massive resource demands of these centres could strain India's already limited environmental resources if sustainability measures are not implemented.
