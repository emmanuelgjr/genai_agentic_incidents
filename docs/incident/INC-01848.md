### INC-01848  [↗](/incident/INC-01848.html)

**Tenable Warns of Rising AI Security Risks in Cloud Environments**  
_2026-02-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

Tenable's 2026 report highlights that rapid AI adoption and integration of third-party AI packages in cloud environments are creating critical security vulnerabilities. Excessive permissions, dormant accounts, and poor identity controls expose organizations to significant cyber risks, with non-human AI identities now posing greater threats than human users.

**Attack vector:** `supply-chain`
