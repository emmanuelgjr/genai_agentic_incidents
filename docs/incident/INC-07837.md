### INC-07837  [↗](/incident/INC-07837.html)

**Potential Risks of Autonomous AI Shopping Agents Highlighted**  
_2026-05-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

Articles discuss the growing use of autonomous AI shopping agents by companies like Amazon and Google, which can independently make purchases and payments for users. Experts warn of potential risks including financial loss, privacy breaches, fraud, and legal liability, though no actual incidents have yet occurred. Legal questions remain, especially in Germany.

**Attack vector:** `rce`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`
