### INC-07837  [↗](/incident/INC-07837.html)

**AI Deepfakes Undermine Identity Verification, Fueling Fraud in the U.S.**  
_2026-05-20 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Research by Veriff and Kantar reveals that Americans struggle to distinguish AI-generated deepfakes from real images, with detection accuracy barely above random guessing. This inability has enabled synthetic identity fraud, causing significant financial harm to businesses relying on image- and video-based identity verification.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
