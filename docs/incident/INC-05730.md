**Japan Trials 5G-Enabled Autonomous Buses, Raising Future AI Safety Concerns**  
_2021-01-13 · real-world · Severity: High_

Multiple Japanese organizations, including NEC, JR East, and Gunma University, are conducting public road trials of AI-driven autonomous buses using 5G technology. While no harm has occurred, these demonstrations highlight plausible future risks of accidents or malfunctions as AI systems move toward higher autonomy in public transportation.
