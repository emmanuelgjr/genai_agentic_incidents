### INC-00097  [↗](/incident/INC-00097.html)

**AI Chatbot Generates Millions of Non-Consensual Deepfake Images, Triggering Global Privacy Concerns**  
_2026-03-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

An AI chatbot enabled users to upload photos of real people, including children and women, and generate non-consensual intimate deepfake images. Within 11 days, about 3 million such images were produced, causing severe privacy violations and prompting global regulatory action and bans in several jurisdictions.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
