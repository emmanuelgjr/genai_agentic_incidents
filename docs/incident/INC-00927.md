**Egypt Plans AI-Driven Military Weapons Development**  
_2026-02-28 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Egypt's Minister of State for Military Production, Salah Suleiman Gomblat, announced plans to integrate artificial intelligence into the development of weapons, equipment, and ammunition. The initiative aims to modernize military production and enhance national defense capabilities, raising credible future risks associated with AI-enabled weaponry. No actual harm has occurred yet.
