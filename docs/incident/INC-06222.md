### INC-06222  [↗](/incident/INC-06222.html)

**AI Camera System Deployed in Japanese Banks to Prevent Phone Fraud**  
_2020-07-01 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

JVC Kenwood and Bizright Technology deployed an AI-powered edge camera system in Hokuyo Bank branches to detect suspicious behavior, such as making phone calls at ATMs, which may indicate phone fraud. The system notifies staff to intervene, aiming to prevent financial harm from scams without recording or transmitting video data.

**Attack vector:** `other`  

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`
