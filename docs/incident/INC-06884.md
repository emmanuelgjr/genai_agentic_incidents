### INC-06884  [↗](/incident/INC-06884.html)

**Robotic Research to Test Fully Autonomous Unmanned Shuttles Without Onboard Attendants**  
_2020-02-22 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Robotic Research LLC announced plans to begin testing fully autonomous, unmanned low-speed shuttles without onboard safety attendants. The company will initially use fixed on-site monitoring, with the goal of remote supervision, raising potential safety concerns if the AI-based autonomy systems malfunction during public operation.
