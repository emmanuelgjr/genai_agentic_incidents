### INC-05627  [↗](/incident/INC-05627.html)

**Facebook's AI Moderation System Censors Vaccine Hesitancy Content Globally**  
_2021-05-25 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Facebook deployed AI algorithms to identify and demote user comments expressing vaccine hesitancy, regardless of factual accuracy, as revealed by whistleblowers and leaked documents. This system, tested on millions of users, led to widespread censorship and suppression of information, raising concerns over violations of free speech and access to information.
