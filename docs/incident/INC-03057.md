### INC-03057  [↗](/incident/INC-03057.html)

**Purported AI-Generated Deepfake of Steven Bartlett Reportedly Used to Promote Fake WhatsApp Investment Group**  
_2025-04-23 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1239`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1239/)

A purported deepfake video of entrepreneur and BBC Dragon&#x27;s Den investor Steven Bartlett circulated on Instagram in April 2025, allegedly claiming to offer exclusive stock tips and inviting users to join a WhatsApp trading group. The reported video referenced U.S. tariff policies to entice viewers with promises of rapid returns. Bartlett&#x27;s team confirmed the video was fake and worked with platforms to remove it, warning that scammers were attempting to harvest contact details for further fraud.
