**California Colleges' AI Chatbots Provide Inaccurate Information, Frustrating Students**  
_2026-03-06 · real-world · Severity: High_

California community colleges have spent millions on AI-powered chatbots to assist students with admissions and campus services. However, these chatbots frequently provide outdated or incorrect information, leading to student frustration and reliance on unofficial sources, thereby hindering access to essential educational support.
