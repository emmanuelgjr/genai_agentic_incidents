**Ukraine Plans AI-Driven Autonomous Combat Systems for Battlefield Use**  
_2026-04-23 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Ukrainian officials, led by Kyrylo Budanov, announced plans to fully integrate artificial intelligence into autonomous combat systems capable of independently identifying targets and maneuvering. This technological advancement, intended for use on the battlefield, raises credible risks of harm due to the deployment of AI in warfare.
