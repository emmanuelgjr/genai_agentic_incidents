### INC-00401  [↗](/incident/INC-00401.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfake Videos Harm Chinese Celebrities**  
_2026-03-10 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI-generated deepfake videos of Chinese celebrities, including Wang Jinsong and Tian Xuning, have led to unauthorized endorsements, defamation, and commercial exploitation. These videos, often sold online, violate portrait and reputation rights, prompting legal actions and calls for stronger platform regulation and AI content labeling in China.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
