**French Court Blocks Facial Recognition in High Schools Over Privacy Concerns**  
_2020-02-27 · real-world · Severity: High_

A French administrative court prohibited the experimental use of AI-powered facial recognition systems for student identification in two high schools, citing privacy, consent, and legal competence issues. The decision, prompted by civil rights groups, averted potential privacy violations and set a precedent for AI surveillance in public spaces.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`
