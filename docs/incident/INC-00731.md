### INC-00731  [↗](/incident/INC-00731.html)

**Canva AI Tool Replaces 'Palestine' with 'Ukraine' in User Designs, Prompting Apology**  
_2026-04-27 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Canva's AI-powered Magic Layers tool was found to automatically replace the word 'Palestine' with 'Ukraine' in user-generated designs, sparking accusations of censorship and bias. The issue, which did not affect related terms like 'Gaza,' caused distress among users. Canva has apologized and implemented fixes to prevent recurrence.
