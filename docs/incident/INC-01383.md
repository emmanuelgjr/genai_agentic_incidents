**MCP Salesforce Connector — Vulnerability (CVE-2026-25650)**  
_2026-02 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: High_

CVEs: `CVE-2026-25650`
CWEs: `CWE-200`
CVSS: **7.5** — `CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:U/C:H/I:N/A:N`
