### INC-01749  [↗](/incident/INC-01749.html)

**Russia Proposes Sweeping Regulations to Restrict Foreign AI Tools**  
_2026-03-20 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Russia's Ministry for Digital Development has proposed regulations that could ban or restrict foreign AI tools like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini if they fail to comply with data localization and content control requirements. The rules aim to protect citizens and promote domestic AI, raising concerns about censorship and restricted access.
