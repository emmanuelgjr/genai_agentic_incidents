### INC-07716  [↗](/incident/INC-07716.html)

**Experts Warn of AI-Driven Fake News Risks in Brazilian Elections**  
_2026-05-16 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Brazilian electoral authorities and experts warn that AI could intensify the spread of fake news during upcoming elections, especially amid political polarization and low digital literacy. The Tribunal Superior Eleitoral is preparing to address these risks, but no AI-driven incidents have yet occurred.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
