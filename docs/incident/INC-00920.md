**Dutch Politician Excluded After AI-Retouched Campaign Photo Causes Controversy**  
_2026-03-31 · real-world · Severity: High_

Patricia Reichman, a local politician in Rotterdam, Netherlands, was excluded from her party, Leefbaar Rotterdam, after using AI to heavily retouch her campaign photo. The AI-generated image, which made her appear much younger and altered her features, sparked public backlash and accusations of misleading voters.
