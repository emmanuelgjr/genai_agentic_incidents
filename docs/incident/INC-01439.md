### INC-01439  [↗](/incident/INC-01439.html)

**Microsoft Research Reveals AI Models Corrupt Documents in Delegated Workflows**  
_2026-05-10 · real-world · Severity: High_

Microsoft researchers found that leading large language models (LLMs), including Gemini 3.1 Pro, Claude Opus 4.6, and GPT 5.4, corrupt up to 25% of document content during extended editing workflows. The study highlights that current AI systems introduce severe, compounding errors, making them unreliable for autonomous document handling.

**Attack vector:** `other`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MEASURE-2.8`
