**AI-Generated Fake Posters Cause Misinformation for 'Singer 2026'**  
_2026-04-28 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

AI-generated posters falsely announcing the lineup for the Chinese music show 'Singer 2026' circulated online, misleading fans and even artists. The realistic visuals led to widespread confusion and reputational harm, prompting official denials and highlighting the risks of AI-driven misinformation in entertainment.
