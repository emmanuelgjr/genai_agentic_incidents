**Clearview AI's Facial Recognition System Linked to Far-Right Extremists and Human Rights Violations**  
_2020-04-05 · real-world · Severity: High_

Clearview AI, a facial recognition company used by law enforcement and commercial clients, has been found to have extensive ties to far-right extremists. Its technology, developed and used with input from individuals linked to racist and authoritarian movements, has led to privacy violations and human rights concerns, particularly through unauthorized data scraping and surveillance.
