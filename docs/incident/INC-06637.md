**German Scientists Develop AI Algorithm to Make Self-Driving Cars Accident-Resistant**  
_2020-09-15 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

German researchers at the Technical University of Munich have developed an AI algorithm designed to make autonomous vehicles 'accident-resistant,' assuming other drivers follow traffic rules. Tested in simulations, the system aims to enhance safety but relies on assumptions about human behavior, highlighting potential future risks if those assumptions fail.
