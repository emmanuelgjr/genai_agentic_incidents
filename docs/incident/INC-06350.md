### INC-06350  [↗](/incident/INC-06350.html)

**Amazon's AI Surveillance Allegedly Used to Suppress Unionization Efforts**  
_2020-08-31 · real-world · Severity: High_

Amazon employs AI-driven surveillance tools—including navigation software, scanners, wristbands, and data analytics—to monitor workers and identify potential union activity. According to the Open Markets Institute, these practices may suppress union organizing and undermine labor rights, directly impacting employees' ability to advocate for better working conditions.
