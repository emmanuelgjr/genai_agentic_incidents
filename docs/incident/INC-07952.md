**AI-Driven Job Losses Forecasted in European Banking Sector**  
_2026-05-28 · real-world · Severity: High_

Morgan Stanley has doubled its forecast, warning that up to 20% of jobs—about 400,000 positions—across European banks could be eliminated by 2030 due to rapid adoption of generative AI automating back-office, risk, and compliance tasks. The projected job losses highlight significant societal risks from AI deployment in finance.
