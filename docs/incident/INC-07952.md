**AI-Governed Micronation Experiment Raises Global Concerns**  
_2026-05-15 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Tech founder Dan Thomson established Sensay Island, a self-declared micronation in the Philippines governed by an AI council modeled on historical leaders. While thousands have applied for residency, experts and the founder warn of potential risks, including undemocratic decisions or misuse of AI power, though no harm has yet occurred.
