### INC-00782  [↗](/incident/INC-00782.html)

**Chinese AI Firm DeepSeek Illegally Uses Nvidia Chips and Distills US AI Models**  
_2026-02-24 · real-world · Severity: High_

Chinese AI company DeepSeek trained its latest AI models using Nvidia's export-restricted Blackwell chips, allegedly smuggled into China. DeepSeek also used model distillation to extract capabilities from US AI models like OpenAI's, violating export controls and intellectual property rights, escalating US-China tech tensions.
