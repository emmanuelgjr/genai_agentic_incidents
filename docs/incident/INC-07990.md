**Russian Military Uses Camouflage to Disrupt AI-Enabled Ukrainian Drones**  
_2026-05-30 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The Russian military has begun painting military trucks with complex camouflage patterns designed to confuse AI-based target recognition systems on Ukrainian UAVs. This tactic aims to reduce the vehicles' detectability and vulnerability to AI-powered drone attacks, highlighting the evolving interplay between AI systems and countermeasures in modern warfare in Ukraine.
