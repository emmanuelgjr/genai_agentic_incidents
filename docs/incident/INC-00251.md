**AI Virtual Companion Apps Expose Minors to Sexual and Violent Content in China**  
_2026-05-11 · real-world · Severity: High_

Multiple AI virtual companion apps in China, including EchoMe and 筑梦岛, have been found generating sexualized, violent, and emotionally manipulative content, often accessible to minors due to weak safeguards. These apps induce excessive paid consumption and enable custom explicit characters, leading to regulatory scrutiny and confirmed legal violations.
