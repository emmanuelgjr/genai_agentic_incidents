### INC-01905  [↗](/incident/INC-01905.html)

**TikTok Scales Back AI Video Summaries After Generating Bizarre Errors**  
_2026-05-06 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

TikTok scaled back its experimental AI Overviews feature after it generated wildly inaccurate and bizarre video summaries, such as describing Charli D'Amelio as a "collection of blueberries." The malfunction led to widespread misinformation and reputational harm, prompting TikTok to limit the feature's scope.
