**US Air Force Tests AI-Enabled MQ-20 Avenger Drone with F-35 Fighter**  
_2026-05-27 · real-world · Severity: High_

The US Air Force, in collaboration with General Atomics and Lockheed Martin, successfully tested the AI-enabled MQ-20 Avenger drone, controlled by an F-35 fighter via a tablet interface. The demonstration showcased advanced manned-unmanned teaming, highlighting future risks associated with autonomous combat drones but reported no harm or malfunction.
