### INC-07927  [↗](/incident/INC-07927.html)

**Danish Newspaper Berlingske Publishes AI-Generated Fake Quotes and Experts**  
_2026-05-27 · real-world · Severity: High_

Berlingske, a Danish newspaper, published an article containing fabricated quotes and fictional experts generated by an internal AI tool, violating editorial guidelines. The incident led to the suspension of the responsible journalist and a review of their previous work, after the misinformation was discovered and corrected.

**Attack vector:** `misinformation`
