**AI Model DAMO COCA Enhances Early Colorectal Cancer Detection in China**  
_2026-04-30 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Chinese researchers from DAMO Academy and Guangdong Provincial People's Hospital developed the AI model DAMO COCA, which analyzes routine CT scans to detect colorectal cancer without invasive procedures. Clinical trials on over 27,000 patients showed the AI identified cases missed by doctors, significantly improving early cancer detection and reducing missed diagnoses.
