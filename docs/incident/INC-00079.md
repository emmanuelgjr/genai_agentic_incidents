### INC-00079  [↗](/incident/INC-00079.html)

**AI Apps Leak Millions of Users' Personal and KYC Data via Misconfigured Cloud Storage**  
_2026-02-23 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

AI-based apps, including Video AI Art Generator & Maker and IDMerit, leaked millions of users' images, videos, and sensitive KYC data due to misconfigured Google Cloud Storage. The breach exposed over 12TB of data from users in about 25 countries, violating privacy and data protection rights.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM05`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`, `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.10`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0057`
