### INC-06428  [↗](/incident/INC-06428.html)

**Chess Board Startup Uses AI-Generated Fake Engineers and Product Demo to Defraud Backers**  
_2020-03-10 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Spanish startup REGIUM used GAN-based AI to create fake engineer profiles and fabricated a product demo video for an automated chessboard, deceiving investors and the chess community. The fraudulent campaign raised over $33,000 on Kickstarter before being exposed and suspended, causing financial and reputational harm.

**Attack vector:** `other`  

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`
