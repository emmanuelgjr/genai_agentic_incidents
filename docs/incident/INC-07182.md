### INC-07182  [↗](/incident/INC-07182.html)

**Fraudsters clone CEO voice to steal USD 243,000 from UK energy firm**  
_2019-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

The CEO of the German subsidiary of a UK-based energy company was impersonated by attackers using deepfake audio of his voice on a phone call, with the resulting wire transfer of EUR 220,000 (USD 243,000) routed through Hungary and Mexico. This was widely cited as the first publicly reported AI-voice-clone BEC fraud.

**Affected:** UK energy company (German subsidiary)  
**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM03`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI02`, `ASI03`, `ASI09`
