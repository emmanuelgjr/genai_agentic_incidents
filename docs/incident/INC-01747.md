**Rome Plans AI-Controlled 'White Light' Traffic Signals for Autonomous Vehicles**  
_2026-04-18 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Rome is considering testing a new 'white light' traffic signal, managed by AI, to coordinate autonomous vehicles at intersections and improve traffic flow. Inspired by U.S. research, the system would allow AI to control traffic when enough self-driving cars are present. No incidents or harm have occurred yet.
