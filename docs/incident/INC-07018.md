### INC-07018  [↗](/incident/INC-07018.html)

**Turkey Develops Autonomous Swarm Military Robots with AI**  
_2020-06-05 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Turkey's defense sector, led by the Presidency of Defense Industries, is developing the Robotim Project, which integrates AI into unmanned aerial and ground vehicles for fully autonomous, GPS-independent, and swarm-coordinated military operations. While no harm has occurred, the deployment of such autonomous AI systems poses credible future risks.
