**LAPD's Extensive Use of Facial Recognition AI Linked to Wrongful Arrests and Transparency Issues**  
_2020-09-19 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Los Angeles Police Department used facial recognition AI nearly 30,000 times since 2009, despite previous denials. The technology, accessed via a regional database, has been linked to wrongful arrests and raises concerns about racial bias, privacy violations, and lack of transparency in law enforcement's AI use.
