**China's First AI-Generated Fake Review Case Ruled: AI Tool Providers Fined**  
_2026-05-12 · real-world · Severity: High_

In Hangzhou, China, two companies operated AI writing tools that generated fake promotional content for a social media platform, misleading consumers and damaging the platform's content ecosystem. The court ruled this as unfair competition, ordering the companies to stop the service and pay 100,000 RMB in damages.
