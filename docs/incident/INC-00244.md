**AI Traffic Cameras in Western Australia Cause License Loss and Fines for Drivers**  
_2026-02-26 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI-powered road safety cameras in Western Australia have issued fines and demerit points to drivers for seatbelt and mobile phone violations, including those committed by passengers or children. Many drivers have lost licenses or faced significant penalties, raising concerns about fairness and the rigid enforcement of AI-detected infractions.
