### INC-06543  [↗](/incident/INC-06543.html)

**Experts Warn of AI Hazards: Unpredictable Algorithms and Societal Risks**  
_2020-06-21 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

AI experts, including mathematician Cathy O'Neil, warn that rapid AI development introduces unpredictable algorithmic behavior, opaque decision-making, and data security risks. As AI systems are integrated into critical sectors like economics and justice, these hazards could threaten privacy, transparency, and societal stability if not properly managed.
