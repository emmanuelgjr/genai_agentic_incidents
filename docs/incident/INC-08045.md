**UK Lawmakers Warn of AI Vendor Lock-In Risks with Palantir in Public Sector**  
_2026-06-02 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

UK lawmakers have raised concerns about the country's heavy reliance on Palantir's AI-powered data analytics systems in critical public sectors, including the NHS. A parliamentary report warns that vendor lock-in could give Palantir excessive bargaining power, posing a significant risk to public services and national infrastructure.
