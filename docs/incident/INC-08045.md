### INC-08045  [↗](/incident/INC-08045.html)

**AI-Generated Legal Documents and AI-Enabled Cyberattacks Cause Harm**  
_2026-06-02 · real-world · Severity: High_

In the US, AI tools like ChatGPT and Claude are generating legal documents with false or misleading information, overwhelming courts with flawed lawsuits. Separately, Google reports the first use of AI by hackers to discover and exploit zero-day software vulnerabilities, escalating cybersecurity threats. Both incidents highlight direct harms caused by AI systems.

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0050`
