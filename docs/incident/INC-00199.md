### INC-00199  [↗](/incident/INC-00199.html)

**AI Recruitment Tool Causes Discriminatory Job Ads, DOJ Fines IT Firm**  
_2026-02-26 · real-world · Severity: High_

Elegant Enterprise-Wide Solutions, a Virginia IT services company, used an AI recruitment tool to generate job ads that unlawfully restricted applicants based on visa status, excluding U.S. workers. The U.S. Department of Justice found this violated the Immigration and Nationality Act, resulting in a legal settlement and fines.
