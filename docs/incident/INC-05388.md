### INC-05388  [↗](/incident/INC-05388.html)

**AI Medical Diagnostic Tools Found to Rely on Shortcuts, Risking COVID-19 Misdiagnosis**  
_2021-05-31 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

University of Washington researchers found that AI models designed to diagnose COVID-19 from chest X-rays often rely on dataset-specific shortcuts, such as text markers or patient positioning, rather than genuine medical pathology. This shortcut learning could lead to misdiagnosis if such AI tools are deployed in clinical settings.
