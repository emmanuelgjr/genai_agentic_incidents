### INC-05116  [↗](/incident/INC-05116.html)

**GAN Artwork Won First Place at State Fair Competition**  
_2022-08-29 · real-world · Severity: Medium_
AIID: [`#369`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/369/)

Jason Allen, president of Colorado-based tabletop gaming company Incarnate Games, won first place in the digital art category at the Colorado State Fair on Monday with a work called 'Theatre D'opera Spatial.' The image was generated using AI software called Midjourney based on Allen's prompts, then printed on canvas for submission. Allen labeled his submission as 'Jason Allen via Midjourney' and noted he used additional processing with Photoshop and upscaling with Gigapixel AI. The win sparked controversy on Twitter, with working artists and enthusiasts expressing concern that AI-generated art threatens creative jobs and represents the 'death of artistry.' Artist Genel Jumalon's viral tweet about the incident gained significant attention, with users expressing fears about job displacement in creative fields. Allen defended his win, arguing that substantial human input was required to create the work through prompt engineering and post-processing. He predicted that art competitions would eventually create separate categories for AI-generated art. The controversy reflects broader concerns in the art community about AI tools potentially replacing human illustrators and artists.
