**AI Drowning Detection System Fails at Taipei Sports Center, Leading to Fatality**  
_2026-06-08 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

An AI-based drowning detection system at Taipei's Da'an Sports Center failed to alert staff during a fatal drowning incident, as the victim was in a blind spot not covered by the system. The malfunction contributed to delayed rescue and raised concerns about over-reliance on AI for public safety.
