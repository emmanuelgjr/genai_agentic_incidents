**Turkey Highlights Strategic Importance of AI-Enabled Military Drones**  
_2026-03-14 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Turkish officials, including AK Party spokesperson Ömer Çelik, emphasized the necessity of developing AI-integrated UAV and SİHA (armed drone) capabilities for national security and deterrence. While no harm has occurred, the advancement and deployment of these AI-powered military systems present plausible future risks, marking them as an AI hazard.
