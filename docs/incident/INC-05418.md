### INC-05418  [↗](/incident/INC-05418.html)

**AI-Enabled Drone Swarms Cause and Pose Risks in Modern Warfare**  
_2021-01-30 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

AI-powered drone swarms have been demonstrated and deployed by militaries, including the Indian Army, and have already caused harm in conflicts, such as attacks on Russian and Saudi facilities. These autonomous systems can coordinate attacks, overwhelm defenses, and pose escalating risks of injury, destruction, and rights violations in future warfare.
