**Meta's AI Smart Glasses Spark Privacy Violations and Legal Action**  
_2026-05-13 · real-world · Severity: High_

Meta's AI-powered Ray-Ban smart glasses have led to widespread privacy violations, with users secretly recording individuals—often women—without consent and sharing videos online. Some videos are used for AI training, exposing workers to graphic content. Lawsuits have been filed over unauthorized data sharing and privacy breaches.
