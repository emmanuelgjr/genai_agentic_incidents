### INC-02221  [↗](/incident/INC-02221.html)

**AI-generated deepfake of NVIDIA CEO used in crypto scam livestream**  
_2025-10-29 · real-world · Severity: High_

A deepfake livestream impersonating NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang ran on YouTube during the real NVIDIA GTC keynote, promoting a fraudulent crypto-doubling scheme. The fake stream attracted up to 100,000 viewers, 5x the official keynote audience, before takedown.

**Affected:** NVIDIA audiences; crypto users  
**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048`, `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
