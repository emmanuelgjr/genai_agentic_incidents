**UK Plans AI Facial Recognition for Asylum Seeker Age Checks Raise Human Rights Concerns**  
_2026-05-27 · real-world · Severity: High_

The UK Home Office plans to deploy AI facial recognition technology to estimate the age of asylum seekers at borders, aiming to identify adults posing as minors. Human rights groups warn the unproven system could misclassify children, risking denial of protections. The technology is set for rollout in 2027.
