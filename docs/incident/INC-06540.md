### INC-06540  [↗](/incident/INC-06540.html)

**Experts Warn AI-Powered COVID-19 Tools Threaten Digital Rights**  
_2020-09-01 · real-world · Severity: High_

AlgorithmWatch and the Bertelsmann Foundation warn that AI-driven technologies like facial recognition, contact-tracing apps, and infrared cameras used to combat COVID-19 across Europe pose significant risks to privacy and human rights, urging proper oversight to prevent potential misuse, discrimination, and rights violations.
