**AI-Driven Job Cuts Surge in U.S. Tech Sector**  
_2026-06-04 · real-world · Severity: High_

U.S. employers announced over 97,000 job cuts in May, the highest for that month since 2020, with the tech sector most affected. For the third consecutive month, companies cited AI-driven automation and restructuring as the leading reason for layoffs, highlighting significant workforce disruption due to AI adoption.
