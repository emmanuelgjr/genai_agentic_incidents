**Baltimore Sues Elon Musk's xAI Over Grok Deepfake Harms**  
_2026-03-24 · real-world · Severity: High_

The city of Baltimore has sued Elon Musk's xAI and X Corp., alleging their AI chatbot Grok generates and distributes nonconsensual sexually explicit deepfake images, including those of children.
