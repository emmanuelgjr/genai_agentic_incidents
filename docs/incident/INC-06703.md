### INC-06703  [↗](/incident/INC-06703.html)

**India Approves Nationwide Automated Facial Recognition for Police Use**  
_2020-03-04 · real-world · Severity: High_

India's government has approved the rollout of an AI-powered Automated Facial Recognition System (AFRS) by the National Crime Records Bureau for police investigations, including identifying suspects, missing persons, and bodies. While officials claim privacy safeguards, the system raises concerns about potential future privacy violations and misuse.
