**Apple Considers Allowing Agentic AI in App Store Amid Security Concerns**  
_2026-05-14 · real-world · Severity: High_

Apple is exploring the integration of agentic AI systems into its App Store, aiming to balance innovation with strict privacy and security standards. The company is reassessing policies to address potential risks, such as autonomous AI actions that could threaten user safety or app store integrity. No harm has occurred yet.
