**Brazilian Court Holds Platform Liable for AI-Generated Fake Nude Images**  
_2026-06-03 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A Brazilian court unanimously ruled that a digital platform is liable for failing to promptly remove AI-generated fake nude images of a woman, created and posted without her consent. The platform must pay damages for violating her rights, as the content caused significant harm and moral damage.
