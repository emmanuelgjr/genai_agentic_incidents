### INC-08068  [↗](/incident/INC-08068.html)

**AI-Driven Spam Campaigns Manipulate Reddit to Influence Chatbot Outputs**  
_2026-06-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

Companies and marketing agencies are flooding Reddit, especially the r/biohackers subreddit, with promotional and fake posts to manipulate AI chatbots like ChatGPT and Google's AI search. This coordinated campaign distorts AI-generated answers, harms community trust, and poses risks to users relying on AI for health information.

**Attack vector:** `rce`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
