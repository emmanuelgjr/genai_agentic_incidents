### INC-00744  [↗](/incident/INC-00744.html)

**ChatGPT and Claude Spread Misinformation via Grokipedia Integration**  
_2026-01-25 · real-world · Severity: High_

OpenAI's ChatGPT (GPT-5.2) and Anthropic's Claude have been found to use Grokipedia, an AI-generated encyclopedia by xAI, as a source. This has led to the dissemination of misinformation and offensive content, including biased narratives and harmful statements, raising concerns about the reliability and societal impact of these AI systems.

**Attack vector:** `rce`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`

**NIST AI RMF:** `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
