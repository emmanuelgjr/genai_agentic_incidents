### INC-07749  [↗](/incident/INC-07749.html)

**Florida News Site Exposed for AI-Generated Fake Reporters and Plagiarized Content**  
_2026-05-14 · real-world · Severity: High_

The "South Florida Standard," a Florida news website, was exposed as a fraudulent outlet staffed entirely by AI-generated fake reporters with fabricated biographies and headshots. The site republished stolen content from legitimate news sources, misleading the public before shutting down after the deception was revealed.
