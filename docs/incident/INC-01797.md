**SPARC AI Deploys Overwatch AI Targeting Platform for Military Testing in Ukraine**  
_2026-03-09 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

SPARC AI Inc. is deploying its Overwatch AI platform, designed for GPS-denied navigation and autonomous targeting, to Ukraine for operational field testing in a conflict zone. The system's use in military operations raises concerns about potential future harm due to its autonomous capabilities in a high-risk environment.
