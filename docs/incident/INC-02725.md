**Image Purporting to Show President Paul Kagame of Rwanda in M23 Uniform Reportedly AI-Generated**  
_2025-01-10 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1098`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1098/)

An image circulating on social media appeared to show Rwandan President Paul Kagame wearing a military uniform with an M23 label, referencing the rebel group active in eastern DRC. Fact-checking organization PesaCheck found no credible sources supporting the image and used AI-detection tools to determine it was likely AI-generated. The image was reportedly used to imply Kagame&#x27;s affiliation with M23 amid ongoing regional tensions and allegations of Rwandan support for the group.
