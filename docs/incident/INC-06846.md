**Police and Stadium Use of Facial Recognition AI Raises Privacy Concerns in France and Europe**  
_2020-02-17 · real-world · Severity: High_

Police forces in France and across Europe are increasingly using AI-powered facial recognition systems for surveillance and security, raising significant concerns about privacy violations and potential breaches of fundamental rights. Recent deployments include police investigations and experimental use in stadiums, often without adequate public consent or regulatory oversight.
