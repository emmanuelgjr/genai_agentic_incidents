**Fox News Misattributes Ukrainian AI Drone Footage as US Military Technology**  
_2026-03-05 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Fox News aired footage of Ukrainian STING interceptor drones, developed by Wild Hornets, intercepting Russian Geran-2 drones in Ukraine, but misrepresented it as showcasing US AI military technology in Iran. The incident highlights the operational use of AI-enabled drones in active conflict and media misattribution.
