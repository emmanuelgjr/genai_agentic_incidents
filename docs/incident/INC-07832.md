### INC-07832  [↗](/incident/INC-07832.html)

**OpenAI Deploys Advanced Cyber Defense AI Model in Japan**  
_2026-05-20 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

OpenAI announced it will provide its advanced AI model, GPT-5.5 Cyber, to the Japanese government and select companies to enhance cyber defense capabilities. This move aims to counter rising concerns over AI-enabled cyberattacks, focusing on protecting critical infrastructure and ensuring secure, monitored deployment.
