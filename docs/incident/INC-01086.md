### INC-01086  [↗](/incident/INC-01086.html)

**Germany Procures AI-Enabled Kamikaze Drones for Bundeswehr**  
_2026-04-22 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Rheinmetall will supply the German Bundeswehr with AI-powered loitering munitions capable of autonomously identifying and attacking targets. The €300 million contract covers a large, undisclosed number of drones, with deliveries starting in 2027. The autonomous nature of these weapons poses significant risks of harm in future military operations.
