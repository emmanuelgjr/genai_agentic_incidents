### INC-01469  [↗](/incident/INC-01469.html)

**Music Publishers Sue Anthropic Over AI Copyright Infringement**  
_2026-03-24 · real-world · Severity: High_

Universal Music Group, Concord, and ABKCO have sued Anthropic, alleging its AI chatbot Claude was trained on and reproduces copyrighted song lyrics without permission. The publishers argue this infringes their intellectual property rights and competes with their market, challenging the AI's 'fair use' defense in California court.
