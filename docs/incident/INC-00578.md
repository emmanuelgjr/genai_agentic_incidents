**Amazon Deploys AI to Combat Counterfeits and Phishing Globally**  
_2026-04-27 · real-world · Severity: High_

Amazon has implemented advanced AI systems, including Sentrix and Omniscan, to proactively detect and block counterfeit products, phishing websites, and fraudulent reviews. In 2025, these tools enabled the seizure of 15 million fake items and the shutdown of over 100 fraudulent sites, significantly reducing consumer and brand harm worldwide.
