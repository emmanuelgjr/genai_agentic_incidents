**AI-Driven Attacks Fuel Major Crypto Thefts in 2026**  
_2026-04-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

In 2026, over $600 million was stolen in crypto hacks, with AI systems enabling large-scale attacks. North Korean-linked groups used AI for social engineering, deepfakes, and automated vulnerability scanning, leading to major breaches at Kelp DAO, Drift Protocol, and Zerion. AI's role has amplified the scale and sophistication of these incidents.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM03`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI04`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-6.1`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0010`, `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
