**Elon Musk's Neuralink Plans Human Brain Implant Within a Year**  
_2020-05-08 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Elon Musk announced that Neuralink aims to implant an AI-enabled brain chip in humans within a year to restore lost sensory and motor functions. While no harm has occurred yet, the technology poses credible future risks if it malfunctions or is misused.
