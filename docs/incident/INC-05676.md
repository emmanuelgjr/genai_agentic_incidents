### INC-05676  [↗](/incident/INC-05676.html)

**Google Tests FLoC AI Ad Targeting, Raising Privacy Concerns**  
_2021-03-30 · real-world · Severity: High_

Google began testing its AI-powered Federated Learning of Cohorts (FLoC) in millions of Chrome browsers, grouping users for targeted ads based on browsing behavior. The system operates without user consent, sharing cohort IDs with third parties, raising significant privacy and rights concerns over potential misuse and exposure of sensitive information.

**Attack vector:** `other`
