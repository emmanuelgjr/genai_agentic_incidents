### INC-07017  [↗](/incident/INC-07017.html)

**Turkey Develops AI-Powered Swarm Drone Technology for Military Use**  
_2020-11-19 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Turkish defense company MilSOFT has developed AI-enabled swarm drone software for both fixed and rotary-wing UAVs, enhancing military capabilities in reconnaissance, target detection, and attack. While no harm has yet occurred, the deployment of autonomous armed drone swarms poses credible future risks of harm in military operations.

**Attack vector:** `other`
