### INC-06322  [↗](/incident/INC-06322.html)

**AI-Powered Surveillance in China During COVID-19 Leads to Widespread Privacy Violations**  
_2020-02-07 · real-world · Severity: High_

During the COVID-19 pandemic, Chinese authorities deployed AI systems—including facial recognition, thermal imaging, and big data analytics—for mass surveillance, tracking individuals' movements and health status. This led to significant privacy violations and social control, with authorities and employers intervening based on AI-generated data, raising serious human rights concerns.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`
