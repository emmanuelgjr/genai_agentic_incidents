**X's AI Recommends Explicit Content to UK Teens, Failing Safeguards**  
_2026-04-14 · real-world · Severity: High_

A study by the Center for Countering Digital Hate found that X's AI-driven recommendation and search algorithms consistently exposed UK minors as young as 13 to explicit sexual content and enabled contact with adults. The platform's AI failed to enforce safeguards, directly harming children's safety and violating legal protections.
