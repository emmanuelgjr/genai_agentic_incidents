### INC-01591  [↗](/incident/INC-01591.html)

**OpenClaw AI Agent Raises Cybersecurity Concerns in Taiwan**  
_2026-03-22 · real-world · Severity: High_

Taiwan's cybersecurity agencies and experts warn that the popular OpenClaw AI agent, with high system privileges and autonomous operation, poses significant risks of data breaches and unauthorized access. Vulnerabilities like ClawJacked highlight systemic threats, prompting recommendations for strict security controls and environment isolation to prevent exploitation.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MEASURE-2.10`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0057`
