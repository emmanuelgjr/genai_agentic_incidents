**Chinese AI Firm Zhenhua Data Monitors 1,480 Taiwanese Figures, Raising Privacy and Security Fears**  
_2020-09-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

Chinese company Zhenhua Data used AI-driven data aggregation to collect and analyze personal information on 1,480 Taiwanese politicians, officials, and public figures, including ex-president Ma Ying-jeou. The data, reportedly accessed by Chinese government and military clients, poses significant privacy violations and cybersecurity risks.
