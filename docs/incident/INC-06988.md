### INC-06988  [↗](/incident/INC-06988.html)

**TikTok Accused of Violating Children's Privacy Laws Through AI Data Collection**  
_2020-05-14 · real-world · Severity: High_

A coalition of privacy groups filed complaints with the FTC, alleging TikTok uses AI-driven algorithms to collect and process personal data from children under 13 without parental consent, violating U.S. children's privacy laws. Investigations have also been launched in Colombia and the Netherlands over similar concerns.
