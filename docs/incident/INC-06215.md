### INC-06215  [↗](/incident/INC-06215.html)

**AI and Automation Threaten Millions of Jobs by 2030**  
_2020-01-05 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Multiple reports warn that AI, robotics, and automation could displace between 400 and 800 million workers globally by 2030, with professions like waiters, delivery drivers, and industrial operators at high risk. Currently, machines already perform nearly 30% of workplace tasks, highlighting significant future employment risks.
