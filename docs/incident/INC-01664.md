**Chinese AI Startup Publishes Satellite Intelligence on US Military in Middle East**  
_2026-03-09 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Chinese AI startup MizarVision used AI to analyze and publicly share near real-time satellite imagery of US military assets across the Middle East. The AI-annotated intelligence, widely disseminated online, reportedly coincided with subsequent attacks on identified bases, raising concerns about AI-enabled exposure of sensitive military operations and indirect facilitation of harm.
