### INC-04022  [↗](/incident/INC-04022.html)

**Mumbai Businessman Reportedly Defrauded via Purported AI-Cloned Voice Impersonating Son**  
_2024-03-30 · real-world · Severity: Medium_
AIID: [`#1053`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1053/)

A Mumbai businessman reportedly identified as KT Vinod reportedly lost Rs 80,000 after receiving a call from someone claiming to be a representative of the Indian Embassy in Dubai, who said his son had been arrested. The caller allegedly used AI-generated voice cloning to simulate the voice of Vinod&#x27;s son, pleading for help. Reportedly convinced of the urgency, Vinod instructed a money transfer via Google Pay. The scam was discovered only after he contacted his son directly.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
