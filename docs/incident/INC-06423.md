**Canadian Predictive Policing AI Raises Human Rights Concerns, Report Warns**  
_2020-09-01 · real-world · Severity: High_

A University of Toronto report warns that Canadian police are increasingly using AI-based predictive policing tools, raising risks of constitutional and human rights violations. Experts urge the federal government to implement legal safeguards, as current laws may not adequately protect against potential harms such as discrimination and privacy breaches.
