### INC-07000  [↗](/incident/INC-07000.html)

**TikTok's Secret Development of Deepfake Face Swap Feature Raises AI Misuse Concerns**  
_2020-01-03 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

ByteDance, owner of TikTok and Douyin, secretly developed a deepfake-based face swap feature requiring users' biometric scans. Although not yet released, the technology raises significant concerns about potential misuse for impersonation, misinformation, and privacy violations, highlighting the risks associated with advanced AI-driven video manipulation tools.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
