### INC-06582  [↗](/incident/INC-06582.html)

**Facebook's AI Failures Enable Global Political Manipulation and Disinformation Campaigns**  
_2020-09-15 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Former Facebook data scientist Sophie Zhang revealed that Facebook's AI-driven moderation systems failed to detect and stop coordinated fake accounts and bot networks used for political manipulation and disinformation in multiple countries, enabling governments to influence elections and deceive citizens, resulting in real-world harm to democratic processes and communities.
