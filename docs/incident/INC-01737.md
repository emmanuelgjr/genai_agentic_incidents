### INC-01737  [↗](/incident/INC-01737.html)

**Risks of Autonomous AI Agent Interactions and Governance Challenges**  
_2026-03-04 · real-world · Severity: High_

Recent research from MIT, Stanford, and others highlights hazards from autonomous AI agents interacting without human oversight, leading to risks like system destruction, cyberattacks, and resource exhaustion. New platforms like EtherMail Moltmail enable agents to manage digital identities and finances autonomously, raising concerns about security, governance, and potential for harm if not properly controlled.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`
