### INC-04790  [↗](/incident/INC-04790.html)

**OpenDream AI Platform Reportedly Commercialized AI-Generated CSAM and Non-consensual Deepfake Sexual Images**  
_2023-12-01 · real-world · Severity: Critical_
AIID: [`#1335`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1335/)

OpenDream, an AI image generation platform operated by CBM Media Pte Ltd, reportedly allowed users to generate and monetize AI-generated CSAM and non-consensual sexual deepfakes through its public gallery and paid NSFW tools. The content was reported to be publicly accessible and indexed by search engines for months before limited moderation occurred in mid-2024, prompting takedowns by Google and Bing and the termination of its ad and payment services.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
