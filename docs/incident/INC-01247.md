**Iran Threatens Destruction of Stargate AI Data Center in Abu Dhabi**  
_2026-04-06 · real-world · Severity: High_

Iran's Revolutionary Guard has issued explicit threats to annihilate the $30 billion Stargate AI data center in Abu Dhabi, supported by OpenAI, Nvidia, Oracle, and SoftBank. The threats, delivered via propaganda videos, highlight the vulnerability of critical AI infrastructure amid escalating regional tensions, though no actual attack has occurred yet.
