### INC-01073  [↗](/incident/INC-01073.html)

**German Court Bans AI-Based Biometric Checks in Online Exams**  
_2026-03-25 · real-world · Severity: High_

A German court ruled that using AI-powered facial recognition for identity verification in online university exams violates GDPR by unlawfully processing biometric data. The court recognized psychological harm to a student and awarded compensation, establishing that such AI proctoring practices breach fundamental rights.
