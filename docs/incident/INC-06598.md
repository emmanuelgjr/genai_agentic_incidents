### INC-06598  [↗](/incident/INC-06598.html)

**Facial Recognition AI Services Raise Privacy and Safety Concerns in EU and Beyond**  
_2020-06-15 · real-world · Severity: High_

European regulators and privacy advocates warn that AI-powered facial recognition tools like Clearview AI and PimEyes could violate privacy laws and enable stalking or surveillance. While no direct harm is reported, these technologies pose significant risks to individuals' rights and safety if misused or deployed without strict legal safeguards.
