**AI-Generated Singer in Romania Sparks Racism and Discrimination Debate**  
_2026-04-21 · real-world · Severity: High_

The AI-generated singer Lolita Cercel has become a sensation in Romania, but has drawn criticism for perpetuating racist stereotypes against the Roma minority and causing economic and reputational harm to real Roma musicians.
