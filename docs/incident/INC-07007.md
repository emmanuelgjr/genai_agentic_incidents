### INC-07007  [↗](/incident/INC-07007.html)

**Toyota Plans AI-Driven 'Woven City' to Test Autonomous Technologies**  
_2020-01-07 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Toyota announced plans to build 'Woven City,' an experimental smart city near Mount Fuji, Japan, where around 2,000 residents will live and new AI technologies, including autonomous vehicles and smart infrastructure, will be tested in real-world conditions. No AI-related harm has occurred yet, but future risks are plausible.
