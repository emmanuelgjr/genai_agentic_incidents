### INC-06316  [↗](/incident/INC-06316.html)

**AI-Powered Remote Proctoring Causes Privacy Violations and Discrimination in Education**  
_2020-11-06 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI-based remote proctoring tools used in online education have led to significant harms, including invasive surveillance, biometric data collection without consent, privacy breaches, psychological distress, and discriminatory impacts on minorities. These issues have resulted in lawsuits, regulatory scrutiny, and negative effects on students’ academic outcomes and well-being.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM05`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`, `ASI05`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MEASURE-2.10`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0057`
