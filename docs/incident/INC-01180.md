### INC-01180  [↗](/incident/INC-01180.html)

**IMF Warns of AI-Driven Cybersecurity Risks to Global Financial System**  
_2026-04-12 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

IMF Managing Director Kristalina Georgieva warned that the international monetary system is unprepared for growing AI-driven cybersecurity risks. The warning follows Anthropic's decision to delay its advanced AI model, Mythos, due to concerns it could expose unprecedented vulnerabilities, prompting urgent calls for risk assessment and mitigation.
