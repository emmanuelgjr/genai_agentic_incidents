### INC-06278  [↗](/incident/INC-06278.html)

**AI-Driven Social Media Algorithms Linked to Teen Suicide in Molly Russell Case**  
_2020-09-25 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Fourteen-year-old Molly Russell died by suicide after being exposed to disturbing content on Instagram, recommended by AI-driven algorithms. The inquest is investigating how these algorithms, designed to maximize user engagement, contributed to her exposure to harmful material related to self-harm and suicide, ultimately leading to her death.
