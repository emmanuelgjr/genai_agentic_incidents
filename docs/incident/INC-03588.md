### INC-03588  [↗](/incident/INC-03588.html)

**Arup Hong Kong $25M deepfake CFO multi-person video call scam**  
_2024-01 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Scammers tricked a Hong Kong-based employee of British engineering firm Arup into paying HKD 200 million (USD ~25-26M) via a fake group video call in which every other attendee, including a UK-based 'CFO', was a deepfake recreation of real colleagues built from public video footage.

**Affected:** Arup (UK engineering)  
**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM03`, `LLM07`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI02`, `ASI03`, `ASI09`
