### INC-05715  [↗](/incident/INC-05715.html)

**Inter-Homines AI System Deployed to Prevent COVID-19 Crowding Risks**  
_2021-06-21 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Inter-Homines, an Italian AI system developed by AlmageLab, GoatAl, and ForteSecurGroup, is being used in hospitals and public spaces to monitor crowd density and social distancing. The system issues alerts when gatherings exceed safe limits, aiming to reduce COVID-19 transmission and enhance public safety without compromising privacy.

**Attack vector:** `other`
