**US Sanctions on Chinese AI Chip Customers Cause Major Losses for Taiwanese Semiconductor Firms**  
_2021-04-18 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

US export controls targeting Chinese firms using AI-enabled chips for military supercomputers have led to severe financial losses for Taiwanese IC design companies like Alchip and TSMC. The sanctions, prompted by concerns over AI technology's military use, caused stock crashes and disrupted supply chains, highlighting the geopolitical risks of AI system development.
