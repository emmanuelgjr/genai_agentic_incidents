**Lockheed Martin Demonstrates AI-Powered Autonomous ISR System in Simulated Denied Communications Environment**  
_2020-05-07 · real-world · Severity: High_

Lockheed Martin, in partnership with the U.S. Air Force Test Pilot School, demonstrated an AI-powered autonomous ISR system integrated into an F-16. The system autonomously detected, located, and confirmed targets in a simulated denied communications environment, showcasing potential future military applications but without any reported harm or malfunction.
