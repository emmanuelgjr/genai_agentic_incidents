### INC-03097  [↗](/incident/INC-03097.html)

**Purported Deepfake Video of Donald Trump at NATO Summit Allegedly Used in YouTube Crypto Scam**  
_2025-07-07 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1153`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1153/)

Multiple YouTube Live streams reportedly featured a purported AI‑generated deepfake video depicting U.S. President Donald Trump at a NATO Summit press event. In the video, Trump reportedly appears to promote a Bitcoin &quot;double-your-money&quot; offer, directing viewers to scan an on‑screen QR code and send cryptocurrency to a specified address. The video was reportedly not authentic and is alleged to have been part of an ongoing YouTube-based crypto scam campaign.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
