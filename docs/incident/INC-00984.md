### INC-00984  [↗](/incident/INC-00984.html)

**Facial Recognition System at Milan Linate Airport Declared Unlawful for GDPR Violations**  
_2026-04-15 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Italian privacy authority (Garante Privacy) found the FaceBoarding facial recognition system at Milan Linate Airport unlawfully processed passengers' biometric data, violating GDPR. Issues included excessive data retention, lack of encryption, and collecting facial images without valid consent. The system was suspended in September 2025 following these privacy violations.
