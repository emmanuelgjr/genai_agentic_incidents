**AI-Generated Misinformation Campaign Targets NIO, Legal Action Taken**  
_2026-04-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

Criminal suspects used AI to generate and spread defamatory content about NIO via over 4,000 social media accounts, amassing 80 million views and causing reputational and economic harm. Chinese authorities have taken criminal measures, and courts ordered compensation and public apologies. NIO continues to pursue legal action to protect its interests.
