**Delaware Court Found Krafton Followed Most of ChatGPT&#x27;s Recommendations in Campaign that Wrongfully Terminated Unknown Worlds Executives and Seized Operational Control**  
_2025-07-01 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1449`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1449/)

A Delaware Chancery opinion found that, after being warned that firing Unknown Worlds leaders would not eliminate earnout obligations, Krafton&#x27;s CEO turned to ChatGPT for a &quot;no-deal&quot; strategy and then followed most of its recommendations. The court tied that strategy to Krafton&#x27;s lockout of publishing control, unilateral public messaging, wrongful termination of Ted Gill, Charlie Cleveland, and Max McGuire, and seizure of studio control.
