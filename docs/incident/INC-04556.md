### INC-04556  [↗](/incident/INC-04556.html)

**ChatGPT-Next-Web (NextChat) SSRF / open-proxy**  
_2023-12 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

CVEs: `CVE-2023-49785`
CVSS: **9.1**
