### INC-07928  [↗](/incident/INC-07928.html)

**Chongqing Authorities Investigate AI-Generated False Advertising in Car Sales**  
_2026-05-27 · real-world · Severity: High_

Chongqing's market regulators investigated a car company for using AI-generated videos to fabricate scenes of consumer demand and sales, misleading buyers. The AI content lacked proper labeling, violating advertising laws and consumer protection regulations. This marks the city's first case of AI-driven false marketing, prompting stricter oversight.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
