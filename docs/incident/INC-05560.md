### INC-05560  [↗](/incident/INC-05560.html)

**Dahua AI Facial Recognition Enables Uyghur Surveillance, Raises Human Rights Concerns**  
_2021-02-09 · real-world · Severity: High_

Chinese company Dahua developed AI facial recognition systems that can identify Uyghur Muslims and send real-time alerts to police, facilitating ethnic profiling and surveillance. Despite U.S. sanctions, Dahua sold equipment to Amazon and other U.S. entities, prompting senators to question Amazon's involvement amid concerns of human rights abuses.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`  

**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-4.1`, `MEASURE-2.10`
