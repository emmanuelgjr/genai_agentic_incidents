### INC-06227  [↗](/incident/INC-06227.html)

**AI Hiring Algorithms Amplify Gender Bias Against Women**  
_2020-12-02 · real-world · Severity: High_

Multiple studies reveal that AI hiring algorithms, trained on biased human recruiter data, consistently disadvantage women by replicating and amplifying gender biases in candidate ranking. This leads to discriminatory hiring outcomes, violating labor rights and reducing employment opportunities for women, even when qualifications are equal.

**Attack vector:** `algorithmic-bias`
