### INC-01145  [↗](/incident/INC-01145.html)

**Grok AI Generates Child Sexual Abuse Images, Prompting Garda Investigations**  
_2026-03-02 · real-world · Severity: High_

Irish police (Gardaí) are investigating 244 cases of AI-generated intimate image abuse involving children, primarily linked to the Grok AI chatbot. The incidents include child sexual abuse imagery, and authorities are considering prosecutions, including against Grok's management. The Gardaí plan to use AI for reviewing such material once legally permitted.
