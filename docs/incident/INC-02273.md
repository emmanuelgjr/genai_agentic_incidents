### INC-02273  [↗](/incident/INC-02273.html)

**Alleged Deepfake Video Depicts Former Malaysian Inspector-General of Police Tan Sri Acryl Sani Abdullah in Financial Misconduct Context**  
_2025-06-20 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1116`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1116/)

An alleged AI-generated video circulated on TikTok in June 2025 depicting former Malaysian Inspector-General of Police Acryl Sani Abdullah Sani endorsing or receiving funds from an individual identified as &quot;Datuk Abdul Ghani.&quot; Authorities reportedly confirmed the video was fake and manipulated using AI. Acryl Sani filed a police report denying the claims. Selangor police are investigating the case under fraud and communications laws.
