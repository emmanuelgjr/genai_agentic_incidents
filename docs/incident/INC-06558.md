### INC-06558  [↗](/incident/INC-06558.html)

**Facebook Bans Israeli AI Firm for Subconscious Manipulation of Users**  
_2020-01-18 · real-world · Severity: High_

Facebook banned Israeli company The Spinner for using AI-driven targeted campaigns to manipulate users’ subconscious behavior through personalized editorial content. The campaigns, designed to influence actions like quitting smoking or losing weight, violated user rights and Facebook’s policies, prompting a permanent ban from Facebook and Instagram.
