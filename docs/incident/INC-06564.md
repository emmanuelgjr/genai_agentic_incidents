**Facebook Executives Ignored Internal Warnings About AI-Driven Polarization and Extremism**  
_2020-05-26 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Internal research at Facebook revealed that its AI-powered recommendation algorithms promoted divisive and extremist content, fueling polarization and hate group growth. Despite clear evidence and proposed solutions, top executives, including CEO Mark Zuckerberg, dismissed or weakened efforts to mitigate these harms, allowing ongoing negative societal impacts.
