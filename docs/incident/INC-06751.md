### INC-06751  [↗](/incident/INC-06751.html)

**Karsan's Level-4 Autonomous Bus Receives First Order for Pilot Deployment in Romania**  
_2020-05-15 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Turkish manufacturer Karsan, in partnership with ADASTEC, received its first order from Romanian tech firm BSCI for a Level-4 autonomous electric bus. The bus will operate in a defined area within Ploieşti's Industrial Park as a pilot project, marking Europe's first such sale in the 8-meter class. No incidents or harm reported.

**Attack vector:** `other`
