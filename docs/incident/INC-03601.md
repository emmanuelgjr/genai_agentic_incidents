**Baidu Robotaxi Allegedly Involved in Collision with Pedestrian in Wuhan**  
_2024-07-07 · real-world · Severity: Medium_
AIID: [`#821`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/821/)

On July 7, 2024, a Baidu Robotaxi reportedly collided with a pedestrian at a traffic intersection in Wuhan. The incident occurred as the autonomous vehicle started moving on a green light while the pedestrian was allegedly crossing against a red light. The pedestrian sustained minor injuries, and Baidu was reported to have been cooperating with local authorities for further investigation.
