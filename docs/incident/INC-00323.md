### INC-00323  [↗](/incident/INC-00323.html)

**AI-Driven Scams Surge, Increasing Financial Harm and Public Concern**  
_2026-03-30 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Criminals are increasingly using AI to create more convincing and harder-to-detect scams, leading to a rise in financial fraud, especially in the UK and Australia. Older adults in the US are particularly affected by AI-enabled scam ads on social media, prompting calls for platform accountability and reform.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
