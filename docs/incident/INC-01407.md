**Meta Faces Legal Action Over AI-Driven Harms to Children in New Mexico**  
_2026-04-30 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Meta is considering shutting down its social media services in New Mexico after being found liable for using AI-driven features that harmed children's mental health and facilitated child sexual exploitation.
