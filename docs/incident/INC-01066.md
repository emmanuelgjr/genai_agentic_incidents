### INC-01066  [↗](/incident/INC-01066.html)

**Generative AI Data Poisoning Leads to Misinformation and Consumer Harm in China**  
_2026-03-13 · real-world · Severity: High_

Malicious actors in China exploited generative AI systems by mass-publishing fabricated content online, causing AI assistants to recommend non-existent products as real. This 'AI poisoning' manipulates AI-generated answers, misleading consumers and undermining trust in AI-driven information, as exposed by the 315 Gala investigation.

**Attack vector:** `rce`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM04`, `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI06`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-4.2`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`
