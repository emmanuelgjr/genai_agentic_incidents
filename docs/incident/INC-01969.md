**UN Warns AI in Advertising Risks Accelerating Global Misinformation Crisis**  
_2026-04-29 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The United Nations and the Conscious Advertising Network warn that unchecked adoption of AI in advertising is intensifying risks to global information integrity. AI-driven content generation and media buying may accelerate misinformation, erode trust, and undermine journalism, prompting calls for urgent industry and governance action.
