**Legislators and Scientists Warn of Potential AI Robot Hazards**  
_2021-06-19 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Legislators in New Hampshire and researchers in the UK and Netherlands are raising concerns about the risks of autonomous delivery, farming, and self-replicating robots. While no harm has occurred yet, potential dangers include job loss, safety risks, and even existential threats, prompting calls for responsible regulation and oversight.
