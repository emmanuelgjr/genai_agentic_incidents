**Colombian Election Software Faces Allegations of Vulnerabilities and Manipulation Risks**  
_2026-03-01 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Colombian President Gustavo Petro raised concerns about potential vulnerabilities and lack of transparency in the electoral software managed by private contractor Thomas Greg & Sons, warning of risks to election integrity. The Ministry of Defense, however, denied evidence of software manipulation or malfunction, emphasizing ongoing security monitoring.
