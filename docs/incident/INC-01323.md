### INC-01323  [↗](/incident/INC-01323.html)

**Leonardo and Baykar Develop AI-Enabled Military Drones for European Market**  
_2026-03-13 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Italian defense firm Leonardo and Turkish company Baykar are jointly developing AI-enabled medium-sized drones, with production set to begin in April. These drones, intended for integration into advanced air defense systems, present future risks of harm due to their military applications, though no incidents have occurred yet.

**Attack vector:** `other`
