**Hyundai Recalls Palisade SUVs After AI Power Seat Malfunction Causes Child's Death**  
_2026-03-13 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Hyundai halted sales and recalled over 68,000 Palisade SUVs in the US and Canada after a two-year-old died in Ohio due to a malfunction in the AI-powered anti-pinch function of the vehicle's power seats, which failed to detect an occupant. Hyundai is issuing a software update to address the issue.
