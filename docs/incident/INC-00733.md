**Capgemini Announces Mass Layoffs in Spain Citing AI Impact**  
_2026-04-10 · real-world · Severity: High_

French tech consultancy Capgemini has announced a collective layoff process (ERE) in Spain, explicitly attributing the decision to the impact of artificial intelligence and technological innovation. The restructuring will affect its 11,000-strong Spanish workforce, with significant concern for employees in Asturias, particularly Langreo.
