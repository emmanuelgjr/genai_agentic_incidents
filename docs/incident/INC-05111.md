**Footballer's "X-Rated" Comment Created by Instagram's Mistranslation**  
_2022-12-19 · real-world · Severity: Medium_
AIID: [`#447`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/447/)

After Argentina won the 2022 FIFA World Cup, footballer Alexis Mac Allister posted a comment in Spanish on his partner's Instagram post. The comment included the phrase 'Vamos Carajo', which is a common celebratory expression in Argentine football meaning 'let's f***ing go'. However, social media translation features or users interpreted this as a literal X-rated message, causing the comment to go viral on social media platforms like Twitter. The incident involved a mistranslation or misinterpretation of a culturally specific Spanish phrase, leading to widespread sharing and discussion of what appeared to be inappropriate content. The report indicates that multiple users shared screenshots of the comment on Twitter, with some users expressing amusement at what they perceived as an explicit message. The actual meaning of the phrase was later clarified in the reporting, explaining that 'Vamos Carajo' has been a common phrase in Argentine football since the 1990s and is typically used as an enthusiastic celebration rather than having sexual connotations.
