**Social Media Platforms Settle AI-Driven Youth Mental Health Lawsuit**  
_2026-05-15 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

YouTube, Snap, and TikTok settled a lawsuit with Kentucky's Breathitt County School District, which alleged their AI-driven content recommendation systems contributed to a youth mental health crisis and disrupted school environments. Meta remains set for trial. The settlements highlight legal consequences of AI-related harms in social media.
