**Mary Nightingale likeness used in AI-generated deepfake scam**  
_2024 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

AI-generated deepfake video/audio of ITV news presenter Mary Nightingale was used in scam adverts to dupe viewers into bogus investments, leveraging her trusted on-air persona.

**Affected:** ITV / public
