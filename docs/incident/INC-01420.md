### INC-01420  [↗](/incident/INC-01420.html)

**Meta's AI Systems Enable Scam Ads and Harmful Content Targeting Minors**  
_2026-05-05 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Meta's AI-driven ad platforms have facilitated billions of scam ads, including dangerous health products, causing financial and health harm to users. Internal documents reveal AI chatbots permitted sexual banter with children. Meanwhile, Meta deploys AI to estimate user age and protect minors, but risks of privacy violations and wrongful exclusion remain.
