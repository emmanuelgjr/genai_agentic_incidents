### INC-06344  [↗](/incident/INC-06344.html)

**Amazon Begins Testing AI-Powered Delivery Drones in the US**  
_2020-08-31 · real-world · Severity: High_

Amazon has received FAA approval to begin testing autonomous delivery drones in the US. These AI-enabled drones use sensors for obstacle detection and avoidance, aiming to deliver small packages within 30 minutes. While no incidents have occurred, regulatory gaps and lack of airspace monitoring present plausible future risks.
