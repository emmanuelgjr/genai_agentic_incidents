**Tesla FSD AI System Linked to Fatal Accidents and Ongoing Safety Concerns**  
_2020-11-29 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Tesla's Full Self-Driving (FSD) AI system has been updated to improve pedestrian detection and traffic signal recognition, but hardware limitations—especially in strong light or with white vehicles—have led to fatal accidents. Despite ongoing software improvements, these vulnerabilities persist, raising continued safety concerns about the AI's reliability.
