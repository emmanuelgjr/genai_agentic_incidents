**New Zealand pensioner loses NZD 224,000 to deepfake Luxon Bitcoin scam**  
_2024 · real-world · Severity: High_

A New Zealand pensioner lost roughly NZD 224,000 (USD 140,000) after being induced to invest by an AI-generated deepfake advert featuring Prime Minister Christopher Luxon promoting fraudulent Bitcoin investments.

**Affected:** New Zealand retail investors  
**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
