### INC-09162  [↗](/incident/INC-09162.html)

**gosaml2 CBC Padding Panic — Unauthenticated Process Crash**  
_2026-03 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: High_
CWEs: `CWE-129`

## Summary The AES-CBC decryption path in `DecryptBytes()` panics on crafted ciphertext whose plaintext is all zero bytes. After decryption, `bytes.TrimRight(data, "\x00")` empties the slice, then `data[len(data)-1]` panics with `index out of range [-1]`. There is no `recover()` in the library. The panic propagates through `ValidateEncodedResponse` and kills the goroutine (or the entire process in non-`net/http` servers).
