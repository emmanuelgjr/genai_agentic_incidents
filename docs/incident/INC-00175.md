### INC-00175  [↗](/incident/INC-00175.html)

**AI Language Models Fail at Early Clinical Reasoning, Raising Patient Safety Concerns**  
_2026-04-13 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A study by Mass General Brigham found that large language model AI systems, including GPT-5 and Gemini, fail to provide adequate early differential diagnoses in over 80% of cases. While accurate with complete data, their lack of clinical reasoning poses risks if used unsupervised in medical settings.

**Attack vector:** `other`
