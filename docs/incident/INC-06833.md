### INC-06833  [↗](/incident/INC-06833.html)

**Pandemic-Era AI Surveillance Raises Global Privacy Concerns**  
_2020-03-29 · real-world · Severity: High_

Governments worldwide have deployed AI-enabled smartphone geolocation and contact tracing to contain COVID-19, prompting human rights groups to warn of potential privacy violations and threats to civil liberties. While no direct harm is reported, experts caution these surveillance measures could normalize rights infringements beyond the pandemic.
