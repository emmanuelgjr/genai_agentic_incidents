**Children Circumvent AI Age Verification with Simple Tricks in the UK**  
_2026-05-04 · real-world · Severity: High_

In the UK, AI-based facial recognition systems for online age verification are being easily bypassed by children using simple methods like drawing fake mustaches. This failure has allowed minors to access age-restricted and potentially harmful content, undermining legal protections intended to safeguard children online.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`  

**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-4.1`, `MEASURE-2.10`
