### INC-00792  [↗](/incident/INC-00792.html)

**Chinese Family Uses AI Avatar to Hide Son's Death from Elderly Mother**  
_2026-04-12 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

In Shandong, China, a family used AI experts to create a digital avatar of their deceased son to conceal his death from his 80-year-old, heart-ill mother. The AI clone interacts with her via daily video calls, raising ethical concerns about emotional harm and deception caused by AI technology.
