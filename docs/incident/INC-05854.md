### INC-05854  [↗](/incident/INC-05854.html)

**Social Media Photo Challenges Fuel AI Facial Recognition and Deepfake Risks**  
_2021-05-16 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Dr. Sadi Vural, founder of Ayonix, warns that social media trends like the '20s challenge' are used to collect facial data, strengthening AI facial recognition systems and enabling deepfake technology. While no harm has yet occurred, these practices raise significant privacy and misuse concerns.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
