### INC-07827  [↗](/incident/INC-07827.html)

**AI Deepfakes Fuel Massive IPL Betting Scam in India**  
_2026-05-20 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Cybercriminals used AI-generated deepfakes of Indian celebrities to promote illegal betting platforms across over 1,200 domains, including government websites, during the IPL season. These scams led to significant financial losses for users, with withdrawal requests intentionally denied and victims subjected to coercion and blackmail.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
