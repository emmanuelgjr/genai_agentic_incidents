### INC-05397  [↗](/incident/INC-05397.html)

**AI Video Surveillance Proposed to Prevent Suicides and Crimes in Prisons**  
_2021-03-15 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Lawmakers in Lower Saxony, Germany, have proposed a research project to deploy AI-powered video surveillance in prisons. The system aims to detect risky behaviors, such as suicide attempts or fights, and alert staff to prevent harm. The initiative is still in the planning phase, with no incidents reported yet.
