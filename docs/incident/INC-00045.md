**Advocacy Groups Urge Google to Ban AI-Generated Videos for Children on YouTube**  
_2026-04-02 · real-world · Severity: High_

Over 200 advocacy groups and experts have urged Google and YouTube to ban AI-generated videos on YouTube Kids, citing concerns that such content harms children's development, distorts reality, and displaces human creators. The coalition warns of degraded content quality and calls for stronger protections for young viewers.
