**Stanford AI Predicts Political Affiliation from Facial Images, Raising Rights Concerns**  
_2021-01-12 · real-world · Severity: High_

Stanford researcher Michal Kosinski developed an AI facial recognition system that predicts political affiliation with over 70% accuracy using images from dating sites and Facebook. The technology raises significant privacy and human rights concerns due to its potential for misuse in profiling and discrimination.
