### INC-01366  [↗](/incident/INC-01366.html)

**Man Arrested for Posting AI-Generated Defamatory Images of Indian PM**  
_2026-03-21 · real-world · Severity: High_

Delhi Police arrested Siddhnath Kumar from Bihar for creating and sharing AI-generated objectionable images of Prime Minister Narendra Modi and female leaders on social media. The images, intended to mislead and disrupt public order, led to charges of forgery, defamation, and criminal intimidation. Investigations into the dissemination network are ongoing.
