### INC-07028  [↗](/incident/INC-07028.html)

**Twitter Bots Amplify Conspiracy Theories and Misinformation Ahead of 2020 U.S. Election**  
_2020-10-28 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

USC researchers found thousands of AI-driven Twitter bots spreading conspiracy theories, including QAnon, and political misinformation during the 2020 U.S. election. These bots generated millions of tweets, reaching hundreds of thousands of users, distorting public discourse and amplifying harmful narratives despite efforts to curb disinformation.

**Attack vector:** `misinformation`
