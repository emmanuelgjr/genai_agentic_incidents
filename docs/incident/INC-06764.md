**Lockport School District's Facial Recognition System Sparks Privacy Concerns**  
_2020-01-02 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Lockport Central School District in New York implemented an AI-powered facial recognition and gun detection system to enhance security. Civil rights advocates and the NYCLU raised concerns about student privacy, potential misuse of biometric data, and rights violations, prompting ongoing controversy and calls for the system's suspension.
