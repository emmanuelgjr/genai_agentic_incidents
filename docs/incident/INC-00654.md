### INC-00654  [↗](/incident/INC-00654.html)

**Australian Regulator Warns of AI-Generated Child Abuse Material on X**  
_2026-03-16 · real-world · Severity: High_

Australia's eSafety Commission warned that child sexual exploitation material is particularly systemic and accessible on Elon Musk's platform X, linked to the AI chatbot Grok generating illegal sexualised images of minors. The regulator highlighted new risks in content moderation and online safety due to AI misuse.
