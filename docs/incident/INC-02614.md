### INC-02614  [↗](/incident/INC-02614.html)

**Global ban on AI chatbot DeepSeek over security and privacy fears**  
_2025-01-31 · regulatory · Severity: High_

Following the discovery of an unsecured ClickHouse database leaking DeepSeek user chats and the model's PRC ties, multiple national regulators (Italy, Australia, Taiwan, South Korea) banned DeepSeek from government use within days of its viral launch.

**Affected:** DeepSeek users globally  
**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.1`, `GOVERN-1.4`, `MEASURE-2.10`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0024`, `AML.T0057`
