### INC-05527  [↗](/incident/INC-05527.html)

**China's 'Sharp Eyes' AI Surveillance Program Enables Mass Privacy Violations**  
_2021-03-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

China's 'Sharp Eyes' program uses AI-powered facial recognition and surveillance cameras to monitor nearly all public spaces, enabling authorities and citizens to observe live footage. This pervasive system has led to widespread privacy violations, social control, and targeting of minority groups, constituting significant human rights infringements.
