**Brazilian Workers' Party Warns of AI-Driven Electoral Disinformation Risks**  
_2026-05-10 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The Brazilian Workers' Party (PT) is preparing strategies to counter electoral disinformation, expressing concern over the potential misuse of artificial intelligence and new viralization techniques to spread misinformation during upcoming elections. The party also notes challenges due to changes in the leadership of the Superior Electoral Court (TSE).
