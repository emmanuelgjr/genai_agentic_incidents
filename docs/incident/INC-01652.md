### INC-01652  [↗](/incident/INC-01652.html)

**Poland Bans Chinese and Tesla Vehicles from Military Bases Over AI-Driven Security Risks**  
_2026-01-25 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Polish authorities have banned Chinese-made and Tesla vehicles from military bases due to concerns that their AI-enabled audio, video, and biometric data collection systems could be exploited by foreign intelligence, posing national security risks. The restriction is a preventive measure, with enforcement left to individual base commanders.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM05`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`, `ASI05`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MEASURE-2.10`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0057`
