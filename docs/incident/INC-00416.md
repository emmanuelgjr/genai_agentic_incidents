### INC-00416  [↗](/incident/INC-00416.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfakes Cause Misinformation and Erode Public Trust**  
_2026-03-16 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

AI systems have been used to generate convincing fake videos, images, and voices, leading to widespread misinformation, scams, and erosion of trust in news and historical institutions. Incidents in Germany include fake news videos and AI-generated Holocaust imagery, prompting calls for stricter controls and institutional responses.
