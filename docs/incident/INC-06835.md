### INC-06835  [↗](/incident/INC-06835.html)

**Paytm Payments Bank Uses AI to Block Fraudulent Transactions and Rogue Apps**  
_2020-01-27 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Paytm Payments Bank has deployed AI-driven security features that scan user devices for dangerous apps and detect suspicious transactions. The system alerts users to uninstall risky apps and blocks or slows payments if threats are detected, successfully preventing several fraud attempts and protecting users' financial assets.

**Attack vector:** `other`  

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`
