**AI Uncovers Long-Standing Banking Vulnerabilities, Prompting Global Warning**  
_2026-04-30 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI systems have uncovered long-standing vulnerabilities in banking systems, serving as a global wake-up call, according to Sheetal Chopra of India's NIELIT.
