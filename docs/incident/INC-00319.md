### INC-00319  [↗](/incident/INC-00319.html)

**AI-Driven Romance Scams Impact 27% of Italians**  
_2026-04-28 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

AI-powered tools are increasingly used in cybercrime, enabling large-scale romance scams in Italy. According to Mastercard, 27% of Italians have encountered fake profiles or online relationships leading to financial exploitation, highlighting the significant harm caused by AI-enabled fraud in the country.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`

**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`
