### INC-06336  [↗](/incident/INC-06336.html)

**Airbus Successfully Tests AI-Powered Autonomous Passenger Jet**  
_2020-07-26 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Airbus has successfully tested its A350-1000 XWB passenger jet using AI-driven image recognition and onboard cameras to autonomously taxi, take off, and land without pilot input. While no harm occurred, the deployment of such autonomous flight systems introduces plausible future safety risks if the AI malfunctions.
