### INC-06796  [↗](/incident/INC-06796.html)

**MIT Researchers Test AI App to Diagnose COVID-19 from Cough Sounds**  
_2020-04-16 · real-world · Severity: High_

Researchers at MIT, led by Brian Subirana, are developing and testing an AI-based app that analyzes cough sounds to detect COVID-19. Clinical trials are underway in Barcelona, with public participation sought for anonymous cough recordings. No harm or misuse has been reported; the project is in the research phase.
