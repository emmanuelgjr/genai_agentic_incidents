### INC-05406  [↗](/incident/INC-05406.html)

**AI-Driven Social Media Failures Contribute to Capitol Hill Violence**  
_2021-01-08 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

AI-powered content moderation and recommendation systems on platforms like Facebook, Twitter, and YouTube failed to prevent the spread of violent and false content that incited the Capitol Hill siege, resulting in five deaths. Delayed action by these platforms allowed harmful content to proliferate, indirectly contributing to real-world harm.
