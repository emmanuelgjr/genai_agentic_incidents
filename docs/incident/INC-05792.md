### INC-05792  [↗](/incident/INC-05792.html)

**P&G Collaborates on AI-Driven Tracking to Bypass Apple Privacy Rules in China**  
_2021-04-08 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Procter & Gamble (P&G) partnered with Chinese tech firms and trade groups to develop and test an AI-enabled device fingerprinting system (CAID) that collects iPhone user data for targeted ads, aiming to circumvent Apple's new privacy protections requiring user consent. No direct harm reported yet, but privacy risks are significant.

**Attack vector:** `other`
