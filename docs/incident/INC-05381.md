### INC-05381  [↗](/incident/INC-05381.html)

**AI Deepfake App Used to Create and Distribute Non-Consensual Fake Nude Images**  
_2021-04-06 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A woman received an AI-generated fake nude image of herself, created from her swimsuit photo using a deepfake app like DeepNude. Despite the app's removal, cracked versions persist online, enabling malicious actors to violate privacy and cause psychological harm by distributing non-consensual explicit images.
