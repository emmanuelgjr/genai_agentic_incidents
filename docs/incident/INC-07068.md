**US Air Force Advances Testing of AI-Enabled Autonomous Swarming Munitions**  
_2020-07-14 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The US Air Force is preparing to test the Golden Horde program, which equips bombs and missiles with AI-driven autonomy modules, enabling them to network, share information, and reprioritize targets mid-flight. While no harm has occurred yet, these autonomous weapon systems raise significant concerns about potential future risks and misuse.
