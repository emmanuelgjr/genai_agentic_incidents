### INC-06535  [↗](/incident/INC-06535.html)

**European AI Surveillance Technology Linked to Human Rights Abuses in China**  
_2020-09-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

Dutch company Noldus and other European firms sold AI-based facial and emotion recognition software to Chinese security agencies and universities, including in Xinjiang. Amnesty International reports these technologies have enabled mass surveillance and contributed to human rights violations against minority groups, prompting calls for stricter EU export regulations.
