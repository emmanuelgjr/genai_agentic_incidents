### INC-00293  [↗](/incident/INC-00293.html)

**AI-Driven Disinformation Fuels Harm Against Migrants**  
_2026-04-16 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

AI technologies are increasingly used to create and spread sophisticated disinformation targeting migrants, leading to real-world harms such as discrimination and violence. Organizations like the International Organization for Migration and EFE are responding with training to help journalists and the public detect and counteract these AI-enabled false narratives.

**Attack vector:** `misinformation`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0058`
