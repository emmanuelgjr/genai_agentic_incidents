### INC-07766  [↗](/incident/INC-07766.html)

**AI Enables Fingerprint Theft from 'Peace' Sign Photos, Leading to Security Risks**  
_2026-05-17 · real-world · Severity: High_

Experts warn that AI-powered image analysis can extract fingerprint data from photos showing the 'peace' sign, posing risks of biometric data theft and financial fraud. Demonstrations in China have shown actual attempts to use such data for unauthorized access, highlighting new security threats from AI misuse.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`  

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`
