### INC-01103  [↗](/incident/INC-01103.html)

**Google and Greystoke Understate Carbon Emissions of UK AI Data Centres**  
_2026-05-09 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Developers for Google and Greystoke significantly understated the carbon emissions of three proposed UK AI datacentres in planning documents, presenting figures five times lower than actual estimates. This miscalculation could lead to underestimating the environmental impact of these AI infrastructure projects, raising concerns about transparency and climate accountability.
