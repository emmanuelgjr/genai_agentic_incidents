### INC-05593  [↗](/incident/INC-05593.html)

**Experts Warn of AI Risks in Viral '20s Challenge' Social Media Trend**  
_2021-05-06 · real-world · Severity: High_

Experts caution that the viral '20s Challenge,' where users share old photos online, enables mass collection of facial data. This data could be exploited to train AI facial recognition and age progression algorithms, posing future privacy and security risks, though no direct harm has yet occurred.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`  

**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-4.1`, `MEASURE-2.10`
