**TRATON and TuSimple Partner to Develop Autonomous Trucks in Europe**  
_2020-09-23 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

TRATON Group and TuSimple have formed a global partnership to develop and deploy SAE Level 4 autonomous driving technology in heavy-duty trucks, launching pilot routes in Sweden. While no incidents have occurred, the deployment of self-driving trucks presents credible future risks of AI-related accidents or malfunctions.
