**Nigeria Plans Deployment of AI-Powered Surveillance for National Security**  
_2026-05-13 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Nigeria's government is partnering with international firms to deploy the Multi-domain Hybrid Intelligence Shield, an AI-driven surveillance and threat identification system, to modernize its defense against terrorism, banditry, and other crimes. The initiative, still in planning and demonstration phases, raises potential future risks of misuse or unintended consequences.
