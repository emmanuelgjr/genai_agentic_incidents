### INC-00717  [↗](/incident/INC-00717.html)

**BSE Warns of Repeated Deepfake Scams Targeting Investors**  
_2026-04-24 · real-world · Severity: High_

India's BSE Limited has warned investors about a fourth deepfake video in four months, falsely depicting CEO Sundararaman Ramamurthy giving investment advice. The AI-generated videos mislead viewers with false promises of high returns, urging them to join private groups, posing financial risks and eroding trust.
