### INC-00215  [↗](/incident/INC-00215.html)

**AI System 'Massima Tranquillità' Blocks Phone Scams in Italy**  
_2026-03-25 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The AI-powered app 'Massima Tranquillità' has been launched in Italy to automatically block spam and fraudulent phone calls, targeting up to 10 million unwanted calls daily. The system aims to prevent economic harm from phone scams, which have caused over €560 million in losses.

**Attack vector:** `other`  

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`
