### INC-00526  [↗](/incident/INC-00526.html)

**AI-Manipulated Images Used to Bypass Facial Recognition in Bank Fraud Scheme in Japan**  
_2026-03-04 · real-world · Severity: High_

A group in Japan used AI-powered apps to create manipulated or 3D images that bypassed facial recognition systems for online banking. This allowed them to fraudulently open bank accounts and secure loans, resulting in financial losses. Police arrested suspects and are investigating the broader criminal network.

**Attack vector:** `other`  

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`
