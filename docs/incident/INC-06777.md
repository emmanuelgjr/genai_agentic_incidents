### INC-06777  [↗](/incident/INC-06777.html)

**Mercadona's Facial Recognition System Sparks Privacy and Legal Concerns**  
_2020-07-02 · real-world · Severity: High_

Mercadona deployed facial recognition technology in 40 supermarkets to identify individuals with restraining orders. The system, supplied by AnyVision, has triggered significant public and expert backlash over privacy invasion, potential legal violations, and risks of misidentification, raising concerns about data protection and human rights compliance.
