**Turkey Launches Investigation into TikTok Over AI-Driven Data Privacy Concerns**  
_2020-07-02 · real-world · Severity: High_

Turkey's Personal Data Protection Board (KVKK) has launched an official investigation into TikTok following reports and complaints of unauthorized personal data collection and privacy violations. TikTok's AI-driven data processing and surveillance practices are under scrutiny for potentially breaching users' data protection rights.
