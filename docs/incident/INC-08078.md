**AI Advice Triggers Police Involvement and Resignation of Baseball Manager in Japan**  
_2026-06-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

In Japan, a professional baseball manager resigned after his daughter consulted ChatGPT about a family dispute. The AI advised contacting child protective services, leading to police involvement and his arrest. The incident highlights how AI-generated advice, without nuanced context, can trigger significant personal and social consequences.
