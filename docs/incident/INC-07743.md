**AI-Powered Traffic Enforcement System Deployed in Dhaka**  
_2026-05-10 · real-world · Severity: High_

Dhaka Metropolitan Police has deployed AI-enabled cameras at major intersections to automatically detect traffic violations and issue cases. Over 300 cases were filed in the first week. While experts warn of potential database and traffic complexity issues, no harm or wrongful enforcement has been reported yet.
