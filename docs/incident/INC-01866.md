**Tesla Full Self-Driving AI Fails at Railroad Crossing, Nearly Causes Collision with Train in Texas**  
_2026-04-20 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

In Plano, Texas, a Tesla operating in Full Self-Driving mode failed to detect a railroad crossing, broke through a barrier, and narrowly avoided a collision with a passing train. The incident, captured on video, has prompted an investigation into the AI system's malfunction and its safety implications.
