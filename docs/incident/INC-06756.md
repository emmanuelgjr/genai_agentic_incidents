### INC-06756  [↗](/incident/INC-06756.html)

**Kubota and NVIDIA Partner to Develop Fully Autonomous AI-Powered Farm Machinery**  
_2020-10-06 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Kubota and NVIDIA have formed a strategic partnership to accelerate the development of edge AI technology for fully autonomous agricultural machinery. The collaboration aims to enable next-generation unmanned tractors capable of real-time decision-making and operation, raising potential future risks if AI malfunctions or makes incorrect decisions.
