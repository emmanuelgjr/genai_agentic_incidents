**Majority of U.S. Teens Use AI to Create Sexualized Images, Study Finds**  
_2026-03-18 · real-world · Severity: High_

A nationwide study led by Chad Steel of George Mason University reveals that over half of U.S. teens aged 13-17 have used generative AI tools to create sexualized images, often without consent. This widespread use has resulted in privacy violations, psychological harm, and legal actions, highlighting serious ethical and legal concerns.
