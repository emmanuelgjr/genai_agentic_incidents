**Ondas Acquires Israeli AI Defense Firm Omnisys for Battlefield Orchestration**  
_2026-05-18 · real-world · Severity: High_

Ondas Holdings announced the $200 million acquisition of Omnisys, an Israeli developer of AI-powered Battle Resource Optimization (BRO) software for autonomous defense planning and real-time battlefield decision-making. The BRO platform, operational for 25 years, poses credible risks of harm in military contexts but no incident has occurred.
