**AI-Powered ECG Systems Enhance Emergency Cardiac Care in Taiwan**  
_2021-04-28 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Taiwanese hospitals, including Tri-Service General Hospital and China Medical University Hospital, have deployed AI-assisted ECG interpretation systems in ambulances and clinics. These systems enable rapid, accurate diagnosis of acute heart conditions, allowing earlier intervention and reducing treatment delays, thereby improving patient outcomes and preventing harm from cardiac emergencies.
