### INC-01265  [↗](/incident/INC-01265.html)

**Jakarta Officials Misuse AI to Fabricate Public Service Reports**  
_2026-04-09 · real-world · Severity: High_

PPSU officers in Kalisari, Jakarta Timur, used AI to manipulate documentation and fabricate responses to citizen complaints, prompting public controversy and disciplinary action, including the temporary suspension of the local head. Jakarta's governor plans a town hall to address the misuse and reinforce ethical AI practices among public service staff.
