**RCMP Faces Lawsuit Over Use of Clearview AI Facial Recognition Technology in Canada**  
_2020-07-13 · real-world · Severity: High_

A Quebec photographer has filed a class-action lawsuit against the RCMP, seeking the destruction of images obtained via Clearview AI's facial recognition system. The lawsuit alleges mass privacy and copyright violations affecting Canadians, prompting investigations and the suspension of Clearview AI's services in Canada by privacy authorities.
