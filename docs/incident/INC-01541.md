### INC-01541  [↗](/incident/INC-01541.html)

**North Korean Konni Hackers Use AI-Generated Malware to Target Blockchain Developers in Asia-Pacific**  
_2026-01-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

The North Korean hacking group Konni has launched a phishing campaign using AI-generated PowerShell malware to target blockchain developers and engineers in Japan, Australia, and India. The AI-assisted malware, delivered via fake project documents, compromises development environments and crypto assets, highlighting the growing use of AI in cyberattacks.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`
