### INC-06887  [↗](/incident/INC-06887.html)

**Romanian Competition Council Warns of AI-Driven Anti-Competitive Risks from Big Data Technologies**  
_2020-12-03 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The Romanian Competition Council warns that the use of Big Data technologies, which rely on AI and predictive analytics, could enable companies to coordinate prices and restrict data access, potentially leading to anti-competitive behaviors and consumer harm. No actual incidents have occurred, but the risks prompt calls for regulatory attention.

**Attack vector:** `other`
