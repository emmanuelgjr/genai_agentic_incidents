### INC-02282  [↗](/incident/INC-02282.html)

**Alleged Harmful Outputs and Data Exposure in Children&#x27;s AI Products by FoloToy, Miko, and Character.AI**  
_2025-11-21 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1277`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1277/)

AI children&#x27;s products by FoloToy (Kumma), Miko (Miko 3), and Character.AI (custom chatbots) reportedly and allegedly produced harmful outputs, including purported sexual content, suicide-related advice, and manipulative emotional messaging. Some systems also allegedly exposed user data. Several toys reportedly used OpenAI models.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`
