### INC-00515  [↗](/incident/INC-00515.html)

**AI-Generated Visual Disinformation Undermines Societal Trust**  
_2026-03-15 · real-world · Severity: High_

Experts warn that AI-generated images and videos are increasingly used in warfare and social media to manipulate public perception and spread disinformation. These synthetic contents, produced and disseminated by conflicting parties and social media users, distort reality, undermine social cohesion, and harm communities by eroding trust and information integrity.
