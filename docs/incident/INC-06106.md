### INC-06106  [↗](/incident/INC-06106.html)

**TikTok AI Algorithm Failure Exposes Users to Pornography and Violent Extremist Content**  
_2021-04-18 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A failure in TikTok's AI-driven content moderation and recommendation system allowed users, including minors, to be widely exposed to pornographic and violent extremist material. The incident, linked to the viral 'Don't search this up' trend, led to over 50 million interactions before TikTok began banning related accounts and hashtags.

**Attack vector:** `other`
