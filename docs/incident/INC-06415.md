**ByteDance AI Algorithms Used to Spread Chinese Government Propaganda via Toutiao App**  
_2020-09-06 · real-world · Severity: High_

Leaked documents reveal ByteDance's AI-powered news app, Toutiao, is used by the Chinese Communist Party to disseminate government propaganda and censor content. The AI system targets users with specific information, raising concerns about manipulation, censorship, and violations of rights to unbiased information and freedom of expression.
