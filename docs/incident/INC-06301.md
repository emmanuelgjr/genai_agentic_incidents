**AI-Powered Autonomous Air Combat Raises Future Warfare Risks**  
_2020-11-10 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

China, the US, and the UK are advancing AI-driven autonomous air combat systems for next-generation fighter jets and drones. These developments, including AI outperforming human pilots in simulations and the UK's Tempest project, raise concerns about future loss of human control, ethical challenges, and potential unintended consequences in warfare.
