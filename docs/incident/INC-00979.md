**Facebook's AI Moderation Restricts Orbán Viktor's Posts Before Elections**  
_2026-03-18 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Facebook's AI-driven content moderation restricted Hungarian Prime Minister Orbán Viktor's posts ahead of national elections, following mass reporting allegedly encouraged by a Tisza Party member. The incident raises concerns about transparency and political influence in AI moderation of political content on social media platforms.
