### INC-07777  [↗](/incident/INC-07777.html)

**ChatGPT Used in Exam Fraud Leads to Arrests in Goiás**  
_2026-05-18 · real-world · Severity: High_

A candidate and his wife were arrested in Goiânia, Brazil, for using ChatGPT to cheat during a public exam for auditor fiscal. The candidate photographed questions in the bathroom and sent them to his wife, who used ChatGPT to generate answers and returned them via WhatsApp, undermining exam integrity.

**Attack vector:** `other`  

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`
