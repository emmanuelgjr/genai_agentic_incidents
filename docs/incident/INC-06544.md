**Experts Warn of AI Sex Robots Simulating Harmful and Illegal Scenarios**  
_2020-02-13 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Researchers and ethicists warn that AI-powered sex robots are being programmed to simulate rape scenarios and resemble children, raising serious psychological, moral, and legal concerns. The lack of regulation enables the creation and sale of such robots, potentially normalizing harmful behaviors and posing risks to individuals and society.
