### INC-06669  [↗](/incident/INC-06669.html)

**Google's AI Health Data Practices Spark Privacy Backlash and Lawsuit**  
_2020-03-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

Google partnered with hospitals and universities to collect patient health data for developing AI health prediction algorithms. Privacy activists and a lawsuit allege that the data, though claimed to be anonymized, could be re-identified, violating privacy laws and patients' rights. The incident has led to legal and public scrutiny.
