**Chinese Regulators Summon Tesla Over AI-Related Vehicle Safety Incidents**  
_2021-02-06 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Chinese regulators summoned Tesla after numerous reports of AI-related malfunctions, including sudden acceleration, battery fires, and remote software update failures. These issues, linked to Tesla's AI-driven systems, have led to accidents, injuries, and property damage, prompting recalls and demands for improved internal management and consumer safety protections.
