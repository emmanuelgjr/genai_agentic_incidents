**AI-Powered Robots and Drones Used in Ukrainian Military Operations**  
_2026-05-12 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Ukrainian and Russian forces are increasingly deploying AI-enabled robots and drones in active combat, with Ukraine reportedly conducting operations to reclaim territory using only autonomous systems. This marks a significant shift in warfare, as AI-driven weapons directly contribute to harm and escalate ethical concerns about future conflicts.
