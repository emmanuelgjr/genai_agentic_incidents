### INC-00751  [↗](/incident/INC-00751.html)

**ChatGPT Linked to Worsening Mental Health and Legal Action Prompts Safeguards**  
_2026-03-01 · real-world · Severity: High_

Research from Denmark and multiple lawsuits in California reveal that AI chatbots, particularly OpenAI's ChatGPT, have contributed to worsening mental health symptoms, including suicidality and psychosis. In response, OpenAI is introducing a 'trusted contact' feature to alert emergency contacts during crises, raising privacy and ethical concerns.
