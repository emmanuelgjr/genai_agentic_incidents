**Tesla Autopilot Misuse: Driver Sleeps at 82 MPH, Stopped by Police**  
_2021-05-18 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

A 38-year-old Illinois man was cited for inattentive driving after being found asleep behind the wheel of his Tesla, which was operating on Autopilot at 82 mph in Wisconsin. Police followed the vehicle for over two miles before the driver responded, highlighting the dangers of overreliance on AI driving systems.
