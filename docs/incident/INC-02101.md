**X Suspends 800 Million Accounts Over AI-Driven Manipulation and Spam**  
_2026-03-10 · real-world · Severity: High_

In 2024, social media platform X (formerly Twitter) suspended 800 million accounts for violating policies on manipulation and spam, largely attributed to state-backed actors, notably from Russia. AI systems were used to detect and counter these large-scale disinformation and manipulation attempts, raising concerns about election interference and community harm.
