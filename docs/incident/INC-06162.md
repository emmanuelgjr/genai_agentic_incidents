**US General Warns of AI and Robotics Increasing Global Instability**  
_2021-05-05 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

US General Mark Milley warned that the rise of China and emerging technologies like AI and robotics could disrupt global stability and play a decisive role in future conflicts. He compared the current era to past periods of major geopolitical upheaval, highlighting the potential risks posed by advanced military technologies.
