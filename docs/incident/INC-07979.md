### INC-07979  [↗](/incident/INC-07979.html)

**AI chatbots used to breach Mexican government data (150GB exfiltration)**  
_2026-02-25 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Attackers manipulated Anthropic's Claude and OpenAI's ChatGPT into bypassing safety measures, generating exploit scripts and identifying vulnerabilities used in cyberattacks that stole 150GB of taxpayer and voter data from Mexican government agencies between December 2025 and January 2026.

**Affected:** Mexican government / taxpayers  
**Attack vector:** `jailbreak`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM01`, `LLM06`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI01`, `ASI02`, `ASI10`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.1`, `GOVERN-1.4`, `MANAGE-2.4`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048`, `AML.T0051`, `AML.T0053`, `AML.T0054`
