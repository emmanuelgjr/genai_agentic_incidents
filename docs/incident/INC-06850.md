### INC-06850  [↗](/incident/INC-06850.html)

**Polish Government Deploys AI App for Quarantine Enforcement**  
_2020-03-19 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Polish government launched the 'Kwarantanna domowa' app, which uses facial recognition and geolocation AI to monitor individuals under quarantine. Users must send periodic selfies to confirm their location. While intended for public health, the system raises concerns about privacy and potential misuse, though no harm has been reported yet.
