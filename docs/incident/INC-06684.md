### INC-06684  [↗](/incident/INC-06684.html)

**Hyundai Recalls 2020 Sonata and Nexo Over AI Parking Assist Malfunction**  
_2020-04-17 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Hyundai recalled nearly 12,000 2020 Sonata and Nexo vehicles due to a software glitch in the AI-powered Remote Smart Parking Assist system. The malfunction could cause cars to move unintentionally or fail to stop, posing a safety risk. Dealers will reprogram the system to address the issue.
