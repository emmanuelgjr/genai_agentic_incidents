### INC-05530  [↗](/incident/INC-05530.html)

**Chinese AI Face-Swapping App Raises Privacy and Security Concerns in Taiwan**  
_2021-02-05 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Chinese AI face-swapping app 'Quyan' collects users' facial images and personal data, raising serious privacy and security concerns in Taiwan. Authorities warn that biometric data may be transmitted to Chinese government surveillance systems, with reports of unauthorized account access and potential misuse for profiling, surveillance, or fraud.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.10`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0057`, `AML.T0058`
