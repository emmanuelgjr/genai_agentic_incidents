### INC-00481  [↗](/incident/INC-00481.html)

**AI-Generated Malware Obscures Cyberattack Origins, Hindering Detection**  
_2026-03-10 · real-world · Severity: High_

Cybercriminals are increasingly using generative AI and large language models to create malware and phishing campaigns that erase human traces, making cyberattacks harder to detect and attribute. This AI-driven approach, highlighted by Kaspersky researchers, has led to more effective and damaging cybercrimes by automating and anonymizing malicious activities.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`, `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.10`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`
