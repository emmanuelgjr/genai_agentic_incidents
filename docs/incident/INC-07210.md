### INC-07210  [↗](/incident/INC-07210.html)

**MegaFace facial-recognition dataset raises privacy and liability concerns**  
_2019 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

University of Washington's MegaFace training dataset, sourced from Flickr photos uploaded under permissive Creative Commons licenses, was used to train face-recognition systems by Chinese surveillance contractors and US defense agencies, raising consent and re-identification concerns.

**Affected:** Flickr users in MegaFace  
**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM04`, `LLM06`, `LLM07`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI04`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.1`, `GOVERN-6.1`, `MAP-3.5`, `MAP-4.2`, `MEASURE-2.10`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0010`, `AML.T0020`, `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0053`, `AML.T0056`, `AML.T0057`
