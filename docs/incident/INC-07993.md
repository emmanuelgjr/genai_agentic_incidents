### INC-07993  [↗](/incident/INC-07993.html)

**AI-Generated Hoax Image Causes Public Panic in Denpasar**  
_2026-05-30 · real-world · Severity: High_

In Denpasar, Indonesia, an AI-generated image depicting a 'pocong begal' (ghost mugger) went viral on social media, causing public unrest. Police investigation confirmed the image was fabricated using AI by minors, leading to widespread misinformation and community concern before authorities clarified it as a hoax.
