### INC-01977  [↗](/incident/INC-01977.html)

**UNESCO Warns of AI-Driven Revenue Losses in Creative Industries**  
_2026-02-18 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

UNESCO reports that generative AI could reduce global revenues for creators in music and audiovisual sectors by up to 24% by 2028. The organization urges stronger public policies to protect artists and address growing inequalities and threats to artistic freedom caused by AI-driven content production.
