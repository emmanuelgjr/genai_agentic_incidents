**Saudi-Led Coalition Intercepts Houthi AI-Enabled Drone Attack on Civilian Airport**  
_2020-09-07 · real-world · Severity: High_

Houthi militants launched AI-enabled drones targeting Saudi Arabia's Abha International Airport, aiming at civilian infrastructure. The Saudi-led coalition successfully intercepted the drones, preventing potential harm. The incident highlights the use of autonomous drone systems in military attacks against civilian targets, raising concerns over AI-driven warfare.
