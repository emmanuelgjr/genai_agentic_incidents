### INC-05369  [↗](/incident/INC-05369.html)

**12-Year-Old Sues TikTok Over Illegal Data Use by AI Algorithm**  
_2021-01-01 · real-world · Severity: High_

A 12-year-old British girl is suing TikTok, alleging the platform's AI-powered recommendation algorithm illegally collects and processes children's data, violating UK and EU data protection laws. The case, supported by the Children's Commissioner for England, seeks stronger safeguards and potential deletion of children's data by court order.
