**Elbit Systems Acquires Bluewhite Robotics to Expand AI-Powered Autonomous Defense Capabilities**  
_2026-05-27 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Elbit Systems' subsidiary FUSE has acquired Israeli company Bluewhite Robotics, which develops AI-powered autonomous solutions for off-road and defense applications. The integration aims to enhance Elbit's portfolio of unmanned ground vehicles and robotic warfare systems, raising potential future risks associated with autonomous military technologies. The event is centered in Israel.
