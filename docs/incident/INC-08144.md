### INC-08144  [↗](/incident/INC-08144.html)

**China Warns of Data and Security Risks from Unregulated AI Relay Stations**  
_2026-06-07 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Chinese authorities have warned that unregulated 'AI relay stations'—platforms providing access to multiple AI models—pose significant risks, including user privacy breaches, data theft, malicious software implantation, and unauthorized data transfers abroad. These incidents have led to privacy violations and potential national security threats due to weak security and improper data handling.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`  

**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-4.1`, `MEASURE-2.10`
