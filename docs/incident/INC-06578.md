### INC-06578  [↗](/incident/INC-06578.html)

**Facebook's AI Algorithms Amplify Misinformation Since 2016**  
_2020-10-12 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Research by the German Marshall Fund shows that engagement with misinformation on Facebook has doubled or tripled since 2016, despite platform efforts. AI-driven recommendation algorithms are amplifying untrustworthy and misleading content, increasing societal harm by spreading falsehoods and undermining public discourse.

**Attack vector:** `misinformation`
