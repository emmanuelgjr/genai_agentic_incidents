### INC-07877  [↗](/incident/INC-07877.html)

**Artist Paco Roca's Poster Plagiarized by AI for Madrid Book Fair**  
_2026-05-26 · real-world · Severity: High_

Spanish illustrator Paco Roca publicly denounced the use of generative AI to create a promotional poster for San Martín de la Vega's Book Fair that closely copied his original artwork for Valencia's Fira del Llibre. The incident sparked debate on AI and copyright, prompting legal action and the poster's removal.
