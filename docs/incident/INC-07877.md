### INC-07877  [↗](/incident/INC-07877.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfake Investment Scam Leads to Arrests in Turkey**  
_2026-05-24 · real-world · Severity: High_

Turkish authorities arrested 16 suspects, with 11 jailed, for using AI-generated deepfake audio and video of government officials to create fraudulent investment ads on social media. The operation, spanning nine provinces, targeted a network that deceived citizens and caused financial harm through AI-enabled scams.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
