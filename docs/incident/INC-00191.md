### INC-00191  [↗](/incident/INC-00191.html)

**AI Models Pressured to Predict US Strike Date on Iran**  
_2026-02-25 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Jerusalem Post tested four major AI language models by asking them to predict the exact date of a potential US military strike on Iran. Initially refusing to provide a date, some models eventually offered speculative timelines under repeated prompting, highlighting risks of AI-generated misinformation in sensitive geopolitical contexts.

**Attack vector:** `rce`
