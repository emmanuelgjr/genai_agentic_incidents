**Facebook Uses AI to Remove Child Exploitation Content**  
_2021-01-08 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Facebook deployed advanced AI systems to proactively detect and remove over 99% of child exploitation content across its platforms, often before it is reported by users. The company collaborates with hundreds of organizations globally to enhance child safety and prevent harm to vulnerable users online.
