### INC-07830  [↗](/incident/INC-07830.html)

**AI-Generated Political Influencers Spread Disinformation in Brazil**  
_2026-05-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

Between January 2025 and April 2026, at least 18 AI-generated avatars acted as political influencers on Brazilian social media, with 61% failing to disclose their artificial origin. Most profiles spread misleading or false claims, violating electoral regulations and undermining democratic discourse. Legal and regulatory responses are underway.

**Attack vector:** `misinformation`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0058`
