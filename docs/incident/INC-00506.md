### INC-00506  [↗](/incident/INC-00506.html)

**AI-Generated TikTok Videos Spread Sexist and Racist Stereotypes**  
_2026-03-30 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

On TikTok, AI-generated animated videos featuring fruit and vegetable characters have gone viral, spreading openly sexist and racist stereotypes. These short clips, created and monetized by content creators, have reached millions, raising concerns about the harmful impact and normalization of discriminatory narratives through AI-driven content.
