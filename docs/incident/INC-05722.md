**Israel Unveils AI-Enabled Autonomous Sea Breaker Missile System**  
_2021-06-30 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Israel's Rafael Advanced Defense Systems unveiled the Sea Breaker, a fifth-generation, AI-enabled autonomous missile system capable of precision strikes on land and maritime targets up to 300 km away. The system uses AI for autonomous target identification and engagement, raising significant concerns about future risks of harm from autonomous lethal weaponry.
