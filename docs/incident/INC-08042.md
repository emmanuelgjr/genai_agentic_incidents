**AI Cameras Deployed Across Brisbane to Combat Illegal Dumping**  
_2026-06-02 · real-world · Severity: High_

Brisbane City Council has deployed over 200 AI-assisted cameras in suburbs like Moorooka, Sunnybank Hills, Forest Lake, and Annerley to monitor and identify illegal dumping offenders. The AI system detects vehicles, number plates, and people, leading to increased fines and enforcement, reducing environmental and community harm from illegal waste disposal.
