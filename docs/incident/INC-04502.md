### INC-04502  [↗](/incident/INC-04502.html)

**Books3 dataset of pirated books used to train Llama and Bloom**  
_2023-08 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Books3, a 196,640-book corpus largely drawn from the pirate library Bibliotik, was used to train Meta's LLaMA, EleutherAI's GPT-J/Neo, and Bloom; subsequent legal actions targeted the propagation of pirated copyrighted material through foundation models.

**Affected:** Authors / downstream LLM operators  
**Attack vector:** `model-poisoning`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM03`, `LLM04`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI04`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.1`, `GOVERN-6.1`, `MAP-4.2`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0010`, `AML.T0020`  

**References:**
- [AIAAIC - Books3 dataset](https://www.aiaaic.org/aiaaic-repository/ai-algorithmic-and-automation-incidents/books3-dataset) _(advisory)_
