**Controversial Use of Facial Recognition AI by US Law Enforcement Leads to Rights Violations and Wrongful Arrests**  
_2021-01-21 · real-world · Severity: High_

US law enforcement agencies, including the Staten Island District Attorney's Office, have used Clearview AI's facial recognition technology for investigations and arrests. This has resulted in privacy violations, wrongful arrests, and concerns over civil liberties, particularly affecting marginalized communities due to the technology's inaccuracy and lack of oversight.
