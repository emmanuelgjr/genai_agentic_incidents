**AI-Enabled Bayraktar TB2 Drones Shift Balance in Nagorno-Karabakh Conflict**  
_2021-02-25 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Baykar's AI-powered Bayraktar TB2 armed drones played a decisive role in the Nagorno-Karabakh conflict, destroying over 50 air defense systems, 140 tanks, and hundreds of rocket launchers. Russian AI-enabled defense systems failed to counter these drones, highlighting the significant impact and risks of autonomous AI systems in modern warfare.
