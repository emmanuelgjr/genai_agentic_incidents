### INC-01401  [↗](/incident/INC-01401.html)

**Meta and Google Fined for AI-Driven Social Media Harm to Teen**  
_2026-03-25 · real-world · Severity: High_

A Los Angeles court found Meta (Instagram) and Google (YouTube) liable for a young Californian's mental health issues, attributing her depression to addiction fostered by the platforms' AI-driven content recommendation systems. The companies were ordered to pay $6 million in damages, setting a precedent for similar lawsuits.
