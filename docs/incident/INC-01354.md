**Lockheed Martin Tests AI-Enhanced Target Identification on F-35 Fighter Jets**  
_2026-02-23 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Lockheed Martin has successfully tested an AI-powered combat identification system on F-35 fighter jets at Nellis Air Force Base, Nevada. The AI system autonomously suggests potential combat targets to pilots, aiming to improve situational awareness and decision-making. While no harm has occurred, the technology raises concerns about future military risks.
