### INC-12490  [↗](/incident/INC-12490.html)

**Cross-site Scripting in microweber**  
_2022-03 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: Medium_

CVEs: `CVE-2022-0928`
CWEs: `CWE-79`
CVSS: **6.8** — `CVSS:3.0/AV:N/AC:L/PR:H/UI:R/S:U/C:H/I:H/A:H`

Microweber drag and drop website builder and CMS with e-commerce. Cross-site Scripting (XSS) discovered in microweber prior to 1.2.12. There is currently no known workaround, users are recommended to update to version 1.2.12.

**Affected:** composer/microweber/microweber  
**Attack vector:** `xss`
