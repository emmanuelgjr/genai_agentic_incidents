**Australian Watchdog Warns of AI-Driven Money Laundering Surge**  
_2026-05-12 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Australia's financial intelligence agency, Austrac, warns that criminals are increasingly using AI to automate and scale money laundering, fabricate identities, and forge documents. This has led to a significant rise in sophisticated financial crimes, with AI-assisted illicit flows through decentralized exchanges surging 45% year-over-year.
