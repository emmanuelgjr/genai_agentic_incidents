**Fujitsu AI System Reduces Ship Collision Risks in Tokyo Bay Trial**  
_2020-04-16 · real-world · Severity: High_

Fujitsu, in partnership with the Japan Coast Guard, tested its Zinrai AI system in Tokyo Bay from December 2019 to March 2020. The AI accurately predicted vessel collision risks, enabling earlier warnings and improved maritime safety, demonstrating AI's effectiveness in preventing accidents and reducing harm in marine traffic control.
