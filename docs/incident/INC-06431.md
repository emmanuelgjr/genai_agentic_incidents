**China Deploys AI-Enabled Suicide Drone Swarms for Military Use**  
_2020-10-15 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

China has unveiled and tested AI-powered suicide drone swarms capable of autonomous, coordinated attacks on ground targets, including tanks and troops. These drones, launched from trucks or helicopters, use AI for flight, targeting, and decision-making, raising significant concerns about their potential for lethal harm in military conflicts.
