### INC-06889  [↗](/incident/INC-06889.html)

**Russia Develops AI-Based Autonomous Satellite Control Systems**  
_2020-11-21 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Russian company 'Russian Space Systems' is developing AI-driven autonomous systems to control large satellite constellations with minimal human intervention. While aiming to automate management of thousands of satellites, the technology poses potential risks, such as system collapse or disruption of critical infrastructure, if malfunctions occur.

**Attack vector:** `other`
