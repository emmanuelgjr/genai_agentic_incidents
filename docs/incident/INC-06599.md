### INC-06599  [↗](/incident/INC-06599.html)

**Facial Recognition AI Sparks Global Privacy and Rights Violations Debate**  
_2020-02-17 · real-world · Severity: High_

Law enforcement agencies worldwide, including in Sacramento and Australia, have used AI-powered facial recognition systems like Clearview AI, leading to privacy violations, unauthorized data scraping, and wrongful identifications. These practices have triggered legal challenges, moratoriums, and widespread concerns over fundamental rights and discriminatory impacts, especially for marginalized communities.
