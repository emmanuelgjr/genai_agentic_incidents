### INC-06607  [↗](/incident/INC-06607.html)

**Facial Recognition Misuse in China: Property Theft and Legal Disputes Highlight AI Risks**  
_2020-06-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

A Guangxi homeowner lost his property after being tricked into facial recognition verification, leading to unauthorized transfer and financial loss. Separately, a legal case in Hangzhou challenges the mandatory use of facial recognition for zoo entry, raising concerns over privacy, data misuse, and inadequate legal protections for biometric data in China.
