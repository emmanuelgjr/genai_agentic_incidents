**Utah Suspends AI Policing Contract Over CEO's Extremist Past and Bias Concerns**  
_2020-05-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

Utah suspended its $20 million contract with Banjo, a company providing AI-powered crime detection software, after revelations of the CEO's white supremacist past.
