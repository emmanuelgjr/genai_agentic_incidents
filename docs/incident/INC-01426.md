**Mexico City Airport to Deploy AI-Powered Anti-Drone System for 2026 World Cup**  
_2026-03-14 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Mexican authorities plan to install an AI-driven anti-drone system at Mexico City International Airport (AICM) ahead of the 2026 World Cup. The system will use intelligent algorithms and advanced sensors to detect, identify, and neutralize unauthorized drones, aiming to prevent potential security risks to passengers and airport operations.
