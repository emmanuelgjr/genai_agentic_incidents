**Norwegian Student Reportedly Used AI-Generated Deepfake Videos in Spanish Coursework at University of South-Eastern Norway**  
_2024-08-02 · real-world · Severity: Medium_
AIID: [`#1245`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1245/)

A student at the University of South-Eastern Norway reportedly submitted multiple deepfake videos for Spanish coursework, featuring an AI-generated likeness and synthetic voice. Faculty reportedly noted unnatural facial movements and inconsistent language ability. The case was reportedly ruled academic misconduct, resulting in annulled grades and a year-long suspension, marking one of Norway&#x27;s first confirmed deepfake-related cheating cases.
