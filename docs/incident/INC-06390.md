**BAE Systems Develops AI Analytics for WMD Threat Detection**  
_2020-02-12 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

BAE Systems, funded by DARPA, is developing an AI-driven analytics system called MATCH to detect and deter weapons of mass destruction threats. Using machine learning, data fusion, and adversary modeling, the technology aims to automate and enhance identification of chemical, biological, radiological, nuclear, and explosive risks. No harm reported yet.
