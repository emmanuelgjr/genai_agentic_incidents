**Kaiser Permanente Therapists Strike Over AI Screening System Delays and Patient Harm**  
_2026-03-21 · real-world · Severity: High_

Therapists at Kaiser Permanente in Northern California went on strike, alleging that an AI-driven mental health screening system delays care and misclassifies high-risk patients, leading to harm. The AI system, used for triage and treatment recommendations, has reportedly replaced clinical judgment, sparking labor disputes and concerns over patient safety.
