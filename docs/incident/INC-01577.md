### INC-01577  [↗](/incident/INC-01577.html)

**OpenAI Halts Launch of Explicit Content Chatbot Amid Risk Concerns**  
_2026-03-26 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

OpenAI indefinitely suspended plans to launch a chatbot capable of generating explicit sexual content, known internally as "mode Citron," due to internal criticism and concerns from employees and investors about potential social and reputational risks. The decision was made to avoid possible harm before any incidents occurred.
