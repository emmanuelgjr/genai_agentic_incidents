**Uber, Nissan, and Wayve Plan Tokyo Robotaxi Trial Using AI-Driven Vehicles**  
_2026-03-09 · real-world · Severity: High_

Uber, Nissan, and Wayve are collaborating to deploy AI-powered autonomous vehicles for robotaxi services, with a pilot trial planned in Tokyo by late 2026. The initiative involves integrating Wayve's end-to-end AI driving system into Nissan vehicles, with safety operators present during initial trials. No incidents or harm have been reported yet.
