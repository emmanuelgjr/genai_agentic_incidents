### INC-12290  [↗](/incident/INC-12290.html)

**xapian-core Cross-site Scripting vulnerability**  
_2022-05 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: Medium_

CVEs: `CVE-2018-0499`
CWEs: `CWE-79`
CVSS: **6.1** — `CVSS:3.0/AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:R/S:C/C:L/I:L/A:N`

A cross-site scripting vulnerability in `queryparser/termgenerator_internal.cc` in Xapian xapian-core before 1.4.6 exists due to incomplete HTML escaping by `Xapian::MSet::snippet()`.

**Affected:** rubygems/xapian-core  
**Attack vector:** `xss`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM03`, `LLM05`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI04`, `ASI05`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-6.1`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0010`, `AML.T0050`
