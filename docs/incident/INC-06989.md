### INC-06989  [↗](/incident/INC-06989.html)

**TikTok Algorithm Amplifies Viral Nazi Chant Before Removal**  
_2020-07-12 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

TikTok's AI-driven recommendation algorithm amplified a Nazi-themed, antisemitic song, enabling it to go viral with over 6.5 million views across more than 100 videos. The widespread dissemination of hateful content caused harm before TikTok intervened and removed the material from the platform.
