### INC-03196  [↗](/incident/INC-03196.html)

**Scammer Reportedly Used AI Voice Clone of WCPO Cincinnati Meteorologist in Facebook Fraud Attempts**  
_2025-06-18 · real-world · Severity: Medium_
AIID: [`#1112`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1112/)

WCPO Cincinnati reported that a scammer impersonated its meteorologist Jennifer Ketchmark using AI voice cloning. A reportedly fraudulent Facebook account made friend requests and sent direct messages requesting money, using an AI-generated voice that mimicked Ketchmark&#x27;s. The scam attempted to gain trust by sounding convincingly like her. WCPO warned viewers and urged reporting the fake account to Meta. No confirmed victims or losses were publicly reported.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
