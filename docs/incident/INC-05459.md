### INC-05459  [↗](/incident/INC-05459.html)

**Amazon Japan's AI Fails to Block Defamatory and Obscene Listings**  
_2021-06-18 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Amazon Japan's AI-based monitoring system failed to prevent the sale of numerous illicit products featuring AI-generated obscene and defamatory images of female celebrities. The incident exposes shortcomings in Amazon's AI moderation, leading to reputational harm and potential legal violations, prompting government scrutiny and calls for improved content management.
