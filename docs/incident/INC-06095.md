**Tesla on Cruise Control Crashes into Truck in New Jersey, Prompting Federal Investigation**  
_2021-03-30 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A Tesla using its AI-based cruise control system crashed into a stationary tractor-trailer in New Jersey after the driver lost focus, severely damaging the car but causing only minor injuries. The U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration is investigating the incident, highlighting concerns over driver reliance on AI-assisted driving features.
