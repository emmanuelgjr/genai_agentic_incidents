### INC-00596  [↗](/incident/INC-00596.html)

**Anthropic AI Model Leak Triggers Cybersecurity Risks and Stock Market Fallout**  
_2026-03-27 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

A major data leak exposed details of Anthropic's powerful new AI model, Claude Mythos/Capybara, revealing advanced cybersecurity exploitation capabilities. The leak, caused by human error, led to real-world misuse attempts by hacking groups and triggered a sharp decline in cybersecurity stocks, highlighting significant AI-driven cybersecurity risks.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`
