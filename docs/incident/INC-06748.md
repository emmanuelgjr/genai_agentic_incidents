### INC-06748  [↗](/incident/INC-06748.html)

**Kamenz Launches AI-Driven Autonomous Flight Research Center**  
_2020-09-24 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A new research center at Kamenz airfield will develop and test AI-powered autonomous drones and air taxis, focusing on technologies like autonomous navigation and swarm applications. The initiative, involving universities and industry partners, aims to advance electric and hybrid aviation, but no AI-related harm or incidents have occurred yet.
