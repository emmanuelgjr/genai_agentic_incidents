### INC-01171  [↗](/incident/INC-01171.html)

**Hyderabad Police Warn of AI-Driven Biometric Identity Theft Scam**  
_2026-02-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

Hyderabad Cybercrime Police have issued alerts about a new scam where fraudsters use AI to create deepfake impersonations from stolen facial and voice data. Criminals trick victims in public places into handling phones, secretly capturing biometrics, which are then used for identity theft and fraud.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
