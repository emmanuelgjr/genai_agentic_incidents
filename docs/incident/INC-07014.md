### INC-07014  [↗](/incident/INC-07014.html)

**Turkey Deploys AI-Enabled Armed Drone Songar in Military Operations**  
_2020-07-21 · real-world · Severity: High_

Turkey's first domestically produced AI-enabled armed drone, Songar, developed by ASİSGUARD, is operationally used by Turkish military and security forces. Featuring autonomous targeting and advanced AI modules, Songar's deployment marks a significant instance of AI-driven weapon systems being used in real-world military contexts, raising concerns about AI-enabled harm.
