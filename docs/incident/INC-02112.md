### INC-02112  [↗](/incident/INC-02112.html)

**ZDF Broadcasts Unlabeled AI-Generated Fake Videos in News Segment**  
_2026-02-17 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

German broadcaster ZDF aired AI-generated and miscontextualized videos about US immigration enforcement in its "heute journal" news program without proper labeling, misleading viewers. Following public criticism, ZDF admitted the error, removed the content, and issued an apology, citing violations of journalistic standards and harm to public trust.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`
