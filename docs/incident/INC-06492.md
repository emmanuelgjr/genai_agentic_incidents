### INC-06492  [↗](/incident/INC-06492.html)

**Data breach reveals data of 400,000+ ProctorU users**  
_2020-08 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI-based remote-proctoring provider ProctorU was hit by a data breach exposing 444,000+ users' names, emails, passwords, addresses and other PII, putting student biometric and behavioral data at risk and underscoring the value of proctoring databases to attackers.

**Affected:** ProctorU customers and students  
**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM03`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI02`, `ASI03`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `GOVERN-6.1`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.10`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0010`, `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0024`, `AML.T0053`, `AML.T0057`
