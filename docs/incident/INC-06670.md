### INC-06670  [↗](/incident/INC-06670.html)

**Google's AI Health Screening Tool Fails in Real-World Clinical Trials**  
_2020-04-27 · real-world · Severity: High_

Google Health's AI tool for detecting diabetic retinopathy, despite high lab accuracy, failed in real-world clinics in Thailand. The system's strict image quality requirements and slow processing led to workflow disruptions, patient and nurse frustration, and delays in care, indirectly harming patient health and reducing effective screening.
