### INC-08029  [↗](/incident/INC-08029.html)

**AI-Driven 'Selfie Fingerprint' Scam Exposes Biometric Data from Social Media Photos**  
_2026-06-01 · real-world · Severity: High_

Authorities in Oklahoma warn that scammers are using AI-enhanced tools to extract fingerprint details from high-resolution selfies, especially those showing peace signs. This enables identity theft and fraud by reconstructing biometric data, compromising personal security and privacy. The incident highlights growing risks from AI misuse in cybercrime.
