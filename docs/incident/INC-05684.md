**Google's Wing Warns of Privacy Risks from New US Drone Location Broadcast Law**  
_2021-01-01 · real-world · Severity: High_

Google's drone delivery subsidiary, Wing, has criticized new US regulations requiring AI-enabled drones to broadcast their and their pilots' locations, warning this could lead to privacy violations. Wing argues the rule may expose sensitive user information and urges the FAA to consider alternative compliance methods.
