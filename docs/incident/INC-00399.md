### INC-00399  [↗](/incident/INC-00399.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfake Video Used for Extortion in Argentina**  
_2026-04-03 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Mariano Páez, father of Agostina Páez, claims he was targeted with an AI-generated deepfake video depicting him making racist gestures. He alleges the video was used to extort him for 5 million pesos and has caused reputational harm, prompting him to consider legal action to investigate its origin.
