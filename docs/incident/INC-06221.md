### INC-06221  [↗](/incident/INC-06221.html)

**AI Bots Drive Majority of Pro-Bolsonaro Tweets, Study Finds**  
_2020-04-03 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A study by UFRJ and FespSP found that AI-driven bots and automated accounts were responsible for 55% of 1.2 million pro-Bolsonaro tweets using #BolsonaroDay. The coordinated bot activity manipulated political discourse on Twitter, raising concerns about AI's role in influencing public opinion and undermining democratic processes.
