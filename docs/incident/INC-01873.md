**Tesla Robotaxis Rely on Human Remote Control Amid AI Limitations**  
_2026-04-01 · real-world · Severity: High_

Tesla and other autonomous vehicle companies admitted to US Senator Ed Markey that human operators sometimes remotely control robotaxis when AI systems fail or face unexpected situations. This reliance on human intervention highlights safety risks and incomplete autonomy, prompting regulatory scrutiny and concerns about public safety.
