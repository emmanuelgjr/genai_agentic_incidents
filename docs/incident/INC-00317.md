**AI-Driven Precision Therapy Improves Autoimmune Disease Outcomes in Taiwan**  
_2026-04-19 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Taiwan's Tri-Service General Hospital developed an AI-powered system to predict drug responses in autoimmune disease patients, reducing trial periods from months to 14 days with 90% accuracy. Combined with hydrogen molecule therapy, this approach has improved patient outcomes, reduced steroid dependence, and advanced precision medicine.
