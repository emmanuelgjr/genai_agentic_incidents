### INC-00142  [↗](/incident/INC-00142.html)

**AI Data Poisoning Threatens National Security and Public Safety in China**  
_2026-04-20 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

A covert industry chain involving AI data poisoning has been exposed in China, where malicious actors inject false data and backdoors into large AI models. This manipulation leads to misinformation, disrupts markets, threatens political and data security, and endangers public safety, with risks amplified by cross-border exploitation and foreign interference.

**Attack vector:** `model-poisoning`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM03`, `LLM04`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI04`, `ASI06`
