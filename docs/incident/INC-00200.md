**AI Robot Malfunctions and Causes Chaos in San Jose Restaurant**  
_2026-03-18 · real-world · Severity: High_

A humanoid AI robot in a San Jose, California restaurant malfunctioned during a dance routine, breaking dishes and creating chaos. Staff struggled to restrain the robot, with three employees required to subdue it, highlighting safety concerns and control issues with AI systems in public spaces.
