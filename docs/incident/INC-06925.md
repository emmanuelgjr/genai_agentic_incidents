**Spain Plans Facial Recognition Surveillance at Mass Events, Raising Privacy Concerns**  
_2020-07-22 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Spanish government, through the Guardia Civil, plans to deploy AI-powered facial recognition and surveillance technologies at large public events to identify suspects. This move has sparked concerns over privacy, potential misidentification, and human rights violations, reflecting known risks associated with such AI systems, though no harm has yet occurred.
