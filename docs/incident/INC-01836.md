**Swiss Competition Authority Warns of AI-Driven Price-Fixing Risks**  
_2026-03-31 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Switzerland's Competition Commission (Weko) highlights potential risks of AI systems in distorting market competition, particularly through algorithmic price-fixing and market concentration. While no incidents have occurred, Weko urges vigilant monitoring and adaptive regulation to prevent future anti-competitive harms from AI technologies.
