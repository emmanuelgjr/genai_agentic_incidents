**AI Surveillance System Aids Arrest After Hit-and-Run in Teresina**  
_2026-04-21 · real-world · Severity: High_

In Teresina, Brazil, a woman who struck a homeless man with her car was swiftly located and arrested after police used the SPIA AI surveillance system. Despite the vehicle's partially illegible license plate, the AI-enabled system identified and tracked the suspect, enabling law enforcement to apprehend her following the incident.
