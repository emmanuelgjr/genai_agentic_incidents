### INC-05859  [↗](/incident/INC-05859.html)

**Spotify Patents AI to Analyze User Emotions via Voice for Music Recommendations**  
_2021-01-27 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Spotify has been granted a patent for an AI system that analyzes users' speech and background noise to infer emotional state, gender, age, and environment, aiming to personalize music and content recommendations. While not yet deployed, the technology raises privacy concerns and potential risks of intrusive data collection.
