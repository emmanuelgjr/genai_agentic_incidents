### INC-06651  [↗](/incident/INC-06651.html)

**Google Ad Tool's AI Links Searches for 'Black Girls' to Pornographic Keywords**  
_2020-07-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

Google's AI-powered Keyword Planner tool suggested pornographic keywords when users searched for terms like 'Black girls,' 'Latina girls,' or 'Asian girls,' but not for 'White girls' or 'White boys.' This racial bias, discovered by The Markup, perpetuated harmful stereotypes until Google intervened after being notified.
