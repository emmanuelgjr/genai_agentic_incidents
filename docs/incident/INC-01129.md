**Google's Gemini AI Blocks Fraudulent Ads and URLs in Taiwan**  
_2026-04-14 · real-world · Severity: High_

Google deployed its Gemini AI system to analyze behavioral patterns and ad content, intercepting over 99% of fraudulent ads and removing 3564 violating URLs in Taiwan. The AI's advanced detection prevented scam exposure, reduced erroneous account suspensions by 80%, and enhanced user safety and platform trust.
