### INC-06228  [↗](/incident/INC-06228.html)

**AI Image Analysis Trial to Enhance Railway Crossing Safety in Japan**  
_2020-08-11 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Optage, Sanyo Electric Railway, and Sanyo Electric Railway Information Center began a field trial using AI image analysis to detect people and abnormalities at railway crossings. The system aims to prevent accidents by alerting train operators in real time, with full deployment targeted for spring 2021. No incidents have been reported yet.
