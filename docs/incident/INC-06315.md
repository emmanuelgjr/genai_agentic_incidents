**AI-Powered Police Drones Spark Privacy and Civil Rights Concerns in U.S. Cities**  
_2020-12-05 · real-world · Severity: High_

Police departments in cities like Chula Vista, California, are deploying AI-enabled drones to autonomously respond to emergency calls and track individuals. While these drones enhance law enforcement capabilities, their use has raised significant privacy and civil liberties concerns due to potential surveillance and rights violations.
