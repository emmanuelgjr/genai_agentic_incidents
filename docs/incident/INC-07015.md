**Turkey Deploys AI-Enabled Bayraktar TB2 Drones for Border Patrols at Evros**  
_2020-03-14 · real-world · Severity: High_

Turkey has begun deploying AI-enabled Bayraktar TB2 drones for patrols along the Greek-Turkish border at Evros, as announced by Selçuk Bayraktar, Baykar Defense's technical director. While no harm has occurred, the use of armed, autonomous drones in this tense region raises credible risks of escalation or future incidents.
