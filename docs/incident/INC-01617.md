### INC-01617  [↗](/incident/INC-01617.html)

**Palantir AI Systems Used in Israeli Military Operations Causing Civilian Harm**  
_2026-05-10 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

US-based Palantir's AI technologies, including data analysis and targeting platforms, have been used by the Israeli military in Gaza, Iran, and Lebanon, directly contributing to lethal operations and civilian harm. These AI systems facilitated surveillance, target identification, and decision-making in military actions, raising concerns over human rights violations.
