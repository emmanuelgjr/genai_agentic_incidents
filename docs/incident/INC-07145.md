### INC-07145  [↗](/incident/INC-07145.html)

**YouTube's AI Over-Enforcement During COVID-19 Lockdown Leads to Mass Video Removals**  
_2020-08-25 · real-world · Severity: High_

During the COVID-19 lockdown, YouTube relied heavily on AI for content moderation, resulting in the removal of over 11 million videos between April and June 2020. The automated system, used due to reduced human staff, led to many videos being wrongly taken down, harming content creators through over-censorship.
