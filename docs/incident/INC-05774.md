**Myanmar Military Uses AI Surveillance to Suppress Protesters**  
_2021-03-18 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Myanmar's military authorities have deployed Chinese AI-powered facial and license plate recognition systems to monitor and track protesters following the 2021 coup. Human rights groups warn this surveillance enables repression, threatens civil liberties, and has contributed to a violent crackdown resulting in over 200 deaths.
