### INC-07797  [↗](/incident/INC-07797.html)

**US Deploys AI-Powered LUCAS Kamikaze Drone Swarms in Military Operations**  
_2026-05-19 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The US Department of Defense has partnered with Shield AI to integrate its Hivemind AI software into LUCAS kamikaze drones, enabling autonomous swarm coordination and strike missions. LUCAS drones, now operational in the Middle East, can be controlled by a single operator and are designed for large-scale, coordinated attacks, raising significant risks of AI-enabled harm.
