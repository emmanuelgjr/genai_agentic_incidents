**US Military Forms Task Force to Safely Deploy AI on Classified Networks**  
_2026-05-18 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The US military, led by Gen. Joshua Rudd, has created a joint NSA and Cyber Command task force to accelerate and safely deploy advanced AI models from companies like OpenAI and Anthropic on top-secret networks. The move addresses growing concerns about AI-enabled cyber threats and national security risks.
