**Ukraine Deploys AI-Driven Military Systems in Ongoing Conflict**  
_2026-04-18 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Ukraine has rapidly integrated AI-powered systems, including autonomous drones and computer vision technologies, into its military operations, with over 70 such systems actively used on the battlefield. These AI applications directly contribute to targeting, detection, and automated firing, impacting the ongoing conflict and causing harm to enemy combatants.
