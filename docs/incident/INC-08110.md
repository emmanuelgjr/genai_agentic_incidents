**Foodstuffs Expands Facial Recognition to Reduce Harmful Behavior in Christchurch Supermarkets**  
_2026-06-05 · real-world · Severity: High_

Foodstuffs South Island expanded facial recognition technology to a fourth Christchurch supermarket after a successful trial. The AI system identified individuals with a history of harmful behavior, resulting in fewer repeat offenders and reduced incidents. No misidentifications or false positives were reported, raising privacy and rights concerns.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`
