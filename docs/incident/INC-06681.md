### INC-06681  [↗](/incident/INC-06681.html)

**Human Rights Watch Warns COVID-19 Tracking Apps Violate Human Rights**  
_2020-05-13 · real-world · Severity: High_

Human Rights Watch criticized the use of AI-powered mobile tracking and geolocation apps by governments during the COVID-19 pandemic, citing risks to privacy and other fundamental rights. The organization highlighted cases in China, Israel, and Russia where such technologies led to arbitrary quarantines, increased repression, and difficult-to-contest decisions.
