**Romanian Company Launches AI-Powered Autonomous Drone Countermeasure System**  
_2026-03-04 · real-world · Severity: High_

Romanian deep-tech firm Qognifly has launched Drone Wall, an AI-driven autonomous system for detecting, tracking, and intercepting drones. Validated in operational conditions, the system aims to protect airspace and critical infrastructure from drone threats, aligning with EU and NATO standards. No incidents or harm have been reported.
