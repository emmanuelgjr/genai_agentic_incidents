### INC-06368  [↗](/incident/INC-06368.html)

**Apple's AI-Powered Photo Scanning Sparks Privacy Concerns**  
_2020-01-09 · real-world · Severity: High_

Apple confirmed using AI algorithms, likely including PhotoDNA, to automatically scan all user photos stored on iPhones and iCloud for child sexual abuse material. Detected accounts are blocked. The practice, aimed at harm prevention, has raised significant privacy and human rights concerns over automated surveillance and potential legal challenges.
