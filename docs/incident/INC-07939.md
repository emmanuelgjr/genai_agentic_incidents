**Tesla Autopilot Crash in Florida Results in Fatality**  
_2026-05-27 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

An 87-year-old man died and a 75-year-old woman was injured after a Tesla Model Y, operating in autopilot mode, veered off the road, struck an electrical box, and submerged in a pond in Pasco County, Florida. The incident highlights potential safety concerns with Tesla's AI-driven autopilot system.
