### INC-05781  [↗](/incident/INC-05781.html)

**NFL's Race-Normed Algorithm Discriminates Against Black Players in Brain Injury Settlement**  
_2021-05-14 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The NFL used an AI-based scoring algorithm in its $1 billion brain injury settlement that applied 'race-norming,' assuming Black players had lower baseline cognitive skills. This practice systematically reduced compensation for Black former players, resulting in direct discriminatory harm and legal challenges demanding equal treatment.

**Attack vector:** `algorithmic-bias`
