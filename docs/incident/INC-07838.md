**AI Surveillance System in Bari Detects Over 2,800 Illegal Waste Dumping Incidents**  
_2026-05-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

In Bari, Italy, an AI-powered surveillance system called "Multivideo Trappola" detected over 2,800 illegal waste disposal incidents in eight months. Using advanced algorithms and mobile cameras, the system identified offenders and vehicle license plates, enabling local authorities to issue sanctions and address environmental harm.
