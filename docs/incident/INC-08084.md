### INC-08084  [↗](/incident/INC-08084.html)

**AI-Enabled Fraud Targets Vietnamese Individuals and Businesses**  
_2026-06-03 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Fraudsters in Vietnam are increasingly using AI-generated images, voices, emails, and fake digital identities to impersonate individuals and businesses, leading to financial losses. These sophisticated scams exploit popular platforms like Zalo and target both individuals and export companies, making detection and prevention more challenging.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
