### INC-06410  [↗](/incident/INC-06410.html)

**British Manufacturer Plans Fully Autonomous Passenger Planes by 2030**  
_2020-10-06 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Britten-Norman, in partnership with Blue Bear, plans to introduce AI-powered pilotless passenger planes by 2030, starting with single-pilot aircraft and progressing to full automation. The initiative raises safety and regulatory concerns, as AI system failures could pose significant risks to passengers and public confidence.
