### INC-00762  [↗](/incident/INC-00762.html)

**Chilean Legislative Proposal Sparks Copyright Concerns Over AI Data Use**  
_2026-04-22 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Chilean government proposed a law allowing AI systems to use large volumes of copyrighted content without authorization or compensation for data mining and training. Media organizations and creators warn this could undermine intellectual property rights and threaten journalism’s economic base, urging the article’s withdrawal or revision.
