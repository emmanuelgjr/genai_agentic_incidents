### INC-00728  [↗](/incident/INC-00728.html)

**Canadian Firms Collaborate on AI-Powered Autonomous Navigation for Arctic Defence**  
_2026-03-11 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Calian Group and Tessellate Robotics are collaborating to develop and deploy AI-driven autonomous navigation systems for defence operations in Canada's Arctic, focusing on environments where GPS is unreliable or denied. While no harm has occurred, the deployment of such systems poses potential future risks in military contexts.
