### INC-07867  [↗](/incident/INC-07867.html)

**Anthropic's Mythos AI Uncovers 10,000+ Critical Software Vulnerabilities**  
_2026-05-22 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Anthropic's restricted AI model, Mythos Preview, identified over 10,000 high- or critical-severity software vulnerabilities in one month through Project Glasswing. While partners like Cloudflare and Mozilla rapidly fixed many flaws, the overwhelming volume has strained maintainers, raising concerns about patching capacity and potential risks if vulnerabilities remain unaddressed.
