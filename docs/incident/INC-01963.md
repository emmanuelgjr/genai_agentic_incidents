**Ukraine Launches Defense AI Center with UK Support for Military Innovation**  
_2026-03-17 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Ukraine's Ministry of Defense, supported by the UK government, has launched Defense AI Center "A1" to integrate AI into military operations. The center will focus on autonomous systems, battlefield data analysis, and predictive tools, raising credible risks of future AI-related harm in warfare. No incidents reported yet.
