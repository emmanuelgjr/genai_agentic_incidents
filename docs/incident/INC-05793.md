**Pakistan Raises Alarm Over India's AI-Driven Military Advancements**  
_2021-05-27 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Pakistani officials have expressed concern over India's integration of artificial intelligence, robotics, and autonomous weapons into its military arsenal, supported by Western countries. They warn that these developments, including anti-satellite and cyber warfare technologies, pose emerging threats to regional strategic stability and Pakistan's security.
