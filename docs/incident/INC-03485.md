### INC-03485  [↗](/incident/INC-03485.html)

**AI voice-cloning scam targets Florida family**  
_2024-10-01 · real-world · Severity: High_

Scammers cloned 15 seconds of a young man's voice from a TV appearance and used it to impersonate him in a distress call to his parents, who almost paid USD 30,000 in a fake-bail scam before discovering the deception.

**Affected:** Florida family  
**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048`, `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
