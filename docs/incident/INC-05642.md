**Facial Recognition AI Systems in China Lead to Privacy Violations and Regulatory Action**  
_2021-05-12 · real-world · Severity: High_

Multiple incidents in China reveal the unauthorized deployment of facial recognition AI systems in residential and commercial settings, resulting in privacy violations, personal data leaks, and regulatory penalties. Authorities fined companies for collecting biometric data without consent, while public resistance and legal scrutiny highlight ongoing risks and harms from misuse of AI-driven surveillance.
