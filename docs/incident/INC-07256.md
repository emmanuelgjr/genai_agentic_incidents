### INC-07256  [↗](/incident/INC-07256.html)

**Yang Mi and Athena Chu face-swap deepfake video**  
_2019 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

An AI face-swap clip replaced the faces of actresses Yang Mi and Athena Chu into a 1990s television scene, going viral in China and prompting one of the earliest national-level deepfake regulatory responses.

**Affected:** Yang Mi, Athena Chu  
**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
