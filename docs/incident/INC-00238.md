### INC-00238  [↗](/incident/INC-00238.html)

**AI Tools Enable Sophisticated Fraud and Scams in Latvia**  
_2026-02-19 · real-world · Severity: High_

In Latvia, criminals are increasingly using AI tools such as generative text, deepfakes, and voice cloning to create convincing scams, making fraudulent content difficult to distinguish from genuine communication. This has led to financial and psychological harm, highlighting the urgent need for improved public AI literacy and critical thinking.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
