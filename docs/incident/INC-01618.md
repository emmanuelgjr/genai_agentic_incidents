**Palantir AI Systems Used in Middle East Military Operations and Israeli Conflict Response**  
_2026-03-12 · real-world · Severity: High_

Palantir's AI technologies, including Project Maven, have been used by the US and its allies for real-time military coordination and satellite analysis in Middle East conflicts, potentially enabling targeted strikes. In Israel, Palantir's AI assisted intelligence agencies in analyzing data and identifying hostages after a deadly attack, directly impacting conflict outcomes.
