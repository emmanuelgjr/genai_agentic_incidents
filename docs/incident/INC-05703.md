**Indian Army Demonstrates AI-Enabled Autonomous Drone Swarms for Military Use**  
_2021-01-15 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The Indian Army showcased AI-powered autonomous drone swarms during the 2021 Army Day Parade, demonstrating their capability for simulated offensive missions and support tasks. While no harm occurred during the demonstration, the deployment of such AI-enabled weapon systems poses credible risks of future harm in military conflicts.
