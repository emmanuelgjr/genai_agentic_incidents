**US Military Deploys AI Systems for Battlefield Targeting and Strike Decisions**  
_2026-03-10 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The US military, in partnership with Palantir and AI startups like Anthropic, has integrated advanced AI systems (including Maven and Claude) into battlefield operations. These AI tools process intelligence and generate targeting recommendations, directly influencing lethal strike decisions in ongoing conflicts, raising concerns about AI-driven harm and accountability.
