**UAE Plans Massive Government Automation with Agentic AI**  
_2026-04-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

The UAE government has announced plans to automate 50% of its federal operations using Agentic AI systems within two years. These autonomous AI agents will analyze data, make decisions, and execute tasks independently, raising potential future risks related to large-scale AI-driven governance. No actual harm has yet occurred.
