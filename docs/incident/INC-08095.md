**Legal Action Over Unauthorized Commercial Use of AI-Generated Images of Footballer Szoboszlai Dominik**  
_2026-06-04 · real-world · Severity: High_

Several companies used AI-generated images based on footballer Szoboszlai Dominik's viral outfit for commercial purposes without his consent. His management has initiated legal action, citing violations of personality and intellectual property rights. The incident highlights the misuse of AI in creating unauthorized promotional content. The events occurred in Hungary.
