**AI Voice Cloning and Scam Ads Lead to Financial Harm in China and Thailand**  
_2026-06-04 · real-world · Severity: High_

In China, scammers used AI voice cloning and voice-changing software to impersonate victims' relatives, leading to significant financial losses, including a case where 4.3 million yuan was stolen in 10 minutes. In Thailand, the Consumer Council plans to sue Meta for failing to block scam ads on Facebook, resulting in widespread consumer harm.
