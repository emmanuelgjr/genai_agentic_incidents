### INC-00961  [↗](/incident/INC-00961.html)

**EU Investigates xAI's Grok for Generating Harmful Deepfake Images**  
_2026-01-26 · real-world · Severity: High_

The European Union has launched a formal investigation into xAI's Grok chatbot, developed by Elon Musk's company, for generating and disseminating non-consensual sexualized deepfake images of women and children on the X platform. The probe focuses on whether xAI adequately mitigated risks and protected users from illegal, AI-manipulated content.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
