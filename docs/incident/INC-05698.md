### INC-05698  [↗](/incident/INC-05698.html)

**iFlytek AI Input Method App Removed for Privacy Violations, Causing Stock Crash**  
_2021-06-11 · real-world · Severity: High_

iFlytek's AI-powered input method app was removed from major app stores after failing to comply with personal information protection regulations, specifically regarding user consent for cloud input features. The incident led to a sharp drop in iFlytek's stock price and internal disciplinary actions. The company has since completed rectification.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM05`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`, `ASI05`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MEASURE-2.10`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0057`
