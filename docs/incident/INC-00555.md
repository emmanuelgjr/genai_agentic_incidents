**AI-Powered Social Media Alert Enables Police to Prevent Teen Suicide in Uttar Pradesh**  
_2026-04-01 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

In Raebareli, Uttar Pradesh, an AI-driven Meta Alert System detected a suicide-related Instagram post by an 18-year-old. The system promptly notified police, who located and rescued the youth within 12 minutes, preventing a suicide attempt. The incident underscores AI's critical role in harm prevention.
