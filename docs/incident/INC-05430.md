### INC-05430  [↗](/incident/INC-05430.html)

**AI-Generated Fake Female Profiles Used in Hotel Social App Scam Busted by Shanghai Police**  
_2021-06-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

Shanghai police dismantled a fraud ring behind the 'Quzhu' app, which used AI-driven software to create over 10,000 fake female profiles and generate seductive content. The scam lured users into paying for chat services, resulting in financial losses. Ten suspects were arrested, and the app's fraudulent use of AI was central to the scheme.

**Attack vector:** `other`  

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`
