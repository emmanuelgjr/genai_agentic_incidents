**US Grants Nuro Exemption to Deploy Driverless Delivery Vehicles Lacking Standard Safety Features**  
_2020-02-06 · real-world · Severity: High_

The US government has granted Nuro a temporary exemption to deploy its R2 driverless delivery vehicles, which lack standard human-driver safety features like mirrors and windshields. While no harm has occurred, the regulatory approval raises concerns about potential risks from deploying AI-driven vehicles that do not meet traditional safety standards.
