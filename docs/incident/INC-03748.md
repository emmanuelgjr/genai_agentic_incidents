**Elon Musk Reportedly Shared an AI-Generated Image Depicting Kamala Harris Dressed as a Communist Ruler**  
_2024-09-02 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#775`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/775/)

Tech billionaire Elon Musk reacted to a post by Kamala Harris on X that said Donald Trump 'vows to be a dictator on day 1.' Musk reshared the post along with an AI-generated image supposedly depicting Kamala Harris as a communist ruler. Musk wrote in the caption 'Kamala vows to be a communist dictator on day one. Can you believe she wears that outfit!?' The post generated various reactions on social media, with some users criticizing the image quality, noting it looked more like actress Eva Longoria than Harris. Other users expressed concerns about the dangerous nature of such manipulated content coming from the richest person on earth who owns one of the largest platforms. Some users defended the post while others warned that such content contributes to X being banned in other countries. The incident highlights concerns about AI-generated political content and misinformation on social media platforms.
