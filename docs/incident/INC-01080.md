### INC-01080  [↗](/incident/INC-01080.html)

**German Interior Minister Proposes AI Surveillance Cameras at Train Stations**  
_2026-03-28 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

German Interior Minister Alexander Dobrindt has announced plans to deploy AI-powered cameras with facial recognition and behavior detection at train stations across Germany. The initiative aims to enhance security but requires new legislation. The proposed use of AI surveillance raises potential privacy and human rights concerns.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`
