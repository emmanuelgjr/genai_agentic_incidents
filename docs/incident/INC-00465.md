### INC-00465  [↗](/incident/INC-00465.html)

**AI-Generated Fake Rice Video Causes Public Panic and Legal Action in China**  
_2026-05-12 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

In Hunan, China, a woman used AI tools to create and spread a false video about 'artificial rice' production in Hubei, alleging food safety issues. The AI-generated misinformation caused public panic and widespread discussion. Authorities investigated, confirmed the content was false, and imposed administrative penalties, ordering the video’s removal.

**Attack vector:** `misinformation`
