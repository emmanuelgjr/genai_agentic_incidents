### INC-03507  [↗](/incident/INC-03507.html)

**AI-Powered Transcription Services Allegedly Leak Confidential Workplace Discussions**  
_2024-10-02 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#811`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/811/)

AI-powered meeting assistants, such as Otter.ai’s OtterPilot and Zoom&#x27;s AI Companion, have reportedly shared sensitive and private conversations beyond the intended audience. These AI tools, which are set to automatically record and distribute meeting transcripts, allegedly sent confidential discussions after participants had left the meeting, the consequences of which led to unintended exposure of proprietary information, privacy breaches, and potential reputational harm.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`
