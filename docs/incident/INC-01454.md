### INC-01454  [↗](/incident/INC-01454.html)

**Mistral AI Accused of Massive Copyright Infringement in Model Training**  
_2026-02-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

French company Mistral AI is accused of training its large language model, Mistral Large 3-2512, on thousands of copyrighted books, songs, and articles without permission. Investigations revealed the AI reproduces substantial portions of protected works, violating intellectual property rights and disregarding opt-out requests from content owners.

**Attack vector:** `other`
