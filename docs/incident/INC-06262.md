### INC-06262  [↗](/incident/INC-06262.html)

**AI Tool Guides Life-or-Death Covid-19 Treatment Decisions in China**  
_2020-03-21 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Chinese researchers developed an AI system to analyze Covid-19 patient data and predict survival rates, directly influencing doctors' decisions on treatment prioritization. The tool's use in allocating scarce medical resources raises ethical concerns about AI involvement in life-or-death decisions and potential risks of depriving some patients of care.
