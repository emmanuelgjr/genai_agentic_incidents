### INC-07466  [↗](/incident/INC-07466.html)

**A Tesla Crashed into and Killed a Road Sweeper on a Highway in China**  
_2016-01-20 · real-world · Severity: Critical_
AIID: [`#231`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/231/)

On January 20, 2016, a Tesla Model S crashed into a road-sweeping truck on a highway near Handan, Hebei Province, China, killing 23-year-old driver Gao Yaning. The victim's father, Gao Jubin, believes the vehicle was operating under Tesla's Autopilot system at the time of the crash. Dashcam footage shows the Model S traveling at consistent speed in the left lane when a car ahead moved to the center lane, revealing an orange street sweeper straddling the road's left shoulder directly in the vehicle's path. The Tesla never slowed or braked before impact, and no warning alerts were audible in the video. Police investigation concluded that Gao Yaning was primarily responsible for the accident, finding no evidence that brakes were applied. Tesla initially stated that extensive damage made it impossible to determine whether Autopilot was engaged, but later data analysis reportedly confirmed the system was active. The family filed a lawsuit against Tesla in July 2016, seeking 5 million yuan (approximately $750,000) in compensation and alleging that Tesla's sales staff had misrepresented Autopilot's capabilities. The case highlighted concerns about Tesla's marketing of the semi-autonomous system and whether drivers understood its limitations. Following this incident and others, Tesla updated its Autopilot system to lock out drivers who ignore repeated warnings to resume control.
