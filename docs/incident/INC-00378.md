### INC-00378  [↗](/incident/INC-00378.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfake Images Used to Harass Slovenian Activist**  
_2026-03-08 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Artificial intelligence was used to create and distribute fake nude images and videos of Nika Kovač, director of Inštitut 8. marec, in Slovenia. These deepfakes, shared online without consent, were used for harassment and discrediting, highlighting the growing harm of AI-enabled image abuse against women.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
