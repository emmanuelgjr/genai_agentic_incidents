### INC-01663  [↗](/incident/INC-01663.html)

**Potential Developmental Risks of AI Exposure for Young Children**  
_2026-03-12 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Experts warn that increasing exposure of young children to AI-generated content and AI-mediated interactions—such as on YouTube Kids and in educational settings—may pose risks to their social, emotional, and cognitive development. While no concrete harm is reported, concerns center on possible irreversible developmental consequences.
