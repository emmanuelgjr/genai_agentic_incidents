**Canon's AI Smile Recognition System Forces Employees to Smile for Workplace Access**  
_2021-06-17 · real-world · Severity: High_

Canon's Chinese offices implemented an AI-powered facial recognition system that requires employees to smile to gain entry or access workplace facilities. The system, intended to promote positivity, has raised concerns over emotional manipulation, privacy, and labor rights violations due to its compulsory enforcement of emotional expression.
