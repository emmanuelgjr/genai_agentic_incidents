### INC-04021  [↗](/incident/INC-04021.html)

**Muah AI companion app hack reveals attempts to simulate child abuse**  
_2024-10 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

A breach of AI companion app Muah exposed millions of user prompts, including many requesting child sexual abuse role-play and depicting attempts to generate CSAM-adjacent content, illustrating policy and safeguard failures in 'uncensored' companion bots.

**Affected:** Muah users / children  
**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM03`, `LLM05`, `LLM06`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI02`, `ASI03`, `ASI08`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.1`, `GOVERN-1.4`, `GOVERN-6.1`, `MANAGE-4.1`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.10`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0010`, `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0024`, `AML.T0048`, `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0053`, `AML.T0057`, `AML.T0058`
