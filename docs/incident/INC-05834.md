**Russia Announces Serial Production of AI-Powered Combat Robots**  
_2021-05-21 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Russian Defense Minister Sergei Shoigu announced the start of serial production of AI-enabled combat robots capable of autonomous warfare. While no specific harm has occurred yet, the deployment of such autonomous weapons systems poses significant risks of future harm, including injury, death, and potential violations of international law.
