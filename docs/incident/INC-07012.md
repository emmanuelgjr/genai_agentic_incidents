### INC-07012  [↗](/incident/INC-07012.html)

**Turkey Begins Serial Production of Armed Autonomous Ground Vehicles**  
_2020-05-09 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Turkey's Presidency of Defense Industries and ASELSAN have signed a contract to begin serial production of armed, autonomous unmanned ground vehicles ('mini tanks'). These AI-enabled vehicles, capable of reconnaissance, surveillance, and remote or autonomous operation, pose credible future risks of harm due to their weaponized and autonomous nature.
