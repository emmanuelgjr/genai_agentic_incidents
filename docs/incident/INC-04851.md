**Russia Reportedly Using Artificial Intelligence in Disinformation Campaigns to Erode Western Support for Ukraine**  
_2023-10-02 · real-world · Severity: Medium_
AIID: [`#602`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/602/)

The Russian government has been stepping up its foreign influence campaigns by using artificial intelligence and emerging technologies to spread disinformation and sow distrust in policies supportive of Ukraine. Part of the strategy includes carrying out influence laundering operations by disseminating their messages to the American public via allies inside nominally independent organizations, according to a recent declassified analysis.
