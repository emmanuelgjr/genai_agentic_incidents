### INC-00363  [↗](/incident/INC-00363.html)

**AI-Generated 'Skill' Clones Lead to Job Losses and Privacy Risks in China**  
_2026-04-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI systems like '同事.skill' are being used to distill employees' work habits and personalities into digital 'skills,' enabling companies to automate roles and leading to layoffs and privacy violations. These AI tools have caused job losses, disrupted career paths, and raised legal and ethical concerns in China.
