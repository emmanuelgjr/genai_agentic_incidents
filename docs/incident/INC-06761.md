**Libyan Army Shoots Down Turkish AI Drones in Tripoli Conflict**  
_2020-05-16 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Libyan National Army announced it shot down multiple Turkish Anka-S AI-powered drones, including reconnaissance models, near the strategic Al-Watiya airbase outside Tripoli. The incident highlights the direct involvement of AI-enabled military drones in the ongoing conflict, resulting in property damage and escalating tensions between Libyan and Turkish-backed forces.
