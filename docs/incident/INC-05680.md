### INC-05680  [↗](/incident/INC-05680.html)

**Google's AI Algorithms Suppress Independent Media in Turkey, Favor Pro-Government Outlets**  
_2021-03-03 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Reports by the International Press Institute reveal that Google's AI-driven search and news algorithms in Turkey overwhelmingly promote pro-government media while suppressing independent outlets. This algorithmic bias limits access to diverse information, amplifies disinformation and hate speech, and undermines media freedom and pluralism.
