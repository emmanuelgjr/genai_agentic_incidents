**US Approves $9 Billion for AI Chips Amid National Security Concerns**  
_2026-05-22 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The US government approved a secret $9 billion funding package to help intelligence agencies like the NSA and CIA acquire advanced AI chips and infrastructure. This move addresses a critical chip shortage, forcing reliance on Anthropic's AI models, which are officially blacklisted as a national security threat.
