### INC-01234  [↗](/incident/INC-01234.html)

**Indian Organisations Face Surge in AI-Driven Deepfake Attacks**  
_2026-03-09 · real-world · Severity: High_

A Thales report reveals that 65% of Indian organisations have experienced AI-generated deepfake attacks, leading to reputational damage and heightened data security risks. As AI adoption accelerates across sectors, weak controls and broad system access have increased vulnerability to cyber incidents and misinformation campaigns.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`, `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.10`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0057`, `AML.T0058`
