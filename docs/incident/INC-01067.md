### INC-01067  [↗](/incident/INC-01067.html)

**Generative AI Drives Surge in Cybercrime Effectiveness and Volume**  
_2026-02-17 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Brian Cute, CEO of the Global Cyber Alliance, warned at the India AI Impact Summit that generative AI is enabling cybercriminals to conduct more effective and cheaper phishing, scams, and deepfake attacks. This AI-driven surge has led to increased harm to individuals and communities through more sophisticated cybercrime.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`
