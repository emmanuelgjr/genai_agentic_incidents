### INC-00867  [↗](/incident/INC-00867.html)

**Ctrip's AI Pricing Tools Cause Market Disruption and Regulatory Scrutiny**  
_2026-03-02 · real-world · Severity: High_

Chinese travel platform Ctrip's AI-powered pricing assistant led to unfair price competition, market disruption, and consumer complaints, including price discrimination and extreme airfare pricing due to algorithmic errors. In response to regulatory pressure and an ongoing antitrust investigation, Ctrip will remove the AI tool to restore fairer market practices.
