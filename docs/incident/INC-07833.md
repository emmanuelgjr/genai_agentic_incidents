**AI-Generated Content Causes Celebrity Defamation and Public Backlash in China**  
_2026-05-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

Chinese influencer Han Anran used an AI digital human to generate livestream content ranking female celebrities' alleged cosmetic surgery. The AI's output led to reputational harm, privacy violations, and public backlash. Han Anran apologized, citing lack of content review due to being abroad. Legal experts confirmed responsibility for AI-generated defamatory content.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`
