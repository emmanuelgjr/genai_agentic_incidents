**Bayraktar AKINCI Armed Drone Receives Critical Design Approval**  
_2020-09-09 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Baykar's AI-enabled armed drone, Bayraktar AKINCI, has received critical design approval following a review with Turkish defense authorities. While no harm has occurred, the development of this autonomous weapon system raises concerns about potential future risks associated with AI-driven military technologies.
