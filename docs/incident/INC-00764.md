**China Demonstrates AI-Controlled Drone Swarm Warfare**  
_2026-01-23 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

China's People's Liberation Army has showcased AI-enabled drone swarm technology, allowing a single soldier to control over 200 drones. Developed by the National University of Defence Technology, these drones use autonomous algorithms for coordinated reconnaissance and strike operations, highlighting significant future risks in military applications. No actual harm reported yet.
