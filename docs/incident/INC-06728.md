**Israel Deploys AI-Driven Military Intelligence Unit 9900**  
_2020-07-02 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The Israeli military has established Unit 9900, an intelligence division that uses AI technology to analyze and integrate data from satellites, drones, sensors, and human sources. The unit provides real-time battlefield intelligence to combat forces, raising concerns about the potential risks of AI use in warfare, though no specific harm has been reported.
