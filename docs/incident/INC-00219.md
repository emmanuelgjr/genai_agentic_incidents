### INC-00219  [↗](/incident/INC-00219.html)

**AI System KURGAN Targets Massive Tax Fraud in Turkey**  
_2026-04-02 · real-world · Severity: High_

Turkey's Tax Inspection Board deployed the AI-supported KURGAN system to detect and combat large-scale fake invoice fraud, targeting 2,300 businesses across nine major cities. The operation, covering approximately 100 billion TL, aims to identify and penalize tax evasion and financial crimes using advanced AI analysis.
