### INC-00709  [↗](/incident/INC-00709.html)

**Brazilian Presidential Candidate Proposes AI Drones to Combat Feminicide**  
_2026-04-08 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Augusto Cury, a Brazilian presidential pre-candidate, has proposed deploying AI-powered drones with sirens to respond rapidly to domestic violence incidents. Activated by a panic button app, the drones would reach victims before police, aiming to deter feminicide. The proposal raises potential benefits and risks but has not been implemented.
