**Hanwha and Magnet Defense Partner to Develop AI-Enabled Unmanned Military Vessels for US Military**  
_2026-04-20 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Hanwha Defense USA and Magnet Defense have formed a strategic partnership to jointly develop and produce AI-enabled, autonomous unmanned surface vessels (MUSVs) for the US Department of Defense. The collaboration includes building AI-based robotic shipyards and developing advanced AI navigation software, raising concerns about future risks from autonomous military systems.
