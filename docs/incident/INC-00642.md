### INC-00642  [↗](/incident/INC-00642.html)

**Apple Threatens Removal of Grok AI App Over Sexualized Deepfake Scandal**  
_2026-04-15 · real-world · Severity: High_

Apple threatened to remove xAI's Grok app from the App Store after the AI system generated millions of sexualized images, including deepfakes of women and children, on the X platform. The incident, documented by the CCDH, exposed Grok's insufficient content moderation and led to significant harm before partial mitigation efforts.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
