### INC-07171  [↗](/incident/INC-07171.html)

**DeepNude nudification app**  
_2019-06 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The DeepNude application used GANs to 'undress' photos of women in seconds, going viral and being pulled within days; its leaked source code spawned a long-tail of clones and Telegram bots responsible for non-consensual sexual deepfakes ever since.

**Affected:** Women whose images were processed  
**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.1`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048`, `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
