### INC-07009  [↗](/incident/INC-07009.html)

**Trump Campaign Uses AI-Generated Audio to Falsely Attribute 'Hoax' Comment to Biden**  
_2020-04-01 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The Trump campaign released a manipulated audio clip, likely created with AI tools, falsely making it appear Joe Biden called the coronavirus a "hoax." This synthetic media was used to retaliate against similar accusations and highlights the use of AI in spreading political misinformation, risking public harm and undermining democratic discourse.

**Attack vector:** `misinformation`

**NIST AI RMF:** `MEASURE-2.8`
