**Spanish Ombudsman Investigates Harmful AI-Driven Social Media Content Affecting Minors**  
_2026-05-21 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The Spanish Ombudsman launched an investigation after a minor suffered self-harm and suicidal tendencies due to harmful content recommended by social media algorithms (AI systems) on X.
