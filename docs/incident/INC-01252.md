**Israel Deploys AI-Enabled Robotics for Large-Scale Border Demining**  
_2026-04-20 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The Israeli Ministry of Defense awarded Ondas Inc. and its subsidiary 4M Defense a $10 million initial order, part of a $50 million program, to deploy AI-enabled autonomous robotic systems, drones, and sensors for large-scale demining along Israel's eastern border, enhancing security and safety infrastructure.
