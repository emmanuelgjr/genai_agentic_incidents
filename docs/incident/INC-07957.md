**Study Finds AI Chatbots Frequently Give Inaccurate Medical Advice**  
_2026-05-28 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A Penn State study found that popular AI chatbots answer everyday health questions with about 76% accuracy, meaning roughly one in four responses is incorrect. Physicians warn that these errors could pose health risks if users rely on AI-generated advice instead of consulting medical professionals.
