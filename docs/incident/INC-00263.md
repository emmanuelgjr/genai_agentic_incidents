### INC-00263  [↗](/incident/INC-00263.html)

**AI-Assisted IPL Ticket Counterfeiting Scam Busted in Lucknow**  
_2026-05-08 · real-world · Severity: High_

An inter-state gang in Lucknow used ChatGPT and graphic design software to create highly convincing fake IPL tickets, defrauding cricket fans. The AI system provided technical details, enabling the production of counterfeit tickets. Four suspects from Chhattisgarh were arrested after victims reported financial losses at the stadium.

**Attack vector:** `other`  

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`
