### INC-00450  [↗](/incident/INC-00450.html)

**AI-Generated Fake Injury Used in Attempted Nail Salon Fraud in South Korea**  
_2026-03-12 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A Turkish national in South Korea used generative AI (ChatGPT) to manipulate photos and medical documents, falsely claiming injury from a nail salon procedure to extort money. The fraud attempt failed, but the incident highlights AI's role in enabling sophisticated deception and attempted financial harm.

**Attack vector:** `other`  

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`
