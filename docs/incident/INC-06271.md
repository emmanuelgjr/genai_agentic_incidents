### INC-06271  [↗](/incident/INC-06271.html)

**AI-Driven Bots and Algorithms Manipulate Social Media, Fueling Misinformation and Division**  
_2020-06-24 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Social media platforms use AI algorithms and automated bot accounts to curate and amplify content, often spreading misinformation and divisive material. Malicious actors, including hackers and foreign governments, exploit these systems to manipulate public opinion, leading to societal harm and erosion of trust in online information.

**Attack vector:** `other`
