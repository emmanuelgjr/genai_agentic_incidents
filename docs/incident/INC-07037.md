### INC-07037  [↗](/incident/INC-07037.html)

**Uber Resumes Self-Driving Car Testing in San Francisco After Fatal Crash**  
_2020-03-10 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Uber has resumed testing its self-driving cars in San Francisco, two years after a fatal accident in Arizona. The new tests involve two Volvo XC90s operating only during daylight with two safety drivers and no passengers, reflecting increased safety measures but ongoing potential risk from autonomous vehicle AI systems.
