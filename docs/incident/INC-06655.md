### INC-06655  [↗](/incident/INC-06655.html)

**Google AI Search Algorithm Suppresses Conservative News Sites**  
_2020-09-20 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Google's AI-driven search ranking system has significantly reduced the visibility of conservative news sites like Breitbart, the Daily Caller, and the Federalist in search results. Reports and data from Sistrix and RealClearPolitics indicate this suppression limits access to certain viewpoints, raising concerns about informational bias and potential influence on democratic processes.
