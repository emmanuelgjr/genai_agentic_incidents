**AI Adoption Leads to Job Losses and Wage Reductions for UK Workers**  
_2026-05-27 · real-world · Severity: High_

In the UK, widespread adoption of AI systems in sectors like translation, writing, and data analysis is displacing workers, reducing job opportunities, and lowering wages. Many professionals, such as translator Jessica Spengler, report being replaced or undercut by AI, resulting in significant economic harm and career disruption.
