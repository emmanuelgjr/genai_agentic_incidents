### INC-06329  [↗](/incident/INC-06329.html)

**AI-Powered Workplace Harassment Detection Raises Privacy and Trust Concerns**  
_2020-01-03 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

AI systems are being developed and deployed to monitor employee communications and detect potential harassment without employees' knowledge. While intended to address workplace misconduct, these tools raise significant concerns about privacy violations, data security, and workplace distrust, as errors or misuse could harm employees' rights and confidentiality.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM05`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`, `ASI05`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MEASURE-2.10`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0057`
