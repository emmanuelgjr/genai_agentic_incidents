**Chinese Secondhand Platform Xianyu's AI Auto-Lists Users' Private Photos for Sale, Prompting Apology**  
_2026-06-01 · real-world · Severity: High_

Chinese secondhand platform Xianyu's AI system automatically accessed users' private photos, including museum artifacts and personal items, and listed them for sale without consent. The incident sparked privacy and legal concerns, leading to public backlash and a platform apology, with promises to improve user confirmation and system safeguards.
