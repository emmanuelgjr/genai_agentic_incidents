**Activists Deploy Facial Recognition AI to Identify Anonymous Police Officers**  
_2020-10-21 · real-world · Severity: High_

Activists have developed and used facial recognition AI systems to identify police officers who conceal their identities during protests, aiming to address police misconduct and lack of accountability. This use of AI responds to harm caused by anonymous law enforcement actions, raising significant concerns about privacy, rights, and surveillance.
