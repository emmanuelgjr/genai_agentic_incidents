### INC-06240  [↗](/incident/INC-06240.html)

**AI Proctoring Software Wrongly Accuses Students of Cheating, Raises Bias and Mental Health Concerns**  
_2020-10-04 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

ProctorU, an AI-based exam proctoring system, falsely flagged a student for cheating after she read a question aloud, resulting in a failing grade. Other students reported racial bias in facial recognition and mental health impacts, highlighting significant harm and rights violations caused by the AI's use in remote education.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`
