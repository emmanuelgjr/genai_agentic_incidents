### INC-05533  [↗](/incident/INC-05533.html)

**Chinese AI Input Methods Collect and Upload User Data, Raising Privacy Concerns**  
_2021-01-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

Investigations revealed that four out of five popular Chinese AI-powered input method apps collect and upload users' typed content, including sensitive information, often by default. This data is used for targeted advertising, raising significant privacy concerns and indicating violations of user rights due to insufficient consent and data protection.

**Attack vector:** `other`
