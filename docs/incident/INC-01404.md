### INC-01404  [↗](/incident/INC-01404.html)

**Meta Faces Antitrust Investigation in Africa Over WhatsApp Business AI Restrictions**  
_2026-02-23 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The Common Market for Eastern and Southern Africa (COMESA) has launched an investigation into Meta's October 2025 updates to WhatsApp Business terms, examining whether they unfairly restrict third-party AI service providers while maintaining full access to Meta's own AI tools. The inquiry covers 21 African markets and focuses on potential anti-competitive harm.

**Attack vector:** `other`
