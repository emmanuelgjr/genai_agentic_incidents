**First Conviction in Ireland for Possession of AI-Generated Child Sexual Abuse Material**  
_2026-05-27 · real-world · Severity: High_

Stephen Buckley of Tralee, Ireland, became the first person in the country convicted for possessing AI-generated child sexual abuse material. He was fined €400 after pleading guilty to possessing images and videos created or manipulated using AI, following an investigation triggered by a US tip-off.
