**Indian Army Workshop Partners to Advance AI-Enabled Drone Technology**  
_2026-03-25 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The 515 Army Base Workshop in Bengaluru has signed strategic agreements with startups and institutions to develop AI-enabled flight control systems, autonomous drones, and enhance drone cybersecurity. These initiatives aim to boost indigenous defense capabilities, but also introduce potential future risks associated with military AI systems.
