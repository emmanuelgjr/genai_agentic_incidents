### INC-00738  [↗](/incident/INC-00738.html)

**Chacao Deploys AI-Powered Robotic Dogs for Public Security Patrols**  
_2026-03-21 · real-world · Severity: High_

The municipality of Chacao, Venezuela, has become the first in Latin America to deploy autonomous AI-powered robotic dogs, "Voltio" and "Turbo," for public security patrols. Equipped with cameras and sensors, these robots monitor public spaces, detect crimes, and communicate incidents to police, raising potential privacy and safety concerns.
