**Kentucky Lawsuit Against TikTok's AI Algorithms Moves Forward**  
_2026-02-23 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A Kentucky judge ruled that the state's lawsuit against TikTok can proceed, alleging the platform's AI-driven recommendation system intentionally exploits young users, causing addiction and mental health harms. The court found sufficient evidence that TikTok targeted Kentucky residents and misrepresented its safety features.
