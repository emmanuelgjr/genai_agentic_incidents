**AI-Enabled Drones Escalate Warfare in Ukraine**  
_2026-04-13 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Russia has deployed AI-integrated drone systems in the Ukraine conflict, enabling autonomous coordination, long-range remote control, and target recognition. These advancements reduce soldiers' exposure but increase the scale and lethality of drone warfare, directly contributing to harm in the ongoing conflict.

**Attack vector:** `other`  

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI10`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`
