### INC-00619  [↗](/incident/INC-00619.html)

**Anthropic's Mythos AI Model Raises Global Cybersecurity Concerns**  
_2026-04-17 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Anthropic's new AI model, Mythos, has demonstrated the ability to autonomously identify and exploit thousands of high-severity software vulnerabilities, surpassing most human experts. Fearing misuse and potential large-scale digital disruption, Anthropic has withheld public release, prompting urgent discussions with the U.S. government on AI safety and critical infrastructure risks.

**Attack vector:** `rce`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM03`, `LLM05`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI04`, `ASI05`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-6.1`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0010`, `AML.T0050`
