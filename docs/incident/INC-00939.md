### INC-00939  [↗](/incident/INC-00939.html)

**Encyclopedia Britannica Sues OpenAI for Unauthorized Use of Copyrighted Content in AI Training**  
_2026-03-16 · real-world · Severity: High_

Encyclopedia Britannica and Merriam-Webster have sued OpenAI in Manhattan, alleging nearly 100,000 articles were used without permission to train ChatGPT. The lawsuit claims AI-generated summaries divert web traffic and infringe intellectual property and trademark rights, seeking damages and an injunction against further unauthorized use.
