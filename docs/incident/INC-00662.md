**Automated Traffic Fines System in Kenya Sparks Legal and Public Backlash**  
_2026-03-09 · real-world · Severity: High_

Kenya's National Transport and Safety Authority (NTSA) has launched an AI-driven Instant Fines Management System that automatically detects traffic violations and issues fines via SMS. The system has triggered public outcry and legal challenges over concerns about due process, constitutional rights, and potential for unfair penalties.
