### INC-07021  [↗](/incident/INC-07021.html)

**Turkey Unveils First Armed AI-Driven Unmanned Naval Vehicle (SİDA) Prototype**  
_2020-12-15 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Ares Shipyard and Meteksan Defense have completed the prototype of SİDA, Turkey's first armed unmanned surface vessel featuring autonomous and AI-driven capabilities. Designed for military operations, SİDA can be remotely or autonomously controlled, raising concerns about potential future harm due to its weaponization and autonomous functions.
