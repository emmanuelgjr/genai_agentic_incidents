**Georgia Prosecutor Disciplined for Submitting AI-Generated Fake Legal Citations**  
_2026-05-05 · real-world · Severity: High_

Georgia Supreme Court disciplined prosecutor Deborah Leslie for submitting court documents with AI-generated, fabricated, or misattributed legal citations in a murder appeal. The court vacated a lower court's order, suspended Leslie from practice before the justices for six months, and mandated ethics education, highlighting AI misuse's impact on legal proceedings.
