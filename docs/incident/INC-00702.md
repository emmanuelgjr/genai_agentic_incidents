### INC-00702  [↗](/incident/INC-00702.html)

**Bot Auto and Ryan Transportation Launch Driverless Freight Operations in Texas**  
_2026-02-25 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Bot Auto, an autonomous trucking startup, is partnering with Ryan Transportation to launch AI-driven, driverless freight runs between Houston and Dallas. The initiative aims to demonstrate the viability of autonomous trucks for overnight logistics, potentially reducing human driver fatigue and operational constraints. No incidents or harm have been reported.
