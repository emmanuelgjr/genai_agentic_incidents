### INC-06119  [↗](/incident/INC-06119.html)

**TikTok Users Distressed by Unremovable AI Beauty Filter Altering Faces Without Consent**  
_2021-06-10 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

TikTok users reported that an AI-powered beauty filter was automatically altering their facial features—such as slimming jaws and smoothing skin—without their consent and with no option to disable it. The incident caused psychological distress and raised concerns about user rights and control over personal image, prompting TikTok to remove the filter.

**Attack vector:** `other`
