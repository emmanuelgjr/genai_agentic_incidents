### INC-07746  [↗](/incident/INC-07746.html)

**AI Data Centers Threaten Water-Stressed California Communities**  
_2026-05-14 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI-driven data centers are rapidly expanding into California's drought-prone regions, significantly increasing water consumption and straining local resources. Lack of transparency and regulatory oversight on water usage heightens the risk of environmental harm to vulnerable communities, prompting legislative efforts to mandate disclosure and planning.
