**Albania's AI Minister 'Diella' at Center of Corruption and Legal Scandal**  
_2026-05-26 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Albania's AI-powered virtual minister 'Diella', developed to combat corruption, is embroiled in scandal. Authorities allege its operators manipulated public contracts, illegally redirected millions of euros, and misused an actress's likeness without consent. Legal and constitutional challenges have been launched, raising concerns about AI governance and accountability in government.
