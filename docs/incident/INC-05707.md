### INC-05707  [↗](/incident/INC-05707.html)

**Instagram AI Filters Criticized for Racial Bias Against Black Users**  
_2021-06-15 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

João Luiz Pedrosa, ex-BBB21 participant, publicly criticized Instagram's AI-powered filters for distorting the facial features and skin tones of Black users, highlighting a lack of inclusivity and racial bias in the platform's design. The incident underscores harm to identity and representation caused by biased AI image processing.

**Attack vector:** `algorithmic-bias`  

**NIST AI RMF:** `MEASURE-2.11`
