**Seeming Pattern of Gemini Bias and Sociotechnical Training Failures Harm Google&#x27;s Reputation**  
_2024-02-21 · real-world · Severity: Medium_
AIID: [`#645`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/645/)

Google&#x27;s Gemini chatbot faced many reported bias issues upon release, leading to a variety of problematic outputs like racial inaccuracies and political biases, including regarding Chinese and Indian politics. It also reportedly over-corrected racial diversity in historical contexts and advanced controversial perspectives, prompting a temporary halt and an apology from Google.
