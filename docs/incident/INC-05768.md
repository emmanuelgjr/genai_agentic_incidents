### INC-05768  [↗](/incident/INC-05768.html)

**Mobileye and Udelv Announce Large-Scale Autonomous Delivery Vehicle Deployment**  
_2021-04-12 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Mobileye and Udelv plan to deploy over 35,000 fully autonomous delivery vehicles, called Transporters, by 2028, starting commercial operations in 2023. The vehicles will use Mobileye's AI-driven self-driving system, raising credible future risks of AI-related incidents due to their scale and driverless operation.

**Attack vector:** `other`
