**Facebook's AI Moderation Fails to Curb Hate Speech in India Due to Policy Interference**  
_2020-08-14 · real-world · Severity: High_

Facebook's AI-driven content moderation flagged hate speech by BJP politicians in India, but company executives, prioritizing business interests, overruled enforcement. This allowed inflammatory and violent content to remain online, contributing to real-world harm and inciting violence against minority communities.
