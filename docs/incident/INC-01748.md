### INC-01748  [↗](/incident/INC-01748.html)

**Rubrik Research Warns of Security Gaps as Enterprise AI Agent Adoption Outpaces Governance**  
_2026-04-21 · real-world · Severity: High_

Rubrik's new research highlights that rapid enterprise adoption of autonomous AI agents is creating significant security risks, including identity sprawl and increased attack surfaces. The lack of adequate governance and controls could plausibly lead to future security breaches and operational disruptions, especially in sectors like healthcare and cloud services.

**Attack vector:** `prompt-injection`
