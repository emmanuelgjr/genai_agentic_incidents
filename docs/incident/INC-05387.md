### INC-05387  [↗](/incident/INC-05387.html)

**AI Malfunction Causes Golden Horde Swarming Munitions to Miss Intended Targets in USAF Test**  
_2021-02-04 · real-world · Severity: High_

During a December test of the US Air Force's Golden Horde program, AI-enabled swarming munitions failed to update flight profiles due to software issues, causing them to strike a failsafe target instead of higher-priority targets. This malfunction highlights operational risks in autonomous military AI systems.
