### INC-05506  [↗](/incident/INC-05506.html)

**Biased Algorithms Cause Discrimination in Key Decision-Making Systems**  
_2021-02-23 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Multiple reports highlight how poorly designed AI algorithms have led to discriminatory outcomes in areas like hiring, healthcare, and vaccine distribution. Examples include Amazon's recruiting tool penalizing women and Stanford's vaccine algorithm disadvantaging frontline workers, demonstrating how algorithmic bias can perpetuate systemic discrimination and harm vulnerable groups.

**Attack vector:** `algorithmic-bias`
