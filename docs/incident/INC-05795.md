### INC-05795  [↗](/incident/INC-05795.html)

**Pennsylvania Mother Uses Deepfake AI to Harass Daughter's Cheerleading Rivals**  
_2021-03-13 · real-world · Severity: High_

Raffaela Spone, a Pennsylvania mother, used AI-generated deepfake images and videos to depict her daughter's cheerleading rivals engaging in inappropriate behavior, sending them to coaches in an attempt to get the girls removed from the team. The victims suffered harassment and emotional distress, leading to Spone's arrest and cyber harassment charges.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
