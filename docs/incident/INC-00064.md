**AI Adoption Outpaces Cybersecurity in Bosnia and Herzegovina, Leading to Increased Cyberattack Risks**  
_2026-03-22 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

A March 2026 report by Check Point Software Technologies reveals Bosnia and Herzegovina faces the highest risk from AI-driven cyberattacks. Rapid AI adoption in public and private sectors has outpaced cybersecurity investments, resulting in vulnerabilities and ongoing exposure to botnets, ransomware, and other AI-enabled threats.
