**Bengaluru Techie Fires Cook After AI Surveillance Detects Theft**  
_2026-03-02 · real-world · Severity: High_

A Bengaluru tech professional, Pankaj Tanwar, used an AI-powered surveillance system in his kitchen to monitor his cook. The AI, integrating vision and language models, detected the cook taking fruits without permission, leading to her dismissal. The incident sparked online debate over privacy, ethics, and labor rights.
