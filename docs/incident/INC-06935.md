### INC-06935  [↗](/incident/INC-06935.html)

**Students Exploit AI Grading Algorithm with Keyword Trick to Obtain Higher Scores**  
_2020-09-04 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Students discovered they could manipulate Edgenuity's AI grading system by inserting relevant keywords into their exam answers, resulting in undeserved high scores without demonstrating real understanding. This vulnerability undermined the fairness and reliability of automated assessments, raising concerns about academic integrity and the effectiveness of AI in education.

**Attack vector:** `other`
