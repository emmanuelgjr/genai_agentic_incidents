**Katsina State to Deploy 50,000 AI-Powered Smart Streetlights**  
_2026-04-29 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Katsina State, Nigeria, has signed an agreement with Conflow Power Group Limited and Mora Energy to deploy 50,000 solar-powered streetlights equipped with AI-enabled cameras and monitoring systems. These iLamp units will function as distributed AI data centers, raising potential privacy and data governance concerns.
