### INC-06539  [↗](/incident/INC-06539.html)

**Expert Warns of China's AI-Driven Global DNA Collection and Bioweapon Risks**  
_2020-12-04 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

China is reportedly building the world’s largest DNA database, using AI for genetic analysis and surveillance. Expert Gordon Chang warns this data could enable the development of bioweapons targeting specific populations, raising significant concerns about future AI-enabled harm and human rights violations, though no actual incident has yet occurred.

**Attack vector:** `other`
