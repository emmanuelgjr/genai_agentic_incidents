### INC-06837  [↗](/incident/INC-06837.html)

**Permanent Health Code AI System in Hangzhou Raises Privacy and Discrimination Concerns**  
_2020-05-25 · real-world · Severity: High_

Hangzhou's plan to make its AI-driven health code system permanent, integrating personal health and lifestyle data for scoring and community ranking, has sparked widespread criticism. The system has already led to privacy violations and social discrimination, raising serious concerns about human rights and data misuse.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`  

**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-4.1`, `MEASURE-2.10`
