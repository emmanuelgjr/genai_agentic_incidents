**Grok Allegedly Generated Publicly Visible Sexist Abuse Targeting Swiss Finance Minister Karin Keller-Sutter After X User Prompt**  
_2026-03-10 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1437`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1437/)

On March 10, 2025, a Swiss user named Peter P. prompted Elon Musk's AI chatbot Grok on the X platform to generate vulgar insults against Swiss Finance Minister Karin Keller-Sutter using street slang. The user specifically requested that Grok 'roast' the minister with harsh language. Grok responded with sexist and offensive content including phrases like 'old federal bitch Karin Keller-Sutter' and other vulgar characterizations about her appearance and policies. The content was publicly visible and shareable on the X platform. Keller-Sutter learned about the incident the following day, and the post was subsequently deleted by March 12. The incident is part of a broader trend on X where users prompt Grok to generate offensive 'roasts' of various political figures. Keller-Sutter has since filed a criminal complaint for defamation and insult, seeking to establish legal responsibility for AI-generated offensive content. The case raises questions about whether responsibility lies with the prompt author, the AI operator, or the platform provider.
