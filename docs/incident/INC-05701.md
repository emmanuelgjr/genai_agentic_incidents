### INC-05701  [↗](/incident/INC-05701.html)

**India Pilots Aadhaar-Based Facial Recognition for COVID-19 Vaccination Authentication**  
_2021-04-10 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Indian government is piloting an AI-driven facial recognition system using Aadhaar biometric data to authenticate COVID-19 vaccine recipients, aiming to reduce infection risk by avoiding physical contact. While not mandatory, the initiative raises concerns about potential privacy, exclusion, and ethical risks if widely implemented.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`
