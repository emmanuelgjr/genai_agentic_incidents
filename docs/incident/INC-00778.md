### INC-00778  [↗](/incident/INC-00778.html)

**China's First AI-Generated Short Drama Copyright Infringement Case**  
_2026-05-09 · real-world · Severity: High_

In Guangzhou, China, two individuals were convicted for illegally recording and selling over 1,700 AI-generated short dramas created with a game company's AI tool. The court recognized these works as protected by copyright law due to human creative input, marking a landmark case for AI content copyright protection.
