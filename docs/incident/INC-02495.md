### INC-02495  [↗](/incident/INC-02495.html)

**Deepfakes Reportedly Impersonated David Taylor-Robinson and Other UK Health Experts to Promote Wellness Nest Supplements**  
_2025-08-11 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1397`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1397/)

In the UK, purported deepfake videos reportedly impersonated Professor David Taylor-Robinson and other health experts on TikTok and other platforms to promote supplements linked to Wellness Nest. The videos allegedly altered real footage and cloned voices to spread misleading medical claims about menopause, misuse identifiable individuals&#x27; likenesses, and expose viewers seeking health advice to health misinformation and deceptive marketing.
