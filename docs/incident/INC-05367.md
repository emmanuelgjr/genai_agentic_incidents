### INC-05367  [↗](/incident/INC-05367.html)

**"Ant Ya Hey" AI Face-Swapping App Removed Over Privacy and Rights Concerns**  
_2021-03-07 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The viral AI-powered face-swapping app "蚂蚁呀嘿" was removed from the App Store after privacy and portrait rights concerns emerged. Experts and lawyers warned of risks including unauthorized use of personal images, potential privacy violations, and misuse for fraud, highlighting the dangers of unregulated AI face-swapping technology.
