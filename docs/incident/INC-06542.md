### INC-06542  [↗](/incident/INC-06542.html)

**Experts Warn German Parliament of AI-Controlled Weapons and Loss of Human Oversight**  
_2020-11-04 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Researchers and experts warned the German Bundestag about the risks of autonomous weapon systems powered by AI, highlighting dangers such as loss of human control, escalation of conflict, and destabilization of security. They urged international agreements to regulate these technologies before fully autonomous, lethal systems are deployed.
