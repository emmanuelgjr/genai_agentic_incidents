**Ford Recalls Over 4 Million Vehicles Due to AI-Controlled Trailer Module Software Defect**  
_2026-02-26 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Ford is recalling approximately 4.4 million vehicles in the U.S. after a software defect in the AI-controlled integrated trailer module was found to disable trailer lights and brakes, posing significant safety risks. The recall affects multiple models from 2021-2026, with a free software update offered as a fix.
