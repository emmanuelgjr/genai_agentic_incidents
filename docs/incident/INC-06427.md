### INC-06427  [↗](/incident/INC-06427.html)

**Cense AI exposes 2.5 million personal records on open database**  
_2020-07 · real-world · Severity: High_

Healthcare-AI startup Cense AI left a 2.5 million-record database of patient names, contact information and insurance details on an internet-facing instance with no authentication, demonstrating ongoing exposure of AI-pipeline data assets.

**Affected:** Cense AI / 2.5M individuals  
**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM03`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI02`, `ASI03`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `GOVERN-6.1`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.10`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0010`, `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0024`, `AML.T0053`, `AML.T0057`
