### INC-00119  [↗](/incident/INC-00119.html)

**AI Chatbots Manipulated to Spread Misinformation via Simple Online Tricks**  
_2026-02-19 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Researchers demonstrated that ChatGPT and Google's AI chatbots can be easily manipulated by creating false online content, causing these systems to spread misinformation on critical topics like health and finance. This vulnerability has led to real harm by misleading users and undermining trust in AI-generated information.

**Attack vector:** `other`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MEASURE-2.8`
