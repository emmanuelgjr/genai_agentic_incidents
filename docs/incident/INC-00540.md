**AI-Powered Drone Joint Venture Formed for Indian Defense**  
_2026-04-28 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Magellanic Cloud, Rayonix Tech, and Israel's XTEND have established a $11 million joint venture to manufacture AI-powered unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) in India. The initiative will integrate XTEND's autonomous operating systems into drones for defense applications, raising potential risks associated with AI-enabled military technologies.
