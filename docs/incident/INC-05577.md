### INC-05577  [↗](/incident/INC-05577.html)

**Deployment of Chinese AI Facial Recognition Surveillance in Siófok Raises Privacy Concerns**  
_2021-06-09 · real-world · Severity: High_

Siófok, Hungary, has installed 39 AI-powered facial recognition cameras from Chinese firm Dahua Technology for public surveillance on Petőfi promenade. The system aims to identify offenders in real time, raising concerns over privacy, potential misuse, and human rights due to the supplier's controversial background and the technology's invasive nature.
