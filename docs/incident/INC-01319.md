### INC-01319  [↗](/incident/INC-01319.html)

**Lawyer Submits AI-Generated Fake Legal Citations in Navarra Court**  
_2026-03-26 · real-world · Severity: High_

A lawyer in Navarra used AI tools to generate eight fabricated legal citations in a judicial filing, undermining the integrity of the legal process. The Tribunal Superior de Justicia de Navarra warned of ethical and legal risks of careless AI use but archived the sanction process after the lawyer apologized and retracted the errors.

**Attack vector:** `misinformation`  

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`
