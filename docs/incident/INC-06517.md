### INC-06517  [↗](/incident/INC-06517.html)

**Dutch Benefits Agency Reviews AI Fraud Detection After Privacy Ruling**  
_2020-03-05 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The Dutch benefits agency UWV is reviewing the legality of its AI-based fraud detection systems after a court banned a similar government system (SyRI) over privacy and discrimination concerns. No harm has been reported, but the review addresses potential risks of privacy violations and wrongful suspicion from algorithmic profiling.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`
