### INC-05413  [↗](/incident/INC-05413.html)

**AI-Enabled Automated Gun Used in Assassination of Iranian Nuclear Scientist**  
_2021-02-09 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Israeli intelligence agency Mossad used a one-tonne automated gun, reportedly equipped with AI capabilities, to assassinate Iranian nuclear scientist Mohsen Fakhrizadeh near Tehran. The weapon was smuggled into Iran and remotely operated, resulting in Fakhrizadeh's death and highlighting the lethal use of AI-driven autonomous weapon systems.
