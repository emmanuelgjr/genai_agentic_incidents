### INC-06318  [↗](/incident/INC-06318.html)

**AI-Powered Skin Cancer Detection Apps Found Unreliable and Poorly Regulated**  
_2020-02-10 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Multiple studies led by UK researchers reveal that AI-based smartphone apps for skin cancer detection frequently miss cases or give false positives, potentially delaying treatment or causing unnecessary anxiety. The apps' poor accuracy and inadequate regulation pose health risks, highlighting the need for stricter oversight and improved validation.
