### INC-01796  [↗](/incident/INC-01796.html)

**Spanish Universities Deploy AI-Detection Tech to Prevent Exam Cheating**  
_2026-05-11 · real-world · Severity: High_

Universities in Spain, particularly in Galicia, Murcia, Catalonia, and Aragón, are implementing frequency detectors and stricter controls during university entrance exams to prevent students from using AI-powered devices for cheating. These measures aim to address the growing risk of academic dishonesty enabled by advanced AI and microelectronic tools.
