**China's First Facial Recognition Lawsuit: Court Orders Deletion of Biometric Data**  
_2021-04-10 · real-world · Severity: High_

A Chinese court ruled in favor of plaintiff Guo Bing, ordering Hangzhou Safari Park to delete his facial and fingerprint data after the zoo unilaterally switched from fingerprint to facial recognition for entry without proper consent. The case highlights privacy risks and legal requirements for AI-based biometric data collection.
