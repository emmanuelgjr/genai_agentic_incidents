**Russia Deploys and Mass-Produces AI-Enabled Combat Robots**  
_2021-05-22 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Russia has begun mass production and deployment of AI-powered autonomous combat robots, such as the Uranus-9 and Marker platforms, for military use. These robots are capable of independent operation and target engagement, raising significant concerns about potential future harm from autonomous weapon systems, though no actual incidents have yet occurred.
