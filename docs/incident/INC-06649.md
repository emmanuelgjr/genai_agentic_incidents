### INC-06649  [↗](/incident/INC-06649.html)

**Gojek's AI System Helps Police Dismantle Fraud Syndicates and Protect Drivers**  
_2020-10-13 · real-world · Severity: High_

Gojek deployed AI and machine learning features to detect fake orders and illegal apps, which have harmed drivers through fraud and financial loss. The system enabled automatic detection and reporting, supporting police in arresting criminal syndicates and enhancing safety for drivers and users.

**Attack vector:** `rce`

**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`
