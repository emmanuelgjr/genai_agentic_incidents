### INC-11753  [↗](/incident/INC-11753.html)

**Reflected Cross site scripting (XSS) in kairosdb**  
_2022-11 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: Medium_

CVEs: `CVE-2019-19040`
CWEs: `CWE-79`
CVSS: **6.1** — `CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:R/S:C/C:L/I:L/A:N`

KairosDB through 1.2.2 has XSS in view.html because of showErrorMessage in js/graph.js, as demonstrated by view.html?q= with a '"sampling":{"value":"<script>' substring.

**Affected:** maven/org.kairosdb:kairosdb  
**Attack vector:** `xss`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM03`, `LLM05`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI04`, `ASI05`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-6.1`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0010`, `AML.T0050`
