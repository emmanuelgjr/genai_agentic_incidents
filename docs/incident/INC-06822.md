### INC-06822  [↗](/incident/INC-06822.html)

**NSO Group's Pegasus Spyware Used for Targeted Surveillance of Activists and Journalists**  
_2020-08-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

The NSO Group's AI-enabled Pegasus spyware was deployed to hack the phones of journalists, activists, and political and religious leaders in countries like Togo and Mexico. The spyware exploited WhatsApp vulnerabilities, enabling authoritarian surveillance, privacy violations, and human rights abuses, including targeting critics before elections and in connection with high-profile murders.
