**Unauthorized AI Clone of Zhang Xuefeng Sparks Legal and Ethical Controversy**  
_2026-04-12 · real-world · Severity: High_

Developers released an AI skill package mimicking deceased educator Zhang Xuefeng, trained on his copyrighted works and personal data without consent. This led to legal and ethical concerns over copyright and personality rights violations, with his company investigating the incident. The controversy highlights risks of AI-driven digital cloning and rights infringement.
