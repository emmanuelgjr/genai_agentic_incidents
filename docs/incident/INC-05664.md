### INC-05664  [↗](/incident/INC-05664.html)

**Google AI Search Links Palestinian Keffiyeh to Terrorism, Sparking Outrage**  
_2021-05-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

Google's AI-driven search algorithm displayed the Palestinian keffiyeh as a 'terrorist symbol' in response to certain queries, leading to widespread anger and accusations of racism and cultural insult from activists. The incident highlights reputational and cultural harm caused by biased AI-generated search results.
