**Israel Halts AI-Enabled Phone Surveillance of COVID-19 Patients Amid Privacy Concerns**  
_2020-06-08 · real-world · Severity: High_

Israel's Shin Bet used AI-driven phone tracking to monitor COVID-19 carriers, sparking criticism over privacy violations. Following opposition from human rights groups and Shin Bet's chief, the government froze legislation to extend the program, suspending its use and highlighting the risks of AI surveillance to civil liberties.
