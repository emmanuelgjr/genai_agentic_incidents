**UK Government AI Allegedly Targets Disproportionate Numbers of Certain Nationals for Fraud Review**  
_2021-12-06 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#611`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/611/)

The UK&#x27;s Department for Work and Pensions (DWP) faced scrutiny after many Bulgarian nationals reported unexplained suspensions of their Universal Credit benefits. The MP for Edmonton raised concerns about potential nationality-based targeting for benefit fraud investigations, leading to poverty and homelessness among affected individuals. The Home Office&#x27;s own equality impact assessment found it was flagging a disproportionate number of marriages from Greece, Albania, Bulgaria and Romania.
