### INC-07029  [↗](/incident/INC-07029.html)

**Twitter Labels AI-Manipulated Biden Video Shared by Trump as 'Manipulated Media'**  
_2020-09-16 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Twitter labeled a widely viewed, AI-manipulated video shared by President Trump that falsely depicted Joe Biden playing an anti-police song as 'manipulated media.' The video, originally posted by a satirical account, spread misinformation and prompted criticism over Twitter's response and the impact of AI-generated political disinformation.

**Attack vector:** `misinformation`
