### INC-01765  [↗](/incident/INC-01765.html)

**Senior Journalist Suspended for Publishing AI-Generated Fake Quotes**  
_2026-03-19 · real-world · Severity: High_

Peter Vandermeersch, a senior journalist at Mediahuis, was suspended after admitting to publishing newsletters containing AI-generated fake quotes. He relied on language models like ChatGPT and Perplexity without proper verification, resulting in misinformation and violating journalistic standards. The incident affected outlets in Ireland and the Netherlands.

**Attack vector:** `misinformation`
