### INC-07895  [↗](/incident/INC-07895.html)

**Kaspersky Warns of Emerging Cognitive AI Risks to Privacy and Autonomy**  
_2026-05-25 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Kaspersky has highlighted four emerging risks from cognitive AI, including advanced phishing, manipulation, digital abuse, and threats to brain-computer interfaces. While no specific incidents have occurred, the company warns that evolving AI capabilities could endanger mental privacy, autonomy, and cybersecurity in the near future.

**Attack vector:** `phishing`  

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`
