**US Cities Push Back Against AI License Plate Reader Surveillance**  
_2026-03-19 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI-powered license plate reader cameras, notably from Flock Safety, have been widely deployed across US cities, including Nevada. Concerns over mass surveillance, privacy violations, and unregulated data sharing have led at least 53 cities to deactivate or reject the technology, highlighting ongoing harm and public backlash against AI-driven policing tools.
