### INC-01403  [↗](/incident/INC-01403.html)

**Meta Encryption Hinders AI Child Safety Systems, Leads to Harm**  
_2026-02-24 · real-world · Severity: High_

Meta executives implemented end-to-end encryption on Facebook and Instagram messaging despite internal warnings that it would severely limit AI-driven content moderation, reducing the detection and reporting of child exploitation. This decision, revealed in court documents from a New Mexico lawsuit, allegedly enabled increased harm to underage users.
