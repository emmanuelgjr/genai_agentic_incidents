### INC-00449  [↗](/incident/INC-00449.html)

**AI-Generated Fake Images Used in Disinformation Campaign Against Taiwanese Baseball Fans**  
_2026-03-10 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI-generated fake images falsely depicting Taiwanese baseball fans littering at Tokyo Dome were spread online, primarily by accounts linked to Chinese disinformation networks. The images, later debunked by Japanese media, caused reputational harm and fueled misinformation, highlighting the malicious use of AI in coordinated disinformation campaigns targeting Taiwan.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
