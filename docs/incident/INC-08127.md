### INC-08127  [↗](/incident/INC-08127.html)

**AI Smart Glasses Used for Covert Recording Spark Privacy Scandal in China**  
_2026-06-06 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

AI-enabled smart glasses, notably from Rokid, were used to secretly record flight attendants and other individuals in China. The use of '遮光貼' stickers to disable recording indicators enabled covert filming, leading to privacy violations and unauthorized online sharing. Rokid responded by removing offending content and upgrading privacy safeguards.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`  

**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-4.1`, `MEASURE-2.10`
