**AI-Enabled Drone Swarms Used in Modern Warfare Cause Harm in Conflict Zones**  
_2020-11-19 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

UK defence officials confirm that AI-powered drone swarms are already being deployed in active conflict zones such as Libya, Azerbaijan, and Armenia, resulting in attacks and harm to people and communities. The UK plans further investment in AI and autonomous military technologies, raising concerns about the risks of AI-driven warfare.
