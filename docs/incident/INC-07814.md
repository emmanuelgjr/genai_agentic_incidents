**German Police Arrest Couple for Espionage Targeting Military AI Technology**  
_2026-05-20 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

German authorities arrested a German-Chinese couple in Munich for espionage, accusing them of stealing advanced military-related AI technology by infiltrating academic circles. The couple allegedly worked for Chinese intelligence, targeting aerospace, computer science, and AI research, and deceived scientists into sharing sensitive information with Chinese state-owned defense companies.
