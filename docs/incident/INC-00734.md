### INC-00734  [↗](/incident/INC-00734.html)

**Car Telemetry Data Sold to Insurers Without Clear Consent**  
_2026-02-19 · real-world · Severity: High_

Automated telemetry systems in new cars collected and transmitted drivers' behavioral data, which automakers like Toyota sold to insurance companies such as Progressive. This led to increased insurance costs and privacy violations for consumers, often without their informed consent or transparency about data use in the United States.
