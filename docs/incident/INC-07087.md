**US Eases Export Restrictions on AI-Enabled Armed Drones, Raising Global Risk Concerns**  
_2020-07-24 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The Trump administration eased export restrictions on AI-enabled armed drones, reclassifying certain models to allow broader sales to allies, including India. While no immediate harm is reported, the policy shift increases the risk of future misuse or escalation of conflict involving autonomous weapons systems.
