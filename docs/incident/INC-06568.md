### INC-06568  [↗](/incident/INC-06568.html)

**Facebook Removes 'Pseudoscience' Ad Category After AI-Driven Misinformation Targeting**  
_2020-04-23 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Facebook's AI-powered ad targeting system enabled advertisers to reach users interested in 'pseudoscience,' facilitating the spread of COVID-19 misinformation and conspiracy theories. After media reports exposed this, Facebook removed the category to prevent further abuse, acknowledging the harm caused by its AI-driven ad platform.
