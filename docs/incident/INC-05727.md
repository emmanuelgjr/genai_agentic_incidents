### INC-05727  [↗](/incident/INC-05727.html)

**Italian Privacy Authority Blocks Police Facial Recognition System Over Mass Surveillance Risks**  
_2021-04-16 · real-world · Severity: High_

Italy's data protection authority rejected the Interior Ministry's Sari Real Time facial recognition system, citing lack of legal basis and risks of indiscriminate mass surveillance. The AI-driven system, not yet active, would process biometric data in real time, potentially violating fundamental privacy and human rights if deployed.
