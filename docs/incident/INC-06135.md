### INC-06135  [↗](/incident/INC-06135.html)

**Turkish Official Warns of AI Risks in '20s Challenge' Social Media Trend**  
_2021-05-07 · real-world · Severity: High_

Deputy Minister Ömer Fatih Sayan warned that the popular '20s challenge' social media trend enables the collection of personal and biometric data, which can be used to train AI algorithms for facial recognition and aging. He highlighted privacy risks and the uncertain security of sensitive data stored on unknown servers.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`
