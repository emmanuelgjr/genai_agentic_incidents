### INC-07073  [↗](/incident/INC-07073.html)

**US Air Force Tests AI-Powered Robotic Dogs in Military Exercises**  
_2020-09-09 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The US Air Force deployed AI-powered robotic dogs, developed by Ghost Robotics, in large-scale military exercises to detect threats and enhance base security. While no harm occurred, the use of autonomous AI systems in military contexts raises concerns about potential future risks and the evolving nature of warfare.
