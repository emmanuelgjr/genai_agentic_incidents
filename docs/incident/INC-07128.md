**Widespread Harms and Legal Backlash from Facial Recognition AI in China**  
_2020-12-19 · real-world · Severity: High_

The deployment of facial recognition AI in China has led to privacy violations, unauthorized data collection, economic losses, and legal disputes. Incidents include forced biometric authentication for park entry, misuse in real estate and public services, and illegal data trade, prompting lawsuits, regulatory interventions, and growing public concern over personal rights and data security.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`
