### INC-01993  [↗](/incident/INC-01993.html)

**US Court Blocks Perplexity AI's Shopping Bots on Amazon**  
_2026-03-10 · real-world · Severity: High_

A US federal court has temporarily barred Perplexity AI from using its Comet AI shopping agent to make purchases on Amazon. Amazon sued Perplexity for unauthorized access and computer fraud, alleging the AI bot shopped on users' behalf without Amazon's consent and ignored requests to stop.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM05`
