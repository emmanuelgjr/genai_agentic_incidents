### INC-08072  [↗](/incident/INC-08072.html)

**Delhi High Court Orders Removal of AI-Generated Deepfakes Targeting Indian Actors**  
_2026-06-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Delhi High Court has issued interim orders protecting actors Naga Chaitanya and Varun Dhawan from unauthorized use of their identities in AI-generated deepfake images, pornographic content, and merchandise. The court directed online platforms to remove such content, citing reputational harm and violation of personality rights caused by AI misuse.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
