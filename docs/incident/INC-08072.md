**Attorneys Sanctioned for Submitting AI-Generated Legal Briefs with Fabricated Content**  
_2026-06-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

Two Orange County immigration attorneys, Mike Singh Sethi and William Rounds, were suspended and fined by the U.S. 9th Circuit Court of Appeals after submitting legal briefs containing fabricated cases and misattributed quotations generated by AI. Their lack of candor about the AI's role led to stricter sanctions.
