### INC-05877  [↗](/incident/INC-05877.html)

**Teens Misuse Tesla Autopilot, Crash Into Police Car in Florida**  
_2021-03-25 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Two underage girls, ages 14 and 15, used a Tesla's autopilot feature while traveling without a licensed driver, resulting in the vehicle backing into a police cruiser during a traffic stop in Florida. The misuse of the AI-driven autopilot system led to property damage and highlighted safety risks of unauthorized autonomous vehicle operation.
