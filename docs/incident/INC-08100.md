### INC-08100  [↗](/incident/INC-08100.html)

**Rapid Deployment of AI Agents Outpaces Security Controls, Creating Cyber Risks**  
_2026-06-04 · real-world · Severity: High_

Rubrik Zero Labs research reveals organizations, especially in the Netherlands, are deploying autonomous AI agents faster than they can secure them. Limited oversight and control over these agents create a 'shadow workforce,' increasing the risk of cyberattacks and operational disruptions. Experts warn of a widening gap between innovation and security.
