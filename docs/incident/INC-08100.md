### INC-08100  [↗](/incident/INC-08100.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfake Child Abuse Images Surge in Hessen**  
_2026-06-04 · real-world · Severity: High_

Authorities in Hessen, Germany, report a rising number of AI-generated sexualized deepfake images of children. Criminals use publicly available photos to create these illegal materials, causing significant harm. The government is expanding resources and training to combat this digital abuse and protect victims.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
