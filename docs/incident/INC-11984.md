### INC-11984  [↗](/incident/INC-11984.html)

**Apache Superset Cross-site Scripting (XSS) vulnerability on the Explore page**  
_2022-05 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: Medium_

CVEs: `CVE-2021-32609`
CWEs: `CWE-79`
CVSS: **5.4** — `CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:L/UI:R/S:C/C:L/I:L/A:N`

Apache Superset up to and including 1.1 does not sanitize titles correctly on the Explore page. This allows an attacker with Explore access to save a chart with a malicious title, injecting html (including scripts) into the page.

**Affected:** pip/apache-superset  
**Attack vector:** `xss`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM03`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI04`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-6.1`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0010`
