**Tesla's AI Self-Driving System Prevents Accident After Driver Passes Out**  
_2026-02-20 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Rishi Vohra, a Tesla Cybertruck owner, lost consciousness due to a medical emergency while driving on a freeway. Tesla's Full Self-Driving (FSD) AI system detected his incapacitation, safely slowed the vehicle, activated hazards, and pulled over, preventing a potential accident and harm. Elon Musk acknowledged the incident.
