**Australia Investigates Social Media Giants Over AI Failures in Age Verification**  
_2026-03-30 · real-world · Severity: High_

Australia is investigating major social media platforms, including Meta, TikTok, and YouTube, for failing to enforce AI-driven age verification systems, allowing children under 16 to access harmful content despite strict new laws. The incident highlights AI system inadequacies in protecting minors online and potential legal consequences for tech companies.
