### INC-00966  [↗](/incident/INC-00966.html)

**European Companies Face AI Governance Gaps Amid Rapid Adoption**  
_2026-03-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

ISACA's AI Pulse Poll 2026 reveals that European companies are rapidly deploying AI systems without adequate governance or protocols. Most organizations lack the ability to quickly stop malfunctioning AI systems or explain failures, creating significant risks of security incidents and operational disruptions. The findings highlight urgent vulnerabilities in AI oversight.
