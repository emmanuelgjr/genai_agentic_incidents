**Student's University Offer Revoked After Biased Grading Algorithm Downgrades Results**  
_2020-08-17 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Jessica Johnson, an award-winning student who wrote about algorithmic bias, had her A-level results downgraded by a government grading algorithm, causing her to lose a university place and scholarship. The incident highlights the harm caused by algorithmic decision-making in education. A government U-turn later restored her original grades and university offer.
