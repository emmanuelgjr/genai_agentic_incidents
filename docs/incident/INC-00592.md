### INC-00592  [↗](/incident/INC-00592.html)

**Angolan Journalist Targeted by Predator Spyware**  
_2026-02-18 · real-world · Severity: High_

Amnesty International revealed that Angolan journalist Teixeira Cândido was targeted with the AI-enabled Predator spyware, developed by Intellexa, in May 2024. The spyware enabled unauthorized access to his device, violating his privacy and potentially threatening press freedom. This marks the first confirmed use of Predator in Angola.

**Attack vector:** `other`
