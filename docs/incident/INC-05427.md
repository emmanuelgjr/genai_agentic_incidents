### INC-05427  [↗](/incident/INC-05427.html)

**AI-Generated Adversarial Glasses Bypass Facial Recognition on 19 Android Phones**  
_2021-01-27 · real-world · Severity: High_

Researchers from Tsinghua University's RealAI used AI-generated adversarial patterns on glasses to bypass facial recognition on 19 Android phones and several financial and government apps, enabling unauthorized access and identity spoofing. Only the iPhone 11 resisted the attack, highlighting significant security vulnerabilities in 2D facial recognition systems.

**Attack vector:** `rce`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`
