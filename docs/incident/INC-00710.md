### INC-00710  [↗](/incident/INC-00710.html)

**Brazilian Regulator Fines Meta for Anti-Competitive Use of AI Chatbots on WhatsApp**  
_2026-04-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

Brazil's competition authority, Cade, upheld a daily fine against Meta for allegedly abusing its dominant position by favoring its own AI chatbots on WhatsApp and excluding competitors. The investigation followed complaints from rival chatbot companies, Luzia and Zapia, citing harm to market competition.
