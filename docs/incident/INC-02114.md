**ZenaTech Develops Autonomous Interceptor Drone for Military Use**  
_2026-03-17 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

ZenaTech has begun developing an AI-powered, single-use autonomous interceptor drone, Interceptor-P1, designed to physically stop hostile drones in flight. Intended for deployment in US defense, the Middle East, and Ukraine, the system poses credible risks of harm due to its autonomous operation in conflict zones.
