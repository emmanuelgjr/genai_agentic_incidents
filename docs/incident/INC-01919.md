### INC-01919  [↗](/incident/INC-01919.html)

**Turkey Plans AI-Based Biometric Tracking for Legal Supervision**  
_2026-04-22 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Turkey's Justice Ministry is preparing to implement the Biometric Signature and Tracking System (BİOSİS), using AI-driven biometric verification and GPS tracking to monitor 450,000 individuals under judicial supervision via smartphones. While aiming to increase efficiency, the system raises concerns about potential privacy violations and rights infringements.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`
