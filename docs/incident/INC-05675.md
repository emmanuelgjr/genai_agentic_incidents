### INC-05675  [↗](/incident/INC-05675.html)

**Google Sued for AI-Driven Location Tracking Despite User Opt-Outs**  
_2021-05-27 · real-world · Severity: High_

Google's AI-enabled location tracking continued collecting user data even after users disabled location services, leading to a lawsuit by Arizona for consumer fraud. Evidence revealed Google made privacy settings difficult to access, causing privacy violations and infringing on user rights through its AI system design and deployment.
