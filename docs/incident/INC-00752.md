### INC-00752  [↗](/incident/INC-00752.html)

**ChatGPT Model Spreads Misinformation by Citing Grokipedia**  
_2026-01-24 · real-world · Severity: High_

OpenAI's GPT-5.2 model has been found to repeatedly cite Grokipedia, an AI-generated encyclopedia by Elon Musk's xAI, as a source for sensitive topics such as Iranian politics and Holocaust denial. This reliance has led to the spread of misinformation and raised concerns about the credibility and harm caused by AI-generated content.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
