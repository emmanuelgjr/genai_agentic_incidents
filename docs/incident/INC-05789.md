### INC-05789  [↗](/incident/INC-05789.html)

**OpenAI's CLIP Vision AI Vulnerable to Simple Handwritten Attacks**  
_2021-03-06 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

OpenAI's experimental vision AI, CLIP, can be easily fooled by handwritten notes attached to objects, causing it to misidentify them. This vulnerability, known as a typographic attack, highlights potential risks if such systems are deployed in safety-critical applications, though no actual harm has yet occurred.

**Attack vector:** `adversarial-input`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0058`
