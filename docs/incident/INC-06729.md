**Israel Deploys AI-Driven Technology to Detect Hezbollah Tunnels on Lebanon Border**  
_2020-01-19 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The Israeli military has begun deploying advanced technological infrastructure, likely incorporating AI, to detect and monitor underground tunnel activity along the Lebanon border. This preventive measure aims to identify and thwart potential tunnels by Hezbollah, though no new tunnels or harm have been reported so far.
