### INC-06917  [↗](/incident/INC-06917.html)

**Smart Speakers' AI Raise Privacy Concerns for Remote Workers**  
_2020-03-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

Law firm Mishcon de Reya and others warned employees to mute or turn off AI-powered smart speakers like Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant while working from home, after incidents of these devices unintentionally recording confidential conversations, raising concerns about privacy breaches and unauthorized data collection by AI systems.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MEASURE-2.10`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0057`
