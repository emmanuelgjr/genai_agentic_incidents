**U.S. Coast Guard Deploys AI-Enabled Autonomous Sail Drones on Great Lakes**

The U.S. Coast Guard is deploying AI-powered autonomous sail drones on the Great Lakes to enhance maritime monitoring, weather data collection, and border security.
