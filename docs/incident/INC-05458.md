### INC-05458  [↗](/incident/INC-05458.html)

**Amazon Invests in AI-Powered Autonomous Trucks for Logistics**  
_2021-06-22 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Amazon has ordered at least 1,000 AI-based autonomous driving systems from startup Plus to automate its semi-trailer trucks, signaling a major move toward self-driving logistics. While no incidents have occurred, the deployment of these AI systems introduces potential future risks related to autonomous vehicle operation.
