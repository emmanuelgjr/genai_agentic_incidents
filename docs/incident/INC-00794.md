### INC-00794  [↗](/incident/INC-00794.html)

**Chinese Lawmaker Raises AI Assistant Privacy Risks; ByteDance Responds**  
_2026-03-05 · real-world · Severity: High_

Chinese lawmaker Li Mengjiao warned of privacy and security risks from mobile AI assistants, citing potential for unauthorized access and data misuse. ByteDance VP Li Liang refuted claims of malicious behavior in compliant AI assistants, emphasizing user control and offering test devices for investigation. The debate highlights concerns over AI system safety and regulation in China.
