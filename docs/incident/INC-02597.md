**Gaggle AI Monitoring at Lawrence, Kansas High School Reportedly Misflags Student Content and Blocks Emails**  
_2025-08-01 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1213`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1213/)

In Lawrence, Kansas, students allege the Gaggle Safety Management AI wrongly flagged benign schoolwork, including art photos and casual messages, as child pornography or threats. The system reportedly deleted content, blocked an email records request, and led to questioning of students. Critics cite chilling effects and privacy risks. A lawsuit filed in August 2025 challenges the district&#x27;s use of Gaggle as unconstitutional surveillance. Gaggle reportedly denies compromising privacy.
