**AI-Driven Cyberattack Threatens Global Satellite Infrastructure**  
_2026-03-21 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Experts warn that agentic AI used by hackers could autonomously exploit vulnerabilities in satellite systems within two years, potentially disrupting GPS, banking, and defense services worldwide. The risk stems from outdated satellite security, raising concerns about a possible 'satellite apocalypse' affecting critical infrastructure.
