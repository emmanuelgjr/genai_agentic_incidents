**AI-Enabled Aksungur Armed Drone Successfully Tests Long-Range Munition**  
_2021-04-25 · real-world · Severity: High_

Turkey's AI-powered Aksungur armed drone successfully hit a target 30 kilometers away using a 340 kg KGK-SİHA-82 munition in a test. While no harm occurred, the demonstration highlights the growing capabilities and future risks of AI-enabled autonomous weapons in military applications.
