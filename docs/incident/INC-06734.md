**Israel Reinstates AI-Powered Shin Bet Surveillance for COVID-19 Tracking Amid Privacy Concerns**  
_2020-07-01 · real-world · Severity: High_

Israel temporarily reinstated Shin Bet's AI-driven cellphone tracking to monitor COVID-19 carriers, sparking privacy and human rights concerns. The program, previously halted due to legal and legislative challenges, was reapproved for three weeks as a stopgap, with safeguards debated amid ongoing public and judicial scrutiny.
