**AI-Driven 3D Modeling Reduces Risks in Cerebral Aneurysm Treatment at San Camillo Hospital**  
_2026-03-21 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

San Camillo Forlanini Hospital in Rome integrated an AI-powered 3D simulation system for planning endovascular treatments of cerebral aneurysms. Over 120 cases were treated in a year, with the AI system significantly reducing patient risk and unnecessary device use, improving safety and lowering costs.
