### INC-04430  [↗](/incident/INC-04430.html)

**Alleged False Accusation of AI-Generated Essay by Turnitin**  
_2023-07-24 · real-world · Severity: Medium_
AIID: [`#572`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/572/)

A university student contacted an organization reporting that they received a zero grade on an assignment after Turnitin's AI detection system flagged 67% of their paper as AI-generated content. The student claims they wrote the assignment by hand and maintains a 4.0 GPA while being on the President's Honor Roll. The student attempted to appeal the decision through their professor and department head but received no help or response. Turnitin's support team also refused to provide assistance when contacted multiple times. The student expressed concern about the impact on their final grade and mentioned considering appeals and media attention if the issue is not resolved. The incident highlights broader concerns about false positives in AI detection tools affecting honest students.

**Affected:** Turnitin, Unspecified University
