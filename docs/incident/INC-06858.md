### INC-06858  [↗](/incident/INC-06858.html)

**Privacy Concerns Over Palantir's AI Use in NHS and Covid-19 Data Projects**  
_2020-05-04 · real-world · Severity: High_

Palantir's AI-driven data analytics platform is being used by the NHS and pitched to other health agencies for Covid-19 tracking, raising significant privacy and data protection concerns. Civil liberties groups and privacy officials warn of potential misuse and lack of transparency, though no actual harm has yet occurred.
