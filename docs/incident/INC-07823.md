### INC-07823  [↗](/incident/INC-07823.html)

**AI Traffic Cameras in Greece Issue Erroneous Fines**  
_2026-05-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

In Greece, AI-powered smart cameras used for traffic enforcement have mistakenly issued fines to drivers, such as one case where a driver was wrongly fined for not wearing a seatbelt due to the camera misidentifying a dark seatbelt against dark clothing. The incident highlights concerns over AI reliability and drivers' rights to contest such penalties.
