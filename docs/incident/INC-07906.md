### INC-07906  [↗](/incident/INC-07906.html)

**AI Note-Taking in Therapy Breaches Patient Trust and Privacy**  
_2026-05-26 · real-world · Severity: High_

A therapist in Fayetteville, Arkansas used an AI tool to record and transcribe sessions without clear patient consent, causing the patient to feel violated and lose trust. The incident highlights privacy and ethical concerns over AI use in sensitive mental health settings, with broader public distrust of such technologies.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MEASURE-2.10`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0057`
