**AI-Generated Fan Edit of Shah Rukh Khan's 'King' Sparks Security Crackdown After Leaks**  
_2026-05-21 · real-world · Severity: High_

An AI-generated fan video using leaked and paparazzi footage from Shah Rukh Khan's upcoming film 'King' went viral online, violating intellectual property rights and prompting the production team to tighten security on set in Mumbai. The incident led to mass reporting and removal of the unauthorized content from social media platforms.
