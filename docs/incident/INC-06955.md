**Tesla Autopilot Design Flaws and Regulatory Gaps Blamed in Fatal 2019 Crash**  
_2020-03-17 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

A fatal 2019 crash in Florida involving a Tesla on Autopilot was attributed to the AI system's design flaws, which allowed driver inattention and activation in inappropriate conditions. The NTSB and IIHS criticized Tesla, drivers, and lax regulations, urging stricter oversight and improved safety measures for autonomous vehicle systems.
