**Pronto Faces Backlash Over AI Training Data Collection in Customers' Homes**  
_2026-05-24 · real-world · Severity: High_

Bengaluru-based startup Pronto faced public and industry backlash after it was revealed that video footage recorded inside customers' homes was being used to train physical AI and robotics systems. The practice, even with opt-in consent, raised serious privacy concerns and potential violations of individuals' rights.
