**DARPA Launches GARD Program to Defend AI Against Adversarial Attacks**  
_2020-04-09 · real-world · Severity: High_

DARPA has selected Intel and Georgia Tech to lead the GARD program, a four-year, multimillion-dollar initiative to develop defenses against adversarial attacks on AI and machine learning systems. The effort aims to prevent future harms, such as misclassification in autonomous vehicles, by improving AI robustness against deception and data poisoning.
