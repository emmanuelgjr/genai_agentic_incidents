**Google Blocks AI-Driven Cyberattack Exploiting Zero-Day Vulnerability**  
_2026-05-10 · real-world · Severity: High_

Google successfully blocked a major cyberattack in which criminals used Anthropic's AI model, Mythos, to discover and attempt to exploit a previously unknown software vulnerability. The incident highlights the growing threat of AI-powered cyberattacks, particularly against critical infrastructure like banking systems.
