**Uber and Rivian Announce Major Investment in Autonomous Robotaxi Fleet**  
_2026-03-19 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Uber will invest up to $1.25 billion in Rivian to deploy up to 50,000 AI-powered autonomous robotaxis by 2031, starting with 10,000 vehicles in San Francisco and Miami in 2028. The partnership aims to expand across 25 cities in the US, Canada, and Europe, raising future AI safety concerns.
