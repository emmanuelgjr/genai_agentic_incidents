**Doomsday Clock Highlights AI-Driven Global Risks**  
_2020-01-23 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The Doomsday Clock was moved to 100 seconds to midnight, its closest ever, with experts citing AI technologies like deepfake media and autonomous weapons as compounding global threats. While no specific AI incident occurred, the warnings highlight the potential for AI systems to contribute to future harm and global instability.
