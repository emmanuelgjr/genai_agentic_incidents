### INC-00400  [↗](/incident/INC-00400.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfake Videos Cause Public Misinformation in Montenegro Fugitive Case**  
_2026-03-24 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI-generated deepfake videos depicting fugitive Miloš Medenica circulated on social media, causing public confusion and undermining trust in institutions. Forensic analysis by Montenegro's Ministry of Interior confirmed AI manipulation, complicating law enforcement efforts and spreading misinformation about Medenica's status.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
