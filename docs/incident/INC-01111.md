**Google Deploys AI Agents for Pentagon Amid Anthropic Legal Dispute and Sanctions**  
_2026-03-10 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Google announced its Gemini AI agents will support the Pentagon's GenAI.mil portal, with potential expansion to classified military networks. This follows the U.S. government's blacklisting of Anthropic, an AI firm, for refusing to allow its technology's use in autonomous weapons and surveillance, prompting Anthropic to sue the Trump administration.
