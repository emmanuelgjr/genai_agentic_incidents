### INC-08052  [↗](/incident/INC-08052.html)

**Lawyers Use Prompt Injection to Manipulate Judicial AI Systems in Brazil**  
_2026-06-02 · real-world · Severity: High_

In Brazil, lawyers inserted hidden commands (prompt injection) into legal documents to manipulate AI systems used by courts, notably the Tribunal de Justiça de Minas Gerais (TJMG). This led to judicial sanctions, ethical and criminal investigations, and raised concerns about the integrity of AI-assisted judicial processes.

**Attack vector:** `prompt-injection`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM01`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI01`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0051`
