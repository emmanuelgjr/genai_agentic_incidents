### INC-07081  [↗](/incident/INC-07081.html)

**US Army Tests AI-Enabled Warfare in Project Convergence Exercises**  
_2020-09-25 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The US Army's Project Convergence 2020 involved live-fire combat exercises using AI-enabled systems, including autonomous drones and the FIRESTORM targeting system, to coordinate attacks and optimize battlefield decisions. While no harm occurred, the deployment of these AI systems in military contexts poses credible risks of future harm in real combat scenarios.
