**AI-Generated Disinformation Causes Social Harm and Legal Action in Vietnam**  
_2026-05-01 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

In Vietnam, authorities have addressed multiple incidents where AI was used to create and spread false or manipulated content online, leading to social unrest, misinformation, and political destabilization. Actions included prosecuting individuals for using AI to fabricate images and videos, and for sharing AI-generated disinformation targeting public opinion and state institutions.
