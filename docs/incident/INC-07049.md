**UK Military Develops AI-Enabled Shotgun Drone for Indoor Combat**  
_2020-09-28 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The UK Ministry of Defence has developed an AI-powered drone equipped with shotguns and machine vision, capable of flying indoors and identifying targets. While weapon firing remains human-controlled, the AI enables autonomous navigation and target recognition, raising concerns about potential harm in future military deployments.
