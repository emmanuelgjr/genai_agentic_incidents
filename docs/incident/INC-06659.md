### INC-06659  [↗](/incident/INC-06659.html)

**Google and Wikipedia AI Error Misidentifies Celebrity Relationships**  
_2020-12-24 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Due to algorithmic errors in Google Search and Wikipedia data integration, Turkish actress Esra Bilgiç was incorrectly listed as the mother of Pakistani actor Yasir Hussain. This AI-driven misinformation led to public confusion and reputational harm, highlighting risks of automated data aggregation in widely used platforms.

**Attack vector:** `misinformation`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MEASURE-2.8`
