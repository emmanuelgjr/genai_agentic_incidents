### INC-07760  [↗](/incident/INC-07760.html)

**AI Voice Cloning Scams Cause Major Financial Losses in the US**  
_2026-05-29 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

AI-powered voice cloning technology has enabled scammers to convincingly impersonate victims' loved ones, leading to significant financial losses in the US. The FBI and FTC report hundreds of millions lost to such scams, with criminals using brief audio samples to create fake voices and deceive individuals and institutions.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
