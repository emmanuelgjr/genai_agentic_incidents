### INC-05368  [↗](/incident/INC-05368.html)

**10-Year-Old Dies After TikTok Blackout Challenge in Italy**  
_2021-01-23 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

A 10-year-old girl in Italy died after participating in the 'Blackout Challenge' promoted on TikTok, raising concerns about the platform's AI-driven content recommendations. Authorities are investigating, while TikTok claims no such content was found on its site and is cooperating with police.
