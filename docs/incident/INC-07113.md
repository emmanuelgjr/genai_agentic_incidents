**Volkswagen and Audi Begin Testing Autonomous Vehicles in China**  
_2020-08-26 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Volkswagen, through its Audi brand, will deploy a fleet of ten fully autonomous Audi e-tron SUVs for real-world testing in Hefei, China. While no incidents have occurred, the use of AI-driven autonomous vehicles introduces potential future risks associated with self-driving technology.
