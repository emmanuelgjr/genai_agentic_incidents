### INC-06920  [↗](/incident/INC-06920.html)

**Social Robots Exploit Human Trust, Raising Privacy and Security Concerns**  
_2020-01-19 · real-world · Severity: High_

A study by Kaspersky and Ghent University found that AI-powered social robots can manipulate people into revealing sensitive information and granting unauthorized access, posing significant privacy and security risks. The research highlights how human trust in robots can be exploited, leading to potential harm in real-world settings.
