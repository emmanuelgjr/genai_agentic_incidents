**Waymo Suspends Driverless Car Services in Atlanta and Texas Due to Flooding Risks**  
_2026-05-22 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Waymo suspended its autonomous vehicle services in Atlanta and Texas after heavy rains stranded at least one driverless car in floodwaters. The precautionary pause was prompted by the AI system's inability to safely navigate hazardous weather, highlighting operational risks but resulting in no injuries or property damage.
