### INC-08132  [↗](/incident/INC-08132.html)

**AI Medical Assistants Show High Error Rate in Health Advice, Raising Patient Safety Concerns**  
_2026-06-06 · real-world · Severity: High_

A study by the University of Pennsylvania found that AI-powered virtual assistants, including ChatGPT and similar models, correctly answered only 76% of common medical questions, with an error rate exceeding 20%—double that of human doctors. This significant inaccuracy raises concerns about patient harm when non-experts rely on AI for medical advice.
