**U.S. Mandates AI-Driven Driver Monitoring Systems in All New Vehicles by 2027**  
_2026-04-27 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The U.S. government has mandated that all new vehicles sold from 2027 must include AI-based driver monitoring systems to detect impairment and potentially prevent driving. Critics warn of privacy risks, false positives, and loss of autonomy, while automakers and regulators acknowledge the technology's readiness and error concerns.
