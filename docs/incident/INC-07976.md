**New Zealand Passes Law Allowing AI to Automate Welfare Benefit Decisions**  
_2026-05-29 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

New Zealand's Parliament passed a law enabling the Ministry of Social Development to use AI-driven automated systems for welfare benefit decisions. The government claims this will improve efficiency and consistency, with safeguards in place, but opposition warns of risks similar to past Robodebt incidents.
