### INC-00493  [↗](/incident/INC-00493.html)

**AI-Generated Podcasts Flood the Market, Disrupting Discovery and Content Quality**  
_2026-05-03 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

AI systems are generating over a third of new podcasts, with companies like Inception Point AI producing thousands of episodes weekly. This surge of low-quality, automated content is overwhelming podcast directories, making it harder for human creators to be discovered and potentially degrading the overall podcast ecosystem.
