### INC-07011  [↗](/incident/INC-07011.html)

**Turkey Advances AI-Enabled Autonomous Military Swarm Technologies**  
_2020-09-10 · real-world · Severity: High_

Turkish defense firms ASELSAN, TUSAŞ, ROKETSAN, and HAVELSAN are developing AI-powered autonomous unmanned vehicles and swarm technologies for coordinated military operations across air, land, and sea. While no incidents have occurred, these advancements present future risks associated with autonomous weapons and AI-driven military systems.
