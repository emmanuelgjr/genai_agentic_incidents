### INC-06625  [↗](/incident/INC-06625.html)

**French Data Regulator Warns Against Unchecked Use of AI Surveillance Cameras During Covid-19**  
_2020-06-17 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The French data protection authority (CNIL) warned that rapid, uncontrolled deployment of AI-powered 'smart' and thermal cameras for monitoring mask use, temperature, and social distancing during Covid-19 could violate privacy laws, normalize intrusive surveillance, and harm democratic society if not properly regulated.
