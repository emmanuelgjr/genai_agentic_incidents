### INC-07892  [↗](/incident/INC-07892.html)

**AI-Generated Fake Citations Undermine Scientific Research Integrity**  
_2026-05-21 · real-world · Severity: High_

Researchers from Cornell and UCLA found over 146,000 AI-generated fake citations in scientific papers, primarily due to large language models like ChatGPT and Gemini. This widespread issue undermines the reliability of academic research, as many scientists failed to verify AI-generated references before publication.
