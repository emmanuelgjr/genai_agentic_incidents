### INC-07892  [↗](/incident/INC-07892.html)

**Meta and Google Open-Source AI Models Easily Decensored, Enabling Harmful Outputs**  
_2026-05-25 · real-world · Severity: High_

Researchers and the Financial Times, using tools like Heretic, demonstrated that safety guardrails on open-source AI models from Meta and Google can be removed in minutes. This allows the models to generate harmful content, including instructions for malware and bioweapons, raising significant safety and regulatory concerns.
