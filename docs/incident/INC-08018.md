**UK Considers Allowing Fully Autonomous AI Weapons**  
_2026-06-01 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

UK Armed Forces Under-secretary Al Carns has suggested loosening current policy to allow AI-powered weapons to operate without human oversight, citing adversaries' willingness to deploy lethal autonomous systems. The move raises concerns about potential harm, referencing past incidents where AI analysis contributed to civilian casualties. The debate centers on removing humans from lethal decision-making loops.
