### INC-11407  [↗](/incident/INC-11407.html)

**Electron affected by libvpx's heap buffer overflow in vp8 encoding**  
_2023-09 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: High_

**🚨 Exploited in the wild** (CISA KEV, added 2023-10-02)

CVEs: `CVE-2023-5217`
CWEs: `CWE-787`
CVSS: **8.8** — `CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:R/S:U/C:H/I:H/A:H`
