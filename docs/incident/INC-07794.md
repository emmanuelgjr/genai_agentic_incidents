### INC-07794  [↗](/incident/INC-07794.html)

**AI Chatbots Spread CCP Propaganda and Generate False Academic Citations**  
_2026-05-19 · real-world · Severity: High_

Studies reveal that global AI chatbots, including ChatGPT, are influenced by Chinese state propaganda embedded in training data, leading to biased outputs favoring CCP narratives. Additionally, large language models generate false academic citations, undermining trust in scientific research. These incidents highlight widespread harm from AI-generated misinformation and bias.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
