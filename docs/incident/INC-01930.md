**Uber Accelerates Investment and Testing of AI-Powered Robotaxi Fleet**  
_2026-04-14 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Uber is rapidly advancing its autonomous Robotaxi project in the US, investing over $10 billion to purchase and deploy AI-driven vehicles from partners like Lucid and Nuro. Employees are already testing the service with human safety drivers. No harm has occurred, but large-scale AI deployment poses future risks.
