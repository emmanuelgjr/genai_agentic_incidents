**BYD's 'God's Eye' AI Driver-Assist System Causes Dangerous Malfunctions in China**  
_2026-03-24 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

BYD's 'God's Eye' AI-powered driver-assistance system, deployed across millions of vehicles in China, has caused dangerous malfunctions including unintended acceleration and erratic lane changes. Multiple owners report near-collisions and safety risks, highlighting the hazards of prematurely deploying advanced AI driving technology.
