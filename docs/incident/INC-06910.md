**Senators Probe Google and Ascension Over Project Nightingale's AI-Driven Health Data Practices**  
_2020-03-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

U.S. senators are investigating Google and Ascension's 'Project Nightingale,' which uses AI to collect and analyze millions of patients' health records without patient consent. Lawmakers cite incomplete disclosures, privacy concerns, and possible HIPAA violations, raising alarms about transparency and the protection of sensitive health data.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`
