### INC-05580  [↗](/incident/INC-05580.html)

**Dubai Police Use AI 'Memory Fingerprint' to Solve Murder Case**  
_2021-01-25 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Dubai Police successfully used an AI-powered 'memory fingerprint' device to analyze suspects' brainwave responses to crime-related stimuli, leading to the identification and confession of a murder suspect. This marks the first use of this AI forensic technology in a criminal investigation, significantly aiding evidence gathering and justice.
