### INC-03199  [↗](/incident/INC-03199.html)

**Scammers Reportedly Used AI-Generated Image of Missing Puppy Hazel to Solicit Fraudulent Vet Payment from St. Petersburg, Florida Couple**  
_2025 · real-world · Severity: Medium_
AIID: [`#1477`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1477/)

Scammers reportedly used an AI-generated or AI-altered image of a missing German Shepherd puppy on an operating table to convince a St. Petersburg, Florida couple that the dog had been hit by a car and needed emergency surgery. The caller allegedly impersonated police and veterinary staff while requesting $1,900 through Zelle. The payment was flagged as fraud, and the dog later returned home unharmed.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
