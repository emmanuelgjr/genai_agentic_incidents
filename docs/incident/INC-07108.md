### INC-07108  [↗](/incident/INC-07108.html)

**Verkada Employees Used AI Facial Recognition to Harass Female Colleagues**  
_2020-10-26 · real-world · Severity: High_

At Silicon Valley startup Verkada, male employees misused the company's AI-powered facial recognition security cameras to take unauthorized photos of female coworkers and share them with sexually explicit jokes on Slack. This abuse of AI technology led to workplace harassment and violations of privacy and labor rights.
