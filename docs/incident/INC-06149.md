**US Air Force Funds AI-Enabled Radar for Autonomous eVTOL Aircraft**  
_2021-01-06 · real-world · Severity: High_

The US Air Force has awarded Metawave Corporation a contract to adapt its AI-enabled SPEKTRA radar system for autonomous electric Vertical Take-Off and Landing (eVTOL) aircraft. While no incidents have occurred, the development of these AI systems poses potential future safety risks if misused or malfunctioning.
