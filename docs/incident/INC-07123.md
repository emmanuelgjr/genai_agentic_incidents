### INC-07123  [↗](/incident/INC-07123.html)

**Weibo's Algorithmic Content Moderation Harms Musicians and Creators**  
_2020-11-23 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Weibo's AI-driven content moderation and recommendation algorithms have repeatedly misclassified musicians' event and promotional posts as marketing, leading to widespread content suppression, reduced visibility, and economic harm. This has triggered collective complaints from musicians, highlighting the negative impact of automated systems on creators' rights and community engagement.
