### INC-11415  [↗](/incident/INC-11415.html)

**x/net/html Vulnerable to DoS During HTML Parsing**  
_2023-09 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: High_

CVEs: `CVE-2018-17846`
CWEs: `CWE-835`
CVSS: **7.5** — `CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:U/C:N/I:N/A:H`

The html package (aka x/net/html) through 2018-09-25 in Go mishandles `<table><math><select><mi><select></table>`, leading to an infinite loop during an `html.Parse` call because `inSelectIM` and `inSelectInTableIM` do not comply with a specification.

**Affected:** go/golang.org/x/net  
**Attack vector:** `dos`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM03`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI04`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-6.1`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0010`
