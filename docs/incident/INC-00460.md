### INC-00460  [↗](/incident/INC-00460.html)

**AI-Generated Fake Police Image Leads to Arrests in Egypt**  
_2026-03-29 · real-world · Severity: High_

In Kafr El-Sheikh, Egypt, a shop owner used AI tools to fabricate an image falsely depicting an employee as a criminal with the Ministry of Interior's logo. The image was shared online as a joke and further disseminated for views, resulting in police intervention and legal action against those involved.
