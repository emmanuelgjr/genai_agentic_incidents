**Emotional Distress Among Chinese Users Following Shutdown of AI Companion Apps**  
_2026-03-15 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

In China, widespread emotional distress has emerged as users mourn the loss of AI-generated companions due to system updates and app shutdowns. The phenomenon, dubbed "cyber widowhood," highlights the real mental health impact caused by the abrupt termination of AI relationships, sparking debate about dependence on virtual partners.
