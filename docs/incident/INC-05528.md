### INC-05528  [↗](/incident/INC-05528.html)

**China's Emotion Recognition AI Used for Mass Surveillance and Human Rights Violations**  
_2021-02-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

Chinese authorities have widely deployed AI-based emotion recognition systems, such as the 'Alpha Eagle Eye,' to monitor, profile, and preemptively arrest individuals based on micro-expressions and inferred intent. This technology, integrated with surveillance cameras, has led to human rights abuses, targeting minorities, dissenters, and ordinary citizens, and is being exported abroad.
