**Metropolitan Police Trials AI to Identify Child Abuse Victims Faster**  
_2026-04-13 · real-world · Severity: High_

The UK's Metropolitan Police is trialling AI technology to rapidly grade and triage child sexual abuse imagery, aiming to identify and safeguard victims more quickly. The AI system is intended to reduce officers' exposure to distressing material and accelerate intervention, with human oversight and victim care remaining central to investigations.
