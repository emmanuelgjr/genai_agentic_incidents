**Italian University Fined for Unlawful Use of Facial Recognition in Online Courses**  
_2026-02-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Italian Data Protection Authority fined eCampus University €50,000 for unlawfully using facial recognition AI to verify student attendance in online teaching courses. The university processed biometric data without proper legal basis or impact assessment, violating privacy laws and affecting hundreds of participants.
