**Nigeria Partners with EIB Group to Localize AI-Enabled Military Technology**  
_2026-04-04 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The Defence Industries Corporation of Nigeria (DICON) signed an MoU with EIB Group to localize production of AI-enabled unmanned aerial systems, tactical drones, and secure intelligence software. This initiative aims to boost Nigeria's defense self-reliance but introduces potential future risks associated with military AI technologies.
