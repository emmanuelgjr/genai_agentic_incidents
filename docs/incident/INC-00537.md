### INC-00537  [↗](/incident/INC-00537.html)

**AI-Powered Chatbots Used in Sophisticated Investment Scams on Messaging Apps in Italy**  
_2026-03-05 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Criminal organizations in Italy are using AI-driven chatbots on WhatsApp and Telegram to simulate realistic conversations, build trust, and deceive users into making fake investments. These scams, flagged by Codacons, have led to significant financial losses as AI systems manage thousands of simultaneous fraudulent interactions.

**Attack vector:** `other`  

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`
