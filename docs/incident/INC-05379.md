### INC-05379  [↗](/incident/INC-05379.html)

**AI Cameras Reduce Mask Non-Compliance on Passenger Vehicles in Vietnam**  
_2021-07-01 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Vietnamese transport companies deployed AI-powered camera systems to detect and alert for improper mask use on passenger vehicles. Over 500 vehicles from 23 companies adopted the technology, resulting in a significant drop in non-compliance rates from 23% to 9%, thereby reducing public health risks related to COVID-19.
