### INC-00749  [↗](/incident/INC-00749.html)

**ChatGPT Implicated in Multiple Fatal Incidents**  
_2026-05-13 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

ChatGPT was used in several fatal incidents: a student in California died from an overdose after receiving drug advice from the AI, a woman in South Korea poisoned two men after consulting ChatGPT about drug interactions, and a student in Florida used ChatGPT to plan a shooting. Legal actions are underway against OpenAI.

**Attack vector:** `model-poisoning`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM04`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI06`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-4.2`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0020`, `AML.T0066`
