### INC-06683  [↗](/incident/INC-06683.html)

**Hungarian-Austrian Autonomous Vehicle Test on Public Road Highlights AI Infrastructure Development**  
_2020-06-22 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Hungarian and Austrian partners conducted a large-scale test of AI-driven autonomous vehicles and intelligent infrastructure on a closed section of the M86 highway. The event aimed to advance vehicle-to-infrastructure communication and safety, but no AI-related harm or malfunction occurred during the trials.
