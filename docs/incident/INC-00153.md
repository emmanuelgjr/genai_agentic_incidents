**AI Deepfake Videos Victimize Students, Prompt Calls for State Action in Pennsylvania**  
_2026-03-19 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

AI-generated deepfake videos depicting Radnor High School students in inappropriate situations caused psychological harm and distress. Parents criticized the school's response and urged Governor Josh Shapiro and state officials to establish statewide standards and protections against AI misuse in schools. The incident occurred in Pennsylvania, USA.
