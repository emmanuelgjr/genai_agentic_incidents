**US Deploys AI-Enabled Israeli Drone Defense System for Special Forces**  
_2020-09-08 · real-world · Severity: High_

The US military, in partnership with Israel, is deploying the AI-enabled Sparrowhawk (Skylord) drone defense system to protect Special Forces from aerial threats. The system uses augmented reality and autonomous capabilities for drone interception, raising potential future risks of harm if malfunction or misuse occurs, though no incidents are reported yet.
