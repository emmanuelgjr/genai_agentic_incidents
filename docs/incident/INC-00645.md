**Arizona Man Indicted for AI-Generated Child Sexual Abuse Images in Landmark Case**  
_2026-05-12 · real-world · Severity: High_

William Powderly, a Chandler, Arizona resident, was indicted on ten felony counts for possessing child sexual abuse material, including AI-generated images that superimposed a real child's face onto explicit photos. This marks Arizona's first prosecution under a new law equating AI-generated child abuse images with traditional offenses.
