### INC-01909  [↗](/incident/INC-01909.html)

**TON and Telegram Launch Autonomous AI Agents for Blockchain Transactions, Raising Future Financial Risks**  
_2026-04-28 · real-world · Severity: High_

TON Tech and Telegram have introduced Agentic Wallets, enabling AI agents to autonomously execute blockchain transactions, including trading, transfers, and staking, without user approval for each action. While users retain control, this innovation poses future risks of unauthorized transactions or financial loss if AI agents malfunction or are compromised.

**Attack vector:** `prompt-injection`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM01`, `LLM05`

**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`
