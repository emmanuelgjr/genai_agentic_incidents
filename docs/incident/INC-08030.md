**Asda Cuts 1,000 Jobs Due to AI-Driven Warehouse Automation**  
_2026-06-01 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Asda is set to cut up to 1,000 jobs as it accelerates the use of AI-enabled automation and robotics in its warehouse operations. The restructuring consolidates logistics at a single Derby site, replacing many roles with automated systems, causing significant economic and social harm to affected workers.
