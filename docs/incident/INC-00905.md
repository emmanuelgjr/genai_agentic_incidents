### INC-00905  [↗](/incident/INC-00905.html)

**Doctors Warn of Risks from AI Self-Treatment for Musculoskeletal Pain in Spain**  
_2026-03-23 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Spanish patients facing long healthcare wait times are increasingly using generative AI tools like ChatGPT for self-treatment of musculoskeletal pain. The Spanish Society of Rehabilitation and Physical Medicine warns that AI-generated advice may be incorrect or fabricated, potentially leading to injury or delayed urgent care without medical supervision.
