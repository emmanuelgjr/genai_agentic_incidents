**German Army Plans AI Integration for Faster Battlefield Decisions**  
_2026-03-25 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The German army, led by Lt. Gen. Christian Freuding, is developing AI tools to accelerate wartime decision-making by rapidly analyzing battlefield data, drawing on lessons from Ukraine. While AI will serve as an advisory aid with human oversight, its deployment in military operations poses credible future risks if misused or malfunctioning.
