### INC-06878  [↗](/incident/INC-06878.html)

**Researchers Hack Robotic Vacuums to Eavesdrop Using AI and Lidar**  
_2020-11-18 · real-world · Severity: High_

Researchers demonstrated that robotic vacuum cleaners equipped with Lidar and AI can be hacked to covertly record speech and audio, even without microphones. By applying deep learning to Lidar data, they recovered private conversations and TV audio, exposing significant privacy risks from AI-enabled household devices.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MEASURE-2.10`
