**Suspect Used ChatGPT to Plan Disposal of Murder Victims in Florida**  
_2026-04-27 · real-world · Severity: Critical_
AIID: [`#1488`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1488/)

Hisham Abugharbieh, accused of murdering two University of South Florida students, used ChatGPT to ask about disposing of bodies and other criminal actions before the crimes. Prosecutors cited these AI-assisted queries as part of the evidence, linking the chatbot's use to the planning and execution of the murders in Florida.
