**Russia Expands AI Facial Recognition for Surveillance and Payments, Raising Rights Concerns**  
_2021-03-10 · real-world · Severity: High_

Russian authorities and retailers have rapidly deployed AI-powered facial recognition systems in Moscow for surveillance and payment, with plans for nationwide expansion. While promoted for convenience and security, rights activists report illegal use, data leaks, and tracking of protesters, highlighting significant privacy and human rights violations linked to the technology's use.
