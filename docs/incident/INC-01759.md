### INC-01759  [↗](/incident/INC-01759.html)

**Security Incidents and Warnings Over OpenClaw AI Agent Vulnerabilities**  
_2026-05-11 · real-world · Severity: High_

OpenClaw, an AI agent platform developed by Peter Steinberger, has caused security incidents including data loss, password theft, and malware distribution due to insecure design and unrestricted access. Singapore's IMDA and security experts have issued warnings, urging organizations to restrict OpenClaw's access to sensitive systems and implement stronger controls.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MEASURE-2.10`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0057`
