**Ukrainian Company Develops AI-Powered Interceptor Drone UEB-1**  
_2026-03-25 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Ukrainian company OSIRIS AI has developed the UEB-1 interceptor drone, which uses artificial intelligence for autonomous target prediction, tracking, and interception of high-speed aerial threats. Publicly demonstrated in Düsseldorf, the AI-enabled drone poses potential risks if deployed in military or security contexts, though no harm has yet occurred.
