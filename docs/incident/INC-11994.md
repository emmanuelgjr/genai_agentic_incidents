### INC-11994  [↗](/incident/INC-11994.html)

**Nokogiri has vulnerable dependencies on libxml2 and libxslt**  
_2022-05 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: High_

CVEs: `CVE-2019-5815`, `CVE-2021-30560`
CWEs: `CWE-416`, `CWE-787`, `CWE-843`
CVSS: **8.8** — `CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:R/S:U/C:H/I:H/A:H`

Use after free in Blink XSLT in Google Chrome prior to 91.0.4472.164 allowed a remote attacker to potentially exploit heap corruption via a crafted HTML page.

**Affected:** rubygems/nokogiri  
**Attack vector:** `other`

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI04`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-6.1`
