### INC-01414  [↗](/incident/INC-01414.html)

**Meta Uses Employee Computer Activity to Train AI Agents and Deploys 'Zuckerbot' Avatar**  
_2026-04-20 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Meta has admitted to extensively monitoring employees' computer activity—including clicks, cursor movements, and app usage—to train advanced AI agents. Additionally, Meta is developing 'Zuckerbot,' a digital avatar of Mark Zuckerberg, to interact with staff. These practices raise concerns about surveillance, privacy, and potential job displacement within the company.
