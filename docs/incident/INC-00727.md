**Campaigners Raise Concerns Over AI Data Centre Expansion on Scottish Green Belt**  
_2026-05-03 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Campaigners and local groups in Lanarkshire, Scotland, are demanding greater transparency over plans to expand an AI growth zone involving data centres on green belt land. They warn of potential environmental harm, high energy use, and community disruption linked to the rapid development of AI infrastructure.
