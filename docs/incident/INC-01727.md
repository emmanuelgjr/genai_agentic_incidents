**Remote-Controlled AI Shuttle Bus Pilot Raises Safety Concerns in Düsseldorf**  
_2026-03-26 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Rheinmetall and its subsidiary Mira, in partnership with Rheinbahn, are piloting AI-powered teleoperated shuttle buses in Düsseldorf. While a safety driver is currently onboard, future plans to remove them raise concerns about potential risks if the AI system malfunctions, highlighting plausible hazards in public transport.
