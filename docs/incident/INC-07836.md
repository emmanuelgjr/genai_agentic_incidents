### INC-07836  [↗](/incident/INC-07836.html)

**AI Reconstructs and Leaks Protected Cockpit Audio from UPS Crash, Forcing NTSB to Restrict Access**  
_2026-05-22 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

AI tools were used to reconstruct and publicly share cockpit voice recordings from spectrogram images released in the NTSB's UPS Flight 2976 crash investigation. This violated federal privacy laws, prompting the NTSB to suspend public access to its investigation dockets to prevent further unauthorized disclosures.
