**Ontario Bans Chinese-Made Drones Over Data Security Concerns**  
_2026-05-20 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The Ontario government has banned the use and future purchase of Chinese-made drones in sensitive police and government operations, citing risks of data security and privacy breaches. The policy aims to prevent potential harm by replacing these AI-powered drones with models from Canada or approved jurisdictions.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`
