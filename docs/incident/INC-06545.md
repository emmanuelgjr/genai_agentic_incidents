### INC-06545  [↗](/incident/INC-06545.html)

**Experts Warn of Deepfake AI Risks for Misinformation and Social Manipulation**  
_2020-11-03 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Lithuanian cybersecurity experts and technologists warn that AI-powered deepfake technology, while currently used mainly for entertainment, poses significant future risks. They highlight the potential for deepfakes to deceive, mislead, and divide society, emphasizing the ease of creating convincing fake videos with just a few photos.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
