### INC-01974  [↗](/incident/INC-01974.html)

**Unauthorized AI-Driven Biometric Data Collection Leads to Arrests in Assam**  
_2026-05-14 · real-world · Severity: High_

Two men from Mizoram were arrested in Silchar, Assam, for illegally collecting facial biometric data from over 200 local youths using AI-based facial recognition technology. The data was gathered without proper authorization or documentation, violating privacy rights and legal protections under the Information Technology Act.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`
