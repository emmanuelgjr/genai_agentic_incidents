### INC-02041  [↗](/incident/INC-02041.html)

**Vietnamese Police Warn Parents of AI Image Processing Risks for Children**  
_2026-05-01 · real-world · Severity: High_

Vietnamese police in Ninh Binh have warned parents about the risks of uploading children's photos to AI-powered image editing apps, which can lead to privacy breaches and potential misuse such as deepfakes or identity theft. The warning highlights the need for caution but reports no actual incidents yet.
