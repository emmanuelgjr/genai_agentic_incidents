**JR East to Trial Level 4 Autonomous Buses on Kesennuma Line BRT**  
_2026-05-08 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

JR East will conduct Level 4 autonomous driving trials on a 15.5 km section of the Kesennuma Line BRT in Miyagi, Japan. The AI system will handle all driving and emergency stops under specific conditions, with staff onboard for safety. No harm has occurred, but future risks remain plausible.
