### INC-04576  [↗](/incident/INC-04576.html)

**Clearview AI tests live facial-recognition cameras and AR glasses**  
_2023 · real-world · Severity: High_

Clearview AI extended its scraped-image facial-recognition database into live-camera and AR-glasses prototypes for law enforcement and military pilots, broadening the surveillance attack-surface of scraped biometrics.

**Affected:** Public surveilled by Clearview-equipped officers  
**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.1`, `GOVERN-1.4`, `MEASURE-2.10`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0024`, `AML.T0057`
