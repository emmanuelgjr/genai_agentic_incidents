### INC-06518  [↗](/incident/INC-06518.html)

**Dutch Court Rules AI Welfare Fraud Detection System Violates Human Rights**  
_2020-02-05 · real-world · Severity: High_

A Dutch court ordered the government to halt its AI-driven welfare fraud detection system, SyRI, after finding it violated privacy and human rights. The system used algorithmic profiling to target low-income and minority neighborhoods, leading to discriminatory surveillance and unjust suspicion of welfare recipients.
