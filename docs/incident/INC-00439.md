### INC-00439  [↗](/incident/INC-00439.html)

**AI-Generated Faces in Chinese Short Drama Spark Portrait Rights Infringement Controversy**  
_2026-04-26 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI-generated short drama 'Chinese Legend: White Snake,' broadcast on Zhejiang TV, used AI to create characters resembling celebrities Xiao Zhan and Wang Yibo without consent, leading to widespread allegations of portrait rights infringement. Legal experts and the celebrities' studios are pursuing legal action, highlighting governance and copyright challenges in China's AI-driven content industry.
