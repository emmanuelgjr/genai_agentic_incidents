**Apple's Siri Glitch Links Police Departments to Terrorists, Prompting Outrage and Apology**  
_2020-09-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

Apple's AI assistant Siri mistakenly directed users to police stations when asked about terrorists, causing public outrage and reputational harm to law enforcement. The incident, widely shared on social media, led Apple to apologize and quickly fix the AI system's error.
