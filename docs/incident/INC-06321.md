### INC-06321  [↗](/incident/INC-06321.html)

**AI-Powered Suicide Prevention on E-Commerce Platforms in China**  
_2020-01-05 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Chinese e-commerce platforms, notably Alibaba, use AI systems to detect suicide risk signals in online orders and communications. These systems alert human 'suicide interventionists,' who coordinate with authorities to intervene, successfully preventing thousands of suicide attempts by identifying at-risk individuals and triggering timely, life-saving responses.
