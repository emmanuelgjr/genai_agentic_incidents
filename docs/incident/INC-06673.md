### INC-06673  [↗](/incident/INC-06673.html)

**Google's Project Nightingale Sparks Privacy Concerns Over AI Access to Patient Data**  
_2020-01-15 · real-world · Severity: High_

Google Health's partnership with Ascension, known as Project Nightingale, used AI to develop a search tool for electronic health records, granting Google employees access to confidential patient data. This raised significant privacy concerns, internal whistleblower complaints, and public backlash over potential violations of patient rights and data protection laws.
