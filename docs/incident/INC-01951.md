**UK Retailers' AI Facial Recognition System Falsely Accuses Shoppers of Theft**  
_2026-05-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

Multiple UK retailers using Facewatch's AI facial recognition system have falsely accused innocent shoppers of theft, leading to public embarrassment and distress. The incidents highlight flaws in the technology, lack of clear recourse for victims, and regulatory gaps, raising concerns about privacy and rights violations.
