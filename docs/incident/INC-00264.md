**AI-Assisted Targeting by Project Maven Leads to Civilian Deaths in Iran**  
_2026-03-13 · real-world · Severity: Critical_
AIID: [`#1492`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1492/)

The AI system Project Maven, developed by Palantir and used by the US and Israeli militaries, played a central role in accelerating battlefield decisions and target selection in Iran.
