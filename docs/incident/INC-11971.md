### INC-11971  [↗](/incident/INC-11971.html)

**Improper Neutralization of Input During Web Page Generation in Jenkins**  
_2022-05 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: Medium_

CVEs: `CVE-2015-7536`, `CVE-2017-1000392`, `CVE-2019-10401`
CWEs: `CWE-79`
CVSS: **5.4** — `CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:L/UI:R/S:C/C:L/I:L/A:N`

In Jenkins 2.196 and earlier, LTS 2.176.3 and earlier, the f:expandableTextBox form control interpreted its content as HTML when expanded, resulting in a stored XSS vulnerability exploitable by users with permission to define its contents (typically Job/Configure).

**Affected:** maven/org.jenkins-ci.main:jenkins-core, maven/org.jenkins-ci.main:jenkins-core  
**Attack vector:** `xss`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM03`, `LLM05`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI04`, `ASI05`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-6.1`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0010`, `AML.T0050`
