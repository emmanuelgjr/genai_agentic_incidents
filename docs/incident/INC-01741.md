### INC-01741  [↗](/incident/INC-01741.html)

**Romanian AI Integrated into US Military Counter-Drone Systems in the Middle East**  
_2026-03-11 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Nemesis AI, developed by Romanian company OVES Enterprise, has been integrated into the US Army's EAGLS counter-drone systems deployed in the Middle East. The AI enables real-time detection, classification, and interception of aerial threats, directly influencing military operations and decisions with potential for harm in active conflict zones.
