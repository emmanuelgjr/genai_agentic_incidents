### INC-06873  [↗](/incident/INC-06873.html)

**Researchers Demonstrate AI Vulnerability in Autonomous Vehicle Perception Systems**  
_2020-03-06 · real-world · Severity: High_

Researchers from the University of Michigan showed that AI-based perception systems in autonomous vehicles can be tricked by spoofed LiDAR signals, causing cars to detect nonexistent obstacles. This manipulation can lead to sudden braking or freezing, posing risks to traffic safety and potentially causing accidents or disruptions.
