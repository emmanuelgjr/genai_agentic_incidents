### INC-00301  [↗](/incident/INC-00301.html)

**AI-Driven Gig Platforms Cause Global Labor Rights Violations**  
_2026-05-13 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Human Rights Watch reports that gig workers in nine countries face labor rights abuses, unsafe conditions, and economic harm due to AI-driven algorithmic management by platform companies. These systems control pay, task assignments, and account status, leading to exploitation and lack of protections for workers.
