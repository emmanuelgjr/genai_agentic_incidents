### INC-06257  [↗](/incident/INC-06257.html)

**AI System Uncovers Debtor's Hidden Assets, Enables Court Enforcement in Guangzhou**  
_2020-09-21 · real-world · Severity: High_

Guangzhou Internet Court used the 'E-Chain Cloud Mirror' AI system to analyze a debtor's financial and behavioral data, revealing hidden income and spending. This led to the identification of the debtor's ability to pay, resulting in successful enforcement of a court judgment after the debtor initially claimed inability to pay.
