### INC-01241  [↗](/incident/INC-01241.html)

**Instagram Algorithm Amplifies Antisemitic Content to Millions, Report Finds**  
_2026-03-26 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Research by the Combat Antisemitism Movement's Antisemitism Research Center reveals that Instagram's AI-driven recommendation system systematically promoted antisemitic content, including AI-generated fake personas, to millions of users. Over a 96-hour period, 100 such posts reached up to 280 million users, generating millions of likes and shares.
