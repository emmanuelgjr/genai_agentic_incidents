**In writeToParcel of CursorWindow.cpp, there is a possible out of bounds read due to uninitialized data.**  
_2025-09 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: Medium_

CVEs: `CVE-2025-26448`
CWEs: `CWE-457`
CVSS: **5.5** — `CVSS:3.1/AV:L/AC:L/PR:L/UI:N/S:U/C:H/I:N/A:N`
