### INC-01052  [↗](/incident/INC-01052.html)

**French Voice Actors Win Removal of AI-Cloned Voice Models**  
_2026-04-02 · real-world · Severity: High_

Twenty-five French voice actors secured the removal of 47 AI-generated voice models from U.S. platforms Fish Audio and VoiceDub, which had cloned their voices without consent or payment. Legal action highlighted violations of intellectual property rights, though actors continue to seek damages and further legal protections.
