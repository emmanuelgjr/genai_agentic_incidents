### INC-06264  [↗](/incident/INC-06264.html)

**AI Uses Smartphone Data to Predict Schizophrenia Relapses**  
_2020-10-13 · real-world · Severity: High_

Cornell Tech researchers developed an AI system using smartphone data—such as movement, sound, and sleep patterns—to predict schizophrenia relapses up to a month in advance. The system aims to provide early warnings to patients and clinicians, potentially preventing harmful episodes and hospitalizations.

**Attack vector:** `other`  

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`
