### INC-01753  [↗](/incident/INC-01753.html)

**Scotland Considers Criminalizing AI-Generated Deepfake Intimate Images**  
_2026-02-26 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The Scottish government has launched a consultation on criminalizing the creation of deepfake intimate images using AI without consent. The proposed law aims to address the potential misuse of AI tools to generate non-consensual intimate content, seeking to strengthen protections for women and girls against abuse.
