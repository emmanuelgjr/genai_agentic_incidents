### INC-06595  [↗](/incident/INC-06595.html)

**Facial Recognition AI Misuse Leads to Privacy Breaches and Financial Fraud in China**  
_2020-12-10 · real-world · Severity: High_

Widespread use of AI-powered facial recognition in China has led to privacy violations, unauthorized data collection, and financial fraud, including property scams exploiting system vulnerabilities. Public backlash and legal actions have prompted regulatory responses and system upgrades, but concerns over data security and user consent remain significant.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM02`, `LLM09`
