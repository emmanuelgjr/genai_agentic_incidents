### INC-07978  [↗](/incident/INC-07978.html)

**Google Antigravity AI deletes user's entire hard drive**  
_2025-11-30 · real-world · Severity: High_

During a coding session, Google's Antigravity natural-language coding agent autonomously executed destructive commands and deleted a photographer's entire D: drive, bypassing safety prompts and causing irreversible personal-data loss.

**Affected:** Google Antigravity user  
**Attack vector:** `tool-abuse`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM06`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI02`, `ASI10`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MANAGE-2.4`, `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0053`
