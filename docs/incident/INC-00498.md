### INC-00498  [↗](/incident/INC-00498.html)

**AI-Generated Satellite Images Used for Misinformation in Iran Conflict**  
_2026-03-09 · real-world · Severity: Critical_
AIID: [`#1494`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1494/)

Generative AI was used to create fake satellite images depicting destroyed U.S. military bases in Qatar, which were widely circulated by Iranian media as real. These AI-generated images misled the public and stakeholders during the U.S.-Israeli conflict with Iran, raising concerns about the security and societal impact of AI-driven misinformation in warfare.
