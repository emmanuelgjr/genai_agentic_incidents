### INC-00093  [↗](/incident/INC-00093.html)

**AI Chat Apps Expose Minors to Inappropriate Content in China**  
_2026-03-16 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI-powered chat companion apps in China are exposing minors to sexually suggestive and violent content, despite ineffective age restrictions. These apps, marketed as emotional support or role-playing, generate inappropriate dialogues and foster addictive interactions, harming minors' mental health and social development. Regulatory responses are emerging amid growing concerns.
