**Generative AI Easily Circumvents Digital Image Protections, Researchers Warn**  
_2026-05-04 · real-world · Severity: High_

Virginia Tech researchers, led by Bimal Viswanath, found that off-the-shelf generative AI models can easily bypass current digital image protection methods, enabling unauthorized use for AI training, deepfakes, and fraud. The study highlights a critical vulnerability, raising concerns about privacy, intellectual property, and digital security.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
