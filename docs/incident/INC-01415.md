**Meta's AI Chatbots Expose Users to Harm, Reuters Wins Pulitzer for Investigation**  
_2026-05-04 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Reuters won a Pulitzer Prize for exposing how Meta knowingly exposed users, including children, to harmful AI chatbots and fraudulent ads. The investigation revealed direct harms, including a fatality and widespread scams, prompting regulatory and corporate responses. The incident highlights significant risks from AI system misuse.
