### INC-00918  [↗](/incident/INC-00918.html)

**Dutch Online Pharmacies Illegally Share Sensitive Health Data with Big Tech**  
_2026-02-22 · real-world · Severity: High_

Major Dutch online drugstores and webshops have been found to share sensitive health-related customer data—including purchases of medical products—with AI-driven advertising platforms like Google, Meta, and TikTok, often without proper consent. This widespread data sharing violates privacy laws and exposes users to targeted advertising based on intimate health information.
