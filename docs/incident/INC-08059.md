### INC-08059  [↗](/incident/INC-08059.html)

**Palantir's AI Technologies Spark Human Rights and Dependency Concerns in Europe and UK**  
_2026-06-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

European governments and UK officials are moving to reduce reliance on Palantir's AI-powered data and surveillance platforms due to ethical, political, and human rights concerns. Amnesty International and UK parliamentary reports highlight Palantir's role in alleged human rights abuses and urge ending its public sector contracts, especially in healthcare.
