**AI-Enabled Sea Drone Swarms and Robotic Vehicles Deployed in Ukraine's Defense**  
_2026-06-02 · real-world · Severity: High_

Ukrainian defense units, supported by startups like BlueShadow, are deploying AI-powered autonomous sea drone swarms and robotic vehicles to protect against Russian attacks, particularly near Odesa. These AI systems, armed with missiles and interceptor drones, are part of a broader effort to enhance military capabilities using advanced autonomous technologies.
