### INC-05828  [↗](/incident/INC-05828.html)

**Ride-Hailing Platforms Use AI Algorithms for Discriminatory Pricing and Overcharging in China**  
_2021-04-26 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Chinese ride-hailing platforms used AI-driven algorithms and big data to discriminate against users based on device type, leading to higher fares and reduced discounts for iPhone users. Official investigations and academic studies confirmed systematic overcharging and inaccurate billing, resulting in direct financial harm and unfair treatment of consumers.

**Attack vector:** `algorithmic-bias`
