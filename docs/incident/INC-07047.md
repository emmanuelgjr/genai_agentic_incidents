**UK Government Considers Legalizing Automated Lane Keeping Systems on Roads**  
_2020-08-18 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The UK government is consulting on allowing AI-powered Automated Lane Keeping Systems (ALKS) and other driverless technologies on public roads. While aiming to improve safety and efficiency, experts warn of potential risks and legal challenges if these autonomous systems malfunction or are inadequately regulated.
