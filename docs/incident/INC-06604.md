**Facial Recognition in China Sparks Privacy Backlash and Legal Action**  
_2020-12-04 · real-world · Severity: High_

Chinese wildlife parks and public restrooms faced public and legal backlash after deploying facial recognition systems without proper consent, leading to privacy infringement rulings and device removals. These incidents highlight societal harm and legal consequences from AI-driven biometric surveillance in public spaces.
