### INC-00508  [↗](/incident/INC-00508.html)

**AI-Generated Video Falsely Links Politicians to Crime, Sparks Legal Action in Brazil**  
_2026-03-16 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Senator Flávio Bolsonaro published AI-generated videos falsely associating Gleisi Hoffmann, Lula, and the PT party with criminal organizations. The manipulated content led to reputational harm and misinformation, prompting legal actions by Gleisi Hoffmann and the PT federation against Flávio Bolsonaro and the PL party. The incident occurred in Brazil.

**Attack vector:** `misinformation`
