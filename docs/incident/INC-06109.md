### INC-06109  [↗](/incident/INC-06109.html)

**TikTok Algorithm Promotes Banned Far-Right Content in the US**  
_2021-03-29 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

A Media Matters for America investigation found that TikTok's AI-driven recommendation algorithm promotes and facilitates the spread of banned far-right extremist content, including groups linked to the January 6 Capitol insurrection. Despite platform policies prohibiting such content, users are easily exposed to and encouraged to follow these accounts.
