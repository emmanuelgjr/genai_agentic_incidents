**Anthropic Expands Access to Mythos AI Cybersecurity Model Amid Global Risk Concerns**  
_2026-06-01 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Anthropic is expanding access to its AI cybersecurity model, Mythos, to 150 partners in over 15 countries, including the EU. While Mythos can identify critical software vulnerabilities, experts and authorities warn of potential misuse for cyberattacks, posing significant risks to critical infrastructure. No actual harm has occurred yet.
