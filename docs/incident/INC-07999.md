**China Develops AI Algorithm for Autonomous Lethal Drone Swarms**  
_2026-05-30 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Chinese researchers have developed the HG-STR AI algorithm, enabling drone swarms to autonomously identify and eliminate enemy targets even under communication jamming and limited visibility. While tested only in simulations, the technology poses significant risks of autonomous lethal action without human oversight, raising global concerns about AI-driven warfare.
