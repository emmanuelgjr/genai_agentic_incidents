**Amsterdam University Students Sue Over AI Exam Surveillance Software**  
_2020-05-31 · real-world · Severity: High_

The University of Amsterdam's student council has filed a lawsuit against the university over its use of Proctorio, an AI-based online exam surveillance system. Students argue the software violates privacy and want the right to refuse its use, while the university claims a justified interest in preventing exam fraud.
