**Ofcom Criticizes TikTok and YouTube for Failing to Protect Children from Harmful AI-Driven Content**  
_2026-05-19 · real-world · Severity: High_

UK regulator Ofcom has criticized TikTok and YouTube for failing to implement meaningful safety improvements to their AI-driven recommendation feeds, resulting in widespread exposure of children to harmful content. Despite regulatory demands and evidence of harm, both platforms have not committed to significant changes, prompting further regulatory scrutiny.
