**UK Police Urge Ban on Unsafe AI-Driven Social Platforms for Children**  
_2026-05-19 · real-world · Severity: High_

UK police and crime agencies have called for a ban on social, AI, and gaming apps for under-16s unless platforms disable high-risk AI features like private messaging and harmful content recommendations. They cite ongoing harm to children due to tech firms prioritizing profit over safety.
