### INC-00443  [↗](/incident/INC-00443.html)

**AI-Generated Fake Court Documents Used in Fraud Attempt in Batman, Turkey**  
_2026-04-14 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

In Batman, Turkey, scammers used AI and deepfake technology to create fake court documents and attempted to defraud a citizen via WhatsApp, demanding 30,000 TL under threat of imprisonment. The fraud was detected and prevented by the victim's lawyer, highlighting the risks of AI-enabled document forgery.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
