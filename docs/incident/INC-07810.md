### INC-07810  [↗](/incident/INC-07810.html)

**Amnesty International Exposes Generative AI's Systemic Privacy and Human Rights Violations**  
_2026-05-28 · real-world · Severity: High_

Amnesty International's investigation reveals that leading generative AI platforms, including ChatGPT, Gemini, and others, systematically violate privacy and human rights by scraping massive amounts of personal data without consent for model training. These practices, embedded in AI system design, also perpetuate bias and environmental harm globally.
