### INC-07810  [↗](/incident/INC-07810.html)

**AI Systems Enable Biometric Data Theft from Hand Photos**  
_2026-05-19 · real-world · Severity: High_

Experts warn that AI systems can analyze photos showing hands to extract and clone fingerprints, posing risks of identity theft and unauthorized access. High-resolution images shared on social media may expose sensitive biometric data, making users vulnerable to digital security breaches. The warnings are particularly relevant in Mexico.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`  

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`
