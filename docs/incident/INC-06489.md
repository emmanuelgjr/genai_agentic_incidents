**DARPA Develops AI for Autonomous Jet Dogfighting, Raising Future Risk Concerns**  
_2020-03-11 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

DARPA is advancing its Air Combat Evolution (ACE) program to develop AI algorithms for automating aerial dogfights, aiming to build pilot trust in AI-controlled combat. While still in development and simulation, the intended military use of autonomous AI in combat poses credible future risks of harm or misuse.
