**Delivery of AI-Enabled Armed Drones to Turkish Gendarmerie Raises Future Risk Concerns**  
_2020-02-29 · real-world · Severity: High_

Six Bayraktar TB2 AI-enabled armed drones were delivered to Turkey's Gendarmerie General Command. While no harm has occurred, the deployment of these autonomous military systems presents a credible risk of future incidents involving injury or rights violations due to their potential for autonomous lethal operations.
