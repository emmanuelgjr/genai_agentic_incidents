### INC-02115  [↗](/incident/INC-02115.html)

**ZenaTech Develops Autonomous Maritime Drone Defense System**  
_2026-03-10 · real-world · Severity: High_

ZenaTech has begun developing the IQ Glider, an autonomous marine launch and refueling station to support its AI-powered ZenaDrone 2000 interceptor drones for maritime defense. The system enables continuous, ship-based, multi-drone operations, raising future risks associated with autonomous military AI deployment. Patent protection for the system has also been filed.
