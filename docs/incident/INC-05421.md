**AI-Enabled Drones Used in Repeated Attacks on Saudi Military Base**  
_2021-04-21 · real-world · Severity: High_

Yemeni forces used AI-enabled Qasef 2K drones to launch multiple precise attacks on Saudi Arabia's King Khalid Air Base, causing damage to military infrastructure. The incidents highlight the direct use of autonomous drone systems in military operations, resulting in harm to property and escalating regional conflict.
