### INC-00565  [↗](/incident/INC-00565.html)

**AI-Powered Video Surveillance Pilot at Berlin Government Sites Raises Privacy Concerns**  
_2026-03-09 · real-world · Severity: High_

Berlin officials plan to test AI-based video surveillance at the Red Town Hall, House of Representatives, and Interior Administration. The system will analyze camera footage to detect suspicious behavior and trigger alarms. While data is promised to be anonymized, concerns remain about potential privacy and rights violations.
