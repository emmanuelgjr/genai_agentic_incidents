**AI Threatens Up to 5 Million Jobs in France Within Five Years**  
_2026-03-18 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Multiple studies warn that AI, especially generative and agentic systems, could threaten up to 5 million jobs in France within two to five years. Highly qualified and well-paid professions, including engineering, IT, and creative sectors, are particularly vulnerable to automation and productivity gains from AI technologies.
