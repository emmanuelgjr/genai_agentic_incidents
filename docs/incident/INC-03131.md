**Purportedly AI-Generated Video Allegedly Depicted Radnor High School Students Inappropriately, Prompting Police Investigation**  
_2025-12-09 · real-world · Severity: Critical_
AIID: [`#1378`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1378/)

In December 2023, an incident occurred at Radnor High School in Pennsylvania involving AI-generated inappropriate content depicting several students. The incident was reported to school administration, who immediately began an internal investigation and contacted Radnor Township Police. The police conducted an investigation with the Delaware County District Attorney's Office, and harassment charges were filed against a juvenile in late January 2024. The AI-generated content was described as sexualized images of students created using unregulated apps that can transform innocent photos into explicit content. Parents reported that affected students experienced psychological distress and safety concerns. The incident prompted discussions about policy changes, improved communication protocols, and the need for education about AI risks. Parents criticized the school's handling of the investigation, citing delays and inadequate support for victims. The expert Dr. Robert D'Ovidio noted this was not an isolated incident and that similar cases are occurring nationwide.
