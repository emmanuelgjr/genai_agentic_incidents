### INC-01658  [↗](/incident/INC-01658.html)

**Polish Teacher Victimized by AI-Generated Deepfake; Data Protection Authority Involvement**  
_2026-03-27 · real-world · Severity: High_

A Polish teacher became the victim of a deepfake, with her image manipulated by AI to create a nude photo that was then posted online without consent. The incident caused emotional harm and violated data protection laws. The Polish Data Protection Authority reported the case to prosecutors, highlighting the criminal nature of such AI misuse.
