**Turkish Drone Strike Kills Iraqi Soldiers, Triggers Diplomatic Crisis**  
_2020-08-11 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

A Turkish drone strike in Iraq's Sidakan region killed two Iraqi officers and a soldier, prompting Iraq to cancel a planned visit by Turkey's defense minister and summon the Turkish ambassador. The incident, involving an AI-powered drone, led to strong Iraqi condemnation and heightened diplomatic tensions over sovereignty violations.
