**Tesla Launches Unsupervised Robotaxi Service in Dallas and Houston**  
_2026-04-19 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Tesla has expanded its autonomous Robotaxi rideshare service to Dallas and Houston, deploying fully unsupervised vehicles without human safety drivers. The service, powered by Tesla's AI-driven full self-driving software, marks a significant step in commercial autonomous vehicle deployment, though no incidents or harm have been reported.
