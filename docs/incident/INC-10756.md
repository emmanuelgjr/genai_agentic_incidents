### INC-10756  [↗](/incident/INC-10756.html)

**Kubernetes Nil pointer dereference in KCM after v1 HPA patch request**  
_2024-11 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: High_

CVEs: `CVE-2024-0793`
CWEs: `CWE-20`, `CWE-476`
CVSS: **7.7** — `CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:L/UI:N/S:C/C:N/I:N/A:H`

A flaw was found in kube-controller-manager. This issue occurs when the initial application of a HPA config YAML lacking a .spec.behavior.scaleUp block causes a denial of service due to KCM pods going into restart churn.

**Affected:** go/k8s.io/kubernetes
