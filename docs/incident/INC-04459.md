### INC-04459  [↗](/incident/INC-04459.html)

**Apparent Failure to Accurately Label Primates in Image Recognition Software Due to Alleged Fear of Racial Bias**  
_2023-05-22 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#587`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/587/)

Eight years after Google Photos mislabeled images of Black individuals as &quot;gorillas,&quot; image recognition software by Google, Apple, Amazon, and Microsoft still shows signs of either avoiding or inaccurately categorizing primates. Tests reveal that Google and Apple Photos refrain from labeling primates altogether, possibly to avoid the risk of perpetuating racial stereotypes. Microsoft OneDrive fails to identify any animals, while Amazon Photos overgeneralizes in its labeling.

**Attack vector:** `algorithmic-bias`
