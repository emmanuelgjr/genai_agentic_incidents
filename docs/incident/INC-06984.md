### INC-06984  [↗](/incident/INC-06984.html)

**Tesla's Misleading 'Full Self-Driving' AI Raises Safety Concerns**  
_2020-10-22 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Tesla is rolling out its 'full self-driving' AI software to more vehicles, marketing it as autonomous despite disclaimers requiring driver supervision. Experts warn this misleading promotion could lead to overreliance, misuse, and increased risk of accidents, as previous misuse of the system has already resulted in fatalities.
