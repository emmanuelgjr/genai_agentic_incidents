### INC-00420  [↗](/incident/INC-00420.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfakes Spread Disinformation About India's Defense and Foreign Policy**  
_2026-05-08 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

AI-generated deepfake videos falsely depicting Indian officials making statements about military losses and foreign policy have circulated online, prompting India's Ministry of External Affairs and Press Information Bureau to issue urgent alerts debunking the misinformation. The incident highlights the use of AI in spreading disinformation, potentially harming public trust and India's international image.
