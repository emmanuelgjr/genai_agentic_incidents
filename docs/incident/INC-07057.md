### INC-07057  [↗](/incident/INC-07057.html)

**UK Safety Experts Warn Automated Lane Keeping Systems Pose Road Risk**  
_2020-10-22 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

UK government plans to legalize Automated Lane Keeping Systems (ALKS) for hands-free driving on motorways have drawn strong warnings from safety experts and insurers. They argue current ALKS technology cannot reliably detect hazards or replicate human driving, posing significant risks of accidents and endangering lives if deployed without further safeguards.
