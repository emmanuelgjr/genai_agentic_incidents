**Australian Techie Uses AI to Develop Cancer Vaccine for Dog**  
_2026-03-15 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Australian tech expert Paul Conyngham used AI tools, including ChatGPT and AlphaFold, to analyze his dog Rosie's tumor DNA and design a personalized mRNA cancer vaccine after conventional treatments failed. The AI-assisted intervention led to significant tumor shrinkage and improved the dog's health and quality of life.
