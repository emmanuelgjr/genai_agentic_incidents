**US Government Flags Anthropic's Mythos AI as National Security Risk**  
_2026-05-01 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The US Department of Defense and White House have labeled Anthropic's advanced AI model, Mythos, as a national security and supply chain risk due to its powerful cyber capabilities. Concerns center on potential misuse, unauthorized access, and the need for strict oversight before broader deployment.
