**Warnings of Large-Scale Human Job Displacement by AI Robots**  
_2026-02-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

Multiple reports and experts, including former Citi executive Rob Garlick, warn that AI-powered robots could outnumber human workers within decades as companies accelerate automation to cut costs. Projections suggest up to 4 billion AI robots by 2050, raising concerns about massive workforce displacement and economic disruption globally.
