### INC-07750  [↗](/incident/INC-07750.html)

**Brazilian Deputy Fined for AI-Generated Image Defaming Bolsonaro**  
_2026-05-27 · real-world · Severity: High_

Brazilian federal deputy Rogério Correia was ordered by a Brasília court to pay R$20,000 in damages and issue a public retraction to ex-president Jair Bolsonaro. Correia published an AI-generated image falsely associating Bolsonaro with banker Daniel Vorcaro and alleged corruption, causing reputational harm. The court recognized the direct harm caused by the AI-manipulated content.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
