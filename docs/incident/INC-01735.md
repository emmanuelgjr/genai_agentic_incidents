**Researchers Warn of Risks from Evolvable Artificial Intelligence**  
_2026-04-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

Researchers from Hungary and Belgium warn that evolvable AI systems, capable of autonomous evolution and self-improvement, could soon emerge. These systems pose unique risks, such as loss of human control and resource competition, and require new regulatory approaches to mitigate potential future harms.
