**China Successfully Tests AI-Powered Autonomous Cargo Aircraft HH-200**  
_2026-04-15 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

China's state-owned AVIC completed the inaugural flight of the HH-200, an unmanned cargo aircraft equipped with AI-based autonomous flight and obstacle avoidance systems. The test in Shaanxi province was successful, highlighting the potential for large-scale autonomous logistics, though future deployment could pose risks if the AI malfunctions.
