### INC-07989  [↗](/incident/INC-07989.html)

**SEC Charges Texas Man for $12.3M Crypto Fraud Using Fake AI Trading Bots**  
_2026-05-30 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The SEC charged Texas resident Nathan Fuller for orchestrating a $12.3 million crypto fraud by falsely claiming to use AI-powered trading bots. Fuller misled around 150 investors with promises of high returns and fake AI technology, resulting in significant financial losses. The AI claims were central to the deception.

**Attack vector:** `other`
