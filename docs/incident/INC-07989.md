**Model Sues Fashion Brand Over Unauthorized AI-Generated Images**  
_2026-05-30 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

New York model Francheska Pujols sued Rainbow Shops for using AI to generate and publish altered images of her in suggestive poses without her consent, after their contract expired.
