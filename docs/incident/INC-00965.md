**European Banking Authority Warns of AI-Driven Cybersecurity Risks to Banks**  
_2026-04-16 · real-world · Severity: High_

Francois-Louis Michaud, the new president of the European Banking Authority, warned that while European banks are currently resilient, they must prepare for emerging cybersecurity threats posed by artificial intelligence. Regulators are prioritizing stress tests and risk assessments to address potential AI-driven cyberattacks on the banking sector.
