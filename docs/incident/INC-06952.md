### INC-06952  [↗](/incident/INC-06952.html)

**TensorFlow before 1.7.0 has an integer overflow that causes an out-of-bounds read, possibly causing disclosure of the contents of process memory.**  
_2020-05 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: Medium_

CVEs: `CVE-2018-21233`
CVSS: **6.5**

TensorFlow before 1.7.0 has an integer overflow that causes an out-of-bounds read, possibly causing disclosure of the contents of process memory. This occurs in the DecodeBmp feature of the BMP decoder in core/kernels/decode_bmp_op.cc.

**Affected:** google/tensorflow  
**Attack vector:** `other`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM03`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI04`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-6.1`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0010`
