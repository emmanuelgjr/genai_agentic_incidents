### INC-00181  [↗](/incident/INC-00181.html)

**AI Misuse and Fraud Prevention in China's Financial and Social Platforms**  
_2026-03-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

In China, AI technologies have been misused for deepfake scams, including impersonating analysts and bypassing biometric authentication, causing financial losses. Conversely, platforms like MiLian Technology and Yiren Zhike deploy AI-driven risk control systems to prevent fraud, significantly reducing scam cases and protecting users' property and rights.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`
