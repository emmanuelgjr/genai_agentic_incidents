**SsangYong Begins Public Road Testing of Level 3 Autonomous Driving in Korea**  
_2020-12-02 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

SsangYong has started testing its Level 3 autonomous driving system on public roads in Korea, using the Korando SUV. The AI-driven system allows the vehicle to control most driving tasks, but no incidents or harm have been reported during these initial tests.
