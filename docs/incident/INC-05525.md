**China Tests AI Emotion-Detection on Uyghur Detainees in Xinjiang**  
_2021-05-25 · real-world · Severity: High_

Chinese authorities have deployed AI-based emotion-detection and facial recognition systems on Uyghur detainees in Xinjiang, using the technology to monitor and pre-judge individuals' emotional states. The AI is tested on restrained subjects in police stations and detention camps, contributing to mass surveillance and human rights abuses against the Uyghur minority.
