### INC-07809  [↗](/incident/INC-07809.html)

**US Regulators Pause Bank Cybersecurity Exams Over AI Model Risks**  
_2026-05-19 · real-world · Severity: High_

US regulators, including the Federal Reserve and the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency, have paused cyber-related examinations of major banks to address security risks exposed by Anthropic's Mythos AI model. The pause allows banks to strengthen defenses against potential AI-enabled cyber threats, though no harm has yet occurred.

**Attack vector:** `other`
