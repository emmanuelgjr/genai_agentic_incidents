### INC-05605  [↗](/incident/INC-05605.html)

**Facebook Alleged in Lawsuit Misleading Public about Effects of Algorithms on Children**  
_2021-04-29 · real-world · Severity: Medium_
AIID: [`#534`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/534/)

Between April 29 and October 21, 2021, Meta (formerly Facebook) and its executives allegedly violated federal securities law by intentionally misleading the public about the negative impact of its products on minors. The lawsuit was filed by Ohio Attorney General Dave Yost on behalf of Meta investors and the Ohio Public Employees Retirement System. Internal research conducted by Facebook showed that Instagram was harmful to some teenage girls, with the photo-sharing app worsening body issues for one in three teenage girls. These revelations emerged through The Wall Street Journal's 'Facebook Files' series beginning September 13, 2021, and testimony from whistleblower Frances Haugen. The disclosures caused Facebook stock to fall by $54.08 per share, resulting in over $100 billion in losses for investors including the Ohio Public Employees Retirement System. During earnings calls, CEO Mark Zuckerberg allegedly downplayed concerns about how Facebook's algorithm might amplify controversial content, claiming the company's practices were 'quite robust' and that they 'go out of our way to try to reduce' extremist content. The company had also planned to build an Instagram app for kids under 13, but suspended the project in September 2021 amid growing bipartisan political pressure following the harmful effects revelations.
