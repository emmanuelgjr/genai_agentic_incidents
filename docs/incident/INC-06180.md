**Waymo and Cruise Apply for Permits to Launch Paid Autonomous Rides in San Francisco**  
_2021-05-11 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Alphabet's Waymo and GM-backed Cruise have applied for permits to offer paid autonomous vehicle rides and deliveries in San Francisco. While Waymo plans to start with human-supervised rides, Cruise aims for fully driverless operations. Regulatory approval is pending, raising potential future risks associated with large-scale urban deployment of AI-driven vehicles.
