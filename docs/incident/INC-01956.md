**Ukraine Deploys AI Turrets for Autonomous Drone Interception in Combat**  
_2026-05-09 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Ukraine has deployed AI-powered turrets developed by Brave1, which autonomously detect, track, and intercept enemy drones, including fiber-optic UAVs. Defense Minister Mykhailo Fedorov confirmed their combat use along the front lines, marking direct AI involvement in military operations and harm to enemy assets.
