**Spanish Army Tests AI-Enabled Drones and Robots for Future Combat**  
_2026-04-15 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Spanish Army is conducting large-scale testing of AI-enabled drones, robots, and autonomous systems at its Viator base in Almería, inspired by warfare in Ukraine. These experiments aim to modernize military capabilities, presenting plausible future risks of harm if such AI systems malfunction or are misused in combat scenarios.
