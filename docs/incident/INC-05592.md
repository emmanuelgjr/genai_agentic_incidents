### INC-05592  [↗](/incident/INC-05592.html)

**Experts Warn AI Sex Robots Could Be Hacked to Cause Physical Harm or Death**  
_2021-06-03 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Cybersecurity experts warn that AI-powered sex robots, which can move autonomously and mimic human voices, could be hacked by criminals to gain full control over their actions. This raises credible risks of physical harm or even death to users, as attackers could manipulate the robots' movements and strength.

**Attack vector:** `other`
