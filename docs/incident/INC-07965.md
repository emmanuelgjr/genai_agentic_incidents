### INC-07965  [↗](/incident/INC-07965.html)

**BYD Offers Uncapped Liability Coverage for Self-Driving Car Accidents in China**  
_2026-05-29 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

BYD announced it will assume full financial liability for accidents caused while using its Urban Navigate on Autopilot feature in China, covering all economic losses including repairs, property damage, and personal injury. The policy applies if the system is used in compliance with regulations, marking an industry-first commitment.
