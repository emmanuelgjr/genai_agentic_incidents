**Romanian AI-Powered Military Drone INTERJET Raises Global Security Concerns**  
_2026-05-28 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Romanian company OVES Enterprise has developed INTERJET, an autonomous interceptor drone powered by Nemesis AI, capable of high-speed flight and carrying explosive payloads. Designed for rapid aerial threat interception, the system is being marketed for use in conflict zones like Ukraine and the Middle East, raising concerns over potential autonomous weapon-related harm.
