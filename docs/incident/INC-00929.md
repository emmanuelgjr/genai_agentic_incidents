### INC-00929  [↗](/incident/INC-00929.html)

**El Salvador Entrusts Public Healthcare Management to Google's AI System**  
_2026-04-15 · real-world · Severity: High_

El Salvador's government, led by President Nayib Bukele, has launched the second phase of Dr. SV, an AI-powered healthcare platform developed with Google Cloud. The system autonomously manages patient data, diagnoses, and chronic disease monitoring. Experts warn of potential privacy violations and labor rights issues, raising concerns about future AI-related harms.
