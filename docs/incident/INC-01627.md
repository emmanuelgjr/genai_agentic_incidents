### INC-01627  [↗](/incident/INC-01627.html)

**Penguin Random House Sues OpenAI Over ChatGPT's Alleged Copyright Infringement**  
_2026-03-31 · real-world · Severity: High_

Penguin Random House has filed a lawsuit against OpenAI in Munich, alleging that ChatGPT reproduced and generated texts and illustrations closely resembling the copyrighted children's book series "Der kleine Drache Kokosnuss" by Ingo Siegner. The publisher claims unauthorized use of protected works in AI training and output, raising significant copyright concerns.
