### INC-07040  [↗](/incident/INC-07040.html)

**Uber Sued Over Automated Driver Account Deactivations**  
_2020-10-27 · real-world · Severity: High_

Former UK Uber drivers, supported by the ADCU union, have sued Uber in a Dutch court, alleging that automated algorithms unjustly accused over 1,000 drivers of fraud and deactivated their accounts without appeal. The drivers claim this AI-driven process violated their labor and data protection rights under GDPR.
