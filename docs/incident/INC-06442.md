### INC-06442  [↗](/incident/INC-06442.html)

**China's First AI-Generated Article Copyright Infringement Case Decided**  
_2020-03-14 · real-world · Severity: High_

Tencent sued Net Loan Home for unauthorized reposting of a news article generated by its AI system, Dreamwriter. A Shenzhen court ruled the AI-generated article is protected by copyright, found infringement, and ordered compensation. This landmark case affirms legal protection for AI-generated works in China.
