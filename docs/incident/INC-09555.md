### INC-09555  [↗](/incident/INC-09555.html)

**Denial of service in jackson-dataformat-toml**  
_2023-08 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: High_

CVEs: `CVE-2022-38749`, `CVE-2022-38750`, `CVE-2022-38751`, `CVE-2022-38752`, `CVE-2022-40149`, `CVE-2022-40150`, `CVE-2022-40152`, `CVE-2022-40153`, `CVE-2022-40154`, `CVE-2022-40155`, `CVE-2022-40156`, `CVE-2022-41854`, `CVE-2022-42003`, `CVE-2022-42004`, `CVE-2023-3894`
CWEs: `CWE-121`, `CWE-20`, `CWE-400`, `CWE-502`, `CWE-674`, `CWE-787`
CVSS: **8.6** — `CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:C/C:N/I:N/A:H`

Those using jackson-dataformats-text to parse TOML data may be vulnerable to Denial of Service attacks (DOS). If the parser is running on user supplied input, an attacker may supply content that causes the parser to crash by stackoverflow. This effect may support a denial of service attack.

**Affected:** maven/com.fasterxml.jackson.dataformat:jackson-dataformat-toml  
**Attack vector:** `dos`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM03`, `LLM05`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI04`, `ASI05`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-6.1`, `MEASURE-2.7`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0010`, `AML.T0010.001`, `AML.T0050`
