### INC-00156  [↗](/incident/INC-00156.html)

**AI Deepfakes Used in Financial Fraud Targeting Investors in China**  
_2026-03-14 · real-world · Severity: High_

Scammers in China used AI face-swapping and voice cloning to create fake videos of well-known financial experts, luring investors into fraudulent investment schemes. These deepfake videos, distributed via social media and chat groups, led to significant financial losses for victims by exploiting the credibility of public figures.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
