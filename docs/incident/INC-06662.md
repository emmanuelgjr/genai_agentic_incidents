### INC-06662  [↗](/incident/INC-06662.html)

**Google Maps Route Leads to Teen's Death in Siberian Cold**  
_2020-12-10 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Two 18-year-olds in Russia followed a Google Maps AI-recommended shortcut onto an abandoned, hazardous road in extreme cold. Their car broke down, leaving them stranded in -50°C weather. One teen died from hypothermia, while the other survived with severe frostbite. Google has since removed the dangerous route.
