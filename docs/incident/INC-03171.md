### INC-03171  [↗](/incident/INC-03171.html)

**Reported Use of Deepfake Video Impersonating Owen Wilson in Romance Scam with Fake Job Payments**  
_2025-05-16 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1126`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1126/)

A Reddit user reported in May 2025 that her mother is being groomed by scammers using an AI-generated deepfake video impersonating actor Owen Wilson. The scam reportedly began on a game app (Yahtzee with Friends) and shifted to WhatsApp voice calls, with the victim promised a fake Warner Bros job paying small sums via Cash App. The scammer allegedly claims he will buy a house for the victim&#x27;s family as part of the deception. The video is cited as the main “proof” of authenticity.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
