### INC-01933  [↗](/incident/INC-01933.html)

**Uber Announces Major Investment in Autonomous Vehicle Partnerships**  
_2026-04-15 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Uber has announced plans to invest over $10 billion in autonomous vehicle technology, partnering with companies like Baidu, Rivian, and Lucid to develop robotaxi services. The strategy marks a shift from Uber's traditional gig-economy model, but no AI-related harm or incidents have been reported. The initiative targets multiple cities globally.
