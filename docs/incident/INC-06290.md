### INC-06290  [↗](/incident/INC-06290.html)

**AI-Enabled Military Drones Cause Harm in Nagorno-Karabakh Conflict**  
_2020-09-30 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Armenia and Azerbaijan have actively deployed AI-enabled reconnaissance and strike drones in the Nagorno-Karabakh conflict, leading to increased casualties and destruction of military equipment. These autonomous or semi-autonomous systems have directly contributed to harm through targeted strikes and surveillance, marking a significant escalation in AI-driven warfare.
