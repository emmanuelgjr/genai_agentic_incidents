### INC-06236  [↗](/incident/INC-06236.html)

**AI Navigation Errors Endanger Tourists in Sardinia's Gorropu Canyon**  
_2020-07-14 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Google Maps and Apple Maps, using AI-driven navigation, have repeatedly directed tourists to dangerous, closed roads near Sardinia's Gorropu canyon. This has led to safety risks, emergency rescues, and economic harm to local operators, prompting local authorities to demand urgent corrections from the tech giants.
