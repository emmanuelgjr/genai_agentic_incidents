### INC-00044  [↗](/incident/INC-00044.html)

**Advocacy Group Urges US to Screen AI Models for Security Risks Before Release**  
_2026-05-11 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Americans for Responsible Innovation urged the Trump administration to require safety reviews of advanced AI models, such as Anthropic's Mythos, for cyberattack and weapons development risks before public release. They recommend withholding government contracts from companies whose models fail these security screenings.
