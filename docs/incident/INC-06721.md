**Instagram's AI Moderation Faces Backlash for Biased Censorship of Body Types**  
_2020-10-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

Instagram's AI content moderation system censored a parody nude photo by comedian Celeste Barber, while allowing a similar image by a professional model to remain. The incident sparked public outcry over algorithmic bias favoring thin, white, cisgender bodies, highlighting discriminatory harm caused by the platform's AI-driven censorship.
