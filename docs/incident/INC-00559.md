### INC-00559  [↗](/incident/INC-00559.html)

**AI-Powered Toys Cause Emotional Harm to Young Children Due to Misinterpretation**  
_2026-03-11 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A University of Cambridge study found that AI-powered conversational toys, such as Gabbo, often misinterpret young children's emotions and provide inappropriate or dismissive responses. These malfunctions have led to emotional harm, including confusion and anxiety, raising concerns about psychological safety and the need for tighter regulation in the UK.
