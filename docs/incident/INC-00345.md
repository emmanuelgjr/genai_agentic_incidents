**AI-Enabled Emergency Totem Facilitates Rapid Recovery of Stolen Motorcycle in Teresina**  
_2026-04-18 · real-world · Severity: High_

In Teresina, Brazil, a stolen motorcycle was recovered within 30 minutes after the victim used an AI-powered emergency totem (SPIA system) to report the crime. The system enabled real-time communication with police, leading to immediate action and successful recovery of the vehicle.
