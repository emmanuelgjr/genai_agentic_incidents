### INC-01120  [↗](/incident/INC-01120.html)

**Google Restricts and Suspends AI Ultra and Antigravity Users Over OpenClaw OAuth Abuse**  
_2026-02-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

Google abruptly restricted and suspended accounts of AI Ultra and Antigravity users who accessed Gemini AI models via the third-party OpenClaw OAuth tool. The enforcement, citing "malicious usage" and service degradation, disrupted access to AI services and, in some cases, broader Google accounts, impacting paying subscribers and developers.
