**Canadian Liberal Party's Use of AI Facial Recognition in Candidate Selection Raises Privacy Concerns**  
_2021-06-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Liberal Party of Canada’s use of AI-powered facial recognition for candidate selection has prompted privacy concerns from civil liberties groups and scrutiny from B.C.'s privacy commissioner. Critics warn of potential privacy violations and reliability issues, urging transparency and regulatory review, though no direct harm has yet been reported.
