**AI-Powered Wearable ECG Devices Prevent Sudden Cardiac Deaths in Taiwan**  
_2020-11-26 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Taipei Veterans General Hospital and Tri-Service General Hospital have developed AI-powered wearable ECG devices that use deep learning to detect dangerous heart arrhythmias in real time. These systems enable early diagnosis and rapid intervention, significantly reducing the risk of sudden cardiac death among high-risk patients.
