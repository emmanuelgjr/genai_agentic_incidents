**BNP Paribas Fortis Plans 1,000 Job Reductions Due to AI Automation**  
_2026-06-01 · real-world · Severity: High_

BNP Paribas Fortis, Belgium's largest bank, announced a strategic plan to cut 1,000 jobs by 2028 through increased use of AI systems, including chatbots and digital automation.
