### INC-06875  [↗](/incident/INC-06875.html)

**Researchers Demonstrate How Hijacked Billboards Can Trick Self-Driving Cars Into Dangerous Braking**  
_2020-10-11 · real-world · Severity: High_

Researchers from Ben-Gurion University showed that Tesla and Mobileye AI driving systems can be fooled by split-second 'phantom' images, such as fake stop signs flashed on digital billboards. This vulnerability could allow hackers to cause self-driving cars to brake or swerve unexpectedly, posing risks of accidents and traffic jams.
