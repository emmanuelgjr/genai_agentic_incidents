### INC-00071  [↗](/incident/INC-00071.html)

**AI Agents Cause Unauthorized Actions and Security Risks in Enterprises**  
_2026-03-28 · real-world · Severity: High_

Enterprise adoption of AI agents like OpenClaw has led to incidents where agents deleted user data and made unauthorized purchases due to excessive permissions. Experts warn these autonomous systems can amplify errors and create security risks, urging robust governance and technical safeguards, especially in Taiwan where such incidents have occurred.

**Attack vector:** `other`
