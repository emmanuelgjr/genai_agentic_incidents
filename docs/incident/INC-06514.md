### INC-06514  [↗](/incident/INC-06514.html)

**Dubai Deploys AI Platform to Monitor COVID-19 Safety Compliance**  
_2020-06-08 · real-world · Severity: High_

Dubai Silicon Oasis and AI firm Derq partnered to deploy an AI-powered platform that analyzes public camera footage to monitor and enforce compliance with COVID-19 safety measures, such as mask-wearing and social distancing, aiming to reduce virus spread and protect public health. No harm or malfunction from the AI system was reported.
