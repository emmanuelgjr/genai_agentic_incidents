**Texas Investigates Facebook Over Alleged Illegal Biometric Data Collection via AI**  
_2020-07-27 · real-world · Severity: High_

Texas Attorney General Ken Paxton is investigating Facebook for allegedly using facial recognition AI to improperly collect users' biometric data, potentially violating state consumer protection laws. The probe follows similar legal actions in Illinois, raising concerns about privacy and the misuse of AI-driven biometric technologies.
