**Self-Driving Bus Collides with Tram on First Passenger Day in Gothenburg**  
_2026-05-25 · real-world · Severity: High_

On its first day carrying passengers in Gothenburg, Sweden, an autonomous bus operated by Västtrafik was rear-ended by a tram after braking near Kapellplatsen. No injuries were reported, but the incident caused property damage and service disruption, highlighting risks in deploying AI-driven public transport systems.
