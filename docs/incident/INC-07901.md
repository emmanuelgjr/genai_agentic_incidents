### INC-07901  [↗](/incident/INC-07901.html)

**Robinhood Launches AI Agents for Autonomous Trading and Credit Card Use**  
_2026-05-25 · real-world · Severity: High_

Robinhood has introduced AI agents that can autonomously execute stock trades and credit card purchases for users via dedicated accounts. While safeguards like notifications and account isolation are in place, experts and regulators warn of potential financial risks and liability issues, though no actual harm has been reported yet.

**Attack vector:** `other`

**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`
