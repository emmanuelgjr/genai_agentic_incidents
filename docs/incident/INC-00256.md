### INC-00256  [↗](/incident/INC-00256.html)

**AI Voice Cloning Used in Silent Call Fraud Scheme in Indonesia**  
_2026-04-17 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Authorities in Tangerang, Indonesia, warn the public about a new scam where fraudsters use AI to clone victims' voices from silent phone calls. The cloned voices are then used to deceive victims' acquaintances, enabling identity theft and financial fraud. Residents are urged to be vigilant against such AI-driven scams.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`
