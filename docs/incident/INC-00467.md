### INC-00467  [↗](/incident/INC-00467.html)

**AI-Generated Fake Sequels Sold Without Author's Consent on Kindle**  
_2026-04-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

Unauthorized sequels to Ayatsuji Yukito's novel "The Decagon House Murders" were created and sold on Amazon Kindle using generative AI, falsely attributed to other authors. The original authors and publishers are taking action against this intellectual property violation, which also included AI-generated cover art mimicking existing artists.
