**AI Face-Swapping Alters Fan Bingbing's Film Role, Damaging Career and Image**  
_2020-12-11 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI technology was used to replace actress Fan Bingbing's face in the film 'Legend of Ravaging Dynasties 2,' erasing her on-screen presence while retaining her voice. This AI-driven alteration harmed her professional reputation and career, highlighting risks of AI misuse in media and personal rights violations.
