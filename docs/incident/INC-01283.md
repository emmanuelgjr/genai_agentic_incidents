### INC-01283  [↗](/incident/INC-01283.html)

**Kimi AI Model Leaks User Resume Data, Causing Privacy Breach in China**  
_2026-04-21 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Kimi large language model, developed by Moonshot AI, mistakenly disclosed a user's private resume—including name, phone, and work history—to another user during a routine task. The leaked data was verified as authentic, raising serious concerns about AI data isolation and privacy protection. Legal action is underway.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`
