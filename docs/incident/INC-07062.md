**UN Disarmament Chief Warns of AI Military Risks**  
_2020-02-24 · real-world · Severity: High_

UN disarmament chief Izumi Nakamitsu warns that AI technologies, such as autonomous weapons and AI-enabled hacking, pose significant military risks, including loss of human control and potential nuclear security breaches. She urges governments to strengthen measures to prevent harmful military applications, highlighting the urgent need for global oversight.
