**UK Government Warns of Escalating AI-Driven Cyberattacks**  
_2026-04-15 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The UK government and its AI Security Institute have warned that advanced AI models, such as Anthropic's Mythos, are enabling criminals to autonomously conduct complex cyberattacks at unprecedented speed and scale. These AI-driven attacks have already caused financial and operational harm to businesses, prompting urgent calls for enhanced cybersecurity measures.
