**OpenDream AI art generator accused of generating child sex images**  
_2023-12 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

AI art generator OpenDream was reported to allow generation of sexualized images of minors despite stated content policy, drawing pressure from child-safety groups and contributing to broader scrutiny of open-source SD-based tools.

**Affected:** Children depicted
