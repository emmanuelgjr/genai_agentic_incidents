**Metropolitan Police Use Palantir AI to Flag Officer Misconduct Raises Rights Concerns**  
_2026-02-20 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The Metropolitan Police in the UK are using Palantir's AI tools to analyze internal data and flag potential officer misconduct. The Police Federation criticizes this as "automated suspicion," warning that opaque, untested AI could misinterpret data and violate officers' labor and human rights.
