**Anthropic's AI Claude Used in US Military Operation Sparks Ethical Standoff**  
_2026-03-15 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Anthropic's AI system, Claude, was used by the US military during an operation in Venezuela, leading to the capture of a political leader. The Pentagon demanded unrestricted access to the AI, but Anthropic refused, citing ethical limits. This incident highlights governance gaps and risks of harm from military AI use.
