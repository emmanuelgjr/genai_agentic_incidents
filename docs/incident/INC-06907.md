**Secretive Palantir NHS Data Deal Sparks Privacy and Legal Concerns**  
_2020-12-21 · real-world · Severity: High_

The UK government secretly extended a £23 million contract with Palantir, an AI-driven data analytics firm, to manage NHS health data beyond the COVID-19 crisis. The lack of transparency, public consultation, and legal safeguards has raised serious concerns about privacy violations and potential breaches of citizens' rights.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`
