### INC-01548  [↗](/incident/INC-01548.html)

**NZ News Hub Reportedly Used AI-Rewritten News Posts and Synthetic Images to Mislead New Zealand Facebook Users**  
_2026-02-05 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1403`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1403/)

At least 10 Facebook pages were identified taking existing New Zealand news stories, running them through artificial intelligence to rewrite them, and publishing them with synthetic images. One page called 'NZ News Hub' with over 4,700 followers was analyzed, showing 209 posts in January 2026 with no original reporting. None of the AI-generated images were labeled as synthetic, and some featured fabricated photos of real people, including a manipulated image of a minor killed in the Mount Maunganui landslide shown dancing, and edited photos of parents who lost their daughter to suicide. Natural disasters were consistently dramatized beyond reality, with authentic highway slips depicted as more destructive, and a grounded tourist boat edited to appear packed with more passengers. Police were often shown in incorrect British or American uniforms with guns drawn when no indication existed that officers were armed. Some posts accidentally included raw AI prompts like 'Here's a news-style rewrite with a clear headline, emojis, and top hashtags.' Individual posts regularly attracted over 1,000 likes and comments, with many users criticizing the fake images and blaming 'the media' for fake news, though these pages had no connection to legitimate news organizations. Google Image searches revealed several pictures contained 'SynthID' digital watermarks indicating they were created using Google's AI image-generation tools.
