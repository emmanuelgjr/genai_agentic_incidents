**AI-Generated Misinformation Causes Social Harm and Legal Action in China**  
_2026-05-20 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

In China, AI-generated fake news and images have been used to spread misinformation about disasters, including earthquakes and floods, leading to public confusion, panic, and disruption of social order. Authorities have taken legal action against individuals who used AI tools to fabricate and disseminate false information online.
