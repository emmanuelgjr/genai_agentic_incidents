### INC-06233  [↗](/incident/INC-06233.html)

**AI Moderation on Facebook and Twitter Struggles to Contain COVID-19 Misinformation**  
_2020-05-12 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Facebook and Twitter deployed AI systems to detect and flag COVID-19 misinformation, labeling millions of posts. However, limitations in AI effectiveness allowed significant amounts of harmful misinformation to persist, indirectly contributing to public health risks by failing to fully contain the spread of false information.
