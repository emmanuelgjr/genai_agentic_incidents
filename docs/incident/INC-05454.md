### INC-05454  [↗](/incident/INC-05454.html)

**Amazon Faces Legal Action Over Alexa's Unauthorized Voice Recordings**  
_2021-06-01 · real-world · Severity: High_

Amazon is facing over 75,000 complaints alleging its Alexa AI assistant illegally recorded and stored users' conversations without consent, violating privacy rights. In response, Amazon changed its terms of service to allow class-action lawsuits instead of mandatory arbitration, enabling customers to sue the company in court.
