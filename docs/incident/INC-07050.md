**UK Ministry of Defence Funds AI Projects for Future Warships**  
_2020-01-14 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The UK Ministry of Defence, through its Defence and Security Accelerator (DASA), has awarded £1 million to nine AI research projects aimed at helping warship crews manage information overload. The initiative seeks to revolutionize decision-making and human-AI collaboration on military platforms by the 2040s, raising potential future risks.
