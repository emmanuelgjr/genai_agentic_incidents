### INC-07938  [↗](/incident/INC-07938.html)

**AI-Assisted Plagiarism in Tower of Saviors Promo Video Sparks Backlash**  
_2026-05-27 · real-world · Severity: High_

The game Tower of Saviors used AI-assisted tools to create a promotional video that closely copied animation from the game Wuthering Waves, leading to accusations of plagiarism and intellectual property violation. The developer admitted to using AI, apologized, withdrew the video, and pledged stricter review processes.

**Attack vector:** `other`
