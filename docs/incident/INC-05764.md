### INC-05764  [↗](/incident/INC-05764.html)

**Microsoft Contracts to Supply AI-Enabled AR Headsets to U.S. Army**  
_2021-03-31 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Microsoft secured a $22 billion contract to provide over 120,000 HoloLens-based augmented reality headsets with AI capabilities to the U.S. Army. The IVAS system aims to enhance soldiers' situational awareness and decision-making, but its large-scale military deployment raises credible risks of harm if the technology malfunctions or is misused.
