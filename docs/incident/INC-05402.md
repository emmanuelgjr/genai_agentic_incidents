**AI-Driven Pre-Crime Surveillance Program Raises Civil Rights Concerns in US**  
_2021-05-22 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The Biden Administration's Department of Homeland Security has launched a pre-crime surveillance program, the Center for Prevention Programs and Partnerships (CP3), using AI-driven behavioral threat assessment and automated monitoring of communications. Critics warn this could lead to violations of civil rights and privacy through mass surveillance and profiling.
