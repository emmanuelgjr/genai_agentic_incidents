**US Senators Criticize Trump Administration Over AI Chip Export Loophole to Chinese Firms**  
_2026-06-01 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

US Senators Elizabeth Warren and Andy Kim criticized the Trump administration for failing to update export controls, potentially allowing advanced American AI chips to be exported to overseas subsidiaries of Chinese firms. This regulatory gap raised concerns about boosting China's military capabilities. The Commerce Department has since moved to close the loophole.
