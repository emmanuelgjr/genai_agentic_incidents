### INC-06719  [↗](/incident/INC-06719.html)

**Instagram's AI Anti-Spam System Wrongly Blocks Black Lives Matter Posts**  
_2020-06-01 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Instagram's automated anti-spam system mistakenly blocked users from posting or sharing content with the #blacklivesmatter hashtag during a surge in related activity. The AI system, designed to detect spam, incorrectly flagged legitimate posts, restricting communication about the movement and prompting Instagram to address the malfunction.

**Attack vector:** `other`
