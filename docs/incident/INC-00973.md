### INC-00973  [↗](/incident/INC-00973.html)

**Experimental Deployment of AI-Driven Autonomous Taxis Planned in Budapest**  
_2026-01-23 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Hungarian officials announced plans to launch experimental level-4 autonomous taxis in Budapest, using Mobileye's AI-based technology. The vehicles, integrated into Főtaxi and Uber fleets, will be tested in real urban environments. While no incidents have occurred, the deployment raises potential future risks due to regulatory and safety uncertainties.
