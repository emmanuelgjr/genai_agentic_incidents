**Draganfly Acquires AI-Enabled Autonomous Drone Technology for Defense Applications**  
_2026-05-18 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Draganfly Inc. has agreed to acquire Skip Dynamix's ultra-low-cost, mass-producible fixed-wing drone technology, which features AI-enabled autonomy for surveillance, electronic warfare, and one-way missions. The acquisition expands Draganfly's defense portfolio, raising concerns about future risks from deploying autonomous AI systems in military operations.
