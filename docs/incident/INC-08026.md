### INC-08026  [↗](/incident/INC-08026.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfake Scam Targets French TV Host Michel Drucker**  
_2026-06-01 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Scammers used AI to create deepfake images and voice of French TV host Michel Drucker to promote and sell a fake joint pain medication online. Drucker publicly warned his followers about the fraudulent use of his identity, highlighting the growing misuse of AI for celebrity impersonation and scams in France.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0058`
