**Tesla Autopilot Prevents Collision with Wild Boar in Belgium**  
_2020-06-10 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A Belgian baker narrowly avoided a collision with a wild boar when his Tesla's Autopilot system intervened, taking control to steer the car away from the animal. The incident, captured on the vehicle's cameras, went viral online and drew praise from Tesla CEO Elon Musk for the AI system's safety capabilities.
