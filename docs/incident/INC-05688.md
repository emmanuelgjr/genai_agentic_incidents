### INC-05688  [↗](/incident/INC-05688.html)

**Hanson Robotics Plans Mass Deployment of AI Humanoid Robots in Healthcare and Education**  
_2021-01-25 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Hanson Robotics, a Hong Kong-based company, announced plans to mass-produce AI-powered humanoid robots, including models like Sophia and Grace, to replace human workers in healthcare and education. The rollout aims to address pandemic-related needs but raises concerns about potential job displacement and social disruption.
