### INC-06435  [↗](/incident/INC-06435.html)

**China's AI Surveillance System Leads to Mass Detention of Uyghur Muslims**  
_2020-12-09 · real-world · Severity: High_

Human Rights Watch revealed that Chinese authorities used an AI-powered platform, the Integrated Joint Operations Platform, to analyze surveillance data and flag Uyghur Muslims in Xinjiang for 'suspicious' but legal behaviors, resulting in the arbitrary detention of thousands and widespread human rights violations between 2016 and 2018.

**Attack vector:** `other`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0058`
