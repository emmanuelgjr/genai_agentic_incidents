### INC-00193  [↗](/incident/INC-00193.html)

**AI Pilot Program Assists Judges in Los Angeles County Courts**  
_2026-03-18 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Los Angeles County civil court judges are piloting the AI software Learned Hand to help distill legal motions and draft tentative rulings. While the AI aids judicial tasks under human oversight, concerns about potential errors and bias highlight risks to public trust, though no harm has occurred yet.
