### INC-01814  [↗](/incident/INC-01814.html)

**Students Use AI to Create and Circulate Nude Images of Classmates and Teachers in Crete**  
_2026-03-16 · real-world · Severity: High_

In Heraklion, Crete, secondary school students used AI platforms to manipulate photos of female classmates and teachers into nude images without consent. The images were circulated among students, reaching the victims and causing psychological harm. Two students confessed, and educators are addressing the incident pedagogically.
