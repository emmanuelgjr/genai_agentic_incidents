**US Scrutinizes TikTok-Oracle Partnership Over AI Algorithm Control and Security Risks**  
_2020-09-14 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

US officials, including President Trump and Senator Rubio, raised concerns over ByteDance retaining control of TikTok's AI-driven algorithm in a proposed partnership with Oracle. Fears center on potential misuse of user data and foreign influence, prompting regulatory review to address national security risks, though no actual AI-related harm has occurred.
