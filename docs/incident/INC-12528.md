### INC-12528  [↗](/incident/INC-12528.html)

**Prototype Pollution in undefsafe**  
_2022-02 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: Medium_

CVEs: `CVE-2019-10795`
CWEs: `CWE-74`
CVSS: **6.3** — `CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:L/UI:N/S:U/C:L/I:L/A:L`

undefsafe before 2.0.3 is vulnerable to Prototype Pollution. The 'a' function could be tricked into adding or modifying properties of Object.prototype using a `__proto__` payload.

**Affected:** npm/undefsafe  
**Attack vector:** `other`
