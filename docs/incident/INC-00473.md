### INC-00473  [↗](/incident/INC-00473.html)

**AI-Generated Film Poster Sparks Outrage Among Sikh Community in Mumbai**  
_2026-03-22 · real-world · Severity: High_

An AI-generated poster for the film Dhurandhar: The Revenge, depicting Ranveer Singh in Sikh attire holding a cigarette, sparked outrage among the Sikh community in Mumbai. Complaints were filed with police, alleging the poster and film scenes disrespect Sikh religious sentiments, leading to community backlash and formal investigations.
