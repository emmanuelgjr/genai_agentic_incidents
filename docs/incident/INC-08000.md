**VinFast, NVIDIA, and Autobrains Partner to Develop Level 4 Autonomous Robotaxi for Southeast Asia**  
_2026-05-31 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

VinFast, NVIDIA, and Autobrains announced a strategic partnership to develop level 4 autonomous robotaxi for Southeast Asia, using NVIDIA DRIVE Hyperion 10 and Agentic AI software. The project targets complex urban traffic environments, presenting plausible future risks associated with autonomous vehicle deployment, but no harm has occurred yet.
