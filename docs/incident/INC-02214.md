### INC-02214  [↗](/incident/INC-02214.html)

**AI Voice Scam Allegedly Defrauds Game and Coffee Store in Havre, Montana**  
_2025-02-04 · real-world · Severity: Medium_
AIID: [`#922`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/922/)

A Montana business, Sugar and Dice, was reportedly targeted by an AI voice scam that cloned the store owner&#x27;s voice to deceive an employee over the phone. The scammer allegedly spoofed both the owner&#x27;s and the employee&#x27;s phone numbers, making the call appear legitimate. Believing he was following his employer’s instructions, the employee unknowingly compromised financial details. The store suffered monetary losses but plans to recover.
