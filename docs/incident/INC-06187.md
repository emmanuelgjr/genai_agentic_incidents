### INC-06187  [↗](/incident/INC-06187.html)

**Widespread Misuse of Facial Recognition AI Leads to Privacy Violations in China**  
_2021-01-25 · real-world · Severity: High_

Multiple investigations reveal that facial recognition AI is widely misused in China, with many apps and public venues forcing its use without proper user consent or alternatives. This has resulted in privacy breaches, unauthorized data exposure, and black market sales of facial data, raising significant concerns over user rights and information security.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
