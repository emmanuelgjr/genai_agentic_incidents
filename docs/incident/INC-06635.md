**German Parliament Approves Procurement of Armed AI-Enabled Drones for Bundeswehr**  
_2020-10-05 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

The German Bundestag's defense committee approved the procurement and planned armament of Heron TP drones, which use AI for navigation and targeting. The move raises ethical and legal concerns about the automation of warfare and potential future harm, but no actual incidents have yet occurred.
