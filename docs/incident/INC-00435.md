### INC-00435  [↗](/incident/INC-00435.html)

**AI-Generated Disinformation Threatens Local Elections in Hesse**  
_2026-02-19 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Hesse state intelligence agency warns of increasing use of AI-generated deepfakes and fake news on social media to manipulate public opinion during local election campaigns. Authorities highlight the rapid spread of misleading content, which undermines democratic trust and poses particular risks to smaller communities with limited resources.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
