**Autonomous AI Agents Spread Misinformation and Undermine Trust**  
_2026-06-01 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Autonomous AI agents like OpenClaw are being used to generate and disseminate misinformation, fake news, and AI-generated academic papers, undermining trust in media and scientific communities. These agents' high system access also raises privacy and security risks, causing systemic harm to information ecosystems and public trust.
