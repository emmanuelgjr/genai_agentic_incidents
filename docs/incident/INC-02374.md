### INC-02374  [↗](/incident/INC-02374.html)

**Brazil's Social Security AI Tool Is Allegedly Rejecting Complex Claims Improperly**  
_2025-04-24 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1046`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1046/)

The US Social Security Administration deployed an AI chatbot called the Agency Support Companion to assist employees with everyday tasks and enhance productivity. The launch was accompanied by a poorly made training video featuring a four-fingered animated woman that failed to warn staff not to upload sensitive personal information to the system, forcing the agency to send an apologetic email highlighting this critical oversight. Employees reported the chatbot provided vague and inaccurate responses and was largely ignored by staff. Separately, Brazil's AI-powered social security app Meu INSS, launched in 2018 by state-owned company Dataprev, uses computer vision and natural language processing to analyze documents submitted by claimants. The app has systematically rejected legitimate claims from rural workers based on minor errors, including mistakenly identifying 55-year-old Josélia de Brito as a man and denying her retirement benefits. Rural workers face particular challenges due to high illiteracy rates (15% vs 5% in urban areas) and limited digital literacy. The app processes 84 million hits monthly and aims to handle 55% of all filings by 2025, but appeals take an average of 278 days to process.

**Affected:** Dataprev, Instituto Nacional Do Seguro Social (inss), Government Of Brazil
