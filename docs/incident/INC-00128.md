### INC-00128  [↗](/incident/INC-00128.html)

**AI Chatbots Spread False Medical Information After Experiment With Fabricated Disease**  
_2026-04-09 · real-world · Severity: High_

Researchers at Sweden's Gothenburg University created a fictitious eye disease, 'bixonimania,' and published fake papers online. Major AI chatbots, including ChatGPT, Gemini, and Microsoft Copilot, accepted and propagated this false medical information, misleading users and highlighting AI vulnerabilities in filtering and verifying health data.
