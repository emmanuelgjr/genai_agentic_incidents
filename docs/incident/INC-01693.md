### INC-01693  [↗](/incident/INC-01693.html)

**Purported AI-Generated Impersonations of Albanian Cardiologist Spiro Qirko and Journalist Ilir Topi Were Reportedly Used on Facebook to Promote Hypertension Product in Kosovo**  
_2026-03-25 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1452`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1452/)

An allegedly AI-generated video circulating on Facebook appeared to impersonate Albanian cardiologist Spiro Qirko and journalist Ilir Topi in order to market &quot;Hyper Caps&quot; as a treatment for hypertension in Kosovo. Reporting indicated that both men denied involvement. An AI-detection tool reportedly assessed the media as likely AI-generated, and the product was reportedly not listed as holding marketing authorization in Kosovo.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
