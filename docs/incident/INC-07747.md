### INC-07747  [↗](/incident/INC-07747.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfake Audio of Thai PM Sparks Misinformation on Border Policy**  
_2026-05-14 · real-world · Severity: High_

An AI-generated audio clip falsely depicting Thai Prime Minister Anutin Charnvirakul promising to reopen the Cambodia border circulated widely online, misleading the public amid ongoing border tensions. The prime minister and analysts confirmed the clip was a deepfake, highlighting the risks of AI-driven misinformation in sensitive political contexts.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`
