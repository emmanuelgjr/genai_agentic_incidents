**Cloudflare CEO Predicts AI Bots Will Surpass Human Web Traffic by 2027**  
_2026-03-19 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Cloudflare CEO Matthew Prince warns that AI bot traffic, driven by generative AI agents, could exceed human web traffic by 2027. This surge may disrupt internet infrastructure and business models, as bots visit far more sites than humans, potentially reshaping online search and monetization strategies.
