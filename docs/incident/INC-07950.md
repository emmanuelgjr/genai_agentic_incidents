**Tesla Faces Lawsuits and Investigations Over FSD AI Safety and Misleading Claims**  
_2026-05-27 · real-world · Severity: High_

Tesla's Full Self-Driving (FSD) AI system is under scrutiny after former employees revealed failures to detect obstacles, resulting in animal collisions and near-misses with children. In China, car owners filed a collective lawsuit alleging Tesla misled consumers about FSD's capabilities, causing financial harm. U.S. authorities are also investigating related accidents.
