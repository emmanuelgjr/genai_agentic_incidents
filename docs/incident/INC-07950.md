**AI System ChatCPR Outperforms 911 Dispatchers in CPR Guidance**  
_2026-05-18 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Researchers from UC San Diego, University of Pittsburgh, and Johns Hopkins developed ChatCPR, an AI-powered CPR coaching agent. In studies, ChatCPR provided more accurate and guideline-compliant CPR instructions than human 911 dispatchers, demonstrating potential to improve emergency cardiac arrest outcomes. The system is open-source and tested on real emergency call data.
