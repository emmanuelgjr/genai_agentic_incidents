### INC-01127  [↗](/incident/INC-01127.html)

**Google's AI Search Feature Causes Major Traffic and Revenue Loss for News Publishers in Turkey**  
_2026-03-09 · real-world · Severity: High_

Google's AI-powered search feature, now active in Turkey, generates direct answers to user queries, significantly reducing traffic to news sites and blogs. This has led to substantial economic harm for media publishers, threatening their sustainability and raising concerns over information access and intellectual property rights.
