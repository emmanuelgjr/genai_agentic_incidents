### INC-05666  [↗](/incident/INC-05666.html)

**Google Alters Search Algorithm to Combat Defamation and Extortion Sites**  
_2021-06-10 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Google's AI-driven search algorithm previously surfaced defamatory and unverified content about individuals, enabling extortion and reputational harm. In response, Google is changing its algorithm to suppress such sites in search results and has introduced a system to protect known victims, aiming to reduce these AI-enabled harms.

**Attack vector:** `misinformation`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MEASURE-2.8`
