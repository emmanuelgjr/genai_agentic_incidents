### INC-00711  [↗](/incident/INC-00711.html)

**Brazilian Senate Approves AI Monitoring to Protect Domestic Violence Victims**  
_2026-03-11 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

The Brazilian Senate approved a bill establishing a national program using AI to monitor aggressors and protect domestic violence victims. The system will employ real-time tracking, biometric analysis, and machine learning to identify risks and alert authorities, aiming to prevent harm. The bill now moves to the Chamber of Deputies.
