### INC-00763  [↗](/incident/INC-00763.html)

**China Approves and Advances AI-Powered Brain Implants for Commercial Use**  
_2026-03-30 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

China has become the first country to approve invasive AI-powered brain-computer interface implants for commercial use, with devices like Neuracle's BMI and NeuCyber's Beinao-1 and Beinao-2. These AI systems interpret neural signals to control external devices, presenting potential future health and safety risks as deployment scales up.

**Attack vector:** `other`
