### INC-03069  [↗](/incident/INC-03069.html)

**Purported AI-Generated Voice Reportedly Impersonated Washington Man&#x27;s Daughter in $13,000 Extortion Scam**  
_2025 · real-world · Severity: Medium_
AIID: [`#1482`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1482/)

A man in Washington state, Mark A. Young, reportedly wired $13,000 after scammers used a purportedly AI-generated copy of his daughter&#x27;s voice to convince him she had been kidnapped after a staged car-crash story. The caller allegedly kept him on the phone for about 30 hours, directed him to banks and money-transfer locations, and sought another $17,000 before a Pullman bank manager and police confirmed his daughter was safe.
