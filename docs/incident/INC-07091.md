**US Military Plans AI-Controlled Command Systems Raise Risk of Catastrophic Harm**  
_2020-08-25 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Multiple articles report that the US military is rapidly developing AI systems to autonomously command combat operations, potentially replacing human generals. Experts warn these AI-driven command and control systems could misinterpret data or escalate conflicts, including nuclear war, posing significant future risks of large-scale harm.
