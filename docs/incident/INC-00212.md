### INC-00212  [↗](/incident/INC-00212.html)

**AI Swarms Undermine Democratic Discourse Through Coordinated Disinformation**  
_2026-01-23 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Researchers report that coordinated AI swarms, combining large language models and multi-agent systems, are already being used to spread disinformation and simulate public consensus online. These AI-driven personas infiltrate groups, manipulate opinions, and threaten democratic discourse by creating the illusion of widespread agreement, causing harm to communities.

**Attack vector:** `misinformation`
