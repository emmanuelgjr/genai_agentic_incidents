**AI License Plate Readers Cause Privacy Violations and False Accusations**  
_2026-05-24 · real-world · Severity: High_

Flock Safety's AI-powered license plate readers, used by police across several U.S. cities, have led to privacy violations, unauthorized data sharing, and at least one false criminal accusation. Concerns over misuse, lack of oversight, and data access by federal authorities have prompted some cities to suspend or cancel contracts with Flock.
