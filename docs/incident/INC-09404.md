**LibreNMS has a Stored XSS in Alert Rule**  
_2026-02 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: Medium_

CVEs: `CVE-2026-26989`, `CVE-2026-27016`
CWEs: `CWE-116`, `CWE-79`
CVSS: **4.3** — `CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:H/UI:R/S:U/C:L/I:L/A:L`
