### INC-00391  [↗](/incident/INC-00391.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfake Video Causes Misinformation and Reputational Harm to Indonesian Actor**  
_2026-03-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

An AI-generated deepfake video falsely depicted Indonesian actor Ari Wibowo marrying Clara Oktavia, leading to widespread misinformation and reputational harm. Ari Wibowo publicly clarified the hoax, expressing concern over the increasing misuse of AI for creating fake news and misleading the public.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
