### INC-11864  [↗](/incident/INC-11864.html)

**Use of Hard-coded Credentials in Nacos**  
_2022-07 · vulnerability-disclosure · Severity: High_

CVEs: `CVE-2021-43116`
CWEs: `CWE-287`, `CWE-798`
CVSS: **8.8** — `CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:L/UI:N/S:U/C:H/I:H/A:H`

An Access Control vulnerability exists in Nacos 2.0.3 in the access prompt page; enter username and password, click on login to capture packets and then change the returned package, which lets a malicious user login.

**Affected:** maven/com.alibaba.nacos:nacos-client  
**Attack vector:** `auth-bypass`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM03`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`, `ASI04`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `GOVERN-6.1`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0010`, `AML.T0012`
