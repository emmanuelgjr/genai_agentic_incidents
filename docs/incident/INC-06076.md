**Tesla Autopilot Fails to Prevent Crash with Parked Police Car in Washington**  
_2021-05-17 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A Tesla Model S operating on autopilot crashed into a parked Snohomish County deputy's patrol car in Arlington, Washington, causing significant property damage but no injuries. The incident highlights the risks of overreliance on Tesla's AI-driven autopilot system, which failed to detect and avoid the emergency vehicle.
