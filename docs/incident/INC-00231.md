**AI Systems Used for Target Selection and Attacks in Global Conflicts**  
_2026-02-28 · real-world · Severity: High_

AI technologies are being deployed in military conflicts, including in Gaza and Ukraine, for target identification and attack support. These systems, such as Anthropic's Claude, assist armed forces and intelligence agencies, raising concerns about collateral damage, human rights violations, and the ethical implications of AI-driven warfare.
