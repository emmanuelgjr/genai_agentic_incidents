### INC-00427  [↗](/incident/INC-00427.html)

**AI-Generated Disinformation Becomes Routine, Undermining Public Trust**  
_2026-05-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

European media watchdogs report a sharp rise in AI-generated disinformation, including deepfakes and manipulated content, now integrated into daily news flows. These AI tools are increasingly used to spread false narratives and discredit authentic evidence, causing widespread confusion and harm to public perception and trust.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`, `AML.T0050`, `AML.T0058`
