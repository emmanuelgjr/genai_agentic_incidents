### INC-07097  [↗](/incident/INC-07097.html)

**US Officials Warn of AI-Driven Security Risks from TikTok, Propose Government Ban**  
_2020-03-05 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

US officials and lawmakers raised concerns about TikTok's AI-driven data collection and potential exploitation by Chinese intelligence, prompting a proposed ban on the app for government devices. The warnings highlight risks of misuse of TikTok’s AI systems but report no actual incident of harm.
