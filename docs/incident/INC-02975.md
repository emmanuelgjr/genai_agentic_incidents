**NYPD Facial Recognition System Allegedly Produced Erroneous Match That Reportedly Resulted in Wrongful Detention of Trevis Williams**  
_2025-04-21 · real-world · Severity: High_
AIID: [`#1191`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1191/)

The NYPD&#x27;s facial recognition system allegedly misidentified Trevis Williams as a suspect in a Union Square indecent exposure case. Despite reportedly notable physical differences and exculpatory phone data, Williams was arrested, jailed for more than two days, and charged. The case was later dismissed.
