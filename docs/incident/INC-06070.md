### INC-06070  [↗](/incident/INC-06070.html)

**Tesla AI Sensor Malfunctions Cause Phantom Pedestrian and Vehicle Detections**  
_2021-02-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

Multiple Tesla drivers reported that their vehicles' AI-powered sensors falsely detected pedestrians and vehicles in empty environments, such as graveyards and tunnels. These malfunctions, attributed to sensor or algorithm errors, did not cause harm but raise safety concerns about potential risks if the vehicle or driver reacts to nonexistent obstacles.
