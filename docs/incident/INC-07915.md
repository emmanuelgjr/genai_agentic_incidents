### INC-07915  [↗](/incident/INC-07915.html)

**AI-Generated Deepfake Video of Cazzu and Sebastián Rulli Causes Public Confusion**  
_2026-05-26 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

An AI-generated deepfake video falsely depicting Argentine singer Cazzu and actor Sebastián Rulli in a romantic context went viral on social media, leading to widespread misinformation and speculation. Both artists denied involvement, highlighting concerns over reputational harm and the misuse of AI for creating deceptive content.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`
