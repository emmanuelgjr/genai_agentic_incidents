**Tesla Autopilot Prevents Fatalities During Storm Dennis by Emergency Braking**  
_2020-02-18 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

During Storm Dennis in the UK, Tesla's Autopilot AI system activated emergency braking in two Model X vehicles, preventing a 400-year-old tree from crushing eight occupants. The AI's timely intervention avoided serious injury or death, demonstrating the system's direct role in safeguarding passengers during a hazardous event.
