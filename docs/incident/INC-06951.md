### INC-06951  [↗](/incident/INC-06951.html)

**Tencent Disables QQ Group Recommendations After AI System Exposes Minors to Harmful Content**  
_2020-04-11 · real-world · Severity: High_

Tencent's QQ platform used AI-driven group recommendation algorithms that matched educational keyword searches with inappropriate groups, exposing minors to scams and harmful content. Following media reports and public concern, Tencent disabled these recommendation features and pledged to strengthen protections for minors online.

**Attack vector:** `other`  

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`
