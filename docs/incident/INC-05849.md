### INC-05849  [↗](/incident/INC-05849.html)

**Singapore Police Granted Access to COVID-19 Contact Tracing App Data, Sparking Privacy Concerns**  
_2021-01-04 · real-world · Severity: High_

Singapore's government reversed its privacy stance on the TraceTogether contact tracing app, allowing police to access its centrally stored data for criminal investigations. This repurposing of AI-collected data, originally intended for pandemic control, has raised significant privacy and human rights concerns among the public.

**Attack vector:** `other`
