### INC-00505  [↗](/incident/INC-00505.html)

**AI-Generated Slopoly Malware Used in Hive0163 Ransomware Attacks**  
_2026-03-13 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Cybercrime group Hive0163 deployed AI-generated malware, Slopoly, in ransomware attacks during early 2026. Developed with large language models, Slopoly enabled persistent unauthorized access and data theft, demonstrating how AI accelerates malware creation and amplifies harm in extortion campaigns. IBM X-Force researchers uncovered the incident.
