**Bot Auto Completes First Fully Humanless Commercial Truck Delivery in Texas**  
_2026-04-30 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Bot Auto, an autonomous trucking startup, successfully completed the first fully humanless commercial truck delivery in Texas, transporting freight 230 miles without a safety driver, remote operator, or in-cab observer. The AI-driven truck operated independently, marking a milestone in commercial autonomous vehicle deployment.
