### INC-06692  [↗](/incident/INC-06692.html)

**IMF Proposes Using AI to Analyze Web History for Credit Scoring**  
_2020-12-19 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

IMF researchers suggest banks could use AI to analyze individuals' web search, browsing, and purchase histories to determine credit scores. While this could expand credit access, it raises significant privacy and fairness concerns, as no actual implementation or harm has yet occurred.
