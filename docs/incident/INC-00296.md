**AI-Driven Electricity Demand Strains Power Infrastructure and Fuels Environmental Concerns**  
_2026-02-24 · real-world · Severity: High_

The rapid expansion of AI technologies is causing a significant surge in global electricity demand, straining power infrastructure and leading to shortages of gas turbines. This may force increased reliance on coal, raising environmental concerns and potentially slowing AI growth due to energy constraints.
