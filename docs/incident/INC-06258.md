### INC-06258  [↗](/incident/INC-06258.html)

**AI Systems Cause Job Losses and Data Breaches, Raise Privacy Concerns**  
_2020-02-27 · real-world · Severity: High_

Amazon's AI system autonomously fired over 900 warehouse workers for low productivity, highlighting job loss risks from workplace automation. Separately, facial recognition firm Clearview AI suffered a major data breach, exposing sensitive client information and sparking privacy lawsuits and investigations. These incidents underscore the harms and risks associated with AI deployment in employment and data management.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`
