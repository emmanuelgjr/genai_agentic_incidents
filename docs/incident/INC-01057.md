**G7 Shares Concerns Over AI-Enabled Cyberattack Risks to Financial Systems**  
_2026-05-11 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

At a meeting in Paris, G7 finance ministers and central bank governors plan to discuss concerns about advanced AI systems, specifically Anthropic's Claude Mutos, which can identify vulnerabilities in financial infrastructure. The group aims to coordinate responses to prevent potential cyberattacks and financial market disruptions enabled by such AI technologies.
