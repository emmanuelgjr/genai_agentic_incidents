### INC-06714  [↗](/incident/INC-06714.html)

**Innocent Man Wrongly Targeted by AI-Driven Geofence Warrant**  
_2020-03-04 · real-world · Severity: High_

Florida police used Google's AI-powered geofence warrant system to collect location data, wrongly identifying Zachary McCoy as a burglary suspect because his bike route passed the crime scene. The AI system's data sharing led to legal and emotional harm for the innocent man, highlighting risks of AI-driven surveillance.
