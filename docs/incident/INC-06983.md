### INC-06983  [↗](/incident/INC-06983.html)

**Tesla's AI Cameras in Cars Spark Data Privacy Violations in Germany**  
_2020-09-17 · real-world · Severity: High_

Tesla vehicles in Germany use AI-driven cameras to continuously record interior and exterior scenes, storing personal data without user control or transparency. Data is reportedly transferred to US servers and used for business purposes, prompting data protection authorities to warn of clear violations of privacy and German data protection laws.
