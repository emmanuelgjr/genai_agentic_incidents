**Noelle Martin Deepfaked Without Consent in AI-Generated Pornography**  
_2020-02-06 · real-world · Severity: Critical_
AIID: [`#771`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/771/)

In 2017, Noelle Martin discovered explicit deepfake videos online that used AI technology to superimpose her face onto pornographic scenes. This incident was a continuation of the abuse she had experienced since at least 2012, when she first found doctored still images of herself in similar contexts. Despite the initial lack of legal protections, her advocacy efforts were instrumental in making image-based abuse a criminal offense in Australia.
