**US Army Receives AI-Enabled Robotic Combat Vehicles, Raising Future Risk Concerns**  
_2020-11-12 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

QinetiQ and Pratt Miller Defense delivered the first Robotic Combat Vehicle-Light (RCV-L), an AI-enabled unmanned ground combat vehicle, to the US Army. While no harm has occurred, the deployment of autonomous, weaponized AI systems poses plausible future risks of injury or rights violations in military contexts.
