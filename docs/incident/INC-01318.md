### INC-01318  [↗](/incident/INC-01318.html)

**Lawyer Sanctioned for Submitting AI-Generated Fake Legal Precedents in Siracusa Court**  
_2026-03-17 · real-world · Severity: High_

A lawyer in Siracusa, Italy, was sanctioned after submitting four fabricated legal precedents generated by an AI system in a civil case. The court found the cited rulings did not exist, highlighting the risks of unverified AI-generated content in legal proceedings and resulting in a breach of professional conduct.
