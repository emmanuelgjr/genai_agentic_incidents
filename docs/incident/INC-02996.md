### INC-02996  [↗](/incident/INC-02996.html)

**OpenAI ChatGPT Models Reportedly Jailbroken to Provide Chemical, Biological, and Nuclear Weapons Instructions**  
_2025-10-10 · real-world · Severity: Critical_
AIID: [`#1238`](https://incidentdatabase.ai/cite/1238/)

An NBC News investigation found that OpenAI&#x27;s language models o4-mini, GPT-5-mini, oss-20b, and oss-120b could be jailbroken under normal usage conditions to bypass safety guardrails and generate detailed instructions for creating chemical, biological, and nuclear weapons. Using a publicly documented jailbreak prompt, reporters repeatedly elicited hazardous outputs such as steps to synthesize pathogens or maximize harm with chemical agents. The findings reportedly revealed significant real-world safeguard failures, prompting OpenAI to commit to further mitigation measures.

**Attack vector:** `jailbreak`
