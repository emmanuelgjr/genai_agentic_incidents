### INC-00649  [↗](/incident/INC-00649.html)

**Asian Regulators Heighten Cybersecurity Over Anthropic's Mythos AI Risks**  
_2026-04-20 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Regulators in Singapore, South Korea, and Australia are increasing scrutiny of financial institutions' cybersecurity due to concerns over Anthropic's AI model Mythos, which can identify previously undetected security flaws. Authorities are urging banks to strengthen defenses, though no actual harm has occurred yet.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI03`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `GOVERN-1.4`, `MEASURE-2.10`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0012`, `AML.T0057`
