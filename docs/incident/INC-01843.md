### INC-01843  [↗](/incident/INC-01843.html)

**Teen Uses ChatGPT for Childbirth, Newborn Abandoned in Argentina**  
_2026-04-13 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

In Ezpeleta, Argentina, a 15-year-old girl used ChatGPT to guide her through childbirth alone, without medical assistance. After the birth, her 16-year-old boyfriend abandoned the newborn at a train station, leading to emergency medical intervention. The AI's involvement in the unsupervised delivery contributed to health risks for both mother and child.

**Attack vector:** `other`
