### INC-06802  [↗](/incident/INC-06802.html)

**Motional and Lyft to Deploy Fully Driverless Robotaxi Services in US Cities**  
_2020-12-16 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Motional and Lyft plan to launch fully driverless robotaxi services in major US cities starting in 2023, following Nevada's approval for testing autonomous vehicles without safety drivers. The initiative marks a significant expansion of AI-powered transportation, raising potential safety and operational risks associated with large-scale autonomous vehicle deployment.
