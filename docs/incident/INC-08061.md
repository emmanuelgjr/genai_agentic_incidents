### INC-08061  [↗](/incident/INC-08061.html)

**Google Gemini's Hyperrealistic Avatar Feature Raises Deepfake Concerns**  
_2026-06-03 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Google has launched a Gemini AI feature allowing users to create hyperrealistic video avatars of themselves using the Omni model. Despite safeguards like SynthID watermarks and age restrictions, experts and users express concerns about potential misuse for deepfakes, misinformation, and identity abuse. No actual harm has been reported yet.
