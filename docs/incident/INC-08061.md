### INC-08061  [↗](/incident/INC-08061.html)

**Ukrainian Military Deploys Vepr AI-Powered Robotic Medevac**  
_2026-06-03 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Ukraine's Ministry of Defense has approved the domestically produced Vepr robotic medevac for combat use. The AI-enabled platform can autonomously evacuate two wounded soldiers, transport 350 kg of cargo, and perform various combat support tasks, enhancing frontline medical and logistical operations. No harm or malfunction has been reported.
