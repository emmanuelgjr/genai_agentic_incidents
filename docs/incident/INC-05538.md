### INC-05538  [↗](/incident/INC-05538.html)

**Chinese Consumer Authorities Expose AI-Driven Price Discrimination on Platforms**  
_2021-03-11 · real-world · Severity: High_

Chinese consumer protection authorities revealed that major internet platforms use opaque AI algorithms to discriminate against users, charging loyal customers higher prices and targeting vulnerable groups with low-quality or predatory ads. These practices violate consumer rights and fair competition, prompting calls for urgent algorithm regulation to ensure fairness and transparency.
