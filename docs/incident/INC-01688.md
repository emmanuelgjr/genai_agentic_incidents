**Punjab Government Partners with IIT Ropar to Deploy AI for Crime Control**  
_2026-04-16 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Punjab government has partnered with IIT Ropar to develop and deploy AI-driven systems for crime prevention and targeting organized crime. The initiative includes creating structured criminal databases, real-time tracking, and intelligence-led policing, aiming to dismantle gangster networks and enhance public safety in Punjab.
