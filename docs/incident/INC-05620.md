**Facebook Sued Over AI Moderation Failures Allowing Anti-Muslim Hate Speech**  
_2021-04-08 · real-world · Severity: High_

Civil rights group Muslim Advocates sued Facebook and its executives, alleging the company's AI-driven content moderation failed to remove pervasive anti-Muslim hate speech. Despite claims of effective AI moderation, the lawsuit argues these failures have led to real-world harm and violated consumer protection laws.
