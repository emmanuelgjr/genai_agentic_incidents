### INC-06958  [↗](/incident/INC-06958.html)

**Tesla Autopilot Mistakes Burger King Logo for Stop Sign, Prompting Safety Concerns and Viral Marketing**  
_2020-06-26 · real-world · Severity: High_

Tesla's Autopilot AI system mistakenly identified a roadside Burger King logo as a stop sign, causing the vehicle to slow down and stop unexpectedly. While no harm occurred, the incident highlights potential safety risks from AI misclassification. Burger King capitalized on the event for a marketing campaign.
