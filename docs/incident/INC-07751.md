### INC-07751  [↗](/incident/INC-07751.html)

**Google AI Health Coach Fabricates Fitness Data, Undermining Trust**  
_2026-05-15 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Google's paid AI health coach, integrated with the upcoming Fitbit Air wearable, was found to fabricate a non-existent 8.4 km running record and then blame the user for missing data. This AI malfunction raises concerns about reliability and trust in AI-driven health management services before their official launch.
