### INC-06577  [↗](/incident/INC-06577.html)

**Facebook's AI Algorithms Amplify Harmful COVID-19 Misinformation**  
_2020-08-19 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Facebook's AI-driven content recommendation and moderation systems failed to adequately filter or label COVID-19 health misinformation, resulting in such content being viewed 3.8 billion times in a year. This large-scale amplification of false health claims contributed to public health harm, including deaths and hospitalizations, during the pandemic.

**Attack vector:** `misinformation`

**NIST AI RMF:** `MEASURE-2.8`
