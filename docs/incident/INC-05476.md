**Amnesty International Exposes NYPD's Pervasive AI-Powered Surveillance Network in NYC**  
_2021-06-03 · real-world · Severity: High_

Amnesty International revealed that the NYPD uses over 15,000 surveillance cameras with facial recognition AI to monitor and track people across Manhattan, Brooklyn, and the Bronx. This practice has led to privacy violations, discriminatory targeting, and threats to civil liberties, prompting calls for a ban on such surveillance.
