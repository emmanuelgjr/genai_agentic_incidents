**Amazon's AI Algorithms Promote Extremist and Conspiratorial Content, Report Finds**  
_2021-04-29 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

A report by the Institute for Strategic Dialogue found that Amazon's AI-powered recommendation and search algorithms steer users toward books promoting conspiracy theories and extremism, including QAnon and vaccine misinformation. This algorithmic promotion of harmful content raises concerns about the role of AI in spreading misinformation and radicalization.
