**Chinese Consumer Association Exposes AI-Driven Algorithmic Harms in E-Commerce Platforms**  
_2021-01-07 · real-world · Severity: High_

The China Consumers Association criticized major online platforms for using AI and big data algorithms to harm consumers, including price discrimination, misleading recommendations, fake reviews, opaque rankings, and unfair game odds. These practices violate consumer rights and fair competition, prompting calls for stronger regulation and algorithmic transparency.
