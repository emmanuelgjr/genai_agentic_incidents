**Google Wins $179 Million in Self-Driving AI Trade Secrets Case Against Ex-Uber Executive**  
_2020-03-05 · real-world · Severity: High_

Anthony Levandowski, former head of Uber's self-driving unit, was ordered to pay Google $179 million for stealing trade secrets related to AI-based autonomous vehicle technology from Waymo. The misappropriated information was used to found a startup later acquired by Uber, resulting in significant legal and financial consequences.
