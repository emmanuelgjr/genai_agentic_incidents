**Global Push to Ban AI-Powered Killer Robots Intensifies Amid Regulatory Delays**  
_2020-08-10 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Human Rights Watch and allied groups are urging a global ban on fully autonomous weapons, or 'killer robots,' which use AI to select and engage targets without human control. Delays in UN discussions and growing international support highlight concerns over the potential for grave harm if such systems are deployed unregulated.
