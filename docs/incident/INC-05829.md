**Rights Groups Condemn AI-Driven Digital Surveillance of Immigrants by ICE**  
_2021-06-01 · real-world · Severity: High_

Human rights and advocacy groups criticize U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) for using AI-powered surveillance tools—such as facial recognition, GPS ankle monitors, and monitoring apps—on nearly 100,000 immigrants. Reports highlight emotional, physical, and rights-based harms, urging the Biden administration to end these 'digital prisons' and associated human rights violations.
