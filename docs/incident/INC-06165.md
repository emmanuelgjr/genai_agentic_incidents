**US Marine Corps Commissions AI-Enabled Autonomous Naval Weapons System**  
_2021-01-25 · real-world · Severity: Critical_

Metal Shark has been selected to develop the Long Range Unmanned Surface Vessel (LRUSV) System for the US Marine Corps. The AI-powered vessels will autonomously navigate, track, and engage targets with munitions, raising concerns about potential future risks from autonomous lethal military systems, though no harm has yet occurred.
