### INC-07874  [↗](/incident/INC-07874.html)

**Unauthorized AI-Generated Video Misrepresents Joaquim Barbosa as Presidential Candidate**  
_2026-05-23 · real-world · Severity: High_

The Democracia Cristã party in Brazil used AI to create and publish a synthetic video depicting Joaquim Barbosa as a presidential candidate without his consent. The deepfake video, which falsely attributes statements to Barbosa, triggered internal party conflict and reputational harm, highlighting the misuse of AI in political processes.

**Attack vector:** `deepfake`  

**OWASP LLM Top 10:** `LLM05`, `LLM09`  
**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI05`, `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`, `MEASURE-2.7`, `MEASURE-2.8`
