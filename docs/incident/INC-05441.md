### INC-05441  [↗](/incident/INC-05441.html)

**AI-Powered Surveillance Cameras Violate Privacy During COVID-19 Response**  
_2021-06-19 · real-world · Severity: High_

During the COVID-19 pandemic, many US buildings deployed AI-driven thermal cameras with facial recognition to monitor visitors' health and movements without consent. This widespread use of AI surveillance technology led to significant privacy violations and raised concerns about ongoing infringements of constitutional and human rights.

**Attack vector:** `privacy-violation`
