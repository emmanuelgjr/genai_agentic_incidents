**Korean Go Prodigy Banned for One Year After Using AI to Cheat in Competition**  
_2020-11-20 · real-world · Severity: High_

13-year-old Korean Go prodigy Kim Eun-ji was banned for one year by the Korea Baduk Association after admitting to using AI assistance to cheat during an official online match. The incident, confirmed by AI analysis, highlights growing concerns over AI-enabled cheating in professional Go competitions.
