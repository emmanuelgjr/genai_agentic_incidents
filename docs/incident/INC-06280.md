### INC-06280  [↗](/incident/INC-06280.html)

**AI-Driven Voice Phishing Enables Financial Fraud in Cyberattacks**  
_2020-02-16 · real-world · Severity: Medium_

Hackers used AI-based software to imitate the voices of company executives, enabling them to issue fraudulent instructions for financial transfers. This AI-powered voice phishing has led to successful cybercrimes, highlighting the growing threat of AI technologies in facilitating large-scale, sophisticated cyberattacks and financial theft.

**Attack vector:** `other`  

**OWASP Agentic (ASI):** `ASI09`  
**NIST AI RMF:** `MAP-3.5`  
**MITRE ATLAS:** `AML.T0048.003`
