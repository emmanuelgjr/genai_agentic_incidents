### INC-06775  [↗](/incident/INC-06775.html)

**Meituan Accused of Using AI-Driven 'Big Data Discrimination' to Overcharge Loyal Members**  
_2020-12-17 · real-world · Severity: High_

Meituan, a major Chinese food delivery platform, is accused of using AI-powered big data and algorithmic pricing to charge loyal members higher delivery fees than non-members. Users experimentally confirmed the discrepancy, sparking public outcry, regulatory scrutiny, and significant financial and reputational harm to Meituan.

**Attack vector:** `data-exfiltration`
